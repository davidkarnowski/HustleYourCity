Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted more than 1,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (365 requests), graffiti (198), and e-scooter issues (165).

Based on closed requests, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 68.5 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 38.5 hours. Some issues were handled much faster, with reports of shoes on a utility wire resolved in about 45 minutes. Sidewalk repair requests show an average closure time of only 6 minutes, which may indicate administrative closures rather than completed work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure it is logged and tracked by the city.
