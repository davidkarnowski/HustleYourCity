Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data. In the past week, residents submitted over 1,100 requests for city services.

The most common reports were for dumped items (440 calls), e-scooters (246 calls), and graffiti (221 calls). The average time to close a graffiti report was about 26 hours, while dumped item reports averaged about 39 hours. E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to the scooter companies.

Some services saw quick response times. The single restroom maintenance request was closed in about 51 minutes, though very fast closures can sometimes be administrative. Tree maintenance requests were closed in an average of about 3 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 22, 2025 at 8:05 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 22, 2025 at 7:00 PM PST.
