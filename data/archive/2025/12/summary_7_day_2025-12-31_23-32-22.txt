Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,180 service requests were made in the last seven days. The most common reports were for dumped items (442 requests), graffiti (196), and e-scooters (173).

Based on the latest data, the average response time for graffiti removal was just under two days. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 1.5 days on average. Dead animal pickups were handled in an average of about 7 hours. Some categories, like Park Maintenance, showed unusually fast response times of about 37 minutes, which may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
