Hustle Long Beach! is here with the latest 30-day city service response data. As an independent accountability project, this interpreter analyzes the numbers to keep citizens informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood issues.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 2,323 reports. The city took an average of 7.8 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti removal was the second most reported issue, with 1,038 calls and an average response time of 37 hours.

E-Scooter complaints reached 918 reports, though all of these were closed and referred to outside vendors rather than handled directly by city staff.

Street Repair received 203 reports, with an average response time of 3 days. Meanwhile, Park Maintenance handled 73 calls with an average response time of 3.5 days.

Please note that unusually low response times, such as those measured in just minutes, may be due to administrative closures or duplicate ticket merges rather than actual physical work completions.

If you see an issue in your community, make sure to report it. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit requests directly to the city.
