Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen requests.

Over the last 90 days, the highest volume of reported issues involved Dumped Items with 7,000 calls, followed by Graffiti with 3,245 reports, and E-Scooter complaints at 2,886.

Response times vary significantly depending on the type of request. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to Dumped Items and about 9 days for Tree Maintenance. Graffiti removal averaged a 2.3-day response time.

Some categories show exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
