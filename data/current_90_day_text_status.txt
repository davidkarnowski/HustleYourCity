In the last 90 days Long Beach recorded about 16,430 service requests across all categories.

Key highlights:
- Graffiti reports topped the list with 3,607 calls; the average response time was 133 hours (≈ 5.5 days).
- Dumped items generated 6,460 requests; average response ≈ 375 hours (≈ 15.6 days).
- Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were handled quickly, with average response times of 18 hours and 17 hours respectively.
- Tree Maintenance, Light repairs and Sidewalk Repair saw average responses of 58 hours, 51 hours and 78 hours (≈ 2‑3 days).
- Traffic Signal issues took about 110 hours (≈ 4.5 days) to address.

Overall, most categories are being closed within a few days, but some, like dumped items and graffiti, still take longer. Residents can help speed up resolution by reporting issues directly through the Go Long Beach service app.
