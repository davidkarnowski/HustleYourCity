Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service data for the last 90 days.

Dumped Items remains the most frequent request type with 6,556 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 11 days.

Graffiti abatement received 3,078 reports. The average time to close these cases is approximately 3 days.

Street Repair saw 839 requests with an average response time of 5 days. Tree Maintenance received 846 reports and averaged 6 days for response.

Dead Animal Pickup had 429 reports and averaged 14 hours for completion.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.

This data summary was updated at 3:42 AM PST on March 6, 2026 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 3:00 AM PST on March 6, 2026.
