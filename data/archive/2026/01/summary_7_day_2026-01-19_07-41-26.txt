Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,440 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (563 reports), e-scooters (262 reports), and graffiti (211 reports).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 25 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 45 hours on average. Vegetation maintenance requests were notably quick, with an average closure time of about 61 minutes. Some requests, like one for restroom maintenance, were closed in about 16 minutes. Unusually fast closures may be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
