In the last 90 days Long Beach recorded roughly 16,500 service requests across many categories.

The quickest average response times were for Animal Services (about 19 hours) and Dead Animal Pickup (about 17 hours), showing the city’s rapid handling of animal‑related issues.

The slowest average response was for Dumped Items, taking about 351 hours — roughly 14.6 days. Graffiti requests also averaged about 7 days (169 hours), and Street Repair averaged 5.6 days (134 hours).

Overall, most categories fell between 2 and 8 days, with Light, Sidewalk Repair and Trash & Debris averaging under 5 days.
