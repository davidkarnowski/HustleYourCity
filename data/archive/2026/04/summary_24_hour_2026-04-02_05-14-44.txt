Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data from the City of Long Beach. Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 102 cases, the vast majority of which remain in new status. Graffiti followed with 35 total reports, averaging a response time of about 2.9 hours.

Several service categories showed unusually fast resolution times. Vegetation issues averaged a response of just 4 minutes, Tree Maintenance averaged 31 minutes, and Animal Services averaged 85 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures or system reclassifications rather than the actual completion of physical work.

Other notable response averages over the last day include Dead Animal Pickup at 1.7 hours and Sidewalk Repair at 1.6 hours.

Civic engagement helps keep the city accountable and neighborhoods maintained. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
