Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day city service data to keep you informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Over the last 90 days, Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 6,649 reports. The city took an average of 9.8 days to respond to these cases. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,082 calls, averaging a 69-hour response time.

E-Scooter complaints generated 2,808 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies. Infrastructure issues like Street Repair (826 calls) and Tree Maintenance (914 calls) averaged response times of 6.9 days and 6.8 days, respectively.

Some services saw much faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup handled 429 reports in an average of 11.9 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns showed an unusually low response time of just 12 minutes for 6 cases. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Hustle Long Beach! encourages all residents to report neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application. Your reports help track where resources are needed most.
