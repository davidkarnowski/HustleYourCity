Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past seven days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling local issues.

Dumped Items led the request volume with 510 reports. The average response time for these was 3.2 days, though 380 of these requests are currently still marked as new.

Graffiti removal saw 225 requests, with an average response time of just over 25 hours. Most of these cases have already been closed.

Street Repair received 52 requests, averaging a 27-hour response time. Dead Animal Pickup had 34 requests, averaging a 4.2-hour response.

Please keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than the actual completion of the physical work.
