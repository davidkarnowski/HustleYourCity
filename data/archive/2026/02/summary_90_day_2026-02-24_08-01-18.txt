Here is the latest evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days. This data reflects the time it takes for the city to close service tickets submitted by residents.

Dumped Items remain the most frequent request with 6,462 total reports. The average response time for these requests is approximately 12.8 days.
