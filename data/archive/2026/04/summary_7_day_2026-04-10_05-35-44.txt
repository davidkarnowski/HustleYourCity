Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update evaluating city service response times over the past 7 days.

The highest volume of citizen reports involved Dumped Items with 464 calls, Graffiti with 313 calls, and E-Scooter complaints with 271 calls.

Response times varied significantly by category. Dumped Items took an average of 71.1 hours to process. Graffiti reports were handled in an average of 38.5 hours. Damaged Drinking Fountain issues took an average of 4 days to resolve.

Some services showed extremely fast turnaround times. Running Water reports averaged just 33 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
