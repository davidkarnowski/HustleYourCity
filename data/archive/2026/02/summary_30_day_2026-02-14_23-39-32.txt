Here is the latest evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests for the past 30 days. This summary highlights the most frequent service calls and the average time it took for the city to resolve them.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 2,437 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 7.4 days. Graffiti reports were the second most common issue with 1,117 total calls, showing an average response time of 2.3 days.

Infrastructure repairs showed varied completion times. Street Repair requests totaled 224 and took an average of 3.6 days to resolve. Sidewalk Repair requests, with 102 reports, were addressed more quickly with an average response time of 1.3 days.

For public health and safety, Dead Animal Pickup generated 154 reports with an average response time of 8.3 hours. The fastest recorded response category was for Business Licenses and Permit Concerns, which averaged 31 minutes to resolve.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting these issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
