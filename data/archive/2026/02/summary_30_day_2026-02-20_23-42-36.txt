Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service metrics for the last 30 days.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 2,388 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 7.7 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 1,152. The city averaged a response time of 2.2 days for these issues.

Tree Maintenance saw 334 reports with an average response time of 1.2 days. Sidewalk Repair had 108 reports and averaged 1 day for response.

Dead Animal Pickup services handled 149 reports with an average response time of 8.7 hours.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues.
