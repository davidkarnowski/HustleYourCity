Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,223 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (450 requests), followed by e-scooter issues (226) and graffiti removal (180).

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. The average time to address graffiti reports was about 25 hours. Requests for trash and debris cleanup took about 3 days. Restroom maintenance requests were closed in an average of about 83 minutes, though some fast closures can be administrative.
