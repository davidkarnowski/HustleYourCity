Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data. Here is your accountability update on how the City of Long Beach handled service requests over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 512 reports. The city averaged a response time of 28 hours for these issues.

Graffiti removal was the second most reported issue, totaling 206 calls with an average response time of 18 hours.

E-Scooter complaints accounted for 184 reports. All of these were referred to outside agencies, resulting in no direct city response time data.

Dead Animal Pickup handled 44 calls with a rapid average response time of 3 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Street Repair and Tree Maintenance saw 56 and 65 reports respectively. Street repairs averaged an 18-hour response, while tree maintenance requests were addressed in just under 9 hours on average.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted and addressed. Hustle Long Beach! encourages all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
