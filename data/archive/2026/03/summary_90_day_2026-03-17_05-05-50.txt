Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs over the last 90 days.

Dumped items continue to generate the highest volume of calls with 6,669 reports. The city takes an average of 10 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti removal is the second most requested service with 3,084 reports, averaging a response time of nearly 3 days. 

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also show high volumes. Tree maintenance received 911 reports with an average response time of almost a week. Street repair saw 831 requests, taking about 6 days on average to address.

Some services show much faster turnarounds. Dead animal pickups average a 12-hour response time across 441 requests. Meanwhile, business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Public reporting makes a difference. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
