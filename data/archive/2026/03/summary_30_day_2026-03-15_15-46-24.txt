Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 30 days. Here is a factual look at how the city is responding to community issues.

Dumped Items led the request volume with 2,121 calls, taking an average of 5 days to address. Graffiti removal saw 1,040 reports, with crews responding in about 44 hours. E-Scooter complaints generated 983 calls, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable service times include Tree Maintenance with 482 calls averaging a 53-hour response, and Street Repair with 260 calls taking about 3 days to resolve. Dead Animal Pickup was handled efficiently, averaging a 14.5-hour response for 148 calls. Park Maintenance received 66 calls and took an average of 3.5 days to process.

Some categories showed unusually low response times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged just 3 minutes for 2 calls. These rapid times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
