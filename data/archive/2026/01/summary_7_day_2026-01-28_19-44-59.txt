Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Residents submitted a total of 1,520 requests for service.

The most common reports were for Dumped Items (662 requests), Graffiti (290 requests), and E-Scooter issues (223 requests).

Looking at average response times for closed cases, Dumped Items requests took about 3.3 days to resolve. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 31 hours, while streetlight repairs averaged about 3.7 days. Faster responses included Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged under 4 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
