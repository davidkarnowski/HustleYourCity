Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service requests for the last 90 days.

Dumped Items continue to be the highest volume category with 6,572 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 11.5 days.

Graffiti removal received 3,087 reports. Crews are clearing these requests with an average response time of 3.5 days.

Street Repair requests totaled 840, showing a response time of approximately 5 days. Tree Maintenance had a similar volume with 844 reports and a response time of 6 days.

The fastest response category was Business Licenses and Permit Concerns, which were handled in 12 minutes. Dead Animal Pickup also remains responsive, averaging 13.5 hours per request.

We encourage all residents to continue reporting issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
