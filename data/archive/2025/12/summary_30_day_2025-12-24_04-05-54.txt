Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service data.

Residents made over 4,800 service requests this past month. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,952 requests), e-scooter issues (1,073), and graffiti removal (1,062).

Average response times for closed cases varied. Graffiti reports were handled in about 2.5 days, while dumped items took an average of 5.5 days. Street repairs were completed in just under 3 days. Some services were faster, with dead animal pickups averaging about 13 hours. Note that unusually low response times can be due to administrative closures and not always work completion.

To report an issue, use the city's Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 23, 2025, at 8:03 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 23, 2025, at 7:00 PM PST.
