Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2,332 requests in the last month. The average response time for these is about 8 days. Graffiti removal saw 997 reports, with the city responding in an average of 33 hours. E-scooter complaints reached 938, though all were referred to outside agencies or operators.

Street repair requests totaled 204, taking just over 3 days on average to address. Dead animal pickups had 166 reports with an average response time of under 5 hours. Please note that unusually fast response times can sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

Residents are encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application to help keep the city accountable and neighborhoods clean.
