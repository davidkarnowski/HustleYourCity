The latest review of City of Long Beach service requests focuses on activity from the last 90 days. Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,679 reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 12 days.

Graffiti removal received 3,130 reports with an average completion time of roughly 3.5 days. Street Repair requests totaled 834, showing an average response time of nearly 5 days. E-Scooter reports were also high in volume at 2,991, though the majority are referred to third-party operators.

Faster response times were noted for Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged about 12.5 hours across 432 reports.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. Consistent reporting helps track necessary improvements in city services.
