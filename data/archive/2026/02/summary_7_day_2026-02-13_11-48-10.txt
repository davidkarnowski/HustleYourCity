Service request data for the City of Long Beach over the past 7 days indicates high activity volumes for sanitation and maintenance issues.

The category with the highest volume was Dumped Items, totaling 521 reports with an average response time of approximately 34 hours. Graffiti reports followed with 248 total entries and an average response time of roughly 34 hours. There were 247 E-Scooter reports, all of which were closed and referred to the appropriate operators.

Infrastructure maintenance saw 98 reports for Lights with an average response time of 50 hours. Street Repair received 46 reports, averaging a response time of 20 hours. Tree Maintenance recorded 38 reports with a response average of 26 hours.

The fastest response times were recorded for Business Licenses and Permit Concerns, which averaged 31 minutes. Dead Animal Pickup services averaged just under 4 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
