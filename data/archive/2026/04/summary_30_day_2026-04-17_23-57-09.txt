Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at the response times and call totals for key services across Long Beach.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 2,382 reports. The city averaged 8.5 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti removal saw 1,046 reports with an average response time of 53.8 hours.

Street Repair handled 212 calls, averaging 68.3 hours for a response.

Park Maintenance received 75 reports, taking an average of 3.2 days to respond.

Dead Animal Pickup processed 173 calls with a quick average response time of 6.7 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or automatic referrals rather than actual work completions. For example, all 1,094 E-Scooter complaints were immediately closed and referred elsewhere.
