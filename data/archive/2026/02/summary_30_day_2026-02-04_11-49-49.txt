Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the last 30 days.

Illegal dumping was the most reported issue with 2,549 service requests. This was followed by graffiti removal with 1,080 requests and e-scooter issues with 1,005 requests.

The average time to resolve an illegal dumping report was about 9.6 days. In contrast, graffiti reports were closed in an average of 2.6 days. Slower responses included damaged drinking fountains, which took an average of nearly 11 days to close.

Quicker responses included dead animal pickup, averaging about 12 hours. It is important to note that some unusually fast closures can be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion. All 1,005 e-scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies.
