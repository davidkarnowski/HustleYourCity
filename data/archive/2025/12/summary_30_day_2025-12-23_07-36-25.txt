Here is the latest 30-day snapshot of Long Beach city service requests.

The most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (1,947 requests), e-scooters (1,075 requests), and graffiti (1,058 requests). Reports about e-scooters are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Average response times varied across different services. The city closed requests for dumped items in about 5 and a half days. Graffiti removal took an average of 56 hours, while street repairs were completed in just under 3 days. Some of the quickest responses were for Animal Services calls, which were handled in about 10.5 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
