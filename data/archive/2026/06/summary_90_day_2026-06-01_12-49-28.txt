The latest data from the City of Long Beach service call system shows continued activity across key maintenance categories over the last 90 days.

Graffiti removal remains a high-volume service with 3,262 reports, maintaining an average response time of approximately 54 hours. Dumped items continue to be the most frequent request, totaling 6,695 reports with an average response time of about 9 days.
