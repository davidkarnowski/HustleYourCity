The latest data review for City of Long Beach service requests focuses on activity from the last 30 days.

Dumped Items represented the highest volume of service requests with 2,547 total reports. The average response time for this category was approximately 7.7 days.

Graffiti removal requests totaled 1,092. The average time to resolve these issues was roughly 2.4 days.

Street Repair requests saw 262 total reports with an average response time of just over 3 days.

Dead Animal Pickup services recorded 149 reports with an average response time of 11.6 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in certain categories may be the result of administrative closures rather than the completion of physical work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
