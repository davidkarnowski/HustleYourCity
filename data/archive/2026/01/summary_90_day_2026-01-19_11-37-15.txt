Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from the public were for illegally Dumped Items (6,030 reports), Graffiti (3,394 reports), and E-Scooter issues (2,723 reports).

Average city response times to close a service request varied by type. The quickest responses were for Animal Services (about 14 hours) and Dead Animal Pickup (about 15 hours). Response times for Graffiti removal averaged nearly 5 days. For illegally Dumped Items, the average closure time was about 12.5 days. Some requests, like for damaged drinking fountains, show unusually fast closures that may be due to administrative processing rather than completed work.
