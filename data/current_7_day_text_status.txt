Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 421 reports for dumped items and 252 for graffiti, making them the most frequent requests.

The longest response times were for traffic signal issues, which took an average of about 4 days to close. Reports for trash and debris took about 3.5 days.

The quickest responses were for sidewalk repairs, averaging about 55 minutes. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead animal pickups were completed in under 4 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
