Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted nearly 1,400 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (560 requests), graffiti removal (265 requests), and e-scooter issues (241 requests).

For requests that were completed and closed this past week, the average time for graffiti removal was about 34 hours. Dead animal pickups were handled in an average of about 10 hours, while street repair requests were closed in approximately 15 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to notify the city.
