Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service requests over the past 30 days.

The City of Long Beach received a high volume of service requests this past month. The most frequently reported issues were Dumped Items with 2,312 reports, Graffiti with 1,041 reports, and E-Scooter concerns with 1,024 reports. 

Average response times varied widely depending on the type of service. Dumped Items took an average of 8.3 days to address, while Marina Repairs averaged 6.9 days. Graffiti reports were handled more quickly, averaging about 55 hours. Dead Animal Pickups saw an average response time of 7 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting ensures accurate data and helps maintain community standards.

This data summary was updated at April 16, 2026, 1:08 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 16, 2026, 11:00 AM PDT.
