Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 30 days. Here is a factual breakdown of how city services are responding to public requests.

Dumped Items remain the most frequently reported issue with 2262 total calls. The city took an average of 6.8 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most common issue with 982 reports, which saw an average response time of 34.8 hours. 

E-Scooter complaints generated 860 reports. However, all of these were marked as closed referred. Unusually low response times or immediate closures like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. 

Other notable service times include Street Repair, which had 253 reports and an average response time of 70.6 hours. Park Maintenance received 71 reports and took an average of 4.4 days to address. Marina Repair had the longest average wait, taking over 1 week to respond to 5 reports. On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup averaged a response time of 8.8 hours across 155 reports.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps maintain accurate data and holds city departments accountable for their response times.
