Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times over the last 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were "Dumped Items" (6,339 reports) and "Graffiti" (3,275 reports). The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" request was about 14 days. For "Graffiti" reports, the average completion time was about 5 days.

Response times vary widely depending on the type of service needed. Urgent issues like "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were handled quickly, with an average response time of about 12.5 hours. Slower responses included "Marina Repair", which took an average of 5 weeks to close out.
