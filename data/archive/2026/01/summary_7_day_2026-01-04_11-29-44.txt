Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Residents submitted 1,255 requests for service. The most reported issues were for illegally dumped items with 449 requests, followed by e-scooter issues at 229 requests, and graffiti removal with 199 requests.

Based on the data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 28 hours. Pickups for dumped items took an average of 45 hours. Trash and debris cleanups were completed in just over 3 days on average. Some requests, like restroom maintenance, were closed in an average of 83 minutes, though such fast times may reflect administrative closures.
