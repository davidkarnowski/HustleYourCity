Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted more than 1,200 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (455 requests), graffiti (204 requests), and e-scooter issues (180 requests).

Average response times for key services included approximately 35 hours for dumped items and 36 hours for graffiti removal. Some categories showed very fast resolutions, such as Park Maintenance (about 37 minutes) and Restroom Maintenance (about 85 minutes). Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not always reflect on-site work completion.
