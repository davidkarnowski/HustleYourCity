Hustle Long Beach community update on city service response times.

Over the last 30 days, Dumped Items remain the most frequent service request with 2,353 total calls. The average response time for these requests is approximately 4 days. Graffiti removal saw 859 total calls with an average response time of about 2 days.

Tree Maintenance requests totaled 424 calls in the last 30 days, averaging about 2.5 days for a response. Dead Animal Pickup continues to show efficient turnaround, averaging approximately 7 minutes per request over the same period.

Please note that unusually low response times may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach mobile application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
