Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days.

In the last three months, residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (over 6,000 requests) and graffiti (over 3,700 requests).

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report was about 14 days. "Park Maintenance" requests took an average of about 17 days to close. In contrast, urgent requests like "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were typically handled in under 17 hours.

Recent data shows sidewalk repair requests being closed in an average of 45 minutes. This unusually fast time may be due to administrative closures and not physical repairs.
