Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 30 days.

The most reported issue was "Dumped Items" with 2,417 service requests. The average time to close these requests was about 9.5 days. For the 1,011 "Graffiti" reports, the average response time was significantly quicker at about 2.5 days.
