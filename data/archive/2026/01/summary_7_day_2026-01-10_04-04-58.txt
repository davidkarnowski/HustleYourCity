Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,471 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (581 calls), graffiti (234 calls), and e-scooters (218 calls).

The average response time for dumped items was about 2 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 23 hours, and street repairs were handled in about the same time. Faster responses were seen for issues like dead animal pickups and restroom maintenance, both averaging under 10 hours. Note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
