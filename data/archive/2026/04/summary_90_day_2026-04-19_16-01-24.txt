Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items led the service requests with 6,999 reports, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti followed with 3,243 reports, which were cleared in about 56 hours on average. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 2,887 reports, almost all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies.

Tree maintenance and street repair requests took the longest to address, averaging 9 days and 8 days respectively. On the faster side, dead animal pickups averaged just under 10 hours. Business license and permit concerns showed an unusually low response time of just 12 minutes, which may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
