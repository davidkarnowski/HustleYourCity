Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service requests over the past 30 days.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 2,160 requests, taking an average of 5 days to resolve. Graffiti followed with 1,051 reports, averaging a 39-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 908 reports.

Response times vary significantly by category. Business license and permit concerns saw unusually fast closures, averaging just 3 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. On the slower end, marina repairs took about 7.5 days, and general trash and debris cleanups averaged 4 days.

Citizens are encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests directly to the city.
