Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service requests based on city data.

Dumped Items (6,143 requests) and Graffiti (3,723 requests) were the most common issues reported by the community. Response times for these high-volume requests averaged over 15 days for Dumped Items and about 6.5 days for Graffiti.

Response times vary widely by department. Park Maintenance requests averaged nearly 3 weeks to close. In contrast, Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests were typically resolved in under 17 hours. Some categories, like the 2,158 E-Scooter reports, are referred directly to vendors and do not have a city response time listed.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure it is officially logged.

This data summary was updated at December 15, 2025, 7:37 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 15, 2025, 7:00 AM PST.
