Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data to keep the community informed. Over the past 90 days, the City of Long Beach handled thousands of service calls across various departments.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 7,039 reports, taking an average of 9 days to address. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 3,112 reports, averaging a response time of 2.5 days. E-Scooter complaints also saw high numbers, totaling 2,856 reports.

Infrastructure requests showed longer turnaround times. Street repairs generated 828 calls and took an average of 6 days to resolve. Tree maintenance had 927 requests with an average response of nearly 5 days, while sidewalk repairs took about 4.5 days across 355 reports.

Some categories recorded much faster response times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Dead animal pickups averaged 10 hours for 447 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 3, 2026, 1:08 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 2, 2026, 11:00 PM PDT.
