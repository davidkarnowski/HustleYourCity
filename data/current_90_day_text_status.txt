City service response times in the last 90 days show notable improvements across many categories.

Graffiti reports (3,660) are now closed in an average of 137 hours (about 5.7 days), down from the all‑time average of 351 hours. Tree maintenance (820 requests) is being addressed in roughly 54 hours (2.2 days), a dramatic drop from the historic 3,773 hours. Light outages (572) are resolved in about 44 hours, and sidewalk repairs (344) in 58 hours—both far faster than the previous multi‑week averages. Street repair, traffic signal, and trash & debris requests are also seeing average closures within 4‑5 days, compared with historic periods that often stretched over weeks.

Overall, 23 % of the 90‑day requests were closed within a day (average under 24 hours for Animal Services, Dead Animal Pickup, and several other types). Faster turn‑around times suggest the city’s crews are responding more quickly, though some categories like Dumped Items (average 367 hours) still have room for improvement.

Residents can help keep these trends moving forward by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service app.
