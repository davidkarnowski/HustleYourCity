Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests were for Dumped Items (5,912 reports) and Graffiti (3,540 reports). The average response time for dumped item removal was about 14 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 4.5 days.

Response times for other common issues include:
- Park Maintenance: About 16 days
- Street Repair: About 8 days
- Tree Maintenance: About 6.5 days

Quicker responses were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services requests, both averaging under 17 hours.
