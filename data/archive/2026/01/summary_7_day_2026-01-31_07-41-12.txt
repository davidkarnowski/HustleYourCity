Here is the latest weekly summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past 7 days, residents submitted 1,468 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (587 requests) and graffiti (283 requests). Together, these two categories accounted for over half of all service calls.

The average city response time to close a graffiti report was 27 hours. For dumped items, the average response time was approximately 55 hours, or just over two days. Tree maintenance requests were handled the fastest, with an average response time of under 8 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
