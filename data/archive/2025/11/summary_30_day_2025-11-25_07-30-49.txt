Latest 30‑day service request snapshot for Long Beach

In the past 30 days the city logged 1,413 graffiti reports with an average response time of about 72 hours (3 days). Dumped items remained the top volume at 1,978 requests, taking roughly 158 hours (6.6 days) to close on average. Tree maintenance saw 229 calls and was addressed quickly – average response just over 10 hours. Sidewalk repair had 91 requests, averaging 15 hours, while street repair recorded 205 calls with a 21‑hour average. Light outages generated 249 reports, resolved in about 44 hours on average. Other categories: vegetation (35 calls, 39 hours), park maintenance (55 calls, 44 hours), dead animal pickup (150 calls, 19 hours), and trash & debris (51 calls, 69 hours). E‑Scooter reports (691) are recorded as “Closed Referred” with no response time data.

Overall, the city handled over 7,000 service requests in the last month, with most categories showing response times under three days. Faster turnaround is evident for tree work and sidewalk repairs, while larger volume items like graffiti and dumped items still take several days.

Residents are encouraged to report issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app to help maintain these response standards.
