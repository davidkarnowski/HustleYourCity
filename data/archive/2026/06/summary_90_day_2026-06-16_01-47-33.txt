The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, residents submitted 6,634 reports for dumped items and 3,281 reports for graffiti. Response times for these categories averaged about 8 days and 65 hours respectively.

Other notable service volumes include 3,341 reports regarding e-scooters and 684 reports for tree maintenance. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest turnaround times, with dead animal pickups averaging under 6 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
