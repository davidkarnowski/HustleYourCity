Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the past 30 days.

Residents submitted thousands of service requests, with the most common reports being for illegally dumped items (2,020 requests), e-scooter issues (936), and graffiti removal (915).

Based on closed requests, the average response time for illegally dumped items was about 5 days. Reports for trash and debris took an average of nearly 8 days to resolve. Graffiti removal requests were handled more quickly, with an average closure time of about 2 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
