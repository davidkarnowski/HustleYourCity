Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service call data.

In the last 24 hours, residents submitted 209 service requests. Reports for "Dumped Items" were the most common issue, with 102 new requests, making up nearly half of all reports. The second most frequent report was for "Graffiti", with 39 requests.

The city's data shows some completed requests were handled very quickly. These unusually fast times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion. Other closed cases had response times averaging around 9 hours for "Graffiti" and 11.5 hours for "Street Repair".
