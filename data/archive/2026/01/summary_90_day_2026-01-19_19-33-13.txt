Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (over 6,000 requests), graffiti (3,400 requests), and e-scooters (over 2,700 requests). The average response time for graffiti removal was just under 5 days, while dumped items took about 13 days. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups, handled in about 15 hours on average.

It is important to note that some requests, like the 2,700+ for e-scooters, are referred to outside companies. Unusually fast closures can also be due to administrative updates and not on-site work completion.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.
