Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests. In the past month, residents submitted over 5,000 reports through the city's service system. The most frequent issues were illegally dumped items with 1,933 reports, graffiti with 1,080 reports, and improperly parked e-scooters with 989 reports.

Response times for top issues varied. The average time to close a report for dumped items was about 6 days, with a large number of cases still in progress. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 2.5 days on average. Quicker responses were seen for issues like Animal Services, which averaged about 10 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to request service and help track city performance.
