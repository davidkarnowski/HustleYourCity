Here is the latest weekly summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted nearly 1,400 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (591 calls), graffiti (247 calls), and e-scooters (215 calls).

Based on resolved cases, the city's average response time for graffiti removal was just under 20 hours. Dumped items took an average of about 2.75 days to be addressed. Animal-related services were among the quickest responses, with dead animal pickups handled in about 5.6 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
