Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at the numbers and how long it takes for the city to respond to citizen requests.

Over the past month, Dumped Items led the service requests with 2,365 reports. Graffiti followed with 1,089 calls, and E-Scooter issues generated 1,000 reports. Other notable totals include 250 requests for Tree Maintenance and 224 for Light issues.

Response times vary significantly depending on the type of issue. Dumped Items took an average of 8 days to address. Marina Repair requests averaged 6 days, and Park Maintenance took about 3.5 days. For issues resolved more quickly, Graffiti was handled in about 41.5 hours, and Dead Animal Pickup averaged 7 hours. 

Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual completed work.
