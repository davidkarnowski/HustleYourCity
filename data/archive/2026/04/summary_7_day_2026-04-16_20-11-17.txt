Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items led the service requests with 592 calls. The average response time for these was 4.2 days. Graffiti followed with 271 reports, taking an average of 44 hours to address. E-Scooter complaints reached 249, with nearly all being referred to outside agencies.

On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup handled 39 calls with an average response time of 4.6 hours, and Animal Services managed 15 calls in an average of 6 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Several categories experienced response times extending beyond three days. Reports of Shoes on a Utility Wire averaged 4.4 days, Traffic Signal issues took 3.5 days, and Abandoned Shopping Carts averaged 3.4 days.
