Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Residents submitted nearly 1,200 service requests through the Go Long Beach app and other reporting systems. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (426), e-scooters (254), and graffiti (245).

Response times for closed cases varied by issue. The average response for graffiti cleanup was about 28 hours. Reports for illegally dumped items were addressed in about 30 hours on average. Requests for trash and debris cleanup took longer, averaging over two days to close out. Some faster responses, like sidewalk repairs averaging under 4 hours, may be due to administrative closures and not work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
