Here is the latest independent review of City of Long Beach service requests for the past 30 days.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 2,236 reports filed. The average response time for these requests is currently 6 days. Graffiti reports are the second most frequent issue with 1,112 calls, showing an average response time of 2 days.

Street Repair requests totaled 248 with an average wait time of 4 days. Tree Maintenance saw 428 reports with a response time of just under 2 days. Dead Animal Pickup services responded to 137 calls with an average time of 12 hours.

There were 961 reports regarding E-Scooters, which are referred to third-party operators.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
