Here is the latest 30-day snapshot of Long Beach service request data.

The most frequent requests from residents were for Dumped Items (2,564 reports) and Graffiti (1,117 reports). According to city data, the average response time to resolve a dumped item case was about 8.5 days. Graffiti requests were handled more quickly, with an average resolution time of about 2.5 days.

Response times for other common issues included about 2.8 days for street light repairs (422 requests) and just over 3 days for tree maintenance (183 requests). Some of the fastest responses were for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services, which were resolved in about 11 hours and 12.5 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
