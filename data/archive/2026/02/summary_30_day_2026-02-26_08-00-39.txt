An evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests for the last 30 days highlights current response times and call volumes.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 2,274 reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 1 week. Graffiti reports totaled 1,131, with an average response time of roughly 2 days.

Infrastructure data shows that Street Repair received 252 reports with an average response time of 3 days. Tree Maintenance had 412 reports with a response average of 1.5 days. Light repair requests totaled 400 and averaged just over 2 days for a response.

Dead Animal Pickup services handled 140 calls with an average response of 12 hours. Business License concerns recorded an average response of 12 minutes. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
