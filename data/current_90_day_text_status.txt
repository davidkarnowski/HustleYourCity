Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past 90 days, the most frequent requests from residents were for Dumped Items (6,155 calls) and Graffiti (3,727 calls). The average response time to close a Dumped Items request was about 15.5 days, while Graffiti requests were closed in about 6.5 days on average.

Other key service response times include Street Repair at about 9.5 days and Sidewalk Repair at about 5.5 days. Requests for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services were closed much faster, with an average response time of under 17 hours. Note that some rapid closures may be administrative and not reflect on-the-ground work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to request service and contribute to this data.

This data summary was updated at December 15, 2025, at 3:31 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 15, 2025, at 3:00 AM PST.
