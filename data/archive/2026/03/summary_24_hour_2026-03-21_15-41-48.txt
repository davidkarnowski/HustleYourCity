Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 24-hour city service response data to keep residents informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 103 total reports, though 90 of these currently remain in the New status. E-Scooter complaints followed with 55 reports, all of which were immediately closed and referred. Graffiti saw 14 reports with an average response time of about 3 hours.

The data shows several categories with extremely fast average response times. Vegetation reports averaged 11 minutes, Running Water averaged 16 minutes, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 43 minutes, Tree Maintenance averaged 54 minutes, and Sidewalk Repair averaged 64 minutes. Citizens should note that these unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

For issues that took slightly longer, Dumped Items averaged a 6.26-hour response time for the cases that were processed, and Street Repair averaged 2.82 hours across 9 reports.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues to ensure they are tracked. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
