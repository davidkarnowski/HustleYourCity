Here is the latest 24-hour city service data update from Hustle Long Beach! We are tracking how quickly the City of Long Beach responds to citizen reports.

In the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 105 total reports and an average response time of 6.26 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed with 55 reports, all of which were referred and closed. Graffiti issues saw 22 reports with an average response time of 3.29 hours. Street Repair had 10 reports averaging a 6.59-hour response.

Several categories showed unusually fast response times. Park Maintenance averaged just 3 minutes for its 3 reports. Vegetation issues averaged 11 minutes, Running Water reports averaged 16 minutes, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 59 minutes, and Sidewalk Repair averaged 64 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times, particularly those under an hour, may be due to administrative closures in the system rather than actual physical work completions.
