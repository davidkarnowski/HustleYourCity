Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest independent review of city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,926 reports. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,097 calls, averaging a 61-hour response time.

Street Repair saw 844 requests, taking an average of 6 days to address. Meanwhile, Business License and Permit Concerns showed an incredibly fast average response of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure these matters are tracked and resolved.

This data summary was updated at March 31, 2026, at 10:31 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 31, 2026, at 7:00 PM PDT.
