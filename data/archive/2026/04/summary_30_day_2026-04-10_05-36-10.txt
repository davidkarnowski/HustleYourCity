Hustle Long Beach! is here with your latest 30-day city service accountability update. The newest data shows how long it takes the City of Long Beach to respond to community issues.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 2318 calls in the last 30 days. The city took an average of 7.8 days to respond to these reports.

Graffiti was the second most common issue, generating 1034 reports. The average response time for graffiti was about 37 hours.

Other notable service times include street repair, which saw 201 reports and an average response time of 3 days. Dead animal pickups totaled 162 reports with a 7-hour average response time.

Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see a problem in your neighborhood, make sure it gets tracked. Encourage your friends and neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
