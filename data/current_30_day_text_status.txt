City service response update – last 30 days

Graffiti reports (1,268) were closed in an average of 52 hours (≈2 days), down from 111 hours 60 days ago and 170 hours 90 days ago.

Tree‑maintenance tickets (272) averaged just 13 hours to close.

Dumped‑items calls (2,016) took about 114 hours (≈5 days), a big improvement from 318 hours 60 days ago.

Street‑repair requests (183) were resolved in roughly 31 hours, faster than the 94‑hour average a month earlier.

Traffic‑signal issues (44) now average 49 hours, compared with 91 hours two months prior.

Sidewalk‑repair jobs (111) were completed in about 15 hours.

Other categories with quick turn‑around: Light (≈29 hours), Animal Services (≈20 hours), and Vegetation (≈19 hours).

Overall, response times are trending shorter across most service types. Some unusually low times may reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

Residents can report new issues or check status via the Go Long Beach service app.
