Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past week.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue, with 458 service requests submitted. This was followed by 236 reports for graffiti and 220 for e-scooter issues.

According to the city's data, the average response time to close graffiti reports was about 24 hours. For dumped items, the average response was approximately 44 hours. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of about 3 hours, though unusually low times can be due to administrative closures and not work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city.
