Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service request data published by the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at how city services have performed over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,881 reports. The city took an average of 9.5 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,104 calls, which saw an average response time of 65 hours. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 2,820 reports, though all of these were simply referred to outside agencies and closed.

Street Repair and Tree Maintenance also saw significant activity. There were 866 street repair requests averaging a 6.5-day response time, and 944 tree maintenance requests averaging a 5.7-day response time.

On the faster end, Dead Animal Pickups averaged an 11-hour response time across 447 reports. Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times are often due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
