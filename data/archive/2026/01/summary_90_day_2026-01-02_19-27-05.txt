Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for Dumped Items (5,851 reports), Graffiti (3,601 reports), and E-Scooters (2,565 reports).

The data shows varying average response times for closing service requests. Reports for Park Maintenance took about 2.5 weeks to resolve, while requests for Dumped Items took around 2 weeks. Graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of about 5 days. Faster responses were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup (about 17 hours) and Animal Services (about 16 hours).
