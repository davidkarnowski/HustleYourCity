Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

The most frequent issues reported by residents were for dumped items (6,068 reports) and graffiti (3,695 reports). The city also received over 600 reports each for street repairs, tree maintenance, and street light issues.

On average, resolving a dumped item report took about 15 days. Graffiti removal requests were closed in about 6 days on average. Street repair requests averaged about 9.5 days, while street light issues were addressed in about 7.5 days.

Services with quicker response times included Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup, with reports for both categories being closed in under 17 hours on average.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
