Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service request was for "Dumped Items" with over 6,100 reports. The average response time for these requests was about 13 days. The second most common request was for "Graffiti" removal, with over 3,300 reports handled in an average of 5 days. The 2,714 "E-Scooter" reports were referred to outside vendors.

Response times for other services varied. Urgent requests like "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were typically resolved in about 12 hours. Slower responses included "Marina Repair," which averaged about 5 weeks, and "Restroom Maintenance," which took about 12 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
