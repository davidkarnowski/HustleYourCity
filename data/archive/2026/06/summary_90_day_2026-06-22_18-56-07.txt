The latest data on city service requests in Long Beach shows active response times across several categories over the past 90 days.

Graffiti removal remains a high-volume service with 3,313 requests, showing an average response time of approximately 2.8 days. Dumped items continue to be a primary concern for residents, with 6,560 total requests and an average response time of about 8 days.
