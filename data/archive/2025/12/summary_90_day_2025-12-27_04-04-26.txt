Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days. Residents submitted over 15,500 requests for service. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,953 reports), graffiti (3,665 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,385 reports).

Let's look at some average response times for closed requests. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 5.5 days. For dumped items, the average response time was about 2 weeks. Street repair requests were closed in about 10 days on average.

Some services saw much faster resolutions. Reports for dead animal pickups were handled in about 16 hours on average, and animal services requests were closed in about 15 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at December 26, 2025, 8:01 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 26, 2025, 7:00 PM PST.
