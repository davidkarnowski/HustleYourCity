The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows significant activity in service requests over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 8,178 reports and an average response time of approximately 16 days. Graffiti reports totaled 2,786 with a much faster average response time of about 3 days.

Tree maintenance requests saw 952 reports with an average response time of about 4 days. Animal services and dead animal pickup continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging roughly 11 hours and 6 hours respectively.

Please note that unusually low response times can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to continue using the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city services in their neighborhoods.
