Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past week.

Reports of dumped items (668 calls) and graffiti (285 calls) were the most frequent service requests submitted by residents.

The average city response time to close reports for dumped items was about 3.4 days. Streetlight outage reports took an average of 3.5 days.

The city responded more quickly to other issues. The average time to resolve dead animal pickups was about 3.5 hours, and graffiti cleanup requests were closed in about 30 hours on average.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
