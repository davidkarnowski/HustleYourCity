Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days. Residents submitted 4,922 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (1,915 calls), e-scooters (1,048 calls), and graffiti (997 calls).

Based on data for closed cases, the average response time for dumped item reports was about 8.5 days. In contrast, requests for animal services were handled much faster, with an average response time of about 8.8 hours. Some issues, like damaged drinking fountains, show a response time of just over 2 hours. These unusually fast times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

To report non-emergency issues in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application. Your reports provide the data needed to track these trends.
