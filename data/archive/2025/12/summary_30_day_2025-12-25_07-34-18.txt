Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 30 days.

Residents submitted nearly 5,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,932 requests), graffiti (1,034), and e-scooters (1,021).

The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 8 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 56 hours, while street repair requests took about 61 hours. Some response times may appear unusually fast, which can be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
