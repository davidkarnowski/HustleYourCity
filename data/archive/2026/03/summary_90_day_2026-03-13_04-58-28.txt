Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest third-party accountability update on city service response times. As an independent data interpreter, this analysis of the Long Beach service call data from the last 90 days is designed to keep you informed on how local issues are being handled by the city.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue with 6,578 calls. The city's average response time for these items is currently about 10 days. Graffiti is the second most common issue with 3,081 reports, taking an average of 3 days to resolve.

Other notable statistics include 890 Tree Maintenance requests averaging about 6 days for a response, and 836 Street Repair calls also taking about 6 days on average. Dead Animal Pickups are being handled much faster, with 437 reports averaging a 13-hour response time.

Some categories show unusually fast resolution times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or referrals rather than actual work completions. Similarly, all 2,931 E-Scooter complaints were immediately closed and referred elsewhere.
