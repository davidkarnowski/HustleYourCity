City service response times have improved noticeably over the past 90 days.

Graffiti reports (3,737 cases) are now closed in an average of 165 hours (about 7 days), down from the all‑time average of 353 hours.  
Sidewalk repairs (316 cases) are resolved in an average of 122 hours (≈5 days), a dramatic drop from the historic 4,742‑hour average.  
Tree maintenance (746 cases) and vegetation work (108 cases) are now completed in roughly 155 hours (≈6 days) and 45 hours (≈2 days) respectively, far faster than the previous multi‑month averages.  
Animal Services (213 cases) and Dead Animal Pickup (434 cases) are being closed in under 18 hours on average, compared with historic averages of over 3,900 hours.  
Street repairs (646 cases) average 236 hours (≈10 days) versus the old 4,658‑hour benchmark.  
Traffic signal issues (103 cases) are now addressed in about 209 hours (≈9 days), down from 2,500 hours.  

Overall, most service categories are seeing response times reduced to days rather than weeks or months.
