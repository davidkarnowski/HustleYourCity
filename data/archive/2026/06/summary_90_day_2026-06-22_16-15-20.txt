The latest city service data for Long Beach shows a high volume of requests over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 6,562 reports, followed by 3,332 reports regarding E-Scooters and 3,299 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Animal services and dead animal pickups are seeing the fastest turnaround, averaging under 9 hours. Conversely, issues like dumped items and sidewalk repairs are taking longer, with average response times exceeding 7 days. Please note that unusually low response times for some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
