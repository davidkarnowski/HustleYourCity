Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled service requests over the past 7 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 594 reports. The city averaged a response time of 61 hours for these issues, though 422 reports remain in the new status.

Graffiti reports totaled 225. The city responded to these in an average of 21 hours, successfully closing 134 of those cases.

Dead animal pickups showed a rapid turnaround. There were 34 requests with an average response time of 4.5 hours. Tree maintenance also moved quickly, averaging 10.5 hours across 59 reports. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times can sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Street repair and sidewalk repair saw 34 and 19 reports respectively, with average response times hovering around 23 to 28 hours.

Residents are encouraged to stay active in improving the community. You can report local issues directly by downloading and using the Go Long Beach service application.
