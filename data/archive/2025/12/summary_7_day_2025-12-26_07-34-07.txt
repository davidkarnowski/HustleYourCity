Here is the latest Long Beach city service data summary for the past week.

Over 900 service requests were made through the Go Long Beach system. The most reported issues were illegally dumped items (311 reports), improperly parked e-scooters (221 reports), and graffiti (188 reports).

Average response times for closed reports varied. The average time to close a graffiti report was about 34 hours, while reports for dumped items were closed in about 25 hours. Dead animal pickups were completed in an average of 7.5 hours.

Some categories saw very fast closures. The average response for park maintenance was about 69 minutes. It is possible that unusually fast response times reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion. All 11 reports for trash and debris in the last week are still listed as new.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it is the first step. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit a report to the city.

This data summary was updated at December 25, 2025 at 11:31 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 25, 2025 at 11:00 PM PST.
