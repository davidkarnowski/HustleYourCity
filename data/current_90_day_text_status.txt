The latest data for city services in Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city received 2,576 reports of graffiti and 7,539 reports regarding dumped items.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains efficient, with an average response time of approximately 7.5 hours. In contrast, dumped items have seen an average response time of about 17 days, and street repair requests are averaging roughly 6 days.

Please note that unusually low response times for some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
