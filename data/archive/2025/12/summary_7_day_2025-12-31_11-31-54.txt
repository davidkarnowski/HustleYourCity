Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service call data for the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,100 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (413 requests), graffiti (189 requests), and e-scooter issues (166 requests).

Based on the data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 53 hours. Dumped item reports were closed in an average of 39 hours. Some categories, like park maintenance, showed unusually fast closure times of around 37 minutes, which may be due to administrative closures and not work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most direct way to alert the city.
