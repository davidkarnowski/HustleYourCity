Graffiti reports remain the most common issue in the last 90 days, with 3,692 requests. The average response time was 161 hours (about 6.7 days). Dumped Items also saw high volume (6,166 requests) and took an average of 369 hours (about 15 days) to address.

Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were handled much faster. All 226 Animal Services calls and 438 Dead Animal Pickup calls were closed, with average response times of 16.8 hours and 16.6 hours respectively.

Other service categories showed moderate response times:

- Light repairs: 630 requests, avg 38.8 hours.
- Sidewalk Repair: 321 requests, avg 35.9 hours.
- Street Repair: 633 requests, avg 82.1 hours.

Overall, the city processed over 15,000 service calls across all types in the past three months. Faster response times often reflect closed cases, while longer averages may include administrative closures.
