Hustle Long Beach! is here with the latest government accountability update. The City of Long Beach service call data over the past 24 hours has been analyzed to keep citizens informed on local service response times and call volumes.

In the last day, Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 75 reports, though 67 of these remain in the new status. E-Scooter complaints followed with 48 reports, all of which were referred to other agencies. Graffiti was the third most reported issue with 26 calls, and 17 of those are currently in progress.

The city recorded incredibly fast average response times for several categories over the past 24 hours. Vegetation issues averaged 32 minutes, Street Repair averaged 37 minutes, and Dumped Items averaged 40 minutes. Tree Maintenance requests were handled in an average of 86 minutes, while Graffiti responses took about 1.6 hours. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
