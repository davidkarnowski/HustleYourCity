Here's a look at Long Beach's service response data from the past week, based on 1,385 service requests made by the public.

The most common issues reported were dumped items (573 requests), graffiti (246 requests), and e-scooter issues (244 requests). The average response time for dumped items was about 44 hours, while graffiti cleanup took about 32 hours on average. E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Quicker responses this week included dead animal pickups, which averaged about 4.7 hours, and animal service calls, which averaged about 7.1 hours. It is important to note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than the completion of physical work.

You can help improve city services by reporting issues in your neighborhood. Use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone to submit a request.
