In the past week Long Beach residents reported 376 dumped‑item cases, with 205 new requests. The average response time was about 45.5 hours (just under 2 days).  

Graffiti calls totaled 266; 145 were closed and the average response was 28.8 hours.  

Tree maintenance saw 27 reports, resolved in an average of 20.7 hours.  

Street repair requests numbered 31 with an average turnaround of 28.4 hours.

Fastest responses were for traffic signals (8 reports) at just 0.57 hours (≈34 minutes) and dead‑animal pickups (15 reports) at 6.5 hours.
