Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day city service response data. Here is a look at the current call totals and response times for the City of Long Beach.

Over the past 90 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,955 reports. Because the average response time was over 72 hours, this translates to an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,240 calls, taking an average of 58 hours to address. E-Scooter complaints also saw high numbers, totaling 2,923 reports.

Several infrastructure requests took longer than 72 hours to complete. Tree Maintenance requests averaged 9 days, and Street Repair took about 8 days. Conversely, Business License and Permit Concerns showed an unusually low average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickup remained relatively fast, averaging under 10 hours per call.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps ensure that Long Beach neighborhoods receive the necessary attention and maintenance.
