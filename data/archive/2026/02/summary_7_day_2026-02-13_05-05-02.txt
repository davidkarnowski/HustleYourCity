Here is the latest update on Long Beach city service requests from the past 7 days. We are an independent accountability group and do not represent the city government.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 519 reports. The average response time was approximately 1.5 days. Graffiti followed with 247 reports and an average response time of 1.4 days.

Street Repair requests totaled 46 with a response time of about 20 hours. Street Light issues saw 90 reports, taking roughly 2 days for a response.

The fastest service responses were for Business License concerns, averaging 31 minutes, and Dead Animal Pickup, averaging just under 4 hours. Trash and Debris reports had a longer wait time of about 3.2 days.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues.
