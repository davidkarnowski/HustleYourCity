Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the last 90 days.

In the past three months, residents submitted nearly 16,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,187 requests), graffiti (3,719 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,249 requests).

Based on the data for closed cases, the city's average response time for graffiti removal was about 6 days. For dumped items, the average was about 15 days. More urgent requests like dead animal pickups were handled much faster, with an average response time of about 17 hours.
