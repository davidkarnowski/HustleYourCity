The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,805 requests for dumped items, with an average response time of approximately 16.6 days. Graffiti removal remains a high-volume service, with 2,614 requests handled in an average of 2.6 days.

Tree maintenance saw 1,062 requests over the last 90 days, averaging about 4.2 days for a response. Dead animal pickups continue to be addressed quickly, averaging 7.3 minutes per request.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
