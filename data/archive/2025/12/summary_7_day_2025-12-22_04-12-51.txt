Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the past week.

Over 1,150 service requests were submitted through the Go Long Beach system. The most common issue reported was "Dumped Items," with 428 requests. The average time to close these requests was about 1 day and 9 hours. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 233 requests, which were closed in about 21 hours on average.

Some services showed very fast closures. For example, the average response time for Sidewalk Repair was about 45 minutes. These quick closures may reflect administrative updates rather than on-site work completion.
