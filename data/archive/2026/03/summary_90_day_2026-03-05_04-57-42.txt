Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data covering the last 90 days.

The highest volume of service requests continues to be for Dumped Items, with 6,575 total reports. The average time to resolve these issues is currently 11 and a half days.

Graffiti removal remains a priority with 3,089 reports filed. The average response time for graffiti is approximately 3 and a half days.

For infrastructure, Street Repair requests are averaging 5 days for resolution, while Tree Maintenance is taking about 6 days. Trash and Debris reports are currently seeing a response time of roughly 10 days.

Dead Animal Pickup remains one of the faster services, averaging 14 hours per response.

Please note that unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than physical work completion. Additionally, E-Scooter reports are often marked closed quickly because they are referred to private operators.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues.
