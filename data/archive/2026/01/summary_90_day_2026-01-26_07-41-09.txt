Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days. The most common reports from residents were for dumped items (6,159 requests) and graffiti (3,337 requests).

Average response times for these top issues were about 13 days for dumped items and about 5 days for graffiti removal. Other notable response times include approximately 5.5 days for street repairs and nearly 4 weeks for marina repairs.

Faster responses were recorded for other services. The average time to resolve a dead animal pickup was about 14 hours, and animal services calls were closed in about 13 hours on average.
