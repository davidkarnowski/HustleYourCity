Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest daily accountability update on city service response times.

Over the past 24 hours, the highest volume of service requests involved Dumped Items, with 65 reports. Nearly all of these cases are currently marked as new and are awaiting a response. Street lighting issues also saw a notable volume with 17 new reports that have not yet been processed.

For issues that did see action, the city responded to 19 Graffiti reports with an average response time of about 9 hours. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
