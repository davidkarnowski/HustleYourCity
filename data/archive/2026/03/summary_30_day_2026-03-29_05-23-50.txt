Here is the latest 30-day city service data summary from Hustle Long Beach! evaluating how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items led the request board with 2,219 reports. The city took an average of 7 days to process these requests. Graffiti followed with 983 reports, averaging a 40.5-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 900 reports, though all of these were referred to outside vendors.

Street Repair saw 245 requests, taking just over 3 days to resolve on average. Dead Animal Pickup had 155 requests and showed a turnaround of 8.8 hours. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times can sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completion.
