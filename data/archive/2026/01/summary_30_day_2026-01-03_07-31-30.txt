Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past month, the most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (1,898 reports), e-scooter issues (1,012 reports), and graffiti (951 reports).

According to the data, the average response time to close a report for dumped items was about 8.5 days. For graffiti removal, the average response time was about 2.3 days. Quicker responses were recorded for issues like dead animal pickups, which were resolved in about 14 hours on average. E-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure it is logged by the city.

This data summary was updated at January 2, 2026 at 11:28 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 2, 2026 at 11:00 PM PST.
