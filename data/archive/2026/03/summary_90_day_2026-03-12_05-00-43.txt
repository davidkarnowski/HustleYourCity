Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest service call data to track how the City of Long Beach is responding to community issues. Over the last 90 days, the city received thousands of requests, with significant variations in response times across different departments.

The most frequently reported issue was Dumped Items, totaling 6,592 calls with an average response time of 10.4 days. Graffiti was the second most common issue, generating 3,111 reports that took an average of 3.2 days to address. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 2,933 calls, though all of these were referred to outside agencies rather than closed by the city directly.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw high volumes. The city received 1,038 reports for Light issues, averaging a 3.3-day response time. Tree Maintenance generated 890 calls with an average response of 5.8 days, and Street Repair saw 841 calls averaging 5.7 days. Dead Animal Pickup was handled much faster, with 435 calls averaging a 13.3-hour response time.

At the extremes, Marina Repair requests took an average of 32.5 days to resolve. Conversely, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues. Consistent reporting provides the data needed to hold city services accountable and improve neighborhood conditions.
