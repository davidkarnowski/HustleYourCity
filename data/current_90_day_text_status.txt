The latest 90‑day service report shows how quickly Long Beach city departments are handling requests.

- Graffiti: 3,699 reports with an average response time of 152 hours (about 6 days). This is a big improvement from the all‑time average of 351 hours (≈15 days).
- Dumped Items: 6,407 reports, avg. response 363 hours (≈15 days), slower than the historic 260 hours (≈11 days).
- Tree Maintenance: 818 reports, avg. response 53 hours (≈2 days), far better than the historic 3,772 hours (≈157 days).
- Street Repair: 627 reports, avg. response 100 hours (≈4 days) versus the historic 4,517 hours (≈188 days).
- Light repairs: 579 reports, avg. response 42 hours (≈1.8 days), down from the historic 1,017 hours (≈42 days).
- Animal Services & Dead Animal Pickup: 233 and 457 reports respectively, both resolved in under 18 hours on average (≈17 minutes), showing rapid action.

Overall, 23,000+ service calls were logged in the past 90 days, with many categories seeing faster turn‑around times compared with long‑term averages.

Residents can continue to report issues and track progress using the Go Long Beach service app.
