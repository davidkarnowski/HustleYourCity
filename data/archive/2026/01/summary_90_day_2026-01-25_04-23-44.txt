Here is a look at the latest Long Beach city service data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (6,204 calls) and graffiti (3,384 calls).

Response times vary by the type of service requested. On average, reports of illegally dumped items were closed in about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests were handled more quickly, with an average completion time of about 5 days. Urgent issues like dead animal pickups were typically resolved in under 15 hours.

It is important to note that some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.

To report issues like potholes, graffiti, or illegal dumping, use the Go Long Beach service application.
