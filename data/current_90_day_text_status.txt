The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 7,761 reports and an average response time of approximately 16 days. Graffiti reports totaled 2,578 with a much faster average response time of about 67 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 1,059 reports with an average response time of approximately 4 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging roughly 11 hours and 7 hours respectively over the same 90-day period.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
