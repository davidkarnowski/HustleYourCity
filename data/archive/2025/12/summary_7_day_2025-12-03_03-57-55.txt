Graffiti reports topped the last week with 251 requests. 162 were closed and the average response time was about 33 hours, far faster than the city’s historic average of over 350 hours.

Dumped Items generated 435 calls. 38 were closed, 135 are in progress and the average response time was roughly 58 hours, down from the historic 261‑hour average.

Light issues saw 48 reports, 35 closed, with an average response of 55 hours (historically about 1,006 hours).

Street Repair had 34 requests, split evenly between closed and in‑progress, averaging 32 hours to respond (historically 22 hours).

Dead Animal Pickup logged 20 calls, 18 closed, with a quick 10‑hour average response (historically over 4,500 hours).

Other categories with notable activity include Sidewalk Repair (12 requests, avg 55 hours), Tree Maintenance (22 requests, avg 27 hours) and Animal Services (11 closed calls, avg 8.5 hours).

Overall, response times this week are markedly shorter than long‑term averages, indicating faster city action on many issues.
