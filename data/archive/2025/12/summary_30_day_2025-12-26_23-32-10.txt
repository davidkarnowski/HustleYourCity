Here's a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 30 days.

Residents made nearly 5,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,915 calls), e-scooters (1,052 calls), and graffiti (991 calls).

For the highest volume category, dumped items, the average response time to close a request was about 8 days. The average response time for graffiti removal was about 2 days.

Some of the quickest responses included Animal Services, which averaged under 9 hours, and dead animal pickups, which averaged about 13 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
