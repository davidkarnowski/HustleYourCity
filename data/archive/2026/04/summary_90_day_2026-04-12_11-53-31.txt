Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest third-party accountability update on city service response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of service calls with 7006 reports. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 3262 reports, averaging a 2.3-day response time. E-scooter complaints reached 2924 reports, though nearly all were administratively referred and closed.

Other notable service times include tree maintenance, which took an average of 7.7 days, and street repair requests, which averaged 7.2 days. On the faster end, dead animal pickups averaged 10.6 hours. Business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 12, 2026, 4:48 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 12, 2026, 3:00 AM PDT.
