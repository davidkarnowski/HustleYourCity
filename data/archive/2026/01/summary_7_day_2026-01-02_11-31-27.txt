Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,200 service requests were made through the city's reporting system. The most common reports were for dumped items (451 requests), graffiti (206 requests), and e-scooters (180 requests).

Average response times for closed requests varied. Reports for graffiti and dumped items were addressed in about 34-35 hours on average. Street light issues took longer, with an average response time of about 2.5 days. Park maintenance requests had an unusually fast average response time of about 37 minutes, which may indicate administrative closures rather than completed work.
