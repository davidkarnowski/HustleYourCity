Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood service requests.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue, with 2,202 calls in the last month. The city took an average of 7.3 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most common issue, with 980 reports and an average response time of 1.7 days.

Other notable service times include Street Repair, which saw 244 reports and averaged 3.2 days for a response. Dead Animal Pickup was handled much faster, averaging 8.8 hours across 155 reports. Tree Maintenance had 288 reports with an average response time of 1.7 days.

Keep in mind that unusually fast response times or immediate closures may be due to administrative routing rather than actual work completion. For instance, all 901 E-Scooter complaints were immediately referred and closed out of the system without a recorded response time.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 29, 2026, 8:47 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 29, 2026, 7:00 AM PDT.
