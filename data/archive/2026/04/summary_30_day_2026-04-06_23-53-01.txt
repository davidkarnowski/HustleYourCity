Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest 30-day accountability report on City of Long Beach service requests.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items received the highest number of reports with 2,309 calls, taking an average of 8 days to resolve. Graffiti followed with 999 reports, averaging a 33-hour response time. E-Scooter issues also saw high volume with 936 reports.

Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services had some of the fastest response times, averaging 5 hours and 9 hours respectively. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completion. On the other end, Marina Repair requests took about 7 days on average, and Trash and Debris took just over 3 days.

Residents are encouraged to continue holding the city accountable. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 6, 2026, 4:49 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 6, 2026, 3:00 PM PDT.
