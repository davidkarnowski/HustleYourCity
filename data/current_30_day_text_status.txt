Here's a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents submitted nearly 4,900 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,906 requests) and graffiti (1,117 requests).

The data shows longer average response times for certain issues. Reports for dumped items took an average of about 8 days to close, while street repairs averaged over 5 days. Marina repair requests had the longest average close time at nearly 2 weeks.

Faster responses were recorded for other services. The average close time for graffiti removal was about 2.3 days. Dead animal pickup and animal services requests were typically resolved in under 9 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most direct way to get it into the city's queue.
