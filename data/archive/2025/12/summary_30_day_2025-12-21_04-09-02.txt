Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update, focusing on requests from the last 30 days.

Over 5,000 service requests were submitted by residents. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (1,933 requests), graffiti (1,080), and illegally parked e-scooters (988).

According to the city's data, the average time to resolve a request varied by type. Reports for dumped items were closed in about 6 days on average. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in an average of 62 hours, while reports for dead animal pickups were closed in about 12 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, from potholes to park maintenance, reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the official way to notify the city.

This data summary was updated at December 20, 2025, at 8:06 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 20, 2025, at 7:00 PM PST.
