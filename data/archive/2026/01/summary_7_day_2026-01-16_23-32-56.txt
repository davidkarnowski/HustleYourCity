Here is the latest weekly summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past 7 days, residents submitted 1,429 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (559 calls), e-scooters (259 calls), and graffiti (201 calls).

Average response times for closed requests varied. Graffiti removal averaged about 27 hours. Dumped item reports were addressed in an average of 52 hours. A single restroom maintenance request was closed in approximately 16 minutes, which may be due to an administrative update. The longest response this week was for a marina repair, which took about 4 days to close.
