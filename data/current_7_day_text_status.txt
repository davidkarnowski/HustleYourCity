Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past week.

The most reported issues were dumped items (492 reports), e-scooters (262 reports), and graffiti (237 reports). The city's average time to close a dumped items request was about 2 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 18.5 hours.

Response times for street light reports averaged about 8 hours. Some service types, like sidewalk repair, show very fast closure times. These may be due to administrative updates and not the time it takes to complete the physical work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 14, 2025, at 8:10 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 14, 2025, at 7:00 PM PST.
