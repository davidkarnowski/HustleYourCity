Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest 90-day accountability update on Long Beach city service requests.

Dumped Items led the request board with 6,945 reports. The city took an average of 8.4 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal saw 3,249 reports, with an average response time of 2.3 days.

Other notable services include Dead Animal Pickup, which had 463 reports and averaged a 10-hour response time. Business License and Permit Concerns showed an unusually fast response time of just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
