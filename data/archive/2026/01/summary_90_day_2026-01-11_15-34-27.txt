Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from the public were for Dumped Items (5,947 calls), Graffiti (3,519 calls), and E-Scooters (2,652 calls). According to the data, the average response time to close a Dumped Items report was about 13.5 days, while Graffiti removal averaged about 4.5 days. Reports for E-Scooters are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for resolution.

Some of the quickest city responses were for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services, both averaging about 16 hours to close out a request. Slower response times included Park Maintenance, which averaged about 13 days, and Marina Repair, averaging about one month.

To report issues like dumped items or graffiti, the most direct method is to use the Go Long Beach service application.
