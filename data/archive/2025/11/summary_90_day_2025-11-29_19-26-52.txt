City service response times have shifted noticeably over the past 90 days.

Graffiti reports dropped to 3,694 with an average response of 161 hours (about 6.7 days), down from the all‑time average of 351 hours. Dumped Items remained high at 6,163 reports, but the average response rose to 369 hours (≈15 days) versus the historic 261 hours.

Several categories show dramatic speed‑ups:
- Sidewalk Repair: 321 reports, avg 36 hours (≈1.5 days) vs 4,704 hours historically.
- Light repairs: 629 reports, avg 39 hours (≈1.6 days) vs 1,011 hours.
- Tree Maintenance: 769 reports, avg 36 hours (≈1.5 days) vs 3,763 hours.
- Animal Services & Dead Animal Pickup: avg 17 hours and 16.6 hours respectively, compared with over 3,900 hours historically.
- Street Repair: 632 reports, avg 82 hours (≈3.4 days) vs 4,509 hours.
- Traffic Signal: 102 reports, avg 74 hours (≈3.1 days) vs 2,514 hours.
- Park Maintenance: 188 reports, avg 119 hours (≈5 days) vs 646 hours.

Overall, many services are now being addressed in days rather than weeks or months, indicating faster city action.

Residents can help keep this momentum by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach app.
