Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest accountability update on city service requests over the past 24 hours.

In the last day, Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 77 total reports, though 70 of those remain in the new status queue. E-Scooter complaints followed with 50 reports, all of which were immediately closed and referred to outside vendors. Graffiti was the third most reported issue with 42 calls, 28 of which are currently in progress.

For issues that were resolved in the past 24 hours, Sidewalk Repair and Street Repair averaged response times of about 17 hours and 15 hours, respectively. Faster resolutions were recorded for Animal Services at roughly 2.5 hours and Dead Animal Pickup at just over 2 hours. Trash and Debris reports that were closed averaged about 106 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

If you see issues in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. Residents are strongly encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
