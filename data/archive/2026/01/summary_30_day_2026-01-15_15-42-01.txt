Here is a look at the latest Long Beach service response data from the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,300 service requests, with the most common reports for dumped items (over 2,000 calls), graffiti (over 900 calls), and street light or repair issues. The data shows that average response times have improved for several key services. Graffiti removal now averages just under 2 days, and dumped item cleanup is averaging under 5 days.

Some services continue to have longer response times. Reports for trash and debris cleanup averaged nearly 7 days, and traffic signal repairs took about 5.5 days. Animal-related services, such as dead animal pickup and other animal service calls, were among the fastest, with average responses of about 14 hours.
