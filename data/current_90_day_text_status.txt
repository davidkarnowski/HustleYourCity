Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

Over this period, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (6,116 requests), graffiti (3,743 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,212 requests).

Average response times varied by the type of service requested. The city closed requests for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup in about 15 and 16 hours, respectively. Graffiti removal requests were completed in about 6.5 days on average. More complex issues took longer, with reports for illegally dumped items taking about 15 days and park maintenance requests taking about 18.5 days to be resolved.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it. Use the Go Long Beach service application to send requests directly to the city.
