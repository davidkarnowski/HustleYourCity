Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted more than 1,300 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (542 requests), graffiti (229 requests), and e-scooter issues (217 requests).

According to the city's data, the average time to close a request for dumped items was about 3.5 days. Graffiti reports were addressed more quickly, with an average response time of about 34 hours. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 5.5 hours on average. Some categories show very fast closure times, which can sometimes be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
