Here is an evaluation of Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2,544 total calls. The average response time for these issues is approximately 1 week.

Graffiti reports totaled 1,080. The city is averaging a response time of 58 hours for these requests.

There were 1,008 reports regarding E-Scooters. All of these were marked as 'Closed Referred', meaning they are passed to the scooter operators.

Street Repair requests are averaging 3 days for a response. Street Light issues are averaging 65 hours.

Dead Animal Pickup shows a faster average response time of roughly 12 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
