Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

In the last month, the most frequent request was for dumped item removal, with 1,895 reports. The average response time for these requests was about 7 days. Graffiti removal was also a high-volume category with 918 requests, which were addressed in an average of just over 2 days.

Some of the fastest responses were for dead animal pickups and animal services, with both averaging a response time of about 14 hours.

Response times for infrastructure issues varied. Street light repair requests took an average of 3.5 days to close. Reports for traffic signal issues took an average of 7 days.
