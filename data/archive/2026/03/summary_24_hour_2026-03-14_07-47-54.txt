Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests over the past 24 hours.

In the last day, the highest volume of service calls involved Dumped Items with 80 requests, followed by E-Scooter issues with 50 requests, and Graffiti with 30 requests.

When looking at response times, Dumped Items averaged about 4.2 hours. However, several categories recorded unusually fast average response times. Street Repair and Vegetation requests were processed in an average of just 15 minutes. Tree Maintenance averaged 48 minutes, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 53 minutes, and Graffiti averaged 55 minutes.
