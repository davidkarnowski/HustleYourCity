Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour city service data. Here is a look at what residents are reporting and how the city is handling the volume.

Over the past day, Dumped Items led the board with 92 reports, though 68 of those remain in the New status. Graffiti followed with 50 reports, with 24 of those already closed.

Response times for several categories were remarkably fast. Street Repair issues averaged a response time of just 30 minutes, Park Maintenance averaged 59 minutes, and Dumped Items averaged 76 minutes. Please keep in mind that these unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or system routing rather than actual physical work being completed.

Other services took a bit longer but remained under a day. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports averaged nearly 17 hours, and Animal Services averaged 15 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
