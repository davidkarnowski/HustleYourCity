The latest service request data for the City of Long Beach covers the past 90 days of activity. This update highlights current response times and volume for key city services.

Dumped Items continue to be the highest volume category with 6,426 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 13 days.

Graffiti abatement received 3,112 reports during this period. The average time to close these requests is roughly 4 days.

Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 785 reports. The data indicates an average response time of 4.5 days.

Dead Animal Pickup services show a faster turnaround, with 425 reports averaging a response time of about 11 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
