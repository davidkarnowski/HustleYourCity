Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 90-day service request data to keep the community informed on city response times and service volumes.

Dumped Items and Graffiti continue to drive the highest volume of service calls. Over the past 90 days, the city received 6,626 reports for Dumped Items, with an average response time of 10 days. Graffiti complaints totaled 3,066, with crews responding in approximately 3 days on average.

Other significant categories include Tree Maintenance with 901 requests and Street Repair with 828 requests. Both categories averaged a response time of about 6 days. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 2,854 reports, all of which were referred to outside vendors.

Some services show exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this are often due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Meanwhile, urgent requests like Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services averaged 12 and 17 hours, respectively.

Data transparency helps hold local government accountable. Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues and helping track city performance by submitting requests through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at March 15, 2026, 4:40 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 15, 2026, 4:00 PM PDT.
