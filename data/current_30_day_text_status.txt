Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 30 days.

Illegal dumping (1,969 requests) and graffiti (1,156 requests) were the most reported issues. The data shows the average response time for graffiti removal was just over two days, while illegally dumped items took an average of 7.5 days to be addressed.

Other key service response times include street repairs at about 4 days and tree maintenance at about 3 days. Reports of abandoned e-scooters totaled 866, with data indicating these are referred to third-party operators.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone. It is the most direct way to alert city service crews.
