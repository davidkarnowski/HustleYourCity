City service request activity in the last 90 days shows several notable trends.

Graffiti: 3,683 reports with an average response time of about 6 days (146 hours). This is a marked improvement from the all‑time average of roughly 15 days.

Dumped Items: 6,321 reports, average response time now about 15 days (360 hours), slower than the all‑time average of 11 days.

Tree Maintenance: 787 reports, average response time about 2 days (45 hours), down from an all‑time average of 157 days.

Light repairs: 597 reports, average response time about 1.5 days (39 hours), compared with the all‑time average of 42 days.

Sidewalk Repair: 331 reports, average response time about 2 days (46 hours), versus the all‑time average of 196 days.

Animal Services: 225 reports, average response time just under 17 hours, a huge reduction from the all‑time average of 164 days.

Dead Animal Pickup: 456 reports, average response time about 16 hours, also far better than the all‑time average of 191 days.

Overall, many service categories are being closed much faster than historically, indicating quicker city response for residents.
