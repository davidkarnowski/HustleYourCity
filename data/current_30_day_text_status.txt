Here's the latest 30-day snapshot of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past month, "Dumped Items" was the most reported issue with 1,932 requests, taking an average of about 7.5 days to resolve. The second most common was "Graffiti" with 1,146 reports, which saw an average response time of about 56 hours.

Response times for other services varied. "Street Repair" requests averaged about 4 days, while "Marina Repair" took nearly 2 weeks. Quicker responses included "Dead Animal Pickup" (about 9 hours) and "Animal Services" (about 10 hours).

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the best way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
