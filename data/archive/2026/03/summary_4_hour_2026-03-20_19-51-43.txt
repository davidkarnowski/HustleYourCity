Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest look at city service response times based on data from the last 4 hours.

The city logged 112 service requests during this brief window. The highest volumes came from E-Scooter complaints with 51 requests, all of which were closed and referred, and Dumped Items with 41 requests, the vast majority of which remain in the new status.

Several categories showed extremely fast average response times. Vegetation issues averaged 11 minutes, Tree Maintenance averaged 12 minutes, Running Water averaged 16 minutes, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 46 minutes, Graffiti averaged 49 minutes, and Street Repair averaged 57 minutes. 

Please note that these unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures, duplicate flagging, or system referrals rather than actual physical work being completed on the streets.
