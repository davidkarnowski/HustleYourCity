The latest service call data for Long Beach covers the past 90 days. The highest volume of reports continues to be for dumped items and graffiti.

There were 6,558 reports for dumped items with an average response time of approximately 11 days. Graffiti removal generated 3,078 reports, with the city taking about 3 days on average to close these cases.

Infrastructure repairs show varied response times. Street light repairs, with 1,037 reports, are averaging a 3-day response. Street repairs, which include potholes, had 838 reports and an average response time of 5 days.

E-scooter complaints accounted for 2,940 reports. The data indicates these are closed as referred, meaning they are passed to the scooter companies rather than handled by city maintenance crews.

Residents should continue to report issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city has accurate records of service needs.
