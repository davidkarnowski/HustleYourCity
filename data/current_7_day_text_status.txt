Here is a look at Long Beach service request data for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, the most common issues reported were Dumped Items (425 requests), Graffiti (261 requests), and E-Scooters (261 requests). The average response time to close a Dumped Items report was about 65 hours. Graffiti reports were closed in about 37 hours on average.

Response times for other services varied. Reports for Trash and Debris and Traffic Signal issues took over 3.5 days to close on average. In contrast, Sidewalk Repair requests were closed in an average of 55 minutes. Unusually fast response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
