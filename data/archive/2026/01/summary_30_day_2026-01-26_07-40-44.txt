Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 30 days.

The latest data shows that illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with over 2,300 service calls. These requests took an average of about 8 days to be resolved. Reports for street light issues were also common, with an average response time of about 3 days.

Quicker responses were seen for other frequent issues. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 65 hours on average, while street repair calls were handled in about 69 hours. The fastest responses were for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests, which were both resolved in about 12 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
