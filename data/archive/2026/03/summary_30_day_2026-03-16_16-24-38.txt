Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Over the past 30 days, citizens submitted thousands of requests. Here is a breakdown of the city's response times for the most frequent issues.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,112 reports. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti removal was the second most common issue, totaling 1,050 calls. The average response time for graffiti was 42 hours.

Tree Maintenance received 484 requests, with an average response time of 55 hours. Street Repair saw 262 reports, taking an average of 3 days to address.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help keep the community safe and clean.
