Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the past week.

The most reported issue was illegally dumped items, with 407 service calls. The average response time to close these reports was about 42 hours. Graffiti was the next most common issue, with 187 reports and an average response time of about 54 hours.

Some issues saw much faster responses. Reports for shoes on a utility wire were addressed in an average of 44 minutes. Park maintenance issues were closed in about 36 minutes, though this unusually low time may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work.

You can help the city track and address neighborhood issues by reporting them through the Go Long Beach service application.
