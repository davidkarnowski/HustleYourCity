In the past 90 days Long Beach residents submitted roughly 16,000 service requests across 30 categories.

Response times have improved dramatically for many services.  Sidewalk repairs now average 45 hours (about 2 days) versus the historic 4,706 hours.  Light‑outage tickets are closed in 40 hours on average, down from 1,013 hours.  Tree maintenance requests are resolved in 44 hours, a big drop from 3,767 hours.  Animal‑services cases close in 17 hours compared with 3,929 hours previously, and dead‑animal pickups are completed in 16 hours versus 4,585 hours historically.

Some categories still take longer.  Dumped‑item reports average 360 hours (about 15 days), slightly slower than the historic 260 hours.  Graffiti requests now close in 146 hours (about 6 days), an improvement from the previous 351 hours.

Residents can help keep the city moving faster by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach app.
