Hustle Long Beach! is back with the latest third-party government accountability update. The newest service call data has been analyzed to keep citizens informed about how the City of Long Beach is handling reports over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports this week with 591 calls. The city averaged a response time of nearly 62 hours for these issues, though 398 of these reports remain in the new status.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue, totaling 217 calls with an average response time of about 21 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 138 reports, all of which were referred to a closed status.

Other notable service areas include Tree Maintenance with 59 reports and an 11-hour average response, and Light issues with 50 reports and a 35-hour average response. Dead Animal Pickup saw 35 reports handled swiftly with an average response of 4.4 hours.

Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to help keep the community in top shape. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
