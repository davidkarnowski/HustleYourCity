Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,180 service requests were made in the last 7 days through the Go Long Beach system. Reports for dumped items were the most frequent issue, with 429 requests. The average response time for these was just under 3 days.

Graffiti cleanup requests, the second most common report, were addressed in an average of about 41 hours. Traffic signal repairs had the longest average response time this week at about 3.6 days.

Some requests, like the 14 for sidewalk repair, show an average response time of 81 minutes. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not always on-site work completion.
