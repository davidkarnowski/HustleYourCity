Here is a look at the latest city service data for Long Beach from the past week. Residents submitted 1,532 service requests.

The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (573 requests), E-Scooters (266 requests), and Graffiti (215 requests).

Average response times for closed requests varied. Graffiti removal took about 25 hours, while reports of dumped items were addressed in just under 2 days. A single Marina Repair request took over 4 days to close. Some services were much faster, with Vegetation requests closed in about 61 minutes. An unusually fast response of 16 minutes for Restroom Maintenance may reflect an administrative closure rather than a completed repair.
