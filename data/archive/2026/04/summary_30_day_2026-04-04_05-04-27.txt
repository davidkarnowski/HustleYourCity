Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a breakdown of recent report volumes and average city response times.

Dumped Items received the highest number of reports with 2,317 calls. The average response time for these issues was over 8 days.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 967 calls. The city responded to these reports in an average of 33.5 hours.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 931, though all were referred to outside agencies, resulting in no measurable city response time.

Street Repair saw 221 reports, taking an average of over 3 days to address.

Park Maintenance received 79 reports, with an average response time of over 3 days.

Some services saw faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup had 165 reports with an average response time of 5 hours. Animal Services handled 78 calls in an average of 8.4 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help track service efficiency.

This data summary was updated at April 3, 2026, 10:01 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 3, 2026, 7:00 PM PDT.
