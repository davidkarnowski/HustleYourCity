A review of Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days highlights current trends in response times and request volumes. Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,434 reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 13 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,086, with the city averaging a response time of about 4 days. Street Repair saw 793 requests, which are being addressed in roughly 4.5 days on average.

Faster response times were recorded for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup, both averaging approximately 13 hours to resolve. In contrast, Marina Repair requests showed the longest delay, averaging 23 days for completion.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting local issues using the Go Long Beach service application.
