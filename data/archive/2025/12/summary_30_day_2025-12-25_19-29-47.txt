Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past month, reports for illegally dumped items were the most frequent issue, with over 1,900 calls. The average response time to close these requests was about 8 days. Graffiti and improperly parked E-Scooters were also common, with each category receiving over 1,000 reports.

The city responded more quickly to other common issues. Graffiti removal averaged just over 2 days. Reports for street lights, sidewalk repairs, and street repairs were typically addressed in about 2 to 3 days. More urgent requests, such as Dead Animal Pickup, were handled in about 13 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
