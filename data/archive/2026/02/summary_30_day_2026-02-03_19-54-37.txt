Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past month, residents submitted over 6,100 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items, with 2,496 requests. This was followed by 1,066 reports for graffiti and 1,048 for e-scooter issues.

The average time to resolve a report for illegally dumped items was about 9.7 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of 2.6 days. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups, which took about 11.4 hours, and restroom maintenance, which averaged about 8.1 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
