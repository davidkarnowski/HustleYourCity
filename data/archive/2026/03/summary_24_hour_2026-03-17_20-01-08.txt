Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour city service data to keep residents informed on response times and call volumes.

Over the past day, the city received 198 total service requests. Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 104 reports, though 83 of those remain in the New status. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 49 calls, and city staff closed 34 of those cases.

Average response times for the past 24 hours show quick administrative action. Dumped Items averaged a 4.8-hour response time. Street Repair requests averaged 7.3 hours, while Graffiti and Sidewalk Repair both averaged around 13.2 hours. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports took an average of 17.4 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual physical work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help maintain local neighborhoods.
