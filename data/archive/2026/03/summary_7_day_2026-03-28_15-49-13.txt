Here is the latest service call data summary from Hustle Long Beach! looking at the past 7 days of city operations.

Dumped items led the service requests this week with 516 reports. The city averaged a response time of about 3 days for these issues. Currently, 190 of these requests are in progress and 176 remain marked as new.

Graffiti removal was the second most reported issue with 223 requests. The city closed 162 of these cases with an average response time of 16.4 hours.

There were also 181 e-scooter complaints, all of which were referred to outside agencies or departments.

On the faster side, restroom maintenance requests averaged a response time of just 50 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
