Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days. The most frequent requests from the public were for "Dumped Items" with 5,951 reports and "Graffiti" with 3,669 reports.

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" request was about 14.5 days. For "Graffiti", the average response time was about 5.5 days. Sidewalk repair requests, totaling 273 over the period, took an average of just under 4 days to close.

Some services saw much faster resolutions. The 174 "Animal Services" requests were closed in about 15 hours on average, while "Dead Animal Pickup" requests were handled in about 16 hours.
