Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped items continue to lead city service requests. In the last 30 days, the city received 2336 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 8 days. Graffiti removal also saw high volume with 1004 reports, taking an average of 35 hours to address. E-scooter complaints reached 865, though all were referred to outside entities.

Street repair requests totaled 206, averaging 3 days for a response. Meanwhile, dead animal pickups saw 167 reports and averaged a response time of 4 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
