The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,527 reports for dumped items and 2,608 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 7 minutes. Graffiti removal is also relatively quick, averaging about 63 hours. Conversely, some services like dumped items show longer wait times, averaging about 18 days. Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
