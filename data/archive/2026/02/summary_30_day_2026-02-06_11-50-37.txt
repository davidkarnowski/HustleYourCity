Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the last 30 days.

In total, residents submitted over 5,800 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,543 calls), graffiti (1,110 calls), and e-scooter issues (951 calls).

The average time to close a request for illegally dumped items was about 9 days. For street repairs, the average was just over 3 days, while reports for damaged drinking fountains took an average of nearly 11 days to close.

Graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of about 2.5 days. Reports for dead animal pickup were among the fastest, with an average closure time of about 11 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
