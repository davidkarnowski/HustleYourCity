Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,188 requests), graffiti (3,723 requests), and e-scooters (2,249 requests).

Average response times for some key services include graffiti removal at about 6 days and dumped item pickup at about 2 weeks. Street repairs took an average of about 10 days to close out. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups (about 17 hours) and animal services calls (about 15 hours). Some requests show very fast closure times, which may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.
