The latest data from the City of Long Beach service call system shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 8,447 requests for dumped items, which remains the highest volume category. These requests currently show an average response time of approximately 18 days.

Graffiti removal remains a high-volume service with 2,984 reports in the last 90 days, averaging a response time of about 3 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show the fastest turnaround times, with both categories averaging under 12 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
