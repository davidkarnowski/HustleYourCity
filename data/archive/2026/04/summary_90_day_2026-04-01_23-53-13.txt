Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Here is a summary of the city's response times and request volumes over the past 90 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,994 reports. The city took an average of 9 days to resolve these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,099 requests, averaging a 60-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints were also high at 2,855, though response times are not tracked for these referrals.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests saw significant activity. Street Repair received 835 requests with a 6-day average response time. Tree Maintenance had 922 requests, taking nearly 5 days on average to resolve. Sidewalk Repair requests totaled 339, averaging over 4 days for a response.

The fastest resolutions were for Business License and Permit Concerns, averaging just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickups and Animal Services were also handled quickly, averaging 10 hours and 16 hours respectively.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 1, 2026, 4:49 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 1, 2026, 3:00 PM PDT.
