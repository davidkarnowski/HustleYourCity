Hustle Long Beach! Here is the latest accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests over the past 24 hours.

In the last day, Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 102 reports. Graffiti followed with 64 reports, and E-Scooter complaints reached 60, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

When looking at response times, several categories recorded extremely fast averages. Please note that unusually low response times, especially those under an hour, may be due to administrative closures, duplicate flags, or system referrals rather than the actual completion of physical work.
