Here is the latest look at Long Beach service call data. In the past 24 hours, residents submitted 167 new service requests. The most common issue reported was illegally dumped items, making up 76 of those requests.

For requests closed during this period, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 3.5 hours. Reports for dumped items were addressed in approximately 11 hours on average.

Some service types showed unusually fast response times. For example, tree maintenance requests were closed in an average of 34 minutes, and a running water issue was closed in just 4 minutes. These quick closures may be due to administrative updates and not always reflect the time for work to be completed on-site.
