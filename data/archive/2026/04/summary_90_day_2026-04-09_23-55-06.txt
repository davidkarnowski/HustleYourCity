Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service request data from the City of Long Beach. Looking at the last 90 days, here is a factual breakdown of call totals and response times for major city services.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,908 reports. The average response time for these issues was 8.4 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,163 calls, taking an average of 55.6 hours to process. 

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant volume. The city received 900 tree maintenance requests, averaging 6 days for a response, and 732 street repair requests, which took an average of 6.9 days. Sidewalk repairs totaled 361 requests with an average response time of 5.5 days.

Some categories recorded exceptionally fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps track government performance and ensures community needs are documented.
