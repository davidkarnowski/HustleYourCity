An evaluation of city service data over the last 90 days shows that Dumped Items remains the highest volume category for service requests. There were 6,586 reports filed, with an average response time of 11 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,108, with crews averaging a response time of 3 days. Infrastructure requests also saw activity, with 849 Street Repair reports averaging 5 days for response, and 854 Tree Maintenance requests averaging 6 days.

Response times for Dead Animal Pickup averaged 14 hours across 431 reports. E-Scooter issues generated 2,941 reports, which are almost exclusively referred to outside vendors rather than handled by city staff.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. Consistent reporting helps generate the data necessary to track performance.
