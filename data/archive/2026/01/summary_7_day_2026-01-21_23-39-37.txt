Here is the latest weekly summary of Long Beach service call data.

In the last 7 days, residents made over 1,300 requests for city services. "Dumped Items" was the most common report with 517 calls, and the average time to resolve these cases was about 3.5 days. The city also received 257 reports about E-Scooters, which are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Response times for other services varied. The average time to close a "Graffiti" report was about 32 hours, while "Dead Animal Pickup" requests were handled in just under 6 hours. Reports of "Illegal Fireworks" took an average of nearly 4 days to be addressed.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
