Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests.

Reports for dumped items were the most common issue, with 2,550 requests. The average response time to close these cases was about 8 days. Graffiti was the next most frequent report with 1,105 submissions, which were handled in about 2.5 days on average.

Other key services included street repair requests (266 total), which took an average of almost 3 days to resolve. Reports for street light issues (438 total) were addressed in a similar timeframe. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which were completed in about 11 hours on average.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
