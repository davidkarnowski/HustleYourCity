Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times for the last 90 days.

The latest data indicates that Dumped Items remain the most frequently reported issue, with 6,584 total reports. The average response time for this category is currently approximately 12 and a half days. Graffiti reports followed with 3,123 calls, showing an average resolution time of nearly 4 days.

For infrastructure concerns, Street Repair requests are averaging 4 and a half days for completion. Tree Maintenance requests are taking roughly 6 days to resolve. Dead Animal Pickup remains one of the faster services, with an average response time of about 13 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in the raw data can sometimes be attributed to administrative closures rather than physical work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
