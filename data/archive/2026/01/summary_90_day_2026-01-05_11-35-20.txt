Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most common service requests from residents were for dumped item pickups (5,822 requests) and graffiti removal (3,557 requests). The average time to close a dumped item request was about 14 days. Graffiti removal requests were resolved in an average of just under 5 days.

Other services showed longer response times. The average for park maintenance was about 17 days, and street repairs took an average of about 9.5 days. More urgent requests, like dead animal pickups, were typically resolved in under 17 hours.
