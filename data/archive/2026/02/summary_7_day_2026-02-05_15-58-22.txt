Here is the latest weekly summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past week, residents submitted over 1,400 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (594 requests), graffiti (261 requests), and e-scooter issues (232 requests).

For requests closed this week, the average response time for dumped items was about 28 hours. Graffiti removal took an average of 37 hours. Reports for streetlight issues were addressed in about 2.5 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city.
