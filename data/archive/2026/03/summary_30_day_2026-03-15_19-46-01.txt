Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest third-party evaluation of city service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2,124 requests. The city averaged a response time of 5 days for these issues, with 1,376 cases closed and 312 currently in progress.

Graffiti reports followed closely with 1,041 requests. The average response time for graffiti was nearly 2 days, and 907 of those cases have been closed.

Street Repair saw 260 requests, taking an average of 3 days to address. Tree Maintenance handled 481 requests with an average response time of 2 days.

Some categories showed unusually fast times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
