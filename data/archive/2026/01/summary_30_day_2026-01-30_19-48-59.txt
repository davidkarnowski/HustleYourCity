Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with over 2,300 requests, followed by more than 1,000 reports each for e-scooters and graffiti.

The average city response time to resolve a "Dumped Items" request was approximately 9 days. Graffiti removal took about 2.5 days on average. Traffic signal repairs were completed in an average of 4.5 days. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 11.5 hours.

You can report issues like these directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
