Hustle Long Beach! community members, here is the latest independent accountability summary of city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service requests with 7,039 reports. The average response time for these items is currently about 9 days. Graffiti reports followed with 3,167 calls, averaging a 59-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints were also high, totaling 2,866 reports.

Other notable service times include Street Repair, which saw 801 requests and averages nearly 7 days for a response. Tree Maintenance received 921 requests, taking about 5 days on average to address. 

Some categories show unusually fast resolution times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application. Consistent reporting helps maintain accurate data on city operations.

This data summary was updated at April 4, 2026, 10:21 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 4, 2026, 7:00 PM PDT.
