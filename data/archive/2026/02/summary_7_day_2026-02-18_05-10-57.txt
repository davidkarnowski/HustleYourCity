The latest service data for the City of Long Beach highlights response times and report volumes for the past 7 days. Dumped Items received the highest volume with 373 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 2.1 days. Graffiti reports totaled 243, with an average completion time of 1.4 days.

Street Repair requests averaged just over 1 day for response across 50 reports. Dead Animal Pickup services averaged 2.7 hours. Light repair requests averaged roughly 3 days to resolve. Additionally, 203 E-Scooter reports were recorded, all of which were referred out for handling.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
