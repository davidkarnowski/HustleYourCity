Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days. The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (6,419 reports), graffiti (3,214 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,660 reports).

Average response times for closed requests vary by issue. Reports for dumped items took nearly 2 weeks (about 13.6 days) to resolve on average. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 4.5 days, while street repairs took about 5 days.

Faster responses were seen for urgent requests like dead animal pickups (about 11.6 hours) and other animal services calls (about 12.3 hours). It is important to note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
