Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests, based on data from the last 90 days.

Over 15,800 service requests were made by the public. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (over 6,100 requests), graffiti (over 3,700), and e-scooter issues (over 2,200).

Average city response times for closing a case varied by issue. Reports of illegally dumped items were resolved in about 15 days on average, while graffiti removal took about 6 days. Park maintenance requests were completed in an average of 17 days. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups (about 17 hours) and animal services calls (about 15 hours). Note that some response times may appear unusually fast due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 20, 2025, 7:30 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 20, 2025, 7:00 AM PST.
