Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen requests.

Dumped items lead the service calls with 6,972 reports, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti removal saw 3,247 reports, with an average response time of 55 hours. E-scooter complaints were also high with 2,884 reports, though nearly all were referred to outside agencies.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests show longer wait times. Tree maintenance received 887 reports and takes an average of 9 days to address. Street repair saw 697 reports with an average response time of 8 days. Sidewalk repairs take about 6 days to process based on 384 reports.

Some services show rapid turnaround times. Dead animal pickups are handled in about 9.5 hours. Business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Reporting problems helps track response times and holds local agencies accountable.
