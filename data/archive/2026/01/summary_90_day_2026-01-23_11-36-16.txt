Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 90 days.

Residents submitted thousands of service requests through the Go Long Beach app. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (6,115 calls), graffiti (3,383 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,671 calls).

Response times for closing service requests varied. The average time to resolve a report for illegally dumped items was about 13 days. Graffiti removal averaged about 5 days, and park maintenance requests took over 10 days on average.

More urgent issues were handled faster. The average response time for dead animal pickups was about 15 hours, and animal services calls were closed in about 14 hours.
