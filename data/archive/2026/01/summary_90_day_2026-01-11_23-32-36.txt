Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the last 90 days based on public data.

The most common service request was for "Dumped Items", with 5,918 reports filed. The average time to close these requests was about 13.5 days. "Graffiti" was the next most frequent issue, with 3,496 reports closed in an average of about 4.5 days.

Other key services included 700 "Street Repair" reports, with an average closure time of about 7 days, and 685 "Tree Maintenance" reports, averaging about 6 days. Faster responses were seen for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" requests, which were typically closed in under 16 hours.
