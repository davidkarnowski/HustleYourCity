Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest independent look at city service response times over the past 7 days. As a third-party accountability tracker, the goal is to keep citizens informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed by the city.

Dumped items saw the highest volume this past week with 564 reports, taking an average of 71.6 hours to resolve. Graffiti removal followed with 253 calls and a 29.2-hour average response time. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 244 reports.

Some services showed very fast turnarounds. Dead animal pickups averaged 2.3 hours across 36 calls, and sidewalk repairs averaged 11.6 hours for 49 calls. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
