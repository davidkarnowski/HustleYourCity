Here is the latest data on Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (6,199 requests) and graffiti (3,341 requests).

The average time to close a graffiti report was about 5 days. For dumped items, the average response was about 13 days. Some services saw quicker resolutions, with reports for dead animal pickups and animal services typically closed in about 12 hours.
