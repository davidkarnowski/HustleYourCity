Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,424 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (579 requests), graffiti (272 requests), and e-scooters (236 requests).

The average city response time to close a request for dumped items was about 32 hours. For graffiti removal, the average was about 33 hours. Street light issues were addressed in about 50 hours on average. Note that requests for e-scooters are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
