Here is the latest look at Long Beach service request data for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 929 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (318 calls), e-scooters (220 calls), and graffiti (184 calls).

Response times for closed cases varied. The average time to resolve reports of dumped items was about 26 hours, while graffiti removal took about 33 hours on average. Park maintenance requests were closed in an average of 69 minutes. Note that very fast response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
