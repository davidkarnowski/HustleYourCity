Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped items, with over 6,200 reports, and graffiti, with over 3,300 reports.

Average response times varied by the type of service needed. The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report was about 13 days. In contrast, reports for graffiti removal were completed in about 5 days on average. Some of the quickest responses included "Animal Services" and "Dead Animal Pickup," which were both handled in under 15 hours on average.
