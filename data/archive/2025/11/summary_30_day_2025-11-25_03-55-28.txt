City service response update – last 30 days

Graffiti reports total 1,413 with an average response time of about 72 hours (3 days), down from roughly 91 hours in the prior 60 days.  
Dumped‑item calls rose to 1,977; average response improved to 158 hours (≈ 6.6 days) versus 330 hours two months ago.  
Tree‑maintenance requests numbered 228 and were closed in an average of 10.6 hours, faster than the 16 hour average seen earlier.  
Sidewalk repairs (91 calls) were addressed in about 15 hours on average, a slight improvement over the previous 16.5 hours.  
Traffic‑signal issues (37 calls) now average 27.5 hours, down from 37 hours.  
Light‑maintenance, park maintenance, and vegetation work all show response times near 40–45 hours, comparable to recent periods.  
Dead‑animal pickups (148 calls) remain quick at 18.7 hours on average.

Overall, many service categories are seeing faster turnaround times compared to the prior two months. Residents can continue to report issues and track progress using the Go Long Beach app.

This data summary was updated at 2025-11-24 19:54 PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 2025-11-24 19:00 PST.
