In the last 90 days Long Beach received about 15,800 service requests across all categories.  

The most frequent issues were Dumped Items (6,151 reports) and Graffiti (3,701 reports). Both saw average response times of roughly 6‑7 days (Graffiti ≈ 160 hours, Dumped Items ≈ 370 hours).  

Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were handled quickly – all 228 and 439 calls were closed, with average response times under 17 hours (≈ 0.7 day).  

Other common requests such as Tree Maintenance, Light repairs, Sidewalk Repair and Traffic Signals were typically resolved within 1‑2 days (average 34‑39 hours).  

Overall, the city’s response times are improving, with many categories now closed in under two days.  

Residents can report new issues or check status using the Go Long Beach service app.
