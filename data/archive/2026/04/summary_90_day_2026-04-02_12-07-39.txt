Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs. Here is a look at the numbers.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 7,010 calls in the last 90 days. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti removal saw 3,110 reports, with the city responding in an average of 2.5 days.

Street repair requests totaled 832, taking an average of 6 days to address. Tree maintenance had 926 reports, averaging nearly 5 days for a response.

Some categories showed unusually fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times are often due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Please use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 2, 2026, 4:57 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 2, 2026, 3:00 AM PDT.
