Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs. Here is your weekly accountability update.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 523 reports. The city averaged a 39-hour response time for these issues, though 422 of those requests are still marked as new.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 215 calls. The city responded to these in about 19 hours on average.

Dead Animal Pickup saw 37 requests with an average response time of 3 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures in the system rather than actual work completions.

On the slower end, Marina Repair requests took the longest to process, averaging 3.7 days for a response.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
