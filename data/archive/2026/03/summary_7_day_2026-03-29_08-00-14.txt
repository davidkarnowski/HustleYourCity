Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day city service response data. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach handled recent public requests.

Dumped Items led the request volume over the past week with 529 reports, taking an average of 69 hours to address. Graffiti followed with 243 reports, averaging a 15.5-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 205 reports.

In other categories, Restroom Maintenance requests were closed in an average of 50 minutes. It is important to note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickups averaged a 6-hour response across 37 reports, while 66 Street Repair requests took about 14.3 hours on average. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports took about 59 hours to resolve.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps ensure that local agencies have the information needed to dispatch services.
