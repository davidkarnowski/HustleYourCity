Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from the community continue to be for illegally dumped items, with 5,913 reports filed. This was followed by 3,535 reports for graffiti and 2,630 for e-scooter issues.

On average, the city's response time to close a report for dumped items was about 2 weeks. Graffiti reports were resolved in an average of about 4.5 days. Some services had much quicker resolutions, such as dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 17 hours. More complex requests, like street repairs, took an average of nearly 8 days to complete.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the most effective way to notify the city is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
