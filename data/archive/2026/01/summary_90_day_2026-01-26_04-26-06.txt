Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days, based on the latest public data.

Illegal dumping, graffiti, and e-scooter issues were the most frequent service requests from residents, with over 12,000 reports combined. The data shows the average time to close a graffiti report is about 5 days. For illegally dumped items, the average is around 13 days.

Faster responses were seen for requests like dead animal pickups and animal services calls, which were typically handled in under 14 hours. The average response time for street repair requests was about 5.5 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.
