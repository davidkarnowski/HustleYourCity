Here is a look at the latest Long Beach city service data for the past week.

Over 1,300 service requests were submitted by residents in the last 7 days. The most common reports were for dumped items with 490 requests, followed by e-scooters with 257 requests, and graffiti with 180 requests.

Looking at response times for closed cases, the city addressed graffiti reports in about 35 hours on average. Street repair requests were closed in an average of 25 hours. Reports of illegal fireworks took about 4 days to be closed. Some issues, like vegetation cleanups, were resolved in under 7 hours. Note that very low response times may reflect administrative closures and not the completion of on-site work.
