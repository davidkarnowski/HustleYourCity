Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests over the past 90 days based on the latest public data.

Dumped Items and Graffiti remain the most reported issues, with over 5,800 and 3,600 service calls respectively. The city's average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 14 days, while graffiti reports were addressed in about 5 days on average.

Longer response times were seen for Park Maintenance requests, which averaged over 17 days. Quicker responses were recorded for issues like Dead Animal Pickup (about 17 hours) and Abandoned Shopping Carts (about 40 hours).
