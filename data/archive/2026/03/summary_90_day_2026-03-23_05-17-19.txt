Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual breakdown of how the City of Long Beach is responding to local issues.

Dumped Items generated the highest call volume with 6,696 reports. The city took an average of 9.5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,048 calls, averaging a 2.9-day response time. E-Scooter complaints reached 2,793 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Response times for infrastructure repairs varied. Street Repair saw 841 reports with an average response time of 6.5 days. Tree Maintenance had 945 reports averaging 6.7 days, while Sidewalk Repair took an average of 4 days for its 305 reports.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Public reporting is essential for tracking city performance. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.
