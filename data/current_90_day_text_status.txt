In the past 90 days Long Beach residents filed 2,440 e‑scooter reports, 3,582 graffiti complaints, 6,440 dumped‑item requests, 818 tree‑maintenance tickets, 519 street‑light issues, 355 sidewalk‑repair calls and 609 street‑repair requests.

Average response times have improved for several services. Animal Services requests were closed in just 18.3 hours on average, and dead‑animal pickups in 16.8 hours. Tree maintenance and street‑light issues were addressed in about 59 hours and 56 hours respectively. Graffiti reports took longer, averaging 137 hours, while dumped‑item requests averaged 371 hours.

Faster response times mean quicker fixes for the community, but some categories still need attention. Residents can help by reporting problems promptly through the Go Long Beach service app, which routes requests directly to the appropriate city department.
