Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

The most frequent service request was for "Dumped Items," with 1,894 reports and an average response time of about 7.5 days. "Graffiti" removal was the next most common, with 1,011 reports handled in about 2.5 days on average. The city also received 1,056 reports for "E-Scooters," which were referred directly to the scooter companies for resolution.

Other key services included 184 "Street Repair" requests, which took an average of about 2.5 days to close. "Tree Maintenance" requests totaled 138, with an average completion time of about 31 hours. "Dead Animal Pickup" requests were handled in about 13 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
