Here is a look at Long Beach service requests from the last 90 days. Dumped items were the most reported issue with over 6,400 calls. The average response time to close these requests was about two weeks. Graffiti was the second most common report, with over 3,200 calls and an average response time of about 4.5 days.

Response times varied across services. Reports for Marina Repair took the longest, averaging about five weeks to resolve. In contrast, requests for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services were closed in about 12 hours on average. These faster times can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

Over 2,600 reports were filed for E-Scooters. These issues are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling and are not resolved by city staff.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, from potholes to park maintenance, report it. Using the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
