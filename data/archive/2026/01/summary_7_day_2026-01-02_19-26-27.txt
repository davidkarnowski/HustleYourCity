Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,289 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (457 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (225), and graffiti (212).

Average city response times for key services included about 1.5 days for graffiti removal and about 2 days for dumped items. Requests for trash and debris cleanup took longer, averaging about 5 days to close. The average response for restroom maintenance was 85 minutes, a time that may reflect an administrative closure and not a completed work order.
