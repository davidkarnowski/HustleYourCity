Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days based on the latest data.

Dumped Items and Graffiti were the most reported issues, with over 6,100 and 3,700 requests filed, respectively. The average response time to close a Dumped Items request was about 15 days, while Graffiti removal averaged about 6.5 days.

Response times for other common requests include about 7.5 days for streetlight repairs and 9.5 days for street repairs. Faster responses were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup (about 16 hours) and Animal Services (about 15 hours). Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.
