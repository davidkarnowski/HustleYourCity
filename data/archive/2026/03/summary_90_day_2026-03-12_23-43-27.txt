Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest third-party accountability update on city service response times. The service call data from the last 90 days has been analyzed to see how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 6587 calls. The average response time for these reports is currently about 10 days. Graffiti removal saw 3089 reports, with the city taking an average of 3 days to respond.

Other notable response times include street repairs, which had 835 reports and an average response time of 6 days. Tree maintenance requests totaled 890, also taking about 6 days on average to process. 

Some categories show extremely fast turnarounds. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
