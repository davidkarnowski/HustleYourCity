Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past month, the most frequent reports from residents were for Dumped Items (1,910 requests), Graffiti (1,138 requests), and E-Scooters (857 requests).

The average response time to close a "Dumped Items" report was about 7.7 days. For "Graffiti" reports, the average completion time was about 2 days. Other notable response times include Street Repair at 4.5 days and Dead Animal Pickup at just under 9 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
