Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 30 days.

Illegal dumping was the most reported issue with 2,189 service calls. The average time to close these requests was about 7.7 days. Graffiti removal requests, totaling 905, were addressed in an average of about 70 hours.

Services with faster response times included dead animal pickups and other animal service calls, which were resolved in an average of about 12 hours. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
