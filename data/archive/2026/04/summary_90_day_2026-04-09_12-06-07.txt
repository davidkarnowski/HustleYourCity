Here is the latest government accountability update for the Hustle Long Beach! community. This review examines the City of Long Beach service call data over the past 90 days to keep citizens informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,923 calls. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these reports. Graffiti removal was another major issue, totaling 3,192 reports with an average response time of 56.4 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests showed longer turnaround times. Street Repair handled 735 issues, averaging 7.1 days for a response. Sidewalk Repair saw 361 requests, taking about 5.5 days on average. Tree Maintenance recorded 905 calls with a 4.7-day average response time.

Some services saw much faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup cleared 459 calls in an average of 10.7 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns showed an unusually fast average response time of just 12 minutes for 6 calls. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. All followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
