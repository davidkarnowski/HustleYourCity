Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1155 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (429 calls), e-scooter issues (238 calls), and graffiti removal (234 calls).

The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 1.5 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 22 hours on average.

Some services showed very fast closure times. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of 45 minutes, and restroom maintenance in 51 minutes. These quick closures may be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.
