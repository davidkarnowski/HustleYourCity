Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data from the City of Long Beach. Over the past day, the highest volume of reports came from Dumped Items with 78 calls, followed by E-Scooter issues with 50 calls, and Graffiti with 33 calls. The majority of the Dumped Items reports are currently marked as new, while all 50 E-Scooter reports were closed and referred to outside agencies.

Several service categories showed exceptionally fast average response times over the last 24 hours. Street Repair and Vegetation issues averaged a response time of just 15 minutes. Tree Maintenance averaged 48 minutes, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 53 minutes, and Graffiti reports averaged 55 minutes. Dumped Items had a slightly longer average response time of 4.2 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times, particularly those under an hour for major physical repairs like street and tree maintenance, may be the result of administrative closures, duplicate flagging, or referrals rather than the actual completion of the physical work.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the "Go Long Beach" service application. Consistent reporting helps maintain accurate data and improves community conditions.
