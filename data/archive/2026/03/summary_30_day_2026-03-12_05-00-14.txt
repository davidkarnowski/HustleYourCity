Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days to keep the community informed on how local issues are being handled.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of service requests with 2,100 reports. The city took an average of 4.6 days to respond to these cases. Graffiti was the second most common issue, with 1,081 reports and an average response time of 43.4 hours.

Other significant areas of public concern included tree maintenance, which saw 477 requests and a 50.8-hour average response time, and street repairs, which averaged 3.3 days to address across 269 reports. Park maintenance requests took an average of 4.1 days to resolve.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. However, it is important to note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. On the other end of the spectrum, marina repairs took an average of 7.2 days.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Submitting reports helps ensure that local infrastructure and neighborhoods receive the attention they need.

This data summary was updated at March 11, 2026, 9:57 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 11, 2026, 8:00 PM PDT.
