Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service requests. This data helps track how the city is responding to resident reports.

Over the last three months, the most frequent issues reported by the community were illegally dumped items (6,120 requests), graffiti (3,713 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,290 requests).

Average response times varied by service type. The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 15 days. Graffiti reports were closed in an average of 6 days. Sidewalk repair requests were completed in about 4 days on average.

Some services showed much faster response times. For example, dead animal pickups were handled in about 17 hours and animal services requests in about 15 hours. Note that some unusually fast closures can be due to administrative processing rather than the completion of physical work.

Help keep the data current by reporting issues you see. The best way to submit a service request is through the Go Long Beach application.

This data summary was updated at 11:25 AM PST on December 21, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 AM PST on December 21, 2025.
