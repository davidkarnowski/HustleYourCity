Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 7-day service call data to keep the public informed on city response times and service volumes.

Over the past week, the highest volume of service requests included 463 reports for Dumped Items, 255 for E-Scooters, and 229 for Graffiti. Tree Maintenance and Street Repair also saw notable activity, with 70 and 63 requests respectively.

Response times varied across categories. Dumped Items averaged a response time of 52.7 hours, while Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 18.1 hours. Running Water reports showed a response time of just 13 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help hold the city accountable.
