Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 90 days.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with 5,833 requests, followed by graffiti with 3,571 requests.

The city responded fastest to reports for animal services and dead animal pickups, with average closure times of about 16 hours. Graffiti reports were closed in about 5 days on average. Longer wait times were seen for park maintenance, averaging about 17 days, and marina repairs, which took about 26 days to close. Some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
