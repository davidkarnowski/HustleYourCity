The latest service request data for Long Beach covers activity over the past 90 days. The highest volume of reports continues to be for Dumped Items, with 6,583 total requests recorded. The average response time to close these tickets is currently 12.6 days.

Graffiti removal is the second most active category with 3,127 reports. The average time to resolve these issues is 3.7 days. Street Repair requests totaled 818, with a response average of 4.5 days.

Faster response times were noted for sanitation and animal issues. Dead Animal Pickup requests averaged 12.7 hours for completion, while Animal Services averaged 13.4 hours.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. This ensures the city has an accurate record of necessary repairs and services.
