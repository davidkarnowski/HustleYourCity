Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

The most reported issues were for dumped items and graffiti. Residents submitted 600 reports for dumped items, with an average city response time of about 3 days. For the 246 graffiti reports, the average response time was about 1 day.

Other service requests included 60 reports for street repair, which were addressed in about 2 days on average. The 43 reports for dead animal pickup were handled more quickly, with an average response time of about 5.6 hours.
