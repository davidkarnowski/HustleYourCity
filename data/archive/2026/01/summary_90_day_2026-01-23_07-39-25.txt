Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on the latest public data.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequent issues reported by residents, with over 6,100 and 3,300 requests filed, respectively. The average response time for dumped item removal was about 13 days. Park maintenance requests took around 10 days to close, while reports for marina repairs averaged about 3.5 weeks.

The city responded more quickly to other requests. The average time to resolve reports of dead animals or other animal service issues was under 15 hours. Graffiti removal took an average of 5 days. Some service categories show unusually fast response times, which may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
