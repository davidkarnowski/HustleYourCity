Here is the latest 7-day city service data update from Hustle Long Beach!

Over the past week, Dumped Items led the service requests with 576 reports, taking an average of 69.6 hours to process. E-Scooter complaints followed with 253 reports, though these show no response time because they are immediately referred and closed administratively. Graffiti remains a major issue with 208 reports, with the city responding in 25.7 hours on average.

Infrastructure and maintenance calls show varying response times. Street repair issues generated 39 calls with an average 21.5-hour response, while 52 sidewalk repair calls averaged 15.1 hours. Dead animal pickups are handled quickly, averaging 3.5 hours for 37 calls. Conversely, reports of illegal fireworks took nearly 5 days to resolve.

Residents are encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests directly to the city.
