City service call totals for the last 90 days show 6,463 dumped‑item reports, 3,558 graffiti complaints and 833 tree‑maintenance requests.  

Fastest average response times were seen for Animal Services (18 hours) and Dead Animal Pickup (16.6 hours). Light repairs averaged 2.5 days, sidewalk repairs 3.8 days, and street repairs 5.2 days. More complex issues such as dumped items (average 14.9 days) and graffiti (average 6.3 days) still take over a week to close.  

Overall, 2,033 requests were closed within the period, while many remain in progress, especially for park maintenance (average 5.9 days).  

Residents can help speed resolution by reporting issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app.
