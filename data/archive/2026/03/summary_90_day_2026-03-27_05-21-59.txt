Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest 90-day service call data to keep citizens informed on city response times and service trends.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue, with 6,883 requests. The average response time for these reports is 9.5 days. Graffiti is the second most common issue, with 3,106 reports and an average response time of 65.6 hours. 

Other notable services include Street Repair, which received 867 reports and averaged 6.5 days for a response. Tree Maintenance saw 945 requests, taking an average of 5.7 days to address. Dead Animal Pickup handled 446 requests with an average turnaround of 11.4 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns showed a remarkably fast response of just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help maintain the community.

This data summary was updated at March 26, 2026, 10:18 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 26, 2026, 8:00 PM PDT.
