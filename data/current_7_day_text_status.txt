Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Residents submitted 1185 requests, with the most common issues being illegally dumped items (450 reports), graffiti (252 reports), and e-scooter issues (226 reports).

Response times for closed requests varied. The average time to close a traffic signal repair request was about 3.6 days. Graffiti cleanup requests were closed in an average of 41 hours. Sidewalk repair requests had a very fast average closure time of 57 minutes, though this may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it is the first step to getting it fixed. Use the Go Long Beach app to submit service requests directly to the city.
