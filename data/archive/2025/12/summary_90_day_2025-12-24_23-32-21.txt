Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on the latest data.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues. The average response time for dumped items was about two weeks, while graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of about 5.5 days.

For other common requests, street repairs averaged about 10 days, and tree maintenance took about one week. Reports for animal services and dead animal pickups were typically handled in under a day.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
