An independent review of City of Long Beach service data for the last 30 days highlights current response times for high-volume requests.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue, with 2,116 total reports filed. The average time to resolve these requests is approximately 5 days. Graffiti reports totaled 1,089, with an average completion time of just under 2 days.

Infrastructure requests such as Street Repair showed an average response time of 3 days across 269 reports. Tree Maintenance requests are currently being addressed in just under 2 days.

Faster services include Dead Animal Pickup, averaging 14.5 hours, and Abandoned Shopping Cart removal, averaging 16 hours.
