Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the past 90 days. Residents made over 6,100 reports for illegally dumped items and over 3,700 for graffiti, making them the most common service requests.

Average response times varied by issue. The city closed graffiti reports in about 6 days on average. Requests for dumped items took longer, averaging about 15 days. Park maintenance requests averaged a response time of over 2 weeks.

In contrast, urgent requests like dead animal pickups were typically resolved in under 17 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most direct way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
