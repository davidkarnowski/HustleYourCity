Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,100 service requests were submitted through the Go Long Beach system. The most reported issues were illegally dumped items (436 reports), graffiti (215 reports), and e-scooter issues (204 reports).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 24 hours. Reports of illegally dumped items were addressed in about 41 hours on average. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of 45 minutes. Unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it using the Go Long Beach app to help the city track and respond to community needs.
