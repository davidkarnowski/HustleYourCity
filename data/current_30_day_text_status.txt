In the last 30 days Long Beach residents filed about 5,540 service requests across 22 categories.

Key highlights:
- Graffiti reports total 1,419 with an average response time of 57.5 hours (≈2.4 days), down from 90.7 hours in the prior 60‑day window.
- Dumped Items saw 2,027 requests; average response improved to 193.5 hours (≈8 days) versus 347.9 hours previously.
- Street Repair cases dropped to 184 requests and the average response fell to 21.5 hours, a big gain from 73.2 hours.
- Tree Maintenance had 243 requests with an average response of 9.3 hours, much faster than the 23.6 hours seen earlier.
- Light outages (240 requests) were handled in an average of 36.3 hours, similar to the prior period.

Other categories such as Trash & Debris (52 requests, 82.5 hours), Vegetation (30 requests, 43.3 hours) and Sidewalk Repair (101 requests, 13.9 hours) also show reasonable turnaround times. Very low response times in a few cases may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

Residents can continue to report issues quickly through the Go Long Beach service app, helping the city keep response times moving in the right direction.
