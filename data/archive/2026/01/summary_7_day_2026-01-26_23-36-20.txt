Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,400 new service requests were submitted. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (616 requests), graffiti (293 requests), and e-scooter issues (222 requests).

The average response time to close a "Dumped Items" report was about 3.5 days. For graffiti reports, the average response was just over one day. Reports for "Dead Animal Pickup" were handled in under 4 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
