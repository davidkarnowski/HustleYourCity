Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data to keep you informed on city response times.

Dumped Items and Graffiti remain the most reported issues. Residents submitted 6,673 reports for dumped items, which took an average of 10 days to resolve. Graffiti was reported 3,093 times, with an average response time of 70 hours.

Other major service categories included street repairs, with 831 calls averaging a 6-day response, and tree maintenance, with 905 calls averaging a 7-day response. Dead animal pickups were handled much faster, averaging 12 hours across 437 reports. Business license and permit concerns showed a response time of just 12 minutes, though unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make your voice heard. Please use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 17, 2026, 4:46 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 17, 2026, 4:02 PM PDT.
