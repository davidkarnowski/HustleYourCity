Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service request data to keep residents informed on how quickly the City of Long Beach is responding to neighborhood issues.

Over the past three months, Dumped Items generated the highest call volume with 6,932 reports. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal saw 3,194 reports, with an average response time of 2.3 days.

Street Repair and Sidewalk Repair requests logged 743 and 361 calls respectively. Street repairs took about 7 days on average to address, while sidewalk issues took roughly 5.3 days. Park Maintenance handled 218 requests, averaging a 6-day response time.

Some services saw much faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup handled 457 reports with an average response time of just under 10 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of only 12 minutes across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times measured in minutes may be due to administrative closures or referrals rather than actual field work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting local issues and holding agencies accountable. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit requests and help track city responsiveness.

This data summary was updated at April 8, 2026, 1:04 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 8, 2026, 11:00 AM PDT.
