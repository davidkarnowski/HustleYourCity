The latest data from the City of Long Beach service call system shows significant activity over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 7,601 reports and an average response time of approximately 9.6 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 3,279 reports and an average response time of about 3 days.

Response times for several services have improved significantly in the short term. Dead animal pickups are currently being handled in about 5 hours on average, while traffic signal issues have seen an average response time of approximately 85 hours over the last 90 days, dropping to roughly 21 hours in the last 30 days.

Please note that unusually low response times, such as those seen in the last 24 hours for street repairs, may reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
