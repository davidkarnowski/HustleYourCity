Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 7 days. Here is a factual look at how city services are responding to citizen reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 531 reports. Currently, 394 of these remain marked as "New" with only 39 closed. The average response time for Dumped Items is 66 hours. Graffiti reports were the second highest with 235 calls. The city successfully closed 169 of these graffiti requests, averaging a 25.5-hour response time.

Other notable service areas include E-Scooter complaints, which totaled 131 reports that were all referred to outside agencies. Dead Animal Pickup showed high efficiency, with 26 of 27 requests closed in an average of 4.4 hours. Tree Maintenance received 54 requests, averaging a 15-hour response time.

Citizens are encouraged to report neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application. Consistent reporting helps track response times and keeps the city accountable.
