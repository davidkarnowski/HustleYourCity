Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest 90-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests.

Over the last 90 days, Dumped Items led the service requests with 7,038 reports. This was followed by Graffiti with 3,111 reports and E-Scooter issues with 2,857 reports.

Response times vary significantly depending on the type of issue. The city averaged about 9 days to respond to Dumped Items and roughly 6.5 days for Street Repairs. Graffiti issues were addressed faster, averaging about 2.5 days.

Some categories show incredibly fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

Help keep the city accountable and well-maintained. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
