Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Residents reported over 1,200 issues through the city's service system.

The most common reports were for Dumped Items with 446 calls, followed by E-Scooters with 226 calls and Graffiti with 196 calls.

Average response times for closed requests varied across services. The city addressed reports for Dumped Items in just under 2 days. Park Maintenance requests took longer, averaging about 3.5 days to close. Some requests, like Restroom Maintenance, were closed in an average of 83 minutes, though this may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work.
