Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 90 days.

The most common requests were for "Dumped Items" (6,178 reports) and "Graffiti" (3,721 reports). The average response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 15 days. For "Park Maintenance" requests, the average response time was about 17 days.

Faster responses were seen for "Animal Services" and "Dead Animal Pickup" requests, which were closed in about 15 hours and 17 hours on average, respectively. In the last week, "Sidewalk Repair" requests show an average response time of about 45 minutes, which may reflect administrative closures rather than physical work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
