Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

The most frequent issues reported by residents were for dumped items, with 6,383 reports, and graffiti, with 3,289 reports.

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report was about 14 days. "Graffiti" reports were addressed in an average of about 5 days. Some of the fastest resolutions were for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" requests, which were closed in an average of about 12 hours.
