Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data. In total, residents submitted over 15,800 requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (5,899 requests), graffiti (3,552 requests), and e-scooters (2,686 requests).

Response times to close a service request varied by type. The city took an average of almost 4 weeks for marina repairs and nearly 17 days for park maintenance. Requests for dumped items were closed in about 2 weeks on average, while graffiti removal took just under 5 days.

The fastest responses were for animal-related issues. The average time to resolve requests for dead animal pickups and other animal services was about 16 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
