The latest analysis of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days highlights significant activity in sanitation and infrastructure maintenance.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 6,631 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 12 days. Graffiti removal received 3,133 reports, with an average resolution time of nearly 4 days.

Infrastructure requests show varied response times. Street Repair generated 824 reports with an average completion time of 5 days. Tree Maintenance, with 817 reports, averaged 6 days for resolution. Light repair requests totaled 1,033 and averaged just over 3 days to close.

Faster response times were noted in public health categories, with Dead Animal Pickup averaging 13 hours per request. Conversely, general Trash and Debris reports averaged over 10 days for completion.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues to the city using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure data is captured and addressed.
