Here is the latest weekly summary of Long Beach service call data.

In the last seven days, residents submitted over 1,400 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (586 calls), graffiti (271 calls), and e-scooter issues (247 calls).

City response times for key services include an average of just under 2 days for dumped item pickups and about 22.5 hours for graffiti removal. Tree maintenance requests were closed in an average of under 8 hours. Reports for e-scooters are referred directly to the scooter companies and are not handled by city staff.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
