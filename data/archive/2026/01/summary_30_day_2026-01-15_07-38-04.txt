Here is a look at the latest Long Beach service request data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted nearly 5,000 service requests, with dumped items (2,062 requests) and graffiti (906 requests) being the most common reports. The average response time to resolve a graffiti report was about 2 days. Reports of illegally dumped items took longer, with an average closure time of nearly 5 days.

Requests for trash and debris cleanup had one of the longest response times, averaging nearly one week. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 14 hours on average. Some unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
