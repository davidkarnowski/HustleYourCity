The latest service request data for the City of Long Beach highlights activity and response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 6,417 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 2 weeks.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,188. The average time to close these requests was roughly 4 and a half days.

Street Repair requests totaled 790, with an average response time of 5 days.

Marina Repair showed the longest average response time at over 3 weeks. Conversely, Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services were among the fastest categories, averaging between 11 and 12 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Hour counts below 90 minutes are measured in minutes.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
