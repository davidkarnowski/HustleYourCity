Hustle Long Beach! community update: Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times and call volumes over the past 7 days. E-scooter complaints followed with 222 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti removal saw 191 requests, with an average response time of 19 hours. Tree maintenance requests averaged just under 10 hours for 34 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
