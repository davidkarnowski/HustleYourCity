Here is the latest Long Beach service data update, focusing on requests from the last 90 days.

Over 15,000 service requests were made by residents. The most frequent reports were for dumped items, with 6,155 requests, and graffiti, with 3,737 requests.

Average city response times for closed requests varied by type. Reports for dumped items were addressed in about 15 days. Graffiti removal took an average of about 6.5 days. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 17 hours to resolve.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
