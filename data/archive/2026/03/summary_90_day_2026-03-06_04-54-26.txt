The latest 90-day review of City of Long Beach service requests highlights Dumped Items as the highest volume category. There were 6,554 reports filed in this period. The average time to resolve these dumping issues is currently approximately 11 days.

Graffiti removal remains a high priority with 3,078 total reports. The average response time for graffiti cleanup is roughly 3 and a half days. E-Scooter issues also saw high activity with 2,942 reports, which are generally referred out immediately to operators.

Infrastructure maintenance shows varied response times. Street repairs, with 837 reports, are averaging a 5-day turnaround. Tree maintenance is taking nearly 6 days on average. Conversely, administrative tasks like Business License concerns are being closed in just 12 minutes, and Dead Animal Pickup is averaging under 14 hours.

Residents can help improve these numbers by continuing to report issues directly through the Go Long Beach service application.
