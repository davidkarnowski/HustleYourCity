Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service response data for the past 30 days.

Dumped item pickups were the most common request, with 1,976 reports filed. The average time to resolve these issues was about 5 days. Graffiti was the second most frequent report with 920 requests, which were handled in an average of just over 2 days. The city also received 326 requests for street repairs, with an average completion time of about 3 days.

Over 970 reports were filed for e-scooter issues. These are typically referred directly to the scooter companies, so the city does not track a response time for them.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
