In the past 90 days Long Beach residents filed 6,509 Dumped Items reports and 3,554 Graffiti complaints, the two highest‑volume service types. The city’s average response time for Dumped Items was about 15 days, while Graffiti requests were addressed in roughly 6.5 days.

Other common requests included 202 Park Maintenance jobs (average 6.3 days), 354 Sidewalk Repairs (≈ 4.4 days) and 485 Light issues (≈ 2.5 days). Tree Maintenance saw 834 calls with an average response of 2.7 days, and Street Repair handled 593 calls in about 5.5 days.

Fast‑turnaround services were Animal Services (231 calls) and Dead Animal Pickup (449 calls), both resolved in about 18 minutes on average. Traffic Signal and Vegetation work also showed relatively quick handling, under 6 days.

Residents are encouraged to report issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app to help keep response times short.
