Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data from the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at what citizens are reporting and how fast the city is responding.

Dumped Items lead the past 30 days with 2,186 reports, taking an average of 7.4 days to resolve. Graffiti follows with 980 reports, averaging a 40.5-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints reached 871, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Street Repair saw 241 calls with a 3.3-day average response. Park Maintenance handled 75 reports with a 4.9-day average response. Dead Animal Pickup had 151 reports, handled in about 9 hours. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
