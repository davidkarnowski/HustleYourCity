Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data to keep residents informed on city response times. Here is a look at how long it takes to get things fixed in Long Beach.

Dumped Items generated the most service requests with 6879 reports. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 3094 calls, averaging a 61-hour response time.

For infrastructure, Street Repair handled 842 requests with an average response time of 6 days. Sidewalk Repair saw 327 requests, taking nearly 4 days on average. Tree Maintenance addressed 924 calls, taking about 5 days to resolve.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup averaged a 10-hour response for 438 calls. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues. Consistent reporting helps track city performance and ensures problems are documented.

This data summary was updated at March 31, 2026 at 1:00 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 31, 2026 at 11:00 AM PDT.
