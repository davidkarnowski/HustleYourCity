Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled service requests over the last 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the top issues reported by residents. There were 7007 requests for dumped items, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti followed with 3245 reports, which were addressed in about 56 hours on average.

Tree maintenance saw 888 requests, averaging 9 days for a response. Street repairs took about 8 days for the 708 issues reported. On the faster side, dead animal pickups had 470 calls and averaged just under 10 hours for a response.

Some categories showed unusually fast response times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. These extremely low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
