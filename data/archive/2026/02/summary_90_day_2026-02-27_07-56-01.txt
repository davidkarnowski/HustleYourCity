Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 6,639 total calls. The average response time for these requests is approximately 12 days. Graffiti reports followed with 3,131 total calls and an average response time of nearly 4 days.

Infrastructure requests show varied completion times. Street Repair requests are averaging 5 days for a response, while Tree Maintenance is taking about 6 days. Street Light issues, with over 1,000 reports, are being addressed in roughly 3 days.

Some categories indicate very rapid closure times. For example, Business License concerns show an average closure time of 12 minutes. Be aware that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
