Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 7 days to keep residents informed on local government response times.

Over the last week, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 485 calls. The city took an average of 3.4 days to respond to these reports. E-Scooter complaints were the second most frequent with 244 reports, followed by Graffiti with 212 reports and an average response time of 33 hours.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast processing. Running Water reports averaged a response time of just 13 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Other notable response times include Tree Maintenance at 21 hours and Street Repair at 27 hours.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues to the city. Staying engaged helps hold local agencies accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 11, 2026, 9:26 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 11, 2026, 8:00 AM PDT.
