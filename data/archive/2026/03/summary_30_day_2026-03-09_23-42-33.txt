Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day city service response data to see how quickly issues are being addressed across the community.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to drive the highest volume of service requests. Over the past 30 days, citizens reported 2040 dumped items. The city took an average of 4.7 days to respond to these. Graffiti reports totaled 1078, with an average response time of 1.8 days.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant activity. Street repair received 265 reports, averaging 3 days for a response. Tree maintenance had 465 requests, taking nearly 2 days on average to address.

Some categories reported extremely fast turnarounds. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting provides the data needed to track service efficiency and hold local government accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 9, 2026, 4:39 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 9, 2026, 4:00 PM PDT.
