In the past 30 days Long Beach residents logged 5,527 service requests across all categories.

Key highlights:
- Graffiti reports dropped to 1,313 cases with an average response time of 46.9 hours, down from 162 hours in the prior 90 days.
- Dumped Items remained the most frequent issue (2,000 reports) with an average response of 155 hours.
- Tree Maintenance saw 278 requests and a fast average response of 11.5 hours, a big improvement from 67 hours previously.
- Light outages (224 reports) were addressed in about 27.9 hours on average.
- Sidewalk Repair (111 reports) and Street Repair (184 reports) were resolved in roughly 16 hours and 28 hours respectively.
- All other categories (e.g., Dead Animal Pickup, Vegetation, Traffic Signal, Shoes on a Utility Wire) had average response times ranging from 9.9 to 43 hours.

These faster response times suggest the city’s service teams are handling many issues more promptly than before. Residents can continue to report problems and track progress using the Go Long Beach service app.

This data summary was updated at 06:35 PST on 2025-11-04.
