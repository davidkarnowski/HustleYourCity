Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,336 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (494 calls), e-scooters (256 calls), and graffiti (180 calls).

Average city response times for key services were about 1 day for street repair, 1.5 days for graffiti removal, and nearly 2 days for dumped items. Street light repairs took longer, averaging nearly 3 days. The fastest responses were for vegetation maintenance, averaging about 7 hours.

Some service requests may show unusually fast completion times, which can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work.
