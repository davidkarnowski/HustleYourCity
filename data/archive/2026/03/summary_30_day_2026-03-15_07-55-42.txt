Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data to keep citizens informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling community requests.

Over the past month, the city received the highest volume of reports for Dumped Items with 2,118 calls, followed by Graffiti with 1,041 calls, and E-Scooter issues with 984 calls.

Response times varied significantly depending on the type of service. Dumped Items took an average of 5 days to process, while Graffiti reports were addressed in about 44 hours. Street Repair requests, which totaled 261, took an average of 3.2 days to resolve. Trash and Debris reports took an average of 5 days.

On the faster end, Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to actively report neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests directly to the city and help improve the community.

This data summary was updated at March 15, 2026 at 12:52 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 15, 2026 at 12:00 AM PDT.
