Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the past 90 days.

Over the last 90 days, residents submitted thousands of service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,070 requests), graffiti (3,444 requests), and e-scooters (2,725 requests).

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. Reports for animal services were handled in about 14.5 hours and dead animal pickups in about 16 hours. Graffiti cleanup requests took an average of about 4.5 days to close. Requests for dumped items, the most frequent issue, took nearly two weeks on average.

Reporting issues helps the city track and address community needs. Residents can report problems like graffiti, potholes, and dumped items using the Go Long Beach service application.
