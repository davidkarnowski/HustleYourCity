Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 30 days.

Reports for illegally dumped items were the most common request with over 2,400 submissions. The average response time to close these reports was about 10 days. Graffiti and E-scooter issues were also frequent, with each category receiving over 1,000 reports. The average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.5 days.

Other service times include street repair and light outages, which were addressed in about 2.5 days on average. Reports for dead animal pickups were handled in approximately 11 hours.
