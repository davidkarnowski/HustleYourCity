Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past three months, the most frequent reports from residents were for illegally dumped items, with 5,823 requests, and graffiti removal, with 3,559 requests.

City response times show significant variation. Reports for animal services and dead animal pickups were typically resolved in under 24 hours. In contrast, the average time to close a park maintenance request was about 17 days, while reports for dumped items took an average of about 14 days.

To report issues like potholes, graffiti, or dumped items, use the Go Long Beach service application.
