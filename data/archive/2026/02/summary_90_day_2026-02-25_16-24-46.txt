Here is the latest evaluation of City of Long Beach service request data for the past 90 days.

The highest volume of service requests continues to be for Dumped Items, with 6,535 total reports filed. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 13 days. Graffiti removal also saw high activity with 3,119 reports, showing an average response time of just under 4 days.

Infrastructure maintenance data shows 805 Street Repair requests with an average completion time of about 4 and a half days. Tree Maintenance requests totaled 790, with crews responding in approximately 6 days on average.

Faster response times were recorded for public health issues. Dead Animal Pickup services handled 430 calls with an average response time of 13 hours. Administrative tasks such as Business License concerns showed an average response of 12 minutes, though unusually low times may indicate administrative closures rather than field completion.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
