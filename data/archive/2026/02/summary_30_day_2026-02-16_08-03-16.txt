The latest analysis of Long Beach city service requests over the past 30 days shows significant activity in waste and maintenance categories. Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2,400 total calls. The average response time for these requests is currently recorded at approximately 7 days.

Graffiti abatement followed with 1,139 reports, showing an average resolution time of just over 2 days. Street Repair services logged 224 requests with an average response time of about 3 and a half days. Dead Animal Pickup remained one of the faster services, handling 150 calls with an average response time of roughly 8 and a half hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report maintenance issues. Consistent reporting provides the data necessary to track these response times and service levels.
