Hustle Long Beach! is here with the latest city service data update. A review of the numbers from the past 24 hours shows how city services are handling recent community requests.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 106 reports. E-Scooter complaints followed with 50 reports, and Graffiti saw 36 cases.
