Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest look at city service response times over the past 30 days. This data helps hold local government accountable by tracking how quickly neighborhood issues are addressed.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of service requests with 2,314 reports. The city averaged nearly 8 days to respond to these cases. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,001 calls, taking an average of 1.5 days for a response. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 864 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable response times include street repairs and abandoned shopping carts, both of which took about 3 days on average to process. Dead animal pickups and animal services saw some of the fastest turnaround times, averaging around 7 and 9 hours respectively. Please note that unusually fast response times can sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions. On the slower end, marina repairs took about 6 days to address.

Residents are encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit requests directly to the city and help keep Long Beach maintained.

This data summary was updated at April 8, 2026, 10:21 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 8, 2026, 7:00 PM PDT.
