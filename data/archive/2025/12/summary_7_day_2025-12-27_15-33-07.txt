Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 938 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (313 requests), Graffiti (203 requests), and E-Scooters (187 requests).

Average city response times for closed requests included approximately 26 hours for Dumped Items and 34 hours for Graffiti. Animal Services calls were handled in about 3 hours on average, while Dead Animal Pickups took around 9 hours.

Park Maintenance requests had an average close time of 1.6 hours. Unusually fast response times can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
