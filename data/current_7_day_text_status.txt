Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,180 requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (413 calls), improperly parked e-scooters (258 calls), and graffiti (249 calls).

The average time to close a graffiti report was about 1.3 days, while dumped item reports averaged about 1.5 days. Reports for abandoned shopping carts were addressed in about one day.

Response times for traffic signal issues were longer, averaging about 3.5 days. Some faster response times for maintenance tasks may reflect administrative closures and not necessarily on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application to report it to the city.

This data summary was updated at 11:30 AM PST on Dec 16, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 AM PST on Dec 16, 2025.
