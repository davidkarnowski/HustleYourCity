- Graffiti reports (1,385) were closed in an average of 36.5 hours, a big improvement from the 141‑hour average seen in the prior 90‑day period.
- Tree maintenance (261 requests) had the fastest turnaround, averaging just 11.8 hours from report to closure.

Overall, response times for most service types remain under two days, showing steady progress in city operations.
