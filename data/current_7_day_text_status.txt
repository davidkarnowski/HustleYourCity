Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last seven days, residents submitted nearly 1,250 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (478 requests), graffiti (260 requests), and e-scooter issues (253 requests).

The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 1.7 days. For dumped item pickups, the average response time was about 2.2 days.

Some service requests, like sidewalk repairs and restroom maintenance, showed very fast closure times of around 45 to 51 minutes. These unusually low times can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than the completion of physical work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
