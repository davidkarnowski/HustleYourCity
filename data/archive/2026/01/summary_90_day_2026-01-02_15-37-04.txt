Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests based on public data.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,841 requests), graffiti (3,598 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,521 requests).

Quicker responses were seen for issues like dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 17 hours.
