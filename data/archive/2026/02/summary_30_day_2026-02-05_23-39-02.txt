Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data. In the last month, residents submitted over 6,100 requests for city services.

Illegal dumping was the most reported issue with 2,544 requests. The average time to resolve these cases was about 9 days. Graffiti was the second most common report with 1,110 requests, which were handled in an average of about 2.5 days.

Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups (155 reports), which were completed in an average of about 11 hours. Reports for street light issues (425 reports) were addressed in about 2.4 days on average.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
