Here is a look at Long Beach service response data for the past week.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped items (596 reports), graffiti (276 reports), and e-scooters (235 reports).

For closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 36 hours. Dumped item reports were addressed in about 27.5 hours on average. Streetlight issues took longer, with an average response time of just over 2 days.
