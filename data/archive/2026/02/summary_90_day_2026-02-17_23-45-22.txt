The latest 90-day review of Long Beach city service requests shows Dumped Items as the most frequent report, with 6,372 total calls. The average response time for these issues is currently 13 days.

Graffiti removal received 3,158 reports with an average completion time of 4 days. Street Repair requests totaled 777, with crews taking nearly 5 days on average to close those cases.

Some categories show rapid closure rates. Business License concerns averaged 31 minutes to close, and Dead Animal Pickup averaged 11 hours. Please note that unusually low response times, such as those under 90 minutes, may be due to administrative closures rather than physical work completion.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
