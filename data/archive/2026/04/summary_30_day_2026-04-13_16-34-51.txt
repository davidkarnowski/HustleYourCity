Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest independent look at city service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to be the most reported issues. There were 2,271 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of about 8 days. Graffiti saw 1,080 reports, taking an average of 36 hours to address. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 957 reports.

Street repair requests totaled 204 with an average response of 71 hours. Dead animal pickups had 164 reports and were handled in about 7 hours on average. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative ticket closures rather than the actual completion of physical work.

Residents are encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application. Consistent reporting helps track city performance and keeps Long Beach neighborhoods well maintained.
