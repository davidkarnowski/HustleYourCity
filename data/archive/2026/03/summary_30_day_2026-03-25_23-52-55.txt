Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a summary of service request volumes and response times over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items and Graffiti were the most frequently reported issues. The city received 2306 reports for Dumped Items, which took an average of 6.4 days to resolve. Graffiti generated 1005 requests with an average response time of 35.9 hours. E-Scooter complaints reached 863 reports, though these are referred to outside operators and do not have a tracked city response time.

Other notable service areas include Park Maintenance, which received 73 requests and took an average of 3.9 days to resolve. Street Repair saw 254 requests with an average response time of 68.6 hours. Tree Maintenance handled 314 calls with an average turnaround of 40 hours. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may be due to administrative ticket closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help keep the city accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 25, 2026 4:49 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 25, 2026 4:00 PM PDT.
