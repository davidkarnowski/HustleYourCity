City service updates for the last 90 days show faster response times across many request types.

- Graffiti reports dropped to an average of 169 hours (≈7 days) per case, down from 354 hours a year ago, with 3,699 total reports.
- Dumped items remained high at 6,171 cases; average response is 376 hours (≈16 days), a slight slowdown from the previous 262‑hour average.
- Tree maintenance saw a major improvement: 745 cases closed in an average of 156 hours (≈6.5 days) versus 3,803 hours historically.
- Light outages (632 cases) are now addressed in 180 hours (≈7.5 days), down from 1,012 hours.
- Sidewalk repairs (320 cases) are resolved in 79 hours (≈3 days), a big gain from the prior 4,751‑hour average.
- Traffic signal issues (100 cases) average 216 hours (≈9 days), far better than the historic 2,506 hours.
- Quick‑turnaround services: Animal Services (217 cases) and Dead Animal Pickup (427 cases) are both closed in about 17 hours on average.

Overall, the city is handling more requests faster than in previous periods. Residents can continue to report issues directly through the Go Long Beach app for prompt service.
