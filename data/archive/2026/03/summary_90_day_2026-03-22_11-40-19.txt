Hustle Long Beach! is here with the latest 90-day review of city service response times. The data has been evaluated to show how quickly the City of Long Beach is handling public requests.

Over the past 90 days, Dumped Items and Graffiti generated the highest volume of service calls. The city received 6,728 reports for dumped items, taking an average of nearly 10 days to respond. Graffiti removal saw 3,056 requests, with an average response time of about 3 days.

Other notable service times include tree maintenance, which averaged nearly 7 days for 947 requests. On the faster side, dead animal pickups averaged under 12 hours. Business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
