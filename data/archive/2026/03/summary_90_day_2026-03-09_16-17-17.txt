Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen requests.

Over the last 90 days, Dumped Items led the request volume with 6,476 reports. The city closed 5,740 of these, taking an average of 10.8 days to respond. Graffiti removal followed with 3,094 reports, averaging a 3.3-day response time. E-Scooter complaints reached 2,929, all of which were referred to outside agencies or closed.

Tree Maintenance saw 869 requests with an average response time of 5.7 days. Dead Animal Pickup had 437 requests, averaging a 13.4-hour response. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
