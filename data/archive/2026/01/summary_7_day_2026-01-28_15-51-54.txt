Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,500 service requests were made in the last 7 days through the Go Long Beach system. The most reported issues were for dumped items (659 calls), graffiti (275 calls), and e-scooters (223 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 31 hours. Reports for dumped items and streetlights took longer, with average response times of about 3.5 days. Animal-related calls, such as for dead animal pickup, were handled more quickly, with average responses around 3 hours. All e-scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
