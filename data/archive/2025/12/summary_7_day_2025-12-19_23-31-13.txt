Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,180 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (451 requests), e-scooters (241 requests), and graffiti (230 requests).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 29 hours. For dumped items, the average was about 46 hours. Some service requests, like sidewalk repair, showed very fast response times of around 45 minutes. These may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
