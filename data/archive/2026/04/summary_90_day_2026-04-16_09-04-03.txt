Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of reports across various categories. Here is a summary of the call volumes and average response times.

Dumped Items received the highest number of reports with 6,945 requests. The average response time for these issues was 8.5 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,247 requests, averaging a response time of 58 hours. E-Scooter concerns generated 2,925 reports.

Other major service categories included Tree Maintenance, which had 892 requests and an average response time of 8.7 days. Street Repair saw 706 requests with an average response time of 7.8 days. Light issues totaled 926 requests, taking about 3.6 days on average to address.

Some categories recorded extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help keep the city informed.
