Here is the latest weekly summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past 7 days, "Dumped Items" was the most reported issue with 450 requests, followed by 226 reports for "E-Scooters". The city's data shows that e-scooter reports are referred to third-party operators and not handled directly.

Average response times for closed requests varied. "Trash & Debris" removal took the longest, averaging just over 3 days. Graffiti removal requests were typically handled in about one day. The fastest responses were for "Restroom Maintenance", averaging about 83 minutes, though this may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work.
