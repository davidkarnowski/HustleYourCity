The latest data from the City of Long Beach service call system shows activity across several categories over the past 30 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 2,277 reports, followed by 1,107 e-scooter reports and 1,043 graffiti reports. Average response times for these services were approximately 7 days for dumped items, 2 days for graffiti, and 2.5 days for street repairs.
