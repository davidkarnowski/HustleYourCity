Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs. Here is a look at the numbers from the last 30 days.

The highest volume of citizen reports involved Dumped Items with 2007 requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1070 requests, followed closely by 999 reports regarding E-Scooters.

Response times varied across different city services. The city averaged a response time of 4.7 days for Dumped Items. Graffiti requests were handled in an average of 42.6 hours. Street Repair saw 262 requests with an average response time of 3.1 days.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. Business Licenses and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you notice issues in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. You can help by using the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
