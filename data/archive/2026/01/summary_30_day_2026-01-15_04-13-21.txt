Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the last 30 days, based on public data.

Over the past month, residents submitted over 5,300 requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (2,058), graffiti (905), and e-scooter issues (897).

Average response times varied by issue. Reports for trash and debris took about 7 days to close. Traffic signal repairs averaged over 5 days. In contrast, graffiti removal requests were completed in about 2 days, and dead animal pickups were handled in approximately 14 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to alert city departments.
