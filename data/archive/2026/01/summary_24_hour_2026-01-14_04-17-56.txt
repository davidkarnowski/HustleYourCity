Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests. In the past 24 hours, residents submitted 183 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (85 requests), graffiti (24 requests), and street light issues (20 requests).

The data shows some very quick response times for certain closed cases. Restroom maintenance requests were closed in an average of about 16 minutes, with tree maintenance, park maintenance, and graffiti reports also closed in under an hour. These unusually fast times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion. The average response time for closed street repair cases was about 2.5 hours.
