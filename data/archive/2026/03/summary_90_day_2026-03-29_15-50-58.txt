Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs.

Dumped items and graffiti are the most frequently reported issues. Over the last 90 days, residents submitted 6,897 reports for dumped items and 3,110 reports for graffiti.

The city takes an average of 9.4 days to respond to dumped items. Graffiti reports are handled faster, averaging a response time of about 64 hours. Street repair requests, which totaled 858 calls, take an average of 6.4 days to process. 

Some categories show extremely fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Accountability starts with reporting. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 29, 2026, 8:47 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 29, 2026, 7:00 AM PDT.
