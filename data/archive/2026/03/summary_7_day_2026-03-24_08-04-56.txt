Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 7 days. Here is your weekly accountability update.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports this week with 497 calls. The average response time was about 25 hours, though 418 of these requests remain in the New status. E-Scooter complaints followed with 231 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti was the third most reported issue with 198 calls, taking an average of 27 hours to address.

Some services saw rapid turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup received 40 reports, all of which were closed with an average response time of 3.3 hours. Community Center issues averaged a response time of 71 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep Long Beach accountable and well maintained. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
