In the past 7 days Long Beach residents filed 447 new Dumped Items reports. The city’s average response time for these was about 81 hours (roughly 3.4 days).  

Street Repair received 51 requests with an average response of 6 hours, while Tree Maintenance handled 72 reports and was typically resolved in just 4 hours.  

Light‑related issues (65 reports) were addressed in an average of 16 hours, and Graffiti complaints (299 reports) saw a response time of about 36 hours.  

Many requests remain open: 141 Dumped Items, 32 Street Repair, and 43 Tree Maintenance cases are still “In Progress.”  

Quick turn‑around times for Tree Maintenance and Street Repair suggest focused effort, but longer waits for Dumped Items highlight an area for improvement.
