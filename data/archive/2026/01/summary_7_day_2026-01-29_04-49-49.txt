Here is your weekly summary of Long Beach city service data.

In the last 7 days, illegal dumping remains the most reported issue with 658 service requests. The average time to resolve these cases is currently just over 3 days. Graffiti removal requests totaled 282, with an average completion time of about 1.3 days.
