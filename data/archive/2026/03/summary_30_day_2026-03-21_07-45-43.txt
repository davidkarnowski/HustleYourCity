Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Here is a look at how city services performed over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2275 reports. The city took an average of 5.8 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 1015 reports, showing an average response time of 39.8 hours.

Other notable service categories include Street Repair with 256 calls averaging a 3.3-day response, and Park Maintenance with 70 calls averaging 4.3 days.

For more urgent needs, Dead Animal Pickup handled 152 reports with an average response of 13.9 hours, while Running Water concerns took 7.9 hours to address.

Please note that unusually low response times, especially those measured in minutes, may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help maintain Long Beach.
