Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Dumped Items and Graffiti continue to drive the highest call volumes. Citizens reported 2206 dumped items and 1064 graffiti issues over the last month.

Response times vary significantly by category. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to dumped items, while graffiti removal averaged about 38 hours. Street repair requests, totaling 262 calls, saw an average response time of 3 days.

Some categories show unusually fast resolution times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. These extremely low response times are likely due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Accountability starts with reporting. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city so these metrics can continue to be tracked.
