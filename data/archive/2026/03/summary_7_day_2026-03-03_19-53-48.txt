Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service requests for the past week.

The most frequent service request over the last 7 days was for Dumped Items, with 562 total reports. The average response time for these issues was approximately 2.1 days. There are currently 226 of these requests listed as In Progress.

Graffiti removal received 263 reports. The city closed 208 of these cases with an average response time of roughly 1.1 days.

E-Scooter reports totaled 237, all of which were referred to third-party operators.

Response times varied across other categories. Dead Animal Pickup averaged 4.7 hours per response on 23 calls. Street Repair requests averaged 19 hours. However, Traffic Signal issues averaged just over 2 days for response.

Residents are encouraged to report maintenance issues and safety concerns directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
