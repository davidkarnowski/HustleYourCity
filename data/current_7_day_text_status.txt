Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,053 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (509 requests), e-scooters (265 requests), and graffiti (249 requests).

The average response time for closed graffiti reports was about 20 hours. For dumped items, the average response was just over 2 days. Sidewalk repair requests had a notably fast average response time of about 3 hours, though this may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the city's Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at 3:27 PM PST on December 14, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 3:00 PM PST on December 14, 2025.
