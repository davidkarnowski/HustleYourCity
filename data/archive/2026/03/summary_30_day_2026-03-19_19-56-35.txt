Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest service request data from the City of Long Beach. Over the past 30 days, residents submitted thousands of requests. Here is a factual breakdown of the city's response times and call volumes.

Dumped items generated the highest volume with 2255 requests. The city took an average of 5.3 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti reports were the second most common, totaling 1048 requests with an average response time of 40 hours.

Other notable service volumes included 912 E-Scooter complaints, all of which were referred to outside operators, and 495 tree maintenance requests, which averaged a response time of 3.2 days. Street repair saw 259 requests, taking an average of 3.4 days to address, while park maintenance took about 4.5 days to respond to 70 requests.

Some categories showed incredibly fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
