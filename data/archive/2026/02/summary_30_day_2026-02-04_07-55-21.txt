Here is the latest 30-day snapshot of Long Beach city service requests. In the last month, residents submitted over 6,200 reports. The most common issues were illegally dumped items (2,548 reports), graffiti (1,080 reports), and e-scooter problems (1,005 reports).

Response times for key services have shifted. The average time to resolve a report for dumped items is now about 9.5 days. Graffiti cleanup requests are being closed in about 2.5 days on average. Urgent issues like dead animal pickups are typically handled in about 12 hours. The high volume of e-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies, so the city does not track a response time for them.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
