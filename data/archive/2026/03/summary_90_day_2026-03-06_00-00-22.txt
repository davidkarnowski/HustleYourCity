An independent review of Long Beach service data covers the last 90 days of activity. The most frequent request continues to be Dumped Items, with 6,549 total reports. The average response time for these issues is currently 11 days.

Graffiti removal saw 3,072 reports, with an average response time of approximately 3.5 days. Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 839 calls and averaged 5 days for resolution. Tree Maintenance requests are taking nearly 6 days on average.

The fastest response category for physical services was Dead Animal Pickup, averaging 14 hours. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than physical work completion.
