Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data from the past 90 days. Here is a factual breakdown of call totals and average response times for city services.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 6,542 requests. The average response time for these issues was 10.5 days. Graffiti reports followed with 3,111 calls, averaging a response time of 3.2 days.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 2,965, though all of these were referred to outside agencies. Street Repair saw 838 requests with an average response time of 5.6 days, while Tree Maintenance handled 887 requests in about 5.8 days on average.

Some categories showed extreme response times. Marina Repair requests took an average of 1.5 months to process. Conversely, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
