Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service request data from the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at the numbers and response times for the community.

Over the past day, Dumped Items and Graffiti generated the most service requests. There were 47 reports for Dumped Items, with the vast majority currently in the new status. Graffiti saw 33 total reports, with an average response time of about 11 hours.

Some categories showed unusually fast response times, which may indicate administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Sidewalk Repair requests averaged a response time of 57 minutes, and Trash and Debris reports averaged 88 minutes.

Other notable averages include Vegetation issues being addressed in about 2.6 hours and Tree Maintenance in 4.3 hours. Dead Animal Pickup requests averaged a 15-hour response time across 5 total calls.
