Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past seven days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 537 calls in the last week. The average response time is currently about 3.1 days. Notably, 211 of these reports are still marked as new, while 108 have been closed.

E-scooter complaints saw 258 reports, all of which were closed and referred to outside vendors or agencies.

Graffiti removal teams handled 229 reports, averaging a response time of just over 29 hours. The city has already closed 190 of these cases.

Tree maintenance and street repair received 76 and 52 calls respectively. Both categories are seeing average response times between 25 and 29 hours.

Running water reports had an unusually fast average response time of 84 minutes for 6 calls. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
