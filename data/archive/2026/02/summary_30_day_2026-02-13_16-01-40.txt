The latest data on Long Beach city service requests from the past 30 days highlights current response times and call totals.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 2,483 reports. The average time to resolve these requests is approximately one week.

Graffiti reports totaled 1,119, with an average response time of just over 2 days. Street Repair requests totaled 235, taking over 3 days on average to complete.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup, averaging under 9 hours across 149 reports. Business License and Permit concerns showed an average response of 31 minutes, though unusually low times may indicate administrative closures rather than field completion.

E-Scooter issues generated 971 reports, the majority of which are referred to external operators rather than handled by city staff.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues.
