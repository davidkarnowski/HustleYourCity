The latest city service data for the last 90 days shows a high volume of requests across Long Beach. Dumped items remain the most frequent concern with 7,341 reports, followed by 2,584 graffiti reports and 1,043 tree maintenance requests.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Dead animal pickup continues to be the fastest service, averaging about 7.4 hours. Graffiti removal is also relatively quick, averaging roughly 61 hours. In contrast, dumped items take an average of about 19 days to resolve. Please note that some categories show very low average response times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that service requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
