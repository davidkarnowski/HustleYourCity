Here is the latest 30-day service request data evaluation for the Hustle Long Beach! community. This independent summary tracks how the City of Long Beach is responding to local issues.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported problems. Over the last 30 days, citizens submitted 2,116 reports for dumped items. The city averaged a response time of 4.6 days for these requests. Graffiti was the second highest category with 1,076 reports, which saw an average response time of 43 hours.

Response times vary widely depending on the type of service. Marina repairs took an average of 7.5 days to resolve, while park maintenance requests averaged 4 days. On the other end of the spectrum, business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Accountability starts with reporting. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 12, 2026, 9:17 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 12, 2026, 8:00 AM PDT.
