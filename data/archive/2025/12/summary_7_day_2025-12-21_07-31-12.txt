Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,156 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most common reports were for dumped items (430 requests), e-scooters (238 requests), and graffiti (234 requests).

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 22 hours. For dumped items, the average response was about 1.5 days. Some categories showed unusually fast response times, such as sidewalk repair (about 45 minutes). These may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
