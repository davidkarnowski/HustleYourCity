Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days.

Over 15,800 service requests were made by residents. The most common reports were for dumped items (over 6,100 reports), graffiti (over 3,700 reports), and e-scooter issues (over 2,200 reports).

Based on city data, the average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 15 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 6 days on average, while street repairs took an average of about 9.5 days. More urgent requests, such as for animal services, were typically handled in under 16 hours.
