The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,969 reports for dumped items and 2,656 reports for graffiti. Response times for graffiti averaged approximately 63 hours, while dumped item requests averaged about 13 days.

Dead animal pickup remains one of the most efficient services, with 544 reports in the last 90 days handled in an average of about 7 hours. Meanwhile, tree maintenance saw 1,064 reports with an average response time of about 4 days.

Please note that some categories show very low response times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
