Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,350 service requests were made by residents. The most reported issues were for dumped items (558 requests), e-scooters (241), and graffiti (227). Reports about e-scooters are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Average city response times for closed requests included about 2 days for dumped item pickups and just over 30 hours for graffiti removal. The city responded to dead animal pickup requests in about 6 hours on average. Some faster response times may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it using the Go Long Beach service application to help the city track and respond to community needs.
