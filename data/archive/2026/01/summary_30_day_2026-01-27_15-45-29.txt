Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times for the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents made nearly 6,000 service requests. The most common report was for illegally dumped items, with 2,373 requests. The average response time to close these requests was about 7.5 days.

For other common issues, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.5 days. Reports for dead animal pickup were addressed in about 12 hours on average. Response times can vary, and very fast closures may reflect administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.
