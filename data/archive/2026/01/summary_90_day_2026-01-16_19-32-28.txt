Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues, with over 6,000 and 3,400 service requests respectively. The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of about 4.5 days.

Other key response times include street repairs at about 6.5 days and streetlight outages at about 4 days. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups (about 16 hours) and animal services calls (about 14 hours).
