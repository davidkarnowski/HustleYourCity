The latest data from the City of Long Beach service call system shows significant activity over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,059 reports, followed by 3,384 graffiti reports and 3,376 e-scooter reports.

Response times for completed requests vary by service type. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show the fastest turnaround, averaging approximately 10 hours and 5 hours respectively over the last 90 days. Conversely, dumped items are taking longer to address, with an average response time of about 8 days.

Please note that unusually low response times for certain categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests in their neighborhoods.
