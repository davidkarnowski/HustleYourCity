In the past 7 days Long Beach received 1,236 service requests across all categories.

Graffiti was the most common issue (369 reports) with an average response time of 34.2 hours (about 1 day and 10 hours). Dumped Items generated 517 calls; the city’s average response was 69.1 hours (just under 3 days). Light complaints totaled 57, averaging 41.9 hours to address. Tree Maintenance saw 35 requests and was handled in roughly 16.4 hours. Dead Animal Pickup had 36 reports with an average response of 23 hours. Sidewalk Repair (23 calls) and Street Repair (53 calls) were both responded to in under 20 hours on average.

All 189 E‑Scooter reports were automatically closed as “referred,” so no response time is recorded.

Faster response times are encouraging, but longer waits for dumped items suggest room for improvement.

Residents can report new issues directly through the Go Long Beach service app for quicker city action.
