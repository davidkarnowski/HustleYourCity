Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days to keep the community informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of service calls with 6,671 reports. The city took an average of 10 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,101 calls, averaging a response time of 69 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw high volumes. There were 1,038 reports for lighting issues, taking over 3 days to address. Tree maintenance and street repair received 908 and 829 calls respectively, with average response times between 6 and 7 days. 

Some services showed remarkably fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Dead animal pickups averaged under 12 hours. It is important to note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues and help keep the city running smoothly.

This data summary was updated at March 18, 2026, 4:59 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 18, 2026, 4:00 AM PDT.
