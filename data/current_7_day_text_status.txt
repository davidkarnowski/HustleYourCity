City service activity in the past week shows 1,309 new calls across 18 categories.

The most frequent requests were Dumped Items (489 calls) and Graffiti (362 calls). Dumped Items had an average response time of about 50 hours (just over 2 days), while Graffiti was addressed in roughly 17 hours. Park Maintenance requests were resolved quickly, averaging only 0.5 hours (about 30 minutes). Light repairs took about 22 hours on average, and Sidewalk Repair about 13 hours.

Several categories report only “closed‑referred” status with no response time data (e.g., E‑Scooter, News Rack). A notable share of new requests appeared in Dumped Items (158 new) and Graffiti (88 new), indicating rising community concerns.
