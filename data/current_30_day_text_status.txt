Here is the latest analysis of Long Beach city service data from the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents submitted nearly 5,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,902 requests), graffiti (1,126 requests), and e-scooter issues (778 requests).

According to the city's data, average response times vary by issue. Requests for dumped items are being closed in about 8 days, while street repairs are taking about 5 days. Faster responses were recorded for graffiti removal, which averaged about 2.5 days to complete. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 8.5 hours.
