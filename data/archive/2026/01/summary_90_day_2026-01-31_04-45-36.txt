Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past 90 days, the most reported issues were "Dumped Items" with over 6,300 reports and "Graffiti" with over 3,300 reports.

Average response times for common requests include about 13.5 days for "Dumped Items" and about 5 days for "Graffiti". More complex infrastructure requests took longer, with "Marina Repair" averaging about 5 weeks and "Damaged Drinking Fountain" reports averaging about 4 weeks. Faster responses were seen for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services", both averaging around 13 hours to close.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
