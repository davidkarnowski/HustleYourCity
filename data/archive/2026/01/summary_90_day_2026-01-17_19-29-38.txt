Here is the latest analysis of Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days.

Over this period, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (6,065 requests), graffiti (3,459 requests), and illegally parked e-scooters (2,724 requests).

The average response time for dumped item removal was about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in an average of about 4.5 days, while reports for dead animal pickup were completed in about 16 hours. Some service types may show unusually fast response times due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
