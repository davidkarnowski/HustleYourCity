Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the last month, residents submitted 1,916 requests for dumped item pickups, 1,054 for e-scooter issues, and 1,002 for graffiti removal, making them the most common service calls.

Based on city data for closed requests, the average response time for dumped items was about 8 days. Graffiti removal averaged about 55.5 hours, while traffic signal repairs took an average of nearly 4 days. The average for dead animal pickup was about 12.7 hours.

You can help the city track neighborhood issues by reporting them through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 27, 2025, at 7:30 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 27, 2025, at 7:00 AM PST.
