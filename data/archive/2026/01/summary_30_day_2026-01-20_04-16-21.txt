Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the past 30 days. Over 5,400 service requests were submitted by residents for issues across the city.

The most common reports were for illegally dumped items, with over 2,000 requests. This was followed by nearly 1,000 reports for e-scooter issues and almost 900 for graffiti removal.

Based on data for closed cases, the average city response time for graffiti removal was about 2.2 days. Reports of dumped items took an average of 7.4 days to resolve. More urgent requests, like dead animal pickups, were handled in about 13 hours on average. Longer response times were seen for issues like traffic signal repairs, which averaged about 5 days.
