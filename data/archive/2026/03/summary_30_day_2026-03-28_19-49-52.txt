Hustle Long Beach! brings you the latest accountability update on city service response times. Over the past 30 days, citizens have submitted thousands of requests, and the data shows exactly how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood concerns.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of calls with 2,252 reports. On average, the city took about 7 days to resolve these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 984 calls, which saw an average response time of 41 hours. E-Scooter complaints also remained a major concern for residents, generating 865 reports.

For faster resolutions, Dead Animal Pickups (156 reports) and Running Water concerns (22 reports) were handled quickly, averaging 9 hours and 11 hours respectively. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. 

Other services required more time. Street Repair requests (245 reports) took about 3 days on average, while Park Maintenance (74 reports) took nearly 5 days to address. 

Facts and statistics matter when keeping local government accountable. If you see something that needs fixing in your community, report it. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to submit issues directly to the city.
