Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 6,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,415 reports), graffiti (1,010 reports), and e-scooter issues (1,007 reports).

The average response time to close a report for illegally dumped items was about 9 days. Graffiti removal requests were closed in an average of about 58 hours. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 11 hours on average.
