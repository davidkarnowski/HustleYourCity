Here's the latest on Long Beach service requests from the past week. The city received 600 requests for Dumped Items, with an average response time of about 28 hours. Graffiti was the next most common report, with 277 requests closed in an average of about a day and a half.

Response times for other common issues included about 12.5 hours for Street Repair and Sidewalk Repair requests. Reports for street light issues saw an average response time of about 2.5 days. The 232 reports for E-Scooters were referred directly to the scooter companies.

Reporting issues helps the city track and address community needs. If you see something that needs attention, use the Go Long Beach service application to make a report.
