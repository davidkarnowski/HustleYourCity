Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests and response times.

Over the past month, the most common service requests from the public were for dumped items (2,058 reports), e-scooters (969 reports), and graffiti (896 reports). The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 2 days. For dumped items, the average response time was around 4.5 days.

Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 12 hours on average. Slower response times were seen for reports of illegal fireworks and traffic signal issues, which took an average of about 5.5 days to be closed.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
