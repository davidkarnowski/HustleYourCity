Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 90-day service request data for the City of Long Beach to keep the community informed on local government response times and service volumes.

Over the past 90 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,610 reports. The city took an average of 10.4 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service, totaling 3,106 reports with an average response time of 3.2 days. 

Other notable service categories include Street Repair, which saw 840 requests averaging 5.7 days for a response, and Tree Maintenance, with 891 requests averaging 5.8 days. E-Scooter complaints reached 2,928 total reports, though all were immediately referred to outside agencies rather than handled directly by city staff.

Some categories show exceptionally fast resolution times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. It is important to note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to actively report neighborhood issues and help track city services by using the Go Long Beach service application.
