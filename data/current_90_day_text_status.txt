In the last 90 days Long Beach residents filed 7,236 service requests across 22 categories.

The biggest volumes were Dumped Items (6,401 reports) and Graffiti (3,631 reports). Both saw faster handling than the all‑time averages: Graffiti’s average response time dropped to 135 hours (≈5.6 days) from 351 hours historically, while Dumped Items averaged 372 hours (≈15.5 days) compared with the all‑time 260 hours.

Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were resolved very quickly, with average response times of 17.7 hours and 16.7 hours respectively. Light outages and Tree Maintenance also improved, averaging 50 hours and 55 hours.

A few categories show unusually short times (e.g., Park Maintenance at 0.2 hours). These may reflect administrative closures rather than on‑site work.

Overall, the city is handling most requests within days rather than weeks, but some areas—especially Dumped Items—still take over two weeks to close.

Residents can help keep the city accountable by reporting issues directly through the Go Long Beach service app.
