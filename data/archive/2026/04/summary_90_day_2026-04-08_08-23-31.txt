Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data from the past 90 days. Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues across the city. Residents submitted 6,942 reports for dumped items and 3,204 reports for graffiti during this period.

Response times vary significantly by category. On average, the city takes about 8.6 days to respond to dumped items. Graffiti reports are handled much faster, averaging a 2.4-day response time. Street repairs, which saw 746 requests, take an average of 7.1 days to process.

Some service categories show extremely fast turnaround times. Dead animal pickups average a 10-hour response. Business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep the city accountable and well-maintained. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
