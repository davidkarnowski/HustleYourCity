In the past 90 days Long Beach residents reported 6,167 dumped‑item calls and 3,731 graffiti calls – the two highest volumes of all service types.  

The average city response time for dumped items was about 384 hours (≈ 16 days). Graffiti requests were closed in roughly 164 hours (≈ 7 days). Other busy categories included 740 tree‑maintenance reports (average 157 hours, ≈ 7 days) and 625 light‑issue reports (average 178 hours, ≈ 7 days).  

Some services are being resolved much faster. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup each had an average response of about 17 hours (under 1 day). Sidewalk repairs took about 126 hours (≈ 5 days) and park‑maintenance requests averaged 437 hours (≈ 18 days).  

These figures show where the city is meeting expectations and where longer waits remain. Residents can help by reporting issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app.
