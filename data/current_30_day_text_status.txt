In the past 30 days Long Beach residents filed over 5,700 service requests across all categories.

The fastest average response was for Tree Maintenance – about 10.4 hours from report to action. Traffic Signal issues were addressed in roughly 12 hours, and Light repairs took about 28 hours on average.

Graffiti reports (1,418 total) were closed in an average of 44.9 hours, a notable improvement from the 131‑hour average seen in the prior 90 days. Dumped Items remain the longest‑standing category, with an average response of 245 hours (just over 10 days).

All other services showed steady performance: Street Repair ≈ 22 hours, Sidewalk Repair ≈ 16 hours, Animal Services ≈ 21 hours, and Trash & Debris ≈ 59 hours.

Residents can continue to report issues quickly through the Go Long Beach mobile app, helping the city keep response times moving in the right direction.
