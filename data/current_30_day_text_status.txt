In the last 30 days Long Beach residents reported 1,421 graffiti incidents, the highest volume of any category. The average time to close a graffiti request was about 67 hours (just under 3 days).

E‑Scooter reports totaled 776, almost all marked as “Closed Referred,” meaning they were forwarded for follow‑up. Dumped Items generated 2,035 calls with an average response time of 176 hours – roughly 7 days.

Tree Maintenance had 237 requests and was the fastest to resolve, averaging just under 10 hours per case. Light outages (243 reports) were closed in about 39 hours on average, while Street Repair (192 reports) took about 21 hours.

Overall, most service types are being addressed within a few days, though some categories like Dumped Items still see week‑long waits. Residents can help keep response times short by reporting issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app.
