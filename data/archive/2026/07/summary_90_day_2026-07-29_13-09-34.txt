The latest service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,178 reports for dumped items and 3,404 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 5 hours.
