Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update, focusing on the last 90 days.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,052 calls), graffiti removal (3,390 calls), and improperly parked e-scooters (2,747 calls).

The average time to close a service request varied by issue. Reports for dumped items were addressed in about 13 days, while graffiti removal took about 5 days. Street repairs averaged a 6-day response. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups and other animal services, both averaging around 14 hours to resolve.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
