Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past week.

Dumped items and graffiti were the most frequent issues reported by residents, with a combined total of 947 service requests.

The data shows the average response time for graffiti reports was about 25 hours. Dumped item reports were addressed in about 19 hours on average. Reports for abandoned shopping carts had a very fast average response of about 39 minutes, though this may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at February 8, 2026, 3:35 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at February 8, 2026, 3:00 AM PST.
