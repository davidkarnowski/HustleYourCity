Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data to provide an update on response times over the past 90 days. Here is a look at the current numbers for Long Beach.

The highest volume of reports came from Dumped Items, with 7008 requests. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these cases. Graffiti was the second most reported issue, totaling 3260 calls with an average response time of 2.3 days.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which received 724 reports and averaged a 7.2-day response time. Tree Maintenance saw 902 requests, taking an average of 7.7 days to address. Dead Animal Pickup handled 468 requests with an average turnaround of 10.6 hours.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast resolution times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.
