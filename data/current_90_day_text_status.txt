Here is a look at Long Beach service request data from the last 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 requests, with the top categories being dumped items (6,116 requests) and graffiti (3,743 requests).

Average city response times for closed requests varied. Park maintenance took the longest at nearly three weeks. Reports for dumped items averaged a response of over two weeks, while street repairs took about nine and a half days. Graffiti removal averaged just under one week.

Quicker responses were recorded for urgent issues like dead animal pickups (about 16 hours) and animal services (about 15 hours). Some requests show very fast completion times, which may indicate administrative closures rather than on-site work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
