Hustle Long Beach! community update. This independent review covers city service data from the last 30 days. The highest volume of requests was for Dumped Items, with 2,234 total reports. The average response time for these issues was approximately 5.5 days.

Graffiti reports were the second most frequent issue with 1,129 total calls. The city averaged a response time of just under 2 days for these requests. Street Repair generated 258 reports with an average wait time of roughly 3.2 days.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup, which had 137 reports and an average response of 14 hours. Abandoned Shopping Carts were addressed in approximately 13 hours across 36 reports.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
