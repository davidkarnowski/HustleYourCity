Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times. This review looks at the last 90 days of service call data to help residents understand how quickly issues are being addressed.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 6,940 reports. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti reports were the second most common, totaling 3,245 calls. The average response time for graffiti was about 2.3 days, with the vast majority of these cases already closed.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests like Tree Maintenance with 895 reports and Street Repair with 710 reports are currently averaging response times of about 8 days.

Some services show much faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup averaged a 10-hour response time across 464 reports. Meanwhile, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times measured in minutes are often the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
