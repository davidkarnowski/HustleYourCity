Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on public data.

Over the past 90 days, the most common service requests from residents were for dumped items (6,121 reports) and graffiti removal (3,739 reports). The average response time for graffiti was about 6.5 days, while dumped items took an average of about 15.5 days to be resolved.

Recent data shows significant improvements. In the last 30 days, the average response for graffiti dropped to about 2 days, and dumped items response times were cut to about 7.5 days. Other notable 90-day averages include street repair at 9.5 days and dead animal pickup at 16 hours.
