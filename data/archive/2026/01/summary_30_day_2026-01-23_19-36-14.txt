Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times from the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents made more than 5,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,198 requests), graffiti (911 requests), and e-scooters (915 requests).

Response times varied by service type. The average response time for graffiti removal was under 3 days. In contrast, the average time to resolve a request for illegally dumped items was about 7.5 days. Street repair requests were completed in just over 3 days on average. Some services, like dead animal pickup, were addressed much faster, with an average response time of about 12 hours. Note that some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
