An evaluation of Long Beach city service data over the past 90 days reveals current response times for high-volume requests. The category with the highest volume was Dumped Items, totaling 6,592 reports. The average response time for these issues was approximately 11 days and 17 hours.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,118, with an average resolution time of 3 and a half days. Street Repair requests, which totaled 844, showed an average response time of 5 days. Tree Maintenance issues took approximately 6 days to resolve on average.

Quicker response times were noted for public health and safety issues. Dead Animal Pickup requests averaged 12 and a half hours for completion across 431 reports.
