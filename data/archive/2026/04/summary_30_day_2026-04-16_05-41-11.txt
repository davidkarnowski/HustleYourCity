Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. This independent analysis helps residents track how quickly the City of Long Beach responds to community issues.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2,326 requests in the last 30 days. The average response time for these reports is 8.5 days. Graffiti removal saw 1,063 requests, averaging a response time of 54 hours. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 1,008 reports, almost all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies.

Street repair received 215 calls with an average response time of 67 hours. Dead animal pickups and animal services were handled much faster, averaging around 7 to 8 hours for their 164 and 74 respective calls. Residents should note that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures or referrals rather than completed physical work.

To help keep the community maintained, residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 15, 2026 10:38 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 15, 2026 7:00 PM PDT.
