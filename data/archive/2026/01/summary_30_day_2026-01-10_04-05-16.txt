Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the past 30 days.

The most frequent issues reported by residents were Dumped Items with 1,936 calls and Graffiti with 955 calls. The city also received 968 reports about E-Scooters, which are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Response times for service requests varied. The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report was about 6.5 days. Reports for "Trash & Debris" took longer, averaging nearly 10 days. Faster responses included "Dead Animal Pickup," which averaged about 14 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it. Using the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify city departments.
