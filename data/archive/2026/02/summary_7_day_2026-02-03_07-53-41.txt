Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests for the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,380 requests through the city's service system. The most common report was for illegally dumped items, with 574 requests logged. The average response time to close these requests was just under two days.

Graffiti was the second most frequent issue with 249 reports, which were addressed in about 1.3 days on average. The fastest-resolved requests were for dead animal pickups, averaging about 4.5 hours from report to closure.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it through the Go Long Beach service application.
