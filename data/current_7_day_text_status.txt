Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last seven days, residents submitted more than 1,200 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (431 requests), e-scooter issues (255 requests), and graffiti (247 requests).

The average time to close a graffiti report was about 26 hours. For dumped items, the average was about 34 hours. Some categories, like sidewalk repair, show very fast closure times of around 3 hours. These quick closures may be due to administrative updates and not always on-site work completion.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the best way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 16, 2025, 3:30 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 16, 2025, 3:00 AM PST.
