Here is the latest Hustle Long Beach! summary of city service response times over the past 24 hours.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 68 reports, though no average response time has been recorded yet. E-Scooter complaints followed with 43 reports, all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies.

Several services saw exceptionally fast turnaround times. Tree Maintenance logged 15 calls with an average response time of just 20 minutes. Animal Services resolved its single request in 21 minutes. Running Water and Dead Animal Pickup averaged 61 and 62 minutes respectively. Graffiti cleanup saw 19 reports with an average response time of 85 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Other notable response times include Street Repair, which handled 11 calls with an average response time of 2.5 hours, and Sidewalk Repair, which managed 5 calls in an average of 4.8 hours.

Residents are encouraged to download and use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
