Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted thousands of service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,060 calls), graffiti (3,454 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,719 calls).

Response times varied significantly by issue. The average time to resolve a dumped item request was about 13 days. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickups were closed in about 16 hours on average. The longest wait was for marina repairs, which took an average of nearly 25 days to complete.

Some data can be skewed by a small number of reports. For example, the average response for a damaged drinking fountain was just over 2 hours, but this is based on only one closed request. Unusually low times can sometimes reflect administrative updates rather than completed work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
