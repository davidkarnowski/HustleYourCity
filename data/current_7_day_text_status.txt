Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Residents submitted 431 requests for dumped item pickups, 256 for e-scooter issues, and 248 for graffiti removal, making them the most reported issues. The city's average response time to close graffiti reports was about 26 hours. For dumped items, the average was around 35 hours.

Street repair requests were addressed in an average of about 18.5 hours. Some categories, like sidewalk repair (3 hours), show very fast average closure times. These may reflect administrative updates rather than the completion of physical work.
