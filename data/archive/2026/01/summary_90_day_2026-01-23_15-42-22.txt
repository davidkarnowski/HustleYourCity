Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 90 days.

Over the past three months, the most frequent service requests were for dumped items (6,114 reports), graffiti (3,379 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,670 reports). The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 13 days, while graffiti reports were addressed in about 5 days.

Some services saw much faster response times. Reports for dead animal pickups were typically resolved in about 15 hours, and animal services calls were handled in about 14 hours. Other common requests included street repairs, which took about 6 days on average, and streetlight outages, which were fixed in about 4 days. Response times can vary, and some quick closures may be administrative.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to notify the city.
