Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data. In the past month, residents submitted over 5,100 service requests through the city's reporting system.

The most frequent issues reported were for dumped items, illegally parked e-scooters, and graffiti. These three categories accounted for over 70% of all service requests.

Average response times for closed cases varied by issue. Dead animal pickup requests were resolved in about 14 hours. Graffiti removal took an average of just over 2 days. Requests to clear dumped items were completed in an average of 5.5 days.

To report non-emergency issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
