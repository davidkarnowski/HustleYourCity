Here is the latest 30-day snapshot of Long Beach city service requests.

According to city data, illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with nearly 2,500 calls. Graffiti and e-scooter issues each received over 1,000 reports.

Average response times varied by service. The data shows graffiti removal took about 2.5 days. Street repair requests were handled in about 2.7 days on average. For dumped items, the average response time was longer, at about 10 days.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the best way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your phone.

This data summary was updated at February 2, 2026, at 8:57 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at February 2, 2026, at 7:00 PM PST.
