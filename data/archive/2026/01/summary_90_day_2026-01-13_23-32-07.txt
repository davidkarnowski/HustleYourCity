Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped items (5,985 calls), graffiti removal (3,477 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,703 calls).

The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests were handled in an average of just under 5 days, while street repairs took about 1 week. Faster responses were seen for issues like dead animal pickups, which averaged about 15 hours.

Some categories show unusually fast response times. For example, the average for damaged drinking fountains was about 2 hours. With only four calls in this period, this may reflect an administrative closure rather than a completed physical repair.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
