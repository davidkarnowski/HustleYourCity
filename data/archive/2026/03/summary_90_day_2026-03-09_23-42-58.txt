Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day city service response data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 6,488 calls in the last 90 days. The city took an average of 10.7 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal saw 3,103 reports, with an average response time of 3.3 days.

Infrastructure requests also saw high volumes. There were 1,041 reports for lighting issues, which took an average of 3.4 days to address, and 834 street repair requests, averaging 5.4 days for a response. Tree maintenance received 878 reports, taking about 5.7 days on average.

Some services showed incredibly fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. However, residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead animal pickups were also handled relatively quickly, averaging 13.2 hours across 432 reports.

On the other end of the spectrum, marina repairs took the longest, averaging 45 days to resolve.

Facts and statistics like these help keep the community informed. If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
