Here is a look at Long Beach service request data from the last 90 days.

Over the past 90 days, the most frequent service requests from residents were for "Dumped Items" with 6,186 calls and "Graffiti" with 3,723 calls.

Average city response times for some key services include approximately 2.5 weeks for "Park Maintenance" and about 2 weeks for "Dumped Items". "Street Repair" requests were addressed in about 10 days on average.

Faster responses were seen for "Graffiti" removal, which averaged about 6 days. Reports for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were handled much quicker, with average response times under 17 hours. Some rapid closures can be administrative and may not reflect on-site work completion.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the city's Go Long Beach service application.
