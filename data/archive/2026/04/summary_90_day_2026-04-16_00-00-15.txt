Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the last 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports and service response times.

Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 6,929 reports. The average response time for these requests was about 8.5 days.

Graffiti removal had 3,237 reports, with crews responding in an average of 58 hours.

Tree Maintenance requests totaled 895, taking an average of 8.7 days to address.

Street Repair saw 707 calls, averaging 7.8 days for a response.

Dead Animal Pickup had 461 reports, with an average response time of about 10 hours.

Business License and Permit Concerns had an unusually fast response time of just 12 minutes for 6 requests. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
