Hustle Long Beach! presents an independent review of city service requests over the last 90 days. This analysis is not affiliated with the City of Long Beach or any government agency.

The highest volume of service requests was for Dumped Items, with 6,507 total reports. The average response time for these issues is currently nearly 13 days.

Graffiti removal received 3,107 reports and shows an average completion time of nearly 4 days. Street Repair requests, totaling 802 reports, are currently being resolved in approximately 4 and a half days.

Dead Animal Pickup is performing quickly with an average response of under 13 hours. However, general Trash and Debris reports are averaging over 10 days for resolution.

Residents should continue to report maintenance issues and safety concerns through the Go Long Beach service application.
