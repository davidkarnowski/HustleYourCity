The latest city service data for Long Beach shows ongoing activity across several key categories over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 6,680 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 8.5 days. Graffiti reports totaled 3,209 with an average response time of 55 minutes. Tree maintenance saw 951 reports with an average response time of 8 days.
