Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days to keep the community informed. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling local issues.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 486 reports. The city averaged a response time of 3.2 days for these requests. E-Scooter complaints followed with 239 reports, all of which were referred to closed status.

Graffiti removal saw 226 reports, with an average response time of 33.6 hours. Street Repair handled 56 calls, averaging a 32.7-hour response.

Some services showed very rapid turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services both averaged about 7.5 hours to respond. Running Water reports averaged just 13 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep the community safe and clean. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
