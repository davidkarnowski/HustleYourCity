The latest city service data for Long Beach shows ongoing activity across several key categories over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 7,967 reports logged. While many are closed, a significant number remain in progress. Response times for these items average approximately 13 days.

Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,663 reports. The city has maintained a steady pace here, with an average response time of about 63 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 1,064 reports in the last 90 days. Response times for these requests currently average about 4 days.

For urgent issues like dead animal pickup and animal services, response times remain efficient, averaging roughly 7 hours and 11 hours respectively.
