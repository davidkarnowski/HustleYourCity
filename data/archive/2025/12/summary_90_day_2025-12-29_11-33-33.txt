Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests were for illegally dumped items, with 5,908 reports filed. The average time to resolve these requests was about 15 days. The second most common request was for graffiti removal, with 3,627 reports and an average completion time of about 5 days.

Response times varied by service type. Requests for marina repairs had one of the longest average completion times at about 26 days. In contrast, reports for dead animal pickups were among the fastest, resolved in an average of about 16 hours.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at December 29, 2025, 3:30 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 29, 2025, 3:00 AM PST.
