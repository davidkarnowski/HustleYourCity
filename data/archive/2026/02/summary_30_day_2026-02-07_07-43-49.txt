Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 30 days.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with 2,577 service calls, followed by 1,124 reports for graffiti.

The average response time to resolve a graffiti report was just over 2.5 days. Service calls for dumped items took an average of about 8.5 days to close. One of the quicker response categories was dead animal pickup, which averaged about 11 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
