The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 30 days. Residents submitted 2,391 reports for dumped items, with 884 of those cases now closed. Graffiti removal remains a high-volume service, with 823 reports and 633 cases closed during this period.

Response times for several categories have been notable. Dead animal pickup requests saw an average response time of approximately 7 minutes, while animal services requests averaged about 7 hours. Street repair response times averaged 26 hours, and light maintenance requests averaged 43 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
