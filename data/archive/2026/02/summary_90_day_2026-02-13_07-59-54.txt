Here is the latest review of Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items continue to be the highest volume category with 6,396 reports filed. The average time to resolve these requests is currently running about 2 weeks.

Graffiti removal teams received 3,142 reports and are averaging a response time of just over 4 days. Street repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 793 reports with an average completion time of roughly 5 days.

Faster services include dead animal pickup, which averaged under 12 hours per request across 405 reports.

E-scooter issues generated 2,722 reports, though these are almost exclusively referred to third-party operators rather than handled directly by city crews.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city has accurate data on neighborhood needs.
