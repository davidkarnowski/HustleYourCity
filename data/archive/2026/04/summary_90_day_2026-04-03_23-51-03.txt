Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest third-party accountability update evaluating city service response times over the last 90 days.

The highest volume of citizen reports involved Dumped Items with 7,050 requests, followed by Graffiti with 3,134 requests, and E-Scooter issues with 2,865 requests.

Response times vary significantly by category. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to Dumped Items and about 6.6 days for Street Repair requests. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 58.8 hours.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup averaged 10.3 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or system referrals rather than actual work completions.
