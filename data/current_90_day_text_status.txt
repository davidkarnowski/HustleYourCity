The latest data for the past 90 days shows significant activity across city services. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,622 reports, averaging a response time of approximately 17 days.

Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,561 reports and an average response time of about 2 days and 17 hours. Tree maintenance requests totaled 1,041, with an average response time of 4 days and 11 hours.

Response times for dead animal pickup have been efficient, averaging 7 minutes. Other services like light repairs and sidewalk maintenance are seeing average response times of 2 days and 4 hours, and 4 days and 16 hours respectively.

Please note that unusually low response times may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track city service performance.
