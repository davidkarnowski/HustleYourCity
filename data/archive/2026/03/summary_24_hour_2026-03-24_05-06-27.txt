Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 24-hour service call data to keep citizens informed on city response times and service volumes.

Over the past day, Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 90 new cases, though response times are not yet available as most remain in the new queue. E-Scooter complaints followed with 53 reports, all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies. Graffiti issues saw 44 reports with an average response time of 2.2 hours.

Several service categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Tree Maintenance handled 21 reports with an average response time of 45 minutes. Vegetation issues averaged 50 minutes across 4 reports. Animal Services and Community Center reports averaged 70 minutes and 72 minutes, respectively. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

On the higher end of the 24-hour spectrum, Light repair requests took the longest to process, averaging 16.1 hours for 7 reports. Traffic Signal issues averaged 8.9 hours across 3 reports, and Dead Animal Pickup averaged 7.7 hours for 2 reports.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help maintain neighborhood accountability.
