Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports. Here is a look at the numbers and response times for the past month.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,290 reports. On average, it took the city about 7 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 998 calls, which saw an average response time of about 40 hours. E-Scooter complaints remained high with 904 reports, though these are typically referred to outside vendors immediately.
