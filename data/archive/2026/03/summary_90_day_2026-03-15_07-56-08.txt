Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service request data to keep you informed on how quickly the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs.

In the past 90 days, the highest volume of citizen reports involved Dumped Items with 6,634 requests, Graffiti with 3,075 requests, and E-Scooter concerns with 2,893 requests.

Response times vary significantly by the type of issue. For example, the city took an average of 10 days to respond to Dumped Items and about 3 days to address Graffiti. Street Repair requests averaged a 6-day response time, while Tree Maintenance took nearly 6 days.

Some categories show exceptionally fast turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup averaged just over 13 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this are often due to administrative closures or immediate referrals, rather than the actual completion of the requested work.

Reporting local issues helps keep the city accountable and Long Beach neighborhoods clean. Residents are highly encouraged to download and use the Go Long Beach service application to submit and track community concerns directly.

This data summary was updated at March 15, 2026, at 12:52 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 15, 2026, at 12:00 AM PDT.
