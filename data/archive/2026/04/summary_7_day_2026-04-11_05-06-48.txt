Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest independent look at city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped items generated the highest call volume with 529 reports, taking an average of 68.6 hours to process. Graffiti reports were the second most frequent with 345 calls, averaging a 26.1-hour response time. E-scooter complaints reached 273, though all were referred to outside agencies, resulting in no measurable response time.

Other notable service requests included 52 reports for lighting issues, which averaged 31.6 hours, and 47 tree maintenance calls averaging 17.5 hours. Dead animal pickups saw 41 reports with an average response of 15.6 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help keep the city maintained.
