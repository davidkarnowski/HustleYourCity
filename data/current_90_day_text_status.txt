The latest data update for city service requests shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,371 reports for dumped items and 2,591 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickups have been efficient, averaging about 7 hours. Graffiti removal has averaged approximately 61 hours, while tree maintenance requests have averaged about 8 days. Some reported response times under 90 minutes may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed as efficiently as possible.
