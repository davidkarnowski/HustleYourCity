Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past week.

The most frequent service requests from the public were for "Dumped Items" (625 calls) and "Graffiti" (265 calls). The average time to resolve these issues was approximately 26 hours for dumped items and 26.5 hours for graffiti.

Response times for other core services included Street Repair (about 19.5 hours), Tree Maintenance (about 17 hours), and Sidewalk Repair (about 12 hours). The city received 198 reports for E-Scooters, which were referred to vendors for resolution.
