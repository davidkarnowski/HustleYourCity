The latest 90‑day service data shows how quickly the City is handling requests.

- Graffiti received 3,720 reports; the average response time is about 163 hours (≈ 7 days).  
- Dumped Items had 6,153 calls with an average response of 384 hours (≈ 16 days).  
- Light‑related issues (streetlights, signals) logged 624 requests; average response is 179 hours (≈ 7 days).  
- Tree Maintenance recorded 738 calls; average response is 153 hours (≈ 6 days).  
- Sidewalk Repair saw 316 requests; average response is 127 hours (≈ 5 days).  
- Trash & Debris had 158 reports; average response is 319 hours (≈ 13 days).  

Animal‑related services are much faster:  
- Animal Services: 202 calls, average response 16.8 hours.  
- Dead Animal Pickup: 426 calls, average response 16.5 hours.  

These quicker times may reflect administrative closures rather than on‑site work, so some very low figures could be procedural.

Residents can report new issues or track existing ones through the Go Long Beach app.
