Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest third-party accountability update on city service response times. This review looks at the data from the last 30 days to see how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2331 calls in the past month. The average response time for these reports is currently 8.5 days.

Graffiti removal is the second most requested service, totaling 1104 reports. The city is responding to these in an average of 40 hours.

E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 1003 reports. Meanwhile, dead animal pickups received 161 reports and were addressed in an average of 7 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
