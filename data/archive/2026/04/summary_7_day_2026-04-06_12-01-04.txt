Hustle Long Beach! presents the latest 7-day accountability report on city service requests. This analysis breaks down the numbers so citizens know exactly how long it takes for issues to be addressed in the community.

Over the past week, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 591 reports. The city averaged a 47-hour response time for these issues, with 246 currently in progress and 76 closed.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service, totaling 257 reports. The response time for graffiti averaged 12 hours. E-Scooter complaints reached 271, though all of these were referred to outside agencies rather than handled directly by city staff.

Other notable service volumes include 71 reports for Light issues with a 15-hour average response, and 49 Tree Maintenance requests averaging an 8-hour response. Dead Animal Pickup handled 39 calls with a rapid average response of 2 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Staying active and reporting problems helps keep the government accountable.
