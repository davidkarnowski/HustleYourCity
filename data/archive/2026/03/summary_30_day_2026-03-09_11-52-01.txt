Hustle Long Beach! is here with your latest 30-day city service data update. As an independent accountability project, this summary breaks down public data to show how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood reports.

Over the past month, dumped items were the most reported issue with 2004 calls. The city took an average of 4.8 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most common issue with 1070 reports, which saw an average response time of 1.8 days. E-scooter complaints also remained high, generating 1011 reports.

Other notable service times include dead animal pickups, which took an average of 14.5 hours across 137 reports. Business license and permit concerns showed a rapid response time of just 12 minutes. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. 

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, help keep the city accountable. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
