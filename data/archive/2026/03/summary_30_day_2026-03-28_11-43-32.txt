Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to neighborhood issues.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests over the last month with 2,278 reports. The city took an average of 7 days to respond to these calls. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 990 calls, averaging a 40-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints also remained high, totaling 903 reports.

Response times varied significantly across different departments. Dead Animal Pickup was handled quickly, averaging a 9-hour response for 155 calls. Vegetation issues were also addressed in about 11 hours. On the slower side, Street Repair took an average of 3 days to address 250 reports, and Park Maintenance averaged nearly 5 days for 73 calls. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
