Current Status Update
Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community issues. Dumped Items received the most reports with 6,754 requests, taking an average of 9.5 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,060 requests, averaging a 68-hour response time. Street Repair saw 847 requests with an average response time of 6.4 days, while Tree Maintenance had 965 requests averaging 6.6 days. Some services saw very fast turnaround times. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes, though unusually low response times can sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than completed work. Dead Animal Pickup requests were handled in an average of 11.5 hours. On the other end, Damaged Drinking Fountain reports took nearly 11 days to address, and Community Center issues averaged 8 days.
