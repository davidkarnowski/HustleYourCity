Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents submitted more than 6,100 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,406 requests), illegally parked e-scooters (1,042 requests), and graffiti removal (1,019 requests).

The average time to resolve a dumped items report was about 9 days. Graffiti reports were closed in an average of about 2.5 days. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups, which were completed in about 11 hours on average.

Reports for traffic signal repairs took an average of over 4 days to close, while street light repairs were completed in about 2.5 days.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.
