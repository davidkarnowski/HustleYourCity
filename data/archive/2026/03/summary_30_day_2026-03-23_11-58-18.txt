Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Over the past 30 days, residents have submitted thousands of requests, highlighting the most pressing issues across local neighborhoods.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2,197 requests in the last month. The city took an average of 5.6 days to respond to these cases. Graffiti was the second most common concern, generating 994 reports with an average response time of 37 hours. Electric scooter complaints also saw high volume with 932 reports, though all were referred to outside operators.

Response times varied significantly by category. Urgent issues like running water and dead animal pickups were handled quickly, averaging 8 hours and 9.5 hours respectively. Conversely, reports for illegal fireworks and marina repairs took over one week to process. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

Civic engagement makes a difference. Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
