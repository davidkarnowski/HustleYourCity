The latest data for city service requests in Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 7,719 reports logged. While many of these are in progress, the average response time for completed requests is approximately 16 days.

Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,589 reports. The city has maintained a relatively efficient response time here, averaging about 65 hours. Tree maintenance also saw high volume with 1,060 reports, averaging about 4 days for a response.

Some services show very fast turnaround times. Dead animal pickups are consistently handled quickly, averaging about 7 hours over the last 90 days. Please note that unusually low response times for certain categories may reflect administrative closures or automated referrals rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed as efficiently as possible.
