Hustle Long Beach community, here is an independent review of city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 6,602 requests. The city took an average of 10.4 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,107 calls, averaging a 3.2-day response time.

E-Scooter complaints reached 2,930. All of these cases were referred to other entities, meaning no direct city response time was tracked.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnarounds. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a 12-minute response time across 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
