Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the past 90 days.

Residents submitted thousands of service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (6,073), graffiti (3,709), and e-scooter issues (2,361).

Average city response times for some key services include approximately 2.5 weeks for park maintenance, 2 weeks for dumped items, and about 10 days for street repairs. Faster responses were recorded for graffiti removal, averaging about 6 days. Reports for dead animal pickups were handled in about 16 hours on average.

Of the 2,361 e-scooter complaints, nearly all were referred to third-party operators and not handled directly by city staff. Some categories show unusually fast closure times, which may reflect administrative processing rather than completed physical repairs.
