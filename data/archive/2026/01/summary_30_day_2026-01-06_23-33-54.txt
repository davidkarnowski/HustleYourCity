Here is the latest look at Long Beach service request data from the past 30 days. The numbers show what issues residents are reporting most and how quickly the city is responding.

"Dumped Items" was the most reported issue with 1,944 requests, followed by 970 for E-Scooters and 924 for Graffiti. E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to the scooter companies and not handled by city staff.

Average response times varied by issue. The city closed "Dumped Items" requests in about 8 days on average. Graffiti reports were addressed more quickly, with an average response time of about 2.3 days. Requests for "Tree Maintenance" and "Street Repair" were closed in about 37 and 42 hours, respectively.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
