Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times for the past 7 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of reports with 599 requests, taking an average of 63 hours to address. E-scooter issues followed with 260 reports, and graffiti had 203 reports with an average response time of 27 hours.

Some services required longer turnaround times. Reports of illegal fireworks took nearly 5 days to resolve, and shoes on a utility wire took 3.5 days. On the faster side, running water issues were handled in about 2 hours, and dead animal pickups averaged 2.5 hours. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
