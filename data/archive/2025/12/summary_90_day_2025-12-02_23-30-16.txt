City service call trends for the last 90 days show several key patterns.

- Dumped Items received 6,178 requests, with an average response time of about 15.4 days (369.8 hours).  
- Graffiti had 3,698 reports, averaging 6.4 days (154.2 hours) to respond.  
- E‑Scooter issues totaled 2,145, most of which were closed by referral; no response time is recorded.  
- Light outages (630 calls) were addressed in roughly 41.6 hours on average.  
- Tree Maintenance (752 calls) saw a faster turnaround, about 31.6 hours.  
- Animal Services (223 calls) and Dead Animal Pickup (435 calls) were both resolved in about 16.5 hours on average.

Overall, the city handled over 15,000 service requests in the past three months, with most categories responding well within the 72‑hour benchmark. Very quick responses (under 90 minutes) were not observed in this period.

Residents are encouraged to report issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app to keep response times short.
