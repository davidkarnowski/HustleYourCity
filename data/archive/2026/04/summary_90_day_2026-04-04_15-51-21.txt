Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data to bring you an update on response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items are the most frequently reported issue, with 7,056 calls. The city takes an average of 9 days to resolve these requests. Graffiti removal is the second most common issue, with 3,143 reports and an average response time of 58 hours.

Other notable service times include street repairs, which saw 817 requests and take an average of 6.7 days to complete. Dead animal pickups are handled much faster, averaging a 10-hour response across 447 calls. Business license and permit concerns show a response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times, especially those under 90 minutes, may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help maintain the city.

This data summary was updated at April 4, 2026, 8:47 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 4, 2026, 7:00 AM PDT.
