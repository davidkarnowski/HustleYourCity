Hustle Long Beach! update on city service response times.

Over the last 90 days, the city has processed a high volume of service requests. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 6,702 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 8.7 days. Graffiti reports totaled 3,296 with an average response time of about 2.7 days.

Response times for urgent matters like dead animal pickups have been efficient, averaging roughly 6 hours over the last 90 days. Tree maintenance requests averaged about 2.8 days for the same period. Please note that unusually low response time figures in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

We encourage all residents to continue using the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. This helps ensure that city departments have the necessary data to track and address community needs effectively.
