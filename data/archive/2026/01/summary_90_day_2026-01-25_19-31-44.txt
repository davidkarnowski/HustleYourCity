Here's a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days.

Illegally dumped items remain the most reported issue, with over 6,100 requests. The average time to resolve these cases was about 13 days. Graffiti was the second most common report with over 3,300 requests, which were closed in about 5 days on average.

Some of the quickest responses were for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup, with requests handled in about 13 to 14 hours on average. Slower responses included Marina Repairs, which took an average of over 3 weeks to resolve.
