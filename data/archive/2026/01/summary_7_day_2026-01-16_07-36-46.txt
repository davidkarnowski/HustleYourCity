Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,519 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (588 requests), illegally parked e-scooters (263 requests), and graffiti (240 requests).

The data shows the average response time for graffiti removal was about 29 hours. Reports for dumped items were addressed in about 2 days on average, while street repairs took approximately 21 hours. The average response for a marina repair request was about 4 days.

Some categories show very fast resolutions. For example, a restroom maintenance request was closed in about 16 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
