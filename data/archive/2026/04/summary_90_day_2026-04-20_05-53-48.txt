Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. This independent analysis looks at how long it takes for the City of Long Beach to respond to citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 6,969 requests in the last 90 days. The average response time for these reports is about 8.5 days. Graffiti is the second most common issue, with 3,247 reports and an average response time of 56 hours. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 2,882 reports.

Tree maintenance requests, totaling 886 calls, are taking an average of 9 days to resolve. Street repairs, with 697 reports, are taking about 8 days. On the faster side, dead animal pickups are averaging a 9.6-hour response time across 467 reports.

Some categories show unusually fast response times. For example, business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city and help keep local government accountable.
