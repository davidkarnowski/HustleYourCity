Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues. The City of Long Beach received 6,883 reports for dumped items, taking an average of about 9.5 days to respond. Graffiti saw 3,111 reports, with response times averaging around 2.7 days. Street repairs also saw high volume with 866 calls, taking an average of 6.5 days to address.

Some services show much faster turnarounds. Dead animal pickups, totaling 446 reports, are being handled in about 11.4 hours. Interestingly, business license and permit concerns showed a response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures or automatic system referrals rather than actual work completions.

Help keep Long Beach accountable and local neighborhoods clean. All citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 27, 2026, 9:04 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 27, 2026, 8:00 AM PDT.
