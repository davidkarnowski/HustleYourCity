In the past 90 days Long Beach logged about 16,350 service requests across all categories. The highest volumes were Dumped Items (6,495 reports) and Graffiti (3,537 reports), followed by E‑Scooter issues (2,486) and Tree Maintenance (833).

Average response times varied widely. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were addressed quickly, with average closures in roughly 17.5 hours and 16.4 hours respectively. Light repairs and Tree Maintenance averaged about 62 hours and 66 hours. More complex work took longer: Graffiti reports required an average of 6.7 days, Dumped Items about 15 days, and Report Illegal Firework roughly 9.8 days. Some categories, such as Shoes on a Utility Wire, were resolved in about 33 hours.

Residents can help the city track and resolve issues faster by submitting reports through the Go Long Beach service app.
