The latest data from the City of Long Beach service call system shows consistent activity across key maintenance categories over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 8,012 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 13 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,660 reports and an average response time of about 2.5 days. Tree maintenance saw 1,081 reports with an average response time of just over 4 days.

For urgent matters, dead animal pickup remains efficient, averaging roughly 7 hours for the 527 reports filed in the last 90 days. Other services like street repair and sidewalk maintenance are averaging roughly 6 days and 5 days respectively.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed by the appropriate departments.
