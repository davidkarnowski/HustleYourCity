Last week Long Beach recorded 1,251 service requests across 20 categories. The most common were Dumped Items (442 reports) and Graffiti (290 reports).

Average response times were generally fast. Traffic Signal issues were resolved in about 47 minutes, Restroom Maintenance in about 71 minutes, and Light repairs in roughly 27.6 hours. Tree Maintenance averaged 11.1 hours, and most other categories were addressed within 1–2 days.

New requests made up a large share of the work, for example 218 new Dumped Items reports and 50 new Graffiti reports in the past seven days.

Residents can track and report issues instantly via the Go Long Beach app.
