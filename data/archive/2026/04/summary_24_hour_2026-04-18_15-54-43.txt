Hustle Long Beach! is providing a daily look at the City of Long Beach service request data. Over the past 24 hours, citizens submitted a variety of requests, with some notable trends in volume and response times.

Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 81 total reports, though all remain new or duplicates with no closures yet. E-Scooter complaints followed with 39 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Street Repair also received 12 new reports that are currently awaiting action.

For requests that were addressed, the city logged some exceptionally fast turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup and Graffiti reports both averaged a response time of about 40 minutes. Sidewalk Repair requests averaged 53 minutes. Because these averages are under 90 minutes, it is important to note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or reassignments rather than actual physical work completions.

Other completed requests in the last day included Tree Maintenance, which averaged about 1.9 hours, and Light issues, which averaged 4.75 hours.

Residents are encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report concerns directly to the city and help keep Long Beach moving.
