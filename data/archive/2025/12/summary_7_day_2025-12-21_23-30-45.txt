Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past week. Residents submitted over 1,150 service requests through the Go Long Beach system.

The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (426 calls), followed by E-Scooters (245 calls) and Graffiti (232 calls).

The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 33 hours. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 21 hours. Some categories showed unusually fast response times, such as Sidewalk Repair at 45 minutes. These quick closures may be due to administrative processing and not always a final work completion.

If you see an issue that needs attention, from potholes to park maintenance, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
