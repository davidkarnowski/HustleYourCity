Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data, focusing on the most common issues reported by residents.

Over the past 90 days, "Dumped Items" was the most frequent service request with over 5,900 reports. The average response time for these requests was about 13.5 days. In comparison, the nearly 3,500 "Graffiti" reports were addressed in an average of about 4.5 days.

The city has shown quicker response times for other issues. Reports for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were typically closed in less than 16 hours on average.

Reporting issues helps keep the city accountable and improves our neighborhoods. If you see a problem, use the Go Long Beach app to submit a service request.

This data summary was updated at January 12, 2026, 3:32 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 12, 2026, 3:00 AM PST.
