Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last seven days, residents submitted over 1,240 service requests. The most reported issues were for dumped items (457 calls), illegally parked e-scooters (239 calls), and graffiti (173 calls).

The city's average response time for several key services was under two days. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 32 hours, while dumped items took about 43 hours. Street repair requests were closed in an average of 16 hours. Some services took longer, with reports of trash and debris taking about 3 days to resolve. Note: Response times can vary, and very fast closures may be administrative.
