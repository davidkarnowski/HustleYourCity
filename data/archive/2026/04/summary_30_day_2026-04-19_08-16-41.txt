Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a factual look at the city's response times and call totals over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 2,349 requests. The average response time was 8.3 days.

Graffiti removal saw 1,050 requests, taking an average of 53.4 hours for a response.

Street Repair had 214 requests with an average response time of 69.9 hours.

Light issues accounted for 235 requests, taking an average of 4.3 days to address.

Dead Animal Pickup had 173 requests, averaging 6.7 hours for a response.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
