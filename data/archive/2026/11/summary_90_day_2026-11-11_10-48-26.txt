The latest city service data for the last 90 days shows significant activity across several categories. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,645 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 17 days.

Tree maintenance saw 1,035 reports with an average response time of about 5 days. Graffiti removal remains active with 2,573 reports and an average response time of approximately 3 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging roughly 10 hours and 7 hours respectively.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that your concerns are tracked and addressed efficiently.
