Here is a look at Long Beach's service request data for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 964 service requests. The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (327), Graffiti (181), and E-Scooter issues (178).

The average response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 28 hours. Graffiti reports were closed in an average of about 24 hours. Requests for Dead Animal Pickup were completed in an average of about 7 hours. Some services show very fast completion times, such as Animal Services (about 3 hours). These may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
