Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the city is handling local service requests.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues. The city received 2254 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 5.7 days. Graffiti followed with 1027 reports, averaging a 40-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume, totaling 963 reports.

Some services saw rapid turnaround times. Running water issues averaged an 8-hour response, and dead animal pickups averaged 14 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. On the other end of the spectrum, reports for illegal fireworks and marina repairs averaged over 7 days for a response.

Help keep the city accountable and neighborhoods safe. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
