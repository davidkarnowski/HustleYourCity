Here is your weekly look at Long Beach city service data.

In the past week, residents submitted 1,248 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (455 calls), e-scooters (225 calls), and graffiti (185 calls).

The latest data on average response times for closed requests shows some variation. Reports for trash and debris took about 3 days to resolve. Graffiti removal averaged about 26 hours. Restroom maintenance requests were closed in an average of 83 minutes, though some fast closures can be administrative and may not reflect completed work.
