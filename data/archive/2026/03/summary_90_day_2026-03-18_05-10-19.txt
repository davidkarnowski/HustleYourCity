Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Here is a factual breakdown of city service response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 6674 reports. The city took an average of 10 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 3104 reports, averaging a response time of 69.6 hours. E-Scooter complaints totaled 2846, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Other significant categories included Tree Maintenance with 909 reports averaging a 6.7-day response time, and Street Repair with 829 reports averaging a 6.3-day response time. Dead Animal Pickups totaled 437 requests and were handled in roughly 11.7 hours on average.

Some categories showed unusually fast response times, which may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.
