In the last 90 days Long Beach recorded about 16,400 service calls. The biggest categories were Graffiti (3,540 reports) and Dumped Items (6,521 reports).  

Graffiti requests took an average of 162.7 hours (about 6.8 days) to close, while Dumped Items took 357.3 hours (roughly 15 days).  

Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were resolved much faster – average response times of 17.4 hours and 16.2 hours respectively. Tree Maintenance and Light repairs also saw quick turn‑around, averaging 67 hours (2.8 days) and 64 hours (2.7 days).  

Overall, most issues are being addressed within a few days, though some categories still need faster action.  

Residents can report new issues or check status through the Go Long Beach service app.
