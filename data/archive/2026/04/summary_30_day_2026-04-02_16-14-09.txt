Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest 30-day accountability report on city service requests. Here is a look at the numbers to see how quickly the city is addressing citizen concerns.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,323 reports over the last 30 days. The average response time for these issues is currently about 8 days.

Graffiti removal saw 940 requests, with the city responding in approximately 37 hours. E-Scooter complaints also remain high at 840 reports, though these are typically referred to outside vendors immediately.

Other notable response times include Street Repair, which received 222 calls and averaged a 3.4-day response time, and Park Maintenance, which handled 82 calls with an average response of 4.7 days. On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup had 168 calls and averaged a 5.5-hour response time.

Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative ticket closures rather than actual physical work completion.
