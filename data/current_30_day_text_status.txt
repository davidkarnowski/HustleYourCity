City service response times for the past 30 days show notable improvements across several request types.

- Tree Maintenance processed 274 requests with an average response of 11.5 hours, down from the all‑time average of over 150 days.  
- Dumped Items received 1,991 reports; average response time fell to 155 hours (about 6.5 days) versus the historic 258 hours.  
- Graffiti cases (1,314) were addressed in an average of 46.8 hours, a sharp drop from the previous 352‑hour average.  
- Light outages (219) and Traffic Signal issues (44) were resolved in roughly 28 hours and 43 hours respectively, both well under the historic 1,030‑hour and 2,538‑hour averages.

Other categories also show quick turn‑around: Sidewalk Repair (111 requests) averaged 16 hours, Street Repair (180) 28 hours, and Dead Animal Pickup (151) 22.6 hours.

These faster response times suggest the city’s service teams are handling reports more efficiently. Residents can continue to help by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach app.
