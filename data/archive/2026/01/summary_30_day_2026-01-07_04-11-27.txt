Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the last month, the most reported issues were dumped items (1,936 requests), e-scooters (972), and graffiti (915). The average response time to close a dumped items request was about 8 days, while graffiti reports were handled in about 2.5 days.

Requests for street repair and sidewalk repair were addressed in about 2 days on average. Longer response times were seen for traffic signal issues, which took about 7 days, and trash and debris clean-up, which averaged over 9 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
