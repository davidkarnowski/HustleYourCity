Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the last three months, the most common reports from residents were for dumped items (6,303 calls), graffiti (3,314 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,638 calls).

Average response times varied significantly by service type. The average time to close a report for dumped items was about 13.5 days, while graffiti cleanup took about 5 days. Some of the longest response times were for marina repairs, averaging 5 weeks, and damaged drinking fountains, averaging about 4 weeks.

Faster responses included dead animal pickups and animal service calls, which were closed in about 13 hours on average. Note that unusually fast response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.

To report issues like these, use the city's Go Long Beach service application.
