Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests.

In the past month, "Dumped Items" was the most frequent request with 1,968 reports. The average time to close these cases was about 7 days. "Graffiti" was the second most common report with 1,123 requests, which were resolved in an average of about 2.5 days.

Some services showed quicker response times. The 48 requests for "Animal Services" were closed in an average of about 11 hours. The 130 requests for "Dead Animal Pickup" were handled in about 13 hours.
