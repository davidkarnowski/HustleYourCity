The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows trends in service requests over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 6,986 reports, followed by 3,399 e-scooter reports and 3,305 graffiti reports.

Average response times for several categories have improved significantly compared to all-time averages. Dead animal pickup is currently averaging about 5 hours, while animal services are averaging 10 hours. Graffiti removal is averaging roughly 3 days. Please note that some very low response times may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. This helps the city track and address concerns across our neighborhoods.
