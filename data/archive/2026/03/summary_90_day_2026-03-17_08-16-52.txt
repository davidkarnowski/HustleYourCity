Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service request data to track how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs. Over the past 90 days, the city received thousands of requests across various categories. Here is a breakdown of the most significant numbers and response times.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,677 reports. The city took an average of 10 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal followed closely with 3,089 reports and an average response time of 71.5 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests showed longer turnaround times. Street Repair received 832 calls with an average response of 6.1 days, while Tree Maintenance had 911 reports averaging 6.6 days. Reports regarding city lights totaled 1,042 with a 3.3-day average response.

Some categories saw much faster action. Dead Animal Pickup handled 442 requests in an average of 12 hours. Business Licenses and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes for 6 requests. It is important to note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Additionally, 2,847 E-Scooter complaints were recorded, but all were immediately referred to outside agencies, resulting in no measurable city response time.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Tracking this data helps hold local government accountable for neighborhood maintenance.
