Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Over the past 30 days, citizens submitted thousands of requests. Here is a look at the most reported issues and the city's average response times.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,277 reports. The average response time for these requests was 7 days.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue, totaling 990 requests with an average response time of 40 hours.

E-Scooter complaints reached 903 reports. All of these were referred to outside agencies, so no response time data is available.

Street Repair saw 250 requests, taking an average of 3 days to address. Park Maintenance received 73 requests, with an average response time of nearly 5 days.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
