Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the past 90 days.

Dumped Items and Graffiti continue to be the most frequent service requests, with over 6,200 and 3,300 reports filed, respectively. E-scooter reports followed with over 2,700 submissions.

The average response time for Dumped Items was about 13 days. For Graffiti removal, the average was about 5 days. Sidewalk Repair requests were completed in about 3 days on average.

Some categories showed very low average response times, such as Animal Services (about 12 hours) and Dead Animal Pickup (about 13 hours). These quick times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
