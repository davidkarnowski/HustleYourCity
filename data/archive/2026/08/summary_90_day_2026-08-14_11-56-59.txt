The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across several categories over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 7,634 reports and an average response time of approximately 11 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 3,263 reports and an average response time of about 4 days. Tree maintenance saw 713 reports with an average response time of 4 days.

Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show the fastest response times, averaging 11 hours and 5 hours respectively over the last 90 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track service requests in their neighborhoods.
