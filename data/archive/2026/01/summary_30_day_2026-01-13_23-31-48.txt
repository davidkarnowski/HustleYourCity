Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Over 5,300 requests were submitted by residents. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,008 requests), e-scooter issues (934), and graffiti removal (909).

Response times varied by issue. On average, graffiti reports were closed in about 51 hours. Requests for dumped items took an average of about 5 days to resolve, while reports for trash and debris cleanup took nearly 8 days. Dead animal pickups were handled more quickly, with an average response time of about 14 hours.
