Here is the latest 30-day city service data update from Hustle Long Beach!

Over the past month, Dumped Items and Graffiti were the most frequently reported issues. Citizens submitted 2,247 reports for dumped items, which took an average of 5 days to resolve. Graffiti saw 1,053 reports with an average response time of 37 hours.

Tree maintenance received 497 calls, averaging 3 days for a response. Street repairs had 255 reports and also averaged a 3-day response time. On the faster side, dead animal pickups averaged a 14-hour response. Business license and permit concerns showed an unusually fast response time of just 3 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
