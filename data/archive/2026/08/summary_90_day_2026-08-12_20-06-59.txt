The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across several categories over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,581 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 9.5 days. Graffiti reports totaled 3,281 with an average response time of about 3 days. Tree maintenance saw 698 reports with an average response time of roughly 4 days.

Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging about 10.5 hours and 5 hours respectively over the last 90 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
