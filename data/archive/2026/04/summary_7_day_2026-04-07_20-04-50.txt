Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest independent look at city service response times over the past 7 days.

The highest volume of reports this week came from Dumped Items with 505 requests, followed by E-Scooter issues at 278, and Graffiti at 268.

Response times vary significantly by the type of issue. Dumped Items took an average of 3.3 days to resolve, and Abandoned Shopping Carts took just over 3 days. Graffiti reports were handled in roughly 42 hours.

Some services showed very fast turnaround times. Running Water issues averaged just 78 minutes, and Dead Animal Pickups averaged 2.8 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
