Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a summary of city performance and response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,955 reports. The city closed 5,833 of these cases, taking an average of 9 days to respond. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,115 calls, taking an average of 61 hours to address. E-Scooter complaints also saw high volume with 2,885 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Response times varied significantly depending on the type of service. Dead Animal Pickup requests, totaling 441 calls, were handled in just over 10 hours on average. Business License and Permit concerns were closed in a remarkably fast 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. 

On the slower end, Street Repair requests took an average of 6 days to resolve across 841 calls. Trash and Debris removal took over 8 days on average for 151 reports, and Park Maintenance requests averaged 6 days for 222 calls.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and track resolution progress. 

This data summary was updated at April 1, 2026, 1:02 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 1, 2026, 11:00 AM PDT.
