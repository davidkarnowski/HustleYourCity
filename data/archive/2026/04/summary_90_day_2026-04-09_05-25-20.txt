Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual breakdown of how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 6,921 reports. The average response time for these issues was 8.5 days.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,197 calls, averaging a response time of 56.4 hours.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 2,905. All of these cases were referred elsewhere or closed without a recorded response time.

Street Repair saw 735 reports with an average response time of 7.1 days, while Tree Maintenance had 903 reports averaging 4.7 days.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. It is important to note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Staying active and reporting problems helps maintain accountability and improves local neighborhoods.
