Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service call data.

Over the past 90 days, the most frequent service request was for "Dumped Items" with 6,149 reports, which took an average of about 15 days to resolve. The second most common request was for "Graffiti" with 3,739 reports, which had an average response time of about 6.5 days.

Some of the quickest responses were for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services", with average resolution times of about 16 hours and 15 hours, respectively. In contrast, "Park Maintenance" requests took an average of 18 days to close.
