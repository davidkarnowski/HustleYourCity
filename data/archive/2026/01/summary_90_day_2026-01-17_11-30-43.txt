Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 90 days.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped items (over 6,000 reports) and graffiti removal (over 3,400 reports). The average time to close a dumped item request was about 13 days, while graffiti reports were closed in about 4.5 days on average.

Response times for street and sidewalk repairs averaged about 6 days and 3.5 days, respectively. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups (about 16 hours) and animal services (about 14 hours). Some unusually fast response times, such as for damaged drinking fountains, may be due to administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

Requests for issues like misplaced e-scooters are typically referred directly to the operating companies for resolution and are not handled by city staff.
