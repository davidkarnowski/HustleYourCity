Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the last 90 days. Here is a factual look at what residents are reporting and how quickly the city is responding to these issues.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequent concerns. There were 6,835 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 9 days. Graffiti was reported 3,086 times, taking the city about 63 hours on average to address.

Other notable service areas include street repair, which saw 854 requests and averaged a 6-day response time. Dead animal pickups had 430 requests and were handled much faster, averaging about 11 hours. Business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can help by reporting it. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
