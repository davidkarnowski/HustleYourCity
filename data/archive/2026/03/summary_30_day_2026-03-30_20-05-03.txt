Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the city response times and call totals over the past 30 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 2,214 calls. The average response time for these requests is currently about 8 days. Graffiti is the second most common issue with 985 reports, taking an average of 1.6 days to address. E-Scooter complaints generated 886 calls, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable service requests include street repairs, which totaled 238 calls with an average response time of nearly 3 days. Dead animal pickups saw 159 requests and were handled in an average of 8.7 hours. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
