Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,500 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,109), e-scooters (942), and graffiti (894).

Based on the city's published data, the average time to close a service request varied by type. Reports for dumped items were closed in an average of about 7.6 days, while graffiti removal requests took about 3.1 days. Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which were handled in an average of 13.1 hours. Some unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
