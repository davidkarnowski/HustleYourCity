Graffiti reports remain the most common issue in the last 30 days with 1,427 calls. The city’s average response time is about 67 hours (≈2.8 days), down from 95 hours in the prior 60‑day window.

E‑Scooter reports continue to be handled quickly, with 775 of 776 calls closed as “referred” and no average response time recorded.

Street Repair saw 192 requests; the average response time dropped to 21 hours, a notable improvement over the 66‑hour average in the previous 60 days.

Tree Maintenance received 238 calls, half still in progress, and the average response time fell to just 10 hours, far faster than the 18‑hour average seen earlier.

Light outages generated 243 reports, with an average response of 39 hours, better than the 42‑hour average in the last 60 days.

Sidewalk Repair (98 calls) and Traffic Signal issues (40 calls) were addressed in roughly 13 hours and 18 hours respectively.

Overall, 2,036 Dumped Items were logged, with an average response of 176 hours (≈7.3 days), slightly slower than the 342‑hour average seen in the 90‑day period, indicating a need for faster clean‑up.
