Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items led the request board with 563 reports. E-scooter complaints followed with 220 reports, and graffiti removal saw 197 requests. 

The city averaged 62 hours to respond to dumped items. Graffiti reports were addressed in approximately 16 hours. Street repair requests averaged a 16-hour response time, while dead animal pickups were handled in about 3 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. 

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
