Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

The most reported issues were dumped items with 2,495 requests, followed by graffiti and e-scooter complaints, each with over 1,000 reports.

The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 10 days. For graffiti, the average was about 63 hours, and for street repairs, it was about 65 hours. Faster responses were seen for services like dead animal pickup, which took an average of about 11 hours to resolve. Nearly all e-scooter reports are referred directly to scooter companies, so the city does not track a response time for those issues.
