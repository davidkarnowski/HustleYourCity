Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data for the last 30 days. We are an independent accountability group and do not represent the City of Long Beach.

The most frequent request was for Dumped Items, with 2,079 total reports. The average time to resolve these issues is currently 5 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 1,073. The average response time for graffiti removal is approximately 46 hours.

There were 1,016 reports regarding E-Scooters, which are primarily referred to third-party operators for resolution.

For infrastructure, Street Repair received 269 reports with an average response time of 3 days. Tree Maintenance received 445 reports and averaged a 43-hour response time. Street light issues saw 336 reports and were addressed in about 58 hours.

Dead Animal Pickup remains one of the faster services, responding to 132 calls in approximately 14 hours.
