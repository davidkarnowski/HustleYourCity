In the past week Long Beach recorded 1,012 service requests.

Key highlights:
- Graffiti reports topped the list with 242 calls and an average response time of about 22 hours.
- Light issues were addressed quickly, averaging just under 10 hours for 63 requests.
- Street Repair showed the fastest turnaround, averaging 34 minutes (0.56 hours) across 30 jobs.
- E‑Scooter reports were all marked “Closed Referred” – 251 entries, reflecting administrative processing.
- Dumped Items had 302 calls with an average response of 22 hours.
- Tree Maintenance, Traffic Signal, and Sidewalk Repair also saw rapid responses, under 2 hours each.

Overall, response times are improving for many categories, though some (e.g., Graffiti, Dumped Items) still average around a day.

Residents are encouraged to report issues and track progress through the Go Long Beach service app.
