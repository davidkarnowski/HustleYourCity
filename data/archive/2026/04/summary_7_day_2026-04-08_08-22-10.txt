Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a factual summary of city service response times and request volumes over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 501 reports. The city took an average of over 3 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti reports followed closely with 284 requests and an average response time of 46.6 hours.

E-Scooter complaints accounted for 274 reports. All of these were marked as closed referred, which often indicates an administrative routing rather than direct field work.

Some services saw rapid turnaround times. Running Water reports averaged a response time of 78 minutes. Dead Animal Pickup averaged just over 2 hours for 38 requests. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

On the slower end, Abandoned Shopping Cart reports averaged over 3 days for a response, and a Damaged Drinking Fountain report took 4 days to process.
