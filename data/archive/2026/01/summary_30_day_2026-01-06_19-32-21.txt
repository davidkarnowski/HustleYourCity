Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service response data.

In the past month, residents submitted thousands of service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,909), e-scooters (993), and graffiti (913).

Average response times for key services varied. Reports for dumped items were closed in about 8 days, while trash and debris clean-up took over 9 days. Graffiti removal averaged about 2.3 days. Faster responses were seen for street repairs and tree maintenance, both averaging around 34 hours.

The 993 e-scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies, not handled by city staff. Some unusually fast response times in the data may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
