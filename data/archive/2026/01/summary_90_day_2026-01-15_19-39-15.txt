Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests were for Dumped Items (6,009 calls), Graffiti (3,449 calls), and E-Scooters (2,699 calls). The average response time to close a request for Dumped Items was about 13 days, while Graffiti removal took around 4.5 days. E-scooter reports are referred directly to vendors and do not have a city response time.

Some services saw much longer waits. The average time to close a Marina Repair request was about one month. In contrast, requests for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services were typically resolved in about 16 hours and 14.5 hours, respectively.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
