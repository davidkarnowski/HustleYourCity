The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 6,808 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 9 days. Graffiti reports totaled 3,233 with a much faster average response time of about 50 hours.

Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to see the fastest response times, averaging roughly 11 hours and 7 hours respectively over the same 90-day period. Tree maintenance requests totaled 741, with an average response time of about 6 days.

Please remember that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
