Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

The most common reports from residents were for illegally dumped items (2,063 requests), e-scooter issues (970), and graffiti removal (905). These three categories made up the majority of all service requests submitted.

Based on city data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 2 days. Requests for dumped items took longer, averaging around 4.5 days to be resolved. Street repair requests were closed in an average of 3.5 days. Some services, like dead animal pickup, had quicker average response times of about 12 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
