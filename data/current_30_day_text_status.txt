Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted nearly 4,900 service requests. The most frequently reported issues were illegally dumped items (1,878 reports), graffiti (1,106 reports), and e-scooter issues (813 reports).

The data shows faster responses for issues like dead animal pickups, with an average closure time of about 9 hours. Graffiti removal requests were resolved in about 2 days on average. Longer response times were recorded for reports of illegally dumped items, with an average closure time of about 8 days. Tree maintenance requests took an average of about 4 days to resolve.
