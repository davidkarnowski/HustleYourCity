In the last 90 days Long Beach residents filed 6,492 dumped‑item reports, 3,685 graffiti complaints, 550 light‑issue tickets, 813 tree‑maintenance requests, 349 sidewalk‑repair calls, 237 animal‑service reports and 466 dead‑animal pickups.

Average response times vary by service. Dumped items took about 15.6 days to close, graffiti about 5.6 days, sidewalk repairs 2.8 days, light issues 2.1 days and tree maintenance 2.3 days. Animal services and dead‑animal pickups were faster, averaging ≈ 18 hours each. Very low response times can occur when cases are closed administratively rather than through field work.

Overall, the city is handling thousands of requests each quarter, but some categories still see multi‑day waits. Residents can help speed up resolution by reporting issues directly through the Go Long Beach service app.
