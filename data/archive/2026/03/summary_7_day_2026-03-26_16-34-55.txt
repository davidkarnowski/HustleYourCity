Hustle Long Beach! is tracking the latest city service response times. Here is a look at the service call data from the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 512 reports. The average response time was 3.6 days, though 337 of these requests are still marked as new.

Graffiti removal saw 191 reports with an average response time of 26.4 hours. The city successfully closed 134 of these cases.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 213. All of these cases were referred and closed.

Dead Animal Pickup handled 49 calls with an average response time of 3.8 hours.

Restroom Maintenance recorded a single call that was closed in 35 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Abandoned Shopping Carts took an average of 3.3 days to address across 9 reports.
