Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted thousands of service requests, with the most frequent reports for illegally dumped items (2,056 calls), e-scooters (987 calls), and graffiti (891 calls). E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Average city response times for closed requests varied by service type. Reports for dumped items took about 7.5 days to resolve. Graffiti removal averaged just over 2 days, while street repairs took nearly 3 days. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 13 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app. Your reports provide the data needed to track city performance.
