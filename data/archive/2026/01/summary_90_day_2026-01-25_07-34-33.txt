Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests in the last three months. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,205 reports), graffiti (3,382 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,679 reports).

The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 13 days. Graffiti removal and street repair requests were both closed in an average of about 5.5 days.

Some services saw quicker responses. The average closure time for dead animal pickups was about 14 hours, and animal services calls were closed in about 13 hours. It should be noted that some very low response times may reflect administrative closures. Nearly all e-scooter reports were referred to private companies and not handled by the city.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at January 24, 2026 at 11:31 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 24, 2026 at 11:00 PM PST.
