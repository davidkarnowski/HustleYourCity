The latest data from the Long Beach service call system shows significant activity over the past 7 days. Residents submitted 463 reports regarding dumped items, making it the most frequent service request this week. Graffiti removal remains a high-volume area with 172 reports, while tree maintenance saw 76 requests.

Response times for completed tasks varied across categories. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of 4.19 hours. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
