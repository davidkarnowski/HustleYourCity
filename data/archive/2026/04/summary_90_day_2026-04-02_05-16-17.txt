Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 7,006 calls. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal saw 3,108 reports, with an average response time of 60 hours. Street repair requests totaled 832, taking an average of 6 days for a response.

There were also 2,854 reports regarding e-scooters, all of which were referred to outside agencies or departments.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 1, 2026 10:12 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 1, 2026 7:00 PM PDT.
