The latest data for Long Beach city services shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city received 6,844 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of approximately 8 days. Graffiti remains a high-volume concern with 3,199 reports and an average response time of about 56 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 945 reports in the last 90 days, averaging about 8 days for a response. Meanwhile, animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging 14 hours and 9 hours respectively.
