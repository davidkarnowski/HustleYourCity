Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week based on the latest public data.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,295 service requests. The most reported issues were illegally dumped items (511 calls), e-scooter problems (274 calls), and graffiti (235 calls). These three categories accounted for nearly 80% of all requests.

According to the city's data, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 21 hours. Reports of illegally dumped items were addressed in approximately 2 days on average, while dead animal pickups were handled in about 13 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
