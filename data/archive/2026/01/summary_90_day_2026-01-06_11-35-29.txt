Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the past 90 days.

Reports for illegally dumped items were the most frequent request with over 5,800 submissions, followed by graffiti removal with over 3,500 reports. The average response time to close a report for dumped items was about 14 days, while graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 4.75 days on average.

Other common requests included street repair, which took an average of 9 days to close, and tree maintenance, which averaged about 6.5 days. Animal-related calls were handled much faster, with average response times of about 16 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, from potholes to graffiti, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
