Here is an independent evaluation of Long Beach city service requests from the past 30 days. This summary reviews call totals and response times to keep the public informed on service performance.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 2,381 reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 1 week and 1 day. Graffiti reports totaled 1,142, with an average completion time of just over 2 days.

Street Repair requests showed an average response time of 3 days across 231 reports. Dead Animal Pickup services averaged 8.8 hours per request.

Some categories show very rapid closure times. Reports regarding Business Licenses and Permit Concerns averaged 31 minutes to close. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than physical work completion.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues using the Go Long Beach service application.
