Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data to keep you informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed. Here is a look at the service request numbers and response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 2,225 reports. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 1,053 reports, which were handled in an average of 37 hours.

Other notable service categories include tree maintenance, which saw 497 requests and an average response time of 3 days. Street repair had 258 reports, also averaging a 3-day response time. Dead animal pickups were resolved much faster, averaging under 15 hours across 148 reports.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps direct city resources to where they are needed most.
