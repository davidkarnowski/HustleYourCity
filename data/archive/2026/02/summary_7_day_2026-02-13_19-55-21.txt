Hustle Long Beach! Community Update:

Here is the latest evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests for the past 7 days.

The highest volume of service requests was for Dumped Items, with 486 total reports and an average response time of approximately 36 hours. Graffiti removal followed with 251 reports and an average response time of 41 hours.

Street Repair generated 43 reports with a response average of 26 hours. Dead Animal Pickup services responded to 28 reports with an average time of about 3.5 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times listed in the data may be the result of administrative closures rather than the physical completion of work.

Residents are encouraged to report issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
