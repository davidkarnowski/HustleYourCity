Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

The most reported issue over the last 90 days was Dumped Items, with 6,925 requests and an average response time of about 8.4 days. Graffiti followed with 3,251 reports, taking an average of 54.7 hours to address. E-Scooter complaints also saw high volume with 2,919 reports.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests are taking longer to resolve. Street Repair requests averaged 7 days for a response across 722 reports, while Sidewalk Repair took about 5.3 days for 358 reports. Tree Maintenance averaged 7.6 days for 900 requests.
