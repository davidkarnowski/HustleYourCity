Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,400 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,013), e-scooters (988), and graffiti (903).

The average time to resolve a request for dumped items was about 4.3 days. Graffiti removal took an average of about 2.2 days. For street repairs, the average completion time was about 3.2 days. Response times for dead animal pickups averaged about 12.5 hours.

Some service types with very low request numbers may show unusually fast response times, which can be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.
