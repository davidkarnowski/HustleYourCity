Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past month, the most frequent reports from the public were for illegally dumped items, with 1,910 requests. This was followed by 970 reports for e-scooter issues and 967 for graffiti removal.

Average city response times for closed cases varied by issue. Graffiti removal requests were closed in an average of about 2.3 days. Requests for dumped item cleanup took an average of about 8.6 days to resolve. Animal Services calls were among the fastest, with an average response time of about 11.7 hours.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
