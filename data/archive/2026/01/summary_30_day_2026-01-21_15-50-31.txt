Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests. In the past month, residents made over 5,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach app.

The most frequent request was for "Dumped Items" with 2,110 reports. The average response time for these requests was about 7.5 days. "Graffiti" was another common issue with 894 reports, which were addressed in just over 3 days on average.
