Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times for the last 90 days.

Dumped Items remain the most frequent issue reported, with 6,424 total reports. The average time to resolve these requests is currently approximately 13 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,072, with an average completion time of about 4 days. Street Repair requests, which include potholes, showed 793 reports with a response time of roughly 4 and a half days.

Street Light issues saw 1,004 reports and are being addressed in approximately 3 and a half days. Dead Animal Pickup remains responsive, averaging roughly 12 hours per request.

We encourage all residents to continue reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
