The latest data for city service requests in Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city received 6,771 reports regarding dumped items and 3,218 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. For the last 90 days, the average response time for graffiti removal was approximately 50 hours. Dumped item reports saw an average response time of about 9 days. Dead animal pickup remains one of the faster services, with an average response time of approximately 7 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times for some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
