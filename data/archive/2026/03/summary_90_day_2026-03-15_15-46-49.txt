Hustle Long Beach! is here with your latest 90-day accountability update on city service response times. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood reports.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue by far. Over the last 90 days, the city received 6,633 requests for dumped item removal, taking an average of 10 days to respond. Graffiti was the second most frequent concern with 3,074 reports, which the city addressed in an average of 3 days. Street repairs and tree maintenance also saw high volumes, with average response times of about 6 days for each.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast processing. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work being completed. Meanwhile, dead animal pickups saw 446 reports and maintained a steady 13-hour average response time.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it. Hustle Long Beach! encourages all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to submit issues directly to the city and hold them accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 15, 2026, 8:43 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 15, 2026, 8:00 AM PDT.
