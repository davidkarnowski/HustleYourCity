Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest accountability update on city service response times based on data from the past 7 days.

The highest volume of service requests this past week included 500 reports for Dumped Items, 284 for Graffiti, and 274 for E-Scooter issues.

Looking at response times, Dumped Items took an average of 3.2 days to address. Abandoned Shopping Carts took about 3.3 days, and Damaged Drinking Fountains averaged 4 days. 

On the faster end, Running Water issues were closed in just 78 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickup requests were handled in about 2 hours, and Graffiti reports averaged 46.8 hours.

Help keep the city accountable and our neighborhoods maintained. Please encourage your friends and neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
