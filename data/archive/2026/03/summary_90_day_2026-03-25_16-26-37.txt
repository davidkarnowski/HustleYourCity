Hustle Long Beach! is sharing the latest 90-day accountability check on Long Beach city service response times. Here is a look at how the city is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest call volume with 6,826 reports over the last 90 days. The city took an average of 9.4 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 3,086 reports, averaging a response time of nearly 3 days.

Street Repair and Tree Maintenance saw 856 and 957 calls respectively, with both services averaging response times of about six and a half days.

Some categories show incredibly fast turnarounds. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or immediate referrals rather than actual work completions.
