Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past month, the most frequent reports were for "Dumped Items" with 1,896 requests, which took an average of about 7 days to resolve. "Graffiti" was the next most common, with 913 reports closed in an average of 2.5 days. The 930 "E-Scooter" reports were referred directly to the scooter companies.

Response times varied by service type. Requests for "Trash & Debris" took an average of nearly 10 days to close. In contrast, more urgent requests like "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were typically handled in about 14 hours.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
