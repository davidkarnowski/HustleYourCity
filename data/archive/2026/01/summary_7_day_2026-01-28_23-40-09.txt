Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past week.

Dumped items remained the most reported issue with 650 service calls. Graffiti was the next most frequent report with 283 requests, followed by 222 for e-scooter issues.

The average city response time to close a request for dumped items was about 3.2 days. Graffiti reports were closed in an average of 32 hours. Street light repair requests took an average of 3.6 days. Some requests may be closed for administrative reasons, which can affect these average response times.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application to report it.
