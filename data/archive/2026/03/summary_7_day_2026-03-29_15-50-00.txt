Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest independent review of City of Long Beach service call data for the past 7 days.

Dumped Items and Graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues. There were 522 Dumped Items reported this week with an average response time of 67.8 hours. Graffiti followed with 240 reports and an average response time of 14.7 hours. E-Scooter complaints generated 205 calls, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Some service categories recorded extremely fast turnaround times. Restroom Maintenance requests averaged a 50-minute response, and reports of Shoes on a Utility Wire averaged 44 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times under 90 minutes may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which received 66 reports with an average response time of 14.3 hours, and Tree Maintenance, which saw 67 reports averaging an 11.1-hour response.

To help maintain neighborhood safety and accountability, residents are encouraged to report local issues directly through the Go Long Beach service application.
