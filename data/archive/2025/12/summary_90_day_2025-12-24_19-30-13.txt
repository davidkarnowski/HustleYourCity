Here is a summary of Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Based on the city's data, "Dumped Items" was the most frequent issue, with 6,058 reports filed. This was followed by "Graffiti" with 3,705 reports and "E-Scooter" issues with 2,356 reports.

The average response time for "Dumped Items" reports was approximately 15 days. Park maintenance requests took an average of over 17 days to resolve.

Other services were handled more quickly. The average for "Dead Animal Pickup" was about 16 hours, while graffiti removal took an average of about 5 and a half days.
