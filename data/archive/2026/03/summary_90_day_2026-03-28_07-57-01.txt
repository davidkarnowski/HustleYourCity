Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data from the past 90 days to keep the public informed on local government response times.

Dumped Items generated the highest call volume with 6,921 reports. The city took an average of 9.4 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,115 calls, averaging a response time of 64.8 hours. E-Scooter complaints also saw high volume with 2,833 reports, though the city did not track response times for this category.

Response times varied significantly across other services. Dead Animal Pickup handled 441 reports with an average response of 11.5 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns showed a remarkably fast response time of just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. On the slower side, Street Repair took an average of 6.4 days for 864 reports, and Tree Maintenance averaged 5.6 days for 945 requests.

Residents play a vital role in identifying community needs. Followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 28, 2026 12:52 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 28, 2026 12:00 AM PDT.
