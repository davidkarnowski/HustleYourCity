Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service call data for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,384 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items with 562 calls, followed by E-Scooter issues with 248 calls and Graffiti with 246 calls.

The average response time to close a request for Dumped Items was about 54 hours, or just over two days. Graffiti removal requests were closed in about 20 hours on average. Some categories show very fast response times, which may reflect administrative closures and not the final completion of physical work.
