Long Beach Service Request Data Update: Last 30 Days

The latest data regarding service requests in Long Beach shows significant activity in waste management and graffiti removal over the past month.

Dumped Items remained the most frequent report type with 2,226 total calls. The average response time for these requests was approximately 1 week.

Graffiti removal received 1,118 reports. The city closed 1,001 of these cases with an average response time of roughly 2 days.

Infrastructure requests also saw steady volume. There were 362 reports regarding street lights, which were addressed in about 2 days on average. Street repair requests totaled 239, with an average completion time of 3 days. Tree maintenance showed a faster turnaround, averaging 28 hours across 365 reports.

Dead animal pickup services responded to 151 reports with an average time of nearly 14 hours.

Residents are encouraged to report maintenance issues, dumped items, and graffiti using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of these issues.
