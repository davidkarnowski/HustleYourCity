Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from the public were for dumped items, with 6,174 reports, and graffiti, with 3,736 reports. The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 15 days, while graffiti removal requests were closed in about 6.5 days on average.

Response times for infrastructure have varied. The average for street repair requests was about 9.5 days, while reports for lights were closed in about 7.5 days. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of about 5.5 days.

Some of the quickest responses were for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup, with both averaging under 24 hours to close a request. Note that unusually fast response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures.

If you see an issue in the city, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
