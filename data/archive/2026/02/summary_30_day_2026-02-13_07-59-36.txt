New data regarding City of Long Beach service requests highlights performance metrics for the last 30 days.

The highest volume of reports continues to be for Dumped Items, with 2,490 total requests recorded. The average response time to resolve these issues is currently 7 days.

Graffiti removal received 1,117 reports, showing an average completion time of 2 days. Street Repair requests totaled 235, with crews responding in an average of 3 days.

Dead Animal Pickup services handled 150 requests with a faster average response time of approximately 9 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
