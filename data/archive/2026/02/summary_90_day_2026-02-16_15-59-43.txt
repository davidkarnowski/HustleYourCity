An evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests from the last 90 days highlights volume and response times for key community issues.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,447 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 13.6 days. Graffiti reports totaled 3,179, with an average resolution time of just over 4 days.

Infrastructure requests for Street Repair show an average completion time of roughly 5 days across 774 reports. Tree Maintenance requests are taking approximately one week to resolve. Dead Animal Pickup remains responsive, averaging 11 hours to close out 411 reports.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues.
