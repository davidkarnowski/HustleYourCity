Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs. Here is a look at the numbers.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of reports with 6,820 requests. The city took an average of 9.5 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,088 calls, which took an average of 67 hours to address.

Street repairs accounted for 853 requests, taking an average of 6.5 days to resolve. Tree maintenance saw 958 requests with an average response time of 6.3 days.

Some categories showed very fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. However, citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead animal pickups were consistently addressed in about 11.5 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Your reports help track neighborhood needs and hold local agencies accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 24, 2026 10:04 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 24, 2026 8:00 PM PDT.
