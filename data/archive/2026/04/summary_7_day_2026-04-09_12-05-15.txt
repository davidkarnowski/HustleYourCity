Here is the latest city service data evaluation for the Hustle Long Beach! community, focusing on service requests from the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 461 reports. The city averaged a response time of 3.3 days for these issues. Graffiti removal saw 284 reports, with an average response time of 45.5 hours.

E-Scooter complaints accounted for 268 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Some services saw very rapid closures. Running Water reports averaged a response time of just 78 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

On the longer end, Damaged Drinking Fountain reports took about 4 days to resolve.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and keep the community informed.

This data summary was updated at April 9, 2026 5:02 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 9, 2026 3:00 AM PDT.
