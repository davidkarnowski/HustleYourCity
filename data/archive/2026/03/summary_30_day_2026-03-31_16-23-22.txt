Hustle Long Beach! is sharing the latest 30-day city service response data to keep citizens informed about local government performance. 

Over the past month, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 2,248 reports. The city took an average of 8 days to resolve these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 967 calls, averaging a 38-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 911 reports, all of which were referred to outside vendors.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which saw 234 requests and an average response time of 71 hours. Park Maintenance handled 84 requests, taking nearly 5 days on average to close them. On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup managed 161 calls with an average turnaround of 8 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 31, 2026 09:15 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 31, 2026 07:00 AM PDT.
