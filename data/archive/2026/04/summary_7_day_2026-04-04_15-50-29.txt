Hustle Long Beach! brings you the latest independent look at city service response times. Over the past 7 days, citizens submitted hundreds of requests, and here is the breakdown of how the City of Long Beach handled them.

Dumped Items generated the most volume with 582 reports. The city averaged a 51-hour response time for these issues, closing 98 cases while 302 remain in progress.

Graffiti was the second most common actionable issue. The city received 221 reports and averaged a 19-hour response time, successfully closing 100 of those cases.

Dead Animal Pickup saw 39 reports, all of which were closed with an average response time of 2.5 hours. Please note that unusually low response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

E-Scooter complaints remained high with 263 reports, though all of these were referred to outside agencies rather than handled directly by city crews.

Street Repair and Sidewalk Repair saw 40 and 51 reports respectively, with response times averaging 26 hours for streets and 18 hours for sidewalks.
