Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times over the last 90 days based on the latest public data.

Dumped items and graffiti were the most reported issues, with a combined total of nearly 10,000 service calls. The average city response time to close a dumped item request was about two weeks. Graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of 5.5 days.

Faster responses were recorded for animal-related services. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 16 hours, while other animal service calls were closed in about 15 hours on average.
