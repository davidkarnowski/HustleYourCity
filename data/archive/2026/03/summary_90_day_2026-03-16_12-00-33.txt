Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 6,634 reports. The average response time for these issues was 10 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,069 calls, taking an average of 72 hours to address. E-Scooter complaints totaled 2,855, though all of these were referred to outside agencies.

Some services showed very fast turnaround times. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes, though unusually low times like this may indicate administrative closures rather than completed field work. Dead Animal Pickup requests were handled in an average of 12.5 hours. On the slower end, Trash and Debris complaints took over 8 days to resolve, and Street Repair requests averaged over 6 days.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application to help keep the city accountable.

This data summary was updated at 4:56 AM PDT on March 16, 2026 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 4:00 AM PDT on March 16, 2026.
