Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest independent look at city service response times over the past 30 days. This summary tracks how quickly local issues are being addressed based on public data.

Dumped Items received the highest call volume with 2,342 reports. The average response time for these requests was just over 8 days.

Graffiti removal saw 946 reports with an average response time of 35 hours. E-Scooter complaints totaled 882, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Street Repair handled 224 reports, taking an average of 3.4 days to respond. Park Maintenance took an average of 4 days to address its 83 reports.

On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup managed 166 calls with an average response time of 5 hours. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 3, 2026, 1:08 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 2, 2026, 11:00 PM PDT.
