Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the city's response times and call totals over the last 90 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of reports with 6658 calls. The city took an average of about 10 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 3097 reports, averaging a response time of nearly 3 days.

Street repair and tree maintenance received 829 and 911 calls respectively, with both services averaging a response time of about 6 to 7 days.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues to the city.
