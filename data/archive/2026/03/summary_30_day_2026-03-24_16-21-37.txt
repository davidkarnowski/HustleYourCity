Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 2,268 reports, averaging a response time of 5.8 days. Graffiti followed with 1,026 calls and an average response time of 36.9 hours. E-Scooter complaints totaled 942, all of which were referred to outside agencies or closed without a recorded response time.

Some services took longer to address. Marina Repair requests averaged 7.4 days, Illegal Firework reports took 5.9 days, and Park Maintenance averaged 4.6 days. On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 8.9 hours. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 24, 2026 9:17 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 24, 2026 8:00 AM PDT.
