Here's the latest look at Long Beach service request data for the past 90 days.

The most common reports from residents were for Dumped Items (6,045 requests) and Graffiti (3,406 requests). Street repair and lighting issues were also frequently reported, with over 700 requests each.

Average city response times for top issues were about 13 days for Dumped Items and just under 5 days for Graffiti. Street repair requests averaged a response time of about 6 days. Some issues, like Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup, were handled much faster, with average response times under 16 hours. It is important to note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures and not work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
