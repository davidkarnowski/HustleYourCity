Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service response data for the past 30 days.

Over the last month, the most common service requests were for dumped items (2,030 calls), e-scooters (982 calls), and graffiti removal (888 calls). E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.
