Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the last 90 days.

The most frequent issues reported by residents were for Dumped Items, with 6,052 requests, and Graffiti, with 3,390 requests. These two categories made up the majority of service calls.

Average response times for closed requests varied. Reports for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services were typically handled in under 1 day. The average time to resolve a Graffiti report was about 5 days. For Dumped Items, the average response time was approximately 13 days. Marina Repair requests had the longest average completion time at over 3 weeks.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
