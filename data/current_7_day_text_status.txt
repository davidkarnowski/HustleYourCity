Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,200 requests were submitted through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (430 requests), E-Scooters (255 requests), and Graffiti (249 requests).

For requests that were closed, the data shows average response times for several key services. The average time to close a Graffiti report was about 26 hours. For Dumped Items, the average was about 1.5 days. Street Repair requests were closed in about 14 hours on average. Some categories, like Sidewalk Repair (3 hours), show very fast response times, which may be due to administrative closures and not work completion.
