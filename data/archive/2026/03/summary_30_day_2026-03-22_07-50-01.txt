Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Here is a breakdown of city response times and call volumes over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service requests with 2,225 reports. The city's average response time for these issues was 5.6 days.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service, totaling 1,000 calls. The city responded to these reports in an average of 1.6 days.

When reviewing this data, please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative ticket closures rather than the actual completion of physical work.
