In the past 90 days Long Beach residents filed about 16,000 service requests.

Key response‑time highlights:
- Light repairs were closed in an average of 38 hours (about 1.6 days), a big improvement from the all‑time average of 1,016 hours.
- Sidewalk repairs now average 46 hours (≈1.9 days), down from the historic 4,708 hours.
- Graffiti removal averages 142 hours (≈5.9 days), better than the all‑time 350 hours.
- Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup are the fastest, with averages of 17 hours and 16 hours respectively (under 1 day).
- Dumped Items still take longer, averaging 361 hours (≈15 days), up from the all‑time 260 hours.

Overall, many service categories are seeing faster turn‑around times, though some (like Dumped Items) remain slower and could benefit from more attention.
