Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data, focusing on requests made in the last 90 days.

Illegal dumping was the most reported issue with 6,165 service calls. The average response time to close these reports is about 13 days. Graffiti removal was the second most common report with 3,338 calls, which were addressed in about 5 days on average.

The city's quickest responses were for animal-related issues. The 433 calls for dead animal pickup were closed in an average of about 14 hours, while the 175 calls for Animal Services were closed in about 12.5 hours.

Longer response times were recorded for Park Maintenance, which took an average of 10 days to resolve, and Marina Repair, which averaged about 3.5 weeks.
