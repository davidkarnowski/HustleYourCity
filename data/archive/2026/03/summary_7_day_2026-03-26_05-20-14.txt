Here is the latest City of Long Beach service call data summary from Hustle Long Beach! As an independent accountability project, we are tracking the city's response times over the past 7 days to keep the public informed on how local issues are being handled.

Dumped Items led the request volume with 527 calls over the past week. The city's average response time for these requests was about 3.8 days. E-Scooter complaints followed with 235 calls, all of which were referred to other agencies or departments. Graffiti removal saw 197 requests, with an average response time of about 1 day.

Other notable response times include Abandoned Shopping Cart reports, which took the city an average of 3.3 days to address across 8 calls. Dead Animal Pickup was handled efficiently, averaging 3.8 hours across 51 calls.

On the faster side, Restroom Maintenance averaged 34 minutes for a single call, and Community Center issues averaged 71 minutes across 3 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
