Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 30 days.

In the past month, residents submitted nearly 5,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (1,825 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (1,005 requests), and graffiti (887 requests).

According to the city's data, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 54 hours. Requests for street repair were addressed in about 37 hours on average. Slower responses included clean-up for illegally dumped items, which took an average of about 8 days, and trash and debris clean-up, which averaged about 9 days.

Note that some response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
