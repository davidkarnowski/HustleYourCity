Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on the latest data for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted more than 1,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (544 requests), graffiti (228 requests), and e-scooters (218 requests).

The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 3.4 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 34 hours, while street light repair requests took an average of nearly 3 days to resolve. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickup, which averaged under 6 hours.
