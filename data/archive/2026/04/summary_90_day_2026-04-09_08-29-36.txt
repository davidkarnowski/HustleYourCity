Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data to keep you informed about response times over the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 6,922 reports, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti removal followed with 3,194 reports, averaging a 56-hour response time.

For infrastructure and maintenance, Street Repair saw 735 calls with an average response time of 7.1 days. Tree Maintenance handled 905 calls, taking about 4.7 days on average.

You might notice some categories show incredibly fast turnarounds. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Accountability starts with visibility. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
