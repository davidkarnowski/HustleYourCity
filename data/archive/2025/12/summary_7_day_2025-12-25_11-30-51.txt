Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (378 calls), E-Scooter issues (224 calls), and Graffiti (207 calls). The average time to close a graffiti report was about 1.2 days, while dumped item reports took about 1.5 days. Reports for street repairs were closed in an average of 1.7 days.

Some service requests were closed very quickly. The average response for Park Maintenance was 59 minutes and Restroom Maintenance was 52 minutes. These fast closures may be due to administrative updates and not always on-site work completions.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to notify the city is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
