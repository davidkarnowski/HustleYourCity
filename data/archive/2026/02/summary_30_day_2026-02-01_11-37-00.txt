Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past month, residents submitted more than 5,800 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (2,438 requests), e-scooters (1,043), and graffiti (1,020).

Based on closed cases, the average response time for dumped item removal was about 9 days. Graffiti reports were closed in an average of 2.5 days. For street repairs, the average completion time was just under 3 days. Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 11 hours on average.
