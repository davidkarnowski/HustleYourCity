Hello Hustle Long Beach community. Here is your latest independent accountability update evaluating City of Long Beach service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items led the service requests this week with 461 reports. The average response time for these issues was 3.1 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 291 calls and an average response time of 40 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 271 reports, all of which were referred and closed.

Response times varied significantly across other city services. Street Repair issues averaged a 15.5-hour response time across 34 reports. Running Water reports showed a very fast average response time of just 33 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. On the longer end, Damaged Drinking Fountain reports averaged a 4-day response time.

Help keep the city accountable and our neighborhoods maintained. Encourage your friends and neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
