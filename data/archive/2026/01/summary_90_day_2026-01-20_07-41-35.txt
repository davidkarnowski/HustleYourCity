Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the last 90 days.

Illegal dumping was the most reported issue with 6,025 service calls, followed by graffiti with 3,391 reports. The average time to close a report for dumped items was about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests took an average of about 5 days to complete.

Faster responses were seen for more urgent issues. The average response time for dead animal pickups was about 15 hours, and Animal Services calls were closed in about 14 hours. Some service types show unusually fast response times, which may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
