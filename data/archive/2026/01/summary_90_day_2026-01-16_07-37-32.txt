Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 90 days. Dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues, with residents submitting a combined total of over 9,400 requests.

According to the city's data, the average time to close a dumped item request was about 13 days. Graffiti removal averaged about 4.5 days. The longest waits were for marina repairs, which took an average of nearly 25 days to resolve.

Faster responses were seen for services like dead animal pickup, which were typically handled in about 16 hours. The nearly 2,700 e-scooter complaints were referred directly to private companies, so the city does not track a response time for those issues.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
