Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the last month, residents submitted nearly 6,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,404), graffiti (988), and e-scooter issues (964).

Average city response times varied by issue. Reports for dumped items were closed in about 8 days. Graffiti removal took an average of 59 hours, while street repairs averaged 69 hours. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 12 hours. Note that some fast response times can be due to administrative closures and may not reflect on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
