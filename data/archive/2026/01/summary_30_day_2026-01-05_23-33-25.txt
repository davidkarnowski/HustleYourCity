Here is the latest look at Long Beach service request data from the past 30 days. Residents submitted over 4,900 requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (1,861), illegally parked e-scooters (1,016), and graffiti (906).

Response times for some services remain long. The average time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 8 days. "Trash & Debris" removal requests took over 9 days on average. In contrast, reports for "Dead Animal Pickup" were addressed in about 15 hours, and "Tree Maintenance" requests were closed in about 28 hours on average.

Requests for e-scooters are referred directly to scooter companies, so the city does not report a response time. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than work completion. See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it using the Go Long Beach app.
