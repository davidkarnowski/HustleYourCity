In the last 90 days Long Beach received 23,300 service requests across all categories.

Fastest average response times were for Animal Services (18.3 hours) and Dead Animal Pickup (17 hours), meaning most cases were closed within a day.

Tree Maintenance and Light issues were resolved in about 2.5 days (61 hours) and 2.3 days (56 hours) on average.

Sidewalk Repair took roughly 3.5 days (83 hours) and Street Repair about 5 days (118 hours).

The longest waits were for Dumped Items, averaging 354 hours (≈15 days), and Graffiti, averaging 140 hours (≈6 days).

Overall, 4,443 requests were closed in the “Closed Referred” status, showing many issues are being addressed promptly.
