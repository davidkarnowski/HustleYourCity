Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,400 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (606 requests), graffiti (265), and e-scooters (213).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 19 hours. Reports of dumped items took an average of about 2.5 days to close. Animal Services calls were addressed quickly, with an average response time of about 3.3 hours. Vegetation maintenance requests were closed in an average of 1.8 hours, though this fast time may reflect administrative closures.
