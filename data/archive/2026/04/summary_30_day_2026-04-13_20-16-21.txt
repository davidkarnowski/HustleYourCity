Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at the numbers and response times to keep the community informed.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues. In the last 30 days, there were 2283 reports for dumped items with an average response time of 8.5 days. Graffiti saw 1091 reports, averaging a 37-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 989 reports.

Other notable service requests include street repair with 206 reports taking an average of 69 hours, and tree maintenance with 246 reports taking an average of 63 hours. Dead animal pickups and animal services saw faster turnarounds, averaging 7 hours for 164 reports and 8.5 hours for 79 reports, respectively. 

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep the city running smoothly. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
