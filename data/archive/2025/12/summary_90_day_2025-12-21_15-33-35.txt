Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update for the last 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests during this period. The most frequent reports were for dumped items, with 6,140 requests, and graffiti, with 3,715 requests.

Average response times for closed requests varied by service type. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 6 days, while dumped item reports took about 2 weeks. Faster response times were recorded for other services, such as dead animal pickups, which averaged about 17 hours, and sidewalk repairs, which averaged nearly 4 days. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach application.

This data summary was updated at December 21, 2025 at 7:30 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 21, 2025 at 7:00 AM PST.
