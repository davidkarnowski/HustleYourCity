Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. This information helps residents track how quickly the City of Long Beach handles public service requests.

Over the last 30 days, Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2332 requests. The average response time for these items was nearly 6 days. Graffiti removal saw 1031 requests, with an average response time of about 36 hours. E-Scooter complaints were also high, totaling 909 reports, all of which were referred to other entities to close.

Some services took longer to address. Marina Repair requests averaged 1 week for a response, while Park Maintenance took about 4 days. Sidewalk Repair requests averaged 3 days.

Other services saw much faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup averaged a 9-hour response time, and Running Water concerns were handled in about 11 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 25, 2026, 9:22 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 25, 2026, 8:00 AM PDT.
