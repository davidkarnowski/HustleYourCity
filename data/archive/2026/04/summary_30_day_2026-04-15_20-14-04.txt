Hustle Long Beach! is back with the latest 30-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests. Here is a look at the numbers and response times for the past month.

Dumped Items led the city's service requests with 2,351 reports. On average, these took 8.7 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,079 calls, averaging a 47-hour response time. E-Scooter issues also saw high volume with 1,004 reports, though almost all were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable areas included Street Repair, which handled 218 calls with an average response time of 69 hours, and Light issues, which saw 234 reports taking an average of 4 days to address. Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services saw faster turnarounds, averaging 7 hours and 9 hours respectively. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work.
