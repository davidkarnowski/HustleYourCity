Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest 30-day city service request data to keep the community informed on response times and call volumes.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 2,214 reports, averaging a response time of 5.7 days. Graffiti followed closely with 1,007 reports, which the city responded to in about 37 hours on average. E-Scooter complaints also saw high volume with 939 reports, though all were immediately referred to outside agencies.

Some services experienced longer wait times. Marina Repair requests averaged 7.4 days, and Illegal Firework reports took about 5.9 days. Conversely, Dead Animal Pickup was handled much faster, averaging a 9.2-hour response time for 146 calls. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times across the data may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city tracks and addresses these concerns.

This data summary was updated at March 23, 2026, 12:55 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 23, 2026, 12:00 PM PDT.
