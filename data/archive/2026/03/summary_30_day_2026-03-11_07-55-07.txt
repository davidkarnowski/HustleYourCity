Hustle Long Beach! is providing the latest 30-day accountability update on city service response times. Reviewing this data helps residents understand how quickly local issues are being addressed across the community.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 2,118 reports. The city took an average of 4.7 days to respond to these cases. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,093 calls, averaging a 43.7-hour response time.

Other notable service times include Street Repair, which handled 269 reports with an average response time of 3.1 days, and Tree Maintenance, which saw 477 reports and a 48.3-hour average response.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnarounds. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a 12-minute response time. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues. Submitting reports ensures the city has the data it needs to dispatch services and allows the public to track accountability.
