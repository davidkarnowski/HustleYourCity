Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past week.

Long Beach residents submitted over 1,200 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (461 calls), improperly parked e-scooters (225 calls), and graffiti (211 calls).

According to the data, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 1 day and 8 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 2 days on average. Slower responses included requests for Trash and Debris collection, which took an average of about 4.6 days to close.

Some requests, like Restroom Maintenance, show a very fast average closure time of about 83 minutes. These unusually low times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
