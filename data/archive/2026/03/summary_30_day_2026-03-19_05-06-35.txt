Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items led the service requests with 2,238 calls. The average response time for these reports was 5.1 days. Graffiti removal saw 1,054 requests, taking an average of 37 hours to address. Street Repair had 256 requests with an average response time of 3.3 days.

Reported Business Licenses and Permit Concerns showed an unusually fast response time of just 3 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. On the other end, Illegal Fireworks reports took the longest to resolve, averaging 9 days for the 10 requests submitted.
