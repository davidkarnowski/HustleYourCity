Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data from the last 30 days to keep you informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped items were the most frequently reported issue, with 2096 calls. The city took an average of 4.6 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most common issue, with 1077 reports and an average response time of 43 hours. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 950 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Some services experienced longer delays. Marina repair requests took an average of 7.2 days to address, and general trash and debris reports took about 4.5 days.

On the other end of the spectrum, business license and permit concerns showed a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually fast response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep the city accountable and local neighborhoods clean. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
