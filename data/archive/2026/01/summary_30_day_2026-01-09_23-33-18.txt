Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

The most frequent service requests were for Dumped Items, with 1,935 reports. The average response time for these requests was about 6.5 days. The next most common issue was Graffiti, with 956 reports handled in an average of about 2.3 days.
