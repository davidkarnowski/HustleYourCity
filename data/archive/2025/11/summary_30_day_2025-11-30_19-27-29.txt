In the past 30 days Long Beach residents filed 1,325 graffiti reports, 1,815 dumped‑item calls, 204 tree‑maintenance requests and 256 street‑light issues. Graffiti and dumped items made up the bulk of service calls, together accounting for more than half of all requests.

Average city response times varied by service type. Graffiti tickets were closed in about 73 hours (≈ 3 days), while dumped‑item requests took roughly 205 hours (≈ 8.5 days). Tree‑maintenance jobs were addressed much faster, averaging 10 hours. Street‑light and traffic‑signal issues were typically resolved within 1‑2 days (38 hours and 24 hours respectively). Sidewalk‑repair and street‑repair requests were often completed in under a day (≈ 17 hours and 21 hours).
