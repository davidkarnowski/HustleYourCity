The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across core maintenance categories over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,357 total reports, showing an average response time of approximately 19 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,574 reports and an average response time of 2.5 days. Tree maintenance saw 1,046 reports with an average response time of 8 days.

Response times for dead animal pickup remain efficient, averaging about 7 hours over the last 90 days. Light maintenance reports totaled 647, with an average response time of 46 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach mobile application to report issues and track service requests in their neighborhoods.
