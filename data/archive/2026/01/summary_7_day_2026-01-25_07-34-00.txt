Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted nearly 1,400 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (590 requests), Graffiti (247 requests), and E-Scooter issues (215 requests).
