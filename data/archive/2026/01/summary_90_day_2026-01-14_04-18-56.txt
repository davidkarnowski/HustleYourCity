Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, the most common service requests from residents were for dumped items (nearly 6,000 reports), graffiti (about 3,500 reports), and e-scooter issues (about 2,700 reports).

The average response time for dumped item pickups was about 13 days, while graffiti removal took an average of about 4.5 days. More urgent requests like dead animal pickups and other animal services were handled much faster, typically in under 16 hours. Longer-term projects show longer response times, with marina repairs averaging about one month to close out.

Some service types, like reports for damaged drinking fountains, show unusually fast closure times of about 2 hours. These may reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical repairs.
