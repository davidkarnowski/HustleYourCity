Over the past 90 days Long Beach logged 6,521 dumped‑item reports (average response ≈ 357 hours, about 15 days) and 3,540 graffiti reports (average response ≈ 163 hours, about 7 days). Park maintenance had 202 requests with an average response ≈ 161 hours (≈ 6.7 days).  

The quickest turn‑around was for shoes on utility wires at ≈ 33 hours (about 1.4 days). Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were also relatively fast, averaging ≈ 17 hours and ≈ 16 hours respectively. Most other categories—light, tree maintenance, street repair, traffic signals and trash & debris—showed average response times between 2 and 6 days.
