Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest 24-hour accountability update on city service requests.

Over the past day, Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 95 reports, though 85 of these remain in the new status. For the items that were addressed, the average response time was 1.6 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed with 43 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti reports totaled 27, with an average response time of 6.4 hours.

The data shows some unusually fast response times in infrastructure categories. Street Repair averaged a 21-minute response time across 9 reports, and Sidewalk Repair averaged a 27-minute response time across 2 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

On the higher end of the 24-hour spectrum, Running Water and Vegetation requests took the longest to resolve, averaging 15.8 hours and 14.9 hours respectively.
