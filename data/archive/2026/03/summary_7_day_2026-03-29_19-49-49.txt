Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the most significant numbers and response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 519 calls. The city averaged a response time of 2.8 days for these requests, with 60 cases already closed. Graffiti reports were the second most common with 239 calls, showing an average response time of 14.4 hours. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 171 calls, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Some service categories reported exceptionally fast turnaround times. Restroom Maintenance requests averaged a response time of just 50 minutes, while reports of Shoes on a Utility Wire averaged 44 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
