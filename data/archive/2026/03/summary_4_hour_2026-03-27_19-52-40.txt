Here is the latest city service response data summary from the Hustle Long Beach! accountability project.

Over the past 4 hours, the dataset shows a relatively small number of new updates, so no major systemic changes are available yet. The highest volume of recent reports came from E-Scooter issues with 44 cases and Dumped Items with 36 cases.

There were several unusually fast average response times recorded during this 4-hour window. Citizens should note that unusually low response times, particularly those measured in minutes, may be due to administrative closures or system referrals rather than actual physical work completions.
