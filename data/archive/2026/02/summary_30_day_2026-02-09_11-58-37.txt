Here's a look at Long Beach service response data from the last 30 days. Dumped Items (2,484 requests) and Graffiti (1,062 requests) were the most reported issues.

The average city response to resolve reports of dumped items took about 8 days. Graffiti removal requests were handled in about 59 hours on average, while street repair took about 67 hours. Some of the quickest responses were for Dead Animal Pickup (about 11.5 hours) and Animal Services (about 13 hours).

Over 1,000 reports were made for E-Scooters. These are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling. It is important to note that some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
