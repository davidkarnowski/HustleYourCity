Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests over the last 30 days.

The most common issue reported was illegally dumped items, with 2,430 service calls. This was followed by reports for e-scooters and graffiti, each with 990 calls.

Average response times for top issues varied. The city took about 7 days to close reports for dumped items and about 60 hours for graffiti.
