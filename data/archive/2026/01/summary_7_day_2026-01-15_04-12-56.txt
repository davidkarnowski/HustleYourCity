Here is the latest look at Long Beach service request data for the past week.

In the last 7 days, the most common service requests were for dumped items (593 reports), graffiti (249 reports), and e-scooters (217 reports).

Response times for several key services have shown improvement. The average time to close a "Dumped Items" report was about 2 days, faster than the 30-day average of nearly 5 days. Graffiti reports were closed in about 25 hours on average. Street repair requests were addressed in about 18 hours. A single restroom maintenance request was closed in just 16 minutes, which may indicate an administrative closure rather than a completed repair.
