An analysis of City of Long Beach service requests from the last 90 days highlights current response times and call volumes.

Dumped Items remains the highest volume category with 6,529 reports. The average time to resolve these requests is approximately 13 days. Graffiti reports followed with 3,116 total calls, showing an average response time of nearly 4 days.

Street Repair generated 804 reports with an average completion time of 4 and a half days. Tree Maintenance requests are currently taking about 6 days to resolve.

The fastest administrative response was for Business License and Permit concerns, averaging 12 minutes. Conversely, Marina Repair requests showed the longest average response time at 23 days.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. Accurate reporting helps ensure city services are tracked and measured.
