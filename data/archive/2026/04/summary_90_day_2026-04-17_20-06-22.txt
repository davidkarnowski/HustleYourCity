Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of requests across various categories. Here is a factual breakdown of the latest response times and call totals.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 6,962 reports. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,233 calls, averaging a response time of 56.5 hours. E-Scooter complaints generated 2,887 reports, though specific response times are not tracked in this dataset.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant activity. Tree Maintenance received 892 calls with an average response time of nearly 9 days. Street Repair had 703 reports, taking about 8 days on average to address. Sidewalk Repair requests totaled 377, averaging a 6.3-day response.

Some categories show very fast resolution times. Dead Animal Pickup averaged 9.8 hours for 468 calls. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged just 12 minutes for 6 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
