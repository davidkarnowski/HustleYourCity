Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the last 30 days.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped items (2,065 calls), e-scooters (928 calls), and graffiti (905 calls).

According to the city's data, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 2 days, while reports of dumped items took an average of 4.5 days to resolve. Slower response times were seen for issues like traffic signal repairs, which averaged over 5.5 days, and trash and debris cleanup, averaging nearly a week. Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 13 hours on average.
