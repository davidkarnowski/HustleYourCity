The latest data for city services in Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,181 reports for dumped items and 2,552 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 7 hours. In contrast, street repair requests have averaged about 8 days to address. Graffiti removal has been more efficient, with an average response time of approximately 3 days.

Please note that some categories show high numbers of new reports that are still in progress, which may affect current averages. Unusually low response times in certain categories can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

We encourage all residents to continue using the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. This helps ensure that the city has an accurate record of community needs and can allocate resources effectively.
