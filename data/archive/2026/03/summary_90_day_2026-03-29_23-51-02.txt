Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Over the past 90 days, residents have continued to report thousands of neighborhood issues, and the data shows how long it takes the city to respond.

Dumped items were the most frequently reported issue with 6,850 requests. On average, the city took 9.3 days to respond to these reports. Graffiti was the second highest category with 3,088 reports, averaging a 2.6-day response time. E-scooter complaints totaled 2,828, all of which were immediately referred to outside operators.

Infrastructure requests also saw high volumes. The city received 932 tree maintenance requests, which took an average of 5.3 days to address. Street repairs generated 854 reports with an average response time of 6.3 days, while 325 sidewalk repair requests took about 3.9 days.

Some service categories recorded very fast turnaround times. For instance, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual physical work being completed.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting local issues to the city using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure these problems are tracked and addressed.

This data summary was updated at March 29, 2026 at 4:47 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 29, 2026 at 3:01 PM PDT.
