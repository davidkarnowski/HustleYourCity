Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day city service data to keep the public informed on response times and call totals.

Over the past week, the highest volume of service requests involved Dumped Items with 524 reports, followed by Graffiti with 233 reports, and E-Scooter issues with 199 reports.

Response times varied across different service types. The city averaged 65 hours to respond to Dumped Items and about 14 hours for Graffiti. 

Some categories showed exceptionally fast resolution times. Restroom Maintenance requests averaged 50 minutes, and reports of Shoes on a Utility Wire averaged 44 minutes. Please note that unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
