Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 90 days. This analysis reviews call totals and average response times to keep the public informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 6,941 reports. The average response time for these requests was 8.6 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,187 calls, taking an average of 57.2 hours to resolve. E-Scooter complaints totaled 2,899, though all of these were referred to outside operating agencies.

Other significant service categories included Tree Maintenance with 902 reports averaging 4.6 days, and Street Repair with 751 reports averaging 7.1 days for a response. Sidewalk Repair saw 358 reports with an average turnaround of 5.3 days.

Some categories showed much faster turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup handled 455 reports in an average of 9.9 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting local issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
