Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data for the City of Long Beach. In the past day, residents submitted a high volume of requests, led by 90 reports for Dumped Items, 47 for E-Scooter issues, and 39 for Graffiti. Notably, none of the recent Dumped Items cases have been resolved yet, with the vast majority still marked as new.

Looking at response times over the last 24 hours, city crews showed rapid turnaround in several categories. Dead Animal Pickup requests averaged a response time of just 45 minutes across 5 calls. Light issues and Animal Services also saw quick averages of about 2 hours and 2.8 hours, respectively. Keep in mind that unusually low response times can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

On the slower end of the daily spectrum, Restroom Maintenance averaged a 19.4-hour response time, while Sidewalk Repair took an average of 13.1 hours to address.
