Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 30 days.

Illegal dumping was the most reported issue with 2,561 service calls, followed by graffiti with 1,106 calls. The average response time to close an illegal dumping report was about 8.5 days.

Graffiti reports were addressed in about 63 hours on average. Street repair and tree maintenance requests both took a little over 3 days to be completed. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups (about 11 hours) and animal services calls (about 12.5 hours).

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
