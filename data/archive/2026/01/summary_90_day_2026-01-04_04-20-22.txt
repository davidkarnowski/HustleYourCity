Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Over 15,500 service requests were made. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (5,853 reports), graffiti (3,601 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,600 reports).

Response times varied by issue. The average time to resolve a report for illegally dumped items was about 2 weeks. Park maintenance requests took over 2.5 weeks on average. In contrast, graffiti removal averaged under 5 days, and requests for dead animal pickup were typically resolved in about 17 hours.
