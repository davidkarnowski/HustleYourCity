An evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days highlights current response times for high-volume categories.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 6,586 reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 12 days. Graffiti removal followed with 3,130 reports, showing an average completion time of 3.5 days.

Infrastructure requests show mixed results. Street Repair totaled 836 reports with a response average of nearly 5 days. Tree Maintenance recorded 834 requests with an average wait time of 6 days. Conversely, Dead Animal Pickup services averaged 12.5 hours across 430 reports.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting service needs through the Go Long Beach application to ensure issues are logged and tracked.
