The latest 90‑day service data shows 6,366 Dumped Items reports, 3,634 Graffiti calls and 194 Park Maintenance requests.  

Average response times have improved for many services. Light repairs are closed in about 50 hours (just over 2 days) and Sidewalk repairs in roughly 62 hours (≈2.6 days). Tree Maintenance and Vegetation issues are typically resolved within 55 hours (≈2.3 days).  

Some categories still take longer: Dumped Items average 371 hours, which is about 15 days; Graffiti takes about 133 hours (≈5.5 days); Park Maintenance averages 128 hours (≈5.3 days). Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup are handled quickly, averaging 18 hours and 17 hours respectively.  

These figures reflect the city’s recent performance; unusually low response times may result from administrative closures rather than completed work.  

Residents can report new issues or check status through the Go Long Beach service app.
