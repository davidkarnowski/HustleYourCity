Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the city's response times and report totals over the last 30 days.

Dumped items received the highest volume of reports with 2,021 requests. The city took an average of 4.7 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,073 calls, averaging a response time of 42.7 hours. E-Scooter complaints also saw high numbers, totaling 1,025 reports.

Other notable service requests include tree maintenance with 459 reports averaging a 42.8-hour response, and street repair with 262 reports taking an average of 3 days to address. 

Some categories showed incredibly fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city. Staying engaged helps keep the local government accountable.
