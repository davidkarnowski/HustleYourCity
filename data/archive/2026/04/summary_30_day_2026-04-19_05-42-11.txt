Hustle Long Beach! brings you the latest 30-day accountability update on city service requests. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach has handled recent community reports.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 2,347 reports. The average response time for these requests was 8 days. E-Scooter complaints were the second most frequent with 1,063 reports, nearly all of which were referred to outside vendors rather than closed by the city. Graffiti removal saw 1,048 requests, with an average response time of 53 hours.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which handled 216 calls with an average turnaround of 69 hours. Light maintenance requests totaled 234, taking an average of 4 days to resolve. Dead Animal Pickup showed a rapid turnaround, clearing 172 reports in an average of 6.6 hours. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may be due to administrative ticket closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help keep the community safe and clean.

This data summary was updated at April 18, 2026, 10:37 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 18, 2026, 7:00 PM PDT.
