- Graffiti was resolved in roughly 1.4 days (33 hours).

- Most other categories were handled within 10‑32 hours, with Traffic Signal at 14 hours and Light at 32 hours.

These figures show many requests are being closed within a day, though some, like Dumped Items and Trash & Debris, still require several days.
