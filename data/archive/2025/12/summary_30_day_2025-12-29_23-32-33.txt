Here is the latest look at Long Beach service request data for the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents submitted more than 5,100 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,998 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (1,029 requests), and graffiti (1,008 requests).

Based on the data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.3 days. Requests for dumped item pickup took approximately 10 days to be resolved. A quicker response was seen for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 11.6 hours on average.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
