Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to dominate city service requests. In the last 90 days, citizens reported 6,960 dumped items and 3,186 graffiti issues. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 2,906 reports.

Response times vary significantly by category. The city took an average of 8.7 days to respond to dumped items and 7.2 days for street repairs. Graffiti issues were addressed faster, averaging about 58 hours. Tree maintenance requests averaged 4.7 days.

Some categories show incredibly fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

To help improve Long Beach neighborhoods, citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 7, 2026, 5:01 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 7, 2026, 3:00 AM PDT.
