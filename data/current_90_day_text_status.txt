Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service response data for the past 90 days.

Illegally dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues, with over 6,100 and 3,700 requests submitted, respectively. Response times for these high-volume requests averaged over two weeks for dumped items and about 6.5 days for graffiti removal.

The longest average response times were for park maintenance, taking nearly three weeks to resolve. In contrast, animal-related services were among the fastest, with requests for dead animal pickups and other animal services closed in an average of about 16 hours.

Some requests, like the 2,160 reports for e-scooters, are referred to third-party companies and do not have a city response time.

To report issues like potholes, graffiti, or dumped items, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
