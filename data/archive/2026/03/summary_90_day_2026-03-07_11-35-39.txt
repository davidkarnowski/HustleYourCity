Here is an evaluation of the City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days. This summary highlights the most frequent issues reported by residents and the average time it takes for the city to resolve them.

The most common issue reported continues to be Dumped Items, with 6,583 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 11 days. Graffiti reports are the second highest in volume with 3,099 total calls, showing an average resolution time of about 3 and a half days.

Infrastructure maintenance shows consistent activity. There were 1,040 reports regarding Street Lights, which are being resolved in about 3 and a half days. Street Repairs, which include potholes, totaled 847 reports with an average completion time of roughly 5 and a half days. Tree Maintenance requests are taking nearly 6 days to resolve on average.

Animal-related services show faster response times. Dead Animal Pickup requests are averaging just under 14 hours for resolution, while general Animal Services calls are closed in approximately 17 and a half hours.

Some administrative categories show extremely low response times. Reports regarding Business Licenses and Permits averaged 12 minutes to close. Readers should note that unusually low response times often indicate administrative closures or referrals rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. Consistent reporting helps track the needs of the community.
