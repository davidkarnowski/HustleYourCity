Hustle Long Beach! is here with the latest 30-day accountability update on city service response times. This independent summary makes sense of the numbers so citizens know exactly how long it takes for local issues to get resolved.

Over the past month, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 2,261 reports. On average, the city took nearly 6 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,018 calls, seeing an average turnaround of about 1.5 days.

E-Scooter complaints remained high with 942 reports, though all were referred to outside vendors. Other notable response times include Park Maintenance, which took an average of 4.6 days for 73 reports, and Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged just under 9 hours for 143 calls. 

When reviewing these statistics, please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative ticket closures rather than the actual completion of physical work.

Citizens play a vital role in tracking these metrics. Please continue to report neighborhood issues directly through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city has the data it needs to act.
