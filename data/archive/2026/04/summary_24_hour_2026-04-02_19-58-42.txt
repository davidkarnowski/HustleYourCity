Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Over the past 24 hours, the city received a steady volume of requests, highlighting specific trends in response times and neighborhood needs.

Dumped Items generated the most activity with 85 reports, though the vast majority remain in the new status without an average response time yet. E-Scooter complaints followed with 48 reports, all of which were referred to outside operators. Graffiti was the third most reported issue with 21 calls and an average response time of 12 hours.

For cases with recorded completion times in the last day, Dead Animal Pickup and Sidewalk Repair showed the fastest turnarounds, averaging 4 hours and 4.6 hours respectively. Vegetation reports took the longest to process, averaging 15.5 hours. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city. Tracking these statistics helps maintain transparency and accountability for local services.
