Here is the latest summary of city service request data. In the past 90 days, Long Beach residents made nearly 16,000 service requests for various issues.

The most frequent reports were for dumped items (almost 6,000 calls), graffiti (about 3,500), and e-scooters (about 2,700). The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 13 days. Graffiti removal was faster, averaging about 4.5 days.

Response times for more urgent public health issues were quicker. For example, requests for dead animal pickups were typically resolved in about 16 hours. Some service types show very fast resolutions of only a few hours, which can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed fieldwork.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure it is officially logged and tracked.
