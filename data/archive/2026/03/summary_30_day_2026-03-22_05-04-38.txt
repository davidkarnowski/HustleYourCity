Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a factual look at what citizens are reporting and how quickly the city is responding.

Over the last 30 days, Dumped Items led the request board with 2,221 reports, taking an average of 5.7 days to resolve. Graffiti followed with 1,000 reports and a 1.6-day average response time. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 945 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

The city moved fastest on Running Water issues, averaging a 7.9-hour response, and Dead Animal Pickups at 12.7 hours. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual completed field work.

On the slower end, Marina Repairs and Illegal Firework reports took the longest, averaging 7.4 days and 7.3 days respectively for a response. Park Maintenance requests took about 4.5 days to address.
