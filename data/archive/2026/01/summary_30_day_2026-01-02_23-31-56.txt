Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 4,900 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (1,901), improperly parked e-scooters (1,012), and graffiti (949).

For requests closed in this period, the average response time for dumped items was about 9 days. Trash and debris removal took about 8 days on average.

Quicker responses included graffiti removal, averaging about 56 hours, and street repair at about 48 hours. Reports for dead animal pickup were handled in about 14 hours on average.
