Graffiti reports remain the highest volume in the last 90 days – 3,672 tickets with an average response time of about 150 hours (≈6 days). Dumped‑item calls also surged, totaling 6,401 with an average response of 365 hours (≈15 days). Tree maintenance requests (819) and street‑light issues (579) are being addressed faster, averaging 53 hours (≈2 days) and 42 hours (≈1.8 days) respectively. Animal‑service calls (234) and dead‑animal pickups (456) are seeing the quickest turn‑around, under 18 hours on average.

Overall, the city handled over 13,000 service calls in the past three months. While some response times appear very short, they may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

Residents are encouraged to report issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app to help keep response times improving.
