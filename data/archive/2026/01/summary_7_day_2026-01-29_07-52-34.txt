Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past week.

Illegal dumping was the most reported issue with 662 requests. The average response time to close these cases is currently around 3.1 days. Graffiti removal was the second most common report with 285 requests, which were addressed in about 1.3 days on average.

Reports for street light outages had an average response time of about 3.2 days. The 225 reports regarding e-scooters were all referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
