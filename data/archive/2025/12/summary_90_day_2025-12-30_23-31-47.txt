Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests over the past 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (5,896 requests), graffiti (3,604 requests), and e-scooter problems (2,485 requests).

Average response times for these top issues vary. Reports for dumped items were closed in about 15 days on average, while graffiti removal took about 5 days. Some services were much faster, with dead animal pickups and animal services calls being resolved in about 15-16 hours on average.

To report issues like these, use the city's Go Long Beach service application. This helps create a record of the request and track its progress.
