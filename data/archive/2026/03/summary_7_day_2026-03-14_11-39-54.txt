Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service request data to keep you informed about city operations. Here is a breakdown of the service calls made to the City of Long Beach over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 554 reports. The city averaged a response time of 62 hours for these requests. Currently, 225 of these reports are marked as new and 176 are in progress.

E-Scooter complaints were the second most frequent with 266 reports. All 266 of these cases were closed and referred to other agencies.

Graffiti removal saw 228 reports. The city closed 183 of these cases, averaging a response time of 24 hours.

Tree Maintenance received 83 calls with an average response time of 21 hours, while Street Repair had 42 reports with an 18-hour average response time.

Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
