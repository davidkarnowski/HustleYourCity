Here is the latest weekly summary of Long Beach city service data.

In the last 7 days, illegally dumped items and graffiti were the most common issues reported by residents. There were 425 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of about 2.5 days. The 251 graffiti reports were addressed in about 1.3 days on average.

Response times for other services varied. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of 55 minutes. This unusually fast time may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work. Requests for traffic signal repairs averaged about 3.8 days to close.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at December 17, 2025, at 3:31 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 17, 2025, at 3:00 AM PST.
