Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest city service response times to keep the community informed. Looking at the last 30 days of data, citizens can see exactly how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood concerns.

Dumped items continue to dominate the system with 2246 reports. The city took an average of 5.4 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most common issue with 1038 reports, which saw an average response time of 40 hours. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 912 reports, all of which were referred to outside operators.

Response times vary significantly across different departments. Street repairs, which had 259 reports, took an average of 3.3 days to address. Tree maintenance took about 3.2 days for 505 reports. Meanwhile, illegal firework complaints took nearly 9 days to close.

On the fastest end of the spectrum, business license and permit concerns showed a response time of just 3 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Accountability starts with reporting. Residents are highly encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and ensure the city has an accurate record of neighborhood needs.

This data summary was updated at March 20, 2026, 12:52 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 20, 2026, 12:00 AM PDT.
