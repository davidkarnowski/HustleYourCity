Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at how city services are responding to citizen reports.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue with 2,369 calls over the last month. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these requests.

E-Scooter complaints were the second most common with 1,096 reports, though nearly all were referred to outside vendors rather than handled directly by city staff.

Graffiti removal saw 1,047 reports. The city responded to these issues in an average of 53.7 hours.

Street Repair received 222 requests, with an average response time of 67.7 hours. Dead Animal Pickup had 174 reports, which were handled in an average of 6.6 hours.

Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep Long Beach safe and clean. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
