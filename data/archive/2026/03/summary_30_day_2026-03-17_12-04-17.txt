Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest 30-day city service request data to keep citizens informed on response times and call volumes. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling recent service requests.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,169 reports. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti removal saw 1,057 reports, with an average response time of 39 hours. 

E-Scooter complaints totaled 909, though specific response times were not tracked in the city data.

Street Repair had 265 requests, averaging 3 days for a response. 

Business License and Permit Concerns had an unusually fast average response time of just 3 minutes for 2 requests. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
