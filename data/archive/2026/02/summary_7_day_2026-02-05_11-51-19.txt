Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Dumped Items (600 reports) and Graffiti (276 reports) were the most frequent issues submitted by residents. The average response time to close a Graffiti report was about 36.5 hours, while Dumped Items reports were closed in an average of 27.7 hours.

Response times for infrastructure issues were quicker. The average time to close a Street Repair request was 12.5 hours, and Sidewalk Repair requests averaged 12.5 hours. Dead Animal Pickup requests were handled in an average of 10.6 hours.
