Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is the accountability summary for the past 7 days.

Dumped Items led the service requests with 484 reports, followed by 242 E-Scooter issues and 222 Graffiti reports. Of the 484 Dumped Items reported, 251 remain new and 131 are currently in progress.

Response times varied significantly across categories. The average response time for Dumped Items was 3.1 days. Graffiti reports averaged a 32.89-hour response time, while Tree Maintenance requests averaged 15.91 hours. Running Water issues showed an unusually fast average response time of just 13 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
