Here is the latest analysis of Long Beach city service data from the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,300 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (2,062 requests), graffiti (907 requests), and improperly parked e-scooters (897 requests).

Average city response times for common issues varied. Graffiti removal requests were closed in about 2 days on average. The average response for dumped items was about 4.8 days, while traffic signal issues were addressed in an average of 5.6 days. Dead animal pickups were completed faster, averaging about 14 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it. Use the Go Long Beach service application to send requests directly to the city.
