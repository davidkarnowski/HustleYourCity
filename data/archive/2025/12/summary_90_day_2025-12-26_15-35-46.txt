Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days based on public data.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (5,935 requests), graffiti (3,670), and e-scooters (2,384).

Average response times for some key services include:
- Park Maintenance: about 17 days
- Dumped Items: about 2 weeks
- Streetlight Repair: about 1 week
- Graffiti Removal: about 5.5 days
- Sidewalk Repair: about 4 days

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
