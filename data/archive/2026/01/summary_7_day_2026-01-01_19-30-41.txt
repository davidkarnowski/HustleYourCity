Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data for the past week.

Residents submitted 1,199 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (438 requests), graffiti (196), and improperly parked e-scooters (179).

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. Graffiti removal took about 1 day and 16 hours. Dumped item cleanups averaged around 1 day and 10 hours. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 16.5 hours. Some requests, like park and restroom maintenance, were closed in under 90 minutes. These faster times can sometimes be due to administrative closures and may not reflect completed work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
