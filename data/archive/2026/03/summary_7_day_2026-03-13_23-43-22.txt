Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at how city services performed over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service requests with 548 calls. The city averaged a response time of 65.6 hours, or over two and a half days, with 101 of those cases closed and 176 currently in progress.

E-Scooter complaints were the second most frequent issue with 267 reports. All of these cases were closed and referred to outside agencies.

Graffiti removal crews handled 227 requests, closing 192 of them. The average response time for graffiti issues was 25.5 hours, or just over one day.

Other notable service areas included Tree Maintenance with 82 requests averaging a 22-hour response, and Dead Animal Pickup with 40 requests averaging an 8.2-hour response. Traffic Signal issues saw a rapid average response of 2.5 hours across 5 requests. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city safe and clean.
