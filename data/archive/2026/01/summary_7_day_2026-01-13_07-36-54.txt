Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Over 1,500 requests were submitted by residents through the Go Long Beach system.

Reports for illegally dumped items were the most common issue, with 591 requests. The average response time to close these cases was about 2 days. Graffiti was the next most frequent report with 247 requests, which were addressed in about 1 day on average.

The city responded to requests for dead animal pickups in under 6 hours on average. A single request for restroom maintenance was closed in about 29 minutes. Unusually fast responses may be due to administrative closures and not work completion.
