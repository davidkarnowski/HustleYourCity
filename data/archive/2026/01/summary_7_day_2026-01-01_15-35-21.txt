Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,200 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most common reports were for dumped items (443 requests), graffiti (197 requests), and e-scooter issues (175 requests).

The average response time for closed graffiti reports was about 41 hours. For dumped items, the average was about 34 hours. Some services saw much faster closures. For example, the average for restroom maintenance was about 85 minutes. A low average for park maintenance (about 37 minutes) may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
