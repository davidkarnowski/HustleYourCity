Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service data to bring you an update on response times over the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public requests.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 6,632 calls, taking an average of 10 days to resolve. Graffiti removal followed with 3,086 requests, averaging a 3-day turnaround. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 2,891 reports, though specific response times were not tracked for those issues.

Other major services included street repairs and park maintenance, which both averaged about 6 days for a response. On the faster end, dead animal pickups were handled in an average of 13 hours. Business license and permit concerns showed an unusually low response time of just 12 minutes, which may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep the community accountable and neighborhoods well-maintained. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
