Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the last 30 days.

Over the past month, residents submitted more than 5,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (1,898 requests), e-scooters (1,013 requests), and graffiti (912 requests).

The average time to close a request for illegally dumped items was about 8 days. Reports for trash and debris took an average of just over 9 days to resolve. In contrast, graffiti removal requests were closed in an average of about 2.2 days, and street repair requests were handled in about 1.4 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.
