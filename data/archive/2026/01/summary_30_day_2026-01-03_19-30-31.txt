Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past month, "Dumped Items" was the most frequent report, with 1,864 requests filed. The average response time for these requests was about 9 days. Graffiti was the second most common city-handled issue with 939 reports, which were addressed in about 2.4 days on average. There were also 996 reports for E-Scooters, which are referred directly to the scooter companies.

Some of the faster responses included "Dead Animal Pickup" (average of 14 hours) and "Animal Services" (average of 12 hours). Slower responses included "Trash & Debris" reports, which took an average of about 8.6 days to close. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at January 3, 2026, 11:27 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 3, 2026, 11:00 AM PST.
