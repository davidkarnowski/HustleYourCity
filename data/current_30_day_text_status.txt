Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the last 30 days.

Over the past month, residents submitted over 1,900 requests for dumped item pickups and over 1,100 for graffiti removal, making these the most reported issues.

The city's data shows the average time to close a "Dead Animal Pickup" request was about 8.5 hours. Graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of just over 2 days. Larger requests took more time, with "Dumped Items" averaging about 8 days and "Street Repair" averaging about 5 days for completion.
