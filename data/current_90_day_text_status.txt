In the past 90 days Long Beach recorded 14,013 service requests across all categories. The biggest volumes were Dumped Items (6,644 reports), Graffiti (3,548), Tree Maintenance (858) and Street Repair (597).

Average response times varied widely. The longest average was for Dumped Items at 351 hours (about 15 days). Community Center requests took 194 hours (≈8 days) and Report Illegal Firework took 226 hours (≈9 days). On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup was resolved in 16.7 hours, Animal Services in 18.8 hours, and Damaged Drinking Fountain in 23.3 hours.

Overall, many services are still taking several days to close, but some categories are being addressed within a day. Residents can help speed up resolution by reporting issues directly through the Go Long Beach service app.
