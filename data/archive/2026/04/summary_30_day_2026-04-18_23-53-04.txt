Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service call data from the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at how the city handled citizen reports over the past 30 days.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 2,341 total calls. The average response time for these reports is currently 8 days.

Graffiti is the second most common issue with 1,048 reports. The city is responding to these in an average of 53 hours. E-scooter complaints also remain high with 1,061 reports, though nearly all were referred to outside agencies, leaving no response time data.

For infrastructure, street repair received 219 calls with an average response time of 69 hours. Street light issues generated 237 reports, taking an average of 4 days to address. Sidewalk repairs saw 165 reports with an average response of 60 hours.

Some services show much faster turnarounds. Dead animal pickups averaged a 6.7-hour response across 171 reports, and animal services averaged 8.3 hours for 78 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to keep reporting local issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your concerns directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 18, 2026, 4:50 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 18, 2026, 3:00 PM PDT.
