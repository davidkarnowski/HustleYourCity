The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows significant activity in service requests over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 8,017 reports, followed by graffiti with 3,082 reports and e-scooter issues with 3,049 reports.

Response times for completed requests vary by service type. Dead animal pickups are currently being addressed in about 5 minutes, while graffiti removal averages roughly 82 hours. Tree maintenance requests are seeing an average response time of about 4 days. Please note that some very low response times may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
