Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days.

Over 15,000 service requests were made through the Go Long Beach app and other reporting systems. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,855 requests), graffiti (3,594 requests), and e-scooters (2,487 requests).

The average city response time to close a graffiti report was just under 5 days. For dumped items, the average response time was about 2 weeks. Park maintenance requests took an average of about 18 days to resolve. Animal-related services were among the fastest, with most calls closed in under a day.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
