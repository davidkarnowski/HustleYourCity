In the last 90 days Long Beach logged 6,207 Dumped Items reports – the highest volume of any category – with an average response time of about 16 days (378 hours). Graffiti calls numbered 3,713 and were closed in roughly 7 days on average (163 hours). Light‑related issues (634 reports) and Tree Maintenance (729 reports) also saw average response times near 7 days (175 hours and 153 hours respectively).  

Animal Services (201 reports) and Dead Animal Pickup (423 reports) were addressed much faster, averaging under 1 day (≈ 17 hours) from request to closure. Sidewalk Repair (313 reports) and Traffic Signal work (103 reports) each took about 5‑9 days to resolve.  

Overall, the city handled 23,000 service requests across all categories in the past three months. Residents can help keep response times short by reporting issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app.
