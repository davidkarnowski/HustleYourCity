The latest service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across city departments over the last 90 days. During this period, the city handled 7,754 reports regarding dumped items, with an average response time of approximately 16 days. Graffiti removal remains a high-volume service, with 2,574 reports processed at an average response time of about 3 days.

Tree maintenance saw 1,056 reports in the last 90 days, averaging about 4 days per response. Dead animal pickup continues to be one of the fastest services, with 546 reports in the same period averaging just over 7 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach application to report issues and track the status of city services in their neighborhoods.
