Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service request data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at how long it takes for the city to respond to citizen reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of calls with 6,843 requests. The average response time for these issues was 9.3 days.

Graffiti reports followed with 3,097 calls, taking an average of 2.6 days to address. 

Street Repair requests totaled 852, with an average response time of 6.2 days. 

Dead Animal Pickup saw 438 reports, which were handled in an average of 10.6 hours.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
