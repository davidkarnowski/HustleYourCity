The latest service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across city maintenance categories over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent service request, with 7,848 reports filed in the last 90 days. Response times for these requests currently average about 16 days.

Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service, with 2,633 reports in the same period and an average response time of approximately 61 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 1,066 reports over the last 90 days, with an average response time of about 4 days.

For urgent issues like dead animal pickup and animal services, response times remain efficient, averaging approximately 7 hours and 11 hours respectively over the last 90 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
