Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 30 days.

Over the past month, the most common service requests from residents were for dumped items (1,896 reports), e-scooters (1,014 reports), and graffiti (910 reports).

According to the city's data, the average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 8 days. For trash and debris cleanup, the average was over 9 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed more quickly, with an average completion time of about 2 days. Street repair requests were closed in about 1.5 days on average.
