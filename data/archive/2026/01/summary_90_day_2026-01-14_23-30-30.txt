Here is a look at the latest city service data for Long Beach, focusing on the last 90 days. Dumped items were the most reported issue with over 6,000 service requests, followed by graffiti with over 3,400 reports.

The average city response time to close a request for dumped items was about 13 days. For graffiti removal, the average was just under 5 days. Marina repair requests had the longest average completion time at about one month.

Some services saw much quicker responses. Reports for dead animal pickup and animal services were typically closed in less than 24 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to request service is by using the Go Long Beach application on your smartphone.
