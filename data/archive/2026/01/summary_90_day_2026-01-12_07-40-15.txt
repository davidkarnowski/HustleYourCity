Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

The most frequent requests from residents were for dumped items (5,917 reports) and graffiti removal (3,495 reports). The average response time for graffiti removal was about 4.5 days, while the average for dumped items was about 13.5 days.

Some services saw much quicker responses. For the 165 animal service requests made, the average response time was about 15 hours. In contrast, the few requests for marina repairs took an average of about one month to be addressed.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
