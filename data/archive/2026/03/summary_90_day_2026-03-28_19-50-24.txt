Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest Long Beach city service request data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of requests across various departments. Here is a factual breakdown of call totals and average response times.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,921 calls. The city took an average of 9.4 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service, totaling 3,117 calls with an average response time of 64 hours. 

Street Repair received 861 requests, requiring an average of 6.4 days for a response. Tree Maintenance handled 942 calls, taking an average of 5.5 days to resolve. Light issues accounted for 1,001 calls with an average response time of 3.1 days.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, Business Licenses and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help track community response times.
