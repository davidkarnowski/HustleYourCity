The latest data for city service requests in Long Beach shows ongoing activity across several key categories over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request type, with 8,386 reports. The average response time for these requests is approximately 18 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,830 reports and an average response time of about 3 days.

Tree maintenance requests totaled 904 over the last 90 days, with an average response time of approximately 3 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to see faster turnaround times, averaging between 6 and 12 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
