Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests over the past 90 days.

Residents submitted thousands of requests, with the most common reports for dumped items (5,854), graffiti (3,600), and e-scooters (2,567).

Average response times for closed requests varied by department. Reports for graffiti were addressed in about 5 days. Dumped items took longer, with an average completion time of about 2 weeks. Street repair requests were completed in an average of 9.5 days. Quicker responses were seen for animal-related services, with dead animal pickups and other animal service calls handled in about 17 hours and 16 hours, respectively. Some service types show very fast completion times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work.
