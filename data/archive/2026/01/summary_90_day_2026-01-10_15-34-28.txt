Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past three months, residents made thousands of reports. The most common issues were illegally dumped items (5,958 requests), graffiti (3,568 requests), and e-scooter problems (2,646 requests).

Response times varied by the type of service needed. The city was quickest to handle reports for dead animal pickups and other animal services, resolving them in about 16 hours on average. In contrast, requests for marina repairs took about one month to close. Reports of dumped items and park maintenance were typically resolved in about two weeks. Graffiti removal averaged around 4.5 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the best way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your phone.
