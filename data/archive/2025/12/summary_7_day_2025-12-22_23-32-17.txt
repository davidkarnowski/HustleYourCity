Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last seven days, residents submitted 1,143 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (435 requests), illegally parked e-scooters (245 requests), and graffiti (223 requests).

Average response times for closed service requests varied. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 26 hours. For dumped items, the response time was about 33 hours. Reports for restroom maintenance were closed in an average of 51 minutes, though such a low time may reflect an administrative closure rather than a completed repair.
