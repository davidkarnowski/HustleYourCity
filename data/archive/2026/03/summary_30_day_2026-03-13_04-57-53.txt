Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest service request data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs. Over the past 30 days, residents submitted thousands of reports, and the data shows a wide range of response times across different departments.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,103 requests. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these reports. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,047 calls, averaging a 43-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints reached 995 reports, all of which were closed and referred elsewhere.

Some services saw very rapid closures. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. On the other end of the spectrum, Marina Repair requests took an average of 7.5 days, and Trash and Debris reports averaged 4.6 days.

Other notable response times include Tree Maintenance at 52 hours, Street Repair at 3.4 days, and Dead Animal Pickup at 14 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 12, 2026 at 9:54 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 12, 2026 at 8:00 PM PDT.
