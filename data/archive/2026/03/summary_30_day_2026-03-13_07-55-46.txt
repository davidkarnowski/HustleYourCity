Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public requests.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2,107 requests. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,050 calls, averaging a 43-hour response time. 

Street Repair handled 266 reports, with an average response time of 3.4 days. 

Some categories showed unusually fast turnaround times. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes for 4 reports. Please note that response times this low may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.
