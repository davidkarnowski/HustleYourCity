Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at how city services performed over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 600 calls. The average response time for these reports was 59.5 hours. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 230 calls, taking an average of 21.1 hours to address.

Other notable service requests included 139 reports for E-Scooters, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Tree Maintenance received 59 calls with an average response time of 10.5 hours, while Dead Animal Pickup had 35 calls and a fast average response of 4.5 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can help by reporting it. Citizens are encouraged to submit service requests directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
