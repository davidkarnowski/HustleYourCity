As a third-party data interpreter for the Hustle Long Beach! community, here is a look at the latest City of Long Beach service request data over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items led the service requests with 6,802 reports. Graffiti followed with 3,077 reports, and E-Scooter issues generated 2,854 reports.

Response times varied significantly depending on the type of request. The city took an average of 9.5 days to respond to Dumped Items and about 6.6 days for Street Repair. Graffiti removal was faster, averaging a 67-hour response time.

Some categories showed unusually fast response times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that response times under 90 minutes often reflect administrative closures or system referrals rather than actual physical work completions.
