Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items with 5,953 reports, graffiti with 3,669 reports, and e-scooter issues with 2,384 reports.

Average response times for closing a service request varied significantly by type. Park maintenance requests took about 17 days to resolve, while reports for dumped items averaged around 14.5 days. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which were handled in an average of about 16 hours. Some issues may show unusually fast closure times due to administrative updates rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
