Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data from the past week.

Over the last seven days, residents submitted 1,486 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most common reports were for dumped items (575 requests), graffiti (249), and e-scooters (229).

Average response times varied by issue. Graffiti removal requests were closed in about 20 hours. Reports for illegally dumped items took longer, averaging about 45 hours. Tree maintenance requests were completed in about 5 hours on average.

All 229 e-scooter reports were forwarded to the appropriate companies and do not have a city response time. Note that some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
