The latest data update for city services in Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city received 7,630 reports regarding dumped items and 2,565 reports related to graffiti.

Response times vary by service category. Dead animal pickups have been efficient, with an average response time of approximately 7 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
