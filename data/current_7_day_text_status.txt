Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

The most frequent request was for dumped item pickups, with 513 calls. This was followed by 274 reports about e-scooters and 238 for graffiti removal. In total, residents made over 1,200 service requests in the last seven days.

For cases that were closed this week, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 21 hours. Dumped item pickups took an average of 50 hours, or just over two days. Sidewalk repair requests were addressed in about 6 hours on average.
