Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data from the past 90 days.

In the last three months, residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (6,198 requests), followed by graffiti removal (3,384 requests) and e-scooter issues (2,668 requests).

The average response time for dumped item pickups was about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in about 5 days on average. Some of the fastest responses were for dead animal pickups (about 14.5 hours) and animal services calls (about 13 hours). Longer-term fixes, like marina repairs, took an average of about 25 days.

To report issues like potholes, graffiti, or dumped items, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
