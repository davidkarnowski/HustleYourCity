Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service requests based on public data.

The most common issue reported by residents was illegally dumped items, with over 6,300 requests. The average time to close these requests was about 13.5 days.

Graffiti removal was another frequent request, with over 3,300 reports. These were addressed in about 5 days on average. Street repairs and light outages took a similar amount of time to resolve.

The city also received over 2,600 reports about e-scooters, which are typically referred directly to the scooter companies and not handled by city staff.

Some services saw faster responses. Reports for dead animal pickups and other animal services were typically closed in about 13 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at January 31, 2026, at 7:37 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 31, 2026, at 7:00 AM PST.
