The latest city service data for the past 7 days shows a high volume of reports for dumped items, with 554 total calls. While 102 of these were closed, 231 remain as new requests.

Graffiti removal continues to be a high-activity area with 203 reports this week. The average response time for these requests was approximately 23 hours.

Other notable activity includes 257 reports regarding e-scooters, all of which were closed referred, and 48 reports for dead animal pickup, which saw an average response time of about 5 minutes.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

To help keep our community clean and safe, please continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
