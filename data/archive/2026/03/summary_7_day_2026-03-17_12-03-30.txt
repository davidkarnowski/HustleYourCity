Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Over the past seven days, residents submitted a high volume of requests, providing a clear picture of current neighborhood needs.

Dumped items saw the highest activity with 600 reports and an average response time of 2.5 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue, totaling 230 calls with an average response time of 21 hours. E-scooter concerns also remained prominent with 139 reports.

Other services recorded very fast turnaround times. Dead animal pickups averaged 4.5 hours, and general animal services averaged 6.6 hours. Citizens should keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not necessarily actual work completions.

Accountability starts with reporting. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city responsive.
