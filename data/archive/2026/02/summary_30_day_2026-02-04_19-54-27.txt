Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the last month, residents submitted over 6,200 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (2,563 requests), graffiti (1,093 requests), and e-scooters (976 requests).

Average city response times varied by issue. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 2.5 days. For dumped items, the average response was about 9.5 days. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 12 hours on average.

Some response times may appear unusually fast. This can be due to administrative closures of requests rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
