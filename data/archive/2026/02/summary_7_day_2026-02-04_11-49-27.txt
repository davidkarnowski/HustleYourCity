Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,399 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (562 requests), Graffiti (264 requests), and E-Scooter issues (241 requests). These three categories made up over 75% of all service calls.

The average city response time for both Dumped Items and Graffiti reports was about 1.4 days. Other key service response times included Street Repair at about 15 hours and Sidewalk Repair at about 13 hours. Dead Animal Pickup requests were handled in about 10 hours on average.

If you see an issue that needs attention, reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
