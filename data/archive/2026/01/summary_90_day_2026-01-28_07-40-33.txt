Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on the latest public data.

In the last three months, residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most common issue reported was illegally dumped items, with over 6,200 requests. The average time to resolve these dumped item reports was about 13 days.

Graffiti removal, another frequent request, was completed in an average of 5 days. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 12 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
