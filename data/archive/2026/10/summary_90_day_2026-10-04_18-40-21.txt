The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 8,473 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 18 days. Graffiti reports totaled 2,868 with a much faster average response time of about 74 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 900 reports with an average response time of roughly 83 hours, while sidewalk repair requests totaled 387 with an average response time of about 109 hours. Animal services and dead animal pickup continue to show the fastest turnaround times, both averaging under 12 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track city response times directly.
