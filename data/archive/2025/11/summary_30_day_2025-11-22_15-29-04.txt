Latest 30‑day service request snapshot for Long Beach

In the past month the city logged 1,427 graffiti reports with an average response time of 66.7 hours (about 2.8 days). Street repair requests numbered 199 and were closed on average in 20.7 hours (just under 1 day). Tree maintenance saw 233 requests, averaging 10.3 hours to respond. Light outages had 238 reports with a 41.4‑hour average response. Dumped items generated 2,027 calls; the city took about 172 hours (7.2 days) on average to address them. Sidewalk repair had 100 requests, resolved in roughly 13.4 hours, and traffic signal issues totaled 41 requests with an 18.3‑hour average response. Animal services completed all 54 requests in about 17.6 hours.

Overall, most categories are seeing response times under two days, though some, like dumped items, still take longer. Residents can help keep the city accountable by reporting issues directly through the Go Long Beach service app.
