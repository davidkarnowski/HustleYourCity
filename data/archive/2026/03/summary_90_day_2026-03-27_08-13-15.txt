Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of reports, and the data shows how quickly the City of Long Beach is responding to different community needs.

Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue, with 6,882 requests in the last 90 days. The city takes an average of 9.5 days to respond to these reports. Graffiti is the second most common issue, generating 3,102 requests with an average response time of 2.7 days.

Other major infrastructure requests include tree maintenance with 945 reports averaging a 5.7-day response time, and street repair with 866 reports averaging a 6.5-day response time. Dead animal pickups are handled much faster, with 447 requests averaging just over 11 hours for a response.

Some categories show unusually fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that these extremely low response times may be due to administrative closures or automatic referrals rather than actual work completions.

Public reporting is essential for maintaining neighborhood infrastructure. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
