Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is the accountability summary for city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 541 calls. The average response time for these issues was 3.1 days. E-Scooter complaints followed with 258 calls, all of which were closed and referred to outside entities. Graffiti removal saw 233 requests, averaging a 29.2-hour response time.

Other notable service areas included Tree Maintenance with 76 calls averaging a 25-hour response, and Street Repair with 49 calls averaging a 29.9-hour response. Running Water reports showed an unusually low response time of 84 minutes for 6 calls. Please note that unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and hold the city accountable.
