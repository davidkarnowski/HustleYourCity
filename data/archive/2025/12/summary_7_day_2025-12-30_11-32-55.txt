Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,087 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (381 requests), graffiti (187 requests), and e-scooter issues (165 requests).

The average response time for graffiti removal was just under 3 days. Reports of illegally dumped items were addressed in about 1.5 days on average. Some services saw very quick responses. Reports for shoes on a utility wire were handled in about 45 minutes on average. The average close time for sidewalk repair requests was about 6 minutes. Unusually fast times like this may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
