Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data to keep the community informed. Here is a summary of the city service response times and call totals over the last 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 575 calls. The city averaged a response time of 55.7 hours for these issues, with 296 requests still marked as new.

Graffiti reports totaled 217 calls. The city responded to these in an average of 18.8 hours, and 144 of these cases have already been closed.

E-Scooter complaints accounted for 179 calls. All of these were marked as closed referred. Please note that unusually low response times or immediate closures may be due to administrative routing rather than actual work completions.

Other notable service areas include Tree Maintenance with 71 calls averaging an 11.4-hour response, and Dead Animal Pickup with 37 calls averaging a 5.5-hour response.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
