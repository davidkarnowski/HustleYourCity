Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the response times and call totals over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,992 reports. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,235 calls, averaging a 56-hour response time.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests showed longer wait times. Tree Maintenance received 891 calls with an average response of nearly 9 days. Street Repair had 712 reports, taking an average of 8 days to address. Sidewalk Repair saw 379 calls, averaging a 6.3-day response.

Some services showed rapid turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup handled 469 calls with an average response of 9.8 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a 12-minute response for 6 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens can help keep Long Beach maintained by reporting issues. Followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local concerns directly to the city.
