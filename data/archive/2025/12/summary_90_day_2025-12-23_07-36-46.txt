Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service requests. In the last three months, residents submitted over 15,500 reports for city services.

The most common issues reported were illegally dumped items (over 6,000 reports), graffiti (nearly 3,700 reports), and e-scooter issues (over 2,300 reports).

Average response times for key services varied. The city addressed reports for dumped items in about 14.5 days and graffiti removal in nearly 6 days. Faster responses were recorded for animal-related issues, which were handled in about 15 to 16 hours. Park maintenance requests took about 17 days on average to complete. Note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures and not on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 22, 2025 at 11:33 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 22, 2025 at 11:00 PM PST.
