Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (1,821 reports), e-scooters (1,007 reports), and graffiti (889 reports).

Response times for the most frequent requests varied. The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 8 days. For graffiti removal, the average was just over 2 days. E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to vendors and do not have a city response time.

Quicker responses were recorded for other services, including dead animal pickups (average of 14.5 hours) and street repair (average of about 40 hours).

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at January 4, 2026, at 11:40 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 4, 2026, at 11:00 PM PST.
