The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 6,639 reports for dumped items and 3,341 reports for graffiti. Response times for graffiti averaged approximately 2.6 days, while dumped items took about 8.1 days on average.

Vegetation maintenance reports saw a faster turnaround, averaging roughly 24 hours. Animal services and dead animal pickups remain among the most efficient categories, with average response times of approximately 9 hours and 6 hours respectively.
