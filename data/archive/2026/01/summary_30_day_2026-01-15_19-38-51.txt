Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the last month, the most common reports were for Dumped Items, with 2,065 requests. The average response time for these was just under 5 days. The second most frequent issue was Graffiti, with 910 reports and an average response time of about 2 days.

Some of the longest response times were for Trash & Debris removal, averaging almost 7 days, and reports of Illegal Fireworks, which took over 5 days on average to close. Quicker responses included Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services requests, which were both handled in about 14 hours on average.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
