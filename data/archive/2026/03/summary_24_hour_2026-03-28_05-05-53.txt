Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour city service data to keep the community informed on response times and call volumes.

In the past day, Dumped Items led the service requests with 86 reports, though 73 remain in the "New" status. The average response time for these items was 3.8 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed with 50 reports, all of which were referred and closed. Graffiti saw 43 reports, with 21 already closed at an average response time of 2.6 hours.

Several categories showed exceptionally fast average response times. Street Repair requests averaged 37 minutes, Sidewalk Repair averaged 46 minutes, and Animal Services averaged 32 minutes. Dead Animal Pickup also saw quick turnarounds, averaging 40 minutes. 

Please note that unusually low response times, especially for major physical repairs like streets and sidewalks, may be due to administrative closures or system routing rather than actual work completion.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application to help keep the city accountable.
