The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 6,693 reports for dumped items and 3,277 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 5.5 hours. In contrast, sidewalk repair requests have taken an average of about 9 days to address. Graffiti removal has seen an average response time of roughly 3 days.

Please note that unusually low response times, particularly those under 90 minutes, may reflect administrative processing rather than the completion of field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that service requests are tracked and addressed.
