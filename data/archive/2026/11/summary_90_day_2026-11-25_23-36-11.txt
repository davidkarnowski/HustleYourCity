The latest data for Long Beach city services shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,274 reports for dumped items and 2,567 reports for graffiti. Response times for graffiti averaged approximately 2.8 days, while dumped items took about 19.5 days on average.

Tree maintenance remains a high-volume category with 1,037 reports in the last 90 days, showing an average response time of about 8.7 days. Dead animal pickup continues to be one of the fastest services, averaging roughly 7.5 hours per response across 548 total reports.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This tool helps track service requests and ensures that reports are routed to the appropriate departments for review and action.
