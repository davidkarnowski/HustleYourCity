The latest data for city services in Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,317 reports for dumped items and 2,562 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of about 7 hours. Conversely, dumped items have seen an average response time of approximately 19 days. Please note that unusually low response times for some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. This tool remains the most effective way to notify the city of concerns and track the status of your requests.
