Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data from the past 90 days.

The most common service requests from the public were for dumped items (5,957 reports) and graffiti (3,550 reports). The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 4.5 days. For dumped items, the average response time was longer at approximately 13.5 days.

Response times for more urgent issues were quicker. Reports for dead animal pickup and other animal services were closed in about 16 hours on average. Some service requests show unusually fast closure times, which may be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the best way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
