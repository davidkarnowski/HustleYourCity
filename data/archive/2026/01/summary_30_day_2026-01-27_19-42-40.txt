Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past month, the most frequent reports from residents were for illegally dumped items (2,390 requests), e-scooters (1,028 requests), and graffiti (997 requests).

Average city response times varied by the type of issue. The average time to resolve a report for dumped items was about 7.5 days. Graffiti removal requests were handled more quickly, with an average response time of about 2.5 days. Some of the fastest responses were for dead animal pickups, which were resolved in about 12 hours on average.

To report issues like graffiti, potholes, or dumped items in your neighborhood, use the city's Go Long Beach service application.
