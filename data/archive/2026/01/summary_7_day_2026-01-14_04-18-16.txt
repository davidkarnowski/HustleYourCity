Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted over 1,400 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common report was for "Dumped Items" with 572 requests, followed by "Graffiti" with 239 reports.

The average city response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was just over 2 days. For "Graffiti" reports, the average response was about 25 hours. Requests for "Dead Animal Pickup" were completed in an average of under 2 hours.
