Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days.

The most frequent issue reported by residents was illegally dumped items, with over 6,300 service calls. The second most common was graffiti, with over 3,300 reports. The data shows the average response time for graffiti removal was about 5 days, while pickup for dumped items averaged about 13.5 days.

Faster response times were seen for urgent issues. Dead animal pickups and animal service calls were typically resolved in about 13 hours. Street light outage reports were closed in about 4.5 days on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
