Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Dumped items, e-scooters, and graffiti were the most frequently reported issues. Residents submitted over 430 reports for dumped items and 225 for graffiti.

The average response time for closed graffiti reports was about 26 hours. For dumped items, the average was about 42 hours. Reports for sidewalk repair and restroom maintenance were closed in under an hour on average, around 45 and 51 minutes respectively. These unusually fast times may reflect administrative closures and not necessarily on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
