Hustle Long Beach! is here with your latest 90-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests. Here is a look at the numbers and how long it takes the city to respond to your reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue by far, with 6,933 calls in the last 90 days. The city took an average of 8.7 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti reports followed with 3,178 calls, averaging a 58-hour response time.

Infrastructure and maintenance issues are taking a bit longer. Street repairs, which totaled 756 requests, took an average of 7.2 days for a response. Sidewalk repairs saw 359 requests and averaged 5.3 days. Tree maintenance requests reached 909, taking about 4.8 days on average.

Interestingly, the 6 reports regarding business licenses and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Unusually low response times like this are often due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Meanwhile, all 2,905 e-scooter complaints were simply referred to outside agencies.
