Over the past 90 days the City recorded about 16,200 service requests across 22 categories. The biggest volumes were Dumped Items (6,376 reports) and Graffiti (3,636 reports).  

Average response times varied widely:  
- Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were closed in roughly 17 hours.  
- Dumped Items took about 15 days on average, and Graffiti about 5.5 days.  
- Light issues averaged 2 days, Tree Maintenance 2.3 days, Street Repair 4.4 days, and Traffic Signals 4.3 days.
