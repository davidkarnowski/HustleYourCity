Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is responding to citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2,188 requests in the last 30 days. The city took an average of 7.4 days to respond to these reports. Graffiti was the second most common issue, generating 981 reports with an average response time of 39.9 hours. E-scooter complaints followed closely with 905 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable service areas include street repair, which received 236 requests and took an average of 3 days for a response. Dead animal pickups saw 153 requests, with the city responding in an average of 8.9 hours. Park maintenance received 80 calls, averaging a 4.9-day response time.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application. Tracking these numbers helps maintain accountability for city services.
