Here is the latest summary of City of Long Beach service requests for the past 30 days.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 2,508 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 7 days. Graffiti reports totaled 1,103 with an average response time of 56 hours.

Street Repair requests showed an average response time of just over 3 days across 245 total reports. Dead Animal Pickup services averaged 9 hours per response on 151 reports.

Please note that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than physical work completion.

Residents are encouraged to report issues using the Go Long Beach service application.
