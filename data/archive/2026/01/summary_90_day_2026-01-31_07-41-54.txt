Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

The most frequent requests were for "Dumped Items," with over 6,300 reports filed. The average time to resolve these was about 13.5 days. The second most common issue was "Graffiti," with over 3,300 reports, which took an average of 5 days to address.

Response times varied by service type. Reports for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were closed in about 13 hours on average. These fast closures may sometimes be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion. In contrast, "Marina Repair" requests, though few in number, took an average of 5 weeks to resolve.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
