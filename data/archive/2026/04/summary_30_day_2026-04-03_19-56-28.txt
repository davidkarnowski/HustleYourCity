Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest 30-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests.

Over the past month, Dumped Items led the service requests with 2,336 reports. Graffiti followed with 954 reports, and E-Scooter issues generated 923 calls.

When looking at response times, the city took an average of 8.4 days to respond to Dumped Items. Park Maintenance requests averaged 4.1 days, and Street Repair requests took an average of 3.4 days. Graffiti issues were addressed in about 33.5 hours, while Dead Animal Pickups averaged a 5.2-hour response time. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completion.
