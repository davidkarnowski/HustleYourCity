Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, the most frequent service requests from the public were for dumped items (568 reports), graffiti removal (235 reports), and e-scooter issues (229 reports).

The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 25 hours. Street repair requests were addressed in an average of about 23 hours. The average response for dumped item pickups was just over 2 days.

Some issues were handled more quickly. Dead animal pickups were completed in an average of under 3 hours. One restroom maintenance request was closed in approximately 16 minutes; however, unusually fast closures can sometimes be due to administrative processing.
