Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest independent accountability update on city service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items continue to drive the highest volume of service requests. The city received 2,372 reports in the last month, with an average response time of just over 8 days.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 1,088 reports, taking an average of 41 hours to process. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 999 reports, nearly all of which were immediately referred to outside operators.

Tree Maintenance received 250 requests, taking an average of 2.5 days to address, while Street Repair teams managed 219 requests with an average response of nearly 3 days. On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup handled 169 calls with an average turnaround of 7 hours.

When reviewing this data, please note that unusually low response times, particularly those measured in minutes, may be due to administrative closures or system referrals rather than actual work completions.
