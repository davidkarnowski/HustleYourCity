Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,222 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (513 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (252 requests), and graffiti (196 requests).

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 33 hours. Dumped item pickups were addressed in an average of about 45 hours, or just under 2 days. Street repair requests were completed in an average of about 24 hours. Slower response times were seen for park maintenance (2.6 days) and traffic signal issues (2.5 days).

Some categories, like vegetation and restroom maintenance, showed very fast average response times of around 7 hours. These quick closures may sometimes be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city.
