Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Citizens submitted 5,050 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,941 calls), e-scooters (1,033 calls), and graffiti (1,003 calls).

Looking at average response times for closed cases, dumped item reports took about 10 days to resolve. Graffiti was handled in about 54 hours, while traffic signal issues took around 4 days. Animal services requests were among the fastest, resolved in about 8.5 hours.

Some service requests may be closed for administrative reasons, which can result in unusually fast response times.
