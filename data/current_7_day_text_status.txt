Here is the latest weekly summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the last 7 days, the most frequent requests from residents were for Dumped Items (512 reports), E-Scooters (274 reports), and Graffiti (237 reports).

Average city response times for closed requests included about 50 hours for Dumped Items and 22 hours for Graffiti. Street Repair requests were closed in an average of 21 hours, while Sidewalk Repair requests were closed in about 6 hours. Note that some fast response times can be due to administrative closures.

See an issue in your neighborhood? You can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
