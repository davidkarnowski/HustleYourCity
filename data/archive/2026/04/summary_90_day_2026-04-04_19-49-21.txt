Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest accountability update on city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 7047 reports. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3150 calls, averaging a response time of 59 hours. E-Scooter complaints generated 2868 reports, though the vast majority were immediately referred to outside agencies.

Street repair requests totaled 811, with an average response time of 6.7 days. Tree maintenance saw 919 reports, taking about 4.9 days on average to address. On the faster side, dead animal pickups had 447 reports and were handled in about 10 hours.

Business license and permit concerns showed an incredibly fast average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 4, 2026, 12:45 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 4, 2026, 11:00 AM PDT.
