The latest data for the City of Long Beach service requests covering the past 90 days shows significant activity in waste and maintenance reporting.

Dumped Items remain the most frequent request with 6,397 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 13.5 days.

Graffiti removal received 3,149 reports. The average time to resolve these issues is roughly 4 days. Street Repair requests, which total 793, are showing an average response time of roughly 5 days.

Faster response times were recorded for public safety and health concerns. Dead Animal Pickup requests averaged 11.5 hours for completion, while Animal Services averaged 12.5 hours. The fastest administrative response was for Business Licenses and Permit Concerns, averaging 31 minutes.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
