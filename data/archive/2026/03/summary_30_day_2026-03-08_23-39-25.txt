Here is the latest city service data summary for the Hustle Long Beach! community, focusing on service requests from the past 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest number of reports with 2022 calls. The city average response time for these issues was 4.8 days.

Graffiti removal was another major concern, totaling 1076 reports. The average response time for graffiti was 44.2 hours.

E-Scooter complaints generated 1010 reports. All of these cases were closed and referred to outside agencies, resulting in no measurable city response time.

Street Repair received 262 reports, with an average response time of 3.2 days. Tree Maintenance had 447 reports, averaging a 43.5-hour response time.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business Licenses and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city. Reporting helps maintain accurate data and improves community conditions.
