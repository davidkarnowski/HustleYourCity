Here is the latest Long Beach city service data summary for the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted over 1,100 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (420 requests), illegally parked e-scooters (233 requests), and graffiti (225 requests).

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 27 hours, while dumped item reports were closed in about 37 hours on average. Dead animal pickups were completed in an average of about 9 hours. Some requests, like a recent restroom maintenance report, were closed in under an hour. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application to report it directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at December 24, 2025, 7:33 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 24, 2025, 7:00 AM PST.
