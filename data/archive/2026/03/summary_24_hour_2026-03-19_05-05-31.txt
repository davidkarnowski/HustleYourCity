Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest 24-hour city service data to keep the community informed on response times and call volumes.

Over the past day, Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 62 reports. The average response time was exceptionally fast at 59 minutes. Graffiti followed with 17 reports and an average response time of 81 minutes.
