Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest look at City of Long Beach service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items led the request volume with 520 reports. The average response time for these requests was 3.5 days. Graffiti removal saw 223 requests with an average response time of 19.5 hours. E-Scooter issues generated 187 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable response times include Abandoned Shopping Carts, which took an average of 3.3 days to address across 9 reports. Restroom Maintenance requests showed a very fast response time of just 50 minutes. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
