Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data from the past week.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with 595 service calls, followed by 237 reports for graffiti.

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 23.5 hours. For dumped items, the average response was about 3 days. Animal-related services were handled more quickly, with an average response of 3.3 hours for Animal Services and 5.6 hours for dead animal pickups. Some fast response times may reflect administrative closures and not on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
