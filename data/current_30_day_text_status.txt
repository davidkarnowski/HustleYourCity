Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 30 days.

Dumped Items and Graffiti were the most reported issues, with 1,960 and 1,151 calls respectively. These two categories accounted for the majority of service requests made by the public.

The data shows improved response times for major services. The average response for Graffiti removal was about 55 hours. Dumped Items cleanup averaged about 7.5 days, and Street Repair requests were completed in about 4.2 days on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report problems like dumped items or graffiti is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
