Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data for the past 7 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling local service calls.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 464 reports. The city averaged a response time of about 52 hours for these requests. E-Scooter issues were the second most reported with 256 calls, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti removal saw 232 requests with an average response time of about 18 hours.

Tree maintenance received 70 calls, averaging an 8-hour response time. Running water reports showed an unusually fast response time of 13 minutes for 3 calls. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Street repair requests totaled 63, with an average response time of about 17 hours.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
