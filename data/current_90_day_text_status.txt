The latest data update for city services shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,540 reports for dumped items and 2,585 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 8 minutes. Conversely, dumped items have seen an average response time of about 17 days, while tree maintenance requests have averaged roughly 6 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
