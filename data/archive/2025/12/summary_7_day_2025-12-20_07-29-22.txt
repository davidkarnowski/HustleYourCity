Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. According to the latest data, the most common reports were for dumped items (434 requests), illegally parked e-scooters (241), and graffiti (224).

The average time to close a graffiti report was about 26 hours. Reports for dumped items were closed in just under two days on average. Some categories showed unusually fast response times. For example, the average close time for sidewalk repair was about 45 minutes. These quick closures may reflect administrative updates rather than the time for work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
