Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past seven days. Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 518 calls logged. The city is currently averaging a response time of 64 hours for these requests. Graffiti removal follows with 237 reports and an average response time of about 14 hours.

E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 201 reports, though all were immediately referred to outside agencies. On the faster side, restroom maintenance and shoes on a utility wire requests were closed in 50 minutes and 44 minutes, respectively. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times, particularly those under 90 minutes, may be the result of administrative closures rather than the actual completion of physical work.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application. Tracking these numbers helps hold local government accountable and keeps the community informed.
