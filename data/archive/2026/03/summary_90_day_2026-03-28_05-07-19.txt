Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data to see how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports over the past 90 days.

The most frequently reported issue was Dumped Items, with 6,916 requests taking an average of 9.5 days to address. Graffiti followed with 3,115 reports, averaging a 65-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints reached 2,833, all of which were referred to other agencies. Street Repair saw 864 requests, taking an average of 6.4 days to resolve.

Some issues were closed very quickly. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes, though unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead animal pickups averaged 11.5 hours. On the slower end, damaged drinking fountains took nearly 11 days to resolve, and general trash and debris removal averaged 7.5 days.

Citizens are encouraged to continue using the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 27, 2026, 10:03 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 27, 2026, 8:00 PM PDT.
