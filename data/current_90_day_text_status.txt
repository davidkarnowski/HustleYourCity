Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues, with over 6,100 and 3,700 service requests filed, respectively. The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 15 days, while graffiti removal requests were closed in about 6 and a half days.

Some of the slowest responses were for park maintenance, which took an average of 18 days to resolve. Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 16 hours on average. Sidewalk repair requests show an average closure of about 5 and a half days, but this unusually low time may be due to administrative closures and not physical repairs.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
