Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped items (6,070 reports), graffiti (3,374 reports), and e-scooters (2,668 reports). The average time to close a report for dumped items was about 13 days, while graffiti reports were handled in about 5 days.

Other key services included street repairs, with an average response time of about 6 days, and tree maintenance, which took about 4 days. Reports for dead animal pickup were among the fastest, resolved in an average of about 15 hours. Some requests, like for damaged drinking fountains, show very fast closures of about 2 hours, which may be due to administrative updates.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to alert city services.
