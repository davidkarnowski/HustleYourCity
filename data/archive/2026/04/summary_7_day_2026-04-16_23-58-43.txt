Hustle Long Beach! followers, here is the latest independent accountability update on city service response times based on data from the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service requests with 578 calls. The average response time for these reports was 3.8 days. E-Scooter complaints followed with 272 calls, though most were immediately referred and closed.

Graffiti removal saw 256 requests with an average response time of 43.8 hours. Dead Animal Pickup had 40 calls, averaging a 4.4-hour response. Traffic Signal issues took an average of 3.5 days to address across 14 reports.

Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
