Here is the latest Long Beach service data update for the past week.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 555 service requests. This was followed by 261 reports for e-scooters and 212 for graffiti.

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 25 hours. For dumped items, the average was about 45 hours. Some service requests showed very fast closure times, such as vegetation requests at an average of 61 minutes. These unusually low times may be due to administrative closures and not necessarily work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
