Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted thousands of service requests through the Go Long Beach app. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,535 calls), graffiti (1,107 calls), and e-scooter issues (951 calls).

According to the city's data, the average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 58 hours. For illegally dumped items, the most frequent request, the average response time was about 9 days. Animal-related calls were typically handled in under 13 hours. The 951 e-scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies for resolution.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
