Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data. The most frequent issue reported by residents continues to be illegally dumped items, with over 6,300 requests. The average response time for the city to close these reports was about 13.5 days.

Graffiti removal requests totaled over 3,300, with an average completion time of about 5 days. Street repair requests were closed in an average of about 5.5 days. Reports for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup had much faster closure times, averaging around 13 hours. These faster times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
