Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,469 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common report was for illegally dumped items, with 660 requests. This was followed by 284 requests for graffiti removal.

Average city response times for closed requests included about 25 hours for graffiti and 19 hours for dumped items. Requests for abandoned shopping carts were closed in an average of 39 minutes. Unusually fast response times can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed fieldwork.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to notify the city is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
