Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports and service requests.

Dumped Items and Graffiti remain the most reported issues. Over the last 90 days, there were 6,502 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of nearly 11 days. Graffiti saw 3,110 reports, taking the city an average of 3.3 days to respond.

Street repair and tree maintenance also saw high demand. The city received 839 street repair requests with an average response time of 5.4 days, while 878 tree maintenance calls took about 5.7 days to address.

Some services show very fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes, though unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 13 hours.

Help keep the community in top shape. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 10, 2026, at 4:47 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 10, 2026, at 4:00 AM PDT.
