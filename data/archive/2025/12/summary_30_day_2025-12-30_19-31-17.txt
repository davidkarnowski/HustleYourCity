Here is the latest look at Long Beach service requests from the past 30 days based on city data.

The most common reports were for Dumped Items (2,051 calls), E-Scooters (1,030 calls), and Graffiti (1,017 calls). The average city response time to resolve a Dumped Items report was about 10 days, while Graffiti removal took an average of about 2 days. E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Other key service times include Park Maintenance requests, which took an average of about 3.75 days to close, and Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged about 11.6 hours. Some requests show very fast closure times, which may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
