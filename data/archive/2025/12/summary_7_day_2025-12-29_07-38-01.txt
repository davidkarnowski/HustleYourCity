Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,000 service requests were made through the Go Long Beach app and other city channels. The most reported issues were for dumped items (348 calls), graffiti (189 calls), and improperly parked e-scooters (175 calls).

For requests that were completed, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 22 hours. Dumped items were addressed in about 28.5 hours on average. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of 32 minutes. This unusually fast time may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 28, 2025, at 11:35 PM and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 28, 2025, at 11:00 PM.
