Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest independent look at city service response times over the past 30 days. This data helps track how long it takes for reported issues to be addressed across the city.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 2,309 reports. The average response time for these requests was just over 8 days.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 933 reports, averaging a response time of 35.5 hours.

Street Repair saw 220 reports, taking about 3.5 days on average to process. 

Dead Animal Pickup had 167 reports and was handled relatively quickly, averaging a 5.4-hour response time. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
