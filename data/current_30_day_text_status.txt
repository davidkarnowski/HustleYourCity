Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

The most common issues reported by residents were for Dumped Items (1,968 requests) and Graffiti (1,156 requests).

The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 2.3 days. For dumped items, the average was longer at around 7.5 days. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickups were completed in an average of about 9 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it using the Go Long Beach service application. Your reports help the city track and respond to neighborhood needs.

This data summary was updated at 7:36 AM PST on Dec 17, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 7:00 AM PST on Dec 17, 2025.
