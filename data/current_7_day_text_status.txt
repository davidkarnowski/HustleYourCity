Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,299 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (515 calls), E-Scooters (277 calls), and Graffiti (236 calls). Reports for E-Scooters are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

For completed requests, the average city response time for Dumped Items was about 2 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in an average of about 22.5 hours. Some categories showed faster response times, such as Sidewalk Repair (under 6 hours). Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not on-site work completion.
