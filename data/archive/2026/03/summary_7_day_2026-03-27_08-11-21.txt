Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 525 reports. The average response time for these issues was 3.6 days. Currently, 246 of these reports remain marked as new, while 160 are in progress.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 207 reports and an average response time of 23.35 hours. E-Scooter complaints totaled 195, all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies and closed.

Other notable service times include Street Repair, which saw 67 reports averaging a 23.92-hour response, and Dead Animal Pickup, which handled 48 reports with a fast 4.72-hour average response. Abandoned Shopping Carts took an average of 3.3 days to address across 10 reports.

Restroom Maintenance showed an unusually fast response time of 35 minutes for a single request. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Hustle Long Beach! encourages all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps keep the city accountable and provides the data needed to track government efficiency.
