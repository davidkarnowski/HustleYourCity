Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted thousands of service requests in the past month. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,016 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (988 requests), and graffiti (899 requests).

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. Reports for graffiti were addressed in about 52 hours on average. Dumped items took about 4 days, while traffic signal issues took about 5 days to resolve. Note that some response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app on your smartphone.
