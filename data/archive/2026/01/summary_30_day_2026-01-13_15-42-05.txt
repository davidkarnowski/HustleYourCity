Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past month, residents submitted more than 5,300 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (1,972 requests), illegally parked e-scooters (971), and graffiti (921).

Based on the data, the average response time to resolve a dumped items report was about 5 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 52 hours on average, while reports of trash and debris took about one week. Street light and traffic signal issues took roughly 3 to 5 days to resolve. Note that some response times may appear faster due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
