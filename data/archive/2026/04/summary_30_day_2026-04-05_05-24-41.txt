Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2305 requests in the last month. The average response time for these items is currently about 8 days.

Graffiti removal saw 976 reports, with crews responding in approximately 33 hours on average. E-scooter complaints also generated high call volumes with 930 reports, though these are typically referred to outside vendors.

Street repair requests, totaling 216 calls, are taking about 3 days to address. Meanwhile, dead animal pickups are handled much faster, averaging a 5-hour response time for the 164 reports filed. Please note that unusually fast response times across some categories may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

Help keep the city accountable and neighborhoods clean. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
