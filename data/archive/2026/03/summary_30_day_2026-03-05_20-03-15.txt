An evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 30 days highlights the current volume and response times for issues reported by residents.

The most frequent service request was for Dumped Items, with 2,224 total reports filed. The average time to resolve these issues was approximately 5 and a half days. Graffiti removal followed with 1,111 reports, showing an average completion time of just under 2 days.

Infrastructure data indicates that Street Repair requests, totaling 258 reports, took roughly 3 days to address on average. Tree Maintenance saw 439 reports with a response time of less than 2 days. Street Light issues generated 368 reports and were typically resolved in slightly over 2 days.

Regarding sanitation and safety, Dead Animal Pickup services handled 139 calls with an average response time of 14 hours. Abandoned Shopping Carts were addressed in approximately 13 hours.

Residents are encouraged to report maintenance, safety, and sanitation issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure requests are tracked.
