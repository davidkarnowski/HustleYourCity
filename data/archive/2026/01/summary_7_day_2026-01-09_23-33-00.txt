Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service call data for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, the most frequent service request was for "Dumped Items," with 583 reports. The average time to close these requests was just over 2 days. "Graffiti" removal was the second most common request with 241 reports, which were addressed in an average of about 26 hours.

Some services saw faster response times. Reports for "Vegetation" maintenance were closed in an average of about 8.5 hours, and "Dead Animal Pickup" requests were completed in about 10 hours. Please note that some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

In total, residents submitted over 1,400 service requests through the Go Long Beach system this past week.
