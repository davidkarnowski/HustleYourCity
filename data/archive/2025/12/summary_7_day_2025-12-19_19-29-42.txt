Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,200 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (470 requests) and graffiti (254 requests). The city also received 230 reports about e-scooters, which are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Average response times for key services include:
- Graffiti Removal: 1.5 days
- Dumped Items: 2.3 days
- Streetlight Repair: 38 hours
- Dead Animal Pickup: 22 hours

Sidewalk repair requests show an average response of about 45 minutes. Unusually fast times like this may be due to administrative closures and not the time it takes to complete physical work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.

This data summary was updated at 11:27 AM on December 19, 2025, and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 AM on December 19, 2025.
