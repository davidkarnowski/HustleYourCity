Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to resident requests. 

Dumped items and graffiti continue to generate the highest volume of reports. Over the past 30 days, there were 2,340 calls for dumped items, which took an average of over 8 days to resolve. Graffiti complaints totaled 944, with an average response time of about 35 hours. E-scooter issues also remained a major concern with 882 reports.

Other notable service areas include street repair, which saw 224 requests and took an average of 3.5 days to address. Park maintenance received 83 calls, averaging a 4-day response time. Faster turnarounds were seen in dead animal pickups, where 166 calls were handled in an average of 5 hours.

When reviewing these statistics, keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Staying active and reporting problems helps keep the city accountable.
