New data for the last 90 days shows how quickly the city handles service requests.

Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup are the fastest, with average response times of about 18 hours and 17 hours respectively – under a day for most cases.

On the slower side, Graffiti requests average 169 hours (about 7 days) and Dumped Items average 351 hours (roughly 15 days) before completion.

Overall, 2,456 e‑scooter reports were routed, and 3,543 graffiti complaints were logged, reflecting a busy period for city crews.

Residents can report issues instantly through the Go Long Beach service app to help keep response times low.
