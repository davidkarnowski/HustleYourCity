Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the last 90 days, residents submitted over 16,000 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (6,467 calls), graffiti (3,264 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,710 calls).

Here is a look at the average city response times for closing requests in key categories:
- Dumped Items: about 14 days
- Graffiti: about 4.5 days
- Street Repair: about 5 days
- Dead Animal Pickup: about 12 hours

The 2,710 e-scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies for resolution. Some response times can seem unusually fast, which may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at 11:36 AM PST on February 7, 2026 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 AM PST on February 7, 2026.
