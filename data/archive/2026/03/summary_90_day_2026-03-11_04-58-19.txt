Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items lead the requests with 6536 calls, taking an average of 10.5 days to resolve. Graffiti follows with 3112 reports, averaging a 3.25-day response time. E-Scooter complaints reached 2963, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Street repair and tree maintenance saw 838 and 886 requests respectively, both averaging nearly 6 days for a response. Dead animal pickups (438 requests) and animal services (230 requests) were handled much faster, averaging about 13 and 17 hours.

Business license concerns were addressed in just 12 minutes on average. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completion. Conversely, trash and debris issues took over 9 days to resolve.
