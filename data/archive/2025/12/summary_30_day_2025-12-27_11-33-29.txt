Here's the latest 30-day snapshot of Long Beach city service requests. Over 5,000 requests were submitted by residents through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (1,914 calls), e-scooters (1,054 calls), and graffiti (997 calls).

Average city response times for closed cases varied by issue. Reports for dumped items took approximately 8 days to resolve. Graffiti removal requests were handled in about 2.3 days on average. Animal Services calls were addressed much faster, with an average response time of about 8.6 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
