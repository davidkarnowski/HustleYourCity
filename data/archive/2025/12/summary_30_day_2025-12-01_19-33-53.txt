Latest 30‑day service data for Long Beach shows over 5,000 requests across 20 categories. The highest volumes were Dumped Items (1,820 reports) and Graffiti (1,329 reports).

Graffiti response time fell to 70 hours (about 3 days), down from 155 hours three months ago. Dumped Items are now addressed in roughly 203 hours (≈8.5 days), an improvement from 371 hours previously. Tree Maintenance requests are closed in about 12 hours on average. Light and Sidewalk repairs are typically resolved within 1‑2 days. Traffic Signal issues are being fixed in about 24 hours, a big gain from 69 hours three months ago. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports still take about 2 days.

Overall, response times are trending faster across most services. Residents can continue to report issues via the Go Long Beach app for quicker handling.
