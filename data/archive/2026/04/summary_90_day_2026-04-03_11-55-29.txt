Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day city service request data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items lead the request volume with 7,040 calls in the last 90 days. The average response time for these issues is about 9 days.

Graffiti removal saw 3,112 requests, with an average response time of 59 hours.

Street Repair received 828 reports, taking an average of 6.3 days to address.

Business License and Permit Concerns show an unusually fast average response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that extremely low response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
