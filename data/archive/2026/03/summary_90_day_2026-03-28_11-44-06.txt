The latest city service response data for the past 90 days has been reviewed for the Hustle Long Beach! community. Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues. There were 6921 reports for dumped items, which took an average of about 9.5 days to address. Graffiti had 3116 reports, with an average response time of about 65 hours.

Street repair saw 864 requests with an average response time of about 6.4 days. Tree maintenance had 945 calls, taking roughly 5.6 days on average to resolve. Electric scooter complaints totaled 2831, though all were referred to outside agencies, leaving no average response time recorded by the city.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead animal pickups were also handled quickly, averaging about 11.6 hours across 441 reports.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps track these statistics and ensures neighborhood maintenance is documented.
