Dumped items continue to dominate city service requests with 514 reports this past week. Graffiti reports followed with 232 calls, averaging a 26.5-hour response time.

Keep in mind that unusually fast response times, especially those under 90 minutes, may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

E-scooter complaints accounted for 121 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Street and sidewalk repairs saw 74 combined reports, with response times averaging between 18 and 26 hours.
