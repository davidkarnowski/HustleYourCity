The latest review of city service data focuses on request totals and response times over the last 90 days. The highest volume of service requests during this period was for Dumped Items, totaling 6,676 reports. The average response time for these issues was approximately 12 days.

Graffiti reports were the second most common request type with 3,121 total entries. The data shows an average response time of roughly 3.5 days for graffiti removal. Street Repair requests, which include pothole fixes, totaled 833 with an average completion time of about 5 days.

Some categories showed significantly faster administrative response times. Dead Animal Pickup requests averaged nearly 13 hours to close. Concerns regarding Business Licenses averaged 12 minutes, though unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than physical work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting local issues through the Go Long Beach service application.
