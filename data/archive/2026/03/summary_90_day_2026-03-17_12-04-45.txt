Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data, focusing on the last 90 days of reports. Here is a look at how Long Beach is handling community concerns.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,678 reports. The city averaged a 10-day response time to address these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue, with 3,088 calls and an average response time of 3 days.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which saw 832 reports and a 6-day average response time, and Tree Maintenance, with 911 reports taking nearly 1 week to resolve.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnarounds. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickups were handled efficiently, averaging a 12-hour response across 442 reports.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Download and use the Go Long Beach service application to submit requests directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 17, 2026, 5:00 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 17, 2026, 4:00 AM PDT.
