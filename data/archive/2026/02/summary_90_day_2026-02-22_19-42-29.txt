The latest review of City of Long Beach service requests highlights performance data over the past 90 days.

Dumped Items represent the highest volume of service calls with 6,448 total reports. The average response time for these issues is currently approximately 13 days.

Graffiti removal requests totaled 3,081, with an average completion time of just under 4 days. Street Repair reports, which include potholes, totaled 796 and are being resolved in approximately 4 and a half days on average.

Dead Animal Pickup services showed faster turnaround times, averaging 11.6 hours per response across 426 requests. E-Scooter reports remained high with 2,955 total entries, nearly all of which were referred to external operators for resolution.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
