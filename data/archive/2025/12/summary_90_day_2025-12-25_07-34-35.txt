Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days.

Over 15,500 service requests were submitted. The most common reports were for Dumped Items, with over 6,000 requests, and Graffiti, with nearly 3,700 requests.

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report was about 15 days. For "Graffiti" reports, the average response time was about 5 and a half days. Park Maintenance requests took an average of about 17 days to close. Faster responses included Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged about 16 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
