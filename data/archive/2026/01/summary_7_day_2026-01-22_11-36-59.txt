Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted over 1,300 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (512), E-Scooters (256), and Graffiti (223).

Average response times to close a case varied. Dead Animal Pickups were handled the fastest, in about 5 hours. Graffiti removal requests were closed in an average of 32 hours. Requests for Dumped Items took about 3.5 days, while Trash and Debris cleanups took nearly 4 days.

All 256 E-Scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies for handling, not city staff.
