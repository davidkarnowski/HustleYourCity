Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service response data for the last 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for illegally dumped items (nearly 6,000 reports) and graffiti (over 3,400 reports). The average response time to close a dumped item report was about 13 days, while graffiti reports were addressed in an average of just under 5 days.

Response times varied across other services. Reports for dead animal pickups and animal services were typically handled in under 16 hours. In contrast, the average time to resolve a marina repair request was about one month.
