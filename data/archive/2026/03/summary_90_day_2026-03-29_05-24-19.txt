Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data to keep citizens informed on how long it takes for local issues to be addressed. Looking at the last 90 days, here is a factual snapshot of city response times.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,905 reports. On average, these took 9.4 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,116 calls, averaging a 64-hour response time. Street Repair saw 858 reports, taking an average of 6.4 days to address.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickup also saw quick action, averaging 11.5 hours across 433 reports.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application. Consistent reporting helps hold the city accountable and keeps Long Beach clean.
