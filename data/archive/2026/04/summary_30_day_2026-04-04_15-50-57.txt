Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Dumped items remain the most reported issue by residents, with 2,313 total calls. The city closed 1,372 of these requests, with an average response time of about 8 days.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service, totaling 967 reports. The city responded to these in an average of 33 hours, successfully closing 744 cases. E-scooter complaints followed closely with 932 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies for handling.

For faster services, dead animal pickups averaged a 5-hour response time across 163 reports. Animal services also saw quick turnarounds, averaging about 8 hours for 77 calls. On the longer end, street repair requests took an average of 3.5 days to address, and illegal firework reports averaged nearly 5 days. Keep in mind that unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city. You can submit and track service requests using the Go Long Beach service application on your mobile device.
