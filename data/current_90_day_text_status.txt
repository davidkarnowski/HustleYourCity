The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city received 7,722 reports regarding dumped items, with an average response time of approximately 16 days. Graffiti removal remains a high-volume service, with 2,590 reports and an average response time of 67 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 1,057 reports in the last 90 days, averaging about 4 days per response. Meanwhile, animal services continue to show efficient turnaround times, with 238 reports in the last 90 days averaging 11 hours per response.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
