Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day city service response data to keep citizens informed on how quickly issues are being addressed.

Over the past month, "Dumped Items" received the highest volume of calls with 2,135 reports. The city averaged a 5-day response time for these requests. "Graffiti" was the second most reported issue with 1,052 calls, taking an average of 44 hours to process.

Other notable service times include "Street Repair", which saw 263 requests and averaged a response time of over 3 days. "Tree Maintenance" had 484 reports with an average response time of 53 hours.

Some categories showed unusually fast turnaround times. For example, "Report Business Licenses/Permit Concerns" averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Hustle Long Beach! encourages all followers to use the "Go Long Beach" service application to report issues directly to the city.
