The latest data on city service requests in Long Beach shows a high volume of activity over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue with 7,707 total requests, followed by 3,232 reports for graffiti and 3,048 reports regarding e-scooters.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service to address, with an average response time of approximately 5 minutes. In contrast, dumped items take an average of about 12 days to resolve. Graffiti reports are currently being addressed in an average of 4 days.

Please note that unusually low response times for some categories may reflect administrative closures or automated referrals rather than completed field work. 

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach mobile application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and directed to the appropriate departments for action.
