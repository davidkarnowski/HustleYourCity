Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past seven days. Here is a look at how service requests are being handled across the city.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 557 calls in the last week and an average response time of 58.8 hours. Graffiti followed with 219 reports, taking an average of 21 hours to process. E-Scooter complaints reached 218, though response times for these are not tracked in the same way.

Tree maintenance saw 81 reports with a 20.1-hour average response time. Dead animal pickups averaged 8.4 hours across 41 reports. Traffic signal issues were addressed the fastest, averaging just 2.5 hours for the 6 reported cases. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps ensure that local infrastructure and neighborhoods receive the attention they need.
