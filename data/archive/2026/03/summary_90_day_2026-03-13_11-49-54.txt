Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data from the last 90 days. Here is a factual look at how Long Beach is handling community reports.

Dumped Items received the highest call volume with 6,584 reports. These issues took an average of 10 days to resolve. Graffiti removal was the second most reported issue with 3,086 calls, taking an average of 3 days for a response.

Some services saw much faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup requests, totaling 437 calls, were handled in just over 13 hours on average. Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

On the other end of the spectrum, Marina Repairs had the longest wait times. The 6 reported issues took nearly 4 weeks on average to address. Street Repair and Park Maintenance both averaged about 6 days for their respective 836 and 204 reports.
