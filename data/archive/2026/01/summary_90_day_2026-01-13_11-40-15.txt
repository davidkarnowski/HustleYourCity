Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on public data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted nearly 16,000 service requests to the city.

The most common reports were for dumped items (5,975 requests) and graffiti (3,484 requests). The average response time to close a dumped item report was about 13 days, while graffiti reports were closed in just under 5 days on average.

Response times varied significantly by issue type. Marina repair requests took the longest, averaging about one month to close. In contrast, requests for animal services and dead animal pickup were typically resolved in about 15 hours. Some issues show unusually fast closure times, which may indicate an administrative update rather than a physical work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
