Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest Long Beach city service data. Here is a look at how the city handled service requests over the last 90 days.

Dumped items were the most reported issue, with 7,003 requests. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these calls. Graffiti was the second most common issue, with 3,255 reports and an average response time of 55 hours. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume, totaling 2,918 reports.

Response times vary significantly by category. Dead animal pickups, which totaled 466 reports, were handled in about 11 hours. Tree maintenance took an average of 7.6 days for 899 reports, while street repairs averaged 7.1 days across 731 requests. 

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a 12-minute response. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
