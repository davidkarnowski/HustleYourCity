The latest data on city service requests in Long Beach shows varied response times over the last 90 days.

Animal services and dead animal pickups remain the fastest to resolve, with average response times of approximately 12 hours and 6 hours respectively. Graffiti removal has also been efficient, averaging about 3 days for completion.

In contrast, some services are seeing longer wait times. Dumped items, which saw over 8,000 reports in the last 90 days, averaged about 17 days for resolution. Tree maintenance and sidewalk repairs are currently averaging roughly 4 to 5 days. Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach mobile application to report issues. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and directed to the appropriate departments for action.
