Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Over the past 90 days, citizens submitted thousands of requests, and the numbers show how the city is handling the volume.

Dumped Items generated the most service tickets with 6,903 reports. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,094 calls and an average response time of 62 hours.

E-Scooter complaints remained high with 2,875 reports, though all of these were immediately referred to outside operators rather than handled directly by city staff.

Some categories showed incredibly fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative ticket closures rather than actual work completions.

Accountability starts with transparency. Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues, such as potholes, graffiti, and dumped items, by downloading and using the Go Long Beach service application.
