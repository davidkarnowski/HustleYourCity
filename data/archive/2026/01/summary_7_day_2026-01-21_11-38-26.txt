Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 514 new requests, followed by e-scooter issues (251) and graffiti (211).

The average response time to close illegal firework reports was about 3.7 days. Dumped item requests took an average of 3.2 days to close. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups (6.5 hours) and street repairs (about 26 hours).

It is important to note that some very fast response times can be due to administrative closures and may not always represent the time for work to be completed.
