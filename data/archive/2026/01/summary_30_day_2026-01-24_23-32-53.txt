Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

In the past month, residents submitted nearly 6,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,307 requests), e-scooters (971 requests), and graffiti (953 requests).

Based on the data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.8 days. Street repair and street light issues were addressed in about 3 days on average. Requests to remove dumped items took longer, averaging about 7.7 days to resolve.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
