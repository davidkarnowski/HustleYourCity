Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days. Citizens submitted over 5,200 service requests during this period.

The most common issues reported were illegally dumped items (2,057 requests), graffiti (1,002 requests), and e-scooter issues (987 requests).

Average response times for closed cases varied. Reports for dumped items took about 10 days to resolve. Graffiti removal averaged about 55 hours, while animal services calls were handled in under 9 hours.

Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
