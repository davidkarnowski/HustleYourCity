Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,500 service requests were filed through the Go Long Beach system in the last seven days. Reports for illegally dumped items were the most common issue with 629 requests, followed by graffiti with 285 reports.

The average close time for graffiti reports was about 1 day and 7 hours. For dumped items, the average response time was just under 3 days. Reports of running water took longer to resolve, with an average close time of nearly 6 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city.
