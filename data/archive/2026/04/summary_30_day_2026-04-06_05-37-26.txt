Hustle Long Beach! is back with a 30-day accountability check on Long Beach city services. Here is a look at the volume and response times for community reports over the past month.

Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 2280 reports. The average response time was 8.3 days.

Graffiti removal had 975 reports, with crews responding in an average of 32 hours.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 933, though all were referred to outside agencies resulting in no measurable city response time.

Tree Maintenance received 279 reports, with an average response time of 33 hours.
