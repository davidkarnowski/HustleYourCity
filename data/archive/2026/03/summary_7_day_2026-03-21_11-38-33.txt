Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service call data to keep you informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling community requests. Here is a look at the numbers over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 545 total reports. The average response time was approximately 60 hours, though 397 of these requests are still marked as new.

Graffiti reports followed closely with 220 calls, averaging a 22-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints totaled 126, all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies.

For faster turnarounds, Dead Animal Pickup handled 37 calls with an average response time of 3.5 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may occasionally be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

On the longer end, Marina Repair requests took an average of 90 hours, or nearly 4 days, to process.

If you spot an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
