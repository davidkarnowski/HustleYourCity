Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 90 days. This summary highlights the most frequent requests from residents and the city's average time to resolve them.

Over the last 90 days, the most reported issues were for dumped items (6,157 requests) and graffiti (3,339 requests). E-scooter reports were also high at 2,667, though these are typically referred directly to the scooter companies.

The average time to close a service request varied by issue. Reports for dumped items were resolved in about 13 days, while graffiti removal took approximately 5 days. Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 13 hours. Slower responses included marina repairs, which averaged about 5 weeks.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
