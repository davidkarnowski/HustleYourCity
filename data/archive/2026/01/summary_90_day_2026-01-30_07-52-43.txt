Long Beach Service Response Update

In the last 90 days, residents made over 16,000 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items, with 6,279 requests, followed by graffiti, with 3,348 requests.

Average city response times to close a request varied by service type. For dumped items, the average was about 13 days, while graffiti cleanup averaged about 5 days. Animal service requests were handled much faster, averaging about 14 hours to close. Longer-term repairs, like for damaged drinking fountains, took an average of 4 weeks.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach app.

This data summary was updated at January 29, 2026, at 11:49 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 29, 2026, at 11:00 PM PST.
