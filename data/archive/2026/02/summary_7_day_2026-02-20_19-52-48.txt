Hustle Long Beach! Community Data Update

Over the last 7 days, the city received 422 reports for Dumped Items, with an average response time of roughly 2 days. Graffiti reports totaled 253, also averaging a response time of about 2 days.

Tree Maintenance saw 202 requests with a response time of approximately 1 day. Dead Animal Pickup services responded to 39 calls with an average time of 5.5 hours.

Reports regarding Business Licenses and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

We encourage all residents to report issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
