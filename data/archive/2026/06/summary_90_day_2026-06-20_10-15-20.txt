The latest city service data for Long Beach shows a focus on high-volume requests over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 6,639 requests for dumped items and 3,341 requests for graffiti removal.

Response times for these services have varied. Graffiti removal averaged approximately 2.6 days, while dumped item removal took an average of about 8.1 days.
