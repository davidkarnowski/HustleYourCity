Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day city service data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports and service response times.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items led the service requests with 2,328 reports. The city took an average of 6.4 days to respond to these cases. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,018 calls, averaging a 36-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints remained high with 871 reports, all of which were referred to outside vendors.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which saw 250 requests with an average response time of 69 hours. Dead Animal Pickup handled 149 calls, averaging a 9-hour turnaround. Park Maintenance received 72 requests, taking an average of 4 days to resolve. Please note that unusually low response times across some categories may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
