The latest data for city service requests in Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city received 6,842 reports for dumped items and 3,169 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. For the last 90 days, graffiti reports took an average of 2.4 days to resolve, while dumped items took an average of 8.5 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to see faster turnaround times, with average responses of 14.3 hours and 9.3 hours respectively.

Please note that unusually low response times, such as those seen in the last 24 hours for certain categories, may reflect administrative closures or system updates rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
