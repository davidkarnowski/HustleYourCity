Here is a look at Long Beach service requests from the past week. Residents submitted nearly 1,500 requests through the Go Long Beach app and other channels.

The most common issue reported was illegally dumped items, with 575 requests. The average response time to close these reports was about 45 hours. The second most common was graffiti, with 248 reports and an average response time of about 20 hours.

There were also 229 reports for e-scooters, which are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling. Street repair requests totaled 110, with an average response time of about 14 hours.

It is important to note that some response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
