Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days.

The most common reports from the public were for Dumped Items (6,028 requests), Graffiti (3,397 requests), and E-Scooters (2,724 requests).

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. Graffiti removal took about 4 to 5 days. Reports of illegally dumped items averaged a response time of about 12 and a half days. Longer-term issues like Marina Repair averaged nearly 25 days to close.

Quicker responses were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services, both averaging under 16 hours. Some categories show unusually fast resolutions, like Damaged Drinking Fountains (about 2 hours), which may indicate administrative closures rather than completed physical repairs.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
