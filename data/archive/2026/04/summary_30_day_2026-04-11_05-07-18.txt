Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest third-party accountability update regarding City of Long Beach service response times. This summary focuses on data from the last 30 days to highlight recent trends in city services.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 2,341 reports. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,082 calls, showing an average response time of 37 hours. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 955 reports, all of which were closed and referred to other agencies.

Other notable service areas include Tree Maintenance, which received 253 requests and took an average of 3.6 days to address. Street Repair saw 200 reports with an average response time of 3.4 days. Faster turnarounds were seen in Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 7 hours across 166 reports, and Animal Services, which averaged 8.6 hours for 78 reports. 

Please note that unusually low response times may occasionally be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. 

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps maintain accurate data and holds city departments accountable.
