The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, residents submitted 7,612 requests for dumped items and 2,557 reports regarding graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of 7.2 hours. Conversely, dumped items have seen an average response time of approximately 17 days. Please note that some categories show very low response times, which may indicate administrative closures rather than completed field work.

We encourage all residents to continue using the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. This tool remains the most direct way to notify the city of maintenance needs in our neighborhoods.
