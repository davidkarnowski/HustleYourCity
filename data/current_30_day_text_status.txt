Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,000 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (1,987 requests), Graffiti (1,147 requests), and E-Scooters (871 requests).

According to the data, the average response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was approximately 7 days. For "Graffiti" reports, the average resolution time was about 2.5 days. "Street Repair" requests were completed in an average of about 4 days. Some services were handled more quickly, with "Dead Animal Pickup" requests being resolved in under 9 hours on average.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
