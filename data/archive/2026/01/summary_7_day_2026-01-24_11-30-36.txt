Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times for the past week.

Residents submitted 599 reports for illegally dumped items, the most frequent request. The average response time to close these reports was about 3 days. Graffiti was the next most common issue with 245 reports, which were addressed in about 1 day on average.

Response times for dead animal pickups averaged about 5.6 hours, based on 43 service calls. Street repair requests, totaling 61, had an average completion time of just under 2 days.
