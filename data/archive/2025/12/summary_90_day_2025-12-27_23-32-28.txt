Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Over this period, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (5,956 requests), graffiti (3,677 requests), and e-scooters (2,417 requests).

Average response times to close a service request varied by issue. Reports for dumped items were addressed in about 15 days, while graffiti removal took an average of about 5 and a half days. Street repairs were completed in an average of 10 days. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 16 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 27, 2025, at 3:29 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 27, 2025, at 3:00 PM PST.
