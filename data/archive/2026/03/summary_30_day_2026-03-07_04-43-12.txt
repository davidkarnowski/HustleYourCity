Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 30 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 2,131 reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 5.1 days. Graffiti reports totaled 1,093, with an average resolution time of 45.4 hours.

Street Repair requests, which totaled 265, are taking approximately 3.1 days to complete. Trash and Debris reports averaged 3.6 days for resolution. Tree Maintenance received 442 reports with an average response time of 42.7 hours.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup, averaging 14.2 hours, and Abandoned Shopping Carts, averaging 15.8 hours. E-Scooter reports totaled 968, though these are referred to third-party operators.
