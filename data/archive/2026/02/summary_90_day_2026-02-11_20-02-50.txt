Hustle Long Beach community update on city service data. An evaluation of the last 90 days reveals that Dumped Items remains the most frequent service request with 6,403 total reports. The average response time for these issues is currently approximately 2 weeks.

Graffiti removal requests totaled 3,165 during this period, with an average completion time of roughly 4 days. Street Repair requests, totaling 793, show an average response time of about 5 days.

Faster responses were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup, averaging 11.5 hours. Business License concerns showed an average response of 31 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
