Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past month, the most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items, with 1,919 requests, and graffiti, with 1,074 requests. The city also received 990 reports for misplaced e-scooters, which are referred directly to the scooter companies.

Average response times for key services varied. Dumped item cleanups were completed in about 6 days on average. Graffiti removal took an average of 62 hours. Street repair requests were addressed in an average of about 3.5 days. Faster response times were recorded for dead animal pickups, averaging about 12 hours.

You can help improve our neighborhoods by reporting issues you see. The best way to make a report is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at Dec 21, 2025, 11:25 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at Dec 21, 2025, 11:00 AM PST.
