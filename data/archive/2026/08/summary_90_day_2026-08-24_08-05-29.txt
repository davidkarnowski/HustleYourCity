The latest service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across city departments over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 7,923 total reports and an average response time of approximately 13 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service, with 3,169 reports and an average response time of about 3.7 days.

Response times for several categories have improved recently. Animal services and dead animal pickup are currently showing some of the fastest turnaround times, averaging 11.3 hours and 5.5 hours respectively over the last 90 days. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
