The latest service call data for the last 30 days highlights volume and response times for key city services in Long Beach.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2,220 total calls. The average response time for these requests is currently 7.2 days. Graffiti reports were the second most frequent with 1,110 total calls, showing an average response time of 51 hours.

Street Repair generated 239 reports and shows an average completion time of 3 days. Trash and Debris reports are taking approximately 4.3 days to resolve. Conversely, Tree Maintenance reports, which totaled 360, are being addressed in an average of 26 hours. Dead Animal Pickup remains one of the fastest services, averaging 13.5 hours per response over 150 reports.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
