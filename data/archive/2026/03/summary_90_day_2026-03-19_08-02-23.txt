Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 90-day city service request data to keep the public informed on response times and call volumes.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 6,655 reports. The city took an average of nearly 10 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal followed with 3,082 reports, averaging a response time of 69 hours. E-Scooter complaints generated 2,808 reports, though specific response times are not tracked in the system.

Other significant categories include tree maintenance with 915 requests and street repairs with 826 requests. Both of these services averaged a response time of nearly 7 days. Dead animal pickups accounted for 429 calls, taking an average of 12 hours to resolve.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help track local service efficiency.

This data summary was updated at March 19, 2026, 12:57 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 19, 2026, 12:00 AM PDT.
