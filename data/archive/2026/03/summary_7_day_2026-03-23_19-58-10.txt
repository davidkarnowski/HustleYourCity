Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days to keep citizens informed on how local requests are being handled.

Dumped Items led the service requests this week with 500 reports, though 404 of those currently remain in the "New" status. E-Scooter complaints followed with 229 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti issues generated 208 calls.

When looking at response times, the city averaged about 25 hours to respond to Graffiti reports. Dumped Items averaged a response time of nearly 35 hours. Dead Animal Pickup saw some of the fastest turnarounds, averaging just under 4 hours. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.
