The latest service request data for the City of Long Beach highlights response times and call volumes over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2,377 total entries. The average response time for these requests is currently 184 hours, or approximately one week.

Graffiti abatement recorded 1,144 reports. The average time to close these requests was 52 hours, or just over 2 days. Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 244 and saw an average response time of about 3 days.

Faster responses were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup, averaging under 9 hours across 144 reports. Sidewalk Repair requests were addressed in approximately 24 hours on average.
