Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (2,052 reports), graffiti (905 reports), and e-scooter problems (895 reports). The average time to resolve a dumped item report was just under 5 days. For graffiti, the average response time was about 2 days.

Response times for some other services were longer. The average for traffic signal repairs was over 5 days, and trash and debris cleanups took nearly a week. Quicker responses included dead animal pickups and vegetation maintenance, which were resolved in about 15 hours on average.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it through the Go Long Beach app. Your reports are critical for tracking and addressing problems in the city.
