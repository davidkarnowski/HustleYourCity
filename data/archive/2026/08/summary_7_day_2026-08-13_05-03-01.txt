The latest data from the Go Long Beach service application shows a high volume of requests over the past 7 days. Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue, with 763 total reports, followed by 246 reports for graffiti and 73 for tree maintenance.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Dead animal pickups were addressed quickly, averaging about 5 hours. Conversely, light maintenance requests averaged approximately 81 hours, or 3.4 days. Please note that unusually low response times for some categories may reflect administrative processing rather than the completion of field work.

Residents are encouraged to continue using the Go Long Beach mobile application to report neighborhood issues. This tool remains the most effective way to notify the city of service needs and track progress.
