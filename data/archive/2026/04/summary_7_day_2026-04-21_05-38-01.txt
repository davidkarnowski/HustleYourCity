Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of service requests with 546 reports, taking an average of 3.5 days to address. E-scooter complaints followed with 224 reports, and graffiti issues saw 191 reports with an average response time of 24.6 hours.

Trash and debris removal took the longest to resolve, averaging 4.2 days for 14 requests. Conversely, dead animal pickups averaged a 2.4-hour response across 40 calls, and animal services averaged 5.6 hours for 18 calls. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
