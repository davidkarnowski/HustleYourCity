Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day city service request data to keep residents informed on response times and service volumes.

Dumped items continue to generate the highest volume of requests. Over the last 90 days, citizens submitted 6,483 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of nearly 11 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,098 requests, taking an average of over 3 days to resolve.

Other notable service categories include street repair, which saw 840 requests and an average response time of about 5 and a half days. Tree maintenance received 870 requests, taking nearly 6 days on average. E-scooter complaints totaled 2,929, though all of these were referred to outside agencies rather than handled directly by city staff.

Some categories show unusually fast resolution times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to report neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application. Consistent reporting helps track performance and ensures issues are documented.

This data summary was updated at March 8, 2026, 10:02 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 8, 2026, 8:00 PM PDT.
