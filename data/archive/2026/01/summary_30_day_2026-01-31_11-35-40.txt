Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past month, the most frequent reports from residents were for illegally dumped items (2,422 calls), e-scooter issues (1,041 calls), and graffiti (1,022 calls).

Average response times to close a service request varied. Reports for illegally dumped items took an average of 9 days to resolve. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 2.5 days on average, while street repairs were completed in just under 3 days. Dead animal pickups were handled in an average of about 11 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application to report it directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at 3:32 AM PST, January 31, 2026 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 3:00 AM PST, January 31, 2026.
