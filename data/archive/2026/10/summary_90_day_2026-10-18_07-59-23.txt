The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 8,174 requests for dumped items, with an average response time of approximately 15 days. Graffiti removal remains a high-volume service, with 2,788 reports and an average response time of about 2.7 days.
