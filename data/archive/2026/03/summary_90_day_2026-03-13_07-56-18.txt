Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports and where the highest demand for services is right now.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue in the city, with 6,581 requests submitted over the last 90 days. The city takes an average of 10 days to respond to these calls. Graffiti removal is the second highest category with 3,085 reports, averaging a response time of 3 days.

Infrastructure and maintenance also saw high call volumes. Street repair generated 836 requests with an average response time of 6 days, while tree maintenance saw 890 reports averaging nearly 6 days. Meanwhile, dead animal pickups are handled much faster, averaging 13 hours across 437 reports.

Some categories show unusually fast response times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps track performance and ensures neighborhoods get the attention they need.
