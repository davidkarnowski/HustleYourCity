Here is a look at the latest Long Beach service request data from the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,600 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,952 calls), graffiti (3,658 calls), and e-scooters (2,416 calls).

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 5 days. For dumped items, the average response time was about 15 days. Requests for dead animal pickups were handled more quickly, averaging about 16 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 27, 2025 at 11:29 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 27, 2025 at 11:00 PM PST.
