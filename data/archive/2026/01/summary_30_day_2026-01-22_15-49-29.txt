Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the last month, the most reported issue was "Dumped Items," with 2,122 requests. The average time to resolve these reports was about 7.7 days. The next most common reports were for "E-Scooter" issues (907) and "Graffiti" (893). The average response time for Graffiti removal was about 3.1 days, while E-Scooter reports are referred directly to vendors.

Response times varied across different service types. The 146 reports for "Dead Animal Pickup" were handled in an average of about 11.7 hours. In contrast, the 35 reports of illegal fireworks took an average of 5.3 days to close.

To report issues like dumped items, graffiti, or broken streetlights, use the Go Long Beach service application on your phone.
