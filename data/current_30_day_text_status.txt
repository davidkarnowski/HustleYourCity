Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 30 days.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 1,986 requests, followed by graffiti with 1,154 reports. The city also received 873 reports regarding e-scooters.

Average response times varied across services. The city took about 7 days to resolve reports for dumped items and about 4 days for street repairs. Graffiti removal averaged about 2.5 days. The quickest responses were for dead animal pickup and animal services, which were handled in about 10 hours on average.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
