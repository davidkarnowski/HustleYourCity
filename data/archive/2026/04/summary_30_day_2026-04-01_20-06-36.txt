Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2,300 calls in the last month. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most common issue with 952 reports, which the city addressed in an average of 37 hours.

Street repair saw 230 requests, taking about 3.5 days on average to resolve. Dead animal pickups were handled relatively quickly, with 165 reports averaging a 6.2-hour response time.

There were also 844 e-scooter complaints, but all were immediately referred and closed without a tracked response time. Unusually low or untracked response times like this often indicate administrative closures rather than actual field work completions.
