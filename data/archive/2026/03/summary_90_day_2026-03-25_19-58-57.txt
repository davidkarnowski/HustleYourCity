Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest service request data from the City of Long Beach. Over the past 90 days, citizens submitted thousands of reports, and the data shows varying response times across different departments.

Dumped items generated the highest volume with 6,836 requests. The city took an average of 9.5 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti reports followed with 3,093 requests, averaging a 66.8-hour response time.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw high activity. The city received 1,033 lighting requests, averaging 3.3 days for a response. Street repairs totaled 860 requests with an average response time of 6.6 days, while 954 tree maintenance reports averaged 6.3 days.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Public reporting is essential for tracking city performance. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues and hold local agencies accountable.
