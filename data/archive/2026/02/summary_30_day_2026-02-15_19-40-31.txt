The latest service request data for Long Beach covering the past 30 days highlights significant activity in waste and infrastructure management.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 2,416 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 177 hours, or roughly one week.

Graffiti removal teams handled 1,124 reports with an average completion time of 54 hours, or just over 2 days. Street Repair requests totaled 226, with crews taking approximately 3.6 days to resolve issues.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 8 hours across 151 requests. Tree Maintenance also showed quick turnaround times, averaging just over 1 day per request.

Please note that E-Scooter reports are marked as 'Closed Referred', meaning they are passed to private operators rather than handled directly by city crews.

Residents are encouraged to report issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
