Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service call data from the City of Long Beach. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of requests across various categories. This summary tracks the volume of those calls and how quickly the city is responding to them.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 6,728 reports. The average response time for these requests was 9.7 days. Graffiti removal was the second most reported issue with 3,064 calls and an average response time of 71 hours.

Other significant categories include Street Repair, which had 836 requests and took an average of 6.5 days to address, and Tree Maintenance, which saw 945 calls with an average response time of 6.7 days.

Data for Business Licenses and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps maintain accurate data and holds local agencies accountable.
