In the past 90 days Long Beach residents filed about 15,800 service requests. The biggest volumes were Dumped Items (6,184 reports) and Graffiti (3,694 reports). Animal Services (199) and Dead Animal Pickup (429) also saw steady activity.

Average response times this quarter show mixed results. Graffiti requests were closed in roughly 160 hours (about 6.7 days), a notable improvement from the all‑time average of 353 hours. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were resolved quickly, averaging 17 hours (under a day). Dumped Items took longer, averaging 369 hours (about 15 days). E‑Scooter reports were mostly marked “Closed Referred” with no response time recorded.

Residents can continue to report issues through the Go Long Beach app for faster city action. This data summary was updated at 2025‑12‑10 23:33:00 PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 2025‑12‑10 23:00:32 PST.
