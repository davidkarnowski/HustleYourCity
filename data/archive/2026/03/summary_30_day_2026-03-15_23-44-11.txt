Hustle Long Beach! is sharing the latest 30-day city service call data to keep residents informed on request volumes and response times.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 2099 calls. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti reports were the second most common with 1046 calls, averaging a 43-hour response time. 

E-Scooter complaints totaled 945, but all were immediately closed and referred to outside agencies. Street repairs saw 260 requests, taking an average of 3 days to address. 

Some categories showed exceptionally fast response times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. On the other end of the spectrum, Marina Repairs took an average of 7.5 days to resolve.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help improve community maintenance.

This data summary was updated at March 15, 2026, 4:40 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 15, 2026, 4:00 PM PDT.
