In the past week Long Beach recorded 1,427 service requests across all categories.

Graffiti was the most common issue (367 reports) with 224 closed and an average response time of about 37 hours. Dumped Items generated 542 calls; 62 were closed, 170 are still in progress, and the average response time was 70 hours. Tree Maintenance and Street Repair showed the fastest turn‑around, averaging 8.6 hours and 11.9 hours respectively.

Overall, most requests were addressed within one to two days, and many categories (e.g., Light, Sidewalk Repair, Vegetation) had average response times under 30 hours. E‑Scooter reports were almost entirely “Closed Referred” (225 of 226), reflecting the city’s referral workflow.

Residents can help keep the city accountable by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service app.
