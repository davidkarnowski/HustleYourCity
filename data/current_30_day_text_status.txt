Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service response data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,975), graffiti (1,129), and e-scooter issues (908).

According to the city's data, the average response time for graffiti removal was just under 3 days. Reports of illegally dumped items were addressed in about 6 days on average, while street repairs took around 4 days.

Faster response times were recorded for more urgent issues, with Animal Services requests closed in an average of 11 hours and Dead Animal Pickup in 13 hours. Note that some fast closure times may be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 19, 2025, at 3:29 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 18, 2025, at 11:00 PM PST.
