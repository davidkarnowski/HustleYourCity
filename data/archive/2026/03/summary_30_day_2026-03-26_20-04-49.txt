Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data to keep you informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling community issues.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 2,303 reports. The city took an average of 6.8 days to respond to these calls. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 989 calls, showing an average response time of 34.6 hours. E-Scooter complaints also remained high, totaling 862 reports.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which saw 255 requests and an average response time of 3.1 days. Dead Animal Pickup handled 154 calls with an 8.9-hour average turnaround. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
