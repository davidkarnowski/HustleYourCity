Here is an independent review of City of Long Beach service request data for the past 90 days.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,460 total reports. The average response time to close these requests is currently approximately 13 and a half days.

Graffiti removal generated 3,169 reports. The average time to resolve these issues is roughly 4 days.

Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 782 reports. The data shows an average completion time of 5 days.

Dead Animal Pickup services handled 413 calls with an average response time of 11 hours.

Report Business Licenses and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of 31 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than physical work completion.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
