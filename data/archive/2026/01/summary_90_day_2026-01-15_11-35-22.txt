Here is the latest look at Long Beach's service request data for the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, residents submitted nearly 16,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,025 requests), graffiti (3,455 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,664 requests).

Response times varied by the type of service needed. On average, requests for dumped items were closed in about 13 days, while graffiti removal took about 4.5 days. Some of the fastest responses were for animal services and dead animal pickup, which were handled in about 15-16 hours. Reports of damaged drinking fountains were closed in about 2 hours, though this unusually fast time may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.
