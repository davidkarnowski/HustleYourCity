The latest 90‑day service data shows a high volume of reports and varied response times across city services.

Dumped Items generated the most calls (6,168) with an average response of about 376 hours (≈ 15.7 days). Graffiti also remains a major issue, with 3,700 reports and an average response of 169 hours (≈ 7 days). Both categories have a large share still in progress (735 Dumped Items, 288 Graffiti).

Light repairs and Sidewalk Repair are averaging 180 hours (≈ 7.5 days) and 79 hours (≈ 3.3 days) respectively. Other categories such as Traffic Signals (≈ 9 days) and Park Maintenance (≈ 18 days) show longer timelines, often reflecting administrative processing.

Residents are encouraged to report issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app, helping the city track and address problems faster.
