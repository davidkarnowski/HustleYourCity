Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, the most frequent requests from residents were for dumped items (over 6,000 reports) and graffiti removal (over 3,400 reports). The city also received over 2,600 reports about e-scooters, which are typically referred directly to the scooter companies.

Average response times for major services vary. The average time to close a graffiti report was just under 5 days. For dumped items, the average was about 13 days. Street repair requests were closed in about 6 and a half days on average. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 16 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at January 14, 2026 at 8:10 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 14, 2026 at 7:00 PM PST.
