Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data. Over the last 90 days, citizens submitted thousands of reports, and this summary tracks how long it takes the city to respond to these local issues.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,483 requests. The city took an average of 11 days to respond to these reports.

Graffiti removal saw 3,090 reports, with an average response time of just over 3 days.

Street Repair and Tree Maintenance handled 843 and 861 calls respectively, both averaging between 5 and 6 days for a response.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnarounds. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
