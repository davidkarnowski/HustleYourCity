Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped items (1,880 calls), e-scooters (967 calls), and graffiti (911 calls).

Based on city data, the average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 5.5 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 2 days on average, while street repair requests took just under 3 days. Reports of trash and debris had a longer average response time of about 8.5 days.

Quicker responses were seen for dead animal pickups and animal services, both averaging around 14 hours to close out.
