Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest government accountability data summary evaluating City of Long Beach service requests over the past 7 days.

Dumped items and graffiti generated the highest call volumes this week. Citizens submitted 554 reports for dumped items and 318 reports for graffiti. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 279 total reports.

The city averaged a response time of 65 hours for dumped items and 37 hours for graffiti. Abandoned shopping cart reports took an average of 3 days to process. Animal services showed a faster turnaround at nearly 5 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
