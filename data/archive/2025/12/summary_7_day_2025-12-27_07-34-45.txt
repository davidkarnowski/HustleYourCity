Here is a look at Long Beach service request data from the past week.

The most frequent reports were for "Dumped Items" with 307 requests, followed by "Graffiti" with 199 requests. The average city response time to close dumped item reports was about 26 hours, while graffiti reports averaged about 34 hours. There were also 186 reports for e-scooters, which are referred directly to vendors for handling.

Response times varied for other services. "Animal Services" requests were closed in an average of about 3 hours. Some unusually fast closures were noted for "Park Maintenance" (1.6 hours). These low times may be due to administrative closures and not reflect on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 26, 2025, at 11:29 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 26, 2025, at 11:00 PM PST.
