Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest service request data to keep you informed on city response times. Over the last 90 days, residents have submitted thousands of requests, and here is how the City of Long Beach has responded.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,999 reports. The city took an average of 8.5 days to resolve these issues. Graffiti removal followed with 3,243 requests, averaging a response time of 56 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests took slightly longer. Tree Maintenance saw 888 requests with an average response time of 9 days, while 708 Street Repair requests took an average of 8 days to address.

Some services saw rapid turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup averaged just under 10 hours for 469 requests. Interestingly, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
