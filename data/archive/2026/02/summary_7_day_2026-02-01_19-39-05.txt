Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,395 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (568 requests), graffiti (264 requests), and e-scooter issues (226 requests). These three categories accounted for over 75% of all new reports.

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 21 hours. For illegally dumped items, the average response was just under 2 days. Some services saw quicker responses, with reports for sidewalk repair and tree maintenance addressed in an average of 6 to 8 hours. Note: Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
