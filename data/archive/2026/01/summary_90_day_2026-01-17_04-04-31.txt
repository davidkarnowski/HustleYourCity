Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service requests from the city's public data.

Over the past three months, the most reported issues were "Dumped Items" with over 6,000 requests and "Graffiti" with over 3,400 requests. Reports for E-scooters were also high at over 2,700, though these are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" request was about 13 days. "Graffiti" reports were addressed in an average of about 4.5 days. Other notable response times included approximately 11 days for "Park Maintenance" and about 6.5 days for "Street Repair".

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at January 16, 2026 at 8:01 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 16, 2026 at 7:00 PM PST.
