Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data from the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the city is handling citizen requests.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 541 reports. The city averaged a response time of 3.4 days for these issues. E-Scooter complaints followed with 257 reports, and Graffiti issues accounted for 241 reports with an average response time of 31.7 hours.

Some services showed very fast turnaround times. Running Water reports averaged a response time of just 84 minutes across 6 calls. Traffic Signal issues averaged a 3-hour response time for 7 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Reporting problems helps track performance and ensures issues are documented.
