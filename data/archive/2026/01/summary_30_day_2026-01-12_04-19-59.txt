Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests.

Reports for illegally dumped items were the most common request, with 1,882 submissions. The average city response time for these reports was about 5.5 days. Graffiti was the next most frequent issue with 910 reports, which were addressed in about 52 hours on average.

Requests for trash and debris cleanup took longer, with an average response time of over 9 days. In contrast, the 54 requests for Animal Services were completed in about 14 hours on average.
