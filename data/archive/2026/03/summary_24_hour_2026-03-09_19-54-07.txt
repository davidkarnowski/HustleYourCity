Hustle Long Beach! continues to monitor city service data to keep the public informed. Over the past 24 hours, the City of Long Beach received nearly 200 new service requests across various departments.

The highest volume of calls came from Dumped Items with 62 reports, followed by E-Scooter issues with 46 reports, and Graffiti with 38 reports. A significant portion of these recent reports, including 57 of the Dumped Items, currently remain in the New status and are awaiting city action.

For requests that were processed, response times varied. Sidewalk Repair logged an incredibly fast average response time of 79 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times are often the result of administrative closures or referrals rather than actual completed physical labor. Meanwhile, Tree Maintenance requests averaged a 10-hour response time, and Graffiti removals averaged 13.5 hours.

Public reporting is essential for tracking government efficiency. Residents are encouraged to download and use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
