Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted thousands of requests, with the most common being for Dumped Items (6,390 reports) and Graffiti (3,280 reports). The average time to resolve a Dumped Items report was about 14 days. Graffiti reports were handled faster, with an average closure time of about 5 days.

Some of the quickest responses were for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup, which were both resolved in about 12 hours on average. In contrast, the average response for Trash and Debris removal was about 10 days.

It is important to note that some very fast response times can be the result of administrative closures, such as marking a duplicate request, and may not reflect a service being completed.
