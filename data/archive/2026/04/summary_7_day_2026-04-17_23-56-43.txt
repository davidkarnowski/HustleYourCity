Hello Hustle Long Beach community. Here is the latest third-party accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 562 calls. The average response time for these issues was 3 days. Currently, 231 of these requests are in progress and 192 are newly reported.

E-Scooter complaints were the second most frequent with 271 reports. All but one of these cases were referred to other agencies and closed.

Graffiti removal saw 196 requests, with an average response time of 25.4 hours. The city has already closed 149 of these cases.

Dead Animal Pickup handled 34 calls with an average response time of 4 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Trash and Debris reports totaled 9 calls, taking an average of 3 days for a response.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps track service efficiency and holds local government accountable.
