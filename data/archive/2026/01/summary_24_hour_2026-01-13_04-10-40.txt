Here is the latest Long Beach service data update.

In the past 24 hours, the city received 273 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (121 calls), e-scooter issues (53 calls), and graffiti (39 calls).

Response times varied by issue. Vegetation cleanup requests were addressed in an average of 19 minutes, and dumped items in about 59 minutes. These fast times may reflect administrative closures.
