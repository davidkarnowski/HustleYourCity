Hustle Long Beach! is here with the latest third-party accountability update on City of Long Beach service response times. The city service call data over the past 7 days has been analyzed to keep citizens informed on how quickly neighborhood issues are being addressed.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 523 calls in the last week. The average response time for these reports is currently 3.5 days. However, it is worth noting that 261 of these requests are still marked as new.

Graffiti removal saw 233 reports. The city has closed 199 of these cases, averaging a response time of about 1.3 days.

E-scooter complaints accounted for 264 reports, all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies.

For more urgent safety matters, traffic signal issues had 7 reports with an average response time of about 3 hours. Animal services handled 24 reports, averaging a 7.7-hour response. Dead animal pickups averaged an 8.2-hour response across 38 reports.

Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to help keep the city running smoothly. If an issue arises in the neighborhood, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at March 12, 2026, 12:52 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 12, 2026, 12:00 PM PDT.
