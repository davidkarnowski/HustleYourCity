Here is the latest 30-day service response data for the Hustle Long Beach! community. This independent summary tracks how quickly city departments are handling public requests.

Over the past month, the highest volume of service calls involved Dumped Items with 2,313 reports. This was followed by Graffiti with 1,009 reports and E-Scooter issues with 914 reports.

Response times varied significantly across different service types. Dumped Items took an average of 7.9 days to process. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 36.6 hours. Faster turnarounds were seen in Dead Animal Pickup, averaging 7.1 hours, and Animal Services at 8.9 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.
