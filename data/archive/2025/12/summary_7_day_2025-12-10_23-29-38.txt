City service updates for the past 7 days show a mixed picture of response times.

Graffiti remains the most frequent issue with 231 reports. The average response time was about 31 hours (just over a day) and 167 cases have been closed. Light outages generated 58 calls, averaging 32 hours to resolve, while sidewalk repairs (26 calls) were addressed in roughly 17 hours.

Tree maintenance and traffic signal work are seeing quicker turn‑around: 37 tree reports and 10 traffic‑signal reports were handled in about 16 hours on average. Park maintenance is especially fast – 8 requests were logged and the average response was just over 2 hours.

Animal services (11 calls) and dead‑animal pickups (35 calls) were all closed within roughly 12 hours. Dumped items continue to be a high‑volume category with 550 new reports; the average response time is about 80 hours (just over 3 days) and many remain in progress. Trash & debris (21 calls) took about 88 hours on average.

Overall, most categories are seeing responses within 1‑3 days, but the high volume of dumped‑item reports suggests a need for faster processing.
