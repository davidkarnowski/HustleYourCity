Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service request data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs. Here is a look at the numbers.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,916 reports. The city took an average of 9.5 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 3,115 reports, showing a much faster average response time of 65 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant activity. The city received 1,010 lighting requests, averaging a 3.2-day response time. Street repairs accounted for 866 calls with an average response of 6.4 days, while 946 tree maintenance requests took about 5.6 days on average.

Some categories reported extremely fast turnarounds. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or referrals rather than actual work completions.

To help improve local neighborhoods, encourage your friends and neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
