The latest data from the City of Long Beach service call system shows ongoing activity across key maintenance categories over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent service request with 6,729 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 8.5 days. Graffiti reports totaled 3,201, with an average response time of about 2.2 days. Tree maintenance saw 955 requests with an average response time of roughly 8.3 days.

Dead animal pickups continue to show the fastest turnaround, averaging about 8.8 hours over the last 90 days. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that service requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
