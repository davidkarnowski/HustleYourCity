Hustle Long Beach! is sharing the latest 30-day city service response data. This tracking keeps the public informed on how quickly the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood issues.

Over the last 30 days, Dumped Items led the service requests with 2,312 reports. Graffiti followed with 987 reports, and E-Scooter complaints reached 930.

Response times varied significantly by category. The city took an average of 8.3 days to respond to Dumped Items. Street Repair requests took about 3.2 days on average, and Illegal Firework reports averaged 4.2 days. Faster responses were seen for Graffiti removal, which averaged 32.9 hours, and Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged about 5 hours. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work.
