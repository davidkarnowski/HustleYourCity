The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows significant activity across service requests over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the highest volume request with 6,847 reports, averaging a response time of approximately 8.5 days. Graffiti reports totaled 3,165 with an average response time of 57 hours. E-Scooter reports continue to be the most frequent category, with 2,963 entries, nearly all of which are marked as closed referred.

Response times for tree maintenance have averaged about 8.6 days over the last 90 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging 14 hours and 9 minutes respectively. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
