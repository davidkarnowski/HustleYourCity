Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service call data.

In the past month, residents submitted over 4,900 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,909 calls), graffiti (1,064 calls), and e-scooter issues (1,037 calls).

Here are some of the city's average response times for closed service requests:
- Dumped Items: about 6 days
- Graffiti: about 2.5 days
- Street Repair: about 3.5 days
- Tree Maintenance: about 1.5 days
- Animal Services: about 10.5 hours

Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
