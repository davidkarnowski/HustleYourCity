Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests. In the last month, residents submitted over 5,100 requests for city services.

The most common report was for illegally dumped items, with 1,893 requests handled in an average of about 7 days. Graffiti was the next most frequent issue with 900 reports, which were addressed in about 2.3 days on average. Street repair requests were typically closed in a similar timeframe.

Response times for other common issues include about 4 days for streetlight outages and about 1.8 days for tree maintenance. Slower responses were seen for reports of trash and debris, which took an average of about 9.5 days to resolve.
