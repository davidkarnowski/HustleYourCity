Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

The most reported issues were Dumped Items with 327 reports, Graffiti with 190 reports, and E-Scooters with 179 reports.

The average response time for a Dumped Items request was about 28 hours. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 26 hours on average. Animal Services calls were among the fastest responses, averaging just over 3 hours. Some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city.
