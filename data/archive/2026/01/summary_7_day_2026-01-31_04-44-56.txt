Hustle Long Beach! City Service Data Update

Over the past week, Long Beach residents submitted 1,466 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most common reports were for dumped items (586 calls), graffiti (283 calls), and e-scooter issues (260 calls).

Here is a look at the city's average response times for some key services. Graffiti cleanup requests were addressed in about 27 hours. Reports of dumped items, the most frequent issue, took an average of 2.3 days to resolve. E-scooter reports are referred directly to vendors and do not have a city response time.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
