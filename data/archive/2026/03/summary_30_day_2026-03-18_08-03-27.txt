Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest 30-day city service response data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports and where response times currently stand.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue with 2,204 calls in the last month. The city takes an average of 5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal is the second most requested service with 1,064 reports, taking an average of 38 hours to address.

For infrastructure issues, Street Repair received 263 reports with an average response time of 3 days. Sidewalk Repair had 113 reports, taking an average of 45 hours to process. Trash and Debris reports totaled 53, taking an average of 4 days to resolve.

There are also some unusually fast turnarounds in the data. Reports regarding Business Licenses and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 3 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
