Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted thousands of service requests, with the most common reports being for illegally dumped items (1,931 requests), e-scooters (967), and graffiti (955).

For cases closed in this period, the average response time for illegally dumped items was about 6 days. Reports for trash and debris took about 10 days on average, while traffic signal issues were addressed in about one week.
