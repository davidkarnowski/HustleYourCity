Hustle Long Beach! is here with your latest 30-day accountability update on city service response times. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2275 reports over the last month. The city took an average of 5.7 days to resolve these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1016 calls, averaging a 39.9-hour response time. 

E-Scooter complaints reached 963, though all were referred to outside agencies. Street Repair saw 255 reports, taking about 3.2 days to address, while Park Maintenance handled 70 reports with an average response time of 4.3 days.

On the faster side, Running Water reports averaged a 7.9-hour response. Conversely, Illegal Firework and Marina Repair reports took over 1 week to resolve on average. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
