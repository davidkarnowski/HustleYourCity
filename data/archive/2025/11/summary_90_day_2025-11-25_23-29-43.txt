Graffiti reports remain the highest volume – 3,686 requests in the past 90 days – with an average response time of about 152 hours (≈6.3 days). That is improving; the last‑30‑day average fell to 71 hours (≈3 days).

Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup together accounted for 673 calls and were closed in roughly 16‑17 hours on average, well under a day.

Tree Maintenance saw a faster turnaround, dropping from an average of 42 hours (≈1.7 days) over 90 days to just 9.7 hours in the most recent month.

Light‑out incidents (606 calls) were resolved in about 40 hours (≈1.7 days), while Dumped Items (6,298 calls) improved from a 360‑hour average (≈15 days) to 170 hours (≈7 days) in the last month.

Overall, response times for many service types are trending shorter, with several categories now resolved in under a day.

Residents can continue to report issues quickly through the Go Long Beach app.
