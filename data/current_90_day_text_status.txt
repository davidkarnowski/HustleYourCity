The latest city service data for the past 90 days shows significant activity across Long Beach. Residents submitted 7,351 requests for dumped items, with an average response time of approximately 19 days. Graffiti removal remains a high-volume service with 2,587 requests and an average response time of about 61 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 1,045 requests over the last 90 days, averaging roughly 8 days for a response. Dead animal pickup continues to be the most efficient service, with 551 requests handled in an average of 7 minutes.

Please note that unusually low response times for some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
