Latest data from the City of Long Beach shows service response times and request totals for the past 90 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 6,418 total calls. The average response time for these requests is currently 13.6 days.

Graffiti removal followed with 3,188 reports and an average completion time of 4.5 days. Street Repair requests totaled 788 and are being addressed in approximately 5.2 days on average.

Animal related services show faster turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup and general Animal Services requests are averaging response times between 11 and 13 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
