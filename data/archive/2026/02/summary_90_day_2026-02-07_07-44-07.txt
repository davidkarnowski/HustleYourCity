Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, residents submitted thousands of service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common issues reported were for dumped items, with 6,465 requests, and graffiti removal, with 3,263 requests.

Response times varied significantly by service type. The average time to resolve a request for dumped items was about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in an average of about 4.5 days. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups, which were typically resolved in about 12 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it is the first step. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit a request directly to the city.
