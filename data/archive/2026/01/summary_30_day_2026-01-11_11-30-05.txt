Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the past 30 days. Dumped items, e-scooters, and graffiti were the most frequently reported issues.

The city received 1,934 reports for dumped items and 939 for graffiti. The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 2 days, while dumped item reports took an average of 6 days.

Response times for other key services varied. Reports for traffic signal issues took an average of over 6 days to close, while trash and debris clean-up took nearly 10 days. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickup and animal services were typically closed in about 14 hours. Some unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application. Your reports provide valuable data for tracking city services.
