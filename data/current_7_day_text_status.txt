Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,301 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (513 requests), e-scooters (274 requests), and graffiti (237 requests).

For cases that were closed during this period, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 21 hours. The average for dumped item pickups was about 50 hours, or just over 2 days. Street repair requests were addressed in an average of 21 hours.
