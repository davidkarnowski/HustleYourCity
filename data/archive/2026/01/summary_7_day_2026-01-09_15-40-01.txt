Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,300 service requests were submitted by residents. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (545 calls), improperly parked e-scooters (218 calls), and graffiti (202 calls).

Average city response times for closed requests included 7 hours for vegetation maintenance, 21 hours for street repairs, and 33 hours for graffiti removal. The average response time for dumped item reports was 46 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
