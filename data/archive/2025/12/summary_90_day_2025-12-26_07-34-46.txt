Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days.

Over 15,500 service requests were made by residents. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,942 requests), graffiti (3,676 requests), and e-scooters (2,385 requests).

Average response times for closed requests varied by issue. Reports of dumped items took an average of about two weeks to be resolved. Graffiti removal requests were closed in about 5.5 days on average, while street light issues were addressed in about one week. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests were among the fastest, with average response times of about 15-16 hours.
