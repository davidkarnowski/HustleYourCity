Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,250 service requests were made in the last seven days. The most common reports were for dumped items (477 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (261), and graffiti (236).

Based on city data, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 19 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in just under two days on average. Tree maintenance requests had an average response time of about 32 minutes, though this may reflect administrative closures and not necessarily work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to alert the city.
