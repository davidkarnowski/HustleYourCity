Here is the latest 24-hour city service data update from the Hustle Long Beach! accountability project. Over the past day, the highest volume of citizen reports involved Dumped Items with 100 calls, followed by Graffiti with 63 reports, and E-Scooter issues with 59 reports. Most of the Dumped Item reports are currently marked as new and awaiting review.

When looking at response times over the last 24 hours, several categories showed incredibly fast turnarounds. Restroom Maintenance requests averaged just 2 minutes, Tree Maintenance averaged 5 minutes, and Vegetation issues averaged 24 minutes. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickups also saw rapid averages of 32 minutes and 54 minutes, respectively. Graffiti reports averaged a response time of 86 minutes, while Street Repair averaged about 3.6 hours.

Please keep in mind that unusually low response times, especially those under an hour, may be due to administrative closures, duplicate flags, or system referrals rather than the actual completion of physical work.
