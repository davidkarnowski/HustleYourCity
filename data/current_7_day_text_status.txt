Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,195 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (437 requests), graffiti (262 requests), and e-scooters (259 requests).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 41 hours, while reports of dumped items were addressed in an average of 65 hours. Traffic signal issues had the longest average response time this week at about 3.6 days. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of 57 minutes, though some unusually fast closures may be administrative and not reflect completed physical work.

You can help our community by reporting issues using the Go Long Beach service application.
