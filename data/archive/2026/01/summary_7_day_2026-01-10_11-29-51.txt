Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,479 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (580 calls), graffiti (236 calls), and e-scooter issues (218 calls).

Based on the data for closed cases, the average response time to resolve a graffiti report was about 23 hours. For dumped items, the average response time was about 2 days. Reports for dead animal pickups were addressed in an average of about 9 hours. Some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
