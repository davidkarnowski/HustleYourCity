Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour city service data to keep the community informed on response times and call volumes.

Over the past day, dumped items generated the highest volume with 84 total reports, though 75 of these remain untouched in the new status. E-scooter complaints followed with 53 reports, all of which were immediately referred to outside operators. Graffiti issues saw 27 reports with an average response time of about 3 hours.

Several service categories showed unusually fast response times. Street light issues averaged a 40-minute response time. Animal services requests were addressed in an average of 69 minutes, and running water concerns averaged 84 minutes. Citizens should note that exceptionally low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps track performance and ensures neighborhood concerns are documented.
