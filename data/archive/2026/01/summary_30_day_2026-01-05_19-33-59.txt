Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past month, the most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (1,830 requests), e-scooter issues (1,012), and graffiti removal (895). These three categories made up the majority of all service requests filed.

Average response times for closed cases varied by issue type. Reports of dumped items were resolved in about 8 days on average. Graffiti removal requests were handled more quickly, with an average closure time of about 2 days. Street repair requests were closed in an average of 36 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most direct way to notify the city is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at January 5, 2026, at 11:31 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 5, 2026, at 11:00 AM PST.
