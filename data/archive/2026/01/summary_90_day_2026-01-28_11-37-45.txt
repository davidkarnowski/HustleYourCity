Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, residents submitted more than 16,000 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (6,249 reports), graffiti (3,338 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,715 reports).

Average city response times varied by the type of service requested. Reports for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were handled the fastest, with an average response of about 12 hours. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 5 days on average. Reports of illegally dumped items had an average response time of about 13 days.

For the 2,715 e-scooter reports, nearly all were referred directly to the scooter companies for handling, which is why a city response time is not available for that category.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most direct way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
