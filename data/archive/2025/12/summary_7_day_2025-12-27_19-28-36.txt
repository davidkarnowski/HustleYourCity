Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted over 900 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (307 requests), graffiti (200 requests), and e-scooters (156 requests).

The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 27 hours. For graffiti reports, the average was about 31 hours. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which were handled in an average of 8.5 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
