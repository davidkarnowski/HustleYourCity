Hustle Long Beach! is here with your latest 30-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests. Here is a look at the numbers and how long it takes for the city to respond to public reports.

Dumped items generated the highest call volume over the last month with 2,294 reports. The city took an average of 5.9 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti removal saw 1,038 reports, with crews responding in an average of 1.5 days.

Street repair requests totaled 241, taking an average of 2.7 days to address, while sidewalk repairs took about 3.2 days for 107 reports.

For more urgent matters, dead animal pickups saw 145 reports with an average response time of 9 hours. Animal services handled 79 calls, averaging a 16.5-hour response.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
