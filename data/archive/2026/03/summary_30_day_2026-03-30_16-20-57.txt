Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs. Here is a breakdown of the most recent numbers.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 2198 reports. The city averaged a response time of 7.4 days for these issues. Graffiti was the second most common concern with 988 reports, which saw an average response time of 39.8 hours. E-Scooter complaints came in third with 907 reports, though these were entirely referred to outside agencies.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which received 236 requests and averaged a 68.6-hour response time. Park Maintenance handled 81 requests with an average turnaround of 5 days. For more urgent matters, Dead Animal Pickup processed 154 calls in an average of 8.9 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help keep the neighborhoods clean and safe.
