Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service call data from the City of Long Beach. Over the past 7 days, residents submitted hundreds of requests across various city services. Here is a look at the most significant numbers and response times.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 555 reports. The city averaged a 65-hour response time for these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 318 calls, averaging a 37-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 279 reports.

Other notable service areas included Tree Maintenance with 49 reports and a 19-hour average response, and Street Repair with 45 reports and an 18-hour average response. Animal Services handled 19 calls with an average response of 4.5 hours. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports took an average of 3 days to address.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
