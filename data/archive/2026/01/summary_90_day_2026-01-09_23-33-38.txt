Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,700 service requests during this period. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,953 requests), graffiti (3,570 requests), and e-scooters (2,647 requests).

Average response times to close a request varied by issue. Reports for dumped items were closed in about two weeks, while graffiti was addressed in about 4.5 days. Slower responses included marina repairs, which took about one month on average. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups, handled in under 17 hours. Unusually low response times, such as for damaged drinking fountains (about 2 hours), may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
