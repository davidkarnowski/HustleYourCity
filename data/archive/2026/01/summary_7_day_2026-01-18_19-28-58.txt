Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,440 service requests were made in the last 7 days. The most common reports were for dumped items (565 calls), e-scooters (254 calls), and graffiti (216 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 24 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in an average of 45 hours. Vegetation maintenance requests were handled quickly, with an average response time of about 61 minutes. Some categories show very fast response times, such as a single restroom maintenance request closed in 16 minutes. These may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
