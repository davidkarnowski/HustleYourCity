Here is the latest independent review of City of Long Beach service requests covering the past 90 days. This summary highlights the most frequent issues reported by residents and the current time it takes for the city to resolve them.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,476 reports filed. The average response time for these requests is currently 325 hours, or nearly 2 weeks.

Graffiti removal is the second most active category with 3,189 reports. The data shows an average response time of 100 hours, or roughly 4 days.

Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 778 reports. The average time to close these requests is currently 119 hours, or about 5 days.

Dead Animal Pickup services are operating efficiently, with 414 total reports and an average response time of just over 11 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. Consistent reporting helps document the needs of the community.
