Here's a look at Long Beach's service response data for the last 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti removal continue to be the most frequent service requests from residents, with over 6,200 and 3,700 reports filed, respectively.

Response times for some key services have stretched into weeks. On average, park maintenance requests took about 19 days to close, while dumped item pickups averaged over 15 days. Graffiti removal requests were typically completed in about 6.5 days.

In contrast, more urgent issues like dead animal pickup and other animal services calls were resolved in under 18 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.
