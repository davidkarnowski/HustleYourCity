This is an independent review of Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days. This analysis highlights current response times and call volumes to keep the public informed on service performance.

Dumped Items represent the highest volume of service requests with 2,258 total reports filed. The average response time to resolve these issues is currently tracking at approximately 6 days.

Graffiti removal services received 1,154 reports. The data shows an average completion time of roughly 2 days for these requests. Street Repair generated 256 reports, with an average response time of just over 3 days.

Dead Animal Pickup services managed 137 calls with an average response time of about 12 and a half hours. There were 935 reports regarding E-Scooters, though the data indicates these are almost exclusively referred out to operators rather than closed by city workers.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting creates the data necessary to track these trends.
