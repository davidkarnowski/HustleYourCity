Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day city service data. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public requests.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,994 reports. These issues took an average of 9 days to resolve. Graffiti removal saw 3,101 reports, with an average response time of 60 hours. E-Scooter complaints were also high with 2,849 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Response times vary significantly by category. Business license and permit concerns were addressed the fastest, averaging just 12 minutes for the 6 reports filed. Dead animal pickups averaged a 10-hour response time across 445 requests. Conversely, street repairs took an average of 6.4 days to address across 827 requests.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
