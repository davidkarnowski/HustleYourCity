Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped items, graffiti, and e-scooters. On average, reports of dumped items were addressed in about 14 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed faster, with an average time of just under 5 days.

Response times for other common issues included street repairs at 8 days and tree maintenance at about 6.5 days. More urgent requests, such as dead animal pickups and animal services calls, were typically handled in under 17 hours. Some issues may show unusually fast completion times due to administrative closures.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the best way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your phone.
