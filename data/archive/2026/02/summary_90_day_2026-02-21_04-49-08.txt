Here is an evaluation of the City of Long Beach service request data for the past 90 days.

The highest volume of service requests continues to be for Dumped Items, with 6,461 total reports filed. The average response time for these issues was approximately 13 days. Graffiti removal received 3,106 reports, with the city averaging a 4-day response time.

Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 798 reports and took roughly 4 and a half days to resolve on average. Tree Maintenance requests averaged about 6 and a half days for completion.

Some categories display rapid closure times. Reports regarding Business Licenses were closed in an average of 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Accurate data relies on citizen reporting. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
