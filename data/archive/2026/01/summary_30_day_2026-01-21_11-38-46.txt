Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the last 30 days.

Over the past month, residents submitted more than 5,500 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,110 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (940), and graffiti (891).

The data shows the average time to close a request for dumped items was about 7.5 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 3 days, while reports for street repairs and street light issues were closed in just under 3 days on average. Faster response times were seen for dead animal pickups and other animal services, both averaging around 13 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
