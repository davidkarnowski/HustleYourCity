In the last 30 days Long Beach recorded 5,784 service requests.

The highest volumes were Dumped Items (2,109 reports) and Graffiti (1,448 reports). Dumped Items took an average of ≈ 9.8 days to close, while Graffiti was resolved in about 1.8 days. Tree Maintenance (248 reports) was addressed quickly, averaging 10 hours. Sidewalk Repair (112 reports) and Light issues (246 reports) had average response times of 16 hours and 33 hours respectively.

Overall, most categories are seeing response times under two days, but the Dumped Items backlog remains a concern. Residents can help by reporting issues promptly through the Go Long Beach service app.
