The latest 30-day data from the City of Long Beach service requests highlights significant activity in waste management and maintenance. Dumped Items remains the highest volume category with 2,224 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 7 days.

Graffiti removal received 1,117 reports with an average response time of just over 2 days. Street Repair requests totaled 240, showing an average response time of 3 days.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 13.5 hours across 151 reports. Tree Maintenance and Sidewalk Repair data indicates average response times of approximately 1 day, though unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
