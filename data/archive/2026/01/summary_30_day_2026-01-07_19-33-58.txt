Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service call data.

In the past month, the most frequent service request was for "Dumped Items" with 1,919 reports. This was followed by 938 reports for "E-Scooter" issues and 902 for "Graffiti".

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 2.3 days. For dumped items, the average response was 7.6 days. Street repair requests were closed in an average of 2.3 days. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 14.7 hours.
