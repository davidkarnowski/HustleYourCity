Here is a look at the latest Long Beach city service data, focusing on requests made in the last 90 days.

The most common service requests from residents were for dumped item pickups, with over 6,400 reports. This was followed by over 3,200 reports for graffiti removal and nearly 2,700 for e-scooter issues.

The average response time to close a dumped item request was about 14 days. For graffiti removal, the average was about 4.5 days. Some services saw much faster resolutions, such as dead animal pickups, which were closed in an average of about 12 hours.

It is worth noting that nearly all e-scooter reports are marked as "Closed Referred," which means the city forwards the request to the appropriate scooter company to handle.

To report issues in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.
