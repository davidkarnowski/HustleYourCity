Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,093 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (409 calls), e-scooters (231 calls), and graffiti (218 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 30 hours. Reports of illegally dumped items were addressed in about 1.5 days on average, while street light issues took an average of 2 days to resolve. Some requests, like one for restroom maintenance, were closed in about 51 minutes. Unusually fast closures can sometimes be due to administrative updates and not on-site work.
