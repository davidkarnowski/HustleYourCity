Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling local issues.

Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 526 reports. The average response time for these requests was about 3.5 days. Graffiti removal was the second most common issue with 214 requests, averaging a 21.5-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints totaled 191, with all of them being referred to outside agencies.

Some services saw very fast turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup handled 45 calls with an average response of 4.9 hours. Restroom Maintenance showed an average response of just 35 minutes for a single request. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

On the slower end, Abandoned Shopping Carts took an average of over 3 days to address across 10 reports.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help keep the community clean and safe.
