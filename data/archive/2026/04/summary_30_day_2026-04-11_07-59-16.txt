Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest 30-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items led the service requests with 2,336 reports. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,083 calls, averaging a 37-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints reached 957, though nearly all were immediately referred to outside agencies.

Tree Maintenance saw 254 requests with an average response time of 3.6 days. Street Repair had 200 reports, taking about 3.4 days to address. On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup had 168 reports and Animal Services had 78 reports, averaging response times of 6.9 hours and 8.6 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep Long Beach accountable and well-maintained. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
