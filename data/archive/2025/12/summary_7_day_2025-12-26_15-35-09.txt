Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 932 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (311 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (221), and graffiti (189).

The average response time for dumped item reports was about 24 hours. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 34 hours. Some service types saw very quick resolutions, such as park maintenance requests, which averaged 69 minutes. Unusually fast response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures and not on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit a request directly to the city.
