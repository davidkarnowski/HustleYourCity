Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times based on data from the past 7 days.

Dumped Items led the service requests this week with 574 reports, showing an average response time of 47 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed with 270 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti was the third most reported issue, totaling 241 calls with an average response time of 18 hours.

Other notable service categories include Dead Animal Pickup, which saw 35 reports and an average response time of 2 hours. Tree Maintenance received 47 reports with an 8-hour average response, and Sidewalk Repair had 43 reports averaging an 11-hour response. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
