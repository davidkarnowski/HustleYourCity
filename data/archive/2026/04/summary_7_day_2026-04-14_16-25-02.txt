Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 553 reports. The city averaged a 66-hour response time for these issues.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 309 calls, averaging a 39-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 277 reports.

For issues taking longer to resolve, Abandoned Shopping Cart reports averaged a response time of 3.2 days. On the faster side, Animal Services handled 19 calls with an average response time of 4.5 hours. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
