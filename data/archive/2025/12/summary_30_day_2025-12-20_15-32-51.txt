Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days based on public data.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues. The city received 1,962 requests for dumped item pickups and 1,099 reports of graffiti. E-scooter reports were also high, with 948 submissions.

The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 6.5 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 65 hours, while street repair requests took nearly 4 days. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups (about 12 hours) and animal services calls (about 10 hours).

You can report issues like these directly to the city by using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 20, 2025, 7:30 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 20, 2025, 7:00 AM PST.
