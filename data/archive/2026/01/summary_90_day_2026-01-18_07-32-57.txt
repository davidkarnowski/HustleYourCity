Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 90 days.

The most common reports from residents were for dumped items (6,071 calls), graffiti (3,439 calls), and e-scooters (2,726 calls).

The average time to resolve a request for dumped items was about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in just under 5 days on average, while street repairs took about 6 days. Faster responses were recorded for issues like dead animal pickups (about 16 hours) and animal services calls (about 14 hours). Some requests show unusually low response times, which may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
