Here is the latest 30-day service call data summary for Long Beach, provided by the Hustle Long Beach! accountability project.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service requests with 2128 reports. The city took an average of 5 days to resolve these issues. Graffiti reports were the second most common with 1058 calls, averaging a 43-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 1035 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies rather than closed directly by the city.

Response times varied widely depending on the service. Marina Repair requests took an average of 7.5 days to address. On the faster end, Dead Animal Pickup averaged a 14-hour response. Business License and Permit Concerns showed a response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this are likely due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help track neighborhood needs.
