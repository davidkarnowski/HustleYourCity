Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 4,900 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,001 calls), graffiti (963 calls), and e-scooters (939 calls).

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" request was about 9 days. Reports for graffiti, street repairs, and broken lights were addressed in a little over 2 days on average. Faster responses were recorded for tree maintenance (about 29 hours) and dead animal pickups (about 14 hours).

It is important to note that all 939 e-scooter complaints were referred to the scooter companies for handling, so a city response time is not tracked in this data.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 31, 2025, at 11:32 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 31, 2025, at 11:00 PM PST.
