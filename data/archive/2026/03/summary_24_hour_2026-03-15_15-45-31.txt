Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest third-party accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests over the past 24 hours.

In the last day, the city logged 90 total service calls. The highest volume of reports came from Dumped Items, accounting for 46 calls, almost all of which are currently marked as new. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 12 calls, 10 of which are actively in progress.

Dead Animal Pickup was the only category to record a completed response time in the last 24 hours. The city received 4 calls for this issue and closed 2 of them with an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual field work completions.

Other recent reports, including street repairs, tree maintenance, and abandoned shopping carts, remain in the new queue and are awaiting city action. E-scooter complaints accounted for 3 calls, all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies.
