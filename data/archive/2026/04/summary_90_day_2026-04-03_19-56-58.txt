Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day city service data to keep you informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 7,047 calls in the last 90 days. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal was the second most common issue, totaling 3,133 reports with an average response time of 59 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant volume. The city received 929 tree maintenance reports, which took nearly 5 days on average to resolve. Street repairs accounted for 829 calls, with an average response time of 6.5 days. E-scooter complaints generated 2,862 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies or closed administratively without a recorded response time.

Some services show unusually fast turnaround times, which may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. For instance, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Dead animal pickups, totaling 447 calls, were handled in an average of 10 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 3, 2026, 12:53 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 3, 2026, 11:00 AM PDT.
