Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 30 days.

Over the past month, the most frequent service requests were for dumped items (2,002 reports), graffiti (968 reports), and e-scooters (941 reports).

The average response time to close a "dumped items" report was about 9 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 55 hours on average, while street repair requests took about 53 hours. Faster responses were seen for services like dead animal pickup (about 14 hours) and animal services (about 12 hours). Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
