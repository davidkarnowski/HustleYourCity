In the past week Long Beach residents filed 1,350 service requests across 18 categories.

The most common issues were Dumped Items (507 reports) and Graffiti (363 reports). 188 new Dumped Item reports and 68 new graffiti reports were logged, with average response times of 52.3 hours and 23.7 hours respectively. 144 graffiti cases were already closed.

Overall, the city is handling a steady flow of requests, but many remain in‑progress. Residents are encouraged to report issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app to help keep response times short.
