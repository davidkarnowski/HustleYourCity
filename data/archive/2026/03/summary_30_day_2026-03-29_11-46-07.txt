Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day city service response data to keep the community informed. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach has been handling recent service requests.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 2,212 reports. The city average response time for these issues was 1 week.
