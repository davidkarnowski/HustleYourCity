Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

The most frequent reports from the community were for dumped items (2,062 reports), e-scooters (930 reports), and graffiti (910 reports).

Average response times for key services varied. Graffiti removal requests were closed in a little over two days on average. Reports for dumped items took longer, with an average closure time of about 4.5 days. Requests for trash and debris cleanup averaged nearly seven days to resolve.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach app.
