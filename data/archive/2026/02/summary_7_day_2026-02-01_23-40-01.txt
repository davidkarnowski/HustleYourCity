Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,390 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (563 calls), graffiti (250 calls), and e-scooters (245 calls).

For closed requests, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 20 hours. Reports of dumped items took an average of about 2 days and 4 hours to resolve. Some categories showed very fast average response times, such as sidewalk repair (about 6 hours) and tree maintenance (about 8 hours). These low times can sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
