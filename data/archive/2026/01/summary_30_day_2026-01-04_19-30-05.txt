Here is a look at Long Beach service requests from the last 30 days. The top three issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (1,836 requests), e-scooters (968 requests), and graffiti (905 requests).

Average response times for closed cases varied across different departments. Reports for dumped items and trash/debris took about 8.7 days to resolve. Park maintenance requests were addressed in about 4.1 days on average.

Faster responses were seen for issues like graffiti (55 hours), street repair (40 hours), and tree maintenance (31 hours). Requests for e-scooters are referred directly to vendors, so the city does not provide a response time for those cases.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.
