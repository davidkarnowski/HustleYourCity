Latest 30‑day service request snapshot for Long Beach

In the past month the city logged about 5,456 service calls across all categories. The biggest volumes were:

- Dumped Items: 2,030 requests (average response ≈ 7 days)
- Graffiti: 1,431 requests (average response ≈ 66 hours)
- Street Repair: 200 requests (average response ≈ 21 hours)
- Tree Maintenance: 235 requests (average response ≈ 10 hours)

Other notable averages:
- Light repairs: 41 hours
- Traffic Signal work: 18 hours
- Sidewalk Repair: 13 hours
- Restroom Maintenance: 25 hours

Several categories still have many jobs “In Progress,” especially Street Repair (114) and Graffiti (233), indicating ongoing work.

Overall, response times are improving for many services, but Dumped Items continue to take about a week to close.
