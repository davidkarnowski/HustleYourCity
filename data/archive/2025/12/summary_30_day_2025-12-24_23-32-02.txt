Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests based on public data.

In the last month, residents submitted nearly 5,000 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (1,937 requests), graffiti (1,037 requests), and e-scooters (1,025 requests).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.5 days. More urgent requests for dead animal pickups and other animal services were handled more quickly, with average response times under 13 hours. However, the most common request type, dumped items, took an average of about 8 days to be resolved. Park maintenance requests took about 4 days. Unusually fast response times for some issues may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.
