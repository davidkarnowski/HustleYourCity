Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day service call data to keep the community informed on city response times.

Over the past week, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 513 reports, taking an average of 28 hours to address. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 203 calls and an average response time of 18 hours. E-Scooter complaints totaled 185, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable response times include Dead Animal Pickup, which handled 45 reports in an average of 3 hours. Sidewalk Repair and Light issues both averaged around 8 hours for their response times. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 23, 2026, 4:53 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 23, 2026, 4:00 AM PDT.
