Long Beach Service Response Update

In the last 30 days, residents submitted thousands of service requests through the city's reporting system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (1,897 requests), e-scooters (936 requests), and graffiti (905 requests).

Here is a look at the average response times for closed requests in the past month. Graffiti removal and street repairs were both completed in just over 2 days. The average response for dumped item pickup was about one week, while trash and debris cleanup took about 10 days.

Some unusually fast response times, such as the 2-hour average for damaged drinking fountains, may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
