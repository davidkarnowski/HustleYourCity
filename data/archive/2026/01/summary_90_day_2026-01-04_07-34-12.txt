Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on public data.

The most frequent report was for "Dumped Items" with over 5,800 requests, which took an average of about 14.5 days to close. "Graffiti" was the second most common with over 3,600 reports, addressed in an average of about 4.8 days. Over 2,600 "E-Scooter" reports were made, with nearly all being referred directly to the scooter companies.

Response times for infrastructure have varied. "Street Repair" requests took an average of about 9.7 days to close, while "Tree Maintenance" took about 6.4 days. Faster responses were recorded for "Dead Animal Pickup" (about 17 hours) and "Animal Services" (about 16 hours). Some unusually low response times, like for "Damaged Drinking Fountain" reports, may be due to administrative closures and not the time for physical work completion.
