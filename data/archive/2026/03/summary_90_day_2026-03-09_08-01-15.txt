Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the city's response times and call totals over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 6,484 requests, taking an average of nearly 11 days to resolve. Graffiti follows with 3,102 reports, averaging a response time of 3.3 days. E-scooter complaints reached 2,930, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw high volumes. Street light issues totaled 1,040 reports, averaging a 3.3-day turnaround. Tree maintenance had 869 calls with a nearly 6-day average response time. Street repair saw 839 requests, taking about 5.4 days on average to address.

Some services saw very fast turnarounds, which may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Business license and permit concerns averaged a 12-minute response time across 6 reports. Dead animal pickups, with 437 requests, and animal services, with 222 requests, averaged 13.4 hours and 17.3 hours respectively.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. This ensures that neighborhood concerns are officially tracked and addressed.

This data summary was updated at March 9, 2026, at 12:56 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 9, 2026, at 12:00 AM PDT.
