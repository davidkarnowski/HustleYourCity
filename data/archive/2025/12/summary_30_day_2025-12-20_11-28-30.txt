Here is a look at Long Beach service request data from the last 30 days.

The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (1,964 calls) and graffiti (1,103 calls). The average city response time to resolve a dumped item report was about 6.5 days. Graffiti reports were addressed more quickly, with an average response of about 2.7 days.

Response times varied for other services. Street repair requests were closed in an average of 3.8 days. Dead animal pickups were handled much faster, with an average response time of about 12 hours.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
