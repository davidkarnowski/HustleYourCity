Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response data for the past week.

In the last 7 days, the most frequent service requests were for Dumped Items (645 calls), Graffiti (292 calls), and E-Scooters (222 calls). The average time to close a Dumped Items report was about 3.5 days. Graffiti reports were closed in about 26 hours on average. E-scooter reports are referred directly to vendors and not handled by city staff.

Other key service response times include Street Repair requests, which were addressed in about 24 hours, and Dead Animal Pickups, which took about 3 hours. Reports for street light outages took over 3.5 days on average to be resolved. Note that some service requests may be closed for administrative reasons, which can result in unusually low response times that do not reflect the completion of physical work.
