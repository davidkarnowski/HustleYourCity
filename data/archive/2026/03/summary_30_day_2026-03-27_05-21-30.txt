Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest service request data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at the city's performance and response times over the last 30 days.

Dumped items remain the top issue for residents, generating 2,263 requests. The city closed 1,310 of these cases, with an average response time of nearly 7 days.

Graffiti reports were the second most common issue with 981 requests. The city closed 839 of these reports, averaging a response time of about 1.5 days.

Other notable service areas include street repair, which saw 255 requests and an average response time of nearly 3 days, and tree maintenance, which had 301 requests and an average response time of about 1.5 days.

Some categories show very fast processing times, such as dead animal pickups averaging about 9 hours. Keep in mind that unusually low response times or missing time data can sometimes indicate administrative closures or automatic referrals rather than completed physical work. For instance, all 859 e-scooter complaints were simply marked as closed and referred.
