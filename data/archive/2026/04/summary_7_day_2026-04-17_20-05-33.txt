Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items led the service requests this week with 537 reports, taking an average of 3.3 days to process. E-Scooter complaints followed with 239 reports, almost all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti remains a top concern with 197 reports, but the city is responding to these in about 1.1 days on average.

Dead animal pickups are being handled quickly, averaging just 4 hours per request. Meanwhile, trash and debris reports are taking about 3.1 days to address. Keep in mind that unusually low response times across some categories may be due to administrative closures or referrals rather than actual physical work being completed on the ground.

Accountability starts with accurate reporting. If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure to report it using the Go Long Beach service application so the data reflects what is really happening on the streets.
