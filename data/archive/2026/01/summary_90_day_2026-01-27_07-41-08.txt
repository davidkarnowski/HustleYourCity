Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,400 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (6,199 requests), graffiti (3,342 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,715 requests).

Average city response times for closed requests varied by type. The most reported issue, dumped items, took about 13 days to resolve. Graffiti removal averaged about 5 days, and street repairs averaged about 5.5 days. More urgent requests were handled faster, with dead animal pickups averaging about 13 hours. Longer-term projects, like marina repairs, took an average of 5 weeks. Note that some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
