Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data for the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,496 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (586 requests), graffiti (246 requests), and e-scooter issues (231 requests).

According to the data, the average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 53 hours. The average for graffiti removal was about 25.5 hours, and street repairs were addressed in about 24 hours.
