Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the past week.

Reports for illegally "Dumped Items" were the most common issue, with 610 requests submitted. The second most frequent report was for "Graffiti" removal, with 279 requests.

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" case was approximately 36 hours. For "Graffiti" reports, the average response time was about 32 hours. Faster average response times were recorded for "Abandoned Shopping Cart" removals at about 13 hours and "Dead Animal Pickup" at around 11 hours.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
