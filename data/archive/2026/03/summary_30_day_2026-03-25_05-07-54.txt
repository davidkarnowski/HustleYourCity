Hustle Long Beach! is back with another look at how the City of Long Beach is handling service requests. Here is a breakdown of the numbers over the past 30 days.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 2330 calls in the last month. Because this average response time is 140 hours, it takes the city nearly 6 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti is the second most common issue, with 1035 reports taking about 36 hours to address. E-scooter complaints also remain high with 909 reports, though these are typically referred to outside vendors and do not have response times tracked.

Some services see much faster turnaround times. Dead animal pickups, for example, average a 9-hour response time across 147 reports. On the other end of the spectrum, marina repairs take over a week (about 7.4 days) to resolve, and reports of illegal fireworks average nearly 6 days. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times might sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual completed work.
