Hustle Long Beach community update on city service metrics. This review focuses on service request data from the last 90 days.

The highest volume of reports belongs to Dumped Items, with 6,645 total calls recorded. The average response time for these issues is currently 12 days. Graffiti reports are the second most frequent category with 3,130 calls, showing an average resolution time of just under 4 days.

Infrastructure issues like Street Repair generated 826 reports, with crews taking approximately 5 days to close tickets. Tree Maintenance requests are taking about 6 days to resolve.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup, which averages 13 hours. The fastest administrative response was for Business License concerns, averaging 12 minutes. Note that unusually low response times may indicate administrative closures rather than physical work completion.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
