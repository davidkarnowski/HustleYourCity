Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times for the past 30 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service requests with 2,234 reports. The average response time for these issues is currently 5.5 days. Graffiti reports followed with 1,127 total entries, showing an average resolution time of 1.9 days.

Infrastructure maintenance shows varied response times. Street Repair requests are averaging 3.2 days for a response, while Sidewalk Repair averages 2.1 days. Tree Maintenance requests, totaling 440 reports, are being addressed in approximately 1.8 days.

Faster response times were recorded for public health and safety issues. Dead Animal Pickup requests averaged 14 hours for completion. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports were resolved in approximately 13 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city to ensure data is captured and tracked.
