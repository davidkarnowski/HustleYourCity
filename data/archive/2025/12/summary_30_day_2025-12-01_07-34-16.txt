City service request update – last 30 days

• Graffiti: 1,338 requests (71 hours average response ≈ 3 days). That’s a 26 % faster turnaround than the prior 60‑day period (96 hours).  
• Dumped Items: 1,810 requests (201 hours average ≈ 8.4 days), down 53 % in volume and 38 % quicker than before (326 hours).  
• Tree Maintenance: 196 requests, average response 10 hours – a noticeable speed‑up from 12.6 hours in the previous 60 days.  
• E‑Scooter: 687 reports, all closed as “referred” (no open cases).  
• Light repairs: 251 requests, average response 38 hours (≈ 1.6 days).  
• Sidewalk Repair: 73 requests, average response 14.6 hours, down 60 % in volume from the prior period.  
• Traffic Signal: 35 requests, average response 24 hours.  

Overall, request volumes for graffiti, dumped items, tree work and sidewalk repair have dropped by roughly half, and average response times have improved across most categories.

Residents can continue to report issues quickly through the Go Long Beach app.
