Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service call data to keep you informed on City of Long Beach response times. Over the last 30 days, residents submitted thousands of requests, and here is how the city handled the most frequent issues.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,322 reports. The average response time for these requests was 8.4 days. Graffiti was the second most common issue with 969 reports and an average response time of 1.4 days. E-Scooter complaints reached 932 reports, all of which were closed and referred to outside entities.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which received 219 requests and took an average of 3.4 days to address. Park Maintenance saw 79 requests with an average response time of 3.6 days. Dead Animal Pickup had 163 reports with an average response time of 5 hours. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Accountability starts with reporting. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
