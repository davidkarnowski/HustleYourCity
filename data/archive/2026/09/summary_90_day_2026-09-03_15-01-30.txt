The latest service data for the City of Long Beach shows consistent activity across key maintenance categories over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the highest volume request with 8,150 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 17 days. Please note that some categories show very low response times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
