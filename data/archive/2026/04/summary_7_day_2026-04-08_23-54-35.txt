Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach handled citizen requests over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest call volume with 442 reports. The city averaged 3.4 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 284 calls, showing an average response time of 49 hours.

Some services saw extended wait times. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports took an average of 3.2 days to address, and Damaged Drinking Fountain requests averaged 4 days for a response.

On the faster end, Running Water reports averaged a response time of 78 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
