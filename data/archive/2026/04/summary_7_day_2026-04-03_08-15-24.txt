Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a summary of the service requests and response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 620 calls. This was followed by 261 reports regarding E-Scooters and 221 calls for Graffiti removal.

Response times varied by category. The city averaged 67 hours to respond to Dumped Items and 25 hours for Graffiti. Dead Animal Pickup requests averaged a 2.5-hour response time. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Categories with longer wait times included reports of Illegal Fireworks, which averaged nearly 5 days for a response, and Shoes on a Utility Wire, which took about 3.5 days.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city informed.

This data summary was updated at April 3, 2026, 1:08 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 2, 2026, 11:00 PM PDT.
