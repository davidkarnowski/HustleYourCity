Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped items (1,941 calls), e-scooters (1,017 calls), and graffiti removal (1,001 calls).

The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 10 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 2 days on average, while reports for traffic signal issues took an average of nearly 4 days to resolve. Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which were completed in an average of about 12 hours. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not completed work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
