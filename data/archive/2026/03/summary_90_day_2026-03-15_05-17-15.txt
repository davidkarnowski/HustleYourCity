Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data to track how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs. Over the last 90 days, citizens have submitted thousands of reports, and the data shows a wide range of response times across different departments.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,631 reports. The city took an average of 10 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti reports were also high, totaling 3,076 requests, with an average response time of 3 days.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests show longer turnaround times. Street repair logged 829 calls and tree maintenance saw 906 calls, both averaging about 6 days for a response. Sidewalk repairs, with 292 requests, averaged a 3.5-day response time.

Some services were handled much faster. Dead animal pickups averaged a 13-hour response across 445 requests. Business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes for 6 requests. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Please use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests and help keep the city accountable.
