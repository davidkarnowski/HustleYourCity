Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service call data for the past week.

Residents submitted nearly 1,500 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (586 calls), graffiti (247 calls), and e-scooter issues (228 calls).

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 25 hours. Dumped item reports were addressed in approximately 49 hours on average. A single restroom maintenance request was closed in about 29 minutes, though unusually fast responses can sometimes be due to administrative closures.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application to report it to the city.
