Here is a look at Long Beach city service request data from the past 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were dumped items (5,958 requests), graffiti (3,555 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,655 requests).

Average response times varied by issue. Reports for dead animal pickup and animal services were among the fastest, typically closed in under 17 hours. Graffiti removal requests took about 4.5 days on average. Slower responses were noted for park maintenance (about 13 days) and illegally dumped items (about 14 days).

It is important to note that all 2,655 e-scooter reports were referred to the scooter companies for resolution, not handled by city staff.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at 3:29 PM PST on January 10, 2026 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 3:00 PM PST on January 10, 2026.
