The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain a primary concern with 7,322 total reports, averaging a response time of approximately 19.5 days. Graffiti removal continues to be one of the more efficient services, with 2,580 reports handled in an average of 66.4 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 1,051 reports in the last 90 days, with an average response time of about 8 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show the fastest response times, both averaging under 12 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city to ensure they are tracked in the system.
