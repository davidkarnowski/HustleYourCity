Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with over 5,800 requests. The average time to resolve these cases was about 2 weeks. Graffiti removal was the second most common request with 3,600 reports, which were handled in an average of about 5 days.

Street repair requests took an average of 9.5 days to close. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which were typically completed in about 17 hours.
