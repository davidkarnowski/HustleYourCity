Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports.

Dumped Items led the service requests with 581 reports. The average response time was 63 hours, though 435 of these requests currently remain in the New status.

Graffiti removal saw 239 reports, with an average response time of 24 hours. The city has already closed 177 of these cases.

E-Scooter complaints generated 139 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Dead Animal Pickup had 31 reports with an average response time of 5 hours. Please note that unusually low response times across any category may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
