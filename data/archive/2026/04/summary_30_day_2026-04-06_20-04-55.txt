Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest 30-day accountability report on City of Long Beach service requests.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue with 2,295 requests in the last 30 days. The average response time for these items is currently over 8 days. Graffiti is the second most reported issue with 984 calls, averaging a response time of 32 hours. E-scooter complaints reached 904, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Dead animal pickups are being handled quickly, averaging a 5-hour response time across 166 reports. Conversely, illegal firework reports are taking over 4 days to process, and park maintenance is taking over 3 days. Keep in mind that unusually fast or slow times may be influenced by administrative closures rather than actual work completion.
