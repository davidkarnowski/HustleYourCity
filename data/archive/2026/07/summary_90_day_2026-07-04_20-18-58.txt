The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 6,656 reports for dumped items, which remains the highest volume category, with an average response time of approximately 8 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,318 with an average response time of about 3 days. Tree maintenance requests saw 633 reports with an average response time of roughly 2.5 days.
