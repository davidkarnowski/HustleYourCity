Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day snapshot of city service response times. Here is a look at how Long Beach is handling citizen reports and where the highest volumes of requests are coming from.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2266 calls over the past month. The average response time for these reports is currently about 8 days. Graffiti removal follows with 1071 reports, taking an average of 37 hours to address.

Street repair requests totaled 202, with response times averaging just over 3 days. Dead animal pickups are being handled much faster, averaging a 7-hour response time across 164 reports. Meanwhile, E-scooter complaints generated 954 reports, almost all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies.

Remember that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
