Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the last 30 days.

The most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (2,427 requests), graffiti (1,002 requests), and e-scooters (995 requests). The average time for the city to resolve a dumped items report was approximately 7 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 2.5 days on average.
