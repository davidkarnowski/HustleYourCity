Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Residents made over 5,000 service requests in the past month. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,295 calls), illegally parked e-scooters (959 calls), and graffiti (945 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was just under 3 days. Reports for street repair and street light outages were also handled in about 3 days on average. Dumped item pickups took about 8 days to complete.

Faster responses included dead animal pickups and animal services requests, both averaging about 12 hours. Some requests may show unusually fast completion times, which can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work.
