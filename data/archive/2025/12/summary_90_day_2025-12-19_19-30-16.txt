Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, the most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped items (6,164 reports), graffiti (3,718 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,219 reports).

Average response times for closed requests varied by issue type. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 6 days. For dumped items, the average response time was about 15 days. Some services were handled more quickly, with dead animal pickups averaging about 17 hours. Some unusually fast closures may be administrative and not reflect completed work.

To report issues like graffiti, potholes, or dumped items, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at December 19, 2025, 11:27 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 19, 2025, 11:00 AM PST.
