An independent review of Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days highlights current response times and request volumes. Dumped Items remained the most frequent report with 2,236 total requests. The average time to resolve these dumping issues was approximately 6 days.

Graffiti removal was the second most active category with 1,114 reports. The average response time for graffiti was roughly 2 days. Street Repair requests, which totaled 248, showed an average completion time of 4 days.

Faster response times were noted in Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 12 hours across 136 requests. Tree Maintenance issues were resolved in just under 2 days on average. There were also 958 reports regarding E-Scooters, which are generally referred to operators rather than handled directly by city crews.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues.
