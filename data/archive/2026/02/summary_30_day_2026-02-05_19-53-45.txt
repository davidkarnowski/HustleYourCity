Here's the latest look at Long Beach city service response times from the past 30 days.

Reports for illegally dumped items were the most common service request, with 2,547 calls. The average response time to close these requests was about 9 days. Graffiti was the second most common issue with 1,101 reports, which were addressed in about 59 hours on average.

The city also received 428 requests for light repairs, with an average response time of about 58 hours. Reports for dead animal pickups were resolved much faster, averaging about 11 hours to complete.

You can report issues like these using the Go Long Beach service application.
