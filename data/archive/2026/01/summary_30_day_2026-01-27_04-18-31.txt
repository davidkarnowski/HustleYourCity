Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data. In the past month, residents submitted over 6,000 service requests to the city.

The most frequent report was for "Dumped Items," with over 2,300 submissions. The average time to close these requests was about 7.5 days. Graffiti was the second most common issue, with nearly 1,000 reports and an average response time of about 2.5 days.

Response times varied by service type. Reports for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were handled quickly, with an average closure time of about 12 hours. In contrast, more complex issues like Traffic Signal repairs averaged about 4.5 days.

To report issues like graffiti, potholes, or dumped items, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
