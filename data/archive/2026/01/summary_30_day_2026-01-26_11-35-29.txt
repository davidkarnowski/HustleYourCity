Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

The most frequent request was for dumped items, with 2,315 reports filed. The average city response time to close these cases was just under 8 days. The second most common report, with 1,015 submissions, was for e-scooter issues.

Graffiti removal, the third most common request with 969 reports, took an average of about 2.7 days. Street repair requests were resolved in an average of nearly 3 days.

Faster responses were recorded for services like dead animal pickup (about 11 hours) and animal services calls (about 12 hours).

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach app to report it directly to the city.
