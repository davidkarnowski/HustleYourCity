Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service requests for the last 90 days.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,408 reports filed. The average response time for these requests is currently 13.6 days. Graffiti reports are the second most frequent issue with 3,189 total calls, showing an average response time of 4.3 days.

Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 793 and are averaging 5.3 days for resolution. For faster services, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 11.4 hours across 406 reports. Marina Repair recorded the longest wait times, averaging 24.7 days, though the volume was low with only 7 reports.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
