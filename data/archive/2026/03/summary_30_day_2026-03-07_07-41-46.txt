Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service requests based on data from the last 30 days.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 2,133 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 5 days. Graffiti reports followed with 1,093 total calls, showing an average response time of just under 2 days.

Infrastructure requests showed varied completion times. Street Repair requests totaled 266 with an average response of 3 days. Tree Maintenance saw 443 reports with an average response time of nearly 2 days. Lighting issues, with 343 reports, took roughly 2 days to address.

Faster response times were recorded for public health issues. Dead Animal Pickup received 134 reports and averaged 14 hours for a response. Conversely, Business License concerns showed an average response of 12 minutes. Unusually low response times such as this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
