Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the last month, the most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (2,420 reports), e-scooters (1,042 reports), and graffiti (1,023 reports). These three categories represent the majority of service requests made.

The city's data shows the average response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was approximately 9 days. For "Graffiti" reports, the average response was about 2.5 days. Street repair requests were addressed in just under 3 days on average.

Reporting issues helps create data to hold the city accountable. Residents can report neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at January 30, 2026, 11:38 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 30, 2026, 11:00 PM PST.
