Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests.

Over the last 30 days, residents submitted 5,099 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,975 calls), graffiti (1,132 calls), and e-scooters (908 calls).

Response times for key services varied. The average time to close a graffiti report was about 2.7 days, while dumped item reports took about 6.3 days. Street repair requests were addressed in an average of 4.1 days. Some of the fastest responses were for Animal Services, averaging about 10.6 hours, while marina repairs took the longest at about 2 weeks.

Response times can vary based on the complexity of the request. Unusually fast closures may be due to administrative updates rather than work completion.
