Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past week.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 653 service requests, followed by graffiti removal with 288 reports.

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 25 hours. For dumped items, the average was about 18 hours. Reports for abandoned shopping carts were closed in an average of 39 minutes. Note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.
