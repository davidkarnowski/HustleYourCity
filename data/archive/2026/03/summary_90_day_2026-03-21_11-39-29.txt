Here is the latest 90-day review of Long Beach city service requests analyzed for the Hustle Long Beach! community. This independent summary highlights the most significant service call volumes and response times to keep residents informed.

Dumped Items continue to generate the highest volume of reports with 6,738 requests in the last 90 days. The city's average response time for these items is currently 9.7 days.

Graffiti removal is the second most requested service, logging 3,076 reports. The average response time for graffiti issues stands at 71 hours.

Other major infrastructure requests include Tree Maintenance with 940 calls averaging a 6.7-day response, and Street Repair with 835 calls averaging a 6.6-day response. Lighting issues generated 1,030 reports with an average response time of 3.3 days.

Some categories show exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps track government efficiency and neighborhood needs.
