Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest 24-hour breakdown of city service response times.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 90 reports, though the vast majority remain in the new status. E-Scooter complaints followed with 53 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti issues saw 39 reports with an average response time of about 2.6 hours.

Some case types showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Tree Maintenance requests averaged just 45 minutes, Vegetation issues averaged 51 minutes, and Community Center reports averaged 72 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

On the slower end of the daily spectrum, Light issues took an average of 17.6 hours to process, while Animal Services averaged 16 hours.
