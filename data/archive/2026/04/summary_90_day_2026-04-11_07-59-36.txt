Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest look at city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to dominate service requests. In the last 90 days, there were 7,000 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of about 8.5 days. Graffiti saw 3,251 reports, averaging a response time of just over 2 days.

Infrastructure issues like street and sidewalk repairs logged 732 and 363 calls respectively. Street repairs took an average of 7 days to address, while sidewalk repairs averaged about 5 days. Tree maintenance received 899 requests, taking about 7.5 days on average.

Some categories show unusually fast response times, which may indicate administrative closures rather than completed work. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes.
