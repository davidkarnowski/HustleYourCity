Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Over 1,450 new requests were submitted. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (573 requests), followed by e-scooter issues (266 requests) and graffiti removal (209 requests).

Response times for closed requests varied. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 25 hours. Reports of dumped items were closed in just under 2 days on average. The longest response time was for a marina repair, which took over 4 days. One restroom maintenance request was closed in about 16 minutes; however, unusually fast closures can sometimes be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
