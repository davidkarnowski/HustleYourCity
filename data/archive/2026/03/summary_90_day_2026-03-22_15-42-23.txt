Hustle Long Beach! brings you the latest 90-day review of city service response times. This data helps keep the public informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed across the community.

Over the past 90 days, Dumped Items led the service requests with 6,725 reports, taking an average of 9.6 days to resolve. Graffiti followed with 3,055 reports, averaging a 71-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints also saw high volume with 2,791 reports.

Tree Maintenance requests, totaling 945 calls, took an average of 6.7 days to address. Street Repair saw 838 calls and averaged 6.4 days. Dead Animal Pickup showed a faster turnaround, with 440 calls averaging 11.7 hours.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast resolution times. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city. Reporting problems helps keep the community running smoothly.

This data summary was updated at March 22, 2026, 8:38 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 22, 2026, 8:00 AM PDT.
