Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 2,400 requests for dumped item pickups, making it the most reported issue. The average response time to close these requests was about 8 days. Graffiti was the next most common report with over 1,000 requests, which were addressed in about 2.5 days on average.

Response times for street and light repairs averaged just under 3 days. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 11 hours on average. Some services show very fast closure times, which can be due to administrative closures and not always on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
