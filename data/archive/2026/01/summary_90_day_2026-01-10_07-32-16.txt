Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,961 calls) and graffiti (3,568 calls). The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 4.5 days, while dumped item reports took an average of nearly two weeks.

Response times varied significantly by service type. Reports for animal services and dead animal pickups were closed in under 17 hours on average. In contrast, the average response for park maintenance was about 14 days. Some unusually fast responses, like a 2-hour average for damaged drinking fountains, may reflect administrative closures rather than completed repairs.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
