The latest City of Long Beach service call data has been processed for the Hustle Long Beach! community. A review of the last 30 days shows significant volume in sanitation and infrastructure requests across the city.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 2,275 requests and an average response time of 5.7 days. Graffiti removal saw 1,014 reports, taking an average of 39.5 hours to address. E-Scooter complaints totaled 963, though these are typically referred directly to the operating vendors rather than handled by city staff.

Tree maintenance received 376 calls with an average response of 54.7 hours. Street repair requests totaled 256, taking about 3.2 days on average. Park maintenance requests took an average of 4.3 days to resolve across 70 reports. On the faster side, running water issues averaged a 7.9-hour response time. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures or referrals rather than actual physical work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps ensure transparency and keeps the local government accountable for neighborhood maintenance.
