Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 583 requests, followed by graffiti with 269 reports. The city also processed 247 e-scooter reports, which are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Average response times for key services include about 47 hours for dumped items and about 22.5 hours for graffiti. Tree maintenance requests were addressed in an average of under 8 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it using the Go Long Beach app to help the city track and address problems.
