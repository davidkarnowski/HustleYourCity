Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the last 90 days, residents submitted nearly 6,000 reports for illegally dumped items and over 3,600 for graffiti, making them the two most common service requests.

Based on the data, the average time to resolve a request for dumped items is about two weeks. Park maintenance requests are taking slightly longer, at over 17 days. In contrast, graffiti reports are being addressed in about five and a half days, and sidewalk repairs are being closed in just under four days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city.

This data summary was updated at December 25, 2025, 3:29 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 25, 2025, 3:00 PM PST.
