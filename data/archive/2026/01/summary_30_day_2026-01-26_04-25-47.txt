Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past month, the most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped items (2,312 calls), e-scooters (1,015 calls), and graffiti (972 calls).

The average response time for dumped item pickups was about 8 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in just under 3 days on average. Reports for street repair took about 3 days to close. Dead animal pickups were handled more quickly, with an average response time of about 11 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at January 25, 2026, at 8:23 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 25, 2026, at 7:00 PM PST.
