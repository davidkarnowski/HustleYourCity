Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past 90 days, "Dumped Items" was the most frequent request type with over 6,300 reports. The average response time to close these requests was about 14 days. Graffiti was the second most common report with over 3,200 submissions, which were addressed in an average of about 5 days.

Some services had faster response times. Reports for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services were typically resolved in about 12 to 13 hours. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of about 3.5 days.

Over 2,700 reports were filed for E-Scooters. These are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling and are not resolved by city staff.

To report issues like graffiti, potholes, or dumped items, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
