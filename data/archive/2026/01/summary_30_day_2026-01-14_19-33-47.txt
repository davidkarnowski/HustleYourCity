Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the last month, the city received 2,037 reports for Dumped Items, 935 for E-Scooters, and 903 for Graffiti. The average response time to close a Dumped Items report was about 5 days, while Graffiti reports were closed in about 2 days on average. E-scooter reports are referred directly to vendors.

Response times for other common issues included Street Repair at about 3.5 days and Trash & Debris collection at nearly 7 days. Reports for Dead Animal Pickup were handled more quickly, with an average response time of about 14 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application to report it. This helps ensure the city has the data it needs to respond.

This data summary was updated at January 14, 2026, at 11:31 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 14, 2026, at 11:00 AM PST.
