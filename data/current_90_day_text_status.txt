The latest city service data for the past 90 days shows significant activity across several categories. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,879 total reports and an average response time of approximately 15 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,652 reports and an average response time of about 62 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 1,068 reports over the last 90 days with an average response time of about 4 days. Dead animal pickup remains one of the fastest services, averaging roughly 7 hours per request across 539 reports.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
