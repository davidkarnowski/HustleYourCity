Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 90-day city service request data to keep residents informed on response times and call volumes.

Dumped items generated the highest volume with 6,751 reports over the last 90 days, taking an average of 9.5 days to resolve. Graffiti removal follows with 3,062 reports, averaging a 68-hour turnaround. E-scooter complaints reached 2,844, though these are entirely referred to outside agencies rather than closed by city staff.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests show longer wait times. Tree maintenance saw 966 requests with an average response time of 6.6 days. Street repairs had 847 requests, averaging 6.4 days for a response. Sidewalk repairs, with 304 requests, are taking just under 4 days on average.

Some services are addressed much faster. Dead animal pickups, totaling 431 requests, are handled in about 11.5 hours. A few categories show unusually fast completion times, such as business license concerns averaging just 12 minutes. These extremely low response times are likely due to administrative closures rather than actual field work completion.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at March 24, 2026, 1:01 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 24, 2026, 12:00 AM PDT.
