Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data to keep you informed on city response times.

Dumped items generated the highest call volume with 2332 reports. The city took an average of 5.8 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1035 calls, averaging a 36-hour response time.

Other notable service times include dead animal pickups, which averaged 9 hours across 147 reports. Park maintenance took an average of 4.3 days for 73 reports, while sidewalk repairs averaged 3 days for 109 reports. Marina repairs and illegal fireworks reports took the longest, averaging 7.4 days and 5.8 days respectively. 

Please note that unusually fast response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help maintain the city.
