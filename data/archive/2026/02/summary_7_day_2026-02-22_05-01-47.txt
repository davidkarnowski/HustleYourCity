An independent review of Long Beach city service requests for the last 7 days highlights the most reported issues and current response times.

Dumped Items remained the most frequent report, with 409 total entries. The average time to resolve these issues was approximately 1.2 days.

Tree Maintenance and Graffiti were also high-volume categories. The city received 234 Tree Maintenance requests, averaging a 1 day response time. Graffiti reports totaled 233, with an average resolution time of 1.3 days.

Vegetation reports showed an average response time of roughly 3 minutes. Response times under 90 minutes often reflect administrative closures or automated referrals rather than field work completion.
