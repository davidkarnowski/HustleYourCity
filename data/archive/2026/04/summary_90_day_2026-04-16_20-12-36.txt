Hustle Long Beach! is providing a new accountability update on city service response times. Based on the latest data from the last 90 days, here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public service requests.

Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue with 6,935 calls. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti reports were the second highest with 3,236 calls, averaging a response time of 58 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests require more time. Tree maintenance saw 893 calls with an average response time of 9 days, while street repair had 703 calls averaging nearly 8 days for a response.

Some categories show exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help keep the community data accurate.

This data summary was updated at April 16, 2026, 1:08 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 16, 2026, 11:00 AM PDT.
