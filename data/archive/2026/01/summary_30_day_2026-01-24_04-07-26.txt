Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past month, residents submitted over 5,800 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items with 2,284 calls, followed by e-scooter issues with 955 calls, and graffiti removal with 937 calls.

Response times varied by issue type. Requests for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were completed in about 12 hours on average. The most frequent issue, illegally dumped items, took an average of about 8 days to resolve. Graffiti removal requests were handled in about 3 days on average.
