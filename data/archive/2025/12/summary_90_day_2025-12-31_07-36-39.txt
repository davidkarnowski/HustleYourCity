Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days.

The most frequent requests were for Dumped Items (5,892 calls) and Graffiti removal (3,606 calls). According to city data, the average response time to close a Dumped Items request was about two weeks. Graffiti requests were resolved faster, with an average completion time of about 5 days.

Response times for other key services varied. Park Maintenance requests took over two weeks on average to resolve, while Street Repair calls averaged about 10 days. Faster responses were recorded for Sidewalk Repair (4 days) and Animal Services (about 15 hours).
