Here is a look at the latest Long Beach city service data from the past 30 days. Residents submitted approximately 4,300 service requests during this period.

The most frequently reported issues were illegally dumped items with 1,896 reports, followed by e-scooter issues with 1,012 reports, and graffiti with 951 reports.

Average response times for closed requests varied by issue type. The city's data shows an average response time of about 8.5 days for dumped items and about 2.5 days for graffiti removal. Some services had quicker turnarounds, such as dead animal pickups, which averaged about 14 hours.
