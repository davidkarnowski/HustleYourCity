Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 30 days.

The most reported issues were illegally dumped items with 2,501 requests and graffiti with 1,065 requests. According to the data, the average response time to close a dumped item report was about 8 days. Graffiti reports were closed in an average of about 2.5 days.

Response times vary by issue. Animal-related services were among the fastest, resolved in under 14 hours on average. Slower responses were seen for issues like damaged drinking fountains, which took an average of about 11 days to close. The 968 e-scooter reports were automatically referred to vendors.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the city's Go Long Beach application.
