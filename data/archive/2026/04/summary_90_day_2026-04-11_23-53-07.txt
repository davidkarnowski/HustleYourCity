Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is responding to community issues.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 7,001 requests, followed by graffiti with 3,263 reports, and e-scooter complaints at 2,922. 

Response times vary significantly by category. On average, dumped items take about 8.5 days to resolve, and tree maintenance requests take about 7.6 days. Graffiti is addressed in approximately 55 hours, while dead animal pickups are handled in about 11 hours.

Some categories show unusually fast resolution times. For example, business license and permit concerns average a 12-minute response time. These extremely low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
