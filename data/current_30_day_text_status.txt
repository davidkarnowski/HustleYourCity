Graffiti reports topped the last‑30‑day list with 1,387 requests. The city closed 1,041 cases and the average response time was about 49 hours (just over 2 days), a noticeable improvement from the all‑time average of 351 hours.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume overall (1,996 requests). About half were closed and the average response time dropped to roughly 201 hours (8 days), down from the all‑time average of 260 hours.

Street Repair saw 177 new tickets; 73 were closed and the average response time fell to 16 hours, far quicker than the historic 4,523‑hour average.

Tree Maintenance handled 229 requests with an average response of 10 hours, a big gain compared with the all‑time 3,778‑hour average.

Light outages (238 tickets) were resolved in an average of 33 hours, while Sidewalk Repair (103 tickets) took about 17 hours on average.

Overall, the city is closing a larger share of tickets faster across several service types, though many cases remain “In Progress.”

Residents can track and report issues directly through the Go Long Beach app.
