Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

The most frequent request from the public was for "Dumped Items", with over 6,400 reports. The average time to close these requests was about 14 days. The second most common request was for "Graffiti" removal, with over 3,200 reports and an average response time of over 4.5 days. Reports for street repair and tree maintenance took an average of about 5 days to resolve.

Some services saw faster responses. Requests for "Animal Services" and "Dead Animal Pickup" were closed in about 12 hours on average. All 421 requests for dead animal pickup were marked as closed, which may be due to administrative closures and not reflect actual work completion. Sidewalk repair requests were addressed in about 3 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
