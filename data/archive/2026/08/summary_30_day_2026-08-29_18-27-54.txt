The latest city service data for Long Beach shows a high volume of activity over the past 30 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 2,956 reports recorded. Of these, 1,107 are closed and 1,154 remain in progress, with an average response time of approximately 8 days.

Graffiti removal saw 932 reports, with 726 successfully closed. The average response time for graffiti cleanup is currently 48 hours. Tree maintenance requests totaled 301, with 173 closed and an average response time of about 3 days.

Other notable service categories include street repairs, which averaged 90 hours for a response, and sidewalk repairs, which averaged 113 hours. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging 11 hours and 7 hours respectively.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
