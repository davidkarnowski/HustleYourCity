Here is the latest third-party review of city service response data for the last 30 days. This is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public requests.

Dumped Items led the request volume with 2,272 reports, taking an average of 6.8 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 989 calls, which the city addressed in 34.6 hours on average. Street Repair saw 256 requests, taking an average of 3 days to complete.

Some of the fastest resolutions were for Dead Animal Pickup, averaging 9 hours, and Running Water issues at 10.5 hours. On the other end, Marina Repair took an average of 7.4 days, and Park Maintenance requests took 4.1 days. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

Citizens can help keep the community accountable and well-maintained. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
