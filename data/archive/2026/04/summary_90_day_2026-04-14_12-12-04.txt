Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 90 days. Here is the breakdown of city service response times and call totals to keep the public informed.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues. There were 6941 reports for dumped items with an average response time of 8.5 days. Graffiti had 3264 reports, averaging a 2.3-day response time. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 2959 reports.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests show longer turnaround times. Tree maintenance, with 897 reports, averaged 8.2 days for a response. Street repair had 708 reports and took about 7.7 days to address. Sidewalk repairs, totaling 377 reports, averaged nearly 6 days.

Some services show very rapid closures. Business license and permit concerns averaged a 12-minute response time across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead animal pickups handled 460 reports with an average 10-hour response time.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 14, 2026 5:08 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 14, 2026 3:00 AM PDT.
