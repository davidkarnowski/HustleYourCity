Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest city service data to keep the community informed. Over the past 30 days, residents submitted thousands of requests. Here is a breakdown of the most recent call totals and response times.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 2227 calls in the last month. The average response time for these reports is 5.7 days. Graffiti removal is the second most common request with 1000 reports, averaging a 38.6-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints reached 945, though all of these were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable service areas include street repair, which saw 251 requests and an average response time of 71.8 hours. Park maintenance received 69 calls, taking about 4.4 days to address. Reports of illegal fireworks took 7.3 days to process on average. Please note that unusually fast response times in certain categories may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
