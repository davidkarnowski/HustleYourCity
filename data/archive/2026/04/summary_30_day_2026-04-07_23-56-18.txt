Hustle Long Beach! is providing the latest 30-day accountability update on city service requests. Here is a look at the volume and response times for issues reported to the City of Long Beach.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 2354 reports. The city averaged a response time of about 8 days for these requests.

Graffiti removal saw 996 reports over the last month. The average response time was about 35 hours.

E-Scooter complaints generated 905 reports. All of these were referred to outside vendors or agencies for closure.

Street Repair requests totaled 209, with an average response time of about 3 days.

Dead Animal Pickup was handled relatively quickly. The city received 165 reports and averaged a 4-hour response time.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
