Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests over the past 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti were the most reported issues, with over 6,400 and 3,200 requests filed, respectively. The average response time to close a dumped items request was about 13.5 days, while graffiti removal took about 4.5 days.

The city responded faster to reports of dead animals and other animal service needs, with both categories averaging around 12 hours for closure. Some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures and not always on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
