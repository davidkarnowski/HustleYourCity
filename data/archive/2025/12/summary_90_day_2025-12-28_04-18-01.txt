Here is a look at Long Beach service request data for the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most frequent reports continue to be for dumped items (5,951 requests) and graffiti (3,662 requests).

According to the city's data, the average time to resolve a Park Maintenance request was about 17 days. For Dumped Items, the average response time was just over 15 days. In contrast, requests for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were typically closed in under one day, with average response times of about 15 and 16 hours, respectively. The average for Graffiti removal was about 5.4 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
