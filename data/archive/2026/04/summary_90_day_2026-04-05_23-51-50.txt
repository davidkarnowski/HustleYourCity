Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest independent data summary of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days.

Dumped Items remain the highest reported issue with 6,982 calls. The city average response time for these requests is nearly 9 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,161, with an average response time of just over 2 days. Street Repair saw 782 requests, taking an average of about 7 days to address.

Some categories show extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets tracked. Hustle Long Beach encourages all followers to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
