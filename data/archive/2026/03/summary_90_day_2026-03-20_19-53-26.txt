Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Over the past 90 days, the city received thousands of reports across various departments. Here is a breakdown of call totals and average response times.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6690 reports. The average response time for these requests was nearly 10 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3090 calls, averaging a 71-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 2822 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies with no city response time recorded.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant volume. Tree Maintenance received 932 reports with an average response of nearly 7 days. Street Repair had 838 requests, also averaging nearly 7 days. Sidewalk Repair logged 302 calls with an average response time of nearly 4 days.

Some services saw much faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup handled 434 requests in an average of 12 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes for 6 requests. Unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help track neighborhood improvements.
