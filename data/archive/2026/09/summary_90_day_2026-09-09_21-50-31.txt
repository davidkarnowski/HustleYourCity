The latest data from the City of Long Beach service calls shows significant activity over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 8,212 reports, followed by 3,057 graffiti reports and 2,941 e-scooter reports.

Response times vary by service type. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show the fastest resolution times, averaging 12 hours and 6.5 hours respectively.
