Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

The most frequent requests were for Dumped Items (1,821 calls), E-Scooters (1,009 calls), and Graffiti (893 calls). The average response time to close a Dumped Items request was about 8 days. For Graffiti, the response time was about 2 days. E-scooter reports are referred directly to vendors and not handled by city staff.

Other key services included Street Repair, with an average response time of about 40 hours, and Tree Maintenance, which took about 28 hours. Reports for streetlight issues were addressed in about 2 days on average.

Response times for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were faster, averaging about 12 and 14 hours, respectively. In contrast, requests for Trash and Debris cleanup took an average of nearly 9 days to resolve. Some requests show very fast closure times, which may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it through the Go Long Beach service application.
