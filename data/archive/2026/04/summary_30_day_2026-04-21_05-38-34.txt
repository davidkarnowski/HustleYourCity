Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at how the city responded to citizen reports over the last 30 days.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 2,357 calls. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was another major issue with 1,078 reports, which had an average response time of 51 hours. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume, generating 1,079 reports.

Other notable service areas include street repairs, which saw 208 calls and an average response time of 62 hours. Dead animal pickups were handled faster, averaging a 7-hour response for 164 calls.
