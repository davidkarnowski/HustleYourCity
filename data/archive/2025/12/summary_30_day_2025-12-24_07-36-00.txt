Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 30 days.

The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (1,952 calls), E-Scooters (1,073 calls), and Graffiti (1,062 calls). The average response time to close a Dumped Items report was about 5 and a half days, while Graffiti reports were addressed in just over 2 days. E-Scooter reports are referred directly to vendors and do not have a city response time.

Other key service response times include Street Repair at nearly 3 days and Park Maintenance at about 3 and a half days. More urgent requests like Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were handled in about 10 hours and 13 hours, respectively.
