Here is a look at Long Beach service request data for the past week. City departments handled over 1,200 requests from the public. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (476 calls), graffiti (260 calls), and e-scooter issues (253 calls).

According to the data, the average time to close a request for dumped items was about 2.2 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 1.7 days on average, and street repairs took about 1.1 days.

Some service requests, like sidewalk repair and restroom maintenance, show unusually fast closure times of about 45 and 51 minutes respectively. These may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
