Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 90 days.

The most common service requests were for Dumped Items (6,075 calls) and Graffiti (3,710 calls). The average response time to resolve a dumped item report was about two weeks, while graffiti reports were addressed in just under 6 days on average.

Faster responses were seen for more urgent issues. Reports for Animal Services (179 calls) and Dead Animal Pickup (435 calls) were typically closed in about 15-16 hours.

Longer-term infrastructure requests included Street Repair, with an average response time of about 10 days, and Park Maintenance, which took over two weeks on average to resolve.
