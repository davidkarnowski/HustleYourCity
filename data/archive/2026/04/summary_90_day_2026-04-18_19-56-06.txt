Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest 90-day accountability summary of city service requests.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of calls with 6,984 reports. The city averaged an 8.5-day response time for these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,233 calls, which took an average of 2.3 days to address.

Tree Maintenance requests, totaling 888 calls, experienced some of the longest wait times, averaging 9 days for a response. Street Repair also saw extended waits, with 710 calls averaging an 8-day response time.

On the faster end, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickup handled 467 calls with a standard average response time of 9.8 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Tracking these requests helps ensure transparency and accountability for neighborhood services.

This data summary was updated at April 18, 2026 at 12:52 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 18, 2026 at 11:00 AM PDT.
