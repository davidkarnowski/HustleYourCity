Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (6,284 requests), graffiti (3,344 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,683 requests).

Average response times for closing a service request varied by issue. Reports for dumped items were closed in about 13 days, while graffiti removal took about 5 days. Requests for street repair were completed in an average of 5.5 days. Some of the longest response times were for marina repairs, which took about 5 weeks to close. Quicker responses included dead animal pickups, averaging about 13 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
