The latest data for Long Beach city services shows a high volume of activity over the past 7 days. Residents submitted 511 reports regarding dumped items, making it the most frequent service request this week. While many of these remain in progress or new, graffiti removal continues to be a priority with 100 reports closed in the last week.

Response times for several categories remain efficient. Dead animal pickups were completed in an average of 4 minutes, and sidewalk repair reports showed an average response time of 54 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. This tool is the most direct way to notify the city of maintenance needs and track the status of your requests. Keeping the city informed helps ensure that resources are directed where they are needed most.
