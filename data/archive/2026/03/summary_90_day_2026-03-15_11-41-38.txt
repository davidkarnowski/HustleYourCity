Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the numbers over the last 90 days to keep you informed on how city services are performing.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,636 calls, taking an average of 10 days to resolve. Graffiti reports followed with 3,078 calls, which the city addressed in about 3 days on average. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 2,893 calls, all of which were referred to other agencies.

Other notable response times include Street Repair at just over 6 days and Tree Maintenance at nearly 6 days. On the faster end, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 13 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns showed a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 15, 2026 4:37 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 15, 2026 4:00 AM PDT.
