Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 90 days.

Illegally dumped items and graffiti removal were the most frequent service requests, with a combined total of over 9,600 reports filed by residents.

According to the data, the average response time to close a graffiti report was just under 5 days. For dumped items, the average was about 2 weeks. Reports for marina repairs had the longest average completion time at approximately 5 weeks, while requests for dead animal pickup were typically resolved in about 12 hours. Some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures and not on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
