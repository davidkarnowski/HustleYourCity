City of Long Beach service requests in the past 90 days total about 15,800 calls across all categories.

Key highlights:
- Graffiti reports remain the highest volume (3,704) with an average response time of 159 hours (about 6.6 days). 3,317 have been closed.
- Dumped items (6,194 reports) are being addressed in an average of 372 hours (≈15.5 days). 4,827 are closed or referred.
- Animal Services (199 reports) and Dead Animal Pickup (426 reports) are resolved quickly, averaging 17 hours each.
- Tree Maintenance (725 reports) and Light repairs (636 reports) have average response times of 154 hours and 193 hours respectively (≈6–8 days).
- Sidewalk Repair (315 reports) and Street Repair (662 reports) are being handled in about 5–10 days on average.
- Some categories still see longer waits: Park Maintenance (177 reports) averages 443 hours (≈18.5 days) and Restroom Maintenance (20 reports) averages 424 hours (≈17.7 days).

Overall, most service types are being closed within a week, though a few areas could benefit from faster turnaround.

Residents can help speed up resolutions by reporting issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app.
