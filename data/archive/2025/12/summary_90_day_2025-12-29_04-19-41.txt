Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were for dumped items (5,905 requests), graffiti (3,621 requests), and illegally parked e-scooters (2,457 requests). In total, Long Beach residents submitted over 14,000 service requests during this period.

Average response times for closed requests varied by service type. Requests for dumped items were addressed in about 15 days, while graffiti removal took an average of about 5 days. Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 16 hours. Some service requests show unusually fast response times, which may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? You can report it directly to the city by using the Go Long Beach service application.
