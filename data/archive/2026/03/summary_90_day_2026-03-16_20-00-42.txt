Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of requests, and response times varied significantly depending on the type of issue.

Dumped items generated the highest volume with 6,650 reports. The city took an average of 10 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most common issue with 3,077 reports, which took an average of 71 hours to address.

Infrastructure requests also showed extended wait times. Street repair received 832 calls and averaged 6 days for a response. Tree maintenance had 907 reports, taking an average of nearly 7 days to resolve.

Some services saw much faster turnarounds. Dead animal pickups averaged 12 hours across 443 reports, and general animal services averaged 16 hours for 234 reports. Notably, business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help keep neighborhoods maintained.
