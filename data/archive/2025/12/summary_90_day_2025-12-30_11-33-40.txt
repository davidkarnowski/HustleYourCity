Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days. Residents submitted over 15,400 requests through the Go Long Beach system, with the most common reports for illegally dumped items (5,918 calls), graffiti (3,631 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,489 calls).

Average city response times for closed cases varied by issue. Reports for dumped items were addressed in about 15 days on average, while graffiti removal took about 5 days. Faster responses were recorded for urgent issues like dead animal pickups and other animal services, which were handled in under one day, averaging about 16 and 15 hours respectively.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to get it into the city's queue.
