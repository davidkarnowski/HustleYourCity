Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,500 service requests were made through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (634 calls), graffiti (284 calls), and e-scooters (255 calls).

Response times varied by issue. The average time to resolve a dumped item report was 66 hours. Reports of running water took an average of nearly 6 days to close. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickup and tree maintenance were typically handled in under 18 hours. E-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies for resolution.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
