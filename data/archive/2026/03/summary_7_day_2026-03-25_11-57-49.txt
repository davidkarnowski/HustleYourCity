Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day city service response data to keep citizens informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 529 reports. The average response time for these was 3.6 days, with 396 requests still marked as new. E-Scooter issues followed with 235 reports, all of which were immediately referred to a closed status.

Graffiti removal teams handled 192 reports with an average response time of 25.5 hours. Tree Maintenance received 80 reports, averaging a 19.6-hour response. Street Repair addressed 63 reports in an average of 23.7 hours.

Some services experienced longer delays. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports took an average of 4.7 days to process across 11 total calls. 

On the faster end, Dead Animal Pickup handled 45 reports in an average of 3.7 hours. Restroom Maintenance and Community Center issues were resolved in 35 minutes and 72 minutes, respectively. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
