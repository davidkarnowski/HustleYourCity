Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past 90 days, the most frequently reported issues were for dumped items (5,961 requests), graffiti (3,570 requests), and e-scooters (2,647 requests).

Average response times for key services include about 4.5 days for graffiti removal and about 13.5 days for dumped item cleanup. Street repair requests were addressed in an average of about 7.5 days. Animal service calls, including dead animal pickups, were handled in about 16 hours. Some issues show very fast response times, which may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.

To report issues like graffiti, potholes, or dumped items, use the city's Go Long Beach service application.
