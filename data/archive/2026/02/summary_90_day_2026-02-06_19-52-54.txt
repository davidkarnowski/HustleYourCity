Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data from the past 90 days.

The most frequent issues reported by residents were for dumped items, with 6,391 requests, and graffiti, with 3,263 requests.

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report was about 13.7 days. For graffiti reports, the average response time was about 4.6 days. Some of the quickest responses were for "Animal Services" and "Dead Animal Pickup," with both averaging around 12 hours for completion.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the most effective way to report it is through the city's Go Long Beach service application.
