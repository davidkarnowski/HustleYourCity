Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the past week.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped items (413 calls), graffiti (188 calls), and e-scooters (166 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 50 hours. Dumped item pickups took about 39 hours on average. Tree maintenance requests were addressed in about 24 hours.

Some services saw much faster resolutions. Reports for restroom maintenance were closed in an average of 85 minutes, and park maintenance issues in about 37 minutes. These faster times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
