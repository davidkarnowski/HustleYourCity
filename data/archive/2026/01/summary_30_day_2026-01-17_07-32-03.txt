Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past 30 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (2,066 requests), e-scooter issues (972 requests), and graffiti (898 requests).

Average city response times for key services varied. The average time to close a graffiti report was about 2 days (49 hours). For dumped items, the average response was about 4.5 days (109 hours). Reports for street repair took an average of 3.3 days (80 hours) to close. Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 12 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
