Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 90 days.

Over 16,300 service requests were made by residents. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,318 requests), graffiti (3,333 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,668 requests).

Average response times for key services include:
- Dumped Items: 13.5 days
- Graffiti: 5 days
- Street Repair: 5.4 days
- Dead Animal Pickup: 13 hours

Some services show very fast closure times. For example, animal service calls in the last 24 hours were closed in an average of 16 minutes. These quick closures may be due to administrative processing and not on-site work completion.
