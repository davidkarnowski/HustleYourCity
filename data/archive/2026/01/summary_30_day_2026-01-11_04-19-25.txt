Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service call data.

In the past month, "Dumped Items" was the most frequent report, with 1,931 service calls. The average response time to close these reports was about 6 days. Graffiti was the next most common issue with 945 reports, which were addressed in an average of just over 2 days.

Response times for infrastructure issues varied. Reports for streetlight outages (290 calls) took an average of about 3.5 days to resolve, while traffic signal repairs (41 calls) averaged over 6 days. In contrast, calls for dead animal pickups were typically completed in about 14 hours.
