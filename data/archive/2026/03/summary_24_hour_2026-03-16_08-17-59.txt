Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour city service request data to keep the community informed on local government response times and call volumes.

Over the past day, Dumped Items led the service requests with 60 reports, followed by Graffiti with 35 reports. Other notable request totals include 8 for E-Scooters, 5 for Street Repair, and 5 for Tree Maintenance. The vast majority of these recent requests remain in new or in-progress status.

The average response time for Dumped Items over the last 24 hours was exceptionally fast at 32 minutes. It is important to note that unusually low response times like this are often the result of administrative closures or duplicate logging rather than actual physical work being completed. Animal Services requests that were closed in the last day averaged a response time of 3 hours.
