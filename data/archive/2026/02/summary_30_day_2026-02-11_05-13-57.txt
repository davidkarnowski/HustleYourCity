Here is the latest evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests from the last 30 days.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 2,585 reports filed. The average response time for these requests is currently 8 days. Graffiti reports followed with 1,121 total calls and an average completion time of just over 2 days.

Infrastructure requests show mixed timelines. Street Repair generated 258 reports with an average wait of 3 and a half days. Street Light issues saw 438 reports, taking nearly 3 days to resolve. Conversely, Dead Animal Pickup services averaged 11 hours across 153 requests.
