Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times for the past week.

Over 1,200 service requests were submitted. Dumped items were the most reported issue with 442 requests, and the average response time was just under 2 days.

The data shows longer response times for certain services. Traffic signal repairs averaged nearly 5 days, while trash and debris cleanups took about 3 days.

Some issues were addressed more quickly. The average response time for restroom maintenance was about 83 minutes. Note that very fast response times can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city.
