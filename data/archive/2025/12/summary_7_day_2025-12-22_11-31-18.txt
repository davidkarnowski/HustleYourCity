Here is a look at Long Beach's service request data for the past week.

The most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (429 calls), e-scooters (248 calls), and graffiti (237 calls).

On average, the city closed graffiti reports in about 21 hours. The response time for dumped item reports was about 33 hours. Some categories showed unusually fast response times. For example, sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of about 45 minutes. These short times may be due to administrative closures and not the time for work to be completed on-site.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
