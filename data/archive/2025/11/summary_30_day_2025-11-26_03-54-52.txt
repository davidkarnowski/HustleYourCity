In the last 30 days Long Beach reported 1,993 dumped‑item calls, 1,417 graffiti reports, 231 tree‑maintenance requests and 207 street‑repair tickets.  

The average response time for dumped items dropped to 168.6 hours (about 7 days), down from 359 hours in the prior 90‑day window. Graffiti response improved to 70.9 hours (≈3 days) versus 152 hours before. Tree maintenance is now being addressed in 9.6 hours on average, a big gain from 40.7 hours previously.  

Sidewalk repairs are being closed in 14.7 hours (≈15 hours) compared with 42.4 hours earlier. Light‑related issues average 42.2 hours, and traffic‑signal work is completed in 23.5 hours. Other categories show response times under two days: park maintenance 46.1 hours, trash & debris 71.5 hours, dead‑animal pickup 19 hours, and animal services 18.4 hours.  

These faster times may reflect administrative closures rather than actual work, so very low figures should be interpreted with caution.  

Residents can continue to report issues quickly through the Go Long Beach service app.
