Hustle Long Beach! community members, here is the latest third-party evaluation of City of Long Beach service call data focusing on the past 7 days.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to generate the highest volume of service requests. Over the last week, there were 509 reports for dumped items with an average response time of 3.2 days. Graffiti removal saw 227 reports, averaging an 18.8-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 189 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable service times include street repair with 68 requests averaging a 15.9-hour response, and tree maintenance with 65 requests averaging an 18.1-hour response.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast response times. Restroom maintenance requests averaged a 50-minute response, and dead animal pickups averaged a 6.1-hour response. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 27, 2026, 4:45 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 27, 2026, 4:01 PM PDT.
