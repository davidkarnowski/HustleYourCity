The City of Long Beach service data for the past 90 days shows 23,500 requests across all categories. The highest volumes were Dumped Items (6,160 requests), Graffiti (3,722) and E‑Scooter reports (2,125).  

Average response times varied widely. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were resolved quickly, with average times of 16.7 hours and 16.5 hours respectively. Graffiti took about 162 hours (≈ 6.8 days) on average, while Dumped Items required roughly 384 hours (≈ 16 days). Light repairs, Tree Maintenance and Street Repair each averaged between 150 and 240 hours (≈ 6‑10 days).  

Categories with the fastest turn‑around included Shoes on a Utility Wire (33 hours), Vegetation (46 hours) and Abandoned Shopping Cart (45 hours). Very low response times in a few cases may reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completion.  

Residents can help keep the city accountable by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service app, which streams real‑time updates to city crews.
