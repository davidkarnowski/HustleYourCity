Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,288 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (462 calls), e-scooter issues (225 calls), and graffiti removal (211 calls).

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 32 hours. Requests for dumped items took an average of 2 days to resolve, while park maintenance requests took about 3.5 days. Restroom maintenance calls were closed in an average of about 83 minutes, though this may reflect administrative closures.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.
