Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service requests for the past 90 days.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue with 6,386 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 13 days. Graffiti removal received 3,159 reports with an average completion time of 4 days.

Street Repair requests, which cover potholes, totaled 781 reports and are averaging 5 days for a response. Tree Maintenance requests are taking approximately 8 days to resolve. Dead Animal Pickup shows a faster turnaround, averaging 11 hours per request.

Please continue to report issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
