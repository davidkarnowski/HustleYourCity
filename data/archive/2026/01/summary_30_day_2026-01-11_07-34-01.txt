Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days.

The most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (1,931 calls), e-scooter issues (969 calls), and graffiti (939 calls).

According to the data, the average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 2.25 days. Reports of illegally dumped items took an average of about 6 days to be addressed. Service calls for trash and debris saw a longer response time, averaging nearly 10 days.

Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups and animal services, both averaging around 14 hours.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application. This helps create a record of the request and provides data for public review.
