Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day city service request data to keep the public informed on response times and call volumes.

Dumped items continue to generate the most service calls. The city received 6,632 reports for dumped items over the last 90 days, with an average response time of 10 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,068 calls, taking an average of 3 days to address.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw high volumes. Street repair logged 831 calls with an average response time of 6 days. Tree maintenance received 905 requests, also averaging a 6-day response.

Some categories show unusually fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a 12-minute response time across 6 reports. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead animal pickups showed a 12-hour average response time across 441 reports.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 16, 2026, 9:20 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 16, 2026, 8:00 AM PDT.
