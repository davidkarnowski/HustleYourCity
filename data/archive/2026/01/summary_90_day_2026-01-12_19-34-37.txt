Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on the latest data.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for Dumped Items (5,941 requests) and Graffiti removal (3,491 requests). The average response time to resolve a Dumped Items report was about 13 days. For Graffiti, the average response was about 4.5 days.

Response times for Street Repair and Tree Maintenance requests averaged about one week. Faster responses were seen for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services calls, which were handled in about 15 hours on average. Some service requests, like for damaged drinking fountains, show unusually fast closure times of around 2 hours, which may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
