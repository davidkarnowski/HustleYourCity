Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data. In the past month, residents submitted over 5,200 reports for city services.

The most common issues reported were illegally dumped items (2,029 reports), e-scooter issues (1,030 reports), and graffiti (1,016 reports). These three categories represent the majority of all service requests made.

Response times for closed cases varied by issue type. The average time to resolve a report for dumped items was about 10 days. Graffiti clean-up requests were addressed in an average of about 2 days. Faster responses were recorded for issues like dead animal pickups, which took an average of about 11.5 hours to complete.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at 11:32 PM PST on Dec 29, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 PM PST on Dec 29, 2025.
