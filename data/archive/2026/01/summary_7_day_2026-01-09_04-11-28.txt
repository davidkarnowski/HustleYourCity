Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,359 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (537 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (217), and graffiti (198).

For requests completed during this period, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 34 hours. Street repair requests were closed in an average of 21 hours, while dumped item cleanups took an average of 45 hours. Of the 537 dumped item reports, many are still listed as new or in progress. E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.
