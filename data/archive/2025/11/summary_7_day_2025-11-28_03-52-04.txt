City service response times for the past week show notable improvements.

Graffiti reports dropped to an average response of 30.8 hours (about 1.3 days) for 266 tickets, down from 163 hours in the last 90 days. Light issues were addressed in an average of 26.1 hours for 71 requests, compared with 39 hours previously. Street repair cases now average 11.7 hours for 46 tickets, a big jump from the 82‑hour average seen earlier. Tree maintenance responded in 9.0 hours for 42 jobs, versus 38 hours before. Dumped items were handled in 33.1 hours on average for 319 reports, improving from 368 hours. Dead animal pickups were completed in just 7.0 hours for 23 cases.

Overall, the city processed 1,165 service calls in the last 7 days, with most categories showing faster turnaround. Some unusually low times may reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

Residents can continue to report issues quickly through the Go Long Beach service app.
