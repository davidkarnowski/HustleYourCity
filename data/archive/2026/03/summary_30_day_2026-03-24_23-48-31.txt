Here is the latest Hustle Long Beach! data summary evaluating city service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items led the service requests with 2,317 reports. The city took an average of nearly 6 days to resolve these issues. Graffiti followed with 1,037 reports, which saw an average response time of 36 hours.

Other notable categories include Sidewalk Repair, which received 110 calls and averaged 3 days for a response. Street Repair received 244 calls with an average response time of 68 hours. On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup handled 146 calls with an average response time of 9 hours.

Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
