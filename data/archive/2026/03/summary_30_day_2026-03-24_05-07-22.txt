Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public requests.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues. In the last 30 days, citizens reported 2258 dumped items, taking the city an average of 5.7 days to respond. Graffiti reports totaled 1017, with an average response time of about 37 hours. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 942 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Dead animal pickups were handled quickly, averaging just under 9 hours for 144 requests. Conversely, park maintenance requests took an average of 4.6 days to address, and marina repairs took 7.4 days. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work.
