Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,561 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (658 requests), graffiti (292), and e-scooters (260).

The average response time for dumped item reports was about 3 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 32 hours on average. Reports of running water had one of the longest response times this week, averaging nearly 6 days. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups and tree maintenance, both averaging around 15-16 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach app to help the city track and address community needs.
