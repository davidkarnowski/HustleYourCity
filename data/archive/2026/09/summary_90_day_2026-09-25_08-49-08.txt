The latest city service data for Long Beach shows ongoing activity across key maintenance categories over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 8,449 reports logged. While many are resolved, the average response time for this category is approximately 17 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,939 reports and an average response time of about 3 days.

Tree maintenance requests totaled 853 over the same period, with an average response time of about 3 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show the fastest turnaround times, often resolved in under 12 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

To report issues such as potholes, graffiti, or dumped items, please use the Go Long Beach service application. Providing accurate locations and photos helps ensure requests are routed to the correct departments efficiently.
