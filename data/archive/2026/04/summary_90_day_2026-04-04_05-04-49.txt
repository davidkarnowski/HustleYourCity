Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest 90-day accountability summary of city service requests.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues. Over the last 90 days, there were 7,051 reports for dumped items and 3,142 for graffiti. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 2,866 reports, though nearly all were referred to outside agencies.

Response times vary significantly by category. The average response time for dumped items is currently taking about 9 days. Graffiti is being addressed in approximately 59 hours. Street repair requests, totaling 820 calls, are taking about 6.7 days on average to resolve. Tree maintenance, with 925 reports, is taking about 4.9 days.

Some services show very fast turnaround times. Dead animal pickups average just over 10 hours. Business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Stay engaged and help keep Long Beach accountable. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 3, 2026, 10:01 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 3, 2026, 7:00 PM PDT.
