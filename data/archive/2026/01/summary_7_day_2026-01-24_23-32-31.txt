Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1395 requests. The top issues reported were dumped items with 593 requests, graffiti with 246, and e-scooter issues with 210.

Based on cases closed this week, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 21 hours. Dumped item pickups averaged a response of about 69 hours, while Animal Services calls were handled in about 3 hours.

It is important to note that some fast response times can be the result of administrative closures and not always on-site work completion.
