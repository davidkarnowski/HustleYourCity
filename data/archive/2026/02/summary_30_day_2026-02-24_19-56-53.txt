Hustle Long Beach community update on city service requests for the past 30 days.

The highest volume of requests continues to be for Dumped Items, with 2,253 total reports filed. The average response time for these issues is currently sitting at approximately 7 days.

Graffiti removal remains a major activity with 1,153 reports. The city is responding to these requests in about 2 days on average.

Infrastructure updates show that Street Repair requests totaled 250, with a turnaround time of roughly 3 days. Meanwhile, Tree Maintenance saw 393 reports with a faster response time of just over 1 day.

The fastest administrative response recorded was for Business Licenses and Permit Concerns, which averaged 12 minutes to close.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
