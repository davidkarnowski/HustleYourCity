For the Hustle Long Beach! community, the following is an independent accountability update on city service response times over the past 30 days. This analysis evaluates call totals and resolution speeds across Long Beach.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of reports with 2338 calls. The average response time for these issues was 8 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1007 calls, averaging a 33-hour response time. E-scooter complaints reached 940, though all were referred to outside agencies for handling.

Other notable service areas include street repair, which received 205 calls and took an average of 3 days to address. Park maintenance saw 75 calls with a similar 3-day average response time. Dead animal pickups were handled efficiently, averaging a 5-hour response across 166 calls. Conversely, marina repairs took an average of 7 days to resolve.

Please note that unusually fast response times may occasionally be the result of administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 7, 2026 05:01 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 7, 2026 03:00 AM PDT.
