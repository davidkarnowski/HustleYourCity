Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 30 days.

"Dumped Items" was the most frequent request with 1,935 reports filed. This was followed by 967 reports for "E-Scooter" issues and 954 for "Graffiti."

Response times for closed cases varied by service type. The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report was about 6.5 days. For "Traffic Signal" issues, the average was about 7 days. Graffiti removal was quicker, with an average response time of just over 2 days. The fastest responses were for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services," both averaging about 14 hours.
