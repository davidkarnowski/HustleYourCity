Here is a look at Long Beach service request data for the past week.

The most reported issues were illegally dumped items with 426 requests, followed by improperly parked e-scooters with 262 requests, and graffiti with 251 requests.

Average city response times for closed requests varied. Traffic signal repair requests took the longest to resolve at nearly 4 days. Trash and debris cleanup requests averaged about 3.5 days. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickups averaged about 4 hours. High-volume requests for graffiti removal and dumped item cleanup averaged about 1.3 days and 2.5 days to close, respectively.
