Here is a summary of Long Beach service request data from the last 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,900 service requests during this period. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,221 calls), graffiti (3,752 calls), and e-scooters (2,155 calls).

Average response times for closed requests varied by issue. Reports for dead animal pickup were addressed in about 17 hours. Graffiti cleanup requests took an average of about 6.5 days to complete. Requests for dumped item removal averaged over 15 days, while park maintenance requests averaged about 19 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 13, 2025 at 8:05 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 13, 2025 at 7:00 PM PST.
