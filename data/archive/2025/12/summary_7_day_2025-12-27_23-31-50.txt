Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past 7 days.

The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (325 calls), graffiti (196 calls), and e-scooter issues (179 calls). The average response time to close a dumped item report was about 30 hours, while graffiti reports were closed in about 28 hours on average. E-scooter reports are referred directly to vendors, so a city response time is not calculated.

Other notable average response times this week include dead animal pickup (8 hours), tree maintenance (12 hours), and street repair (13 hours). Some requests show very fast completion times, which may be due to administrative closures and not the actual time to perform the work.

Help keep the city informed by reporting issues you see. The best way to submit a service request is by using the Go Long Beach application on your smartphone.
