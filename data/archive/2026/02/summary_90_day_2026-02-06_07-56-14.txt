Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

The most frequent reports from the public were for Dumped Items (6,395 calls), Graffiti (3,279 calls), and E-Scooter issues (2,655 calls).

Response times varied by issue type. The average time to resolve a report for dumped items was about 2 weeks. In contrast, requests for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were typically closed in about 12 hours. Graffiti removal took an average of just under 5 days. Note that some faster response times can reflect administrative closures.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
