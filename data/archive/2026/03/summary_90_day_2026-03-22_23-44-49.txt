Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of requests, highlighting key areas of focus for city services.

Dumped items generated the highest volume with 6,702 reports, taking an average of 9.5 days to resolve. Graffiti removal saw 3,056 reports with an average response time of 70.6 hours. E-scooter complaints totaled 2,794, all of which were referred to outside operators.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant activity. Street light issues, with 1,030 reports, averaged a 3.3-day response time. Tree maintenance (945 reports) and street repairs (839 reports) took an average of 6.7 days and 6.4 days, respectively.

Some services showed rapid turnaround times. Dead animal pickups (440 reports) averaged 11.4 hours. Business license and permit concerns (6 reports) averaged just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help keep the city running smoothly.
