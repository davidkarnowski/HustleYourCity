An independent review of Long Beach city service data over the last 90 days highlights current response times for high-volume requests. Dumped Items remain the most frequently reported issue with 6,584 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 281 hours, or approximately 11.7 days.

Graffiti removal generated 3,112 reports with an average completion time of 3.5 days. Street Repair requests, totaling 845, are being addressed in roughly 5 days on average.

Response times vary significantly depending on the service type. While Dead Animal Pickup is averaging roughly 12.6 hours, Trash & Debris reports are taking nearly 10 days to resolve. E-Scooter reports remain high at 2,985 but are almost exclusively closed by referral to outside operators rather than city crews.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
