Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (6,171 reports), graffiti (3,731 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,160 reports).

Average response times show a wide range. Park maintenance requests took over 19 days to close on average, while reports for dumped items took over 15 days. On the faster end, requests for dead animal pickups and animal services were resolved in about 16 hours. Some quick resolutions may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
