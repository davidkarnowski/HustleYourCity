Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,188 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (427 calls), e-scooters (262 calls), and graffiti (255 calls).

Based on closed requests, the average response time for graffiti was about 1.3 days. Dumped items took an average of 2.4 days to be addressed. Slower response times included traffic signal repairs, which averaged about 3.75 days. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups (4.7 hours) and animal services (6.8 hours). Some categories show very fast closure times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than the completion of physical work.
