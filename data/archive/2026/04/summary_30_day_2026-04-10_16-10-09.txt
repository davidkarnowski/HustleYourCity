Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times based on data from the last 30 days.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to be the most frequently reported issues in Long Beach. Residents submitted 2,310 reports for dumped items and 1,035 reports for graffiti. E-scooter complaints also generated high volume with 916 reports, though all of these were referred to outside operators.

Response times across different city departments show noticeable variations. The city averaged nearly 8 days to respond to dumped items and about 37 hours for graffiti. Park maintenance requests took an average of 3.5 days to process, while street repairs averaged 69 hours. Dead animal pickups were addressed more quickly, averaging about 7 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Accountability relies on accurate public data. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues so the city can track and address them.

This data summary was updated at April 10, 2026, 9:06 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 10, 2026, 7:00 AM PDT.
