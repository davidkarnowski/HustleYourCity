Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests. In the past 24 hours, residents submitted 218 new requests for service. The most reported issues were for dumped items (83 requests), e-scooters (50 requests), and graffiti (40 requests).

The data shows some quick average response times for closed cases. Reports for dead animal pickups were addressed in about 34 minutes, and animal services cases in about 39 minutes. Tree maintenance requests were closed in an average of 18 minutes. It is important to note that unusually fast closures can sometimes be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.
