Long Beach service data from the last 90 days shows significant activity across several categories. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 6,880 reports, followed by 3,359 e-scooter reports and 3,297 graffiti reports.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickups are currently being addressed in approximately 6 hours, while graffiti removal averages about 3 days. Dumped items are taking longer to resolve, averaging nearly 8 days. Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and directed to the appropriate departments for resolution.
