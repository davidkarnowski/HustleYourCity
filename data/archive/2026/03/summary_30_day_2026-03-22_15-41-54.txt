Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Here is a look at how city departments responded to community issues over the past 30 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of reports with 2,225 requests. The city took an average of 5.6 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 999 requests, which saw an average response time of 38.6 hours.

Response times varied significantly depending on the type of service. Street repair requests totaled 249 and took an average of nearly 3 days to address. Park maintenance saw 70 requests with an average response time of 4.4 days. Conversely, dead animal pickups were handled much faster, averaging a 12.2-hour response across 150 reports. 

Unusually fast response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions, but these numbers provide a factual baseline for how quickly the city is processing citizen concerns.
