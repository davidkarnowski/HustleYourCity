Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days to keep the community informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 2111 reports. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1047 calls, which saw an average response time of 42 hours.

Other notable service categories include Tree Maintenance with 482 requests averaging a 53-hour response time, and Street Repair with 262 reports taking an average of 3 days to resolve. Dead Animal Pickup received 141 calls and was handled relatively quickly, averaging a 15-hour response time.

Some categories showed unusually low response times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. These rapid turnarounds may be due to administrative closures or referrals rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
