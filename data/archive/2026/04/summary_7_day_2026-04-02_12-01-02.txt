Hustle Long Beach! brings you the latest accountability update on city service response times. Here is a look at the service call data from the last 7 days to keep citizens informed on how neighborhood issues are being handled.

Dumped Items led the service requests with 586 reports, averaging a 70-hour response time. Graffiti followed with 224 reports and an average response time of 24.4 hours. E-Scooter issues generated 214 reports.

Dead Animal Pickup had one of the fastest response times at 3 hours for 38 calls. Animal Services also saw quick turnarounds, averaging 6.2 hours for 16 calls. Conversely, Illegal Firework reports took the longest, averaging 4.6 days for 3 calls. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

Residents are encouraged to report neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
