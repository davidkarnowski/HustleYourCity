Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times from the past week.

In the last 7 days, the most reported issues were for dumped items (423 reports), e-scooters (261 reports), and graffiti (248 reports).

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 31.5 hours. Reports for dumped items took an average of 56 hours.
