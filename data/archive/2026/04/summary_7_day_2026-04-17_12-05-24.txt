Here is the latest 7-day city service data update from Hustle Long Beach.

Over the past week, Dumped Items received the highest volume with 596 reports. The average response time for these issues was 3.6 days. E-Scooter complaints followed with 273 reports, nearly all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies. Graffiti was the third most reported issue at 254 calls, taking an average of 41 hours to address.

Street and Sidewalk Repairs saw 48 and 45 reports respectively, with response times averaging 34 hours and 26 hours. Trash and Debris reports took about 3.1 days to process.

Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services showed the fastest response times at 3.8 hours and 5 hours. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 17, 2026, 5:02 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 17, 2026, 3:00 AM PDT.
