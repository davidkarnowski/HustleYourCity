Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service data covering the last 30 days. This report highlights call totals and response times for the most frequent service requests.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 2,278 reports. The average response time for these issues was approximately one week. Graffiti reports followed with 1,122 total calls and an average response time of roughly 2 days.

Infrastructure data shows 392 reports regarding Lights and 253 reports for Street Repair. The average response time for Street Repair was recorded at just over 3 days. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or initial inspections and not necessarily actual work completions.

There were 896 reports regarding E-Scooters, the majority of which are closed and referred to third-party operators. Tree Maintenance saw 405 reports with a response time averaging close to 1.5 days.
