Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues. There were 6,911 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 8.4 days. Graffiti saw 3,248 reports, taking an average of 2.3 days to address. E-scooter complaints were also high with 2,918 reports, though response times are not tracked for this category.

Street repair received 712 calls, averaging 7.1 days for a response. Tree maintenance had 900 requests, averaging 7.7 days. On the faster side, dead animal pickups had 464 reports and averaged 10.2 hours. Business license concerns averaged just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep the city accountable and neighborhoods clean. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
