Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past 24 hours, residents submitted 176 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (60 calls), improperly parked e-scooters (48 calls), and graffiti (33 calls).

City data shows some very fast response times for certain closed cases. Reports for vegetation were addressed in an average of about 12 minutes, street repairs in 15 minutes, and tree maintenance in 24 minutes. Unusually low response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures and not on-site work completion. For comparison, graffiti clean-up requests took an average of 13 hours.
