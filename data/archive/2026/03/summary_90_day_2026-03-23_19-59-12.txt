Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 90-day city service request data to keep residents informed on response times and call volumes.

Dumped items remain the most frequent issue with 6,700 reports. The city takes an average of 9.5 days to address these requests. Graffiti is the second most common issue with 3,047 reports, averaging a 68.9-hour response time.

Infrastructure issues like street repair (841 reports) and tree maintenance (955 reports) are taking an average of 6.4 days and 6.6 days to resolve, respectively. Dead animal pickups are handled faster, averaging 11.5 hours across 432 reports.

Some categories show unusually fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep the community accountable and well-maintained. Followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
