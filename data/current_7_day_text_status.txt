Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data for the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted over 1,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (514 calls), improperly parked e-scooters (288 calls), and graffiti removal (229 calls).

The average time to resolve a request for dumped items was about 2 days. Graffiti and street repair requests were addressed more quickly, with an average closure time of just under 24 hours. Several other services had even faster responses, with tree maintenance requests being closed in about 8 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
