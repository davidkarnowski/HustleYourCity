Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest 90-day accountability summary of city service requests.

Over the last 90 days, Dumped Items and Graffiti generated the highest volume of citizen reports. The city received 6,960 reports for dumped items and 3,245 reports for graffiti. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 2,928 reports.

When looking at response times, the city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to dumped items and about 2.4 days for graffiti. Tree maintenance requests, totaling 892 calls, took an average of 9 days to address. On the faster side, dead animal pickups were handled in just under 10 hours on average.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a 12-minute response time. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
