Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update, focusing on the last 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests during this period. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (6,195 calls), Graffiti (3,382 calls), and E-Scooters (2,667 calls).

Average response times for closed requests varied by service type. Reports for Dumped Items took about 13 days to resolve on average, while Graffiti removal took about 5 days. Street repair requests were completed in just under 6 days. The longest average completion time was for Marina Repair, at about 25 days.

Some services, like Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services, had very fast average response times of under 15 hours. It is possible these faster times reflect administrative closures and not always on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
