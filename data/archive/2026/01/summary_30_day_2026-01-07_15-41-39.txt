Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past month, residents submitted over 4,800 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,927 requests), e-scooters (972 requests), and graffiti (914 requests).

Based on data for closed requests, the average response time for graffiti removal was just over 2 days. Street repair and tree maintenance requests were addressed in about 47 hours and 39 hours on average, respectively. Longer response times were recorded for dumped items, which took an average of about 8 days to resolve.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
