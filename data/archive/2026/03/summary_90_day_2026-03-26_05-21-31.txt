Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data to keep citizens informed on city response times. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,874 reports. The city took an average of 9.5 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,093 calls, which were addressed in an average of 66.4 hours.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 2,822, though all were referred to outside operators rather than handled directly by city staff. Street Repair and Tree Maintenance saw 867 and 950 reports, taking an average of 6.5 days and 6.1 days to resolve, respectively.

On the faster end, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickups were also handled quickly, averaging 11.4 hours across 445 reports.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make your voice heard. Please use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 25, 2026 10:17 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 25, 2026 8:00 PM PDT.
