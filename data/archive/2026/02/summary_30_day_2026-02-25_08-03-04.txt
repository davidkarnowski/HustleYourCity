An analysis of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 30 days highlights current trends in public maintenance and waste management. The highest volume of activity was recorded for Dumped Items, with 2,313 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 7 days.

Graffiti reports were the second most frequent category, totaling 1,150 requests with an average response time of roughly 2 days. Street Repair generated 251 reports, showing an average response time of 3 days. Tree Maintenance saw 398 reports with a turnaround time of 1.5 days.

Some categories display very rapid closure times. For example, Business Licenses/Permit Concerns averaged 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
