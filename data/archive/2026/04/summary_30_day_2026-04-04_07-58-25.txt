Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Over the past 30 days, citizens submitted thousands of requests, and here is how the city responded.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2322 reports. The average response time for these requests was just over 8 days.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 968 reports, taking an average of 33.5 hours to address. E-Scooter complaints reached 932, though all were immediately referred to outside agencies.

Street Repair saw 219 reports with an average response time of about 3.5 days. Meanwhile, Dead Animal Pickup had 163 reports with a rapid average response of 5 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
