Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service requests for the past week.

Over 1,200 service requests were made in the last seven days. The most common reports were for dumped items (490 calls), e-scooters (262 calls), and graffiti (236 calls).

For requests that were closed, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 18.5 hours. Dumped item pickups averaged about 49 hours, and street light issues were addressed in around 8 hours. Some categories, like Sidewalk Repair and Tree Maintenance, had average response times of about 3 hours. These unusually fast times may be due to administrative closures and not always on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the best way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
