Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on the latest public data.

Dumped items and graffiti were the most reported issues, with over 6,200 and 3,300 service requests respectively. The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 5 days, while dumped item reports were addressed in about 13 days on average. Street repair requests were completed in an average of about 5.5 days.

Some of the longest waits were for marina repairs, averaging about 5 weeks to close a request. Quicker responses were seen for sidewalk repairs, averaging about 3.5 days. Dead animal pickups were handled much faster, with an average response of about 13 hours. Note that some very fast response times can be due to administrative closures and not on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
