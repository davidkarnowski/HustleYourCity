Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests over the last 7 days.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to drive the highest call volumes. In the past week, citizens reported 467 dumped items and 316 graffiti issues. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 277 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Response times vary significantly by category. The city averaged about 70 hours to respond to dumped items and roughly 38 hours for graffiti. On the faster end, running water reports averaged a response time of just 33 minutes, though keep in mind that unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Meanwhile, damaged drinking fountain reports took about 4 days to process.

Help keep Long Beach accountable and well-maintained. If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
