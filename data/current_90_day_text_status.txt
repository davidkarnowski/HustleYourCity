Latest 90‑day service data shows faster response times across many city services.

Park maintenance requests (196) were closed in an average of 5.4 days, down from about 27 days historically.  
Sidewalk repairs (339) now average 2.1 days, a dramatic improvement from the previous 196‑day average.  
Graffiti reports (3,707) are resolved in about 6.3 days, roughly half the prior 14.6‑day average.  
Light outages (580) are fixed in under 2 days, compared with 42 days before.  
Tree work (817) averages 2.2 days, far quicker than the historic 157 days.  
Animal services (234) and dead‑animal pickups (457) are now handled in under 18 minutes, versus over 160 days in the past.  
Street repairs (625) average 4.2 days and traffic signal issues (103) 4.3 days, both much faster than earlier averages of 188 days and 105 days respectively.  

Overall, 6,387 dumped‑item reports were logged, with an average response time of about 15 days, still higher than many other categories but showing steady progress.
