Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most reported issues were illegally dumped items (6,093 reports), graffiti (3,710 reports), and e-scooter problems (2,337 reports).

Based on data for closed service requests, the city's average response time for graffiti removal was approximately 6 days. Requests for dumped item pickups took longer, with an average completion time of about 15 days. Sidewalk repairs were completed in an average of about 4 days. More urgent requests, such as dead animal pickups, were handled in about 16 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application. Your reports provide the data needed to track the city's performance.
