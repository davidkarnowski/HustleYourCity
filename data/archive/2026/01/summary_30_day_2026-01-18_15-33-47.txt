Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,400 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,029 calls), improperly parked e-scooters (982 calls), and graffiti (894 calls).

Response times for closed service requests varied by issue. Graffiti removal requests were closed in about 2.2 days on average. Illegally dumped items took an average of 4.3 days to be addressed. Reports of illegal fireworks took longer, with an average closure time of about 5.6 days. Dead animal pickups were handled much faster, with an average response time of about 12.5 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach app.
