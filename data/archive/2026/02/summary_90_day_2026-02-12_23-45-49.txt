An evaluation of Long Beach city service requests over the last 90 days highlights current response times and call volumes.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of activity with 6,378 total reports. The average time to resolve these requests is currently approximately 13 and a half days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,142. The data indicates an average response time of roughly 4 days for these issues.

Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 792 calls with an average completion time of about 5 days. Dead Animal Pickup services are averaging just under 12 hours per request.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report maintenance issues directly to the city.
