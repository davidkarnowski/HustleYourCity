Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data from the City of Long Beach. In the past day, residents submitted over 200 service requests. The most reported issue was Dumped Items with 88 requests, though 74 of these remain in the "New" status. The average response time for Dumped Items currently sits at about 20.5 hours.

Several categories showed very fast response times. Street Repair requests averaged 2.5 hours, Graffiti averaged 2.7 hours, and Dead Animal Pickup averaged 2.9 hours. E-Scooter complaints saw 41 requests, all of which were immediately marked as "Closed Referred." Please note that unusually low response times or immediate referrals often indicate administrative closures or routing to other departments, rather than the physical completion of the work.
