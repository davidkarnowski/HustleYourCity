Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to drive the highest call volumes. The city received 6,926 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 8.5 days. Graffiti complaints totaled 3,195, taking an average of 55 hours to address. E-scooter complaints were also high with 2,915 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant activity. Street repair logged 732 calls with an average response time of nearly 7 days. Tree maintenance received 901 requests, averaging 6 days for a response. Sidewalk repairs took about 5 days to address across 360 reports.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep Long Beach accountable. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 10, 2026, 9:06 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 10, 2026, 7:00 AM PDT.
