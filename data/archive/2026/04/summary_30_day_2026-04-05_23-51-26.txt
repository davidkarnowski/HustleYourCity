Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 2278 calls. The city took an average of 8.3 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti reports were the second most common with 972 calls, showing an average response time of 32.5 hours. E-Scooter complaints also saw high volume with 934 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Some services saw rapid turnarounds. Dead animal pickups averaged a response time of 5 hours across 163 calls, and general animal services averaged 8.6 hours for 79 calls. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Other services required more time. Street repairs averaged 3.2 days for 211 calls, and reports of illegal fireworks took an average of 4.2 days to process.
