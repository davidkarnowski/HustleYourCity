The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,909 reports for dumped items and 3,149 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 5.5 hours. Conversely, dumped items took an average of about 13 days to address. Graffiti removal averaged approximately 3.6 days, while street repair averaged about 5 days.

Please note that some response time averages may appear unusually low due to administrative closures or referrals rather than completed field work.
