Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest third-party accountability update on city service response times. This review looks at the service call data from the last 7 days to track how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 564 calls. The city averaged a 65-hour response time for these issues. Currently, 242 of these requests are still marked as new, while 64 have been closed.

E-Scooter issues generated 224 reports. All of these cases were immediately closed and referred to outside agencies.

Graffiti removal saw 194 requests. The city responded to these in an average of 18 hours, successfully closing 118 of those cases.

Street Repair and Sidewalk Repair saw 58 and 48 requests respectively. Street repairs averaged a 19-hour response time, while sidewalk issues averaged a 16-hour response time.

Dead Animal Pickup handled 37 reports with a rapid average response time of 3.5 hours. Please note that unusually low response times across any category may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
