Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days. The most frequent reports from residents continue to be for illegally dumped items (5,902 requests) and graffiti (3,611 requests).

Response times vary by issue. The average time to resolve a report for dumped items was about 15 days, while park maintenance requests took an average of about 2.5 weeks. Faster responses were seen for issues like dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 17 hours on average.

Some service requests, like for damaged drinking fountains, show an average response time of just over 2 hours. Unusually fast times can sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it is the first step to getting it fixed. Use the Go Long Beach app to submit a service request directly to the city.
