Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service response times covering the last 90 days. This data reflects service calls processed through the city's reporting system.

Dumped Items represent the highest volume of service requests with 6,386 total reports. The average response time for these issues is currently 13.5 days.

Graffiti abatement received 3,157 reports. The average time to close these tickets is approximately 4 days.

Street Repair requests, which total 778 reports, show an average response time of nearly 5 days. Tree Maintenance requests are taking just over one week to resolve on average.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services, which averaged 11 hours and 13 hours respectively.

Citizens are encouraged to continue using the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
