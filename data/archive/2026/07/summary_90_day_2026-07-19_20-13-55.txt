The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city received 6,941 reports for dumped items and 3,292 reports for graffiti. Response times for graffiti averaged approximately 2.7 days, while dumped items took about 7.8 days on average to address.

Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to see some of the fastest response times, with dead animal pickups averaging about 5.5 hours over the last 90 days. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues. This tool remains the most effective way to notify the city of maintenance needs and track the status of your requests.
