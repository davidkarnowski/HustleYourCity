Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day service call data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at the current response times and request volumes.

Over the past week, Dumped Items led the service requests with 521 reports, taking an average of 70 hours to address. Graffiti followed with 353 reports, averaging a 28.6-hour response time. E-Scooter issues also saw high volume with 273 reports referred for closure.

Other notable service areas include Tree Maintenance, which received 46 calls with an average response time of 17.5 hours, and Dead Animal Pickups, which had 40 calls averaging a 16.3-hour response. Sidewalk and street repairs accounted for 36 and 32 requests, taking about 12.6 and 17.6 hours to process, respectively.

Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help maintain neighborhood accountability.
