Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests based on city data.

In the last month, residents submitted over 5,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (1,940), e-scooter issues (1,035), and graffiti removal (1,000).

Response times varied by issue. The average time to close a graffiti report was about 54 hours, while street repair requests were handled in about 58 hours. Dumped item pickups took significantly longer, averaging about 10 days to resolve. It is important to note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than work completion. E-scooter reports are referred directly to vendors and are not completed by city staff.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it is the first step to getting it fixed. Use the Go Long Beach app to submit a service request directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at December 28, 2025 at 7:31 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 28, 2025 at 7:00 AM PST.
