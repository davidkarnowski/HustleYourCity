Here's a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Reports for dumped items (5,899) and graffiti (3,540) were the most common service requests from the community. The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 2 weeks. Graffiti removal requests were handled faster, with an average completion time of about 4.7 days.

Response times for other services varied. Park maintenance requests took over 16 days on average to close, while reports for dead animal pickup were typically resolved in about 17 hours. Some issues show very fast response times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
