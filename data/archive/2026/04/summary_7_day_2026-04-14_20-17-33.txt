Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data. Here is a look at the service request numbers and response times from the past 7 days to see how the city is handling community needs.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of calls with 568 requests. The city took an average of 3.4 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 310 calls, averaging a 42.8-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 275 reports, though response times for these are not tracked in the current data.

Other notable service areas include street repairs with 45 calls averaging a 16.3-hour response, and tree maintenance with 48 calls averaging a 19.4-hour response. Abandoned shopping carts generated 13 calls and took an average of 3.2 days to address.

Animal services responded to 19 calls in an average of 4.5 hours, and dead animal pickups handled 39 calls in about 7.8 hours. Please note that unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
