The latest data summary for the City of Long Beach highlights service request trends over the past 90 days. Dumped Items remains the most frequently reported issue, with 6,532 total requests. The average response time for these reports is currently approximately 12.8 days.

Graffiti removal generated 3,117 reports, with crews averaging a response time of nearly 4 days. Street Repair requests totaled 806, showing an average completion time of about 4.6 days.

Dead Animal Pickup services recorded 430 reports with a faster average response time of 12.7 hours. Marina Repair requests, while low in volume with only 4 reports, showed the longest wait times at approximately 23 days.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. Consistent reporting helps ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
