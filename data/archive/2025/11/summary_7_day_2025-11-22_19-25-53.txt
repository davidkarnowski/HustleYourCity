Graffiti reports jumped to 294 in the past week, with 187 cases closed. The average response time was about 21 hours, showing quicker handling than the 66‑hour average over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items remained the most frequent issue at 410 reports. City crews resolved these in roughly 34 hours on average, down from the 171‑hour average seen in the last 30 days.

Light outages generated 61 calls, with an average response of 32 hours, improving from the 41‑hour average in the prior month.

Tree maintenance, dead animal pickups, and street repairs saw very fast turn‑around times—averaging 5 hours, 5 hours and 8 hours respectively—though some rapid closures may reflect administrative processing rather than on‑site work.

Overall, 9 Trash & Debris reports were logged, with an average response of 38 hours. Traffic signal issues (10 reports) took about 26 hours to address.

These figures highlight steady progress on many service categories while underscoring areas where response times remain longer.
