Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,800 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,438 reports), graffiti removal (996 reports), and e-scooter issues (990 reports).

The average city response time to close a report for dumped items was about 7 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 2.5 days. Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which took an average of about 12 hours to resolve.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
