Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

In the last month, residents submitted nearly 5,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common report was for illegally dumped items, with 1,844 requests filed.

The average time to close a graffiti report was just over 2 days. For street repair requests, the average response time was about 40 hours. Reports of dumped items and other trash or debris took longer, with an average closure time of about 9 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city.
