The latest city service data for the past 90 days shows varying response times across key categories. Dead animal pickup remains the most efficient service, with an average response time of approximately 7 hours. Animal services also show strong performance, averaging about 11 hours per request.

Other categories are seeing longer wait times. Dumped items, which saw 7,586 total requests in the last 90 days, currently average about 17 days for resolution. Tree maintenance requests have averaged roughly 5 days, while sidewalk repair requests have averaged about 5 days as well.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
