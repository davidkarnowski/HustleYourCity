Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,200 service requests during this period. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,034 requests), illegally parked e-scooters (1,030), and graffiti (1,016).

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report was about 10 days. For graffiti removal, the average response time was just over 2 days. The city responded more quickly to other issues, with reports for tree maintenance being handled in about 22.5 hours on average.
