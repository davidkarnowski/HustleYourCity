Here is a look at the latest city service data for Long Beach, focusing on the last 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti removal were the most frequent service requests, with over 5,900 and 3,600 reports filed, respectively. The average response time to close a dumped item report was about 15 days, while graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 5 days.

Other key response times include dead animal pickups at about 16 hours and sidewalk repairs at about 4 days. Tree maintenance requests took nearly a week on average to close.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
