Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach has handled public service requests over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue, with 6,615 requests. The average response time for these reports was about 10 days. Graffiti reports followed closely with 3,085 calls, which took an average of 3 days to address.

Other notable service categories include street repair, which saw 832 reports and an average response time of about 6 days. Tree maintenance received 903 requests, taking nearly 6 days on average to resolve. 

Some categories showed incredibly fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to stay engaged and report neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
