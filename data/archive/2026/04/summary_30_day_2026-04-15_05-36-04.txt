Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

The highest number of service requests over the last month were for Dumped Items with 2366 reports. The city took an average of 8.3 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1088 calls, averaging a response time of 41.6 hours. E-Scooter complaints also saw high volume with 999 reports, though response times were not tracked for this category.

Some of the fastest responses were for Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 7 hours across 169 reports, and Animal Services, averaging 8.5 hours for 76 reports. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completion. On the slower end, Marina Repair requests took an average of 6.9 days, and Light issues took about 4 days to address.

Citizens are encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues. You can submit your service requests directly through the Go Long Beach service application to help keep the city accountable.
