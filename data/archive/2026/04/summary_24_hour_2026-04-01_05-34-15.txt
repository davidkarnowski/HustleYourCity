Hustle Long Beach! followers, here is the latest 24-hour review of city service response times.

Over the past day, dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 119 reports, though none have recorded completion times yet. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 20 calls and an average response time of 2.4 hours.

Several service categories logged response times under 90 minutes.
