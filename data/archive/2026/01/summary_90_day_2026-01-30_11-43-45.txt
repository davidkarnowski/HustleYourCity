Here's an update on Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days.

Over 16,000 service requests were made. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,281 reports), graffiti (3,346 reports), and e-scooters (2,676 reports).

Response times varied by service type. The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 13 days, while graffiti removal took about 5 days. Reports for dead animal pickup were resolved much faster, averaging under 14 hours. Some requests, like certain sidewalk repairs, show completion times under 20 minutes, which may reflect administrative closures and not the time for physical work.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
