City service request update – last 90 days

- Graffiti reports topped the list with 3,690 requests. Average response time was 163 hours (about 6.8 days).  
- Dumped Items generated 6,183 calls; average response was 368 hours (≈15 days).  
- Tree Maintenance saw 776 requests and was addressed quickly – average 38 hours (≈1.6 days).  
- Street Repair had 629 requests; average response 82 hours (≈3.4 days).  
- Light outages (streetlights) recorded 626 calls, average response 39 hours.  
- Animal Services (including dead animal pickup) handled 223 and 438 calls respectively, both with average responses around 17 hours.  
- Sidewalk Repair received 322 calls; average response 36 hours.  
- Traffic Signal issues: 104 calls, average response 80 hours.  
- Report Illegal Fireworks: 27 calls, average response 223 hours (≈9 days).  

Overall, high‑volume categories such as Graffiti and Dumped Items still take several days to close, while tree and light issues are being resolved in under two days.

Residents can report new issues or track existing ones through the Go Long Beach service app.
