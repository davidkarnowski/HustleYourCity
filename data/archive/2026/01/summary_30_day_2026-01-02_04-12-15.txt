Long Beach Service Response Update: Last 30 Days

In the last 30 days, Long Beach residents made over 5,000 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (1,909 calls), e-scooters (969 calls), and graffiti (967 calls).

Based on the latest data, here are the average response times for some key services. Reports for dumped items had the longest average completion time at about 8.5 days. Graffiti and street light repair requests were typically resolved in about 2 days. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 14 hours from the initial report to closure.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report problems like graffiti, potholes, or dumped items is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
