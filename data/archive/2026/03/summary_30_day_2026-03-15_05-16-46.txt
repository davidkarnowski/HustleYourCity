Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public requests.

Dumped items continue to generate the highest volume of reports with 2,118 calls. On average, these requests took 5 days to resolve. Graffiti removal followed with 1,041 reports, averaging a 44-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also remained high at 983 calls, though all were immediately referred to other channels and closed out.

Response times varied significantly across different types of service. Marina repairs took the longest, averaging 7.5 days to address. Conversely, business license and permit concerns were closed in just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times are often due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Meanwhile, street repairs averaged a 3-day turnaround for 261 requests.

Help keep the community running smoothly. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
