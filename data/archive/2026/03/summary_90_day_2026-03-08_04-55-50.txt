An analysis of City of Long Beach service requests over the last 90 days highlights current response times and volume for key community issues.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 6,562 reports filed. The average response time for these requests was approximately 11 days. Graffiti removal received 3,090 reports, with an average completion time of roughly 3 days.

Infrastructure requests showed steady activity. Street Repair, totaling 849 reports, averaged a response time of 5 days. Tree Maintenance recorded 858 reports with an average response time of nearly 6 days. Park Maintenance cases took approximately 6 days to resolve across 194 reports.

Faster response times were noted for Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 14 hours across 432 reports. Conversely, E-Scooter reports totaled 2,938, though the vast majority were closed and referred to third-party operators.

Residents can assist in data accuracy and neighborhood maintenance by using the Go Long Beach service application to report issues.
