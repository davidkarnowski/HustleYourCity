Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day city service request data to keep residents informed on response times and call volumes.

Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 6,959 reports, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti removal had 3,245 requests, with an average response time of about 2.4 days.

Infrastructure issues like Tree Maintenance (891 requests) and Street Repair (703 requests) averaged 9 days and 8 days for responses, respectively. Dead Animal Pickup handled 467 requests with an average response time of just under 10 hours.

Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response of 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 17, 2026, 1:51 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 16, 2026, 11:00 PM PDT.
