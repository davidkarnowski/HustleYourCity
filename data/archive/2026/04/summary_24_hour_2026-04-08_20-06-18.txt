Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest third-party accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests over the past 24 hours.

Dumped Items saw the highest volume of reports with 46 calls, though none have been closed yet. Graffiti followed closely with 36 reports. City crews closed 17 of those graffiti cases with an average response time of just over 14 hours.

Dead Animal Pickup recorded a remarkably fast average response time of 59 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Tree Maintenance and Vegetation requests also saw quick turnarounds, averaging about 5 hours for closed cases. Sidewalk Repair requests averaged a 14-hour response time.
