Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest 30-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests.

Over the past month, Dumped Items led the service requests with 2,309 reports. This was followed by Graffiti with 1,086 reports and E-Scooter issues with 960 reports.

The city's response times vary significantly by category. For Dumped Items, the average response time was about 8 days. Tree Maintenance requests took about 3.6 days on average, and Street Repair issues were addressed in approximately 3.1 days. Graffiti removal averaged a 37-hour response.

Some services showed rapid turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup averaged just under 7 hours, and Animal Services averaged 8.4 hours. Please note that unusually fast response times across some categories may be due to administrative ticket closures rather than actual physical work completion.
