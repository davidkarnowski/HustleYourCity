Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest daily accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests. The following statistics focus on service calls and response times over the past 24 hours.

Dumped Items led the daily volume with 79 total reports, though 68 of these currently remain in new status. The average response time for closed dumped item cases was recorded at just 28 minutes. This unusually low response time may be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

E-Scooter complaints followed with 58 reports, all of which were closed and referred elsewhere. Graffiti issues saw 28 reports with an average response time of 6.4 hours.

Several other services reported rapid turnarounds. Vegetation and Animal Services requests averaged response times of 39 minutes and 60 minutes, respectively. Dead Animal Pickup received 4 requests, all of which were closed with an average response time of 4.7 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
