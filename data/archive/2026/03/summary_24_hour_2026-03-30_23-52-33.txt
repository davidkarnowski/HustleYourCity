Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data to keep you informed about city response times and neighborhood issues.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 89 reports. The average response time for these was 5.8 hours, though 75 cases are still marked as new. E-Scooter complaints followed with 47 reports, all of which were referred and closed. Graffiti issues accounted for 36 reports, showing an average response time of 6.7 hours.

Some services showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Street Repair logged 7 reports with an average response time of just 83 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions. Dead Animal Pickup also saw rapid updates, with 9 of the 10 reported cases already closed and an average response time of 2.8 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets tracked. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
