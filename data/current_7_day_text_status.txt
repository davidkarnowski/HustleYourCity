Last week the city logged 1,268 service requests across 18 categories.

Graffiti had the highest volume (289 reports) with an average response time of about 27.6 hours (≈1,657 minutes).  
Dumped Items received 441 reports, average response 34.9 hours (≈2,092 minutes).  
Tree Maintenance (58 reports) was addressed in roughly 8.5 hours (≈513 minutes).  
Light (44), Street Repair (33) and Sidewalk Repair (23) each saw average responses between 13 and 24 hours.  
The fastest average was Restroom Maintenance at 71 minutes (≈1.2 hours) for three requests.

Overall, most categories were resolved within one to two days. Very low response times may reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completion.
