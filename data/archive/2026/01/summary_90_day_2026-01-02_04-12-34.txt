Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items, with 5,841 requests, and graffiti, with 3,601 requests. E-scooter reports were also high at 2,520.

Average response times varied significantly. Requests for dumped items took about 14.5 days to resolve, while park maintenance averaged about 17 days. In contrast, calls for dead animal pickup and animal services were typically closed in under 18 hours. Some issues with very low request counts, like damaged drinking fountains, show unusually fast closure times that may be due to administrative updates and not work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application to report it to the city.
