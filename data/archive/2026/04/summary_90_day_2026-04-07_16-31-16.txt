Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 90 days.

The highest volume of service requests involved Dumped Items with 6,952 reports, taking an average of 8.7 days to resolve. Graffiti followed with 3,184 reports, averaging a 57-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints reached 2,906, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Street repair requests totaled 754 with an average response time of 7.2 days. Tree maintenance saw 910 reports, taking about 4.7 days on average. Dead animal pickups were handled much faster, averaging 9.8 hours across 454 reports.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. Business license and permit concerns averaged just 12 minutes for closure. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.

This data summary was updated at April 7, 2026, 9:18 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 7, 2026, 7:00 AM PDT.
