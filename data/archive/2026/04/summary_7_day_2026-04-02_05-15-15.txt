Hustle Long Beach! is back with your weekly accountability breakdown of city service response times. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled citizen requests over the past 7 days.

Dumped items continue to drive the highest volume of requests with 588 reports this week, taking an average of 70.7 hours to address. Graffiti followed with 222 reports, averaging a 24.3-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 212 reports, all of which were referred and closed.

Response times varied significantly depending on the issue. Reports of illegal fireworks took the longest to resolve, averaging 4.6 days. On the faster side, dead animal pickups averaged 3 hours, and animal services requests averaged 6.2 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
