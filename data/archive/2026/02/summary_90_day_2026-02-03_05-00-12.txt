Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days based on the latest public data.

Illegal dumping continues to be the most reported issue with 6,311 service calls. The average response time to close these requests is currently almost two weeks. Graffiti removal was the second most frequent request with 3,292 reports, which are typically resolved in about 5 days.

Some services are seeing quicker responses. For example, requests for dead animal pickups and other animal services are handled in about 12 hours on average. In contrast, marina repairs have the longest average response time at approximately 5 weeks.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
