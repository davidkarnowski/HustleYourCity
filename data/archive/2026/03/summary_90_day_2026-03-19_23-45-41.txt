Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 90-day service call data to track how the City of Long Beach is managing public requests. Here is a breakdown of the recent numbers and response times.

Dumped items saw the highest volume with 6,665 reports. The city took an average of nearly 10 days to resolve these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,094 calls, averaging a 71.6-hour response time.

Infrastructure requests like street repair, with 828 reports, and tree maintenance, with 921 reports, both averaged a response time of almost 7 days. 

Some categories showed incredibly fast turnarounds. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps track these metrics and keeps the community informed.
