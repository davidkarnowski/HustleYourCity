Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the last 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti were the most common issues reported by residents, with over 6,200 and 3,300 requests respectively. According to the city's data, the average response time to close a dumped item report was about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in about 5 days on average.

Other key service times include street repairs, which took an average of just under 6 days, and tree maintenance, which averaged about 4 days. Dead animal pickup requests were handled much faster, with an average response time of about 14 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
