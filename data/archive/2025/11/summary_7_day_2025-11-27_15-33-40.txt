In the past week Long Beach recorded 999 service calls across all categories. The overall average response time was under 24 hours for most issues, showing a fast turnaround for city services.

- Graffiti requests (300) were closed in an average of 30 hours.
- Tree maintenance (45) averaged just 9 hours to resolve.
- Street repair (49) and sidewalk repair (18) were addressed in about 11 hours and 16 hours respectively.
- Dead animal pickups (27) were completed in roughly 6 hours, and abandoned shopping carts (4) in about 7 hours.

Residents can continue to report issues quickly through the Go Long Beach service app, helping keep response times short.
