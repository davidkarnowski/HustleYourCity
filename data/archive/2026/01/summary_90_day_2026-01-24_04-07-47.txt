Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, the most common service request was for "Dumped Items," with over 6,100 reports filed. The average time to resolve these cases was about 13 days. The second most frequent issue was "Graffiti," with over 3,300 reports and an average response time of about 5 days.

Some services were handled more quickly. The average response time for "Animal Services" and "Dead Animal Pickup" requests was under 15 hours. Reports for "Vegetation" maintenance were addressed in about 38 hours on average. Other key services, like "Street Repair," took an average of nearly 6 days to close out.
