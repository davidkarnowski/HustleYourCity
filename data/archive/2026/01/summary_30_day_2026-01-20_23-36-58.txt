Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past month, residents submitted more than 5,400 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items, with 2,093 calls. This was followed by 942 reports for e-scooters and 889 for graffiti removal.

Average city response times for closed cases varied by service type. Dumped item cleanups took about 7.5 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in about 3 days. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which were handled in approximately 13 hours on average.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach application to report it directly to the city.
