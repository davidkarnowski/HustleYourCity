The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows service trends over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 6,718 requests for dumped items and 3,302 reports regarding graffiti.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 7 hours. Conversely, street repair requests averaged about 6 days, and dumped item reports averaged roughly 9 days. Please note that unusually low response time averages for some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
