Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,155 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (433 requests), e-scooters (240 requests), and graffiti (219 requests).

The average response time to close a "Dumped Items" report was about 42 hours. The 219 graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 26 hours. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of 45 minutes. Unusually fast response times can sometimes indicate an administrative closure rather than a physical work completion.
