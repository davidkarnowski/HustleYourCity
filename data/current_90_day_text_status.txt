Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,155 requests), graffiti (3,744 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,177 requests).

The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 6.5 days, while dumped item reports took an average of about 15 days. Park maintenance requests had the longest average completion time at approximately 18 days. Some of the quickest responses were for animal-related services, which were typically resolved in under 17 hours.
