In the past 90 days Long Beach recorded 6,181 dumped‑item reports and 3,690 graffiti reports. The average response time was about 15 days for dumped items and 6.8 days for graffiti.

Other service areas showed quicker turn‑around: sidewalk repairs averaged 1.5 days, street repairs 3.4 days, light repairs 1.6 days, tree maintenance 1.6 days, and park maintenance 4.9 days.

Graffiti response time has improved from the all‑time average of 14.6 days to 6.8 days, while dumped‑item response is currently longer than the all‑time average of 10.9 days.
