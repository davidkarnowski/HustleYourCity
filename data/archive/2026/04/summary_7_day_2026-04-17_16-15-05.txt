Hustle Long Beach! presents the latest independent look at city service response times. Over the past seven days, the data reveals how the city is handling community reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 587 reports. The average response time for these was about 3.6 days. E-Scooter issues followed with 270 reports. Graffiti removal saw 240 reports, taking an average of 39 hours to address.

On the faster end, Dead Animal Pickup handled 35 calls with an average response time of nearly 4 hours. Meanwhile, Trash and Debris reports took an average of 3.1 days to resolve. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Stay engaged and help improve the community. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
