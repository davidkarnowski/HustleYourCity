Here is the latest look at Long Beach service request data from the past 30 days.

Dumped Items was the most reported issue with 2,029 requests, followed by nearly 1,000 reports for E-Scooters and 904 for Graffiti.

Regarding response times, reports for illegal fireworks took the longest to resolve with an average of about 5.6 days. The city also took an average of 5.3 days to close reports for Dumped Items. Faster responses included Dead Animal Pickups, which were handled in about 13 hours on average. Graffiti reports were closed in an average of about 2 days.

Keep Long Beach beautiful. If you see an issue, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
