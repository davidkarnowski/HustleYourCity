Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service call data.

Illegal dumping continues to be the most reported issue with 1,897 calls. The average response time to close these requests was around 9 days. Graffiti was the next most frequent report with 949 calls, which were handled in an average of about 56 hours.

Response times for other common issues include street repairs at about 47 hours and tree maintenance at about 30 hours. Reports of abandoned shopping carts were addressed in an average of 34 hours.

To report non-emergency issues in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.
