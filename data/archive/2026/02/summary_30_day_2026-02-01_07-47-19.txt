Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Illegally dumped items remain the most reported issue with 2,438 service calls. This is followed by 1,043 reports for e-scooters and 1,020 for graffiti.

The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 9 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 2.5 days on average. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups, which were handled in an average of 11 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
