Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service call data.

In the past month, the most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (1,983 requests) and graffiti (1,126 requests). These two categories represent a significant portion of all service calls.

According to the city's data, average response times varied widely by service type. The longest waits were for marina repairs, which averaged about two weeks to close. Requests for dumped items took an average of about 7 days, while street repairs averaged around 4 days. Faster responses were recorded for animal services and dead animal pickups, both averaging under 14 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app on your smartphone.
