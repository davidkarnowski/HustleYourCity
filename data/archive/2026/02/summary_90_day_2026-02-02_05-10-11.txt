Here's a look at Long Beach's service request data over the past 90 days. Dumped items and graffiti were the most reported issues by a large margin.

Residents submitted over 6,200 requests for dumped item removal, with an average closure time of about 13.5 days. For the nearly 3,300 graffiti reports, the average response time was about 5 days.

Response times for other common services varied. Animal service requests were addressed in about 13 hours on average. Street light repairs took an average of about 4.5 days, while reports for marina repairs took an average of 5 weeks to close.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
