Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,948 requests), e-scooters (1,074), and graffiti (1,063).

Based on city data, the average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 5.6 days. Graffiti removal requests were handled in about 2.3 days on average. Some of the quickest responses included Animal Services (about 10.5 hours) and Dead Animal Pickup (about 13 hours). E-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies, which is why a city response time is not tracked for those requests.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
