Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service requests based on city data.

The most common issues reported by residents were dumped items (5,847 calls) and graffiti (3,542 calls). These two categories made up the bulk of service requests.

Average city response times to close a case varied. Graffiti removal requests were completed in about 4.7 days. Dumped item reports took an average of 14 days to resolve. Street repairs were addressed in about 8.5 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
