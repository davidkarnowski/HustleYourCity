Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data. Here is the accountability update on Long Beach service requests and response times from the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 544 calls. The average response time for these issues was 3.2 days. It is worth noting that 312 of these requests are still marked as new and have not yet been addressed.

Graffiti reports totaled 191, with the city averaging a 21-hour response time. Street Repair saw 50 requests with an average response time of 33.8 hours.

Dead Animal Pickup handled 40 calls, averaging a quick response of 2.5 hours. Tree Maintenance received 31 reports with a 12.9-hour average response. Please remember that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

E-Scooter complaints remained high with 181 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies, resulting in no measurable city response time.
