Here is your weekly summary of Long Beach city service data, focusing on requests made in the last seven days.

Over 1,400 service requests were submitted by residents. The most common reports were for dumped items (584 requests), graffiti (238 requests), and e-scooter issues (223 requests).

For cases closed this week, the average response time for dumped items was about 47 hours. Graffiti reports were handled in an average of 21 hours. Faster responses were recorded for park maintenance (about 6 hours) and vegetation management (under 6 hours). Some requests, like those for e-scooters, are referred to private companies and do not have a city response time. Unusually fast closures can sometimes be due to administrative updates rather than on-site work completion.
