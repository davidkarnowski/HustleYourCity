Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,400 requests were submitted through the Go Long Beach app and other reporting systems. The most common issue was Dumped Items, with 659 reports. The second most frequent was Graffiti, with 288 reports.

The average time to close a Dumped Items request was about 19 hours. For Graffiti, the average response time was about 26 hours. Some requests, like Abandoned Shopping Carts, were closed in an average of 39 minutes. Note that unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than immediate work completion.
