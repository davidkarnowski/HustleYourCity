In the last 30 days Long Beach recorded 5,057 service calls. The most frequent issues were Dumped Items (1,865 calls), Graffiti (1,361), and E‑Scooter reports (686). Other notable volumes included Street Repair (198), Tree Maintenance (198) and Light problems (250).

Average response times varied by service type. Dumped Items were closed in about 8.4 days (202 hours), Graffiti in 2.9 days (68.7 hours), and Light repairs in 1.7 days (40.6 hours). Tree Maintenance and Animal Services were addressed faster, averaging 12 hours and 14.5 hours respectively. Street Repair and Traffic Signal work typically took around 24 hours, while Report Illegal Firework cases took about 6.4 days (154 hours). Some categories, such as Trash & Debris and Abandoned Shopping Carts, saw average closures in roughly 2.5 days and 2.1 days.

Residents can help keep the city moving by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service app.
