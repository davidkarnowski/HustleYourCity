The latest review of City of Long Beach service requests highlights performance data over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 2,391 total reports. The average response time to resolve these issues was approximately 1 week.

Graffiti reports totaled 1,174 for the month. The data shows an average response time of 2 days for graffiti removal.

Street Repair saw 233 reports filed, with crews averaging a response time of 3 days. Dead Animal Pickup was completed more quickly, averaging 8 hours across 148 reports.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
