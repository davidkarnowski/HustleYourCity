Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data for the City of Long Beach. The following statistics highlight the volume of citizen requests and the average time it takes for the city to respond.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2097 reports. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 1049 reports, showing an average response time of 43 hours. E-Scooter complaints totaled 999, all of which were referred to outside operators.

Other notable service categories over the past month include Tree Maintenance with 472 requests averaging a 52-hour response, and Street Repair with 267 requests taking 3 days on average. Park Maintenance received 66 requests, which took 4 days to address.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.
