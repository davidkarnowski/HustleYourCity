Here is a look at the latest city service data for Long Beach.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted more than 15,400 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,076 reports), graffiti (3,709 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,364 reports).

Based on the city's data for closed requests, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 5.7 days. Reports for dumped items were addressed in about two weeks on average. Street repair requests took around 10 days, while park maintenance requests averaged about 17 days to resolve.
