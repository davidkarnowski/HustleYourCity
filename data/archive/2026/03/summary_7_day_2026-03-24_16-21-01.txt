Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items were the most frequently reported issue, with 487 calls over the last week. The city averaged a 42.6-hour response time for these requests. E-scooter complaints followed with 228 reports, all of which were closed and referred to outside agencies. Graffiti was the third most common issue with 205 reports, averaging a 26.5-hour response time.

Some services saw rapid turnaround times. Dead animal pickups averaged a 3.3-hour response across 40 reports. Community center requests averaged a 72-minute response time. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Street repairs saw 57 reports with an average response time of 24.2 hours, while tree maintenance had 77 reports averaging 17.2 hours.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting issues to help keep the city accountable. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood concerns directly to the city.
