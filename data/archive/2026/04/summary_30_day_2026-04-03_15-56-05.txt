Hustle Long Beach! is here with your latest independent accountability update on city service response times. An analysis of the last 30 days of service requests shows how quickly the City of Long Beach is addressing community concerns.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of calls with 2,335 reports. On average, it took the city about 8 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 935 reports, showing an average response time of 33 hours.

Street Repair saw 222 requests, taking an average of 3 days and 9 hours to process, while Dead Animal Pickup handled 165 reports with an average response of just over 5 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures or referrals rather than actual physical work completions.
