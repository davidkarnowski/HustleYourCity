Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days.

Residents made over 15,000 service requests, with dumped items (6,098) and graffiti (3,727) being the most reported issues.

Average response times varied significantly. Reports for dumped items took about 15 days to resolve, while park maintenance requests averaged nearly 20 days. Graffiti removal averaged about 6.5 days. More urgent issues like dead animal pickup were handled much faster, averaging about 16 hours.

If you see an issue, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
