Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest 30-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues. In the last 30 days, citizens reported 2,302 dumped items and 1,045 instances of graffiti.

The city took an average of 8 days to respond to dumped items, while graffiti was addressed in about 53 hours. Street and sidewalk repairs saw 375 combined reports, with average response times of 66 hours and 57 hours, respectively. Street light issues took an average of 4 days to resolve across 234 reports.

Dead animal pickups and animal services had the fastest turnaround times, averaging 7 hours and 8 hours respectively. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep Long Beach accountable and well-maintained. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
