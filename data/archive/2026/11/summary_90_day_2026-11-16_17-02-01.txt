The latest city service data for the past 90 days shows significant activity across Long Beach. Residents submitted 7,548 reports for dumped items and 2,560 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickups are currently the fastest, averaging about 8 hours. Graffiti removal is averaging approximately 63 hours, while street repair requests are taking about 6 days on average.

Please note that some response times may appear unusually low due to administrative closures rather than completed work.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. This helps ensure that service requests are tracked and addressed by the appropriate departments.
