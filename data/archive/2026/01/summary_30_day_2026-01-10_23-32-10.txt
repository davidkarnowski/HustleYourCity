Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 30 days.

Over the past month, residents made 1,927 requests for "Dumped Items" cleanup, making it the most reported issue. This was followed by 970 reports for "E-Scooter" issues and 943 for "Graffiti" removal.

The average time to close a "Graffiti" report was about 2 days. For "Dumped Items", the average was about 6 days. "Street Repair" requests were closed in an average of just under 3 days. Faster responses included "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services", both averaging around 14 hours.

Note that all 970 "E-Scooter" reports were marked as "Closed Referred", meaning they were forwarded to another agency. This is why no city response time is available for that category.
