Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data to keep citizens informed about response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items were the most frequently reported issue, with 6,966 requests. The city took an average of nearly 9 days to respond to these calls. Graffiti removal was also a major concern, generating 3,165 requests with an average response time of 58 hours. Street repair requests totaled 769, taking an average of 7 days to address.

Some categories show extremely fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

To help improve the community, please continue to report local issues. Encourage your friends and neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
