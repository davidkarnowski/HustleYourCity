Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach has handled citizen requests over the past 90 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,667 reports. The city took an average of 10 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti reports were the second most common with 3,098 calls, averaging a response time of 69.6 hours.

Street repair requests totaled 826, with an average response time of 6.3 days. Tree maintenance saw 909 requests, taking an average of 6.8 days to address.

Some services saw much faster turnaround times. Dead animal pickups averaged an 11.8-hour response across 435 reports. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help hold the city accountable.
