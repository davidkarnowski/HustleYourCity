Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest city service response data to keep you informed about how Long Beach is handling neighborhood issues. Here is a look at the numbers over the last 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to drive the highest call volumes. The city received 6,958 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 8.5 days. Graffiti issues generated 3,238 reports, taking an average of 57 hours to address. E-scooter complaints were also a major factor, totaling 2,928 reports.

Response times varied significantly across other services. Tree maintenance requests, totaling 893 calls, took an average of 9 days to resolve. Street repair saw 702 reports with an 8-day average response. On the faster end, dead animal pickups averaged about 10 hours for 468 calls. Business license and permit concerns showed a remarkably fast response time of just 12 minutes, though it is important to note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Public reporting is essential for accountability and neighborhood upkeep. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 17, 2026 09:12 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 17, 2026 07:00 AM PDT.
