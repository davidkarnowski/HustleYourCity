Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests.

The most reported issue was "Dumped Items" with 2,171 calls, taking an average of about 8 days to resolve. Graffiti removal was the next most common request with 904 reports, which were handled in just under 3 days on average. Street and light repairs both saw around 400 requests each, with similar response times of just under 3 days.

Some of the quickest responses included Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services calls, which were both addressed in about 12 to 13 hours on average. Sidewalk repair requests were handled in about a day and a half.

Reports for Illegal Fireworks and Traffic Signal issues took longer, with average response times of about 5 days. Over 900 reports about E-Scooters were also filed; these are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at Jan. 22, 2026, 3:33 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at Jan. 22, 2026, 3:02 PM PST.
