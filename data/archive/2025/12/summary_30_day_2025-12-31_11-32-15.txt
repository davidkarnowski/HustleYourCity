Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days.

The most reported issue was Dumped Items, with 2,061 requests. The average response time for these requests was about 10 days. Graffiti was the second most common report with 1,003 requests, which were addressed in about 2.3 days on average.

Other key response times include Street Repair (2.3 days), Park Maintenance (3.7 days), and Tree Maintenance (about 1 day). Quicker responses were seen for Dead Animal Pickups, averaging under 12 hours. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.
