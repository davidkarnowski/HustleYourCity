Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the past 90 days based on public data.

The most common requests from residents were for dumped items (5,920 reports) and graffiti removal (3,532 reports).

Average response times for some key services include approximately 14 days for dumped item pickups and about 4.7 days for graffiti removal. Park maintenance requests took an average of nearly 16 days to close. Quicker responses were seen for animal-related services, with both dead animal pickup and general animal services requests closed in under 17 hours on average.

Some requests, like those for damaged drinking fountains, show an average response time of just over 2 hours. These unusually fast times may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
