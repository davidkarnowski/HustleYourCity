Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service request data from the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at the numbers over the past 90 days.

Dumped items generated the most reports with 7,025 requests, followed by graffiti with 3,107 reports, and e-scooter issues with 2,854 reports.

Response times varied significantly by category. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to dumped items and about 6 days for street repairs. Graffiti reports saw an average response time of 59 hours. 

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at April 2, 2026, 10:14 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 2, 2026, 7:00 PM PDT.
