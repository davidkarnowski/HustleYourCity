Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,300 service requests were submitted. The most reported issue was "Dumped Items" with 576 calls, which had an average closure time of 44 hours. The second most common report was for "Graffiti" with 250 calls, which were addressed in about 32 hours on average.

Some services had faster average response times. "Dead Animal Pickup" requests were closed in about 4.7 hours, and "Animal Services" calls were closed in approximately 8.4 hours.

It is important to note that some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.
