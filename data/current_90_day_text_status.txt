In the past 90 days Long Beach residents filed 16,459 service requests. The busiest categories were Dumped Items (6,492 reports), Graffiti (3,629), and E‑Scooter issues (2,430).

Average response times show many services are being handled quickly. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were closed in about 18 hours on average. Graffiti took roughly 131 hours (about 5.5 days) to resolve, while Dumped Items averaged 379 hours (≈ 15.8 days). Light, Tree Maintenance and Sidewalk Repair were completed in under 3 days on average.

Overall, more than 90 % of requests across all categories are marked Closed or Closed Referred, indicating a high completion rate.

Residents are encouraged to report new issues and track progress through the Go Long Beach mobile app.
