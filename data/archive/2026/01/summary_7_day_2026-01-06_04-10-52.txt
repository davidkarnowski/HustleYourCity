Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past week.

Over 1,200 service requests were made by residents. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (451 reports), e-scooter issues (239 reports), and graffiti (172 reports).

City response times for several key services have improved recently. The average response time for Graffiti removal was about 1.3 days, a significant decrease from the 90-day average of over 4 days. Dumped Items requests were addressed in about 1.75 days on average, compared to a 90-day average of over 2 weeks. Other average response times included about 16 hours for Street Repair and 9 hours for Dead Animal Pickup. Note: Some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
