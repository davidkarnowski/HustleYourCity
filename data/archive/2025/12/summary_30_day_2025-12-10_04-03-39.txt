City service request activity in Long Beach over the past 30 days shows both progress and ongoing challenges.

Graffiti reports total 1,235 with an average response time of 55.8 hours (about 2.3 days). This is a marked improvement from the 99 hour average seen in the prior 60‑day window.

E‑Scooter issues remain high in volume (730) and are being routed as “Closed Referred” quickly, though no specific response time is recorded.

Dead animal pickups (126) are being handled in roughly 10.6 hours on average, down from 16–17 hours in earlier periods.

Dumped items continue to be a large workload (2,015 reports) with an average response of 204.6 hours (≈8.5 days), better than the 310‑hour average two months ago but still a concern.

Sidewalk repair (83) and light repairs (253) now average 47.9 hours and 59.4 hours respectively, both faster than the 127‑hour and 128‑hour averages previously.

Street repair requests (212) are resolved in about 145 hours (≈6 days), improving from the 256‑hour average seen earlier.

Animal services (48) show the quickest turnaround at 8.4 hours, a significant gain from the 17‑hour average in the last 60 days.

Other categories such as traffic signals (49 hours), tree maintenance (115 hours), trash & debris (153 hours) and abandoned shopping carts (52 hours) also show reduced response times compared with prior periods.

Despite these gains, many requests remain “In Progress” – for example, 173 graffiti and 636 dumped‑item cases – indicating continued workload.

Residents are encouraged to report issues and track progress through the Go Long Beach service app.
