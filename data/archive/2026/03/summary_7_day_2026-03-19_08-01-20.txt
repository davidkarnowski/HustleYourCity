Hustle Long Beach! is here with the latest government accountability update. An independent review of the City of Long Beach service call data from the past 7 days shows how quickly community issues are being addressed.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 531 requests. While the average response time was 67.69 hours, 393 of these requests remain in "New" status, with only 40 marked as closed.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service. Out of 235 reports, the city successfully closed 169 cases with an average response time of 25.49 hours.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 131 requests. All of these were marked as "Closed Referred." Keep in mind that unusually low response times or immediate closures may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Dead Animal Pickup requests were handled efficiently. Out of 28 reports, 27 were closed with an average response time of 4.49 hours. Tree Maintenance received 54 requests, with 26 closed and an average response time of 15.04 hours.

If an issue arises in a neighborhood, make sure it gets reported. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
