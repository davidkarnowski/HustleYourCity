The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 6,710 reports for dumped items and 3,281 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the most efficient service, with an average response time of approximately 5.4 hours over the last 90 days. Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
