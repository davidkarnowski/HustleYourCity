Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update for the past week.

Residents submitted nearly 1,500 service requests. The most common report was for illegally dumped items, with 577 requests. The average response time to close these reports was about 2 days. The second most frequent issue was graffiti, with 236 reports handled in an average of about 23 hours.

Other services saw quicker resolutions. Vegetation maintenance requests were addressed in an average of about 8.5 hours, and dead animal pickups were completed in about 9 hours. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not on-site work completion.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at Jan 10, 2026, 7:31 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at Jan 10, 2026, 7:00 AM PST.
