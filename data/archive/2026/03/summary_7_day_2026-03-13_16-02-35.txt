Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service call data from the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at the city's response times and call totals over the past 7 days.

Dumped items generated the most requests with 544 reports. The city averaged a response time of 3.1 days for these issues. E-scooter complaints followed with 259 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti removal saw 233 reports with an average response time of 29 hours.

Some services saw rapid turnaround times, though unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Traffic signal issues averaged a 3-hour response time across 6 reports. Dead animal pickups averaged 8.4 hours for 39 reports. Animal services averaged 7.5 hours across 26 reports.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
