Here's the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 24 hours. Residents submitted 180 new requests for service.

The most frequent issue reported was "Dumped Items", with 90 requests and an average close time of about 15.5 hours. Other common requests included Graffiti (average 7.7 hours) and Tree Maintenance (average 6.1 hours).

Response times varied across different services. Sidewalk repair requests had the longest average response time at over 20 hours. On the other hand, Park Maintenance requests were closed in an average of about 37 minutes and Restroom Maintenance in about 85 minutes. These unusually fast times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
