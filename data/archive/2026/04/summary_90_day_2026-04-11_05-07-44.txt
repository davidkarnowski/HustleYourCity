Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest third-party accountability update on city service requests and response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items generated the highest call volume with 7,002 reports. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 3,250 reports, averaging a 54-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also saw high numbers with 2,916 reports.

Response times vary significantly across different departments. Tree maintenance, with 899 requests, averaged a 7.6-day response time. Street repairs, totaling 732 requests, took about 7.1 days. Conversely, dead animal pickups were resolved much faster, averaging 10.7 hours for 464 requests. Business license and permit concerns showed a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.

This data summary was updated at April 10, 2026 10:04 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 10, 2026 7:00 PM PDT.
