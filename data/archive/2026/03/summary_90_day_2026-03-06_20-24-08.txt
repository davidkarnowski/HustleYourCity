Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days shows high activity in refuse and maintenance requests.

Dumped Items remained the most frequent report with 6,554 total calls. The average response time for these items over the three-month period was approximately 11.2 days. However, data from the last 7 days indicates a faster turnaround of roughly 3 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,085 with an average completion time of 3.4 days. Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 839 reports and averaged 5.4 days for response.

Dead Animal Pickup services averaged 13.7 hours per request. E-Scooter complaints saw 2,930 reports, almost all of which were referred to third-party operators immediately.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than physical work completions.
