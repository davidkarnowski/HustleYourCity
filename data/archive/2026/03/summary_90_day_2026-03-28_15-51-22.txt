Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest service call data to keep citizens informed about city response times. Over the past 90 days, the data reveals significant trends in how local service requests are being handled.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of reports with 6,924 calls. These requests took an average of 9.5 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,116 requests, averaging a 64.8-hour response time. E-scooter complaints reached 2,832, though all of these were referred to outside agencies rather than closed by city staff.
