Long Beach Service Request Data Update: Last 90 Days

An evaluation of city service requests over the past three months shows that Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,372 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 2 weeks.

Graffiti removal received the second-highest volume with 3,155 reports, showing an average completion time of roughly 4 days. Street Repair requests, totaling 779 reports, are currently taking about 5 days to resolve.

Dead Animal Pickup remains one of the faster services. With 409 reports filed, the average response time is 11 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. Consistent reporting helps generate the data necessary to track response times and service needs.
