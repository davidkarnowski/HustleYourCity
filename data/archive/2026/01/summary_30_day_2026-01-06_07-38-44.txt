Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past month, data shows significant improvements in response times for several key services. The average time to address reports of illegally dumped items has fallen to about 8 days, down from over 14 days three months ago. Graffiti removal requests are now being closed in an average of just over 2 days, compared to nearly 5 days previously. Street repair requests saw an average completion time of about 34 hours, a sharp decrease from the 9-day average reported three months ago.

In total, residents submitted over 5,000 service requests in the last 30 days, with the top categories being dumped items, e-scooters, and graffiti. Some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.
