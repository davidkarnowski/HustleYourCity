Graffiti reports remain the most common issue in the last 30 days with 1,423 requests. The city closed 1,121 of them, averaging a response time of about 66.7 hours (≈2.8 days). This is an improvement from the previous 60‑day period, where the average was 87.4 hours.

Dumped Items saw 2,002 reports. While 900 were closed, the average response time dropped dramatically to 168.9 hours (≈7 days), down from 335.9 hours in the prior 60 days, indicating faster clean‑up efforts.

Tree Maintenance had 232 requests, with an average response of 10.6 hours—significantly quicker than the 16.8 hours seen in the last 60 days.

Light outages generated 241 reports; the average response was 40.9 hours, slightly slower than the 37.6 hours recorded in the previous two months.

- Street Repair: 199 requests, average 21.2 hours.
