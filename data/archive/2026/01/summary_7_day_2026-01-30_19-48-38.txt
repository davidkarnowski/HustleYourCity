Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

The most reported issue was "Dumped Items" with 615 requests, which took an average of 68 hours to close. This was followed by 281 "Graffiti" reports, with an average response time of about 32 hours.

Response times for other services varied. Reports of "Running Water" took an average of nearly 6 days to be addressed. The 268 "E-Scooter" reports were referred directly to scooter companies and are not handled by city staff.
