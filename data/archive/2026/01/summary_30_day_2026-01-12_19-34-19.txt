Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times from the past 30 days.

Dumped Items and Graffiti continue to be the most frequent issues reported. In the last month, there were 1,904 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of about 5.5 days. Graffiti removal requests totaled 904, with an average response of about 52 hours.

Response times for other services varied. Reports for street light issues averaged about 3 days to close. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 14 hours on average. It is important to note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
