Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last seven days, residents submitted over 1,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (374 requests), e-scooter issues (223), and graffiti (207).

Response times varied by issue. The average time to address graffiti reports was about 30 hours. Reports of dumped items were closed in about 36 hours on average. Streetlight repair requests took longer, averaging over two days to resolve.

Some requests, such as for Park Maintenance or Restroom Maintenance, were closed in under an hour. These fast closures may be administrative and not reflect the completion of physical work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the city's Go Long Beach service application.
