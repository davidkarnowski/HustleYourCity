Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti were the most reported issues, with over 6,100 and 3,700 service calls respectively. The data shows the average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 15 days, while park maintenance requests took an average of 17 days.

In contrast, requests for dead animal pickup were typically closed in about 17 hours, and animal services requests were closed in about 15 hours. A notable change was seen in sidewalk repair response times, which averaged just under 4 days to close.
