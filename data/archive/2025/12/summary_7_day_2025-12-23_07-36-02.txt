Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the past week.

Over 1,160 new service requests were submitted by residents. The most reported issues were for dumped items (445 requests), e-scooters (246 requests), and graffiti (230 requests).

Based on closed requests, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 26 hours. Reports for dumped items were addressed in about 1.6 days on average. Dead animal pickups were completed in an average of about 11.5 hours. A single restroom maintenance request was closed in approximately 51 minutes; however, unusually fast closures can sometimes be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at 11:33 PM PST on December 22, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 PM PST on December 22, 2025.
