The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows significant activity in service requests over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 7,124 reports and an average response time of approximately 8 days. Graffiti reports totaled 3,371 with an average response time of about 3 days.

Tree maintenance requests saw 654 reports with an average response time of approximately 64 hours. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging 10 hours and 5 hours respectively.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track city service performance.
