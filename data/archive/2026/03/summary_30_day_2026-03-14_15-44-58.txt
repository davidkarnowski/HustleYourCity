Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data to keep citizens informed on city response times and service volumes.

Dumped Items remain the highest reported issue with 2132 calls. The city averaged a response time of 5 days, successfully closing 1450 of those requests.

Graffiti removal saw 1053 reports with an average response time of 43 hours. E-Scooter complaints reached 1039 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Infrastructure and maintenance also saw high volumes. Tree Maintenance received 485 reports with a 52-hour average response time. Street Repair had 264 reports, taking an average of 3 days to respond. Park Maintenance handled 68 reports with a 4-day average response time.

Some categories showed unusually fast turnaround times. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help maintain the community.

This data summary was updated at March 14, 2026 8:41 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 14, 2026 8:00 AM PDT.
