Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service requests based on data from the last 90 days.

Dumped Items remain the most frequent issue reported, with 6,428 total reports. The average time to resolve these requests is currently approximately 13 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,081, with an average response time of about 4 days. There were 2,966 reports regarding E-Scooters, though the majority of these are referred to third-party operators immediately.

Street Repair requests show an average completion time of 4 and a half days across 794 reports. Tree Maintenance requests are taking nearly one week to resolve on average.

Dead Animal Pickup shows the fastest response among major categories, averaging just under 13 hours per request.

Residents are encouraged to report issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city has accurate data on service needs.

Please note that unusually low response times listed in raw data may be due to administrative closures rather than physical work completion.
