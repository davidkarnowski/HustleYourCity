The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,295 reports for dumped items and 2,561 reports for graffiti. Response times for graffiti have averaged approximately 66 hours, while dumped items have seen average response times of about 19 days.

Tree maintenance requests totaled 1,048 over the last 90 days, with an average response time of about 8 days. Animal services remain a high-volume area, with 223 reports handled in the same timeframe, maintaining a quick average response time of approximately 11 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This tool helps ensure that service requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
