Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the past week.

The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (632 reports) and Graffiti (284 reports). According to the data, the average response time to close a dumped items case was about 66 hours. Graffiti reports were closed in an average of 31 hours.
