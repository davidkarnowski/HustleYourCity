Hustle Long Beach! Data Update: City Service Responses

Here is a look at Long Beach service request data from the last 24 hours. In total, residents submitted 101 new service requests. The most common reports were for E-Scooters (34), Dumped Items (19), and Graffiti (16).

For requests that were closed during this period, some response times were notably fast. The single closed Animal Services request was handled in about 10 minutes. It is possible that unusually fast closures are due to administrative processing rather than on-site work.

Other closed requests included Dead Animal Pickups, which averaged about 4 hours, and Tree Maintenance, which averaged around 7.5 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the Go Long Beach service application is the official way to report it to the city.
