Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest third-party accountability update on City of Long Beach service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue with 2191 calls. The average response time for these reports is approximately 5 days. Graffiti is the second most common issue with 1054 reports, taking an average of 38 hours to address.

Tree maintenance received 492 requests with an average response time of 66 hours. Street repair saw 264 reports, averaging a 3-day response time. Park maintenance had 70 reports, taking nearly 4 days on average to resolve.

The fastest recorded response was for Business License and Permit Concerns, averaging just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help improve the community.
