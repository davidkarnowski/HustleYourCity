Here is a look at the latest service request data from the City of Long Beach.

Over the past 24 hours, residents submitted 175 new service requests. The most frequently reported issue was "Dumped Items," accounting for 72 of the total reports. Other common requests included reports for streetlights, graffiti, and e-scooters.

The data shows some notably fast average response times for completed requests. It is possible these quick closures are administrative and do not reflect the time for work to be completed.
