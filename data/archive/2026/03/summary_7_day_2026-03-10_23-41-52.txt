Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day city service response data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items led the request volume with 488 calls in the past week. The city took an average of 3.3 days to respond to these issues. E-scooter complaints followed with 238 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti removal saw 225 requests, with an average response time of 1.4 days.

Other notable service times include dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 7.9 hours across 40 calls. Running water complaints showed an unusually fast average response time of just 13 minutes for 5 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep the city accountable and clean. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
