Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest city service data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled service requests over the past 7 days.

The highest volume of reports came in for Dumped Items with 551 requests. E-Scooter issues followed with 220 reports, and Graffiti had 191 requests. Street Repair saw 58 requests, while Sidewalk Repair had 46.

When looking at response times, the city averaged about 69 hours to address Dumped Items. Graffiti requests were handled in an average of 21 hours, and Street Repair took about 21 hours. Dead Animal Pickup was faster, averaging just under 4 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
