Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times. The service call data from the last 7 days has been analyzed to keep citizens informed on how local issues are being handled.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests this past week with 564 calls. The average response time for these reports was approximately 3.5 days. E-scooter complaints followed with 282 calls, all of which were administratively referred and closed. Graffiti was another major issue, bringing in 265 reports with an average response time of about 35 hours.

Other notable service areas included sidewalk repairs, which saw 51 calls and an average response time of 7.5 hours. Dead animal pickups averaged a 2.5-hour response across 36 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens can help improve Long Beach neighborhoods by reporting issues when they see them. Everyone is encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to submit reports directly to the city.
