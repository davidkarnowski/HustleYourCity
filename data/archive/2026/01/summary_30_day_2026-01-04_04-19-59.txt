Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 30 days.

The most common service requests from the public were for dumped items (1,846 calls), e-scooters (1,011 calls), and graffiti (914 calls).

The average response time to close out reports for dumped items and trash or debris was about 9 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 2.5 days. More urgent requests, like dead animal pickups, were handled in about 14 hours on average.
