The latest data for city service requests over the last 90 days shows varied response times across key categories.

Dead animal pickup remains the most efficient service, with an average response time of approximately 7 minutes. Animal services also show strong performance, averaging about 11 minutes per request.

Graffiti removal requests have been addressed with an average response time of about 67 minutes. Meanwhile, light maintenance requests are averaging approximately 54 minutes.

Some services are seeing longer wait times. Dumped items, which saw 7,214 total requests in the last 90 days, are averaging about 20 days for resolution. Tree maintenance requests are averaging about 9 days, while sidewalk repairs are averaging about 5 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
