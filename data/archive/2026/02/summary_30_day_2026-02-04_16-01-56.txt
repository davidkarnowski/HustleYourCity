Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 30 days.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped items (2,546 reports), graffiti (1,076 reports), and e-scooters (1,005 reports). Data shows that average response times have improved across several major categories compared to the previous month.

For example, the average time for graffiti removal is now about 2.5 days. Tree maintenance requests were addressed in about 30 hours on average, and sidewalk repairs were handled in about 33 hours. Reports of illegally dumped items, the most common request, took an average of about 9.5 days to resolve. Some service requests may show unusually fast completion times, which can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work.
