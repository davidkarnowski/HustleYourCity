Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last seven days, residents submitted over 1,100 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (412 requests), graffiti (191 requests), and e-scooter issues (166 requests).

Based on city data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 55 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 39 hours on average, while requests for dead animal pickup were closed in just under 2 hours. Some issues, like park maintenance requests, were closed in an average of 37 minutes. Note that unusually fast times can reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
