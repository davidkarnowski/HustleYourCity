Here is the latest 24-hour accountability update from Hustle Long Beach! evaluating City of Long Beach service requests.

In the past day, Dumped Items led the request board with 90 new reports, though 81 of those remain untouched in the new status. E-Scooter complaints followed with 51 reports, all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies. Graffiti also saw notable activity with 22 total requests.

Looking at response times, several categories showed unusually fast turnarounds. Sidewalk Repair requests averaged just 11 minutes. Please note that this rapid time is likely due to administrative closures or referrals rather than completed physical repairs. Dead Animal Pickup averaged 62 minutes across 12 closed cases, Tree Maintenance averaged 70 minutes, and Graffiti responses averaged 81 minutes. Shoes on a Utility Wire took slightly longer, averaging about 2 hours for a referral.

Residents are encouraged to keep holding the city accountable. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
