Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days based on public data.

Residents submitted nearly 5,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,901 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (1,052), and graffiti (991). E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. Dumped items took about 8 days on average to be resolved. Graffiti removal averaged about 2.3 days, while street light issues were addressed in about 2 days. The average response time for traffic signal repairs was about 4 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
