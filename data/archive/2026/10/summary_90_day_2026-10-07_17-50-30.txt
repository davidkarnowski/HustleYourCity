The latest data on city service requests in Long Beach shows a high volume of activity regarding dumped items and graffiti over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain a primary concern with 8,333 total reports in the last 90 days. The average response time for these requests is approximately 17 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-frequency service, with 2,798 reports in the same period and an average response time of about 70 hours.

Tree maintenance requests have seen a total of 910 reports over the last 90 days, with an average response time of approximately 84 hours. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging between 6 and 12 hours for the last 90 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
