Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests during this period. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,226 calls) and graffiti (3,751 calls). The average time to resolve a dumped item request was about 15 days, while graffiti reports took an average of about 6.5 days.

More urgent requests were handled faster. The average response time for both Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup was approximately 17 hours. Reports for abandoned shopping carts were addressed in under two days on average. E-scooter reports, totaling over 2,100, are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for resolution.
