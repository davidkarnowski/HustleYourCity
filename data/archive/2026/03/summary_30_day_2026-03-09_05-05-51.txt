Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at how the city handled citizen reports over the past 30 days.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues. Citizens reported 2005 dumped items, which took the city an average of 4.8 days to address. Graffiti saw 1070 reports with an average response time of about 43 hours. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 1009 reports, though these are typically referred to outside vendors.

For city infrastructure, street repair received 264 calls, averaging 3.2 days for a response. Tree maintenance had 455 reports with a 43-hour average response time, and 331 lighting issues were reported with a 58-hour average response time.

Some services saw very rapid turnarounds. Dead animal pickups, totaling 137 reports, were handled in an average of 14.5 hours. Business license and permit concerns showed an unusually fast response time of just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep the city accountable and well-maintained. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
