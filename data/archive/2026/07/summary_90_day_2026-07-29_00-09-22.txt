The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,142 requests for dumped items and 3,383 graffiti reports.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickups remain the fastest, averaging about 5 hours. Conversely, dumped item reports are taking longer to resolve, with an average response time of approximately 8 days. Graffiti removal is averaging 3 days.
