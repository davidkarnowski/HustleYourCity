Here is the latest Hustle Long Beach! community update regarding City of Long Beach service response times. This analysis focuses on data from the past 30 days to keep residents informed about local government accountability.

Dumped items generated the highest call volume with 2,346 reports. The average response time for these requests was over 8 days. E-scooter complaints reached 1,053 reports, though nearly all were immediately closed and referred elsewhere, which often reflects administrative routing rather than physical work completion.

Graffiti removal teams handled 1,042 reports, taking an average of 55 hours to respond. Street repair saw 213 requests with an average response time of 71 hours. Meanwhile, the city handled 172 dead animal pickup requests with an average response time of roughly 7 hours.

Residents should note that unusually low response times in certain categories may be due to administrative closures. To help hold the city accountable and improve local neighborhoods, please continue to report issues using the Go Long Beach service application.
