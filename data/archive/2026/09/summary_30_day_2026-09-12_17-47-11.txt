The latest service data for Long Beach shows significant activity regarding city maintenance requests over the past 30 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request type, with 2,861 reports filed in the last month. While 1,166 of these have been closed, the average response time for this category is approximately 8.6 days.

Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service, with 903 reports in the last 30 days. The city has maintained an average response time of about 40.7 hours for these requests.

Tree maintenance saw 321 reports, with an average response time of roughly 2.2 days. Additionally, 107 reports regarding E-Scooters were processed, though these are primarily handled via referral.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Using the app helps ensure that requests are tracked and routed to the appropriate departments for action.
