Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data from the past seven days. The City of Long Beach received the highest volume of requests for Dumped Items with 553 reports. E-Scooter complaints followed with 266 reports, all of which were closed and referred to other entities. Graffiti issues accounted for 224 calls.

Response times for these top issues varied. Dumped Items took an average of 62 hours to address. Graffiti reports were handled in about 24 hours. Other notable response times include Tree Maintenance at 21 hours and Street Repair at 18 hours. Traffic Signal issues saw a fast turnaround at 2.5 hours. It is important to note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps track neighborhood needs and ensures accurate data collection.
