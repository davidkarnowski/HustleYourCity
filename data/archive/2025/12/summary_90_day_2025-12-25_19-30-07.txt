Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most common service requests from the public were for Dumped Items (5,988 reports) and Graffiti (3,683 reports). The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 15 days, while graffiti reports were handled in about 5.5 days on average.

Reports for street light issues took about one week to resolve. Animal-related services were among the fastest, with responses for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services calls averaging under 17 hours. Over 2,300 reports were made for E-Scooters; these are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.
