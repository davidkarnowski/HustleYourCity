The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 30 days. Residents submitted 2,254 reports for dumped items, with 870 of those cases now closed. Graffiti removal remains a high-volume service, with 871 reports filed and 633 successfully closed during this period.

Response times for several categories have shown improvement. Dead animal pickup requests are being addressed quickly, with an average response time of approximately 7 hours. Street repair response times have averaged about 20 hours, while light maintenance requests are averaging roughly 29 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
