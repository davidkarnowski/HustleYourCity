Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past week.

Over 1,500 service requests were made through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (584 calls), improperly parked e-scooters (263 calls), and graffiti (237 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 29 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 2.2 days on average. Street repair requests were closed in an average of 21 hours. A single marina repair request took about 4.2 days to close. Some requests, like one for restroom maintenance, are closed in minutes, which may indicate an administrative closure rather than a completed work order.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
