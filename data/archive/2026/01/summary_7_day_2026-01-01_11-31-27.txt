Here is a summary of Long Beach service request data from the past week.

Reports for dumped items were the most frequent category with 445 requests. The average response time to close these requests was about 34 hours. Graffiti removal was also a high-volume category with 197 reports, taking an average of about 41 hours to resolve.

The city received 98 reports for street light issues, with an average response time of nearly 3 days. For the 10 park maintenance requests filed, the average response time was about 36 minutes. Unusually fast response times can sometimes indicate administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
