Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues. In the last 90 days, there were 6,972 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 8.5 days. Graffiti saw 3,243 reports, taking an average of 55.5 hours to address.

Infrastructure requests also saw significant activity. The city received 894 tree maintenance requests, averaging nearly 9 days for a response, and 698 street repair calls, averaging about 8 days. Street light issues generated 885 reports, taking an average of 3.5 days to resolve.

Some services show very fast turnaround times. Dead animal pickups, with 463 reports, averaged just under 10 hours. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of 12 minutes. Unusually low responses like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 20, 2026 at 10:35 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 20, 2026 at 7:00 PM PDT.
