Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data. Dumped items, graffiti, and e-scooter issues were the most frequently reported problems by residents.

The average city response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 14.5 days. For "Graffiti" removal, the average was just under 5 days. "Street Repair" requests took an average of about 9.5 days to be closed.

Faster response times were recorded for other categories. "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" requests were both closed in less than 24 hours on average. "Abandoned Shopping Cart" reports were addressed in about 1.5 days.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at 11:27 AM PST on January 3, 2026 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 AM PST on January 3, 2026.
