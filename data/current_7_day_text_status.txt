Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted over 1,200 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (475 requests), graffiti (259 requests), and e-scooters (251 requests).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 1.6 days, while dumped item pickups averaged about 2.2 days. Some services, like sidewalk repair (45 minutes) and restroom maintenance (51 minutes), showed unusually fast average closure times. These may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
