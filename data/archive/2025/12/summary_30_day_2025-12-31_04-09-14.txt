Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with 2,064 requests, followed by 1,006 reports for graffiti. The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 10 days. For graffiti removal, the average response time was about 55 hours.

Faster responses were recorded for other services. The average response time for Animal Services requests was under 9 hours, and dead animal pickups were completed in about 12 hours on average.
