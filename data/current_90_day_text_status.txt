Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 90 days.

The most common service request was for "Dumped Items," with over 6,100 reports filed. The average time to resolve these requests was about 15 days. "Graffiti" was the second most common issue, with over 3,700 reports and an average completion time of about 6.5 days.

In contrast, more urgent requests were handled faster. The 429 reports for "Dead Animal Pickup" were resolved in about 16 hours on average, and "Animal Services" calls were handled in about 15 hours.

Reporting issues helps provide the city with data on community needs. Residents can report non-emergency issues using the Go Long Beach service application.
