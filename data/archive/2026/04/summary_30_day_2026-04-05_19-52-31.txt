Hustle Long Beach! is sharing the latest independent data review of city service response times. The goal is to keep Long Beach residents informed about how efficiently local agencies are handling community requests. Here is a look at the service call data over the past 30 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue by far, with 2,305 requests in the last month. The average response time for these reports is currently about 8.3 days. Graffiti removal saw 975 reports, with a much faster average turnaround of 33 hours. E-scooter complaints also stayed high with 909 reports, though nearly all were immediately referred to outside operators.

For infrastructure, street repair requests totaled 212 and are taking an average of 3.2 days to address. Sidewalk repairs are averaging 32.5 hours. On the faster side, dead animal pickups (162 reports) are averaging just 5 hours, and general animal services (79 reports) are taking about 8.6 hours. Keep in mind that unusually low response times in the data may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting local issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit requests directly to the city and help keep the community clean and safe.

This data summary was updated at April 5, 2026, at 12:49 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 5, 2026, at 11:00 AM PDT.
