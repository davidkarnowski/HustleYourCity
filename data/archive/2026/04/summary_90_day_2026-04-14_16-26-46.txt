Hustle Long Beach community, here is an independent review of the City of Long Beach service call data from the last 90 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,935 calls. The average response time for these issues was 8.5 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,263 calls, taking an average of 55 hours to address. E-Scooter issues also saw high volume with 2,958 reports, though response times were not provided by the city.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests showed longer turnaround times. Tree Maintenance received 895 calls with an average response of 8.1 days, while Street Repair had 709 calls averaging 7.7 days. Sidewalk Repair requests took about 5.9 days to process across 377 reports.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
