Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from the public were for dumped items, with 6,200 calls. The average response time to close these requests was about 13 days. The next most common issue was graffiti, with 3,369 reports, which were handled in an average of 5 days.

Some services saw much faster response times. The 175 calls for Animal Services were closed in an average of about 13 hours, and the 427 requests for dead animal pickup were completed in about 14.5 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report problems like graffiti or dumped items is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
