Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past month, residents submitted thousands of service requests. The most common reports were for "Dumped Items" with 1,906 requests and "Graffiti" with 1,118 requests.

Response times vary by issue. The average time to close a "Dumped Items" report was about 8 days. "Graffiti" reports were resolved faster, with an average response time of about 2.3 days. Some of the quickest responses were for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services", both averaging under 10 hours. These unusually fast times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
