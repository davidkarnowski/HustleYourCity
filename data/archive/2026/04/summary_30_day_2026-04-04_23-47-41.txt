Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest service request data for the past 30 days to track how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs.

Here are the most significant service request trends from the last month:

- Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,304 requests. The average response time was 8.3 days.
- Graffiti reports totaled 979, with an average response time of 33.8 hours.
- E-Scooter complaints reached 928, all of which were referred to outside agencies.
- Street Repair saw 218 requests, taking an average of 3.3 days to address.
- Dead Animal Pickup had 164 requests with an average response of 5 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may be the result of administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Submitting reports helps maintain accurate data and holds the city accountable for neighborhood maintenance.
