Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service call data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual look at the request totals and average response times for Long Beach.

Dumped Items led the request volume with 6,938 reports, followed by Graffiti with 3,248 calls and E-Scooter issues with 2,957 reports. Tree Maintenance also saw high engagement with 895 requests.

Response times varied significantly across categories. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to Dumped Items and 8.3 days for Tree Maintenance. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 55 hours. Dead Animal Pickup requests saw faster action, averaging just under 10 hours.

Business License and Permit Concerns showed an unusually fast response time of 12 minutes. Keep in mind that extremely low response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
