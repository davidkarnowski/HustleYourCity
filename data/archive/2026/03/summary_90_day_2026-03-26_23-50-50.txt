Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data from the last 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports.

Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 6,871 reports, averaging a response time of about 9.5 days. Graffiti removal followed with 3,104 requests, with the city responding in roughly 66 hours. 

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw high volumes. Tree Maintenance had 948 reports taking about 5.7 days to address, while 867 Street Repair requests averaged a 6.5-day response time. Street light issues generated 1,026 reports with an average response time of 3.2 days.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to help keep the city running smoothly. Please use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
