Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data to keep you informed about local response times. Over the past 7 days, the City of Long Beach received a variety of service requests, and here is a breakdown of the most significant numbers.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 590 requests, taking an average of 47 hours to address. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 271 reports. Graffiti was another major issue, with 252 requests processed in an average of 12 hours.

Other notable service areas included Light issues with 71 reports averaging a 15-hour response, and Tree Maintenance with 49 reports averaging an 8-hour response. Sidewalk and Street Repairs saw 44 and 35 requests respectively, both averaging between 11 and 17 hours for a response. Dead Animal Pickup handled 39 calls with a rapid average response time of 2 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual physical work completions.
