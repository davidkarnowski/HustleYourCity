The latest service request data for Long Beach highlights response times and volume over the past 90 days. Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,448 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 13 and a half days.

Graffiti reports were the second most frequent issue with 3,169 total entries, showing an average completion time of just over 4 days. Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 790 and are averaging a 5-day response time. Tree Maintenance is currently taking about one week to resolve.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services, both averaging under 13 hours. E-Scooter reports remain high with 2,768 entries, though these are primarily referred to third-party vendors rather than handled directly by city crews.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city to ensure they are tracked in this system.
