Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,200 requests were submitted through the city's service system. The most common reports were for dumped items (453 calls), e-scooters (239 calls), and graffiti (174 calls).

Average response times for closed requests varied by issue type. Reports for dead animal pickup were handled in about 7 hours, while graffiti cleanup took an average of 31.5 hours. Requests for trash and debris cleanup took about 3 days to close. Some fast response times may reflect administrative closures and not work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report problems like graffiti or dumped items is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
