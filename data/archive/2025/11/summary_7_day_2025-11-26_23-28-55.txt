In the past week Long Beach handled 1,025 service requests across 21 categories.  Most issues were closed within a day, and many response times are now much faster than in previous weeks.

Key improvements:
- Dumped Items: average response 42.5 hours (≈1.8 days), down from 209 hours a month ago.
- Graffiti: average response 30.3 hours, versus 73 hours in the prior 30 days.
- Street Repair: average response 11.4 hours, compared with 20 hours in the last month.
- Light repairs: average response 26.5 hours, improved from 42 hours previously.
- Traffic Signal fixes: average response 7.6 hours, down from 24 hours.
- Dead Animal Pickup: average response 6.1 hours, better than 17.5 hours before.
- Trash & Debris: average response 16.9 hours, versus 71 hours in the prior month.
