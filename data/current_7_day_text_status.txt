In the past week Long Beach recorded 1,241 service calls across all categories.

The highest volumes were E‑Scooter reports (211), Dumped Items (451) and Graffiti (304). Most issues were closed within one to two days: Restroom Maintenance averaged 1.2 hours, Dead Animal Pickup 9 hours, and Tree Maintenance 19 hours. The longest average response was for Dumped Items at 60.7 hours (about 2.5 days), while Trash & Debris took 74 hours to resolve.

Overall, the city’s response times remain under 2 days for the majority of requests, showing steady progress on street repair, sidewalk repair and vegetation work.

Residents can report new issues instantly through the Go Long Beach service app to help keep response times fast.
