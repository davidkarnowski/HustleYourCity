Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service response data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests during this period. The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (6,204 calls) and Graffiti (3,746 calls). According to city data, the average response time to close a Dumped Items report was about 15.5 days. The average for Graffiti removal was about 6.6 days.

Response times for other services varied. Park Maintenance requests took an average of 19.5 days to resolve. In contrast, calls for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were typically handled in about 17 hours. E-scooter reports, another high-volume category, are generally referred directly to scooter operators.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.

This data summary was updated at 7:29 AM PST, December 14, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 7:00 AM PST, December 14, 2025.
