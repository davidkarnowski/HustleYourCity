Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data to track how the City of Long Beach is responding to community reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 6,721 calls in the last 90 days. The city takes an average of 9.6 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti is the second most common physical issue with 3,062 reports, which the city is resolving in an average of 71 hours.

E-scooters generated 2,794 reports. All of these were marked as closed referred, which means they were routed elsewhere rather than physically addressed by city crews. 

Some issues show very fast turnaround times. Dead animal pickups are averaging an 11.7-hour response. Business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in Long Beach, report it. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests and help keep the city accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 21, 2026, 10:01 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 21, 2026, 8:00 PM PDT.
