Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times for the last 90 days.

Dumped Items continues to be the highest volume category with 6,556 reports. The average time to resolve these issues is currently 11 days. Graffiti reports are the second most frequent service request with 3,078 total calls, showing an average response time of about 3 days.

Street Repair requests totaled 838 and are taking approximately 5 days to resolve. Tree Maintenance shows a similar timeline, with 846 reports averaging 6 days for a response.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 14 hours across 429 reports. Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average closure time of 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
