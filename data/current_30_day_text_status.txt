Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data. Dumped Items (1,922 requests) and Graffiti (1,147 requests) remain the most reported issues.

The average response time to close out requests for Dumped Items was about 8 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in about 2 days on average. For urgent issues, Dead Animal Pickup requests were addressed in an average of 9 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
