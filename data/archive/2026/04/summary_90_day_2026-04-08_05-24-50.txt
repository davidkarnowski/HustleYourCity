Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on City of Long Beach service response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 6,939 calls. The average response time for these requests is currently about 8.6 days. Graffiti removal saw 3,204 reports, with crews responding in an average of 57 hours.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 2,899, though all were referred to outside operators. Tree maintenance received 905 requests, taking an average of 4.6 days to address. Street repair had 747 requests with an average response time of 7.2 days.

Some categories show unusually fast resolution times, which often indicate administrative closures rather than completed field work. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at April 7, 2026, 10:21 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 7, 2026, 7:00 PM PDT.
