Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests over the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,000 requests for service. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (1,951 requests) and graffiti (1,085 requests).

The average response time to close a dumped item report was about 6.5 days. For graffiti, the average was about 2.5 days. Street repair requests were completed in an average of about 3.7 days.

There were also 955 reports related to e-scooters. These are typically referred directly to the scooter vendors for resolution.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at 11:26 AM PST on December 20, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 AM PST on December 20, 2025.
