Here is the latest 24-hour city service data summary for the Hustle Long Beach! community. This independent review looks at what citizens reported to the city and how quickly those issues were processed over the last day.

In the past 24 hours, Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 90 total reports, the vast majority of which are newly opened. E-Scooter complaints followed with 49 reports, all of which were closed and referred. Graffiti issues saw 41 total calls, with an average response time of about 3.3 hours.

Several categories recorded exceptionally fast response times. Tree Maintenance requests averaged just 57 minutes. Sidewalk Repair and Street Repair both averaged 79 minutes. Citizens should keep in mind that unusually low response times for major physical repairs may be due to administrative closures, initial assessments, or system routing rather than actual work completions.
