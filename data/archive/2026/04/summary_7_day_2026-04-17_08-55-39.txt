Hustle Long Beach! is here with your weekly accountability update. An independent review of the latest service call data shows how the City of Long Beach handled public reports over the last 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 595 reports. The city took an average of 3.6 days to respond to these requests. E-Scooter issues were the second most common with 274 reports, though response times for these were not tracked. Graffiti complaints followed closely with 254 reports and an average response time of 41 hours. 

Some services showed very fast turnarounds, such as Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 3.8 hours across 39 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative ticket closures rather than actual work completions.
