Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past week.

In the last seven days, the most frequent reports were for dumped items (378 calls), e-scooters (227 calls), and graffiti (209 calls). The average response time to close a "dumped items" report was about 1.5 days, while graffiti reports were closed in just over one day on average. All 227 e-scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Other notable response times include street light repairs, which averaged about two days, and dead animal pickups, which averaged about 6.5 hours. Some requests, like Park Maintenance (about 59 minutes) and Restroom Maintenance (about 51 minutes), were closed very quickly. These fast closures can sometimes be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.
