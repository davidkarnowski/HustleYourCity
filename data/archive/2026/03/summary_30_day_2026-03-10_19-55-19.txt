Hustle Long Beach! continues to track city service response times to keep the community informed. Over the past 30 days, residents submitted thousands of service requests, and the data shows how quickly the city is handling them.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 2,095 reports. The average response time for these issues was 4.7 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,091 requests, which took an average of 44 hours to address.

For infrastructure issues, Street Repair received 269 requests with an average response time of 3.1 days. Park Maintenance handled 60 calls, averaging 4.2 days for a response. Tree Maintenance saw 473 requests, taking about 47 hours on average.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or immediate referrals rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application. Consistent reporting helps hold the city accountable and keeps Long Beach clean and safe.
