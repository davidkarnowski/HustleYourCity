Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service data for the past 90 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 6,583 total calls. The average response time for these issues is currently 302 hours, or approximately 12.6 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,128, with an average resolution time of 3.7 days. Street Repair requests totaled 818 and showed an average response time of 4.5 days. Tree Maintenance recorded 804 reports with an average response time of 6.1 days.

Faster services included Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 12.7 hours across 431 reports, and Animal Services, which averaged 13.4 hours.

Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
