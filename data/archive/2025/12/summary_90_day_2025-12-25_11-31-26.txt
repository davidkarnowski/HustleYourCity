Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

Over 15,000 service requests were made. Dumped items were the most reported issue with 6,019 requests, followed by graffiti at 3,697 requests.

Response times for some common issues have been lengthy. The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report is currently about 2 weeks. Park maintenance requests are also taking over 2 weeks to close.

Graffiti removal requests are being closed in an average of about 5 and a half days. Reports for dead animal pickups and animal services show very fast closure times, often under 24 hours. However, these quick closures may sometimes be due to administrative processing and not on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 25, 2025, at 3:28 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 25, 2025, at 3:00 AM PST.
