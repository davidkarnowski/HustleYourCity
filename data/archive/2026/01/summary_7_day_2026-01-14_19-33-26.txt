Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,450 requests were submitted through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (578 requests), graffiti (237 requests), and e-scooter issues (218 requests).

The average response time for illegally dumped items was about 2 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in about one day on average. The 33 requests for dead animal pickup were handled in under 2 hours on average. One restroom maintenance request was closed in about 16 minutes; such a fast closure may be due to an administrative update and not a completed repair.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
