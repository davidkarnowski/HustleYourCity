The latest city service data for Long Beach shows a high volume of requests over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent service request with 7,184 reports, followed by 3,363 graffiti reports and 3,342 e-scooter reports.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Dead animal pickup continues to be the fastest service, averaging approximately 5 hours over the last 90 days. Please note that unusually low response times for some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
