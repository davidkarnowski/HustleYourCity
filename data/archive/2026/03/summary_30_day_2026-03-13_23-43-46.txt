Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the city is responding to citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 2,139 calls in the last 30 days. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,062 calls, averaging a response time of nearly 2 days. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 1,039 reports, all of which were referred to other agencies.

Marina repairs had the longest average response time at over 7 days. On the other end, business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep the city running smoothly. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 13, 2026, 4:40 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 13, 2026, 4:00 PM PDT.
