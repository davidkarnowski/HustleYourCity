Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data. In the past month, the most common reports from residents were for Dumped Items (1,935 requests), E-Scooters (1,037), and Graffiti (1,001).

The average city response time for Dumped Items was about 10 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed more quickly, with an average response time of about 2.2 days. Reports for Tree Maintenance and Street Repair were handled in about 1.3 days and 2.4 days on average, respectively.

It is important to note that some response times may appear unusually low. This can be due to administrative closures rather than the completion of physical work. E-Scooter reports are referred directly to vendors and are not handled by city staff.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
