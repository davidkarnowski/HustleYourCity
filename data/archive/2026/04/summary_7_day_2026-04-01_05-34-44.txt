Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data. Over the past 7 days, the City of Long Beach received a high volume of service requests, with clear trends in response times.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 553 reports, taking an average of 3.5 days to process. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 207 calls, averaging a 1-day response time. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 200 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable response times include Street Repair and Tree Maintenance, both averaging about 1 day. Sidewalk Repair requests averaged a 12.6-hour response.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. Running Water reports averaged just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Tracking these numbers helps ensure transparency and accountability for Long Beach.
