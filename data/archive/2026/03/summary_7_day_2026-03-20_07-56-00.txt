Hustle Long Beach! is here with your weekly accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests. The latest data from the last 7 days shows exactly how quickly issues are being addressed across the neighborhoods.

Dumped Items led the service requests with 517 reports this past week. The average response time for these issues is currently about 3.6 days, though 339 of those requests are still marked as "New" and awaiting action. Graffiti removal saw 233 requests, with the city responding in an average of 27 hours. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 121 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

In other areas, Dead Animal Pickup handled 34 calls with an average response time of 3.7 hours. Animal Services responded to 13 calls in an average of 7.9 hours. Street Repair saw 46 requests with an average response time of just over 25 hours. 

Help keep the city accountable and the streets clean. If you see an issue, report it directly using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at March 20, 2026 12:52 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 20, 2026 12:00 AM PDT.
