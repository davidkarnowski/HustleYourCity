Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Over the past month, citizens submitted nearly 6,200 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (2,497 requests), graffiti (1,045 requests), and e-scooter issues (1,043 requests).

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" request was about 10 days. Graffiti removal requests were handled faster, with an average closure time of about 2.5 days. Reports for street repair and street light issues were also closed in about 2.5 days on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? You can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
