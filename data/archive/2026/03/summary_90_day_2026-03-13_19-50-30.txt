Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how long it takes for reported issues to be addressed.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 6,602 calls. The city took an average of 10 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second highest with 3,081 reports, showing an average response time of 3 days. E-scooter complaints totaled 2,926, though nearly all were immediately referred to other agencies.

Response times vary significantly across different departments. Marina repairs took the longest, averaging about 26 days to resolve. Conversely, business license and permit concerns showed a remarkably fast average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times are often the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Other notable response times include street repairs averaging 6 days, park maintenance taking nearly 6 days, and dead animal pickups being handled in about 13 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues. Consistent reporting helps track city performance and ensures neighborhoods get the attention they need.
