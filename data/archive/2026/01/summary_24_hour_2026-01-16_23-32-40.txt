Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests based on public data.

In the past 24 hours, residents submitted 212 service requests. The most common issues reported were for dumped items, with 67 requests, and e-scooters, with 51 requests.

Response times for closed cases varied by issue. Requests for "Light" repairs were addressed the fastest, with an average completion time of about 1.6 hours. "Sidewalk Repair" requests had the longest average response time at nearly 16 hours. Note that some fast response times may reflect administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.
