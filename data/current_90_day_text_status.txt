The latest data for city service requests over the past 90 days shows varied response times across key departments.

Animal Services remains one of the most efficient categories, with an average response time of approximately 10 hours for 227 total requests. Graffiti removal also shows steady progress, averaging about 62 hours across 2,613 requests.

Other areas are seeing longer wait times. Dumped items, which accounted for 7,476 requests in the last 90 days, show an average response time of about 19 days. Tree maintenance requests averaged about 6 days, while sidewalk repairs averaged roughly 4 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
