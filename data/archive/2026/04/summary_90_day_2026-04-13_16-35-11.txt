Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on Long Beach city service response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items led the call volume with 6,915 reports. The city took an average of 8.4 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti removal saw 3,253 reports, with an average response time of 54.5 hours.

Tree Maintenance had 900 reports, averaging 7.6 days for a response, while Street Repair had 711 reports with an average response time of 7.1 days.

Business License and Permit Concerns showed an unusually fast response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that extremely low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
