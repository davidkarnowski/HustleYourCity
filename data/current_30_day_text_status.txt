Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 30 days.

Reports for dumped items and graffiti continue to be the most common service requests. Over the last month, 1,880 reports were made for dumped items, with an average city response time of about 8 days. For the 1,104 graffiti reports, the average response time was just over 2 days.

Some of the quickest responses included reports for dead animal pickup, which were addressed in under 9 hours on average. Animal services calls were handled in under 10 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it using the Go Long Beach service application to help the city track and address problems.
