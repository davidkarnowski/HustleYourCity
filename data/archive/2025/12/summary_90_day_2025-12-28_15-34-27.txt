Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped item pickups, with over 5,900 reports, and graffiti removal, with over 3,600 reports.

Based on the data for closed requests, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 5 days. Street light repairs averaged about 1 week, while dumped item pickups took an average of about 15 days.

Reports for dead animal pickups and animal services were addressed much faster, typically in under 16 hours. It is important to note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures and may not reflect the time for work to be completed.
