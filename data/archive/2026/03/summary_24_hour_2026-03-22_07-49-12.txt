Hustle Long Beach! continues to track city service response times to keep the community informed. Over the past 24 hours, the City of Long Beach received 108 service requests across various categories.

The highest volume of reports came from Dumped Items with 26 requests, all of which are currently marked as new. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 24 reports, all of which were closed and referred to outside vendors. Graffiti reports also saw activity, with 15 total calls, 11 of which are currently in progress.

Only two categories recorded completed response times in the past day, and both were resolved in under an hour. Animal Services requests averaged a response time of 36 minutes, while Dead Animal Pickup averaged 40 minutes. Citizens should note that these unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues and help keep the city accountable.
