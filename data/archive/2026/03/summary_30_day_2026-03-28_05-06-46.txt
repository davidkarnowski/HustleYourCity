Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest 30-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service response times.

Over the past month, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service requests with 2,272 reports. The city took an average of 7 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 989 calls, averaging a 40-hour response time. E-Scooter concerns also remained high with 903 total reports.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which received 249 requests and averaged a 3-day response time. Park Maintenance handled 73 calls with an average response time of nearly 5 days. On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup processed 155 reports in an average of 9 hours.

When reviewing this data, please keep in mind that unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Accountability starts with reporting. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
