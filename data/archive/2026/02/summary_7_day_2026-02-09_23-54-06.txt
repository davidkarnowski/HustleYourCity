This is an independent review of City of Long Beach service requests for the past week.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 657 total calls in the last 7 days. The average response time for these requests was approximately 22 hours. Data shows that 353 of these items are currently listed as in progress.

Graffiti enforcement saw 290 reports with an average response time of roughly 30 hours, or just over one day. The majority of these cases have been closed.

Response times for Street Repair and Light issues averaged 17 hours and 21 hours respectively. Dead Animal Pickup services averaged roughly 7 hours per response.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
