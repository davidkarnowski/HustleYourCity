Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 6,100 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (2,542), graffiti (1,110), and illegally parked e-scooters (952).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 59 hours. Reports of dumped items took about 9 days on average to be resolved, while street repair requests were closed in just over 3 days. Some services show very fast response times, such as restroom maintenance at about 6 hours. These may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
