Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on City of Long Beach service response times over the past 7 days.

In the last week, the city received a significant volume of service requests. The most frequently reported issues were Dumped Items with 460 calls, E-Scooter complaints with 247 calls, and Graffiti with 228 calls.

Average response times varied widely depending on the service. Graffiti issues were addressed in about 33 hours, while Dumped Items took an average of 67 hours to process. Tree Maintenance requests saw an average response time of 16 hours. Running Water reports showed an exceptionally fast average response time of just 13 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
