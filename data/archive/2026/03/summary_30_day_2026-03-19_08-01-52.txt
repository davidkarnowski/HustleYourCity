Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the city is responding to citizen reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,244 reports. The average response time for these issues was 5 days. Graffiti removal followed closely with 1,054 reports and an average response time of 37 hours. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 878 reports, though response times were not tracked for this category.

Other notable service times include Street Repair, which saw 256 requests averaging 3 days for a response. Reports of Illegal Fireworks took the longest to resolve, averaging 9 days for the 10 reports filed.

On the fastest end, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application. Consistent reporting helps hold city services accountable and keeps Long Beach maintained.
