Here's a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 90 days.

Residents submitted thousands of service requests for issues across the city. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,369 calls), graffiti (3,287 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,685 calls).

Average response times for closed requests varied by service type. The city addressed reports of dumped items in about 14 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of just under 5 days. Urgent issues like dead animal pickups were handled much faster, with an average response time of about 12 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, from potholes to park maintenance, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
