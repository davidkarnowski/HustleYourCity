The latest city service data shows significant activity over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,607 reports, followed by 2,580 graffiti reports and 1,036 tree maintenance requests.

Response times vary by service type. Animal services and dead animal pickups are being addressed quickly, with average response times of approximately 11 minutes and 8 hours respectively. Graffiti removal is averaging about 64 hours, while tree maintenance is averaging about 6 days. Dumped item reports are currently averaging about 18 days for resolution.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track service requests throughout the city.
