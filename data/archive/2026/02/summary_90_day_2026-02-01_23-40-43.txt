Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues, with over 6,200 and 3,300 requests filed, respectively. The city received over 2,600 reports about E-scooters, which are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Average response times for closed requests vary by issue type. Graffiti removal requests were closed in about 5 days on average. Reports of dumped items took longer, averaging about 13 and a half days for closure. Animal-related services, such as dead animal pickup, were resolved much faster, typically within 13 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
