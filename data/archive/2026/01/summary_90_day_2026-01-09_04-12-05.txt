Here is the latest look at Long Beach service request data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,800 requests through the city's service system. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (5,893 requests) and graffiti (3,541 requests).

Average city response times for closed cases varied by issue type. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 17 hours, while graffiti removal took an average of 4.5 days. The average time to close a report for dumped items was about two weeks.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
