The latest city service data for Long Beach shows a focus on maintenance and debris removal over the past 30 days. During this period, the city received 2,360 reports for dumped items and 1,049 reports for graffiti. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to see some of the fastest response times, averaging under 8 hours.
