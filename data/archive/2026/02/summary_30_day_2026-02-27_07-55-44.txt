Here is an independent review of City of Long Beach service request data for the last 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2,245 total entries. The average response time for these requests is currently over 6 days. Graffiti reports followed with 1,137 total entries and an average response time of approximately 2 days.

Infrastructure maintenance shows mixed response times. Street Repair requests, which totaled 253, had an average response time of about 4 days.
