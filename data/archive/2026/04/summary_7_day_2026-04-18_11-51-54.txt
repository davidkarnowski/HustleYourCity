Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest third-party accountability update on city service response times. This is an independent review of the Long Beach service call data from the last 7 days to track how the city is handling citizen requests.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 568 reports. The city averaged a response time of 3 days for these issues. E-Scooter complaints were the second most frequent with 270 calls, all of which were referred to other departments. Graffiti reports totaled 198, with an average response time of 25 hours.

Some services showed very fast turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup received 34 calls and averaged a response time of 3.35 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures in the system rather than actual physical work being completed.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
