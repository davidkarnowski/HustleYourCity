Hustle Long Beach! is here with your latest third-party accountability update on city service response times. Over the past 24 hours, residents submitted a steady stream of requests, and the data shows exactly how the City of Long Beach handled them.

Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 93 total reports, averaging a 3.8-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints followed with 49 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti issues generated 42 reports with an average response time of 1.9 hours.

Several categories showed remarkably fast average response times. Tree Maintenance requests averaged 86 minutes. Sidewalk Repair averaged 46 minutes, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 40 minutes, Street Repair averaged 37 minutes, and both Animal Services and Vegetation issues averaged 32 minutes. 

Please note that unusually low response times, particularly those under an hour for major infrastructure issues like street and sidewalk repairs, may be due to administrative closures or initial assessments rather than actual work completions.
