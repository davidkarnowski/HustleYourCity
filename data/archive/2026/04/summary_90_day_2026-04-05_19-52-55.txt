Hustle Long Beach! presents the latest city service accountability update. An independent analysis of the Long Beach service call data from the last 90 days reveals how quickly issues are being resolved across the community.

Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue, with 7,031 calls in the past 90 days. The city took an average of nearly 9 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti removal saw 3,166 reports, with an average response time of about 2.4 days. Meanwhile, street repair requests totaled 791, taking an average of nearly 7 days for a response. 

Some categories show incredibly fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures or automatic routing rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to keep holding the city accountable. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
