The latest service request data for the City of Long Beach highlights response times and call volumes over the past 90 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of activity with 6,581 reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 11 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,100, with crews responding in approximately 3 days on average. Street Repair requests, which include pothole fixes, saw 846 reports with an average response time of 5 days.

Dead Animal Pickup services recorded 431 reports and maintained a faster turnaround time of roughly 14 hours.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report maintenance issues directly to the city.
