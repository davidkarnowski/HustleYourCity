Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service call data.

In the past month, "Dumped Items" was the most reported issue with 2,374 calls. This was followed by 1,030 reports for "E-Scooter" issues and 994 for "Graffiti".

The average city response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 7.5 days. "Graffiti" clean-up took an average of 2.5 days, and "Street Repair" requests were addressed in about 3 days. Faster responses included "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services," both averaging around 12 hours. Some requests may show unusually fast completion times due to administrative closures.
