In the last 90 days Long Beach received 16,359 service requests.

The biggest categories were Dumped Items (6,462 requests) and Graffiti (3,659 requests). The average response time for Dumped Items was about 374 hours (≈ 15.6 days) and for Graffiti 135 hours (≈ 5.6 days). Light repairs averaged 51 hours (≈ 2 days) and Sidewalk Repair 68 hours (≈ 2.8 days). Park Maintenance took 126 hours (≈ 5.2 days) while Tree Maintenance averaged 56 hours (≈ 2.3 days).

Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were resolved quickly, with average response times of 18 hours and 17 hours respectively – likely reflecting fast handling of these urgent issues.

If you notice a problem, report it through the Go Long Beach app so the city can keep response times transparent and improve service.
