Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service request data from the last 30 days.

The most reported issues were Dumped Items (1,953 calls), E-Scooters (1,054 calls), and Graffiti (1,045 calls). The average response time to close a Dumped Items report was about 7.5 days, while Graffiti reports were handled in about 2.3 days.

Faster responses were seen for Dead Animal Pickups, which were resolved in an average of 13 hours. Some requests show unusually fast closures, which may be due to administrative processing and not the completion of physical work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
