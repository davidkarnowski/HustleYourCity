Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past 90 days, residents made more than 14,800 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,816 calls), graffiti (3,543 calls), and e-scooters (2,639 calls).

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. Dumped items took about 14 days to resolve, while street repairs averaged over 9 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in just under 5 days on average.

Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups (about 16 hours) and animal services calls (about 15 hours). Some unusually fast closures, like for damaged drinking fountains (about 2 hours), may be due to administrative processing and not on-site work completion.

To report an issue, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at Jan. 5, 2026, 11:31 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at Jan. 5, 2026, 11:00 AM PST.
