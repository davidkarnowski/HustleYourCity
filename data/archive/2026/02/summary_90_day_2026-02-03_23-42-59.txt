Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times from the past 90 days. Residents submitted over 16,000 service requests, with illegally dumped items, graffiti, and e-scooter issues being the most reported problems.

According to the city's data, the average response time for dumped item pickups was about two weeks. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 5 days on average. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups and animal services calls, both averaging about 12 hours.

Some services show longer wait times. For example, the average completion time for marina repair requests was about 5 weeks.
