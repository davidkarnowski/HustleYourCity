Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service data covering the past 90 days.

Dumped Items remain the most frequent service request with 6,418 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 13 days. Graffiti reports are the second highest volume category with 3,118 total entries, showing an average response time of approximately 4 days.

Infrastructure maintenance shows varied response times. Street Repair requests are averaging nearly 5 days for a response, while Tree Maintenance is taking roughly 1 week.

Faster response times were recorded for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup, which are averaging between 11 and 13 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
