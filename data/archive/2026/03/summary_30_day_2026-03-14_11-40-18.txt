Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service response data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at how the city is handling citizen requests.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,137 reports. The city averaged a response time of 5 days for these issues.

Graffiti removal saw 1,064 requests, with the city responding in an average of 43.6 hours. E-Scooter complaints reached 1,039, though all were referred to outside agencies, resulting in no measurable city response time.

Street Repair handled 264 calls, taking about 3 days on average to respond. Park Maintenance processed 67 requests with an average response time of 4 days.

Business License and Permit Concerns showed a remarkably fast response time of just 3 minutes for 4 requests. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
