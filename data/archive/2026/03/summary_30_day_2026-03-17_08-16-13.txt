Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 30-day service request data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at the current response times and call volumes for major city services.

Dumped items generated the highest volume with 2,168 reports. The city averaged a response time of 5 days for these requests. Graffiti removal followed with 1,057 reports, averaging a 39-hour response time. 

Other notable service categories include street repair, which received 265 requests and averaged a 3-day response time. Tree maintenance saw 491 requests with an average response time of 64 hours. 

Some categories showed extremely fast closures. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help keep the city maintained.
