Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the past 30 days.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped item removal (1,940 reports), e-scooter issues (1,035 reports), and graffiti cleanup (1,002 reports).

The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 10 days. Graffiti cleanup requests were addressed in an average of about 2.2 days. For the 202 street repair requests, the average completion time was 2.4 days. Animal Services requests were closed more quickly, with an average response time of about 8.5 hours.

Note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures and may not reflect on-site work completion.
