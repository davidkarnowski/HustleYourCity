Here is a look at the latest city service response data for the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with over 6,400 service requests. The average response time to close these cases is currently about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests, the second most common report with over 3,200 submissions, are being closed in an average of about 4.5 days.

In contrast, services like dead animal pickup and animal services requests are resolved much faster, with an average closure time of about 12 hours. Some rapid closures may be administrative and not reflect on-site work. For the 2,670 reports about e-scooters, these are typically referred directly to the scooter companies to resolve.
