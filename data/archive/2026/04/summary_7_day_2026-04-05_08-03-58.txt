Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data to keep you informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling public requests. Over the past 7 days, the city received a wide range of service calls, and here is a breakdown of the numbers.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 591 total calls. The average response time for these requests was approximately 2 days. E-Scooter complaints followed with 267 reports, though no response time data was recorded for those. Graffiti removal saw 232 requests, with an average response time of 14.24 hours.

Other notable service areas included Light issues with 69 calls averaging a 21.51-hour response, and Tree Maintenance with 48 calls averaging an 8.4-hour response. Dead Animal Pickup had 39 calls with an average response time of 2.48 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you spot an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. You can report problems directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
