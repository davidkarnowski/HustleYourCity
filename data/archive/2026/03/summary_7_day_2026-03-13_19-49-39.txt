Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data from the past seven days. Dumped Items continue to be the most reported issue, with 557 total requests and an average response time of 71.6 hours. E-Scooter complaints generated 277 reports, all of which were referred to other entities. Graffiti was also a major concern with 231 reports, though the city maintained an average response time of 28 hours, successfully closing 201 of those cases.

Other notable service metrics include Traffic Signal reports, which averaged a 2.5-hour response time across 5 requests. Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services both averaged response times of roughly 8.2 hours. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
