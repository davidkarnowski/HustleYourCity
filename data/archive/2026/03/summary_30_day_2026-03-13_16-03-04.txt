Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how Long Beach is handling citizen reports and neighborhood issues.

The highest volume of requests over the last month came from Dumped Items with 2111 reports. This was followed by Graffiti with 1049 reports and E-Scooter issues with 996 reports. 

Response times vary significantly depending on the type of service. Dumped Items took an average of 5 days to resolve, while Street Repair requests averaged about 3.4 days. Park Maintenance took just over 4 days, and Marina Repair took the longest, averaging 7.5 days. For issues resolved more quickly, Graffiti removal averaged 43 hours and Animal Services averaged about 25 hours.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged just 3 minutes. These extremely low response times are likely due to administrative closures or immediate referrals rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit and track your requests directly with the city.

This data summary was updated at March 13, 2026 at 8:57 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 13, 2026 at 8:00 AM PDT.
