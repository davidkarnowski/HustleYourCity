Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests. Response times for several key services have seen significant improvement compared to previous months.

The average time to resolve graffiti reports has dropped to about 2.7 days. Response times for dumped item pickups now average about 6.3 days, and street repairs are being addressed in about 4 days on average. In the last month, the city received 1,975 reports for dumped items and 1,133 for graffiti, making them the most common requests.

While most services saw faster responses, marina repair requests took the longest to resolve, with an average completion time of nearly 14 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 18, 2025, 11:31 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 18, 2025, 11:00 PM PST.
