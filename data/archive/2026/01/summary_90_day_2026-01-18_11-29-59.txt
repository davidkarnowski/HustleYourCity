Here is the latest analysis of Long Beach city service data, focusing on requests made in the last 90 days.

Over 15,000 service requests were submitted by residents. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (6,071 requests) and graffiti (3,439 requests). The average time for the city to resolve a "Dumped Items" request was about 12 days, while graffiti removal requests averaged just under 5 days.

Requests for marina repairs had the longest average completion time at about 3.5 weeks. In contrast, requests for animal services and dead animal pickups were resolved much faster, averaging about 14 hours and 15.5 hours respectively.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
