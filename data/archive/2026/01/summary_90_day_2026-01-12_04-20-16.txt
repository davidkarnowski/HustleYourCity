Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

The most frequent service call was for illegally dumped items, with 5,918 requests submitted. The average time to resolve these reports was about 13.5 days. The second most common request was for graffiti removal, with 3,499 reports closed in an average of about 4.5 days.

Some of the quickest responses included dead animal pickups, with 420 requests handled in an average of about 15.5 hours. Animal Services calls were also addressed quickly, with 165 requests closed in an average of about 15.2 hours. Unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.
