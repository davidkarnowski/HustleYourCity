Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times for the past week.

In the last 7 days, the most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (308 calls), graffiti (199 calls), and e-scooters (187 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 1.5 days. Reports of dumped items were addressed in just over one day on average. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups (about 9 hours) and animal services (about 3 hours). Some service requests show unusually fast response times, which may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
