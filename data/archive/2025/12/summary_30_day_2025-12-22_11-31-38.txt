Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents submitted 5,042 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (1,910 requests), e-scooters (1,036 requests), and graffiti (1,064 requests).

Average city response times for closed service requests included:
- Dumped Items: about 6 days
- Graffiti: about 2.5 days
- Street Repair: about 3.5 days
- Animal Services: about 10.5 hours
- Dead Animal Pickup: about 12.5 hours
