Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items led the request totals with 6542 reports, taking an average of 10.5 days to resolve. Graffiti followed with 3105 reports, averaging a 3.2-day response time. E-Scooter issues generated 2967 reports, though all were referred to outside entities.

Marina Repair requests took the longest to resolve, averaging 1.5 months to address. Conversely, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times often indicate administrative closures rather than completed field work. Dead Animal Pickup maintained a response time of about 13 hours across 437 reports.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help keep the city accountable.
