Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of service calls with 516 reports. The city averaged a response time of 3 days for these requests.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue, totaling 227 calls. The average response time for graffiti was approximately 16 hours.

Restroom maintenance requests showed an exceptionally fast average response time of 50 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative ticket closures rather than actual physical work being completed.

Other notable response times include street repairs at 16 hours and abandoned shopping cart removals taking nearly 3 days to address.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
