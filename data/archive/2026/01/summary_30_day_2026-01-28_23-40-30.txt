Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days.

Over 6,000 service requests were submitted. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,409), graffiti (987), and e-scooter issues (965).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.5 days. Reports for dumped items, the most frequent issue, took an average of about 8 days to resolve. Street repair requests were addressed in just under 3 days on average. Some faster responses included dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 12 hours. Note that some quick closures can be administrative and may not reflect completed work.
