The latest 90-day service data for Long Beach highlights significant activity in waste management and infrastructure maintenance. Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,392 reports filed. The average response time for these issues is currently 13 days.

Graffiti abatement recorded 3,134 reports with an average response time of 4 days. Street Repair requests, which include pothole fixes, totaled 785 calls with an average response time of 4 to 5 days. Tree Maintenance saw 702 reports, averaging 1 week for a response.

Faster response times were observed in specific health and safety categories. Dead Animal Pickup services handled 417 calls with an average response of 11 hours. Administrative responses regarding Business Licenses were the fastest recorded, averaging 12 minutes.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure city departments are aware of local needs.
