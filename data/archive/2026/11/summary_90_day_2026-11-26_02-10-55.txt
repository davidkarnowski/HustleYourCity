The latest data for city service requests shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,256 requests for dumped items and 2,560 requests for graffiti removal.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 7 hours. In contrast, dumped item removal currently averages about 19 days, while tree maintenance requests take roughly 9 days on average to address.

Please note that some categories show low response times that may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
