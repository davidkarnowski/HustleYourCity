Hustle Long Beach! is here with the latest 90-day government accountability update. Here is an analysis of the newest service call data to show how the City of Long Beach is responding to neighborhood issues.

Dumped items remain the most reported problem across the city. Over the last 90 days, citizens made 6,922 reports for dumped items, and the city averaged an 8.5-day response time. Graffiti was the second most frequent issue with 3,268 reports, which saw an average response time of 2.3 days. E-scooter complaints also drove high call volumes, totaling 2,960 reports.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests saw steady numbers. The city received 895 tree maintenance requests, taking an average of 8 days to respond. There were 709 street repair calls with a 7.5-day average response, and 376 sidewalk repair requests that took about 5.6 days. On the faster side, the city handled 460 dead animal pickups with an average response time of just under 10 hours.

Some categories show incredibly fast turnaround times. For instance, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizen reports make a difference. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
