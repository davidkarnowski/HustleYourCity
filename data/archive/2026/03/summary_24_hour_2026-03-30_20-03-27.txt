Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data for the City of Long Beach. In the past day, Dumped Items led the request volume with 68 reports, the vast majority of which remain in the new status. Graffiti followed with 34 reports, showing an average response time of about 6.7 hours, with nearly half of those cases already closed.

There were some notably fast response times in the past 24 hours. Street Repair logged 6 calls with an average response time of just 83 minutes. Dead Animal Pickup received 9 calls, closing 8 of them with an average response time of 3 hours. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times can sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
