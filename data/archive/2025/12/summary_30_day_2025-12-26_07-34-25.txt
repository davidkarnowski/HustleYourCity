Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past month, the most reported issues were for Dumped Items (1,896 calls), E-Scooters (1,056 calls), and Graffiti (1,012 calls).

The average city response time for a Dumped Items report was about 8 days. Graffiti reports were handled in an average of about 2.5 days. Response times for Tree Maintenance requests averaged around 31 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the best way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 25, 2025, 11:31 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 25, 2025, 11:00 PM PST.
