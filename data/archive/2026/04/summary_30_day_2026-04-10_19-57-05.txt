Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest 30-day accountability report on City of Long Beach service response times.

Over the past month, Dumped Items and Graffiti were the most frequently reported issues. The city received 2,330 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 8 days. Graffiti saw 1,063 reports, taking the city an average of 37.5 hours to address.

Other notable service requests include tree maintenance, which had 257 reports and an average response time of 3.5 days. Street repair requests totaled 202, taking about 3.4 days on average. Faster services included dead animal pickups, which saw 165 reports and averaged a 7-hour response time. Please note that unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
