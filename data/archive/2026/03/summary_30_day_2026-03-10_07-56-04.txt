Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 30 days. Here is a summary of the most frequent citizen reports and the average time it takes for the city to respond.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 2046 reports. The average response time was 113 hours, which translates to about 4.7 days to address these issues.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1086 calls, showing an average response time of 44.6 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 1029 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies or closed without a recorded response time.

Tree Maintenance received 466 reports with an average response time of 47.3 hours. Street Repair saw 270 reports, taking an average of 70.2 hours to process.

Several categories experienced response times extending beyond three days. Trash and Debris reports averaged 4.2 days, and Park Maintenance requests took an average of 4 days to resolve.

On the other end of the spectrum, Business License and Permit Concerns showed an unusually fast average response time of just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

To help improve the community, please continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 10, 2026 12:51 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 10, 2026 12:00 AM PDT.
