In the past week Long Beach residents filed 1,362 service requests across many categories.  

Key highlights:  

- Graffiti reports were resolved in an average of 25 hours, down from about 64 hours in the prior month.  
- Dumped‑item calls saw an average response of 67 hours, a big improvement from the 244‑hour average previously.  
- Tree maintenance requests were closed in roughly 15 hours, compared with 113 hours a month ago.  
- Street repair, sidewalk repair, light outages and traffic‑signal issues all averaged under 1 day (20 h, 13 h, 18 h and 13 h respectively), far faster than earlier periods.  
- Dead animal pickups and animal‑service calls were completed in about 8 hours and 9 hours on average.  

Overall, response times are trending shorter across the board. Some unusually quick closures may reflect administrative closures rather than on‑site work.  

Residents are encouraged to report issues promptly through the Go Long Beach service app to keep the city’s performance improving.
