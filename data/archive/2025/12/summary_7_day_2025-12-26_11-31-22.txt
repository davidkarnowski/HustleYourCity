Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

The most common reports were for dumped items (311 requests), e-scooters (221 requests), and graffiti (188 requests).

Average city response times for closed requests varied. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 7.5 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about one day, while graffiti removal took closer to a day and a half. Traffic signal issues saw an average response time of nearly 3 days.

The average response for park maintenance was about 69 minutes. Unusually low response times like this can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
