Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on the latest public data.

Dumped items (6,020 reports) and graffiti (3,455 reports) were the most frequent issues submitted by residents. The average response time to close a dumped item request was about 13 days, while graffiti removal took about 4.5 days on average. Street repair requests were completed in an average of 6.5 days.

Some of the longest waits were for marina repairs, which averaged about 3.5 weeks. Faster responses included dead animal pickup (about 16 hours) and animal services (about 14 hours).

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
