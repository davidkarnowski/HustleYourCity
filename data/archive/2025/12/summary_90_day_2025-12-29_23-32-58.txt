Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times for the past 90 days based on the latest public data.

In the last three months, residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (5,901 requests), graffiti (3,624 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,489 requests).

The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 5 days. Requests for dumped item pickup took about 15 days on average. Street repair requests were addressed in an average of 10 days, while reports for dead animal pickup were handled in about 16 hours. Some requests, like for damaged drinking fountains, show a very fast average closure of about 2 hours, which may be due to administrative processing rather than a completed repair.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at 3:29 PM PST on December 29, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 3:00 PM PST on December 29, 2025.
