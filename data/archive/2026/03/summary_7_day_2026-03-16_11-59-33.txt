Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Here is a look at the city response times and call volumes over the past 7 days.

Dumped items continue to lead city service requests with 582 reports in the last week. The city took an average of 53 hours to respond to these issues. Graffiti removal saw 215 requests, with an average response time of 16.7 hours. E-scooter complaints totaled 180, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Tree maintenance received 64 requests with an average response time of 9 hours. Street repair issues totaled 40 calls, taking about 13.2 hours to address. Dead animal pickups were handled in an average of 4.4 hours across 35 reports. Traffic signal issues saw responses averaging 2.5 hours for 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completion.
