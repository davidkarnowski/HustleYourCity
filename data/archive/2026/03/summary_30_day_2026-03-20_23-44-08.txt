Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling local reports.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 2,270 requests. The city took an average of 5.8 days to respond. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,014 calls, seeing an average response time of 40 hours. E-scooter complaints followed closely with 964 reports.

For infrastructure and maintenance, street repairs had 258 reports and averaged 3.3 days for a response. Tree maintenance saw 396 reports and took about 63 hours. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 14 hours across 154 reports. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
