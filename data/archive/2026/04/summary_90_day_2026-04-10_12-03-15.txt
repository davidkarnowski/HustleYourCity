Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest independent accountability update on Long Beach city service requests over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 6929 total calls. The city average response time for these requests is about 8.4 days. Graffiti removal saw 3193 requests with an average response time of 55.5 hours. E-scooter complaints reached 2916, though these are almost entirely referred to outside operators.

For infrastructure, street repair had 734 requests taking an average of 6.8 days to address. Tree maintenance saw 901 requests with an average response time of 6 days, and sidewalk repairs averaged 5.2 days across 360 reports. Light issues took an average of 3.2 days for 972 requests.

Some services show very rapid turnaround times. Dead animal pickups averaged 10.8 hours for 459 calls. Business license and permit concerns averaged just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not necessarily actual work completions.

To help keep the city accountable and well-maintained, residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 10, 2026 4:59 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 10, 2026 3:00 AM PDT.
