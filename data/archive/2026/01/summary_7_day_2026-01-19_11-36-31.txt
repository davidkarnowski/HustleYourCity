Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,400 service requests were submitted by residents. The most frequent report was for "Dumped Items" with 560 requests, which had an average response time of about 45 hours. "Graffiti" was the next most common issue with 211 reports and an average response time of about 25 hours.

Some services showed faster response times. The average time to address "Vegetation" reports was about 61 minutes. A single "Restroom Maintenance" request was closed in approximately 16 minutes. Note that very low response times can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work.
