Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times for the last 90 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 6,449 total calls. The average response time for these issues is currently approximately 13 days. Graffiti reports totaled 3,099, with the city averaging a response time of about 4 days.

Street Repair requests show an average completion time of roughly 5 days. Dead Animal Pickup remains one of the faster services, averaging 11 hours per response. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
