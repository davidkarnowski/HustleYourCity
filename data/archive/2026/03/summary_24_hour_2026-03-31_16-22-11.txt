Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service requests. The following insights are based on data from the past 24 hours.

Dumped Items led the service requests with 97 total reports, though 90 of these remain in new status. E-Scooter complaints followed with 47 reports, all of which were immediately closed and referred to outside agencies. Graffiti issues generated 21 reports, showing an average response time of 7 hours.

The data also shows some exceptionally fast closures. Dead Animal Pickup averaged a response time of just 55 minutes across 6 closed cases. Tree Maintenance cases averaged a 36-minute response time. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
