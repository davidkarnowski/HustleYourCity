Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data based on requests made in the last 30 days.

Over 5,400 service requests were submitted by residents. The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (2,010 calls), E-Scooters (988 calls), and Graffiti (902 calls).

The average response time to close a Dumped Items report was about 4 days. Graffiti reports were addressed more quickly, with an average completion time of just over 2 days. Street Repair requests took about 3 days to resolve.

Some services had faster turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services requests were typically resolved in about 13 to 14 hours. Unusually low response times, such as for Damaged Drinking Fountains, may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
