Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,038 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (354 requests), graffiti (192 requests), and illegally parked e-scooters (172 requests).

Average response times for closed service requests varied. Sidewalk repairs were addressed the fastest, with an average time of about 33 minutes. Such a quick response may indicate an administrative closure rather than a physical repair. Reports of dumped items were closed in an average of about 34 hours, while graffiti cleanups took an average of about 2.5 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to notify the city is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
