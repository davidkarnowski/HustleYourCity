Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the city is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items and graffiti were the most frequently reported issues. The city received 2265 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 1 week. Graffiti followed with 991 reports, averaging a 34-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 860 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Response times varied significantly across different services. Dead animal pickups were handled relatively quickly, averaging nearly 9 hours for 155 reports. On the slower end, park maintenance took about 4.5 days to address 72 reports, and illegal fireworks complaints averaged 6 days for 16 reports. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
