Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data from the past 30 days. Here is a look at how city services are responding to citizen requests.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 2,321 calls. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 942 reports, averaging a 37-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also remained high, totaling 846 reports.

Some services saw rapid turnarounds. Dead animal pickups averaged a 6-hour response time, and animal services averaged 9 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. 

Other services required more time. Street repair requests took an average of 3.5 days to address, while park maintenance and illegal fireworks reports averaged nearly 5 days for a response.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues. Consistent reporting helps track city performance and ensures community concerns are documented.
