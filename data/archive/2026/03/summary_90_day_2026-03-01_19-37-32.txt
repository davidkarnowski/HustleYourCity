Here is an evaluation of the City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days. This summary highlights the volume of calls and the average time required to resolve them based on the latest available data.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 6,657 total calls. The average response time to close these requests is currently 12 days.

Graffiti removal services handled 3,125 reports. The data shows an average completion time of roughly 3.5 days.

E-Scooter reports totaled 2,969. These are almost exclusively marked as 'Closed Referred', indicating they are passed to third-party vendors for resolution rather than handled directly by city crews.

Street Repair, which includes pothole maintenance, received 838 reports. The average time to resolve these issues is approximately 5 days.

Tree Maintenance saw 830 reports with an average response time of roughly 6 days.

Dead Animal Pickup remains a faster service category, with 431 reports averaging 12.5 hours for completion.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city to ensure data is captured and addressed.
