The latest data from the Go Long Beach service application shows a high volume of requests over the past 30 days. Residents submitted 2,843 reports for dumped items and 1,094 reports for graffiti. Response times for these categories averaged approximately 6.5 days for dumped items and 39 hours for graffiti.

Dead animal pickup remains the most efficient service category, with 202 of 203 requests closed in the last 30 days, averaging about 4.5 hours per response. Conversely, reports regarding illegal fireworks saw 364 total requests with an average response time of nearly 16 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to continue using the Go Long Beach service application to report issues, as this helps the city track and address community concerns.
