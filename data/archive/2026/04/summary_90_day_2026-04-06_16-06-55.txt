Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data from the past 90 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is responding to local issues.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,973 reports. The city took an average of nearly 9 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti removal saw 3,170 requests, with an average response time of 58 hours.

Street Repair logged 766 calls, taking 7 days on average to process.

Dead Animal Pickup handled 450 reports, averaging a 10-hour response time.

Some categories showed unusually low response times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually fast responses may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help track neighborhood needs.
