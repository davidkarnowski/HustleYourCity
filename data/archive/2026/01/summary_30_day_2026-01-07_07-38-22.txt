Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests based on public data.

Over the past month, response times for several key services have improved. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was 55.5 hours, a significant decrease from the 90-day average of about 4.7 days. Similarly, reports of dumped items were closed in about 8 days on average, down from 14 days over the last 90-day period. Street repair requests were handled in an average of 47.3 hours, a major improvement from the 90-day average of over 8 days.

In the last 30 days, residents submitted over 5,100 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (1,942), e-scooter issues (972), and graffiti removal (913).

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.
