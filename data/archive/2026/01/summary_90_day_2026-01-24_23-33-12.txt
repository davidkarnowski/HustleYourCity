Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

The most common service requests were for dumped items (6,203 reports) and graffiti (3,378 reports). The average response time to close a dumped item request was about 13 days, while graffiti removal averaged about 5 days.

Some of the longest waits were for marina repairs, which averaged nearly 25 days. In contrast, urgent issues like dead animal pickup and animal services calls were typically closed in less than 15 hours. Note that some fast closures can be administrative and may not reflect the time for work completion.

Over 2,600 reports about e-scooters were submitted. These are referred directly to the scooter companies and do not have a city response time.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the best way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
