Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest independent accountability update regarding City of Long Beach service response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items received the highest call volume with 6,912 reports. The city averaged an 8.5-day response time for these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,193 calls, taking an average of 2.3 days to address. E-Scooter complaints totaled 2,905, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable service categories include Street Repair, which had 740 reports and a 7.1-day average response time. Tree Maintenance saw 902 reports with a 4.6-day average response. Sidewalk Repair had 361 reports and took an average of 5.5 days to process.

Business License and Permit Concerns showed an exceptionally fast response time of just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city. Tracking these statistics helps maintain transparency and accountability for public services.

This data summary was updated at April 8, 2026, 4:51 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 8, 2026, 3:01 PM PDT.
