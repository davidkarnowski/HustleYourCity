Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update, focusing on the last 90 days.

The most frequent service request from residents was for "Dumped Items", with over 6,300 reports filed. This was followed by over 3,200 reports for "Graffiti" removal and over 2,600 reports related to "E-Scooters".

Average city response times varied by issue. Reports of dumped items took an average of about two weeks to resolve. Graffiti removal averaged just under five days. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 12 hours on average. The nearly 2,700 e-scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
