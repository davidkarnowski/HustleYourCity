Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data, focusing on requests made in the last 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequent issues reported by residents, with over 5,900 and 3,500 service calls respectively. The average time to close a dumped item report was about 14 days, while graffiti reports were handled in an average of about 4.5 days.

Response times varied across different service types. Urgent requests for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services were typically closed in under 17 hours. Note that some unusually fast closures can be due to administrative processing and not on-site work completion. More complex infrastructure requests took longer, with Marina Repairs averaging about one month to resolve.

To report issues like potholes, graffiti, or dumped items, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
