Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service requests and response times for the past 30 days.

The highest volume of service requests continues to be for Dumped Items, with 2,376 total reports. The average time to resolve these issues is currently nearly 8 days.

Graffiti reports were the second most frequent category, totaling 1,132 requests. The average response time for graffiti removal is approximately 2 days.

Street Repair requests, which include pothole fixes, totaled 234 reports with an average completion time of about 3 and a half days. Tree Maintenance showed a faster turnaround, averaging roughly 1 day across 155 reports.

Dead Animal Pickup services responded to 148 calls with an average wait time of about 8 and a half hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. Consistent reporting helps track necessary maintenance and service levels across the city.
