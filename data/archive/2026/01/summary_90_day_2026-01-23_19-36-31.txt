Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for Dumped Items (6,121 reports) and Graffiti (3,372 reports). The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 13 days, while graffiti cleanup requests took an average of 5 days.

Other key service response times included about 5.7 days for Street Repair and just under 4 days for Tree Maintenance. Animal Services calls were closed in an average of about 13 hours. It is important to note that some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.
