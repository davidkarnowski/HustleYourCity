Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled public requests over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 2229 reports. Because the average response time was 137.6 hours, this translates to about 5.7 days to address these issues.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service. Citizens submitted 1004 reports, which saw an average response time of 38.6 hours.

E-Scooter complaints remained high with 946 reports. Nearly all of these cases were closed and referred to outside operators rather than being handled directly by city staff.

Street Repair received 251 requests, taking an average of 3.1 days to process. Meanwhile, Running Water reports were addressed in about 7.9 hours. Please note that unusually fast response times can sometimes indicate administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

Residents are encouraged to stay active in maintaining the community. You can report local issues directly to the city by downloading and using the Go Long Beach service application.
