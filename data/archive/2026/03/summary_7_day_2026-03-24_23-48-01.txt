Here is the latest city service data summary for Hustle Long Beach! looking at the past 7 days of service requests.

Dumped items led the request board with 511 reports. The city currently has 425 of these marked as new, with an average response time of 58 hours. E-scooter complaints followed with 237 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti remains a top issue with 190 reports, though the city closed 124 of them with an average response time of 26 hours.

Response times varied significantly depending on the issue. Abandoned shopping cart reports took an average of 6 days to address. On the faster side, restroom maintenance issues were resolved in just 35 minutes, and community center requests took 72 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completion.
