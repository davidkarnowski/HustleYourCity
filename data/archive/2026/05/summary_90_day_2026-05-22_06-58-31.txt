The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows significant activity in service requests over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 6,777 reports and an average response time of approximately 9 days. Graffiti reports totaled 3,202 with a much faster average response time of about 51 hours.

Tree maintenance requests saw 765 reports with an average response time of about 6 days. Street repair requests totaled 611, averaging roughly 6 days for a response. Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
