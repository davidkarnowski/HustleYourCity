Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Residents submitted over 1,300 requests through the Go Long Beach app and other channels.

The most common reports were for dumped items (620 requests), graffiti (287 requests), and e-scooter issues (199 requests).

According to city data, the average time to resolve graffiti reports was about 33 hours. For dumped items, the average was about 29 hours. Reports for street light issues took an average of nearly 43 hours to be resolved.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it is the first step to getting it fixed. Use the Go Long Beach app to submit a service request.
