Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past month, residents submitted over 5,100 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (1,910 calls), e-scooters (970 calls), and graffiti (963 calls).

Based on data for closed cases, the average city response time for dumped items was about 8.6 days. Graffiti removal requests were handled in about 2.3 days, while tree maintenance was addressed in about 1.2 days. Some requests, like those for e-scooters, are referred to outside companies and do not have a city response time.
