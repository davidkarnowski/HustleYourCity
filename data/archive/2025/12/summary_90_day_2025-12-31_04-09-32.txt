Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on the latest public data.

The most common service request was for "Dumped Items," with 5,894 reports. The average time to resolve these cases was about 15 days. The second most frequent request was for "Graffiti," with 3,608 reports, which were addressed in about 5 days on average.

Other key service times include street repairs taking an average of 10 days, tree maintenance in about 6.5 days, and sidewalk repairs in about 4 days.
