Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports.

Dumped items generated the highest volume with 603 reports. The city averaged 3.8 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal followed with 285 reports, showing an average response time of 47 hours.

E-scooter complaints reached 282, though nearly all were administratively referred to outside vendors. Traffic signal issues took the longest to address, averaging 5.6 days for 13 reports.

On the faster side, dead animal pickups saw 35 reports and averaged a 6-hour response time. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
