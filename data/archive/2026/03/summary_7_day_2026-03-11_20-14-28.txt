Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update evaluating City of Long Beach service response times over the past 7 days.

During the last week, the highest volume of citizen requests involved Dumped Items with 496 reports. This was followed by 244 reports for E-Scooter issues and 218 reports for Graffiti.

Response times varied significantly depending on the service category. The average response time for Dumped Items was 85.4 hours, which translates to approximately 3.5 days. Graffiti reports were handled in an average of 33.4 hours, while Street Repair requests averaged 23.8 hours.

Some categories recorded much faster processing. Traffic Signal issues averaged 3.6 hours, and Animal Services averaged 7.8 hours. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual physical work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 11, 2026, 1:11 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 11, 2026, 12:00 PM PDT.
