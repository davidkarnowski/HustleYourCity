Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted over 1,200 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (476 calls), e-scooters (257 calls), and graffiti (174 calls).

For requests that were closed, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 36 hours. Reports of illegally dumped items were addressed in about 2 days, while street repairs took an average of about 24 hours. Longer response times were noted for streetlight issues, averaging over 3 days, and illegal firework reports, which took an average of about 4 days to close.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
