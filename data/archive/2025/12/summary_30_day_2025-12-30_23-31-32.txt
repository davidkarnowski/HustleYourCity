Here's the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 30 days. Residents submitted over 5,200 requests for service.

The most reported issue was illegally dumped items, with 2,066 reports. The average time to close these requests was about 10 days. Graffiti was another common issue with 1,020 reports, which were resolved in an average of about 2.3 days.

The city responded fastest to animal-related calls. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 12 hours on average, and other animal service calls were addressed in under 10 hours. Note that some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
