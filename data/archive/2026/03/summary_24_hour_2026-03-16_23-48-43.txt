Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data to keep citizens informed on city service response times.

Over the past day, Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 95 total reports, the vast majority of which are newly submitted. Graffiti reports were the second most common with 36 calls, showing an average response time of about 7.6 hours.

There were some exceptionally fast turnaround times recorded. Running Water reports averaged a response time of just 5 minutes, and Vegetation issues averaged 13 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like these may be due to administrative closures or referrals rather than actual work completions.

Other notable metrics include Tree Maintenance, which received 11 reports and averaged a response time of just over 2 hours. Dead Animal Pickup received 5 reports, all of which were closed with an average response time of 2.4 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help improve neighborhood conditions.

This data summary was updated at March 16, 2026, 4:46 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 16, 2026, 4:00 PM PDT.
