Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Residents submitted over 1,400 requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (656 requests), graffiti (283 requests), and e-scooters (186 requests).

Average response times for closed requests varied by issue. Reports for dumped items were addressed in about 19 hours, while graffiti clean-up took roughly 26 hours. Some services were faster: abandoned shopping cart requests were closed in an average of 39 minutes, though some unusually low times can be due to administrative closures. Street and tree maintenance requests were handled in about 8 to 9 hours on average.
