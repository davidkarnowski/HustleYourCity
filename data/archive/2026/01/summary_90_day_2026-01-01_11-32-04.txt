Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days.

Over 14,000 service requests were submitted by residents during this period. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (5,893 requests), graffiti (3,606 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,486 requests).

Average response times for closing a request varied by service type. The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 15 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 5 days, while park maintenance requests took an average of over 17 days to close. Reports for dead animal pickups were typically completed in about 17 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to notify the city.
