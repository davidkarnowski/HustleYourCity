Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the past 24 hours.

In the last day, residents submitted 211 service requests. "Dumped Items" was the most reported issue with 93 new requests, which had an average response time of about 4 hours. This was followed by 48 reports for "E-Scooter" issues.
