Hustle Long Beach! is here with your weekly government accountability update. Here is a look at the City of Long Beach service call data over the past 7 days to keep you informed on how local issues are being handled.

The city received over 1,500 service requests this past week. The most reported issues were Dumped Items with 540 requests, Graffiti with 340 requests, and E-Scooter complaints with 288 requests. Out of the 540 Dumped Items reports, 225 are brand new and 173 are currently in progress. 

When it comes to response times, the city averaged 58.5 hours to respond to Dumped Items and 20 hours for Graffiti
