City service call data for the past 30 days shows a noticeable improvement in response times.

Graffiti had 1,389 reports with an average response time of 39.3 hours, down from 140.7 hours in the prior 90‑day period. Dumped Items received 1,976 reports and were addressed in an average 168.9 hours, compared with 358.2 hours previously. Tree Maintenance requests (261) now average 11.5 hours, a sharp decline from 59.7 hours. Light repairs (232) average 27.3 hours, improving from 56.2 hours. Overall, most categories have high closure rates; for example, Animal Services closed 56 of 57 requests with an average of 25.0 hours.

Residents are encouraged to report issues promptly using the Go Long Beach service app to keep these response times moving in the right direction.
