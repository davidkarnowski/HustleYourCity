Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past seven days. Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 525 total calls. However, 401 of those requests remain in the new status, and the average response time for dumped items currently sits at 3.7 days.

Graffiti removal saw 191 reports with an average response time of just over 25 hours. Abandoned shopping cart reports took an average of 4.3 days to address. On the faster side, dead animal pickups averaged 3.7 hours across 45 reports. Restroom maintenance showed an unusually low response time of 35 minutes, though extremely fast turnarounds like this may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

Residents are encouraged to keep reporting local issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit requests and help keep the city running smoothly.
