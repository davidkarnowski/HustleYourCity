Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service requests and response times over the last 90 days.

The most frequently reported issue remains Dumped Items, with 6,390 total reports filed. The average response time for the city to resolve these requests is currently 13 and a half days.

Graffiti removal received the second-highest volume of service calls with 3,141 reports. The average time to close these cases is roughly 4 days.

Street Repair requests totaled 792, with crews responding in roughly 5 days on average. In contrast, Dead Animal Pickup services handled 405 calls with a significantly faster average response time of just under 12 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
