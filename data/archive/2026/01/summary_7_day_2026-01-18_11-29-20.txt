Here is a look at the latest city service response data for Long Beach.

In the last week, residents made 1,455 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (572 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (266 requests), and graffiti (216 requests).

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 25 hours. Dumped item pickups averaged around 46 hours. Some services saw faster responses, with vegetation maintenance requests being closed in an average of 61 minutes. A single restroom maintenance request was closed in about 16 minutes, which may be due to an administrative update and not a completed repair.
