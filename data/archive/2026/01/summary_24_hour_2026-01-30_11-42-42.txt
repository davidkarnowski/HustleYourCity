Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 24 hours.

Residents submitted 189 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (75 calls), E-Scooters (39 calls), and Graffiti (38 calls).

Several categories saw unusually fast average response times for closed cases. Street Repair requests were closed in about 13 minutes and Sidewalk Repair in about 19 minutes. These quick closures may be administrative and not reflect on-site work completion. The longest average response time for closed cases was for Trash and Debris, at nearly 9 hours.
