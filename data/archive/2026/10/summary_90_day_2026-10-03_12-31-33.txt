The latest data on city service requests in Long Beach shows a range of response times over the last 90 days.

Dead Animal Pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 6.5 hours. Animal Services also shows efficient processing, averaging about 12 hours.

Graffiti removal requests have averaged about 3 days, while Tree Maintenance and Sidewalk Repair requests have taken roughly 3.5 days and 4.5 days respectively. Dumped items continue to be a high-volume category with an average response time of over 18 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
