Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 30 days.

Thousands of service requests were submitted by residents. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (1,942 calls), e-scooters (1,076 calls), and graffiti (1,049 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.3 days. Reports for dumped items took longer, with an average closure time of about 5.7 days. Street repair requests were addressed in just under 3 days on average. Some services saw faster responses, with dead animal pickups handled in about 13 hours.
