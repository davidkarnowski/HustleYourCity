Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at how the city handled citizen reports over the last 7 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 512 total calls. The city's average response time for these reports was about 3 days. Graffiti followed with 229 reports and an average response time of 18 hours.

Other notable categories include 189 E-Scooter complaints, all of which were referred to outside agencies, and 68 street repair requests averaging an 18-hour response time. Dead animal pickups were handled efficiently, with 40 requests averaging a 6-hour response time.

Some categories showed incredibly fast turnarounds. For example, restroom maintenance requests averaged a response time of just 50 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
