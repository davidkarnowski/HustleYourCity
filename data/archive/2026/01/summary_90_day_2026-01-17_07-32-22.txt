Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the last 90 days based on public data.

The most frequent issue reported was "Dumped Items," with over 6,000 requests. The average time to close these reports was about 13 days. "Graffiti" was the next most common, with over 3,400 reports and an average response time of about 4.5 days.

Some of the quickest responses were for "Animal Services" and "Dead Animal Pickup," which were typically resolved in under 17 hours. In contrast, "Marina Repair" requests had the longest average completion time at approximately 3.5 weeks.
