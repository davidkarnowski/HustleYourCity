Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a summary of city response times and call totals over the past 7 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of service requests with 539 reports. The city averaged a response time of 3.5 days to address these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported concern with 213 calls, taking an average of 25.5 hours to process. E-scooter complaints totaled 195, all of which were referred to outside agencies and closed.

Other notable service categories included 57 tree maintenance requests averaging a 25-hour response, and 55 street repair calls averaging 28 hours. Dead animal pickups totaled 40 calls and were handled in an average of 4.3 hours. Sidewalk repairs also saw 40 requests, with an average response time of 14 hours.

Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help maintain neighborhood safety and cleanliness.
