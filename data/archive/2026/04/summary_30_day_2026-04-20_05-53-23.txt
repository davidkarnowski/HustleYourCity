Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest 30-day service call data summary evaluating City of Long Beach response times.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 2,290 reports. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,061 calls, averaging a 52-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints remained high with 1,048 reports, though nearly all were immediately referred to outside operators.

Other notable service areas include street lights, which saw 237 requests and averaged a 4-day response time. Street and sidewalk repairs recorded 207 and 169 requests respectively, both averaging around 58 to 63 hours for a response. Dead animal pickups were handled faster, averaging 7 hours across 165 reports.

Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. 

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help track city services.

This data summary was updated at April 19, 2026, at 10:49 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 19, 2026, at 7:00 PM PDT.
