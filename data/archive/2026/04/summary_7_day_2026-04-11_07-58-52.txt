Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times based on data from the past 7 days.

Over the last week, Dumped Items led the service requests with 531 reports. The average response time for these was about 67 hours, or nearly 3 days. Graffiti followed with 346 reports and an average response time of 26 hours, or just over 1 day. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 275 reports, almost all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable areas include Animal Services, which handled 24 calls with an average response time of 8.5 hours, and Dead Animal Pickup, which saw 43 calls averaging 15.6 hours. Please note that unusually low response times across any category may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
