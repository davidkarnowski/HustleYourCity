Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted more than 15,600 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most frequent reports were for dumped items, graffiti, and e-scooters.

The average time to resolve a dumped items report was about 2 weeks. For graffiti removal, the average response time was just under 6 days. Some of the fastest responses were for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests, which were typically handled in less than a day. E-scooter complaints are referred directly to the scooter companies, so the city does not track a response time for those issues.

If you see a non-emergency issue in your neighborhood, you can report it using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 23, 2025, at 3:30 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 23, 2025, at 3:00 AM PST.
