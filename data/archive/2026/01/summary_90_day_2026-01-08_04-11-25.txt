Here is the latest analysis of Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Over the last 90 days, residents submitted more than 15,800 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (5,919 requests), Graffiti (3,536 requests), and E-Scooter issues (2,662 requests).

Response times varied by service type. The average time to close a request for Dumped Items was about 14 days, while Graffiti removal took about 5 days. Faster responses were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services, with both averaging under 17 hours. Some unusually fast response times, like the 2-hour average for Damaged Drinking Fountains, may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
