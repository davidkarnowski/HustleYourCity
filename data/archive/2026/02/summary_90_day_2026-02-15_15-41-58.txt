Hustle Long Beach community update on city service response times for the past 90 days.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,476 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 13 and a half days.

Graffiti removal received 3,187 reports. The city is averaging a response time of just over 4 days for these issues.

Infrastructure requests show mixed results. Street Repair reports totaled 780 with an average completion time of 5 days. Light repair requests totaled 978 and are taking roughly 4 days to resolve.

Tree Maintenance requests are taking just over one week to address on average. Conversely, Dead Animal Pickup remains responsive with an average completion time of about 11 hours.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city has accurate data on neighborhood needs.
