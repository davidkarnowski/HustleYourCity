Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest city service response times over the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 6,482 requests in the past 90 days. The city takes an average of 10.8 days to respond to these. Graffiti is the second most common issue, with 3,101 reports taking an average of 3.3 days to address.

On the faster side, dead animal pickups, which totaled 432 reports, are handled in about 13.3 hours. Business license and permit concerns show a response time of just 12 minutes, though unusually low times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Meanwhile, street repairs saw 839 reports and are currently taking about 5.4 days to resolve.

Help keep the city accountable and well-maintained. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
