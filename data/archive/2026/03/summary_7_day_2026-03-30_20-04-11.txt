Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at the current service request volumes and response times.

Dumped Items received the highest number of reports with 531 requests. The average response time for these issues was 4.2 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 237 calls and an average response time of 19.4 hours. E-Scooter complaints totaled 174, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. Restroom Maintenance requests averaged a 50-minute response time, and Shoes on a Utility Wire averaged 44 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Other notable categories include Street Repair with 66 reports averaging a 1-day response time, and Dead Animal Pickup with 39 reports averaging 5.5 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help keep the community clean and safe.

This data summary was updated at March 30, 2026, 1:00 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 30, 2026, 11:00 AM PDT.
