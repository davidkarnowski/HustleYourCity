The latest data for city service requests over the past 7 days shows a high volume of activity across several categories. Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue with 494 total calls, followed by 272 reports for E-Scooters and 181 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Dead animal pickups continue to be handled quickly, averaging about 5 hours. Some categories, such as street repair and vegetation, show very low average response times, which may indicate administrative closures rather than completed work.
