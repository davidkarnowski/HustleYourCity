Here is the latest Long Beach service data update, focusing on requests from the last 90 days.

Residents submitted over 16,000 service requests during this period. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,282 requests), graffiti (3,299 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,669 requests).

Average city response times for closed requests included approximately 13.5 days for dumped items and about 5 days for both graffiti removal and street repairs. Reports for dead animal pickup and other animal services were addressed in about 13 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to notify the city.

This data summary was updated at February 1, 2026 at 11:56 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at February 1, 2026 at 11:00 PM PST.
