Here is the latest city service data update for the Hustle Long Beach! community. This review looks at the service requests submitted to the City of Long Beach over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of calls with 2,332 reports. The city closed 1,426 of these cases, with an average response time of 8.5 days.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue, totaling 1,103 requests. The city averaged a 40-hour response time for these reports.

Other notable service categories over the last month include E-Scooter complaints with 1,003 reports, Street Repair with 212 reports averaging a 65-hour response, and Tree Maintenance with 250 reports averaging a 63-hour response.

Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services saw faster turnaround times, averaging 7 hours and 8.5 hours respectively. Conversely, Park Maintenance requests averaged 3.7 days, and Light issues averaged 3.4 days.

Please note that unusually fast response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
