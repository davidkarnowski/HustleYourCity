Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. This independent summary provides a look at how long it takes for the City of Long Beach to respond to citizen reports.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 2355 requests in the last month. The average response time for these reports is currently about 8 days. Graffiti removal saw 1007 reports, with crews responding in approximately 35 hours.

Citizens also reported 904 e-scooter issues. All of these were immediately referred to outside agencies, resulting in administrative closures rather than direct city work completions. Street repair requests totaled 208, taking 3 days to address on average. Dead animal pickups were handled quickly, averaging just under 4 hours for the 168 reports filed.

Staying engaged helps keep the city accountable and local neighborhoods clean. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 8, 2026 1:18 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 7, 2026 11:00 PM PDT.
