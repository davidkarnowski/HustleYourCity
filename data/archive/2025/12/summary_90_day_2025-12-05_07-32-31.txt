In the last 90 days Long Beach recorded 6,156 dumped‑item reports and 3,726 graffiti complaints.  

The average city response time for dumped items was about 384 hours (≈ 16 days). Graffiti took 166 hours (≈ 7 days) on average.  

Sidewalk repairs (320 reports) were addressed in 120 hours (≈ 5 days), while street‑light issues (631 reports) took 176 hours (≈ 7 days). Tree maintenance (744 reports) averaged 156 hours (≈ 6½ days).  

Animal‑related services were much faster: animal services (215 reports) and dead‑animal pickups (431 reports) were closed in roughly 17 hours (≈ 0.7 day).  

Traffic‑signal problems (101 reports) required 213 hours (≈ 9 days), and vegetation work (108 reports) was completed in 45 hours (≈ 2 days).  

Overall, the city handled 28,000 service calls across all categories in the past three months.
