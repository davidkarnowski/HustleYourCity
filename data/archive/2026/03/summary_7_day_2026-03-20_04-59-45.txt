Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 514 reports. The average response time is 3.6 days, with 336 of these requests still marked as new.

Graffiti removal saw 228 reports. The city averaged a 26.7-hour response time, and 174 of these cases are already closed.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 121. All of these reports were referred and closed.

Tree Maintenance had 67 reports with an average response time of 17.8 hours. Street Repair followed with 44 reports and a 25.5-hour average response time.

Dead Animal Pickup had 33 reports, all of which are closed, with an average response time of 3.7 hours. Please note that unusually fast response times might be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completion.
