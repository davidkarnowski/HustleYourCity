Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at what citizens are reporting and how quickly the City of Long Beach is responding.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue with 525 calls in the last week. The average response time for these reports is 3.6 days. Graffiti is the second most common issue with 209 reports and an average response time of 23.3 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 195 reports.

Street repair requests generated 67 calls and are taking an average of 23.9 hours to process. Dead animal pickups had 48 calls and are being handled in about 4.7 hours. Abandoned shopping cart reports are taking 3.2 days on average to address.

Some services show very fast turnaround times. For example, restroom maintenance averaged just 35 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets tracked. Please use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
