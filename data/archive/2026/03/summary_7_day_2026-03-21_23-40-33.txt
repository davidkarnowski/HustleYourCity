Here is the latest city service response data for the past 7 days from Hustle Long Beach!. This independent summary tracks how quickly the City of Long Beach is handling citizen requests.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of calls with 525 reports, taking an average of 40 hours to resolve. Graffiti removal followed with 222 reports and an average response time of 20.5 hours. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 148 reports.

Some services showed very fast turnarounds. Dead animal pickups averaged 3.6 hours, and animal services averaged 7 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. On the slower end, marina repairs took an average of 3.8 days to address.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
