Here's the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days.

Illegal dumping, e-scooters, and graffiti continue to be the most reported issues, with the city receiving over 3,700 requests combined for these categories.

According to the city's data, the average time to resolve a 'Trash & Debris' report was about 10 days. Reports for illegally dumped items and broken traffic signals took about one week on average. Faster responses were recorded for graffiti removal (about 2.3 days) and street repairs (about 2.7 days). Dead animal pickups were typically handled in under 14 hours.

A small number of reports, like for damaged drinking fountains, show closure times of about 2 hours. These quick resolutions may reflect administrative updates rather than completed physical repairs.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
