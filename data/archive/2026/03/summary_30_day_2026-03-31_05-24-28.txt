Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the numbers and response times over the last 30 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2,240 requests in the past month. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these calls.

Graffiti removal saw 964 reports, with an average response time of 38 hours. E-scooter complaints were also high at 911 reports, though these are primarily referred to outside vendors and do not have tracked response times.

Some services show quick turnaround times. Dead animal pickups averaged 8 hours for 161 requests, and vegetation issues averaged 11 hours for 79 requests. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes reflect administrative ticket closures rather than the actual completion of physical work.

Other notable response times include street repairs averaging 69 hours for 238 requests, and park maintenance taking about 4.6 days for 84 requests.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 30, 2026, 10:20 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 30, 2026, 7:00 PM PDT.
