Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data to keep the community informed on city response times and request volumes.

Over the past day, Dumped Items saw the highest call volume with 106 reports, though 96 of these currently remain in the New status. The average response time for Dumped Items was 6.08 hours. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 26 calls and an average response time of 1.68 hours.

The data shows unusually fast response times across several other categories. Street Repair requests averaged a response time of 18 minutes. Park Maintenance averaged 22 minutes, Tree Maintenance averaged 32 minutes, Vegetation averaged 44 minutes, and Dead Animal Pickup averaged 53 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times below 90 minutes may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues and track city responses.
