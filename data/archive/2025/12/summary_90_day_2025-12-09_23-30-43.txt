In the last 90 days Long Beach residents reported 6,201 dumped‑item cases and 3,702 graffiti incidents. The city’s average response time was about 15.6 days for dumped items and 6.6 days for graffiti. Park maintenance requests (177 total) took the longest, averaging 18.5 days, while animal‑service calls (199 total) and dead‑animal pickups (428 total) were handled much faster, with average responses of roughly 17 hours and 16.7 hours respectively.

Sidewalk repairs (316 cases) and street repairs (658 cases) saw average response times of 5.3 days and 9.8 days. Light‑related issues (636 cases) were addressed in about 7.4 days on average. E‑Scooter reports (2,172 cases) are automatically marked “Closed Referred,” so no response time is recorded.

Overall, the data show quicker turn‑around for safety‑critical services (animal and dead‑animal) while larger clean‑up and maintenance jobs still take several days to weeks. Residents can help the city track and prioritize issues by reporting them through the Go Long Beach service app.
