Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service response data to keep you informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling community requests. Here is a look at the numbers from the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 580 calls, taking an average of 54 hours for a response. E-Scooter complaints followed with 263 reports. Graffiti removal saw 226 calls, averaging a 21-hour response time.

Some services saw faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup handled 39 calls with an average response time of 2.5 hours. On the other end of the spectrum, the single report for an Illegal Firework took 5 days to process. Please note that unusually low response times across any category may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
