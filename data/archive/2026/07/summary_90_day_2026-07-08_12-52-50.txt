The latest data for city services in Long Beach shows trends across several categories over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent service request with 6,730 reports. The average response time for these requests is approximately 8 days. Graffiti removal follows with 3,346 reports and an average response time of about 3 days.
