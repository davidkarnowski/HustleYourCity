Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days. The data shows residents made over 15,000 service requests, with illegally dumped items (6,283 calls) and graffiti (3,343 calls) being the most reported issues.

Average city response times varied by service type. The average time to close a graffiti report was about 5 days. For illegally dumped items, the average was about 13 days. Street light and street repair requests were typically resolved in about 4.5 to 5.5 days.

More urgent requests, such as dead animal pickups and other animal services calls, were handled much faster, with average response times of about 13 to 14 hours. Longer-term repairs like marina maintenance and damaged drinking fountains took an average of 4 to 5 weeks to complete.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
