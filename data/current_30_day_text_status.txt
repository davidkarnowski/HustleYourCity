The latest city service data for Long Beach shows a high volume of requests over the past 30 days. Residents submitted 2,330 reports for dumped items and 837 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Graffiti reports are being addressed in approximately 47 hours. Tree maintenance requests are averaging about 9 days to resolve, while sidewalk repairs are taking roughly 7 days on average. Dead animal pickups remain the fastest service, with an average response time of about 8 hours.

Please note that some categories show very low response times, which may indicate administrative closures rather than completed field work.
