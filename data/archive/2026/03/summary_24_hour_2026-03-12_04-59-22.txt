Here is the latest 24-hour city service data review from Hustle Long Beach!

Over the past day, the city logged 181 total service requests. Dumped Items led the reports with 97 calls, showing an average response time of 16.19 hours. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 33 calls and an average response time of 2.36 hours.

Some categories showed unusually fast response times. Park Maintenance requests averaged just 10 minutes, Tree Maintenance averaged 36 minutes, and Animal Services averaged 62 minutes. Citizens should note that these extremely low response times may be due to administrative closures in the system rather than actual physical work being completed that quickly.
