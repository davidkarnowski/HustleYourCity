Graffiti reports have dropped to 3,698 in the last 90 days with an average response of 152 hours (about 6 days), down from the all‑time average of 351 hours.  

Tree maintenance, street repair, sidewalk repair and light issues all show rapid turnaround – average responses are now between 42 hours (≈2 days) and 100 hours (≈4 days), a big improvement from historic averages that ranged from 1,000 to 4,700 hours.  

Animal services and dead‑animal pickups are being closed in under 18 hours on average, compared with historic averages of over 3,900 hours.  

Dumped items remain the longest‑standing challenge, averaging 363 hours (≈15 days) to close, slightly slower than the historic 260‑hour average.
