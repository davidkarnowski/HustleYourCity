Hustle Long Beach community update on city service response times for the last 90 days.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,664 total reports. The average time to close these requests is currently over 12 days.

Graffiti removal received 3,118 reports with an average completion time of roughly 3 and a half days. Street Repair requests totaled 828 calls with a response average of nearly 5 days.

The fastest recorded response category was Report Business Licenses/Permit Concerns, averaging 12 minutes. Note that unusually low response times may indicate administrative closures rather than physical work completion.

Residents are encouraged to report issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure data is tracked.
