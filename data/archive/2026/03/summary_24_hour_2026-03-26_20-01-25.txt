Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour city service data to keep citizens informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest call volume with 78 reports. However, the vast majority of these remain in the new queue, meaning no average response time has been established yet. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 31 calls. The city managed to close 18 of those graffiti cases, averaging a response time of about 8 hours.

When looking at the extremes for response times over the last 24 hours, Community Center issues took the longest to resolve, averaging just over 17 hours. On the more efficient side, Dead Animal Pickup received 7 calls and all 7 were closed with an average response time of 7 hours. Street Repair and Sidewalk Repair both averaged around 9 hours. Please note that unusually fast response times can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

If you spot an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
