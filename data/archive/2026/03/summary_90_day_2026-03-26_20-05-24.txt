Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data to keep citizens informed about city response times. Here is a look at how long it takes for the City of Long Beach to address community issues.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 6,875 requests in the last 90 days. The city took an average of 9.5 days to respond to these reports. Graffiti removal followed with 3,108 requests, averaging a 2.7-day response time.

Street repair requests totaled 869, taking an average of 6.5 days to address. Tree maintenance saw 950 requests with an average response time of nearly 6 days.

Some categories showed unusually fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and request city services. Consistent reporting helps track city performance and improves local neighborhoods.

This data summary was updated at March 26, 2026, 12:58 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 26, 2026, 12:00 PM PDT.
