Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 30 days. This data is tracked to keep the public informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped Items received the highest number of service requests with 2370 calls. The average response time for these reports was 8.5 days. E-Scooter complaints were the second most frequent with 1096 reports, followed closely by Graffiti with 1047 reports and an average response time of 54 hours.

Some services saw much faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup averaged a 6.6-hour response time across 174 calls, and Animal Services averaged 8.3 hours for 76 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Other notable service times include Street Light issues, which took an average of 4.2 days to resolve across 237 reports, and Park Maintenance, which averaged 3.2 days for 78 reports.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, please use the Go Long Beach service application to report it directly to the city. Public reports help hold the city accountable and keep Long Beach streets clean and safe.
