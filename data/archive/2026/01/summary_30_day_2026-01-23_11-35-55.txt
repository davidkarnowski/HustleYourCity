Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents submitted thousands of service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (2,187 requests), followed by e-scooter issues (913) and graffiti removal (907).

The average response time to close a report for dumped items was about 7.7 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 71 hours on average, while street repairs took about 68 hours. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which were handled in an average of about 12 hours. Some categories show very quick closure times, which can sometimes be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.
