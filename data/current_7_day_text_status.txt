Over the past week the city processed 427 dumped‑item reports with an average response time of about 34 hours. Graffiti cases (283) were closed in roughly 27 hours on average.

Tree maintenance (55 requests) and vegetation work (6 requests) were among the fastest, averaging 9 hours and 7.5 hours respectively. Restroom maintenance had the quickest turnaround at just 1.2 hours (3 requests). Other services – light (44), street repair (30), trash & debris (14) and sidewalk repair (23) – all averaged under 24 hours, with side‑walk repair at 13.7 hours.

The longest average response this week remained dumped items at 34 hours, while most categories were resolved within a day.
