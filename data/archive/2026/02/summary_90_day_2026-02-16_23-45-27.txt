Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service requests based on data from the last 90 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 6,389 total reports. The average time to resolve these issues is currently approximately 13 and a half days.

Graffiti removal requests totaled 3,158. The average response time for these reports is roughly 4 days. Street Repair issues, which include potholes, saw 784 reports with an average response time of nearly 5 days.

Dead Animal Pickup services are averaging 11 hours per response across 410 total reports. E-Scooter reports remained high with 2,836 entries, the majority of which were referred to external operators.

Please note that unusually low response times in certain categories may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues to the city.
