Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were dumped items (6,174 requests) and graffiti (3,333 requests).

Response times have improved for some services. The average time to close a sidewalk repair request was just under 3 days. Animal services and dead animal pickup requests were resolved in about 13 to 14 hours on average.

Other services saw longer response times. The average time to resolve a report for dumped items was about 13 days. Marina repair requests took an average of about 3.5 weeks to close.

Reporting issues helps the city track problems. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit a request.
