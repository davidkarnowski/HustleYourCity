Here is a look at the latest Long Beach city service response data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (5,961 requests) and graffiti (3,548 requests).

On average, the city took about 13.5 days to resolve reports of dumped items. Graffiti removal requests were completed in about 4.5 days. Response times for marina repairs were longer, averaging about one month.

Faster responses were recorded for issues like dead animal pickups (average 15.6 hours) and animal services (average 16.3 hours). Some categories show unusually fast response times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
