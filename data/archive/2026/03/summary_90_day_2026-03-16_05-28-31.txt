Here is the latest 90-day data summary of City of Long Beach service requests, analyzed for the Hustle Long Beach! community.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to generate the highest volume of service calls. Over the past 90 days, citizens reported 6,630 dumped items. The city took an average of 10 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was reported 3,063 times, with an average response time of 3 days.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant activity. The city received 901 tree maintenance requests and 830 street repair requests. Both categories averaged a response time of about 6 days.

Some service categories show exceptionally fast response times. For instance, reports regarding business licenses and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times are often due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and request city services.

This data summary was updated at March 15, 2026 at 10:24 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 15, 2026 at 8:00 PM PDT.
