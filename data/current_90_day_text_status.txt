Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted thousands of requests through the Go Long Beach app. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,155 requests), graffiti (3,730), and improperly parked e-scooters (2,159).

According to the city's data for closed cases, average response times varied significantly by service type. Park maintenance requests took an average of 19.5 days to resolve. Reports for dumped items were handled in about 15.5 days, while graffiti removal averaged about 6.5 days.

Some services were much faster. Dead animal pickups and animal services requests were typically closed in about 16 hours. It is possible that some unusually fast closures are due to administrative processing and not on-site work completion.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
