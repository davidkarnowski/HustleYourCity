Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (5,865 calls), Graffiti (3,606 calls), and E-Scooter issues (2,564 calls).

Response times varied by issue. The city responded fastest to Animal Services requests, with an average close time of about 16 hours. Reports for graffiti were addressed in about 5 days on average. Slower response times were seen for requests for Park Maintenance (about 17 days) and illegally Dumped Items (about 2 weeks).

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application to report it directly to the city.
