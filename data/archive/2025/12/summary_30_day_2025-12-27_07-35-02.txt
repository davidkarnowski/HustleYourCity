Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data. Response times for several key services have seen significant improvement.

Over the last month, the average time to close a Graffiti report was about 2.3 days, a notable decrease from the previous period's average of over 4 days. Dumped Items, the most reported issue with 1,911 requests, were addressed in an average of about 8 days, down from over 11 days.

Street Repair requests were completed in an average of 2.5 days. Animal-related services continue to be handled quickly, with Dead Animal Pickups averaging under 13 hours.

To report issues like dumped items, graffiti, or potholes, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at December 26, 2025 at 11:29 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 26, 2025 at 11:00 PM PST.
