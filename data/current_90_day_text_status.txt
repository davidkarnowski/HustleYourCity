Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped items, with 6,181 calls, and graffiti removal, with 3,742 calls. The average time to resolve a dumped item request was about 15 days, while graffiti requests averaged about 6.5 days.

Animal-related services were resolved quickly. The 197 requests for Animal Services and 428 for Dead Animal Pickup were all marked as "Closed" and had an average response time of less than 17 hours. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
