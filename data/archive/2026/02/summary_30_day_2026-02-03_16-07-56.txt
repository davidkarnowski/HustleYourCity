Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past month, residents submitted over 6,100 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (nearly 2,500 requests), followed by graffiti and e-scooters, each with over 1,000 reports.

The average city response time to close a request for dumped items was about 10 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 2.5 days. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups (about 11 hours) and restroom maintenance (about 8 hours). Some very low response times can be due to administrative closures and not work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The best way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
