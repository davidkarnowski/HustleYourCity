Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service response data for the past week.

The most frequent requests were for Dumped Items with 446 calls and Graffiti with 198 calls. The average response time to close a Dumped Items request was about 34 hours, while Graffiti removal requests took about 42 hours.

Response times for Street Repair and Tree Maintenance requests both averaged about 1 day and 8 hours. The average response for a completed Park Maintenance request was about 37 minutes. Note that very low response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
