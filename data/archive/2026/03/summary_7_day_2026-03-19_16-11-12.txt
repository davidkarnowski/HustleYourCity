Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a summary of city response times and request volumes over the past 7 days.

Dumped items saw the highest volume with 519 reports. The city averaged a 67.8-hour response time for these issues, though 391 requests currently remain in new status. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 234 calls and an average response time of 25.9 hours.

E-scooter complaints accounted for 126 calls, all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies. Dead animal pickups saw 29 requests with a fast average response time of 4.3 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
