Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

The most reported issues were Dumped Items with 1,964 requests and Graffiti with 1,099 requests. The city also received 947 reports for E-Scooters, which are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Based on city data for closed cases, the average response time for Dumped Items was about 6.5 days. Street repair requests took about 4 days to resolve, while graffiti removal averaged about 65 hours. Some of the quickest responses were for Animal Services (about 10 hours) and Dead Animal Pickup (about 12 hours).

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
