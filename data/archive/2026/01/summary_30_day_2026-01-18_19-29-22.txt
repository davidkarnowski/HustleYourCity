Here's the latest look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,400 requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,026 requests), e-scooter issues (951), and graffiti (897).

Average city response times for top issues include: approximately 4.3 days for dumped items and about 52 hours for graffiti removal. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 12 hours on average.

Some response times may appear unusually low, such as for damaged drinking fountains (about 2 hours). These can be the result of administrative closures and may not reflect on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
