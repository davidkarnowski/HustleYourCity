The latest 7‑day snapshot shows how Long Beach city services are responding to resident requests.

In the past week 72 street‑repair tickets were logged, with an average response time of 8.6 hours (20 closed, 47 still in progress). Dumped‑items reports totalled 403, averaging 39 hours before action, while graffiti complaints reached 291 with a 26.8‑hour average response.

Some categories are being addressed very quickly: tree‑maintenance requests (76 total) were responded to in about 5 hours on average, dead‑animal pickups (20) in 5.8 hours, and vegetation issues (8) in just 4.3 hours. Slower turnaround appears for park‑maintenance (11 requests, 58 hours average) and trash & debris (11 requests, 52.5 hours average).

Very fast response times can sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed work, so a short turnaround does not always mean the issue is resolved on the ground.

Residents are encouraged to report new issues or check status updates through the Go Long Beach mobile app.
