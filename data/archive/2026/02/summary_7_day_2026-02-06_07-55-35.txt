Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. In total, residents submitted over 1,400 requests for various issues.

The most common reports were for dumped items, with 619 requests, and graffiti, with 287 requests.

Based on city data, the average response time to close a graffiti report was about 33 hours. Dumped item reports were closed in an average of 29 hours. Some services were faster; dead animal pickups were handled in about 10 hours on average. Note that these times reflect when a case is closed in the system and may not always align with work completion.
