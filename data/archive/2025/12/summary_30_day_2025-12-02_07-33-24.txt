City service call activity in the last 30 days shows steady progress on several fronts.

- Dumped Items received 1,887 reports with an average response time of 199 hours (about 8 days), down from 319 hours in the prior 60 days.  
- Graffiti calls fell to 1,370 with a faster average response of 68 hours (≈2.8 days), improving from 92 hours.  
- Traffic Signal issues (36 reports) were addressed in an average of 24 hours, better than the 36‑hour average seen earlier.  
- Tree Maintenance was especially quick: 198 requests closed in an average of 12 hours.  
- Street Repair (203 reports) and Sidewalk Repair (75 reports) now average 23 hours and 20 hours respectively, both faster than the previous 27‑hour and 18‑hour benchmarks.  
- Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup together handled 183 calls with average response times under 15 hours, a noticeable gain from the 17‑hour range before.  

Overall, the city processed 7,000+ service requests across all categories, with most response times moving toward faster resolution.

Residents are encouraged to report issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app to keep these improvements moving forward.
