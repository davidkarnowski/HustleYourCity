Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day city service response data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume issue with 2319 reports. Because this category averages 154 hours, the city's response time is about 6.4 days.

Graffiti is the second most reported issue with 1005 calls. The city is responding to these in about 36 hours.

E-Scooter complaints saw 862 reports, but all were referred to outside agencies, meaning the city tracks no response time for them.

Dead Animal Pickups, with 154 reports, are handled in just under 9 hours. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Other notable response times over the 72-hour mark include Park Maintenance at 3.7 days, Trash and Debris at 3.1 days, and Illegal Firework reports taking about 5.8 days to process.
