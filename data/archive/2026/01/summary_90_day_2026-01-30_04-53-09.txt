Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent issue reported was "Dumped Items," with over 6,200 requests. The average response time to close these reports was about 13 days. Graffiti was the next most common report, with over 3,300 requests handled in an average of about 5 days. Over 2,600 reports about E-Scooters were referred directly to the scooter companies.

Some services had faster closure times. Reports for dead animal pickup and other animal services were closed in about 13 to 14 hours on average. Note that some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than the completion of fieldwork. Street repair requests were closed in an average of about 5.5 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
