Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped items (6,336 calls), graffiti (3,276 calls), and e-scooters (2,712 calls).

Average response times for some key services include approximately 13 days for dumped items and about 5 days for graffiti removal. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups and animal services, both averaging around 12 hours. Some unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
