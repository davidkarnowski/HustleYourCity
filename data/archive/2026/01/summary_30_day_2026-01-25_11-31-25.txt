Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on public data for the last 30 days.

The data shows continued improvements in response times for some of the most common service requests. The average time for "Dumped Items" removal is now just over a week, a significant decrease from previous months. "Graffiti" removal has also gotten faster, now averaging under three days. Street and sidewalk repairs are being closed in about three days and just under two days, respectively.

Over the past month, thousands of requests were submitted by residents. Animal-related issues, including "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" calls, were among the fastest responses, with an average completion time of about 12 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
