Here is the latest city service response data for Long Beach over the past 7 days, provided by Hustle Long Beach! as an independent look at local government performance.

Dumped items received the highest volume of calls with 581 reports. The city averaged a response time of 53 hours for these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 215 requests, showing an average response time of 16.6 hours.
