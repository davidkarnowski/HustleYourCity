Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service requests.

Over the last three months, residents most frequently reported illegally dumped items (6,119 reports), graffiti (3,736 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,201 reports).

Response times varied by issue. Animal-related calls were handled the fastest, with an average closure time of under 17 hours. In contrast, park maintenance requests took the longest, averaging nearly 3 weeks to resolve. Graffiti removal averaged about 6.5 days, while reports of dumped items took over 2 weeks.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at 3:30 AM PST on December 16, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 3:00 AM PST on December 16, 2025.
