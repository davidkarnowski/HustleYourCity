Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest look at how the City of Long Beach is handling service requests over the past 7 days.

Dumped items led the city's service calls with 524 reports. The average response time for these requests was about 3.5 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 212 calls, seeing an average response time of 21.5 hours. E-Scooter complaints totaled 196, though all of these were referred to outside agencies.

Some issues were processed very quickly. Reports of shoes on a utility wire averaged a response time of just 44 minutes, while dead animal pickups averaged 4.8 hours. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

Help keep the city accountable and well-maintained. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
