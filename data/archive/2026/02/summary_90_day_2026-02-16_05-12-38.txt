An independent review of City of Long Beach service requests over the last 90 days highlights current response times for common neighborhood issues.

Dumped Items remain the most frequent report type with 6,451 total calls. The average time to resolve these requests is currently approximately 13 and a half days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,193. The average response time for these issues is roughly 4 days.

Street Repair requests, totaling 774, are showing an average completion time of nearly 5 days. Light repair requests are being handled in just under 4 days on average.

Dead Animal Pickup services are averaging 11 hours for completion.

Residents are encouraged to report issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure data is tracked accurately.
