The latest review of Long Beach city service data focuses on activity from the last 90 days. During this period, the most frequently reported issue was Dumped Items, with 6,453 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 13 days.

Graffiti removal remains a high-volume category with 3,099 reports filed. The data shows an average completion time of roughly 4 days for these incidents. Street Repair requests totaled 798, with crews responding in about 4.5 days on average.

Other notable service categories include Dead Animal Pickup, which received 424 reports and maintained an average response time of 11 hours. There were also 2,975 E-Scooter reports, though the majority of these are listed as 'Closed Referred', indicating they are passed to third-party operators.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs. Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than physical work completion.
