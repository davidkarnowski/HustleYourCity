The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across core maintenance categories over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 7,326 reports logged. The average response time for these requests is approximately 8 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 3,286 reports and an average response time of about 3 days.

Tree maintenance saw 673 reports with an average response time of nearly 4 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show the fastest turnaround times, with dead animal pickups averaging roughly 5 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track city service requests.
