Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of service requests with 7,044 reports. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,167 calls, averaging a 58.6-hour response time. E-scooter complaints followed closely with 2,865 reports.

Response times varied significantly across different departments. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes, though unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead animal pickups were also handled quickly, averaging 10.2 hours across 448 calls. On the slower end, street repair requests took an average of 6.8 days to address across 800 reports.

Followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 5, 2026 4:44 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 5, 2026 3:00 AM PDT.
