Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted over 1,300 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (502 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (254), and graffiti (204).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 1 day and 5 hours. For dumped items, the average response was just over 2 days. Quicker responses were seen for dead animal pickups and animal services, both averaging around 8 hours. Some services, like vegetation maintenance, show unusually fast completion times (around 82 minutes). These may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work.
