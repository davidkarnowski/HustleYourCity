The latest data for city services in Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,605 reports for dumped items and 2,562 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 7 hours. In contrast, dumped items have an average response time of about 17 days. Graffiti removal currently averages about 65 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

To help keep our community clean and maintained, please continue to report issues using the Go Long Beach service application. Providing accurate information through the official app is the most effective way to ensure your concerns are tracked and addressed by the city.
