Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data from the past 30 days. Here is a factual breakdown of call volumes and response times for the community.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of service requests with 2,353 reports. The average response time for these issues was 8.3 days. Graffiti reports were also high, totaling 1,041 requests with an average response time of 55.4 hours. Street light issues saw 237 requests with an average response time of 4 days.

Other notable services included dead animal pickup, which had 171 requests and an average response time of 6.8 hours. Animal services handled 74 calls with an average response of 8.4 hours
