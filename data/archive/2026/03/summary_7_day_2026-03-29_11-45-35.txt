Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the most significant service request trends over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 527 reports. The city averaged a response time of 68.4 hours for these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 243 calls and an average response time of 15.5 hours. E-Scooter complaints reached 205, all of which were referred to other agencies.

Some service categories recorded extremely fast turnaround times. Reports for Shoes on a Utility Wire averaged a 44-minute response, while Restroom Maintenance requests averaged 50 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Other notable areas include Dead Animal Pickup, which saw 37 requests that were all closed with an average response time of nearly 6 hours. Street Repair received 66 calls, averaging a 14.3-hour response time.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Please use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
