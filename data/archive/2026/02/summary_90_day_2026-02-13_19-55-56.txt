Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

Dumped Items remains the highest volume category with 6,409 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 323 hours, or nearly two weeks.

Graffiti removal received 3,153 reports. The city is averaging a response time of roughly four days for these issues.

Dead Animal Pickup shows a faster turnaround, averaging about 11 hours across 409 reports.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may be due to administrative closures rather than physical work completion.
