Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,350 service requests were made in the last seven days through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (514 calls), e-scooters (250 calls), and graffiti (218 calls).

The average response time to close a report for illegally dumped items was about 3.3 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 33 hours on average, while dead animal pickups were completed in about 6.5 hours. Animal services requests were closed in an average of 1.7 hours. Some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.
