Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest Long Beach city service data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of requests, and here is how the city responded.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,992 reports. Because this category averages 204 hours, the response time translates to about 8.5 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,256 calls, taking an average of 55 hours to resolve.

Infrastructure requests like Street Repair (728 reports) and Tree Maintenance (902 reports) are currently averaging response times of 7.1 days and 7.6 days, respectively. 

Some categories show extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or system referrals rather than actual work completions.
