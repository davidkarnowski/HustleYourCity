Data from the last 30 days indicates that Dumped Items remain the highest volume service request category, with 2,237 total reports and an average response time of nearly 6 days. Graffiti reports followed with 1,143 total calls, averaging a response time of just under 2 days.

Infrastructure requests showed varied completion times. Street Repair generated 250 reports with an average response of over 3 days. Tree Maintenance received 428 reports, averaging close to 2 days for a response.

Quicker response times were recorded for public health and safety issues. Dead Animal Pickup and Abandoned Shopping Carts both averaged approximately 12 hours for resolution.
