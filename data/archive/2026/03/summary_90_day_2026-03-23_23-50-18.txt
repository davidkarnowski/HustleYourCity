Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest review of Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days.

The highest volume of service requests involved Dumped Items with 6,735 reports, followed by Graffiti with 3,054 reports, and E-Scooter issues with 2,843 reports.

Response times vary significantly by category. Dumped Items took an average of 9.5 days to process. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 68 hours. Tree Maintenance requests averaged 6.6 days for a response. Street Repair requests took an average of 6.4 days.

Some categories show extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business Licenses and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
