Here is the latest look at Long Beach service request data for the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents made 1,473 service requests. The most reported issues were for illegally dumped items (590 requests), graffiti (283 requests), and e-scooter problems (260 requests).

According to the city's data, the average time to close a graffiti report was about 27 hours. For dumped items, the average response time was about 55 hours, or just over two days. Tree maintenance requests were closed in an average of about 8 hours. Some fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not work completion.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
