Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the city response times and call totals over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 587 calls. The average response time for these issues was 3.5 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 293 calls and an average response time of 45 hours. E-Scooter complaints totaled 275, with nearly all of them being referred and closed.

Other notable service requests included 53 reports for Light issues, which took an average of 3.7 days to address. Dead Animal Pickup received 36 reports with an average response time of 5.7 hours. Animal Services handled 19 calls, averaging a 3.8-hour response.

Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
