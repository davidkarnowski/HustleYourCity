Graffiti reports remain the top issue with 1,418 calls in the last 30 days. The city closed 1,084 of them, averaging 66.5 hours (about 2.8 days) to resolve each case.

Light outages generated 240 requests, with an average response time of 39 hours. Tree maintenance saw 224 calls and a fast 10 hour average response.

Dumped items accounted for 1,976 reports; the average time to close was 160 hours (about 6.7 days). Sidewalk repair (88 calls) and street repair (201 calls) had average response times of 14 hours and 20 hours respectively.

Compared with the prior 60‑day window, graffiti reports dropped from 2,653 to 1,418 (≈ 47 % decrease) and dumped items fell from 4,120 to 1,976 (≈ 52 % decrease), indicating fewer new incidents or faster processing.
