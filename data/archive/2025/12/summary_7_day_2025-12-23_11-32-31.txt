Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,171 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (445 calls), illegally parked e-scooters (246 calls), and graffiti (237 calls).

The average time to close a graffiti report was about 26 hours. Dumped item reports were addressed in an average of about 1.5 days. Some services saw faster responses, with tree maintenance requests being closed in an average of 3.5 hours. A single restroom maintenance request was closed in 51 minutes; unusually fast closures can sometimes be due to administrative processing.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach app to help the city track and respond to community needs.
