Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items received the highest number of reports with 2352 calls. The average response time for these issues was about 8 days. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 1063 reports.

Graffiti removal was another major concern, generating 1047 reports. The city responded to these in just over 2 days on average. Street light issues saw 236 reports, taking about 4 days to address, while park maintenance took about 3 days to resolve across 74 reports.

Some of the fastest response times were seen in Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged under 7 hours for 170 calls, and Animal Services, averaging about 8 hours for 79 calls. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completion.
