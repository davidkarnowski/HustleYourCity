An evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 30 days highlights current response times for high-volume categories.

Dumped Items represented the highest volume of service calls with 2,136 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 5 days. Graffiti reports followed with 1,089 total calls, showing an average response time of just under 2 days.

Infrastructure data shows that Street Repair generated 261 reports with an average response time of 3 days. Tree Maintenance received 440 reports, averaging nearly 2 days for a response. Dead Animal Pickup requests were handled in approximately 14 hours.

Certain categories displayed rapid closure rates. Reports regarding Business Licenses and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of 12 minutes. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
