Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data. In the past 30 days, residents submitted nearly 4,900 reports. The most frequent issues were for dumped items (1,879 reports), graffiti (1,107 reports), and e-scooters (813 reports).

Average city response times for closed requests varied. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 2 days. For dumped items, the average was about 8 days. Street repairs took an average of about 5 days to close.

Note that some response times may appear unusually low due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at December 15, 2025, 3:31 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 15, 2025, 3:00 AM PST.
