City service activity in Long Beach over the past week shows 1,438 requests logged.

The most common issues were graffiti (385 reports) with an average response time of 33.3 hours (about 1.4 days) and dumped items (536 reports) taking about 75.2 hours (just over 3 days) to address. Tree maintenance responded quickly, averaging 8.7 hours, while street repairs and sidewalk repairs averaged 10.7 hours and 15.4 hours respectively. Light outages and side‑walk repairs were resolved in roughly 32 hours and 15 hours on average. Other categories such as vegetation (8 reports) and traffic signals (9 reports) saw response times under 15 hours.

Overall, most service types are being handled within a day to a few days. Very fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

Residents can report new issues or track existing requests through the Go Long Beach service app.
