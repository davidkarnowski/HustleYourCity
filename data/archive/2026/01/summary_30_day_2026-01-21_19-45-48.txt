Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past month, the most frequent service request was for "Dumped Items," with 2,118 reports filed. The next most common requests were for "E-Scooter" issues and "Graffiti" removal, each with over 900 reports.

The average city response time to close a "Dumped Items" case was about 7.5 days. "Graffiti" removal requests were completed in an average of about 3 days. Some services had faster turnarounds, such as "Dead Animal Pickup," which averaged about 12 hours.
