Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 6,100 requests for dumped item pickups and over 3,700 for graffiti removal, making them the most reported issues. In total, the city received over 15,000 service calls during this period.

Average response times varied. Graffiti reports were closed in about 6.5 days on average. Dumped item requests took an average of 15 days. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 17 hours.
