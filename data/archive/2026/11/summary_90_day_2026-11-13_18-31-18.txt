The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,598 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 17 days.

Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,569 reports and an average response time of about 2.5 days. Tree maintenance saw 1,027 reports with an average response time of nearly 6 days.

Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging roughly 11 hours and 7 hours respectively over the last 90 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
