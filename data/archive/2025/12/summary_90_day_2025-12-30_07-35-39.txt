Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (5,915 reports), graffiti (3,632 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,489 reports).

The average time to resolve a park maintenance request was about 18 days, while reports of illegally dumped items took an average of 15 days. Graffiti removal was faster, averaging about 5 days. Some of the quickest responses were for Animal Services calls, which were handled in an average of 15 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure it gets logged.

This data summary was updated at December 29, 2025, 11:32 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 29, 2025, 11:00 PM PST.
