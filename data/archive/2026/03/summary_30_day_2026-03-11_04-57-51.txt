Hustle Long Beach! is back with your latest 30-day city service data update. We track these numbers to help keep the City of Long Beach accountable to its residents.

Over the past month, Dumped Items led the service requests with 2,121 reports. Graffiti followed with 1,094 reports, and E-Scooter complaints generated 991 calls.

Response times varied significantly depending on the type of issue. The city took an average of 4.7 days to respond to Dumped Items and 4.5 days for Trash and Debris. Graffiti reports were handled faster, averaging a 43.7-hour response time. 

Some categories showed incredibly fast turnaround times. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

We encourage all followers to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Your reports create the data that drives accountability.
