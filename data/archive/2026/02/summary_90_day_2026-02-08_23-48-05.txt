Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the past 90 days.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 6,426 requests, followed by graffiti with 3,219 reports. Over 2,600 reports about e-scooters were also filed; these are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

The average city response time for dumped items was nearly two weeks. Graffiti removal averaged about 4.5 days. The longest waits were for marina repairs, which took about 5 weeks on average to resolve. Quicker responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 12 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the best way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
