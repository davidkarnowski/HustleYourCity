Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past 30 days.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 2,438 calls. The average time to resolve these requests was about 7.4 days. Graffiti was the second most common report with 994 calls, which were handled in an average of 2.5 days.

Requests for street light and street repairs were also frequent. The city's data shows average response times of just under 3 days for both services. Traffic signal repairs took longer, averaging nearly 6 days.
