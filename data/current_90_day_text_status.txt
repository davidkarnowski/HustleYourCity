Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service response data.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to be the most reported issues, with a combined total of over 9,800 service calls from the public. The average response time to close a dumped item report was about 15.5 days. Graffiti reports were addressed more quickly, with an average completion time of about 6.5 days.

Response times for other key services varied. Park maintenance requests took an average of nearly 20 days to resolve, while street repairs averaged about 9.5 days. Reports for dead animal pickups were typically completed in about 16 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.

This data summary was updated at December 15, 2025 at 11:32 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 15, 2025 at 11:00 PM PST.
