Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 24-hour service call data to keep citizens informed on city response times.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of calls with 69 reports, though the vast majority remain in the new status with no response time logged yet. Graffiti followed with 21 reports and an average response time of 12.4 hours. Street Repair received 14 reports, averaging a 12.8-hour response.

Notably, Dead Animal Pickup recorded a significantly low average response time of just 35 minutes across 7 reports. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Other notable averages include Tree Maintenance at 16.4 hours for 6 reports and Sidewalk Repair at 16 hours for 5 reports. 

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help track city performance.
