Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Over 1,100 new service requests were submitted by residents.

The most reported issues were for Dumped Items (431 requests), Graffiti (238 requests), and E-Scooters (198 requests).

Based on the latest data, the average response time for Graffiti removal was about 22 hours. Dumped Items took an average of about 35 hours to resolve. Sidewalk Repair requests show an unusually fast average response of about 45 minutes. This may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.
