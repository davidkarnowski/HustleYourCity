Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at how the city handled citizen reports over the past 30 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2245 calls. The city took an average of 5.4 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most common issue, with 1032 reports and an average response time of about 40 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 508 requests, taking an average of 3.2 days to address. Street repairs had 259 reports, averaging 3.4 days for a response. Park maintenance requests took an average of 4.5 days to resolve.

Some categories showed incredibly fast response times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep the community in top shape by reporting issues. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local concerns directly to the city.
