Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the last month, residents submitted over 5,200 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (1,913 reports), e-scooter issues (973 reports), and graffiti removal (941 reports).

Response times varied by the type of service requested. The average time to resolve a report for dumped items was nearly 7 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 2.5 days on average. Reports for trash and debris took the longest to close, averaging over 9 days. Among the quickest responses were for dead animal pickup and other animal services, which were handled in about 14 hours.

Please note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures and may not reflect on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
