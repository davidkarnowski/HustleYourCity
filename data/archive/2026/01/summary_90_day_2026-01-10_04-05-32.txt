Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,959 requests), graffiti (3,568 requests), and e-scooters (2,648 requests).

The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 4.5 days. For illegally dumped items, the average response time was longer, at approximately 13.5 days. Faster responses were recorded for issues like dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 16.5 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to notify the city.

This data summary was updated at January 9, 2026 at 8:02 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 9, 2026 at 7:00 PM PST.
