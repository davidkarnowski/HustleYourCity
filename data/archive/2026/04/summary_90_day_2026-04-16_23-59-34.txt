Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of requests, and here is a look at how the City of Long Beach responded.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 6,941 reports. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond.

Graffiti removal saw 3,239 reports, with an average response time of 58 hours.

Tree Maintenance had 892 reports, taking an average of 9 days for a response.

Dead Animal Pickup had 466 reports, with the city responding in about 10 hours on average.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnarounds. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
