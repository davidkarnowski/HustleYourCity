Here is the latest 30-day snapshot of Long Beach city service requests and response times based on public data.

Over the past month, the city received 1,930 requests for dumped item pickups, making it the most reported issue. The average time to close these requests was about 8 days. Graffiti was the next most common, with 1,034 reports closed in an average of about 56 hours.

Response times for other key services varied. The 186 street repair requests were addressed in about 61 hours on average. Animal-related services were handled more quickly, with the 49 animal services calls averaging 9.5 hours. It is worth noting that some categories with very few requests can show unusually fast closure times that may reflect administrative processing rather than completed work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
