Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data from the past 24 hours.

Residents submitted 141 new service requests. The most frequent reports were for improperly parked E-Scooters (45 requests), Graffiti (38 requests), and Dumped Items (37 requests).

The two requests for Dead Animal Pickup were closed in an average of about 16 minutes. It is possible that unusually fast response times reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion. Most other new requests for issues like street repair, graffiti, and dumped items are still awaiting action.
