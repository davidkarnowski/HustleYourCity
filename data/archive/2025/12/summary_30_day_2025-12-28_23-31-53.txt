Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

Reports for illegally dumped items were the most common issue, with 1,951 requests. The average response time for these requests was about 10 days. Graffiti was another frequently reported issue with 993 requests, which were resolved in about 2 days on average. The 1,034 reports for e-scooters were referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Other key service times include street repairs, which took an average of 58 hours to close. Animal-related calls were handled more quickly, with Animal Services requests averaging about 9 hours and dead animal pickups averaging about 11.5 hours. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the city's Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to have it logged and addressed.
