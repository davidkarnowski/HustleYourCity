In the past week Long Beach residents filed 1,358 service requests across 20 categories.

The most common issues were Dumped Items (507 reports) and Graffiti (369 reports). Dumped Items took an average of 76.7 hours (about 3 days) to respond, while Graffiti was addressed in about 36 hours (1.5 days). Tree Maintenance, Light repairs and Street Repair were resolved faster, with average response times of 9 hours, 35.7 hours and 11.9 hours respectively.

New reports were highest for Dumped Items (195 new) and Graffiti (29 new). All other categories saw response times under two days, showing steady progress on city maintenance.

Residents can help keep the city moving by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service app.
