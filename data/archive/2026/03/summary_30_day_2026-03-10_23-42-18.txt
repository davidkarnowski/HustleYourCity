Here is the latest 30-day city service data summary from Hustle Long Beach! evaluating how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Over the last 30 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 2,106 reports. Graffiti followed with 1,091 reports, and E-Scooter issues generated 991 reports.

Response times varied significantly across different service types. Dumped Items took an average of 4.7 days to resolve, while Trash and Debris reports averaged 4.5 days. Graffiti was addressed much faster, averaging about 44 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns showed a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps track performance and ensures neighborhoods get the attention they need.
