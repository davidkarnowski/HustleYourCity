Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a summary of city service requests and response times over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,351 reports. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,007 calls, showing an average response time of 35 hours. 

E-Scooter complaints totaled 905, though all of these were referred to outside agencies rather than handled directly by city staff.

Street Repair saw 207 requests with an average response time of 3 days, while Park Maintenance had 75 reports averaging just over 3 days for a response.

Dead Animal Pickup requests totaled 166, with an average response time of 4 hours. Keep in mind that unusually low response times across any category may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help track city responsiveness.
