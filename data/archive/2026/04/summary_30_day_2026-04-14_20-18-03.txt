Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 30 days. Here is a factual look at the recent numbers and response times to keep the community informed.

The city received the highest number of reports for Dumped Items with 2350 calls. Graffiti followed with 1097 reports, and E-Scooter issues accounted for 1000 calls. Other notable totals include 248 calls for Tree Maintenance and 225 calls for Light issues.

Response times varied significantly across different service types. For Dumped Items, the average response time was about 8 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 41 hours. Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services saw faster turnarounds, averaging 7 hours and 9 hours, respectively. Issues like Marina Repair and Light reports took longer, averaging 6 days and 3.5 days.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
