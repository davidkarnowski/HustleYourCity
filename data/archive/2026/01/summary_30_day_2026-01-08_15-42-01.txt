Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past month, the most frequent reports from residents were for illegally dumped items (1,893 requests), e-scooter issues (934 requests), and graffiti removal (900 requests).

The data shows average response times for some services remain lengthy. Reports for trash and debris took about 10 days to close. Requests for dumped items and traffic signal repairs were addressed in about 7 days on average.

Faster responses were recorded for other common issues. The average time to resolve graffiti reports was just over 2 days. Street repairs were completed in a similar timeframe. Dead animal pickups were typically handled within 15 hours. Some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
