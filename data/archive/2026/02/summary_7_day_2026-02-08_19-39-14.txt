Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data for the past week.

The most frequent service requests were for Dumped Items, with 654 reports, and Graffiti, with 287 reports. The average response time to close a dumped item request was 18.6 hours, while graffiti reports were closed in an average of 25.1 hours.

Other key services showed quicker response times. The average close time for Street Repair was 7.8 hours and Tree Maintenance was 8.2 hours. Abandoned Shopping Cart requests were closed in an average of 39 minutes. Note that unusually fast responses may be due to administrative closures and not on-site work completion.
