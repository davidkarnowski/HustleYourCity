Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 2,400 reports for illegally dumped items, making it the most frequent request. Graffiti was also a top concern, with over 1,000 reports filed.

According to city data, the average response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 9.5 days. For "Graffiti" reports, the average was about 2.5 days. Some services saw quicker resolutions. The 141 requests for "Dead Animal Pickup" were closed in an average of about 11 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the city's Go Long Beach app.
