Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the last 30 days, "Dumped Items" was the most reported issue with 2,472 requests. This was followed by 1,055 requests for "Graffiti" and 1,002 for "E-Scooter" issues.

The average city response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 8 days. "Graffiti" reports were closed in an average of about 2.5 days. Some of the fastest responses were for "Dead Animal Pickup" (about 11.5 hours) and "Animal Services" (about 13 hours).

Note that E-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies and do not have a city response time. Some unusually fast closures may be due to administrative processing rather than work completion. See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at February 8, 2026, at 3:45 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach. A data processing time was not provided in this update.
