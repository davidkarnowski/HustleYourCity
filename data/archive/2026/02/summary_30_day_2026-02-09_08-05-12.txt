Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 30 days.

The most reported issues were dumped items with 2,480 calls, graffiti with 1,062 calls, and e-scooters with 1,005 calls. The average response time to resolve a dumped items report was about 8 days. In comparison, graffiti removal requests averaged about 2.5 days.

The fastest responses were for dead animal pickup and animal services, with both categories averaging under 14 hours for closure. All 1,005 e-scooter reports were referred to outside agencies and not handled directly by city staff. Some unusually fast closures may be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
