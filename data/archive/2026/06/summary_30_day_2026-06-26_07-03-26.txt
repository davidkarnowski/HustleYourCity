Hustle Long Beach! update on city service response times.

Over the past 30 days, residents submitted 1,084 graffiti reports with an average response time of approximately 2.5 days. Dumped items remain a high-volume concern with 2,153 reports, averaging about 6 days for resolution. Animal services continue to show efficient turnaround, with 101 reports handled in roughly 12 hours.

Please note that response times under 90 minutes, such as those seen in recent daily data, often reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work.
