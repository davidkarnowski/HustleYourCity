Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, residents submitted more than 15,700 service requests. The most frequent reports were for "Dumped Items" with 5,925 requests and "Graffiti" with 3,560 requests. According to the data, the average response time to resolve a "Dumped Items" request was about two weeks, while "Graffiti" requests were closed in about 4.5 days.

Response times vary significantly depending on the service. Requests for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were among the fastest, with an average closure time of about 17 hours. In contrast, "Marina Repair" requests took an average of about one month to resolve. Some unusually fast responses, such as the 2-hour average for "Damaged Drinking Fountain" repairs, may reflect administrative closures and not the time for physical work completion.
