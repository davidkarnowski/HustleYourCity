Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the last 90 days based on the city's public data.

The most frequent service requests from the public were for dumped items, with 6,074 reports, and graffiti, with 3,383 reports. The city received over 15,000 total requests in this period.

Average response times for high-volume requests varied. Graffiti reports were closed in about 5 days. Requests to remove dumped items took an average of about 13 days. Reports for street light issues were addressed in about 4 days on average.

Animal-related services were among the fastest. Dead animal pickups were completed in an average of about 15 hours, and other animal service calls were closed in about 14 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
