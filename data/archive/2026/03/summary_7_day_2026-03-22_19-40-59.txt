Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest third-party accountability update on city service response times for the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls this week with 507 reports and an average response time of 36 hours. It is worth noting that 411 of these reports are still marked as new.

Graffiti reports followed with 208 calls, showing an average response time of 18.5 hours. E-Scooter complaints generated 170 calls, all of which were closed and referred to other agencies.

On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup averaged a 3-hour response time across 42 reports. Please remember that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

The longest average wait time this week was for Marina Repair, which took about 3.7 days to respond to 2 calls.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Encourage your friends and neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 22, 2026, at 12:38 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 22, 2026, at 12:00 PM PDT.
