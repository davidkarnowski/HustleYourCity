Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest 30-day service call data evaluation for the City of Long Beach.

Over the past 30 days, the highest volume of service requests involved Dumped Items with 2335 reports, followed by Graffiti with 1083 reports, and E-Scooter issues with 957 reports. 

Response times vary significantly by category. The city averaged about 8 days to respond to Dumped Items and roughly 3.6 days for Tree Maintenance. Graffiti reports saw an average response time of 37 hours. Faster turnarounds were seen in Dead Animal Pickup, averaging just under 7 hours. Keep in mind that unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help keep the community informed.
