Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data to keep you informed about response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 6,620 calls. On average, it takes the city about 10 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal follows closely with 3,086 reports, showing an average response time of 3 days.

Other notable services include tree maintenance, which received 904 reports and took nearly 6 days on average to address. Street repairs saw 833 reports with an average turnaround of 6 days.

Some categories show extremely fast turnarounds. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
