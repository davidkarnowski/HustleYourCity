Hustle Long Beach! is here with your daily accountability update on city service response times. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled service requests over the past 24 hours.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 87 new reports, though the vast majority have not yet been processed. E-Scooter complaints were the second most frequent with 40 reports, all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies.

For requests that saw action, Dead Animal Pickup and Graffiti removal had the fastest processing times, averaging 4.5 hours and 5.25 hours respectively. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not necessarily actual work completions.

On the slower end of the daily spectrum, Sidewalk Repair requests averaged 18.25 hours to process, and Street Repair requests took an average of 17 hours. 

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
