Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

In the past month, the most common reports were for dumped items (2,062 requests), graffiti (1,006 requests), and e-scooters (987 requests).

Based on city data for closed cases, the average response time for dumped items was about 10 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 2.3 days, and street repairs took a similar average of 2.3 days. Some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
