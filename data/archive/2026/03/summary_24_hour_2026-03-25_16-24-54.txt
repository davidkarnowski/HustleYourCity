Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest 24-hour city service data to keep citizens informed on response times and call volumes.

Over the past day, Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 92 reports, though 81 of those remain in the new status. The average response time for these cases was 6.4 hours. Graffiti followed with 25 reports and an average response time of 16.2 hours.

Several categories showed unusually fast response times. Vegetation issues averaged just 12 minutes, Sidewalk Repair averaged 22 minutes, and Restroom Maintenance averaged 35 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
