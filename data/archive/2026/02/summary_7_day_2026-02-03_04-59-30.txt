Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Residents submitted 1,378 requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (572 calls), graffiti (246 calls), and e-scooter issues (241 calls).

Looking at average response times for closed cases, graffiti removal took about 32 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 45 hours on average. The quickest response was for dead animal pickups, averaging under 5 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it is the first step to getting it fixed. Use the Go Long Beach app to submit a service request directly to the city.
