The latest data from the City of Long Beach service call system shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 8,461 reports regarding dumped items and 2,959 reports concerning graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Recent data shows that dead animal pickup requests are being addressed quickly, with an average response time of approximately 6.5 hours over the last 90 days. Please note that unusually low response time figures for certain categories may reflect administrative closures or system processing rather than the completion of physical work.
