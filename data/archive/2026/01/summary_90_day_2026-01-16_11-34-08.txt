Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted thousands of requests, with the most common reports for illegally dumped items (6,022 reports), graffiti (3,453 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,703 reports).

Average response times varied by service type. The city took about 13 days to close reports for dumped items and over 3 weeks for marina repairs. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 4.5 days on average. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 16 hours. Some services show unusually fast response times, which may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
