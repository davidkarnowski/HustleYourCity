Here's a look at the latest city service response data for Long Beach over the past 30 days.

Illegal dumping continues to be the most frequent service request, with 2,419 reports filed. Graffiti removal was also a top concern with 1,023 reports.

According to the city's data, the average time to close an illegal dumping report was about 9 days. Graffiti reports were addressed more quickly, with an average closure time of about 2.6 days. Faster responses were recorded for issues like dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 11 hours on average. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
