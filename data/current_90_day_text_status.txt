Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the last 90 days.

Illegally dumped items (6,116 calls) and graffiti (3,732 calls) remain the most common service requests from the public. The data shows significant differences in city response times depending on the issue.

On average, reports for dead animal pickups were addressed in about 16 hours. In contrast, the average response time for dumped items was about 15 days, and park maintenance requests took nearly 19 days to resolve. Graffiti cleanup requests were completed in about 6.5 days on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
