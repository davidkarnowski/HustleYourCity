Here is an evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests for the past 7 days.

The highest volume of service calls was for Dumped Items, totaling 507 reports with an average response time of approximately 1.5 days. Graffiti removal received 256 reports, averaging a response time of 1.6 days. There were 248 reports regarding E-Scooters, all of which were marked as closed and referred.

Service categories exceeding a 72-hour average response time included Vegetation requests at 3.1 days and Sidewalk Repair at just under 3 days. Street Repair requests averaged 2.9 days.

Faster response times were noted for Abandoned Shopping Carts, averaging 10.5 hours, and Animal Services, averaging 20 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than the physical completion of work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
