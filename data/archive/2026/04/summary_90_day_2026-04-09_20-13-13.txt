Here is the latest 90-day city service data update for the Hustle Long Beach! community. As an independent accountability tracker, this summary reviews recent response times and call volumes to keep residents informed.

Dumped Items generated the highest number of service requests with 6,913 reports. The city averaged a response time of 8.5 days for these issues.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue, totaling 3,176 calls with an average response time of 55.7 hours.

Street Repair received 735 reports, taking an average of 7.1 days to address. Tree Maintenance saw 897 requests, averaging nearly 6 days for a response.

On the faster end, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city. Public reports help track performance and improve the community.
