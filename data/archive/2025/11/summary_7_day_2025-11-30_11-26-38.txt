In the past week Long Beach logged 961 service requests across all categories.

Graffiti had the highest volume (235 reports) with 130 closed and an average response time of about 22 hours. Dumped Items (299 reports) were resolved in roughly 29 hours on average. Light issues (64 reports) saw an average response of 12 hours.

Fast‑turnaround categories included:
- Tree Maintenance: 33 reports, average response ≈ 72 minutes.
- Sidewalk Repair: 12 reports, average response ≈ 16 minutes.
- Traffic Signal: 6 reports, average response ≈ 34 minutes.
- Street Repair: 32 reports, average response ≈ 9 hours.

Other services such as Animal Services (11 reports) and Dead Animal Pickup (20 reports) were closed within about 9 hours and 8 hours respectively.

These figures show where the city is responding quickly and where longer waits remain. Residents can help track and report issues directly through the Go Long Beach service app.
