Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of service requests with 564 calls. The average response time for these requests was 64.7 hours.

Dead Animal Pickup handled 37 calls, averaging a 3.5-hour response time. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times across any category may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
