An evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days highlights current response times and call volumes for key community issues.

Dumped Items remain the most frequent service request, totaling 6,446 reports. The average response time for these issues is currently 13.5 days.

Graffiti removal received 3,168 reports, with an average completion time of 4 days. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 2,768 reports, all of which were closed and referred to operators.

Street repairs totaled 789 reports, with crews taking an average of 5 days to resolve issues. Tree maintenance requests, totaling 588, showed an average response time of nearly 8 days. Dead animal pickup remains one of the faster services, averaging 11 hours for completion.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
