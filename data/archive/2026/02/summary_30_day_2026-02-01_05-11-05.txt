Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update for the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,900 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,435 requests), e-scooter issues (1,043 requests), and graffiti removal (1,019 requests).

Based on closed requests, the average response time for dumped items was about 9 days. Graffiti removal took an average of 60 hours, and street repairs were completed in about 67 hours. Faster response times were recorded for dead animal pickups (11 hours) and animal services calls (13 hours).

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to notify the city.
