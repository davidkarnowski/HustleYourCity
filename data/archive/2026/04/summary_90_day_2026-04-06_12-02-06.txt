Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual look at call totals and response times to help citizens understand how the City of Long Beach is handling local issues.

The highest volume of calls came from Dumped Items, with 6,976 total requests. The average response time for these reports was almost 9 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,174 calls, averaging a 58-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints totaled 2,863, though all of these were referred to outside agencies.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests showed varying timelines. Street Repair received 767 calls with an average response time of 7 days. Tree Maintenance had 915 reports, taking about 5 days on average to address. Light issues generated 987 calls and were handled in an average of 69 hours.

Some services recorded very rapid response times. Dead Animal Pickup handled 450 calls with an average response of 10 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes for 6 calls. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to actively report issues to the city. Please use the Go Long Beach service application to submit and track your neighborhood requests.
