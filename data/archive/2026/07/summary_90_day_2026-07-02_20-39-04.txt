The latest service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city received 6,627 reports regarding dumped items, which remains the highest volume category. Graffiti reports totaled 3,346, with an average response time of approximately 70 hours. E-scooter reports reached 3,334, though the vast majority of these were marked as closed referred.

Response times for urgent matters have remained efficient. Dead animal pickups averaged about 6 hours, while animal services requests averaged roughly 10 hours. Vegetation maintenance has seen a notable improvement in speed, with an average response time of about 29 hours over the last 90 days.

Residents are encouraged to continue using the Go Long Beach service application to report city maintenance issues. This tool remains the most direct way to notify the city of service needs and track the status of your requests.
