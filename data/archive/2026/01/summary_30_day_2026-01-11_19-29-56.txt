Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with 1,928 requests. The average time to close these cases was about 6 days. Graffiti was the next most common report, with 935 requests closed in an average of 54 hours.

Response times for other services varied. Street repair requests took about 70 hours on average, while reports for trash and debris clean-up took nearly 10 days. Over 900 reports were made about e-scooters, which are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
