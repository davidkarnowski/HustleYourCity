Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service call data. In the past 24 hours, residents submitted 148 new service requests.

The most reported issues were for "Dumped Items" with 70 requests and "Graffiti" with 36 requests. The average response time for closed graffiti reports was just under 2 hours.

Some service requests were closed very quickly. The average response time for "Park Maintenance" was under 4 minutes, "Street Repair" was about 15 minutes, and "Sidewalk Repair" was about 36 minutes. These unusually fast times may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work. In contrast, the average response time for "Light" repairs was about 11 hours.
