Hustle Long Beach community, here is an independent look at city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to be the most reported issues. There were 6,917 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 8.5 days. Graffiti saw 3,174 reports, taking an average of 2.3 days to address.

Street repairs had 733 requests, averaging 7.2 days for a response. Meanwhile, business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
