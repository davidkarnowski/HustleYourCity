Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. The City of Long Beach received high volumes of service requests, led by 2120 reports for Dumped Items, 1093 for Graffiti, and 992 for E-Scooter issues.

Response times vary significantly by category. For Dumped Items, the average response time was about 4.7 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in 44 hours on average. Trash and Debris concerns took about 4.5 days to resolve. Park Maintenance requests averaged a 4-day response time.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests directly to the city and help keep the community accountable.
