Here's a look at Long Beach's service request data for the last 90 days.

Dumped items (6,156 calls) and graffiti (3,724 calls) were the most frequent issues reported by residents. The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 15 days, while graffiti removal took about 6 days on average.

Some services saw much faster responses. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests were typically closed in under 17 hours. It is important to note that some unusually fast closures, like certain Sidewalk Repair requests closed in about 45 minutes, may be due to administrative updates and not the completion of physical work.
