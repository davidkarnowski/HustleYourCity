Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. The highest volume of citizen reports involved Dumped Items with 6,951 requests, followed by Graffiti with 3,183 requests, and E-Scooter issues with 2,906 requests.

Response times vary significantly by the type of service. The average response time for Dumped Items is currently 8.7 days. Street Repair requests take an average of 7.2 days, while Tree Maintenance takes about 4.7 days. Graffiti removal is faster, averaging a 57.8-hour response time. Dead Animal Pickup averages a 9.8-hour response.

Some categories show extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or immediate referrals rather than actual work completions.
