Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of service calls with 6,997 reports. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 3,252 reports, which saw an average response time of 2.3 days.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant activity. Street repair received 732 calls with an average response time of 7.2 days, while tree maintenance had 901 requests averaging 7.6 days.

On the faster side, the city responded to 463 dead animal pickup requests in an average of 10.8 hours. Business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
