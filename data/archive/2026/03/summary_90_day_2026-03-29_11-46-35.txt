Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest 90-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests.

Over the past 90 days, Dumped Items and Graffiti generated the most service calls. There were 6,904 reports for dumped items, taking an average of 9.4 days to resolve. Graffiti saw 3,115 reports, with an average response time of 64.1 hours.

Street repair requests totaled 858, averaging 6.4 days for a response. Dead animal pickups had 432 calls, handled in about 11.4 hours. Business license and permit concerns averaged a 12-minute response time across 6 calls. Please note that unusually low response times like this often point to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
