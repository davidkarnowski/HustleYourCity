Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how long it takes for the City of Long Beach to address community reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 6,532 reports. The average response time for these issues was approximately 10.6 days. Graffiti removal followed with 3,112 reports, taking an average of 3.2 days to resolve.

Other notable service areas include Tree Maintenance with 882 reports averaging 5.9 days, and Street Repair with 837 calls averaging 5.6 days.

Some services showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup handled 434 reports in an average of 13.3 hours, while Business License concerns averaged just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help keep the city accountable.
