Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

The most common service requests from the public were for illegally dumped items (1,916 calls), e-scooters (967 calls), and graffiti removal (959 calls). These three categories accounted for the majority of all reports made.

Average response times varied by issue. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 2.3 days. Reports of illegally dumped items, the most frequent request type, took an average of about 8.6 days to resolve. Animal-related services were among the quickest, with dead animal pickups handled in about 14 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
