Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a summary of city performance over the last 90 days.

The highest volume of service requests came from Dumped Items, with 6,698 reports. These took an average of 9.5 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,034 calls, averaging a 70-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints reached 2,794, though all were referred to outside agencies rather than handled directly.

Response times varied significantly across different departments. Business License and Permit Concerns were addressed the fastest, averaging just 12 minutes. Dead Animal Pickups were also handled quickly, averaging 11.4 hours. Conversely, Trash and Debris requests took about 7.9 days to process, and Street Repairs averaged 6.5 days. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 23, 2026, 4:53 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 23, 2026, 4:00 AM PDT.
