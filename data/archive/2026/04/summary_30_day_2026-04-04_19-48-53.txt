Here is the latest government accountability update for the Hustle Long Beach! community. An analysis of the City of Long Beach service call data from the last 30 days shows current response times and request volumes.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2,309 requests. The city took an average of 8.3 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue, generating 970 calls with an average response time of 33.5 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 898 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Street Repair requests totaled 218, taking the city an average of 3.4 days to address. Meanwhile, Dead Animal Pickup saw 163 calls and had a much faster average response time of 5 hours. Unusually fast response times across the data may occasionally reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

Stay engaged and help keep the city accountable. If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application to report it directly to the city.
