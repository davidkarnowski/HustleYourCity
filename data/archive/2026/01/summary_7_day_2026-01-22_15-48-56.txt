Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,350 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (512 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (255 requests), and graffiti (217 requests).

According to the data, the average time to resolve a request for trash and debris was about 5 days. Reports of dumped items took around 3.5 days. Graffiti clean-up requests were completed in about 32 hours on average. Animal-related services were among the fastest, with dead animal pickups averaging 5 hours.

Reports for e-scooters are marked as "Closed Referred," which means the city forwards the issue to the private scooter companies to handle.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
