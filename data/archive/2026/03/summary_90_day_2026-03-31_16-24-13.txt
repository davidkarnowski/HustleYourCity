Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service data to keep residents informed about response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue with 6,870 requests. The average response time for these reports is 9.3 days. Graffiti is the second highest category with 3,094 requests, taking an average of 62 hours to address.

Other notable service areas include street repair, which received 848 requests and averaged 6.2 days for a response, and tree maintenance, which saw 926 requests with an average response time of 4.9 days.

Some categories show unusually fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting local issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at March 31, 2026 09:15 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 31, 2026 07:00 AM PDT.
