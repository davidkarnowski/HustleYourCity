In the past 90 days Long Beach residents submitted about 16,000 service requests across 23 categories.

The highest volume came from Dumped Items (6,319 requests) with an average response time of 360 hours (about 15 days). Graffiti reports (3,688) were resolved in roughly 146 hours (6 days). Light, Sidewalk Repair, Tree Maintenance and Traffic Signal each averaged under 2 days, with Light at 41 hours and Sidewalk Repair at 45 hours.

Animal Services (225 requests) and Dead Animal Pickup (455 requests) were the fastest, averaging 17 hours and 16 hours respectively—well under a day.

Overall, the city’s response times vary widely, but many categories are now being closed within a day or two.

Residents can help keep the city accountable by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach app.
