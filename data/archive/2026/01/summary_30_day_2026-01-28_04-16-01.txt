Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 30 days.

In the last month, residents submitted over 6,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,424 requests), graffiti (998 requests), and e-scooter issues (990 requests).

According to the city's data, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.5 days. Street repair requests were addressed in just under 3 days on average. Requests for illegally dumped items, the most frequent issue, had an average completion time of about 7.5 days. E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to the scooter companies, so the city does not provide a response time for these.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city.
