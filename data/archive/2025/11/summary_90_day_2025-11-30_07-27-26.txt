In the past 90 days Long Beach residents reported 3,702 graffiti incidents and 6,152 dumped‑item cases. The city’s average response time for graffiti was about 160 hours (≈ 6.7 days) and for dumped items about 371 hours (≈ 15.5 days).  

Sidewalk repairs, street lighting and tree maintenance saw much faster turn‑around: average response times were 36 hours (≈ 1.5 days), 39 hours and 34 hours respectively. Animal services and dead‑animal pickups were resolved in under 17 hours on average.  

Compared with all‑time averages, response times for tree work (now 34 hours vs 3,763 hours historically) and graffiti (now 160 hours vs 351 hours) have improved dramatically, showing quicker city action on many issues.
