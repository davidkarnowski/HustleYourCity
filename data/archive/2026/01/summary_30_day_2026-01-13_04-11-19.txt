Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days based on the city's public data.

Residents submitted over 5,300 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (1,964 calls), e-scooters (971 calls), and graffiti (922 calls). E-scooter reports are referred directly to scooter companies.

According to the data, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 53 hours. Reports of illegally dumped items took an average of about 5 days to be resolved. Slower responses included requests for trash and debris cleanup, which averaged about one week. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups and animal services calls, both averaging about 14 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
