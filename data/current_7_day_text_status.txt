Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,200 requests were submitted by the community. The most common reports were for dumped items (433 requests), graffiti (260 requests), and e-scooters (256 requests).

For closed requests, the average response time for traffic signal issues was about 3.6 days. Graffiti removal averaged about 41 hours, and dumped item pickups averaged about 65 hours. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of 57 minutes. This unusually fast time may reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical repairs.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it through the Go Long Beach service application.
