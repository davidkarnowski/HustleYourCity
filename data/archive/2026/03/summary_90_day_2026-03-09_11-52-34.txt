Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data to track response times over the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood concerns.

The highest volume of requests came from Dumped Items, with 6,484 reports. The city took an average of 10.9 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported problem with 3,100 calls, which saw an average response time of 3.3 days. E-Scooter complaints also saw high volume with 2,930 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Some services saw rapid turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup had 437 reports with an average response of 13.4 hours. The fastest recorded category was Business License and Permit Concerns, averaging just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. On the slower end, Trash and Debris reports took an average of 8.6 days to address.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps maintain accurate data and accountability for city services.

This data summary was updated at March 9, 2026 4:48 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 9, 2026 4:00 AM PDT.
