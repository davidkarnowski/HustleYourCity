Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 90 days.

Dumped Items and Graffiti continue to be the most frequent service requests, with nearly 6,000 and 3,700 reports filed, respectively. The average response time to resolve a Graffiti report was about 5.5 days, while Dumped Items reports took an average of about 14.5 days.

Response times for other key services include approximately one week for streetlight repairs and about 10 days for street repairs. Animal Services calls were handled much faster, with an average response of about 15 hours. Some service types, like the 2,384 E-Scooter reports, are referred directly to vendors and do not have a city response time.

Note that unusually low response times for certain issues may be due to administrative closures and not always reflect the time for work completion. To report an issue, use the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 27, 2025, at 7:30 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 27, 2025, at 7:00 AM PST.
