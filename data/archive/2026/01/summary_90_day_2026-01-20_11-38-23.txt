Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service response data for the last 90 days.

Over 15,000 service requests were submitted by residents. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (6,025 requests), graffiti (3,392 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,752 requests).

Based on closed cases, the average response time for dumped items was about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of just under 5 days. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups (15 hours) and animal services calls (14 hours). Longer-term projects like marina repairs took an average of about 3.5 weeks to resolve.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
