Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data to keep the public informed on city response times and request volumes.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,656 calls. The city averaged 9.8 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti reports followed with 3,082 calls, averaging a 68.7-hour response time.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant activity. The city received 1,033 reports regarding lights, averaging a 3.2-day response time. Tree maintenance and street repair had 915 and 824 requests respectively, averaging 6.8 and 6.9 days for a response.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city. Staying active in reporting helps maintain accurate data and improves community accountability.
