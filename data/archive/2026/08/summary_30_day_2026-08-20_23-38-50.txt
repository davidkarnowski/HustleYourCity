The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows significant activity in service requests over the past 30 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 2,999 reports filed. Response times for these items averaged approximately 7 days. Graffiti removal saw 1,023 reports with an average response time of about 2 days.

Other notable activity includes 281 requests for tree maintenance, which were addressed in an average of 2.5 days, and 185 reports regarding street lights, which took an average of 2.5 days to resolve. Dead animal pickups continue to be the fastest service category, with 177 reports handled in an average of 5 hours and 16 minutes.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
