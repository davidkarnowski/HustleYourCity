In the last 90 days Long Beach logged 6,427 dumped‑item reports with an average response time of 362 hours (about 15 days). Graffiti calls totaled 3,565 and took 138 hours on average (≈ 6 days) to close.

Animal Services (231 calls) and Dead Animal Pickup (448 calls) were handled much faster, with average response times of 18 hours and 17 hours respectively – under one day from report to closure.

Other services showed mixed performance: Tree Maintenance (822 calls) averaged 59 hours, Light repairs (515 calls) 56 hours, and Street Repair (609 calls) 115 hours. Sidewalk Repair (354 calls) took 80 hours on average, while Shoes on a Utility Wire (36 calls) were resolved in 32 hours.

Overall, the city processed 23,000 service requests across all categories in the past three months. Residents can report new issues or track existing ones through the Go Long Beach service app for faster assistance.
