City service call trends for the last 90 days show both higher volumes and faster fixes in several areas.

- Graffiti reports total 3,693 with an average response of 157 hours (about 6½ days), down from the all‑time average of 353 hours.
- Dumped‑items calls rose to 6,180; average response time is now 369 hours (≈ 15 days), a bit slower than the historic 262 hours.
- Sidewalk repairs (318 calls) are being addressed in 125 hours (≈ 5 days), a dramatic improvement from the historic 4,727 hours.
- Street‑light issues (645 calls) average 193 hours (≈ 8 days), versus the previous 1,014 hours.
- Tree‑maintenance requests (713 calls) are now closed in 156 hours (≈ 6½ days), compared with the historic 3,997 hours.
- Animal‑services (199 calls) and dead‑animal pickups (429 calls) are resolved in under 18 hours on average.
- Trash & debris (158 calls) see an average response of 314 hours (≈ 13 days), improved from the historic 864 hours.
- Reported illegal fireworks (20 calls) are handled in about 139 hours, down from 336 hours.

Overall, many service categories are seeing faster turn‑around times despite increased demand. Residents can continue to report issues directly through the Go Long Beach app for quicker assistance.
