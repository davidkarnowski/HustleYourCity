An evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days highlights current response times and volume for key community issues.

Dumped Items represented the highest volume of service requests with 6,575 total reports. The average response time for these issues was approximately 11 and a half days.

Graffiti removal services received 3,087 reports. The data shows an average completion time of roughly 3 and a half days for these requests.

Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 840 reports with an average response time of 5 days. Tree Maintenance saw similar volume with 844 reports and a response time of 6 days.

Dead Animal Pickup services remained responsive, averaging 14 hours per request across 434 reports.

E-Scooter complaints generated 2,942 reports. The data indicates that 100% of these were closed by referring them to the respective scooter operators rather than being handled directly by city crews.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
