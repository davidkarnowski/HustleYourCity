Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days. This is an independent review and is not associated with the City of Long Beach government.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,451 reports filed. The average response time for these issues is currently 326 hours, or approximately two weeks.

Graffiti removal saw 3,191 reports with an average response time of roughly four days. Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 773 and are being addressed in about five days on average.

Dead Animal Pickup services cleared 413 reports with an average response time of 11 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues.
