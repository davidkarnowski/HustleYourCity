Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the city's response times and call totals over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 6,738 reports. The city took an average of 9.7 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,075 calls, averaging a 71.5-hour response time.

Infrastructure requests like Tree Maintenance (940 reports) and Street Repair (835 reports) averaged response times of 6.7 days and 6.6 days, respectively.

Some categories show exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Download and use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests directly to the city.
