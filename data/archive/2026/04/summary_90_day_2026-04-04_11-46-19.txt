Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual breakdown of call totals and average response times for the most frequent community concerns.

Dumped items generated the highest volume with 7057 reports, taking an average of 9 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3143 calls, averaging a 59-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 2866 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies without tracked completion times.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests showed varying timelines. The city received 996 reports for lighting issues, averaging a 70-hour response. Street repairs totaled 818 requests with an average response time of nearly 7 days. Tree maintenance saw 922 requests, taking about 5 days on average to address.

Some services recorded much faster turnarounds. Dead animal pickups, totaling 447 requests, averaged a 10-hour response. Business license and permit concerns averaged just 12 minutes to close across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help improve local neighborhoods.
