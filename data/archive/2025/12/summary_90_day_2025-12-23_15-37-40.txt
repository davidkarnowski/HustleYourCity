Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti were the most reported issues, with over 6,000 and 3,700 service requests respectively. The average response time for dumped item removal was about two weeks. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about six days on average. Street repair requests took about 10 days.

Services with quicker response times included dead animal pickups (about 16 hours) and animal services calls (about 15 hours). Some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
