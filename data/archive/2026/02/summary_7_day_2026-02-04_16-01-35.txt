Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,380 service requests were made by the public. The most common reports were for dumped items (564 requests), graffiti (253 requests), and e-scooter issues (242 requests).

The city's data shows the average response time for dumped items was about 1.4 days, while graffiti reports were addressed in about 1.5 days. Reports for street repair, sidewalk repair, and tree maintenance were all handled in approximately 13 to 14 hours. E-scooter reports are referred directly to vendors and are not handled by city staff.
