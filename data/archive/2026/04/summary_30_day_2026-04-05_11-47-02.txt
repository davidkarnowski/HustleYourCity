Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data to track how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs. Over the past 30 days, several key trends have emerged in call volumes and response times.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of reports with 2,309 requests. The average response time for these issues is currently about 8 days. Graffiti reports followed with 977 requests and an average response time of 33 hours. E-scooter concerns were also highly active, totaling 930 reports.

Other notable service areas include street repair, which received 215 reports and averaged a 3-day response time. Dead animal pickups accounted for 164 reports with an average response time of 5 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative ticket closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps hold agencies accountable and ensures the data reflects actual neighborhood conditions.
