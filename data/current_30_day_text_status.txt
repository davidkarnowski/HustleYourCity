Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past month, the most reported issues were "Dumped Items" with 1,983 requests and "Graffiti" with 1,156 requests. Street repair and street light issues were also frequently reported by residents.

Average response times varied by issue. The city closed requests for "Dumped Items" in about one week on average. "Street Repair" requests took an average of four days to resolve.

Other services saw quicker responses. The average time to close a "Graffiti" report was about 64 hours. Reports for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were handled in an average of about 10 hours.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 17, 2025, at 8:01 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 17, 2025, at 7:00 PM PST.
