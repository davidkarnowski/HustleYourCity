Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days.

Over the past three months, residents submitted nearly 16,000 service requests. The most common issues reported were illegally dumped items (6,024 reports), graffiti (3,454 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,664 reports).

The average time to close a service request varied by type. Reports for dumped items took an average of about 13 days to resolve. Graffiti removal requests were completed in about 4.7 days on average, while street repairs took an average of 6.6 days. Some services saw quicker responses, with dead animal pickups being resolved in about 16 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
