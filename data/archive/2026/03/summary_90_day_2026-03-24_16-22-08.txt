Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items received the highest volume of calls with 6,756 reports. The average response time for these issues was about 9.5 days.

Graffiti removal saw 3,069 requests, averaging a 68-hour turnaround. E-scooter complaints reached 2,844, though all were referred to outside agencies or closed administratively.

Infrastructure requests like street repair and tree maintenance averaged 6.5 days and 6.6 days to resolve, with 847 and 962 reports respectively.

The fastest recorded response was for business license and permit concerns, averaging just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
