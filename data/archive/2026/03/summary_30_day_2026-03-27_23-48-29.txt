Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs.

Over the past month, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 2,277 calls. The city average response time for these issues was about 7 days. Graffiti followed with 992 reports, taking an average of 1.7 days to address.

Other notable service requests include 903 E-Scooter complaints, which are typically referred out immediately, and 251 Street Repair reports, which averaged a 3-day response time. Dead Animal Pickup saw 154 reports and averaged a 9-hour turnaround. 

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not necessarily actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to stay engaged and report local issues using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at March 27, 2026, at 4:45 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 27, 2026, at 4:01 PM PDT.
