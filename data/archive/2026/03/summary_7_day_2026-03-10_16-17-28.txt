Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest 7-day city service response data to keep the community informed. Here is a look at how the city handled recent service requests.

Over the past week, Dumped Items led the service requests with 469 reports, averaging a 68-hour response time. Graffiti was the second most common physical issue with 228 reports, taking an average of 32 hours to address, with 154 of those already closed. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 243 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Some services saw rapid turnaround times. Running Water reports averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. However, please keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickups averaged 10.5 hours across 36 requests, and Animal Services averaged 7.4 hours for 16 requests.
