Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days, based on the latest public data.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests during this period. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (6,024 requests), graffiti (3,390 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,752 requests).

The data shows the average time to close a request for illegally dumped items was about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in an average of about 5 days. Requests for dead animal pickups and animal services were among the quickest, with average response times of about 15 and 14 hours, respectively. Longer wait times were seen for marina repairs, which took an average of about 3.5 weeks to resolve.
