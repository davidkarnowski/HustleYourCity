Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days to keep citizens informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped items were the most reported issue this past week with 530 requests. The city took an average of 3.6 days to respond to these calls. Graffiti was the second most frequent issue with 226 reports, which saw an average response time of 19.6 hours.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Restroom maintenance requests averaged a 50-minute response, and reports of shoes on a utility wire averaged 44 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Other notable service requests included 196 e-scooter complaints, which were referred and closed without tracked response times, and 60 street repair reports that averaged a 31.4-hour response.

Help hold the city accountable and keep local neighborhoods clean. Followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
