Hustle Long Beach! is providing another third-party review of the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Looking at the past 7 days, here is a breakdown of how city departments are responding to community reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 516 reports. The city average response time for these requests is currently 3.6 days. Graffiti removal follows with 206 reports and an average response time of 24.5 hours.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which saw 72 reports and an average response time of 24.9 hours. Dead Animal Pickup handled 46 reports, closing all of them with an average response time of 4.9 hours. 

Some response times were exceptionally fast. Restroom Maintenance recorded an average response of 35 minutes for a single report. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
