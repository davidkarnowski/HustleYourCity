Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of reports across various city services. Here is a factual breakdown of the most significant call totals and average response times.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,975 reports, taking an average of 8.8 days to resolve. Graffiti followed closely with 3,170 reports and an average response time of 2.4 days. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 2,863 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests required longer response windows. Street Repair received 767 calls, averaging 7 days for a response. Sidewalk Repair saw 351 reports with an average turnaround of 5.2 days. Tree Maintenance had 915 requests, taking about 4.9 days on average.

Certain categories showed much faster processing. Dead Animal Pickup handled 450 calls with an average response of 9.9 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.
