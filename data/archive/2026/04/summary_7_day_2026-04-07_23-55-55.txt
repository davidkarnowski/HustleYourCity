Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day service call data to keep you informed on City of Long Beach response times. Here is a look at the most recent numbers and how quickly the city is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 502 requests. The city averaged a response time of 3.1 days to process these issues. E-Scooter complaints followed with 276 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti removal saw 271 requests, taking an average of 45.6 hours to address.

Other notable service areas included 79 reports for Light issues, which took an average of 64.4 hours, and 42 Sidewalk Repair requests averaging an 8.6-hour response. Dead Animal Pickup handled 35 calls with a 2.7-hour average response. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports averaged just over 3 days for a response.

Running Water reports showed a highly rapid response time of just 78 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
