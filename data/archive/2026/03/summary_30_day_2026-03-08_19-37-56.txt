Here is the latest independent review of Long Beach city service data covering the last 30 days.

Dumped Items remained the most frequent request with 2,050 total reports. The average response time for these issues was approximately 5 days. Graffiti reports totaled 1,077, with an average response time of just under 2 days.

Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 265 reports with an average completion time of roughly 3 days. Tree Maintenance saw 446 reports and a response time of nearly 2 days.

The fastest response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup and Abandoned Shopping Carts. These services averaged 14.5 hours and 16 hours respectively.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
