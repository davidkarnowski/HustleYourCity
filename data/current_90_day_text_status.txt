In the last 90 days Long Beach recorded about 16,453 service calls across all categories.

Animal Services (233 calls) and Dead Animal Pickup (461 calls) were resolved quickly – average response times were just 18 hours and 17 hours respectively, often within the same day.

More complex issues took longer: Dumped Items (6,480 calls) averaged 380 hours (about 16 days) before closure, while Graffiti (3,633 calls) averaged 131 hours (roughly 5.5 days). Tree Maintenance (816 calls) and Light repairs (541 calls) saw average responses of 57 hours and 50 hours.

Overall, response times vary widely by service type, but many requests are now being handled in under a day. Residents can track and report issues instantly through the Go Long Beach app.
