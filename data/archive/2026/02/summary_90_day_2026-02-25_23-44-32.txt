An evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days highlights current response times for high-volume issues. Dumped Items remains the most frequent report type, with 6,570 total requests filed. The average time to resolve these requests is currently approximately 12 and a half days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,121 for this period, with an average completion time of nearly 4 days. Infrastructure data shows that Street Repair requests are taking about 4 and a half days to resolve, while Street Light issues are averaging roughly 3 days for completion.

Faster response times were recorded for animal-related concerns. Dead Animal Pickup requests averaged roughly 12 and a half hours, and general Animal Services requests were closed in approximately 13 and a half hours.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city has accurate data on service needs.
