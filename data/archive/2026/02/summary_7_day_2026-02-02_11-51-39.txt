Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Illegal dumping was the most reported issue with 573 service calls. The average response time for these requests was about 56 hours. Graffiti was the second most common report with 249 calls, which were addressed in about 19 hours on average.

Response times for other common issues include street repair at about 17 hours and dead animal pickup at around 13 hours. Some requests, like sidewalk repair, showed an average response of about 6 hours, though this may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
