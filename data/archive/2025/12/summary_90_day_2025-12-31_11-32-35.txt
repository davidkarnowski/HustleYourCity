Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 90 days, based on the city's public data.

Over the past three months, residents submitted thousands of service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,893 calls), graffiti (3,606 calls), and illegally parked e-scooters (2,482 calls).

According to the data, the average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 5 days. For dumped items, the average response time was about 2 weeks. Street light repairs took approximately 1 week on average to complete.

Some services saw faster resolutions. Reports for dead animal pickups were closed in about 16 hours on average, while abandoned shopping carts were handled in about 40 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most direct way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
