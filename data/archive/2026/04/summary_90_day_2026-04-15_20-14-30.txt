Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest third-party accountability update on city service response times. An analysis of the service call data from the last 90 days has been completed to keep you informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue with 6,937 calls in the past 90 days. The city average response time for these items is currently 8.6 days. Graffiti is the second most common issue with 3,242 reports, seeing a much faster average response time of 2.3 days.

Other notable service requests include Tree Maintenance with 895 reports taking an average of 8.7 days, and Street Repair with 711 reports taking an average of 7.8 days to address.

On the faster end, Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make your voice heard. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
