Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest city service data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled service requests over the past 7 days.

Dumped items and graffiti led the request totals. The city received 527 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 56.49 hours. Graffiti followed with 325 reports and an average response time of 16.68 hours. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 287 reports, nearly all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Infrastructure and maintenance crews stayed busy. Street and sidewalk repairs saw 41 and 34 requests respectively, both averaging around 10 to 11 hours for a response. Tree maintenance had 49 calls with an average response of 9.22 hours. Dead animal pickups totaled 42 calls with an average response time of 15.84 hours.

Some categories showed very fast turnaround times. Vegetation issues averaged 4.91 hours, and restroom maintenance averaged 4.30 hours. Park maintenance saw 12 calls with an average response of 1.69 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
