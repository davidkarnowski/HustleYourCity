The latest 90-day data summary for Long Beach city services highlights response times for the most frequent community reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,442 reports. The average time to resolve these requests is currently 13.5 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,159. The average response time for removal is roughly 4 days.

Street Repair requests saw 789 total entries, with an average completion time of 5 days.

Dead Animal Pickup services responded to 410 calls with an average response time of 11 hours.
