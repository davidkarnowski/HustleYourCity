Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,400 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (648 calls), graffiti (282 calls), and e-scooter issues (187 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 26 hours. For dumped items, the average was just under 22 hours. Reports of abandoned shopping carts were closed in an average of 39 minutes. Note that unusually fast response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures and not on-site work completion.

To report non-emergency issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at February 7, 2026, 11:36 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at February 7, 2026, 11:00 AM PST.
