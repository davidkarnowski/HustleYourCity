Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service requests from the past week. City departments handled 1,098 new service requests, with the most common reports for dumped items (387), graffiti (184), and e-scooters (165).

Looking at average response times for closed requests, graffiti removal took about 2.5 days. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 1.5 days. Some requests were handled much faster, with reports of shoes on a utility wire closed in about 44 minutes.

The average response time for sidewalk repair was unusually low at about 4 minutes. This may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
