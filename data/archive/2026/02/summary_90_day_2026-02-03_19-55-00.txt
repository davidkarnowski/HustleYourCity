Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (6,293 calls), graffiti (3,277 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,714 calls).

The average time to resolve a request for dumped items was about 2 weeks. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 5 days on average, with street repairs taking a similar amount of time.

Faster responses were recorded for more urgent issues. Reports for dead animal pickup and other animal services were typically closed in about 12 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
