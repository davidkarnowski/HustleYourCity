Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the last 90 days.

Over the past three months, residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,888 requests) and graffiti (3,553 requests).

The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 2 weeks (338 hours). Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 4.7 days (113 hours). Faster responses were recorded for issues like dead animal pickup (about 16.5 hours). Longer waits were seen for park maintenance, which averaged about 17 days to resolve.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
