Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update for the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,500 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items, with 654 calls, and graffiti, with 282 calls.

The average response time for dumped items, trash and debris, and streetlight repairs was around 3 days. Reports of shoes on utility wires took the longest to address, averaging over 3.5 days.

Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 15 hours on average. Graffiti cleanup requests were addressed in about 32 hours. Note that some response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app. Your reports help provide the data needed to track city performance.
