Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items, with 6,030 calls, and graffiti, with 3,395 calls.

The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 12.5 days. For graffiti removal, the average was about 5 days. Response times varied for other services, with dead animal pickups averaging about 15 hours and marina repairs averaging about 3.5 weeks.

If you see an issue that needs attention, reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
