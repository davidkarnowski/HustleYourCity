Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data to keep residents informed about response times over the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue with 6,949 requests. The average response time for these reports is 8.5 days. Graffiti is the second most common issue with 3,261 reports, which the city is addressing in an average of 55 hours.

Infrastructure requests show longer wait times. Street repairs, which had 711 reports, are taking an average of 7.8 days to address. Tree maintenance requests total 893 and take about 8.1 days on average. Sidewalk repairs, with 375 reports, are averaging 5.9 days for a response.

Some services show very fast turnaround times. Dead animal pickups average a 10-hour response across 463 reports. Business license and permit concerns show an average response of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times, especially those under 90 minutes, may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help keep the city accountable.
