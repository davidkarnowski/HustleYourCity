Here is the latest city service data summary for the Hustle Long Beach! community, looking at the past 7 days of service requests.

The highest volume of reports over the last week involved Dumped Items with 449 requests. This was followed by Graffiti with 286 requests and E-Scooter issues with 266 requests.

Response times varied significantly depending on the issue. Dumped Items took an average of 3.3 days to process. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports averaged 3.1 days, and Damaged Drinking Fountain reports took about 4 days. Graffiti reports were handled in an average of 45.8 hours.

Some categories showed very fast turnaround times. Running Water reports averaged just 78 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues and help keep the city informed.
