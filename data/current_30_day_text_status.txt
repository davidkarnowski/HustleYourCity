Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 30 days.

Illegal dumping and graffiti were the most reported issues, with 1,888 and 1,122 service calls respectively.

Response times varied by service type. The average time to address reports of dumped items was about 8 days. Street repair requests were closed in about 5 days on average, while tree maintenance took about 4 days. Graffiti reports were handled more quickly, with an average response time of 54 hours.

Some of the fastest responses were for dead animal pickup and animal services, both averaging under 10 hours. Note that rapid closures can sometimes be due to administrative processing rather than immediate fieldwork.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
