Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data. In the past month, residents submitted over 6,100 requests for city services. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (2,544 requests), graffiti (1,104 requests), and e-scooters (1,011 requests).

Response times varied by the type of service requested. For dumped items, the most common issue reported, the average time to close a request was over 8 days. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickups were resolved in about 11 hours on average. Reports of damaged drinking fountains took nearly 11 days to be addressed.

To report issues like graffiti, potholes, or dumped items, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone. This is the most direct way to alert city departments.
