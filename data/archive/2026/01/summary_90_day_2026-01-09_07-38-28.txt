Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days.

Illegally dumped items remain the most reported issue with over 5,900 requests. The average response time to close these cases is about 2 weeks. Graffiti is the second most common report with over 3,500 requests, which are addressed in about 4.5 days on average.

Response times for other common issues include about 1 week for street repairs and under 5 days for reports about street lights. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 17 hours. Some issues show unusually fast response times, such as a 2-hour average for damaged drinking fountains. These may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
