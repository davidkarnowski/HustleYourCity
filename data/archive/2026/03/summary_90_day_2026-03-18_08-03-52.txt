Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how long it takes for the city to respond to citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 6,674 calls in the last 90 days. The average response time for these reports is currently about 10 days. Graffiti is the second most common issue, totaling 3,102 reports, with an average response time of nearly 70 hours. 

Some services show incredibly fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. However, residents should keep in mind that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead animal pickups are also handled relatively quickly, averaging 11.7 hours for 437 calls.

Help keep the city accountable and neighborhoods clean. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
