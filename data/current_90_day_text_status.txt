In the last 90 days Long Beach recorded 6,473 dumped‑item reports and 3,560 graffiti calls, the two highest volumes of any service type. Other notable totals include 354 sidewalk‑repair requests, 489 lighting issues, 829 tree‑maintenance tickets and 228 animal‑service reports.

Average response times vary widely. Animal services and dead‑animal pickups were handled quickly, averaging about 18 hours and 16.5 hours respectively. Light and tree‑maintenance requests took roughly 2.5 days, while sidewalk repairs averaged about 4.2 days. More complex jobs such as dumped‑item clean‑ups and graffiti removal took longer, with averages of about 15 days and 6.5 days. Very low response times for a few categories may reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

Residents can help the city track and address issues faster by reporting directly through the Go Long Beach service app.
