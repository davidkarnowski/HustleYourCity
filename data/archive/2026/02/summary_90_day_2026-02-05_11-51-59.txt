Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data, focusing on requests made in the last 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests during this period. The most reported issues were for dumped items, with 6,390 requests, and graffiti, with 3,291 requests.

Average city response times varied by issue. Graffiti reports were resolved in about 5 days on average. The average response for dumped items was nearly 14 days. Some of the fastest responses were for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 12 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the best way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
