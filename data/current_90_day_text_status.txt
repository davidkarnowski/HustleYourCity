In the past 90 days Long Beach recorded about 16,400 service calls across all categories.

Key response‑time highlights:
- Graffiti requests are now closed in an average of 6 days (144 hours), down from over 14 days historically.
- Dumped‑items reports average 15 days (355 hours) to close.
- Sidewalk repairs, light outages, tree work and animal‑services are being handled in just 3‑5 days (84 hours, 57 hours, 62 hours and 19 hours respectively), a dramatic improvement from the multi‑week averages seen before.
- Most other services (vegetation, traffic signals, restroom maintenance, running water, trash & debris) are now resolved within 3‑5 days.

Overall, many city services that once took weeks are now responding within hours to a few days, showing faster action for residents.

Residents can report new issues instantly through the Go Long Beach app, helping keep response times short.
