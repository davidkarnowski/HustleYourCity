Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest city service response data to keep the community informed. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled service requests over the last 30 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue across the city. Citizens submitted 2,322 reports for dumped items, which saw an average response time of nearly 8 days. Graffiti followed with 1,000 reports, averaging a response time of about 1.5 days. 

Other common requests include street repairs and park maintenance, both of which are currently averaging a 3-day response time. On the faster end, dead animal pickups are averaging just under 7 hours. It is important to note that unusually fast response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures or referrals rather than actual work completions.

Accountability starts with reporting. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to submit issues directly to the city.
