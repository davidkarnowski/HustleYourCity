Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests and response times.

Over the past 90 days, the most common issues reported by residents were for dumped items (5,963 calls), graffiti (3,491 calls), and e-scooters (2,704 calls).

Average response times for closed requests varied by service type. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 4.5 days. For dumped items, the average response time was about 13 days. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 15.5 hours on average. Some services show unusually low response times, which may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.

Help keep the city informed by reporting issues you see. You can submit a service request using the Go Long Beach application on your smartphone.
