Hustle Long Beach! is here with the latest weekly breakdown of city service response times. Over the past 7 days, residents submitted hundreds of requests, and the data shows exactly how the City of Long Beach handled them.

Dumped items were the most reported issue this week with 518 calls. The city averaged a 39-hour response time to address these reports. Graffiti was the second most common issue, generating 218 reports with an average response time of nearly 20 hours. E-scooter complaints also remained high, with 148 reports that were all referred to outside agencies.

Response times varied significantly depending on the service. Dead animal pickups were handled quickly, averaging just over 3 hours for 38 requests. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. On the slower side, marina repairs took the longest, averaging 3.7 days to resolve.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted and fixed. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
