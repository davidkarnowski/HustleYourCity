In the last 90 days Long Beach recorded about 16,441 service requests. The most common issues were Graffiti (3,607 calls) and Dumped Items (6,473 calls).

Average response times show where work is moving quickly and where delays remain. Graffiti requests took about 5.5 days to close, Dumped Items about 15.6 days, Sidewalk Repair 3.2 days, Light repairs 2.1 days, and Tree Maintenance 2.4 days. Very low response times sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed work, so they may not indicate actual field activity.
