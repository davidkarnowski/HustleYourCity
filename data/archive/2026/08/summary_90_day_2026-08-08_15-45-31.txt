The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows significant activity in service requests over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 7,474 reports logged. Graffiti removal saw 3,298 reports with an average response time of approximately 3 days.

Dead animal pickup continues to be the most efficient service category, maintaining an average response time of about 5 hours. Conversely, reports for dumped items are currently taking an average of about 8 days to resolve. Please note that unusually low response times in recent data may reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach mobile application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that service requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
