Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data to track how long it takes for reported issues to be resolved. Here is a look at the performance numbers over the last 90 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 6838 reports. The city took an average of 9.3 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported problem, totaling 3099 requests with an average response time of 62 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests showed varying response times. Street repairs totaled 855 requests and took an average of 6.2 days to complete. Tree maintenance saw 925 reports, averaging 4.9 days for a response. Sidewalk repairs took about 3.8 days across 326 reports.

Some services were addressed much faster. Dead animal pickups averaged a 10.6-hour response time for 436 reports. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep the city accountable. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
