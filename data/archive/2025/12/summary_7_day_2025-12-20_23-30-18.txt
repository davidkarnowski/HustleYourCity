Here is the latest weekly summary of Long Beach city service response data.

Over the past 7 days, residents submitted 429 reports for Dumped Items, making it the most frequent request. The average response time for these reports was about 38 hours. Graffiti was the next most common issue with 228 reports, which were addressed in an average of about 24 hours.

Some service requests were closed very quickly. Sidewalk Repair and Restroom Maintenance reports were closed in an average of 45 minutes and 51 minutes, respectively. These unusually fast times may be due to administrative closures and not always on-site work completion.

If you see an issue that needs attention, from graffiti to dumped items, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 20, 2025, 3:28 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 20, 2025, 3:02 PM PST.
