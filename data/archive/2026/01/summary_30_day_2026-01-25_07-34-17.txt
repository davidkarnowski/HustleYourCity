Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the last 30 days, the most common reports from residents were for illegally dumped items (2,313 requests), e-scooter issues (976 requests), and graffiti removal (954 requests).

Based on average response times for closed cases, reports of dumped items were addressed in about 8 days. Graffiti removal requests were handled in under 3 days, and street repairs took about 3 days. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups and animal services calls, which were both closed in under 12 hours on average.
