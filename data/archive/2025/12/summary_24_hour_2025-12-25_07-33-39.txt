Here is the latest look at Long Beach service request data from the past 24 hours. A total of 85 new service requests were made by residents.

The most common issues reported were for dumped items (33 requests), tree maintenance (19 requests), and graffiti (16 requests). For the dumped item cases closed in this period, the average response time was about 2.3 hours. All 16 graffiti reports are still new or in progress.

Some services saw very fast closure times. The average for closed tree maintenance requests was about 57 minutes, and street repairs were closed in about 45 minutes. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. In contrast, the average response for a closed street light issue was about 7 hours.
