Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day city service response data to keep the community informed on local government performance.

Over the past week, Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 461 requests. The city averaged a response time of about 52 hours for these issues. E-Scooter complaints followed with 255 reports, all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies. Graffiti was the third most reported issue, logging 226 calls with an average response time of 21 hours.

Some categories showed unusually fast response times. Running Water reports averaged a response time of just 13 minutes. Residents should note that extremely low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Other notable areas included Tree Maintenance with 68 reports and Street Repair with 59 reports. Both categories averaged response times of approximately 18 hours. 

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help maintain the city.

This data summary was updated at March 9, 2026, at 9:11 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 9, 2026, at 8:00 AM PDT.
