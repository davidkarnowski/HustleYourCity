Here is the latest review of City of Long Beach service requests for the past 30 days.

The highest volume of requests continues to be for Dumped Items, with 2,186 total reports. The average response time for these issues is currently approximately 5.4 days.

Graffiti reports were the second most common active service category with 1,112 requests. The average time to resolve these incidents is roughly 1.9 days.

Infrastructure repairs show varied timelines. Street Repair requests are averaging 3.1 days for a response, while Sidewalk Repairs are taking approximately 2.1 days. Tree Maintenance is currently averaging 1.7 days.

Faster response times were noted for Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 14 hours per request.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
