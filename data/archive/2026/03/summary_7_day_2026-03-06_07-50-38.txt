The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows significant activity in waste and maintenance requests over the last 7 days. The most frequent service request was for Dumped Items, with 499 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 71 hours.

Graffiti removal also saw high volume with 237 reports filed. The average time to resolve these issues was 32 hours. E-Scooter reports remained high at 239, though the majority of these are closed by referring them to the scooter operators rather than city crews.

Infrastructure repairs showed faster completion times this week. Street Repair requests averaged 18 hours for a response, while Tree Maintenance issues were resolved in an average of 14 hours. Dead Animal Pickup services averaged 22 hours per request.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues. Consistent reporting helps generate the data needed to track city performance.
