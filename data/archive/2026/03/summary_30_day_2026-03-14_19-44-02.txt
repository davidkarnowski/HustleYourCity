Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 30-day city service response data to keep you informed on how quickly issues are being addressed in the community.

Over the past month, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of calls with 2,139 reports. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,054 calls, averaging a 44-hour response time.

Other notable service times include Street Repair, which had 264 reports and took about 3 days to address, and Tree Maintenance, with 485 reports averaging a 52-hour response.

Some categories showed unusually fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
