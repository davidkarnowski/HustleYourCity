Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service request was for "Dumped Items", with over 6,300 reports filed. The average time to close these reports was about 13.5 days. The second most common report was for "Graffiti", with over 3,300 reports. These were addressed in about 5 days on average.

Other key service response times include Street Repair (about 5.5 days), Light repair (about 4.5 days), and Tree Maintenance (about 4 days).

Some services saw much faster resolutions. Reports for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were closed in about 13 hours on average. It is possible some unusually fast response times are due to administrative closures and not on-site work completion.
