Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service call data.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (6,195 calls) and Graffiti (3,381 calls).

Average response times for closed requests varied by service. Dumped Items took about 13 days to resolve, while Graffiti reports were addressed in about 5 days. Street repair requests were closed in an average of about 5.7 days. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests show very fast closure times of under 15 hours, which may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
