Here is your latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 30 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (1,915 requests), graffiti (1,058 requests), and e-scooter issues (1,036 requests).

On average, the city took almost 6 days to resolve reports of dumped items. Graffiti removal requests were addressed more quickly, with an average response time of just over 2 days. Street repairs took about 3 days on average.

It is important to note that all 1,036 e-scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies for handling, not resolved by city staff.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 22, 2025, 11:28 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 22, 2025, 11:00 AM PST.
