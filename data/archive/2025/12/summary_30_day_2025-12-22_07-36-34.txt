Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data from the last 30 days.

The most frequent requests were for Dumped Items (1,909 reports), Graffiti (1,062 reports), and E-Scooters (1,034 reports). The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 6 days, while graffiti removal requests were closed in about 2.5 days. E-scooter reports are referred to outside agencies and do not have a city response time.

Other key service times include Street Repair (198 requests) at about 3.5 days and streetlight issues (230 requests) at about 2 days. Some of the quickest responses were for Dead Animal Pickup, averaging about 13 hours, and Animal Services, averaging about 10.5 hours. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
