Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest third-party accountability update on City of Long Beach service response times.

Over the past 24 hours, the city saw significant call volumes for several case types. The most reported issues were Dumped Items with 54 calls, E-Scooters with 51 calls, and Graffiti with 47 calls. Dumped Items had an average response time of about 3 hours.

Some service categories reported exceptionally fast average response times. Park Maintenance requests averaged just 12 minutes, Tree Maintenance averaged 30 minutes, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 84 minutes, and Street Repair averaged 86 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Civic engagement helps keep the city accountable. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track neighborhood concerns.
