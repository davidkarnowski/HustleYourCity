Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest independent look at city service response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items lead the service requests with 7,006 reports. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti removal saw 3,260 reports, with an average response time of about 2.3 days. 

Infrastructure requests like Tree Maintenance with 902 reports and Street Repair with 723 reports are taking an average of 7.6 days and 7.2 days to address, respectively.

Some categories show unusually fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
