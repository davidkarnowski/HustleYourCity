The latest data from the City of Long Beach service call system shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,941 requests for dumped items, which remains the highest volume category, with an average response time of approximately 14 days and 19 hours.

Graffiti removal saw 3,085 reports with an average response time of 3 days and 11 hours. E-scooter reports totaled 3,053, though these are primarily categorized as closed referred, indicating they are handled by external vendors.

Response times for urgent matters remain efficient. Dead animal pickups averaged 5 hours and 23 minutes, while animal services requests averaged 11 hours. Vegetation maintenance requests were addressed in an average of 34 minutes.

Please note that unusually low response time averages in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
