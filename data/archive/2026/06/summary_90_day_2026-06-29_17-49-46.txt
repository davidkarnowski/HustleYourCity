The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 6,593 reports for dumped items, which remains the highest volume category. The average response time for these items was approximately 8 days.

Graffiti removal saw 3,351 reports with an average response time of about 3 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, with dead animal pickups averaging roughly 6 hours and animal services averaging about 10 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. Using the app helps ensure that requests are tracked and directed to the appropriate departments for resolution.
