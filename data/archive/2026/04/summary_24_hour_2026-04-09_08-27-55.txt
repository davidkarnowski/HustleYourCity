Here is the latest Long Beach city service data summary from the Hustle Long Beach! accountability project, focusing on the past 24 hours of service requests.

Over the last day, Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 62 new reports, though none have recorded completion times yet. Graffiti followed with 34 total cases and an average response time of 3 hours.

Several service categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Animal Services averaged 49 minutes, Tree Maintenance and Vegetation both averaged 51 minutes, Sidewalk Repair averaged 57 minutes, and Trash & Debris averaged 88 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times under 90 minutes may be due to administrative closures or duplicate merging rather than actual physical work completions.

Dead Animal Pickup requests averaged a response time of 4.25 hours across 5 total cases.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at April 9, 2026 1:25 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 8, 2026 11:00 PM PDT.
