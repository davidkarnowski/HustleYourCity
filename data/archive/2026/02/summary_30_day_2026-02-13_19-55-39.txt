Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times for the last 30 days.

The highest volume of service requests involved Dumped Items, with 2,467 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately one week. Graffiti removal received 1,118 reports, with crews responding in roughly 2 days on average.

Street Repair saw 234 requests with an average completion time of 3.5 days. Dead Animal Pickup services managed 152 reports with a response time of about 8.5 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
