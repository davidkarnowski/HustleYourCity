Here is your weekly summary of Long Beach city service data. In the last seven days, residents submitted over 1,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (392), e-scooters (227), and graffiti (218).

The average city response time to close a graffiti report was about 30 hours. Dumped item pickups were completed in an average of 36 hours, and dead animal pickups were handled in about 6.6 hours.

Some requests were closed very quickly. For example, park maintenance and restroom maintenance reports were closed in an average of 59 minutes and 51 minutes, respectively. It is possible these unusually fast times are due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
