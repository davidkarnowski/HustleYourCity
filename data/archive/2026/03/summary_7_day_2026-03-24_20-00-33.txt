Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data to keep the community informed. Here is a look at how city services performed over the past 7 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 491 calls, taking an average of 57 hours to address. E-Scooter complaints followed with 234 reports. Graffiti was the third most reported issue, with 196 calls and an average response time of 24 hours.

Response times varied significantly depending on the issue. Abandoned shopping carts took the longest to resolve, averaging nearly 7 days for 13 reports. On the faster side, the city responded to dead animal pickups in an average of 3.4 hours across 42 calls. Community center issues saw the fastest turnaround, averaging just 72 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to stay active in maintaining the community. Please use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
