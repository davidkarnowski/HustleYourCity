Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data to keep citizens informed on city response times and request volumes.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 102 reports, averaging a response time of about 6 hours. Graffiti reports were the second most common with 27 requests, showing an average response time of 3.4 hours.

Several service categories showed exceptionally fast average response times. Street Repair requests averaged 18 minutes, Park Maintenance averaged 22 minutes, Tree Maintenance averaged 32 minutes, Vegetation averaged 44 minutes, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 53 minutes, and Animal Services averaged 61 minutes. 

Citizens should note that unusually low response times, particularly those under an hour, may be due to administrative closures or reassignments rather than the actual completion of physical work.
