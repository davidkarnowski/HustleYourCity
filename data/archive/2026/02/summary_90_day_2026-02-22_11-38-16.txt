An evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days highlights current response trends for high-volume categories. Dumped Items received the highest volume with 6,452 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 13 days.

Graffiti enforcement recorded 3,100 reports with an average resolution time of 4 days. Infrastructure data shows that Street Repair requests are being addressed in roughly 4.5 days, while Light repair issues are averaging 3.5 days to close. Tree Maintenance requests are currently averaging just over 6 days for completion.

Faster response times were noted for Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 11 hours. Please note that unusually low response times in certain categories may be the result of administrative closures rather than on-site work completions.

Residents are encouraged to report service issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach application.
