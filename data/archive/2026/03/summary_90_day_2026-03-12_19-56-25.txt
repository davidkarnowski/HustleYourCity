Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public requests.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue with 6,589 calls. The average response time for these requests is currently 10.4 days. Graffiti is the second most common issue with 3,096 reports, taking an average of 3.2 days to address.

Infrastructure requests like Street Repair and Sidewalk Repair received 839 and 288 calls respectively. Street repairs are averaging a 5.8-day response time, while sidewalk repairs are taking about 3.9 days. 

Some categories show exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Public reporting is essential for government accountability. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
