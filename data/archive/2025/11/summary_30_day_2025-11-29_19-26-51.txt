The latest 30‑day service report shows a mixed picture for Long Beach.

Graffiti reports remain the highest volume at 1,328 requests, with an average response time of about 73 hours (just over 3 days). Dumped Items also top the list with 1,845 calls, but they take longer to close—average 206 hours (about 8½ days).

Some categories are being addressed quickly. Tree Maintenance requests (210 total) are resolved in an average of 10 hours, and Dead Animal Pickup (137) in about 14 hours. Animal Services (54) and Street Repair (197) also see average responses under a day.

Other services show longer waits: Report Illegal Firework (5 calls) took an average of 204 hours, and Trash & Debris (50) averaged 65 hours.

Overall, most issues are being handled within one to two days, but high‑volume items like Dumped Items and Graffiti still need faster turnaround.

Residents can help by reporting issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app.
