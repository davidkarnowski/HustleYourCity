Here is the latest 90-day snapshot of Long Beach city service requests.

The most frequent issue reported by residents was "Dumped Items," with over 6,300 requests. The average time to resolve these cases was about 13.5 days. The second most common issue was "Graffiti," with over 3,300 reports, which were addressed in about 5 days on average.

Requests for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" had the fastest closure times, averaging around 13 hours. Some rapid closures can be due to administrative processing and may not always reflect the time for work to be completed. Over 2,600 reports about E-Scooters were automatically referred to scooter companies.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
