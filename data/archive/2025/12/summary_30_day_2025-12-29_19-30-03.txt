Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 30 days.

Reports for dumped items, e-scooters, and graffiti were the most common issues submitted, with nearly 4,000 requests combined.

The latest data shows the average response time to close a request for dumped item removal is about 10 days. For traffic signal issues, the average was nearly 4 days.

In contrast, requests for animal services were addressed in about 9 hours on average, while graffiti removal took a little over 2 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.

This data summary was updated at 11:27 AM PST on December 29, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 AM PST on December 29, 2025.
