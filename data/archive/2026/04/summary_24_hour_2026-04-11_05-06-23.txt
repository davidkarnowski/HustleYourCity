Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest 24-hour accountability update on city service requests and response times.

Over the past day, Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 117 reports, though most remain in the new status. Graffiti followed closely with 78 reports and an average response time of 2.8 hours.

Tree Maintenance showed the fastest turnaround, averaging just 24 minutes for 4 requests. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

On the higher end of the 24-hour spectrum, Street Repair calls averaged 7.9 hours to process across 6 reports. Meanwhile, Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup saw a combined 15 reports, all of which were closed with an average response time of about 3.3 hours.
