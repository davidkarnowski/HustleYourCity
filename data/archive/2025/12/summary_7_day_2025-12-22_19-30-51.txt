Here is a look at Long Beach service request data for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1141 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (430 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (245), and graffiti (225).

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 23.5 hours, while reports for dumped items took an average of 32.7 hours. A single restroom maintenance request was closed in 51 minutes, though unusually low times can be due to administrative closures and not work completion.
