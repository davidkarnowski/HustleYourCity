Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

The most frequent requests from the public were for dumped items (5,833 calls) and graffiti removal (3,549 calls). The average response time to close a dumped item request was about 2 weeks. For graffiti removal, the average response was about 5 days.

Response times for other common issues included street light repairs, which took an average of 5.5 days, and street repairs, which averaged about 9 days. Quicker responses were seen for animal services and dead animal pickups, both averaging under 17 hours to close a request.

To report issues like these, residents can use the Go Long Beach service application.
