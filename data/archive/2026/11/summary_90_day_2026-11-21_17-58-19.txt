The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,364 requests for dumped items and 2,586 reports regarding graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 7 minutes.
