Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data from the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at the most recent numbers and response times to keep you informed about local government performance.

Over the past day, Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 88 total reports, though 78 of those currently remain in the "New" status. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 53 reports, all of which were closed and referred to outside operators. Graffiti reports also remained active with 23 calls, averaging a response time of just under 3 hours.

Several categories showed exceptionally fast response times over the last 24 hours. Light issues averaged a response time of 40 minutes, Animal Services averaged 69 minutes, and Running Water concerns averaged 84 minutes. Please note that these unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or system referrals rather than the actual completion of the physical work.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
