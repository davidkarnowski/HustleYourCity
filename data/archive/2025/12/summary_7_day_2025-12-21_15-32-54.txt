Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,150 service requests were made by the community. The most reported issues were for dumped items (428 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (238 requests), and graffiti (234 requests).

The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 36 hours. Tree maintenance requests were addressed more quickly, with an average closure time of under 5 hours. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of 45 minutes. Unusually fast closures may be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at 7:30 AM on December 21, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 7:00 AM on December 21, 2025.
