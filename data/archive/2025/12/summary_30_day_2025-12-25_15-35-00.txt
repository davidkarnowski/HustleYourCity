Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests. In the past month, residents submitted nearly 5,000 service requests. The top three issues reported were Dumped Items (1,926 reports), Graffiti (1,028 reports), and E-Scooters (1,021 reports), making up over 80% of all requests.

Here are the average response times for closing some of the most common service requests:
- Dumped Items: about 8 days
- Graffiti: about 2.5 days
- Street Repair: about 2.5 days
- Animal Services: about 9.5 hours
Some service requests may show unusually fast completion times, which can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work.

If you see an issue that needs attention, from potholes to park maintenance, the best way to let the city know is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
