Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted over 1,280 requests for service. The most common reports were for dumped items (478 calls), e-scooters (249 calls), and graffiti (179 calls).

According to the city's data, the average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 34 hours. Dumped items were addressed in an average of 44 hours, and street repair requests were closed in about 19 hours. The average response time for streetlight issues was longer, at about 3.5 days. E-scooter issues are referred directly to the scooter companies, so the city does not report a response time for those requests.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to notify the city.
