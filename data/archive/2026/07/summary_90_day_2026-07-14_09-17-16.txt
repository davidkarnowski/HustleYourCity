The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 6,845 reports, followed by 3,407 e-scooter reports and 3,293 graffiti reports.

Average response times for closed requests vary by service type. Dead animal pickups are being addressed quickly, averaging about 5 hours. Some service categories show very low response times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
