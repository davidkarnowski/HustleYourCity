Here is our latest analysis of Long Beach city service data, focusing on requests made in the last 90 days.

Over the past three months, residents submitted nearly 16,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (over 5,900 reports) and graffiti (nearly 3,500 reports).

Average response times for closed requests varied by issue. Reports for dumped items took about 13 days to resolve, while graffiti removal averaged just under 5 days. Slower responses included marina repairs, which averaged about one month. Faster responses included animal-related services, which were typically handled in about 15 hours.

It is important to note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than the physical completion of work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to get it on the city's radar is to report it through the Go Long Beach app.
