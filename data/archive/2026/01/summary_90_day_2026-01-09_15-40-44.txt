Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

Over this period, residents submitted more than 15,700 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (5,902 requests), Graffiti (3,538 requests), and E-Scooters (2,631 requests).

The average time to resolve a Graffiti report was about 4.5 days. For Dumped Items, the average response time was approximately 2 weeks. Park Maintenance requests were also closed in about 2 weeks on average. Some of the quickest responses were for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services, both averaging under 17 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
