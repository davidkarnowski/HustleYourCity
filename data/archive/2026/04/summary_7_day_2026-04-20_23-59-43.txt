Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest third-party accountability update evaluating City of Long Beach service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports this week with 550 calls. The average response time for these issues was 3.4 days. E-Scooter complaints followed with 220 reports, all of which were closed and referred. Graffiti was the third most reported issue with 189 calls and an average response time of 23.9 hours.

Trash and Debris reports took the longest to address, averaging 3.9 days for 13 calls. Dead Animal Pickup had one of the fastest response times, averaging 2.4 hours across 40 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues to the city.
