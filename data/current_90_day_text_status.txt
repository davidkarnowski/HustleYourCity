Over the past 90 days Long Beach handled 6,635 dumped‑item reports, 3,540 graffiti cases, 855 tree‑maintenance requests and 474 lighting issues.

The longest average response time was for dumped items at 351 hours – about 14.6 days. Graffiti took an average of 169 hours (≈7 days) and street‑repair requests averaged 135 hours (≈5.6 days).

Fastest services were animal‑related: animal services responded in an average of 18 hours and dead‑animal pickups in 16.7 hours, both well under a day.

Overall, 2,366 service calls were closed in this period, with the highest volumes in dumped items, graffiti, tree maintenance and sidewalk repair.
