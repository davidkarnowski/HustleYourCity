Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times for the past 90 days based on the latest public data.

Illegal dumping and graffiti remain the most frequent service requests from residents. Over the last three months, the average response time for dumped items was about 13 days. Graffiti removal took an average of 5 days to complete.

Requests for street repair averaged just under 6 days. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups and animal services calls, which were typically handled in under 15 hours. In contrast, marina repairs had the longest average wait time at nearly 25 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app. Please note that some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
