Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service request data to keep you informed on how long it takes for local issues to be addressed.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,931 reports. The average response time for these issues was 220 hours, which translates to just over 9 days. 

Graffiti removal teams handled 3,100 cases over the last three months. The average response time was 61.6 hours, meaning it took about two and a half days to resolve these reports.

Street repair crews responded to 844 calls, taking an average of 148.9 hours, or about 6 days, to address each issue. Sidewalk repairs saw 337 requests, averaging 95.2 hours, or about 4 days, for a response.

Some services showed much faster turnarounds. Dead animal pickups totaled 441 requests with an average response time of 10.4 hours. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. Encourage your friends and neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 1, 2026, 5:00 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 1, 2026, 3:00 AM PDT.
