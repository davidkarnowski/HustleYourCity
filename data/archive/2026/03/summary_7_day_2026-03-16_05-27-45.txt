Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled citizen reports over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 576 calls. The city averaged a response time of 53 hours for these issues. Currently, 320 of these reports remain in the new status.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue, bringing in 211 calls. The average response time for graffiti removal was 16.5 hours.

E-Scooter complaints saw 181 reports. All of these were immediately closed and referred to a third party. Please note that unusually low response times or immediate closures are often due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Other notable response times include Dead Animal Pickup, which handled 35 calls with an average response of 4.4 hours, and Tree Maintenance, which saw 63 reports and an average response time of 9 hours. Traffic Signal issues were addressed the fastest, with 6 reports averaging a 2.5-hour response time.

Citizens are encouraged to stay active in improving the community. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
