Hustle Long Beach! presents the latest review of City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a factual summary of city service response times and call totals over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,352 reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 8 days.

E-Scooter complaints and Graffiti were the next most frequent issues, with 1,054 and 1,041 reports respectively. Graffiti reports saw an average response time of about 2.3 days.

Infrastructure requests included 237 Light issues averaging a 4-day response, 215 Street Repair reports averaging nearly 3 days, and 164 Sidewalk Repair reports averaging about 2 days.

Dead Animal Pickup had 171 reports with an average response time of 6.7 hours. Please note that unusually low response times across any category may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Reporting problems helps ensure the community gets the attention it needs.

This data summary was updated at April 17, 2026, 1:51 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 16, 2026, 11:00 PM PDT.
