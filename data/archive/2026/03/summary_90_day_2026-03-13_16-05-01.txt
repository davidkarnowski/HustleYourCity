Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response times over the past 90 days to keep the community informed.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues. There were 6,587 reports for dumped items, which took an average of 10 days to resolve. Graffiti saw 3,086 reports with an average response time of 3 days. Street repairs had 836 reports and took an average of 6 days to address.

Some services saw much faster turnarounds. Dead animal pickups, totaling 438 reports, were handled in about 13 hours. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 13, 2026, 8:57 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 13, 2026, 8:00 AM PDT.
