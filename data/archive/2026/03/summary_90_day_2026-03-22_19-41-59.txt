Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data from the past 90 days. Here is a factual look at response times and call volumes for the City of Long Beach.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,714 reports. The city averaged 9.6 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti reports followed closely with 3,054 requests, which took an average of 70.9 hours to address.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant volume. The city received 945 tree maintenance requests, averaging 6.7 days for a response, and 838 street repair requests, averaging 6.5 days. E-scooter complaints totaled 2,818, though all were referred to outside agencies rather than handled directly by city staff.

Some services showed rapid turnaround times. Dead animal pickups averaged 11.5 hours across 441 requests. Business license and permit concerns averaged a 12-minute response time for 6 requests. It is important to note that unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help track responsiveness.
