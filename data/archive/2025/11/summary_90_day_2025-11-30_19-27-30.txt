In the last 90 days Long Beach recorded about 15,700 service calls across all categories.

The biggest volumes were:
- Dumped Items: 6,138 reports, average response ≈ 15 days (370 hours)
- Graffiti: 3,676 reports, average response ≈ 6.6 days (159 hours)
- Tree Maintenance: 763 reports, average response ≈ 1.4 days (34 hours)
- Light issues: 631 reports, average response ≈ 1.6 days (39 hours)
- Sidewalk Repair: 318 reports, average response ≈ 1.5 days (36 hours)

Other categories such as Animal Services (226 reports) and Dead Animal Pickup (438 reports) were resolved in under 24 hours on average. Some very quick closures (e.g., abandoned carts at 0.6 hours) may reflect administrative processing rather than field work.

Overall, response times have improved for many services, staying well under the 72‑hour threshold for most requests.

Residents can continue to report issues and track progress using the Go Long Beach service app.
