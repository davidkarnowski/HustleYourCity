Here's the latest look at Long Beach service request data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,190 requests) and graffiti (3,718 requests). According to the city's data, the average response time to close a dumped item report was about 15 days, while graffiti reports were closed in about 6 days on average.

Response times for other services varied. Reports for street light outages were addressed in about one week, while tree maintenance requests took just over 6 days. More urgent requests, such as dead animal pickups, were handled much faster, with an average completion time of about 17 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.

This data summary was updated at December 19, 2025, at 3:28 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 19, 2025, at 3:03 PM PST.
