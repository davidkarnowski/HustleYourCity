Here's the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most common issues reported were illegally dumped items (6,017 requests) and graffiti (3,444 requests). Average response times for these top requests were about 13 days for dumped items and 4.5 days for graffiti removal.

Some services saw much quicker responses. Reports for dead animal pickup were resolved in an average of about 16 hours. In contrast, marina repair requests took the longest, averaging about one month. Note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures and not work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
