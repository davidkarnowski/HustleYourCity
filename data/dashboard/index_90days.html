Current Status Update
Here is an independent review of Long Beach city service requests over the last 90 days. This summary evaluates the volume of calls and the average time it takes for the city to close these requests. The highest volume of service requests continues to be for Dumped Items, with 6,460 total reports. The average response time to resolve these issues is currently approximately 12 days and 20 hours. Graffiti removal is the second most frequent request type with 3,097 reports. The data shows an average completion time of just under 4 days. Infrastructure maintenance shows steady activity. There were 1,000 reports regarding streetlights, with an average repair time of about 3 and a half days. Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 797 reports and are being resolved in approximately 4 and a half days. For sanitation concerns, Dead Animal Pickup received 427 reports and maintains a faster response average of about 13 hours. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach application to report maintenance and safety issues. Accurate reporting helps track the efficiency of city services.
