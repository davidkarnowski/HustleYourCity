Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the past week.

The most frequent requests from residents were for "Dumped Items" with 609 calls and "Graffiti" with 259 calls. The average response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 2.5 days. For "Graffiti" reports, the average was about 18 hours.

Other key service response times include "Street Repair" at about one day and "Abandoned Shopping Carts" at about two days. Faster responses were seen for "Dead Animal Pickup" (under 6 hours) and "Animal Services" (about 3 hours). It is worth noting that some quick closures may be administrative and not reflect on-site work completion.
