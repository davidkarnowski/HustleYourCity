The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,351 reports, followed by 3,299 graffiti reports and 3,194 e-scooter reports.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup continues to be the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 5 minutes. Conversely, dumped items are currently averaging about 8 days to resolve. Please note that very low response times, particularly those under 90 minutes, often reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work.
