Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,460 service requests were made in the last seven days. The most common reports were for dumped items (567 calls), improperly parked e-scooters (262 calls), and graffiti (221 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 24 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in an average of just under two days. Some issues were handled very quickly, such as vegetation requests, which were closed in an average of about 61 minutes. A single restroom maintenance request was closed in 16 minutes; such fast closures can sometimes be due to administrative processing.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, reporting it is the first step to getting it fixed. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit a request to the city.
