Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data to bring you an updated accountability report. Over the past 90 days, citizens have actively reported neighborhood issues, and the data reveals how long it takes for the city to respond.

The most frequently reported issue was Dumped Items, with 6,855 calls. The average response time for these requests was about 9.5 days. Graffiti was the second most common issue, totaling 3,093 reports, which took the city an average of 2.7 days to address. Street Repair also saw significant activity with 864 requests and an average response time of 6.5 days.

Some categories showed incredibly fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting creates the data needed to track performance and improve the community.
