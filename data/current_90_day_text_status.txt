Over the past 90 days Long Beach logged roughly 16,400 service requests. The highest volumes were Dumped Items (6,468 reports) and Graffiti (3,607 reports), followed by Tree Maintenance (819) and Sidewalk Repair (356).

Average response times ranged from about 15.6 days for Dumped Items to 5.5 days for Graffiti and Park Maintenance. Sidewalk Repair closed in roughly 3.3 days, while Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were resolved in about 18 hours and 17 hours respectively. Very short times may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

Residents are encouraged to report new issues through the Go Long Beach service app.
