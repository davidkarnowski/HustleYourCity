The latest data for city service requests in Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,288 reports for dumped items, which remains the highest volume category. Graffiti reports followed with 3,314 total requests.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 5 hours over the last 90 days. Conversely, dumped items and park maintenance requests have seen average response times exceeding 8 days. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
