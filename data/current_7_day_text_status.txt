In the past week Long Beach logged 1,282 service requests across many categories. Most response times dropped dramatically compared with earlier periods. Graffiti tickets were closed in an average of about 25 hours (down from over 50 hours a month ago), and Dumped Items were addressed in roughly 23 hours, a big improvement from the 100‑plus hours seen before.

Fastest turn‑arounds were seen for Tree Maintenance (≈6 hours), Sidewalk Repair (≈6 hours), and Vegetation (under 2 hours). Even minor issues like Running Water were resolved in about 25 minutes. Several other services—Light, Street Repair, Traffic Signal, Trash & Debris, Report Illegal Firework and Shoes on a Utility Wire—all fell under a day, often under 12 hours.

A few areas took longer: Animal Services averaged about 31 hours and Dead Animal Pickup about 29 hours, which is slower than recent averages. These longer times may reflect administrative processing rather than on‑ground work.

Residents are encouraged to report problems quickly through the Go Long Beach service app so the city can keep response times improving.
