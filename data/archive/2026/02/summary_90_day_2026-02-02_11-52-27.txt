Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past 90 days, the most frequent requests from the public were for "Dumped Items" (6,281 reports) and "Graffiti" (3,300 reports). These two categories accounted for the majority of service calls.

Response times varied by issue type. The average time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 13.5 days. In contrast, "Graffiti" reports were resolved in an average of about 5 days, while "Animal Services" calls were handled in about 13 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at 3:49 AM PST, Feb 2, 2026 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 3:00 AM PST, Feb 2, 2026.
