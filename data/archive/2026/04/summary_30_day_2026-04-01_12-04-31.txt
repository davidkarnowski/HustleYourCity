Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a summary of service requests and response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 2,317 requests. The average response time for these issues was 8 days.

Graffiti removal saw 956 requests, with the city averaging a 37-hour response time.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 886. All of these cases were referred to outside agencies or closed without a recorded response time.

Street Repair had 233 requests, taking an average of 3 days to address.

Park Maintenance received 84 requests, with an average response time of 4.7 days.

Dead Animal Pickup handled 166 requests in an average of 7 hours.

Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 1, 2026, 5:00 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 1, 2026, 3:00 AM PDT.
