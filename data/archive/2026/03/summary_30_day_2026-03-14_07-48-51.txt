Here is the latest 30-day city service data summary for the Hustle Long Beach! community.

Over the past month, the most frequently reported issues were Dumped Items with 2,138 calls, Graffiti with 1,060 calls, and E-Scooter complaints with 1,039 calls. 

Response times varied significantly depending on the type of service. Dumped Items took an average of 5 days to process, while Street Repair requests took an average of 3 days. Park Maintenance requests averaged 4 days, and Marina Repairs took about 7 days. For issues with faster turnarounds, Graffiti was addressed in about 43.5 hours, and Dead Animal Pickups averaged 14.4 hours.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
