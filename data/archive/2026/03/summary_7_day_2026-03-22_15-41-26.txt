Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data to keep you informed about how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood issues. Here is a look at the service call numbers and response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 520 reports. However, 421 of these remain in the new status, with the average response time for addressed items sitting at about 37 hours. Graffiti reports followed with 212 calls and a solid average response time of 18.8 hours.

Some services showed very rapid turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup averaged a 3-hour response across 38 reports, and Animal Services averaged just over 7 hours. Please note that unusually low response times can sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

On the longer end, Marina Repair requests took an average of 3.7 days to process. Street Repair saw 55 reports with an average response time of 22.4 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets on the board. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
