Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests based on city data.

Over the past month, residents submitted more than 5,000 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (1,931 requests), graffiti (1,029 requests), and e-scooter issues (1,021 requests).

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. Dumped item cleanups took an average of about one week. Graffiti removal requests were completed in a little over two days. The average response time for tree maintenance was about one day.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at December 25, 2025, 3:28 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 25, 2025, 3:00 AM PST.
