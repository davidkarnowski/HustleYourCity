Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day city service data to keep citizens informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed. Here is a look at the numbers and response times for some of the most frequently reported concerns.

Dumped items remain the highest volume issue with 6,639 reports over the last 90 days. The city took an average of 9.8 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti reports followed with 3,084 calls, showing an average response time of 69 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw high volumes. Tree maintenance generated 912 reports with an average response time of 6.8 days, while street repair had 828 reports taking an average of 6.5 days to resolve.

On the faster side, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual field work completions.

Citizen reports make a difference in how city resources are allocated. Hustle Long Beach! encourages all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep Long Beach neighborhoods in top shape.
