Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,200 requests were submitted through the Go Long Beach app and other channels. The most common reports were for dumped items (432 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (259), and graffiti (242).

Based on city data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 26 hours. For dumped items, the average response was about 40 hours. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of about 3 hours. Some unusually fast closures, which may be administrative, were also noted.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
