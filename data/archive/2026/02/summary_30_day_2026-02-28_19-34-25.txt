The latest data on Long Beach city services for the Last 30 Days reveals current trends in call volumes and response times.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2,222 total calls. The average response time for these requests is approximately 6 days. Graffiti reports followed with 1,106 total calls and an average response time of 2 days.

Street Repair requests showed an average response time of nearly 4 days across 246 reports. Dead Animal Pickup services averaged roughly 12 hours per response.

Reports regarding Business Licenses and Permit Concerns show an average response time of 12 minutes. Unusually low response times such as this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
