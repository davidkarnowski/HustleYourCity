Here is the latest 90-day service request data summary from Hustle Long Beach! evaluating how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 6,651 reports. The city's average response time for these issues is currently 10 days.

Graffiti removal remains a major focus with 3,064 requests. The average response time for graffiti reports is 71 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw high volumes. Tree Maintenance received 911 calls with an average response time of over 6 days. Street Repair had 830 requests, also averaging a response time of over 6 days.

Some categories show extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or automatic referrals rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help keep the community accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 16, 2026 4:46 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 16, 2026 4:00 PM PDT.
