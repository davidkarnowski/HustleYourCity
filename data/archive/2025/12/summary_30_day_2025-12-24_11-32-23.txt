Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Over 5,100 requests were submitted by residents. The most reported issues were dumped items (1,952 reports), illegally parked e-scooters (1,072 reports), and graffiti (1,058 reports).

Response times varied by issue. The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 5.5 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed more quickly, with an average completion time of just over 2 days. Some services, like Animal Services, had an average response time of under 10 hours. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach app to ensure it is logged and tracked by the city.
