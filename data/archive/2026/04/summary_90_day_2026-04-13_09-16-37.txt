Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day data on city service response times. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public requests.

Over the last 90 days, Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 6,918 reports. The city took an average of 8.4 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti removal followed closely with 3,253 reports, averaging a response time of 55 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests require more time. Tree Maintenance saw 901 reports with an average response time of 7.6 days, while Street Repair had 713 reports averaging 7.1 days. Sidewalk Repair requests took about 5.3 days to address across 359 reports.

Some services show rapid turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup handled 464 reports in an average of 10 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Note that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Your reports help track performance and maintain neighborhood standards.

This data summary was updated at April 13, 2026 2:08 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 12, 2026 11:00 PM PDT.
