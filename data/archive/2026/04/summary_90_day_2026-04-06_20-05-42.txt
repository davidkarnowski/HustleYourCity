Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest independent data summary of Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 6,969 reports. The city averaged a response time of 8.8 days for these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,179 calls, which saw an average response time of 58 hours. E-Scooter complaints totaled 2,875, though all were referred to outside entities, leaving no trackable city response time.

For infrastructure issues, Street Repair received 760 reports with an average response time of 1 week. Tree Maintenance had 910 calls, taking about 4.8 days on average to address. Sidewalk Repair saw 356 reports with an average response time of 5.2 days.

Animal-related services showed faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup had 452 reports with an average response of 9.9 hours, while general Animal Services handled 247 calls in an average of 15.3 hours.

Some categories recorded unusually fast resolution times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

To help improve local neighborhoods, citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 6, 2026, 12:58 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 6, 2026, 11:00 AM PDT.
