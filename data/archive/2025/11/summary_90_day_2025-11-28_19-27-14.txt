Latest 90‑day snapshot shows 15,775 service requests across 23 categories.

Graffiti reports dropped to 3,689 with an average response time of 162.7 hours, down from the historic 351 hours. Dumped Items total 6,171 with an average of 368 hours, higher than the historic 261 hours. Light, Tree Maintenance and Sidewalk Repair now average 39 hours, 38 hours and 36 hours respectively, a big improvement from historic averages of 1,011 hours, 3,763 hours and 4,704 hours.

Animal Services (224 requests) and Dead Animal Pickup (438 requests) are being handled quickly, with average response times of 17 hours and 16.6 hours.

Overall, response times are trending faster for many services, helping the city address issues more promptly.

Residents are encouraged to report problems through the Go Long Beach service app.

This data summary was updated at 11:25 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 AM PST.
