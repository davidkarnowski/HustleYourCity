Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,100 requests were submitted through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (423 requests), e-scooters (234 requests), and graffiti (225 requests).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 27 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 36 hours on average. Dead animal pickups were completed in an average of about 9 hours. Some requests, like a single restroom maintenance call closed in about 51 minutes, may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to notify the city is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
