Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times for the past week.

Over the last 7 days, the most common service requests were for illegally dumped items (328 reports), graffiti (189 reports), and e-scooter issues (178 reports).

The average response time to close a report for dumped items was about 29 hours. For graffiti, the average response was about 26 hours. A faster response was seen for dead animal pickups, which took an average of 7.6 hours to complete.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the best way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 27, 2025 at 8:15 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 27, 2025 at 7:00 PM PST.
