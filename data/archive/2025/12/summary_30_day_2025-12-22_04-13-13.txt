Long Beach Service Data Update: The Last 30 Days

In the past 30 days, Long Beach residents submitted over 5,000 service requests through the city's reporting system. The most frequent issues reported were illegally dumped items (1,910 requests), graffiti (1,061 requests), and e-scooter issues (1,034 requests).

Here is a look at the average city response times for closed service requests during this period:
- Dumped Items: about 6 days
- Street Repair: about 3.5 days
- Graffiti: about 2.5 days
- Tree Maintenance: about 36 hours
- Dead Animal Pickup: about 12.5 hours

It is important to note that some service requests may be closed for administrative reasons, which can result in response times that do not reflect the completion of physical work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at December 21, 2025 at 8:10 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 21, 2025 at 7:00 PM PST.
