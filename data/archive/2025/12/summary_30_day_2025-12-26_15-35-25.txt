Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests.

In the past month, the most common reports from residents were for dumped items (1,896 requests), e-scooters (1,056 requests), and graffiti (1,009 requests).

Average response times for closed cases varied. Graffiti removal took about 2.5 days, while dumped item pickups averaged around 7.5 days. Faster responses included dead animal pickups at about 13 hours and animal services calls at roughly 9.5 hours. Some service types show unusually fast resolutions, which may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.

To report issues like graffiti, potholes, or dumped items, use the Go Long Beach service application.
