Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1125 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (423 requests), illegally parked e-scooters (234 requests), and graffiti (226 requests).

The average response time to close a graffiti report was 27 hours. For dumped items, the average response was about 36 hours. Dead animal pickups were handled in an average of about 9 hours. The single restroom maintenance request filed was closed in 51 minutes; such a quick response may be due to an administrative closure.
