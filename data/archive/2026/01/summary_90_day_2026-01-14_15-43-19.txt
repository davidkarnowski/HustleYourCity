Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data, focusing on requests made in the last 90 days.

In the past 90 days, the most common service requests from residents were for illegally dumped items, with 5,988 reports filed, and graffiti removal, with 3,465 reports.

Average response times for closing service requests varied. Reports for dumped items were closed in about 13 days, while graffiti removal took an average of just under 5 days. Sidewalk repair requests were resolved in about 4 days. Some of the longest response times were for marina repairs, which took about one month to close. Faster responses included animal services requests, which were closed in about 14 hours on average. Note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.
