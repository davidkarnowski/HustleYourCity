Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the past week.

Illegally dumped items were the most common issue reported, with 663 requests. The average response time to close these cases was just over 3 days. Graffiti was the second-most reported issue with 286 requests, which were resolved in about 32 hours on average.

Response times for street light outages also averaged just over 3 days. Dead animal pickups were handled in an average of about 15 hours. The city received 225 reports about e-scooters, which are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated on January 29, 2026, at 3:46 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 29, 2026, at 3:00 AM PST.
