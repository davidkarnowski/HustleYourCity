Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the past 90 days based on public data.

Residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests during this period. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (6,014 requests), graffiti (3,383 requests), and street light issues (852 requests).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 4.8 days. Reports for dumped items took an average of about 13 days to resolve. Faster responses included animal services issues, which were handled in about 14 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
