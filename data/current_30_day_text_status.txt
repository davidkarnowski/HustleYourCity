Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 30 days.

Residents submitted nearly 5,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,925 requests), graffiti (1,135 requests), and e-scooters (778 requests).

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" request was about 8.5 days. Graffiti removal requests were closed in about 59 hours on average. Some of the quickest responses were for "Dead Animal Pickup," which were handled in about 8.5 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at Dec 13, 2025, 12:25 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at Dec 13, 2025, 11:00 AM PST.
