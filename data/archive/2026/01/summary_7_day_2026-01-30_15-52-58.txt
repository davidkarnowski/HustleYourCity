Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,500 requests were submitted through the Go Long Beach system. The most common report was for Dumped Items, with 626 requests. The average response time to close these requests was about 2.7 days.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 276 requests, which were addressed in about 1.3 days on average.

Reports of running water took the longest to resolve this week, averaging nearly 6 days. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickup and tree maintenance were typically closed in under 18 hours. Note that some requests, like the 254 for E-Scooters, are referred directly to private companies, so the city does not track their final resolution time.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
