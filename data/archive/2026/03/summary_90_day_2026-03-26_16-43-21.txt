Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Here is a summary of city performance and response times over the last 90 days.

The highest volume of citizen requests involved Dumped Items with 6879 reports. This was followed by Graffiti with 3095 reports and E-Scooter issues with 2822 reports. 

Response times varied significantly depending on the type of service. Dumped Items took an average of 9.5 days to resolve, while Street Repair requests averaged 6.5 days. Graffiti issues were addressed in an average of 66.2 hours. 

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. It is important to note that extremely low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to help maintain the community by reporting issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
