Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the past 30 days.

In total, residents submitted over 6,100 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,432 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (1,041), and graffiti (1,032).

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 62 hours. For illegally dumped items, the average resolution time was about 9 days. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickups were typically resolved much faster, in about 11 hours. Reports for e-scooters are forwarded directly to the scooter companies for handling.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
