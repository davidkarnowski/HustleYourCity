Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. According to the latest data, residents submitted over 1,400 reports through the Go Long Beach app and other city channels.

The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (607 requests), Graffiti (267 requests), and E-Scooter issues (213 requests). These three categories made up more than 75% of all service requests in the last seven days.

Based on the data for closed cases, the average time to resolve a Graffiti report was about 17 hours. Reports for Dumped Items took an average of 62.5 hours to be closed, while Dead Animal Pickup requests were completed in an average of 5.6 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is an effective way to notify the city.
