Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days, based on the latest public data.

Over the past month, residents submitted more than 5,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,971), graffiti (1,127), and e-scooters (908).

Average city response times for some of the top service categories were:
- Graffiti: about 2.5 days
- Street Repair: about 4 days
- Dumped Items: about 6 days

Animal-related services were among the fastest, with most requests closed in under 13 hours. All 908 e-scooter reports were referred directly to the responsible companies.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 18, 2025, 3:29 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 18, 2025, 3:03 PM PST.
