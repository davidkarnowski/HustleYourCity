Here is a look at Long Beach service request data for the past 30 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were for dumped items (1,882 calls), e-scooters (965 calls), and graffiti (912 calls). Reports about e-scooters are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling, not handled by city staff.

Average city response times for closed requests varied. Dumped item pickups took about 5.5 days. Graffiti removal averaged about 2.2 days. Requests for trash and debris removal took longer, averaging over a week to resolve.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
