Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with over 6,000 requests submitted. The average response time for these requests was about 13 days. Graffiti was the second most common report, with an average cleanup time of about 5 days.

Faster responses were recorded for urgent issues. The average time for dead animal pickup was about 15 hours, and for animal services calls it was about 14 hours. Some categories, like damaged drinking fountains, show unusually fast closure times of around 2 hours, which may be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
