Here's a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 90 days.

Dumped Items and Graffiti were the most reported issues, with over 5,800 and 3,500 calls respectively. The average response time to close a dumped items request is currently about two weeks. For graffiti removal, the average is about five days.

Some services show faster response times. Dead animal pickup requests were resolved in an average of about 16 hours. Street repair requests took about eight days on average to complete. Over 2,600 reports for e-scooters were referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
