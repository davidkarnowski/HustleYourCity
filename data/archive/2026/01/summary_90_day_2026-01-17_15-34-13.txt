Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the last three months, the most common reports from residents were for dumped items (6,058 reports), graffiti (3,444 reports), and e-scooters (2,718 reports). The average response time for graffiti removal was about 4.5 days, while dumped item cleanup took an average of just under 13 days.

Some services saw quicker responses. Dead animal pickups were completed in about 16 hours on average, and abandoned shopping carts were addressed in around 40 hours. In contrast, requests for marina repairs had the longest average completion time at over three weeks.

It is important to note that some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.
