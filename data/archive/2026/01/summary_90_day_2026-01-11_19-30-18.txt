Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 90 days.

Over 15,000 service requests were submitted by residents during this period. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,939 requests), graffiti (3,511), and e-scooter issues (2,615).

Based on city data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 4.5 days, while dumped item pickups averaged around 13.5 days. Faster responses included dead animal pickups and animal services calls, which were typically closed in under 16 hours. Slower requests, like marina repairs, took an average of about one month to complete. Some services show very fast closures, which may be due to administrative processing and not on-site work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
