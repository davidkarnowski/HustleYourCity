Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests.

In the past month, the most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (1,822 requests), e-scooters (1,005), and graffiti (889).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 54 hours. For dumped item pickups, the most common request, the average completion time was about 8 days. Reports for trash and debris took an average of 9 days to close. E-scooter issues are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.
