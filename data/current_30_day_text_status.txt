The latest city service data for the past 30 days shows significant activity across several categories. Dumped items remain a high-volume concern with 2,367 total reports, of which 663 have been closed. Graffiti removal continues to be a priority, with 627 of the 859 reports successfully closed.

Response times vary by service type. Animal services and dead animal pickups are seeing quick turnarounds, both averaging under 8 hours. Conversely, business license and permit concerns are showing longer processing times, averaging about 13 days. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that your concerns are tracked and addressed efficiently.
