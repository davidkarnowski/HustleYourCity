In the past 90 days Long Beach residents filed 6,471 dumped‑item reports, 3,677 graffiti complaints, 815 tree‑maintenance requests and 548 light‑service tickets. Overall, the city logged 201 park‑maintenance jobs and 349 sidewalk‑repair cases.

Fastest average response times were seen for animal‑related services: Animal Services averaged 18 hours and Dead Animal Pickup averaged 17 hours. Light repairs were also quick, with an average of 52 hours (about 2 days) to address issues.

The longest waits were for dumped items, taking 376 hours on average (roughly 15.7 days), and graffiti, averaging 130 hours (about 5.4 days). Sidewalk repairs and park maintenance averaged 71 hours and 140 hours respectively.

Residents can help speed up service by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach app, which routes requests directly to the appropriate department.
