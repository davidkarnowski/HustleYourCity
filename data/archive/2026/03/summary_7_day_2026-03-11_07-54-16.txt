Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data from the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community requests.

Dumped items were the most reported issue, with 488 requests this past week. The city took an average of 3.2 days to respond to these reports.

Graffiti and e-scooter complaints also saw high volumes. There were 225 graffiti reports with an average response time of 33.6 hours, and 242 e-scooter reports.

Some services showed incredibly fast turnaround times. For example, 5 running water reports averaged a response time of just 13 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets tracked. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
