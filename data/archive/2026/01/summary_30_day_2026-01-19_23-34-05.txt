Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past month, the most reported issues were for dumped items (2,053 calls), e-scooter issues (988 calls), and graffiti removal (899 calls). E-scooter reports are referred to third-party operators and do not have a city response time.

For requests closed by the city in the last 30 days, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 51 hours. Dumped item pickups averaged about 7 days, and street repairs took about 3 days. Quicker responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 13 hours.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
