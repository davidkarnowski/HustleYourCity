Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents made thousands of service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,032 requests), e-scooters (982), and graffiti (895).

For requests that were completed and closed, the average response time for removing illegally dumped items was about 4 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in an average of 52 hours, while street repair requests took an average of about 3 days to close.
