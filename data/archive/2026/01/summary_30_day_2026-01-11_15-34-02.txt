Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data from the past 30 days.

The most frequent service requests were for Dumped Items (1,933 calls), E-Scooters (967 calls), and Graffiti (938 calls). The average response time to resolve a graffiti report was just over two days. For dumped items, the average response time was about six days.

Response times for other key services include:
- Street Repair: Almost 3 days
- Traffic Signal Issues: About 6 days
- Dead Animal Pickup: About 14 hours

See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it using the Go Long Beach service application. This is the most effective way to alert the city and get a service request on record.
