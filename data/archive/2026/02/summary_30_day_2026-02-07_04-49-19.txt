Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past month, residents made 2,569 reports for illegally dumped items, making it the most frequent service request. This was followed by 1,122 requests for graffiti removal and 984 for e-scooter issues.

Average city response times for closed requests varied. Reports for dumped items took about 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti removal averaged about 2.5 days. One of the quicker responses was for dead animal pickup, which took an average of 11 hours.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application.
