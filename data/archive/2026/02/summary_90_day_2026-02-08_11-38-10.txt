Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 6,476 calls, followed by graffiti with 3,261 calls. The average response time to resolve a dumped items report was about 13.5 days. For graffiti removal, the average was about 4.5 days.

Requests for street repair, tree maintenance, and streetlight outages were typically addressed in about 4 to 5 days. On the faster side, animal-related services, including dead animal pickups, were handled in an average of about 12 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure it is logged in the city's system.
