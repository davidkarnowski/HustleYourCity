Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past seven days. Here is a look at how the city is handling community issues.

Dumped items led the request board with 567 reports. The average response time for these issues was 65 hours. Graffiti removal saw 252 requests, with the city logging an average response time of 24 hours. Trash and debris complaints totaled 17 calls with an average response time of 65 hours.

E-scooter complaints generated 137 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Dead animal pickups had 31 requests with a quick average turnaround of 4.5 hours. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual completed field work.
