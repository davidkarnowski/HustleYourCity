An evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests from the last 30 days highlights the latest response times for high-volume issues. Dumped Items received the most reports with 2,407 total calls, showing an average response time of approximately 1 week. Graffiti reports totaled 1,134, with an average completion time of 54 hours.

Infrastructure maintenance shows mixed response times. Street Repair requests, which totaled 225, were addressed in nearly 4 days on average. Light repair issues generated 382 reports and were resolved in an average of 69 hours.

Faster resolutions were noted for public safety and specific maintenance tasks. Dead Animal Pickup averaged 8.5 hours across 150 reports. Tree Maintenance saw 151 reports with an average response time of 29 hours.
