Here is the latest third-party data summary from Hustle Long Beach! evaluating city service response times. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of requests. Here is a breakdown of how the City of Long Beach is handling the most common issues.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue with 6,904 requests in the last 90 days. The average response time for these reports was over 9 days. Graffiti was the second most common issue with 3,115 reports, taking an average of 64 hours to address.

Street Repair saw 858 requests, with an average response time of over 6 days. Dead Animal Pickup had 433 requests and a much faster average response of 11.5 hours. Interestingly, Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Please use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
