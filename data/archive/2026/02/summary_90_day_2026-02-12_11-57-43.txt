Here is an evaluation of the City of Long Beach service request data for the last 90 days.

The most frequent issue reported was Dumped Items, with 6,393 total requests. The average response time for these issues is currently approximately 2 weeks. Graffiti reports were the second highest volume with 3,143 requests, showing an average resolution time of about 4 days.

Infrastructure repairs show mixed response times. Street Repair requests are averaging just over 5 days for resolution. Traffic Signal issues are taking roughly 6 days to close, while Light repair requests are being handled in about 4 days.

Some categories show faster turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup is averaging 11.5 hours. Reports regarding Business Licenses and Permits show a response time of 31 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Conversely, Marina Repairs are averaging 20 days to resolve.
