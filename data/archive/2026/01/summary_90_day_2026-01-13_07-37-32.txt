Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data from the past 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were for dumped items (5,975 calls), graffiti (3,483 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,701 calls). In total, the city received over 15,000 service requests during this period.

Average response times varied by service type. The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 13 days. Graffiti removal took, on average, just under 5 days, while street repairs were completed in about one week.

Quicker responses were recorded for other services. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 15.5 hours on average, and vegetation maintenance requests were closed in under 2 days.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application.
