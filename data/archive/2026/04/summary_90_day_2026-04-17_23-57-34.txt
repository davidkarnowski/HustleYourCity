Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items lead the city's service requests with 6,993 calls, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti is the second most reported issue with 3,240 calls, averaging a response time of about 57 hours. E-scooter complaints also remain high with 2,922 reports logged.

Some infrastructure repairs are taking over a week to address. Tree maintenance requests average 8.9 days, and street repairs take about 8 days. On the faster end, business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead animal pickups are currently being handled in about 10 hours.

Stay engaged and help keep the city accountable. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
