Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest 30-day accountability update on city service response times.

Dumped Items continue to be the most frequently reported issue, with 2,313 requests in the last 30 days. The average response time for these reports is currently 8 days. Graffiti removal saw 994 requests, with the city responding in an average of 36.5 hours. E-Scooter complaints reached 865, though all of these were referred to outside operators rather than closed by the city.

Other notable service categories include Tree Maintenance with 260 requests averaging a 36.5-hour response, and Street Repair with 197 requests averaging a 71.5-hour response. Dead Animal Pickup received 166 calls, which were addressed in an average of 6.9 hours. Sidewalk Repair had 144 requests with an average response time of 23.2 hours.

Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
