Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data to keep the community informed. Over the past 90 days, citizens have actively reported neighborhood issues, and the data reveals how the City of Long Beach is managing these requests.

Dumped items and graffiti are the most frequently reported problems. There were 6,726 reports for dumped items, which took the city an average of 9.7 days to address. Graffiti was reported 3,059 times, with an average response time of 71 hours.

Other major concerns include street repairs and tree maintenance. The city received 838 street repair requests, taking an average of 6.5 days to respond. Tree maintenance saw 947 reports, averaging a 6.7-day response time.

Some categories show extremely fast turnaround times. For instance, business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests and help hold the city accountable.
