Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the past week.

Over 1,000 service requests were made through the Go Long Beach system in the last 7 days. The most common reports were for dumped items (349 calls), graffiti (186 calls), and improperly parked e-scooters (173 calls).

For requests that were completed, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 23 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in an average of about 29 hours. The average close time for the 3 sidewalk repair requests was about 33 minutes. Unusually fast times can sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report problems like graffiti or dumped items is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
