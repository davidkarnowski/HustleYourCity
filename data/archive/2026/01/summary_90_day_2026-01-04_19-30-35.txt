Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 90 days.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped items (5,837 calls) and graffiti removal (3,576 calls). The average time to close a dumped item request was about 14.5 days, while graffiti removal took an average of just under 5 days.

Other key service response times include street repair at 9.5 days and tree maintenance at 6.5 days. Some of the fastest responses were for dead animal pickups and animal services, which were both resolved in about 16 hours on average. The average response for a damaged drinking fountain was just over 2 hours, though this may reflect an administrative closure and not a completed repair.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application. This is the most direct way to submit a service request to the city.
