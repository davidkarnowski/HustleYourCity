Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service requests for the last 90 days.

The most frequent service request was for Dumped Items, with 6,423 total reports. The average response time for these issues is currently approximately 2 weeks.

Graffiti removal received 3,191 reports, with an average response time of about 4 and a half days. Street Repair requests totaled 789, with an average wait time of 5 days.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup, averaging 11 hours across 407 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
