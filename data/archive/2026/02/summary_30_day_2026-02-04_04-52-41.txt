Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past month, residents submitted over 6,100 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,543 calls), graffiti (1,078 calls), and e-scooter issues (1,005 calls).

The average time to resolve a report for dumped items was about 9.5 days. Graffiti removal requests were handled faster, with an average response time of about 2.6 days. Reports for dead animal pickups were addressed in approximately 12 hours. For the 1,005 e-scooter reports, these are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.
