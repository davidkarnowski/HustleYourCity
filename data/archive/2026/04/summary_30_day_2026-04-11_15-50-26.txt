Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on City of Long Beach service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 2324 calls in the last month. The city is averaging about 8.1 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti reports followed with 1084 calls, showing an average response time of 36.8 hours.

E-Scooter complaints reached 951, though most were immediately closed or referred. Tree maintenance saw 254 calls with a response time of about 3.6 days. Street repair requests totaled 200, taking about 3.4 days on average to address.
