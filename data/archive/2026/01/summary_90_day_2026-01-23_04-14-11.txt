Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times over the last 90 days.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with over 6,100 service calls. The average time to resolve these requests was about 13 days. Graffiti was the second most common report, with over 3,300 requests closed in an average of about 5 days.

Other key response times include street repairs at nearly 6 days, park maintenance at about 10 days, and streetlight issues at about 4 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
