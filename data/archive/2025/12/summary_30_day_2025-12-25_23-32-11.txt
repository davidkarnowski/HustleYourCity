Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests. In the past month, residents submitted nearly 5,000 requests for city services. The most reported issues were illegally dumped items (1,901 requests), e-scooter issues (1,055 requests), and graffiti (1,007 requests).

Response times for these top issues varied. The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 7.5 days. Graffiti reports were addressed more quickly, with an average response time of about 2.3 days. Some of the fastest responses were for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 13 hours. Slower responses included traffic signal repairs, which averaged about 4 days.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
