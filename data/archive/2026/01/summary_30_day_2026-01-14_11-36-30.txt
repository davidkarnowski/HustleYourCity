Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the past month, "Dumped Items" was the most reported issue with 2,024 requests. This was followed by reports for illegally parked "E-Scooters" (937) and "Graffiti" (916).

Based on the data for closed cases, the average response time for dumped items was about 5 days. Graffiti and tree maintenance requests were handled in about 2 days.
