Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 6,579 reports. The city closed 5,748 of these cases, taking an average of 10.4 days to respond.

Graffiti removal remains a major task with 3,106 reports. The city closed 2,923 of these cases, averaging a response time of 3.2 days.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 2,928, all of which were referred to outside agencies or closed administratively without a tracked response time.

Street Repair and Tree Maintenance requests took an average of 5.7 days and 5.8 days respectively to address.

Business License and Permit Concerns showed an unusually fast average response time of 12 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
