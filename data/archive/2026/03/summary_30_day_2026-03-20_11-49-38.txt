Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at the current response times and call totals to keep the public informed on local government operations.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 2,246 calls in the last 30 days. The city took an average of 5.4 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti reports followed with 1,038 calls, averaging a 40-hour response time. E-scooter complaints reached 912, all of which were closed and referred to outside vendors.

Tree maintenance received 502 requests, taking an average of 3.2 days to address. Reports of illegal fireworks took the longest to resolve, with 11 calls averaging nearly 9 days for a response. On the other end of the spectrum, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Tracking these statistics helps ensure public awareness of local government response times.
