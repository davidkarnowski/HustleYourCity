The latest City of Long Beach service request data for the past 30 days highlights current response times for high-volume categories.

Dumped Items received the highest number of reports with 2,506 total calls. The average response time for these requests is currently 7.6 days. Graffiti reports followed with 1,103 total entries and an average completion time of 2.3 days.

Infrastructure maintenance shows varied response times. Street Repair requests averaged 3.2 days for resolution across 245 reports. Street Light issues, with 428 total reports, were addressed in approximately 2.9 days.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup, averaging 9.4 hours. Conversely, general Trash & Debris reports averaged 4.6 days to close. E-Scooter reports remained high at 923, though these are primarily referred to third-party operators.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
