Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. This information helps residents understand how quickly local issues are being addressed by city departments.

Over the last 90 days, Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 6,773 requests, taking an average of 9.5 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,075 calls, averaging a response time of 67 hours. Street Repair and Tree Maintenance both saw significant activity, each averaging 6.5 days for a response.

Some categories show extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

To help keep Long Beach neighborhoods clean and safe, citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
