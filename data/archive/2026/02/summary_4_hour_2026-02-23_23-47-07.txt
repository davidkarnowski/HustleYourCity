The latest 4-hour window of city service data highlights a high volume of cleanup requests. Dumped Items received the most activity with 41 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 18 minutes. Graffiti reports followed with 15 total cases and an average response time of 23 minutes. These rapid response times suggest administrative processing or movement to In Progress status rather than immediate physical resolution.

Abandoned Shopping Cart reports showed an average response time of roughly 2 hours. Several other categories, including Street Repair, Light, and Sidewalk Repair, logged new reports but have not yet generated response time data.
