Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest independent accountability update on city service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues. The city received 2263 reports for dumped items, which took an average of 5.8 days to process. Graffiti was reported 1005 times with an average response time of 38.8 hours.

Other notable service areas include street repairs, which saw 255 requests and averaged a 3.2-day response time. Running water reports were addressed more rapidly, averaging 7.9 hours across 17 requests. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit requests and help track city responsiveness.
