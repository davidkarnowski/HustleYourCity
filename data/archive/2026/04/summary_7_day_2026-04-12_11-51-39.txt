Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest look at city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the top reported issues. There were 540 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of about 58 hours. Graffiti saw 342 reports, averaging a 20-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 288 reports.

Other notable services include street and sidewalk repairs, which both received 35 reports and averaged about 12 hours for a response. Park maintenance had 11 reports with an average response of 1.7 hours, and vegetation issues averaged just under 6 hours for 24 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Reporting helps keep the city accountable and well-maintained.
