Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Over the past 90 days, residents have continued to rely on city services to address neighborhood concerns, with significant variations in response times and call volumes.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,623 reports. The city took an average of 10 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue, totaling 3,090 calls with an average response time of 3 days. 

Other major categories included Tree Maintenance with 904 requests averaging a 6-day response, and Street Repair with 835 calls taking about 6 days to address. E-Scooter complaints reached 2,933, but all of these were referred to outside agencies, resulting in no measurable city response time.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnarounds. For example, Business Licenses and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times are often due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
