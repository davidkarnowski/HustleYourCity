Here is the latest city service response data evaluated for the Hustle Long Beach! community, focusing on the last 90 days of reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 6,530 reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 10.5 days. Graffiti removal saw 3,111 reports, with an average response time of 3.25 days.

Infrastructure requests also show significant volume. The city received 1,039 reports for lighting issues, averaging a 3.4-day response time. Street repair generated 836 calls with a 5.6-day average response, and tree maintenance saw 883 reports averaging 5.8 days.

Some categories show unusually fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns average a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
