Here is a look at the latest city service data for Long Beach based on requests from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted 4,852 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,907 requests), graffiti (1,114 requests), and e-scooter issues (778 requests).

According to the city's data, some of the quickest average response times were for dead animal pickups (about 8.5 hours) and animal services calls (about 9 hours). Graffiti removal requests were addressed in an average of about 2.3 days. Longer response times were seen for requests like dumped item removal, which took an average of about 8 days to resolve. Marina repair requests took the longest, averaging almost 2 weeks.
