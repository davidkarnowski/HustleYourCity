Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day city service data to keep you informed on how quickly Long Beach is responding to community issues.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 2,341 reports. The city took an average of 8.3 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,041 calls, averaging a response time of 53.5 hours.

Other notable service times include street repairs, which saw 219 requests and averaged a 69.5-hour response time. Street light issues generated 237 reports and took an average of 4.2 days to resolve. Dead animal pickups were processed much faster, averaging 6.8 hours across 171 requests. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times can sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Hustle Long Beach! encourages all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps hold agencies accountable and improves local neighborhoods.
