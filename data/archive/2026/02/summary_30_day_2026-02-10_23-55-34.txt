Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service requests based on data from the last 30 days.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 2,588 total reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 8 days. Graffiti reports totaled 1,125, with the city showing an average completion time of 2.4 days.

Infrastructure data shows that 443 reports were made regarding Lights, which were resolved in roughly 2.7 days on average. Street Repair received 259 total calls with an average response time of 3.2 days.

Faster response times were recorded for public health and safety issues. Dead Animal Pickup had 153 reports with an average response of 11 hours. Vegetation issues, with 59 reports, were addressed in approximately 22 hours.
