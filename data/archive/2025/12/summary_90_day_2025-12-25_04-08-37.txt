Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service response data for the last 90 days.

The most frequent requests were for Dumped Items (over 6,000 reports) and Graffiti (nearly 3,700 reports). The average response time to close a Dumped Items request was about two weeks. For Graffiti, the average response time was about 5 and a half days.

Other common requests included Street Repair, which took about 10 days on average to resolve. Reports for Light maintenance and Tree Maintenance were both addressed in about one week on average. Faster responses were seen for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services requests, which were typically closed in about 15 to 16 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
