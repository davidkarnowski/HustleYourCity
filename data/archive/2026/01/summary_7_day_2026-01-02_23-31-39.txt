Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update for the past week.

Dumped item pickups were the most frequent request with 447 calls, followed by 226 for e-scooters and 209 for graffiti. The average response time to close a dumped item request was about 2 days, while graffiti reports were handled in about 1.5 days.

Response times for other services varied. Trash and debris clean-up requests took an average of about 5 days to resolve. Restroom maintenance requests were closed in an average of 83 minutes. Unusually fast responses may be due to administrative closures.
