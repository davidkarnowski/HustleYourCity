Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted thousands of requests for service. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (6,053), graffiti (3,389), and e-scooter issues (2,704).

Response times varied by issue. Reports for animal services and dead animal pickups were typically closed in about 14 hours. The average response time for graffiti removal was about 5 days, while street repairs took about 6 days. The longest response times were for dumped items, averaging about 13 days to close out a report.

Some response times, like the 2-hour average for damaged drinking fountains, may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
