Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days based on public data.

Over 15,000 requests were made, with the top categories being dumped items (6,070 reports) and graffiti (3,376 reports).

Response times vary by issue. The average time to resolve a report for dumped items was about 13 days. Reports for graffiti took about 5 days. Some of the fastest responses were for dead animal pickups and animal services, both averaging under 15 hours. It is possible that some unusually fast closure times are due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.
