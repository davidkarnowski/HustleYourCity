Here is your weekly look at Long Beach city service response data.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,392 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (597 requests), graffiti (269 requests), and e-scooter issues (199 requests).

Average response times varied across services. Requests for dead animal pickups were handled in about 10 hours, while sidewalk repairs took around 11 hours. Graffiti removal averaged about 1.5 days. The longest average response time was for streetlight repairs, at just over 2 days.
