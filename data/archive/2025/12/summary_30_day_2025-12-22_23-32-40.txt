Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days. During this period, residents submitted over 5,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items, e-scooters, and graffiti.

For the most common requests, the average city response time to close a case for illegally dumped items was about 5.7 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 55.5 hours.

Some of the quickest responses were for Animal Services issues, which were resolved in about 10.5 hours on average. Reports for dead animal pickups were handled in an average of about 12.8 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
