Here is the latest 30-day snapshot of Long Beach city service requests and response times.

In the past month, the most common reports were for dumped items (1,894 requests) and graffiti (1,011 requests). The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 8 days. Graffiti reports were closed in an average of 56 hours.

Response times for other services varied. The city responded to dead animal pickup requests in about 13 hours on average. Traffic signal issues took an average of about 4 days to close out.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
