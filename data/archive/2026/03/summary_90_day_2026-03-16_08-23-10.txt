Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day city service data to keep you informed on how quickly issues are being addressed in the Long Beach community.

Over the past three months, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,634 reports. The city took an average of 10 days to respond to these cases. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,068 calls, averaging a 3-day response time.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant volume. Tree Maintenance received 902 reports with an average response time of nearly 6 days, while Street Repair had 831 reports averaging just over 6 days.

Some services show incredibly fast turnarounds. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Meanwhile, Dead Animal Pickup handled 440 calls with a realistic average response time of 12.5 hours.

If you spot an issue in your neighborhood, make your voice heard. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 16, 2026, 1:14 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 16, 2026, 12:00 AM PDT.
