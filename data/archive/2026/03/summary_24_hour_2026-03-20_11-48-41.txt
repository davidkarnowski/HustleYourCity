Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest 24-hour accountability update on city service requests.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 69 reports, though the vast majority remain untouched in the new status. E-Scooter complaints followed with 43 reports, all of which were immediately referred to outside agencies. Graffiti also saw significant activity with 25 reports.

The data shows several unusually fast response times over the last 24 hours. Tree Maintenance requests averaged a response time of just 20 minutes, while Animal Services averaged 21 minutes. Vegetation concerns were handled in an average of 50 minutes. Running Water and Dead Animal Pickup reports averaged 61 and 62 minutes, respectively. Graffiti and Light issues also averaged under 90 minutes. Keep in mind that these extremely low response times may be the result of administrative closures in the system rather than actual physical work being completed on the streets.

Other notable response averages include Sidewalk Repair at 4.85 hours and Street Repair at 2.51 hours.

Accountability starts with reporting. Please encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
