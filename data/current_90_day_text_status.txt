The latest data for city services in Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,626 requests for dumped items and 2,547 reports regarding graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains one of the fastest services, with an average response time of approximately 7 minutes. In contrast, dumped item removal has averaged about 17 days over the last 90 days. Please note that some categories show very low response times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. This tool remains the most direct way to notify the city of concerns and track the status of your requests.
