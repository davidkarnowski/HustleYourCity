Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,440 requests through the city's service system. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (586 requests), graffiti (269 requests), and e-scooter issues (247 requests).

Average city response times for closed cases show graffiti removal took about 22 hours. Dumped item reports were resolved in just under two days, while tree maintenance requests were handled in about 8 hours. Note that some requests, like for e-scooters, are referred to third-party companies and their response times are not included in this city data.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at February 1, 2026, 7:38 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at February 1, 2026, 7:00 AM PST.
