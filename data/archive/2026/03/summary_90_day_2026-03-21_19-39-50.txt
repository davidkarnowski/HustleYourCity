Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Dumped Items and Graffiti continue to drive the highest volume of citizen reports. There were 6,731 requests for Dumped Items, which took an average of 9.7 days to resolve. Graffiti saw 3,068 requests with an average response time of 71.4 hours. E-Scooter issues generated 2,840 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies and lack response time data.

Street Repair and Tree Maintenance also saw significant activity, with 836 and 945 requests respectively, both averaging about 6.6 and 6.7 days for a response. On the faster end, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickup maintained a steady response time of 11.8 hours across 438 requests.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. You can submit your service requests directly through the Go Long Beach service application to help keep the city accountable and maintain local streets.
