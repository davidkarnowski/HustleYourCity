Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,470 service requests. The most reported issues were illegally dumped items (593 reports), graffiti (245 reports), and e-scooter issues (216 reports).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 25 hours. Reports for dumped items took an average of just over 2 days to close. Some service requests may be closed for administrative reasons, which can result in unusually fast times, such as a restroom maintenance ticket that was closed in about 16 minutes.
