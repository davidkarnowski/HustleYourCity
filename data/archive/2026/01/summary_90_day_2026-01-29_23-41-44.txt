Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service response data.

Dumped Items and Graffiti remain the most reported issues, with over 6,200 and 3,300 calls respectively. The average response time for Graffiti removal was about 5 days, while Dumped Item pickups averaged around 13 days.

Street repair requests were addressed in an average of 5.5 days. Faster responses included Dead Animal Pickups, which were handled in about 13 hours. Slower responses included reports for damaged drinking fountains, which took about 4 weeks to resolve.
