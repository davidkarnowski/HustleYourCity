Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the last 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2,091 calls in the past month. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most common issue, generating 1,051 reports with an average response time of 43 hours.

E-Scooter complaints also saw high volume with 1,001 reports. However, all of these were immediately marked as referred and closed, generating no measurable response time.

For infrastructure issues, street repair requests totaled 269 and took an average of over 3 days to resolve. Sidewalk repairs took a similar amount of time, averaging 3 days for 111 reports. Marina repairs took the longest to address, averaging 7.5 days.

On the faster side, business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this are often due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead animal pickups were handled efficiently, averaging 14 hours for 147 reports.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application. Consistent reporting helps hold the city accountable and keeps the community safe and clean.
