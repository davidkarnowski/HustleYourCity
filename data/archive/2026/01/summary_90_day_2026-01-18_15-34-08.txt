Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

The most common service requests from the public were for Dumped Items (6,052 calls) and Graffiti (3,425 calls). The average time to resolve a dumped items report was about 13 days, while graffiti removal requests took nearly 5 days on average to complete.

Quicker responses were recorded for other services. The average response time for Dead Animal Pickup was about 15.5 hours, and Sidewalk Repair requests were closed in just over 3 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city. Use the Go Long Beach service application on your phone to submit a request and help keep the city informed.
