Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service request data to keep citizens informed on city response times and call volumes.

Over the past 90 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 6,664 requests. The city took an average of nearly 10 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,093 calls, averaging a response time of just under 3 days.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair and Tree Maintenance. Street Repair saw 833 requests, while Tree Maintenance had 929. Both categories averaged a response time of almost 1 week.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep the city accountable and neighborhoods clean. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 20, 2026, 4:46 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 20, 2026, 4:00 AM PDT.
