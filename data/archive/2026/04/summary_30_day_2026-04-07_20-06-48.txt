Hustle Long Beach! is here with the latest 30-day city service accountability update. A review of the most recent data shows how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen requests.

Over the past month, Dumped Items led the service requests with 2,340 calls, taking an average of 8 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,004 calls, averaging a response time of 34 hours. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 902 reports.

Some services saw very quick turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup averaged just over 4 hours for 164 requests, and Animal Services averaged about 8 hours for 80 requests. On the slower side, Marina Repair requests took 7 days on average to complete. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep the city accountable and well-maintained. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
