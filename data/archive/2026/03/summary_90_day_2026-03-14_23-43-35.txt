Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data for the last 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

The highest volume of service requests over the past 90 days included Dumped Items with 6,628 reports, Graffiti with 3,075 reports, and E-Scooter issues with 2,891 reports.

Response times vary significantly by category. The city averaged about 10 days to respond to Dumped Items and roughly 3 days for Graffiti removal. Tree Maintenance requests, which totaled 906 calls, took an average of 6 days to process. 

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting local issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application to help maintain accountability and improve neighborhood conditions.
