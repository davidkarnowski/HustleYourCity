Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data. Here is a look at how the city handled community issues over the last 90 days.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most reported issues. Citizens reported 7,056 dumped items, which took an average of 9 days to resolve. Graffiti generated 3,142 reports with an average response time of 58 hours. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 2,866 reports, though these are entirely referred to outside operators.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests showed varying response times. Street repair requests totaled 818, taking nearly 7 days on average to address. Tree maintenance saw 923 calls with an average turnaround of nearly 5 days.

On the faster side, dead animal pickups had 447 reports and averaged a 10-hour response. Business license concerns showed an unusually fast response time of just 12 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep the community accountable and well-maintained. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 4, 2026, 12:55 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 3, 2026, 11:00 PM PDT.
