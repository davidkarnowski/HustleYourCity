Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days. The most frequent requests from the public were for illegally dumped items, graffiti removal, and e-scooter issues.

Data shows that response times for several key services have improved in the last month. The average time to close a graffiti report has decreased from about 5 days to less than 3 days. Over the past 90 days, the average response time for over 6,300 "Dumped Items" requests was about 14 days. In contrast, requests for "Dead Animal Pickup" were typically handled in about 12 hours.
