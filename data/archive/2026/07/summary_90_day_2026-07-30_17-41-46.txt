The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,146 reports for dumped items and 3,358 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service category. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 5 hours. Conversely, dumped items take an average of about 8 days to address. Graffiti removal averages roughly 3 days.

Some service categories show high volumes of new reports that are still in progress. For example, of the 644 tree maintenance requests in the last 90 days, 220 are currently in progress. Residents are encouraged to continue using the Go Long Beach service application to report issues, as this helps the city track and manage service requests efficiently.
