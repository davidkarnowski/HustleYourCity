Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at city service response times and request volumes over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 6,505 calls. The city takes an average of 10.7 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti reports are the second highest with 3,113 calls, averaging a 3.3-day response time. E-scooter complaints generated 2,971 reports, all of which were referred to outside operators.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests show varying response times. Street repairs, totaling 839 calls, average 5.4 days for a response. Tree maintenance takes about 5.7 days based on 878 reports. On the extreme end, the 6 reported marina repairs are taking an average of 45 days to resolve.

Some services show rapid turnaround times. Dead animal pickups are handled in about 13 hours. Business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps track performance and ensures neighborhoods get the attention they need.
