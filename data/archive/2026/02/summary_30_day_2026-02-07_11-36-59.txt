Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 6,200 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (2,580 calls), graffiti (1,126 calls), and e-scooters (984 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 63 hours. For dumped items, the average was about 8.5 days. Street repair requests were addressed in about 3 days on average, while reports for dead animal pickup were closed in an average of about 11 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
