Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update for the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,700 service requests this past month. The most common reports were for dumped items with 2,547 requests, followed by graffiti with 1,108 requests, and e-scooter issues with 1,013 requests.

Based on data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.5 days. Reports for dumped items took an average of about 8.5 days to resolve. Street repair requests were addressed in an average of just under 3 days.
