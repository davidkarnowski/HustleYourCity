Hustle Long Beach! is tracking the latest city service request data to keep the community informed. Over the past 90 days, residents have submitted thousands of requests, and the data reveals how long it takes for the city to respond.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of calls with 6,971 reports. The average response time for these issues was about 8.5 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,239 calls, taking an average of 2.3 days to address.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw high volumes. Tree maintenance received 891 reports with an average response time of 8.8 days. Street repair had 696 requests, averaging 7.9 days for a response, while sidewalk repairs took about 6.3 days across 388 reports.

Some categories recorded extremely fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. Submitting requests through the app ensures the city has the data needed to dispatch services and allows the public to hold agencies accountable.
