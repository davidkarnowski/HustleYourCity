Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service response data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests were for Dumped Items (6,091 reports), Graffiti (3,715 reports), and E-Scooters (2,340 reports). The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 15 days, while graffiti reports were closed in about 6 days on average. E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Response times vary by department. Park Maintenance requests had one of the longer averages, taking about 17 days to resolve. In contrast, Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup calls were among the fastest, with average closure times under 17 hours.

Some recent service requests, such as for Sidewalk Repair, show closure times of less than 45 minutes. These unusually fast times may reflect administrative closures for issues like duplicate reports, not the completion of physical work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 22, 2025, 3:29 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 22, 2025, 3:00 AM PST.
