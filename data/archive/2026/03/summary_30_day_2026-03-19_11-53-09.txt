Hustle Long Beach! is back with your latest 30-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests. Here is a look at the actual response times and call volumes for city services over the past month.

Dumped Items remain the highest reported issue with 2,245 calls. The city's average response time for these requests was just over 5 days. Graffiti reports followed closely with 1,052 calls and an average response time of about 37 hours. 

E-Scooter complaints generated 878 reports, all of which were immediately closed and referred to outside vendors. Tree Maintenance saw 497 requests, taking an average of 3 days to address. 

Some services took significantly longer. Reports of Illegal Fireworks averaged a 9-day response time for 10 incidents. Conversely, Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times, especially those under 90 minutes, are often the result of administrative closures rather than actual work being completed in the field.
