Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past week.

Over 1,200 service requests were made by residents. The most common reports were for dumped items (451 requests), graffiti (212 requests), and e-scooter issues (178 requests).

The data shows the average time to close a service request for graffiti was about 1.6 days, while dumped items took about 1.5 days. Street repair requests were resolved in an average of about 1.3 days. Park maintenance requests had an unusually fast average response time of about 37 minutes, which may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
