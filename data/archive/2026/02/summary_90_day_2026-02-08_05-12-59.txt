Here is the latest analysis of Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped items, with 6,475 reports. This was followed by 3,261 reports for graffiti and 2,671 for e-scooter issues.

The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report was about 14 days. For "Graffiti" reports, the average response time was about 4.5 days, while "Street Repair" requests took an average of about 5 days to close. Some services had quicker resolution times, with "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" requests both averaging about 12 hours.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
