In the past 30 days Long Beach city services logged more than 5,300 requests.

The biggest volumes were Dumped Items (1,982 reports) and Graffiti (1,418 reports). Average response times have improved across several categories:

- Dumped Items: ≈ 158 hours (about 6.5 days), down from ≈ 329 hours in the prior 60 days.  
- Graffiti: ≈ 72 hours, down from ≈ 91 hours.  
- Sidewalk Repair: ≈ 15 hours, down from ≈ 45 hours.  
- Street Repair: ≈ 21 hours, down from ≈ 28 hours.  
- Tree Maintenance: ≈ 10.5 hours – the fastest average response.

Some services still take longer, for example Trash & Debris at ≈ 69 hours and Report Illegal Firework at ≈ 84 hours. Very low response times in a few categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
