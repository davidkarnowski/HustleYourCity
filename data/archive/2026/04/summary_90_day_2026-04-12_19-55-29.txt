Hustle Long Beach! is sharing a new data summary evaluating city service response times over the last 90 days. Here is a look at the latest numbers and how long it takes for issues to be addressed.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 6,972 calls in the past 90 days. The average response time for these reports is currently about 8.5 days. Graffiti is the second most common issue, with 3,250 reports and an average response time of roughly 2.2 days.

Infrastructure requests are taking a bit longer. Tree maintenance saw 899 reports with an average response time of 7.6 days. Street repair had 724 reports, taking an average of 7.1 days to process.

Some services show incredibly fast turnaround times. For instance, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 12, 2026, 12:52 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 12, 2026, 11:00 AM PDT.
