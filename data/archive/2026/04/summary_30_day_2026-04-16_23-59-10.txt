Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the city service response times and request totals over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 2324 requests. The city took an average of 8.3 days to respond to these reports.

E-Scooter complaints were the second most common issue with 1048 reports, though response times were not recorded for this category.

Graffiti reports totaled 1038, with an average response time of 55.1 hours.

Light issues generated 231 calls, taking an average of 4.1 days to resolve.

Street Repair requests reached 212, with an average response time of 71.6 hours.

Dead Animal Pickup had 171 reports, averaging a 6.8-hour response time.

Citizens should keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Reporting helps keep the streets safe, clean, and accountable.
