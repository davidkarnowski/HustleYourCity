Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service request data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,000 requests through the city's reporting system. The most common issues were illegally dumped items (1,986 requests), graffiti (1,141 requests), and e-scooter problems (874 requests).

Response times vary by the type of service needed. The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 7 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed more quickly, averaging about 2.6 days. Reports for dead animal pickups were among the fastest, typically closed in about 10 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
