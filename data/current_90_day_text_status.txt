Over the past 90 days Long Beach residents filed about 16,300 service requests across 22 categories.

Response times have improved markedly for many services:
- Sidewalk repairs are now closed in about 2.8 days (67.5 hrs) versus over 190 days historically.  
- Light outages are addressed in roughly 2.1 days (50.5 hrs) compared with 42 days before.  
- Tree maintenance, park maintenance and animal services average under 3 days.  
- Graffiti requests are resolved in about 5.6 days (135 hrs).  
- Dumped items are being handled in about 15.6 days (374 hrs), still the longest among the top categories.

These trends show faster city response overall, though some issues still take longer to close. Residents are encouraged to report problems through the Go Long Beach service app.

This data summary was updated at 2025-11-16 07:27 PST.
