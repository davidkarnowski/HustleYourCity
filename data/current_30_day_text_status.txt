Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the last 30 days.

Illegal dumping (1,968 requests) and graffiti (1,156 requests) were the most reported issues. The average response time to close an illegal dumping report was about 7.5 days. Graffiti cleanup requests were completed in just over 2 days on average.

Some of the quickest responses included dead animal pickups (about 9 hours) and animal services requests (about 10 hours). Slower responses included marina repairs, which took an average of about 2 weeks to resolve.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.
