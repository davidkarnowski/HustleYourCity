Here is the latest 7-day city service data update from Hustle Long Beach!

Over the past week, Dumped Items led the service requests with 528 calls, taking an average of 3.8 days to process. E-Scooter complaints followed with 235 reports, all of which were referred and closed. Graffiti remains a major issue with 194 calls, but the city maintained an average response time of 25.5 hours.

Some services showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Restroom Maintenance requests averaged just 35 minutes, and Community Center calls averaged 72 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completion. Dead Animal Pickup successfully closed all 48 of its requests with an average response time of 4.1 hours.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
