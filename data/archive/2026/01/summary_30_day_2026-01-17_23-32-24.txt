Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service call data.

In the past month, the most reported issues were dumped items (2,029 requests), e-scooters (981 requests), and graffiti (889 requests).

According to the city's data, the average time to close a graffiti report was about 2 days. For dumped items, the average response time was just over 4 days. Slower responses were noted for traffic signal repairs, which averaged about 5.5 days, and trash and debris cleanup, averaging over 6 days to resolve.

Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 12.5 hours. Some unusually fast response times in the data may be due to administrative closures and not on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure it is officially logged.
