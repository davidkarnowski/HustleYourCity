Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the last 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue, with 2,144 calls in the past month. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most common issue, with 1,038 reports and an average response time of 40 hours.

Street repair requests totaled 265, with an average response time of just over 3 days. Tree maintenance saw 491 reports, averaging about 64 hours for a response. 

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. E-scooter complaints totaled 908, but all were immediately referred to outside agencies, resulting in no measurable city response time.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application. Consistent reporting helps keep the city accountable and provides accurate data for the community.
