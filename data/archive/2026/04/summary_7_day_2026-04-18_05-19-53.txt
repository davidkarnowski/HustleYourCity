Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past seven days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community issues and call volumes.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 565 requests in the last week. The city averaged a response time of 3 days for these reports. E-scooter complaints followed with 270 reports, and graffiti removal saw 197 requests with an average response time of 25 hours.

Dead animal pickups were handled quickly, averaging just over 3 hours for 33 calls. Sidewalk repairs and tree maintenance requests both averaged around 16 hours. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
