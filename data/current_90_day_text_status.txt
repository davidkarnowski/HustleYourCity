Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service requests.

Over the last three months, residents filed more than 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (6,222 requests) and graffiti (3,741 requests).

The average city response time to close a request for dumped items was about 15.5 days. For graffiti removal, the average response was about 6.6 days. Park maintenance requests had one of the longer average response times at approximately 19 days. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickups were typically handled in about 17 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 13, 2025, at 12:25 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 13, 2025, at 11:00 AM PST.
