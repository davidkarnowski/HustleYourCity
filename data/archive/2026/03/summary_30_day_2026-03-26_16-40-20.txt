Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the last 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports and where response times currently stand.

Dumped Items led the board with 2,313 reports. The city took an average of 6.5 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 982 calls, seeing an average response time of 36 hours.

Street Repair saw 252 reports, taking an average of 71 hours to address.

Dead Animal Pickup was handled swiftly, with 153 reports averaging just under 9 hours for a response. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
