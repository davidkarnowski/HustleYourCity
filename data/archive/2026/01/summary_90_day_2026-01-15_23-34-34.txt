Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the past 90 days.

Over the last 90 days, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (6,019), graffiti removal (3,455), and e-scooter issues (2,700).

The average time to close a graffiti report was about 4.5 days. For illegally dumped items, the average response time was about 13 days. Faster responses were recorded for issues like dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 16 hours on average. Some service requests may show unusually fast completion times, which can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work being completed.
