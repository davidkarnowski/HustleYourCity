In the last 30 days Long Beach residents filed about 5,200 service requests.

Key highlights:
- Graffiti reports dropped to 1,403 with an average response time of 75 hours (about 3 days), down from 107 hours a month earlier.
- Dumped‑item calls fell to 1,980 and are now closed in an average of 183 hours (≈ 7.6 days), a big improvement from 360 hours in the prior 90‑day period.
- Tree‑maintenance requests total 233 and are being addressed in just 9.5 hours on average, faster than the 14.6 hours seen two months ago.
- Light‑out issues (254 calls) are resolved in about 42 hours (≈ 1.8 days), while street‑repair requests (204) average 20 hours.

Overall, response times are trending shorter across most service categories.
