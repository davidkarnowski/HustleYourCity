Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times for the past week.

This past week, residents submitted 615 requests for dumped items and 286 for graffiti, making them the most common service calls.

The average response time for streetlight repair was about 43 hours. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 33 hours, while dumped items took about 29 hours. Faster responses were seen for issues like dead animal pickup (under 10 hours) and sidewalk repair (under 13 hours). Some response times may appear unusually low due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
