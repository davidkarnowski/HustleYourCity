In the last 30 days Long Beach recorded 4,916 service calls across all categories.

The biggest volumes were Dumped Items (1,934 calls) with an average response time of 172 hours (about 7 days), Graffiti (1,156 calls) averaging 67 hours, and E‑Scooter reports (693 calls). Light issues (251 calls) were addressed in about 50 hours on average, while Street Repair saw 219 calls with a 131‑hour average response.

Fastest turn‑around times were seen for Animal Services (55 calls, 8 hours) and Dead Animal Pickup (124 calls, 8 hours). Park Maintenance had a longer average of 181 hours (≈ 7 days). Some categories, such as E‑Scooter, have no recorded response time because they are automatically closed as “referred.”

Overall, response times vary widely by service type, and occasional unusually low times may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
