Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,413 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (528 requests), e-scooters (254), and graffiti (212).

The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 2.2 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 28 hours. Some categories had much quicker closures, such as vegetation maintenance requests, which averaged about 82 minutes.

Unusually fast response times can sometimes indicate an administrative closure or referral, not necessarily a physical work completion.
