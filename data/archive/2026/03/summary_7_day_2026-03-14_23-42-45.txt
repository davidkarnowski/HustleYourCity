Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the city is handling community reports.

Dumped items were the most reported issue, with 562 calls. The city took an average of 57.5 hours to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most common issue, generating 222 reports with an average response time of 20.9 hours. E-scooter complaints also remained high, totaling 216 reports.

Other notable service requests included 77 calls for tree maintenance, which averaged a 16.7-hour response, and 62 reports regarding street lights, averaging 21.8 hours. Dead animal pickups saw 41 reports and were handled in an average of 8.3 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Your reports help keep the community informed and hold local agencies accountable.
