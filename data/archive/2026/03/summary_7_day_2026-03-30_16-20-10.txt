Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a summary of city service requests and response times over the last 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 518 calls. The city averaged 64.5 hours to respond to these reports. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 231 calls, averaging a 15-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 198 reports.

Some categories recorded extremely fast turnaround times. Restroom Maintenance requests averaged a 50-minute response, while reports of Shoes on a Utility Wire averaged 44 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
