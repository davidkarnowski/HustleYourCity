An evaluation of Long Beach city service data covering the last 90 days indicates that Dumped Items remains the highest volume request category, with 6,579 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 12 and a half days.

Graffiti reports were the second most frequent service request, totaling 3,129. The average time to resolve these issues is nearly 4 days. Street Repair requests, which totaled 818 in this period, are showing an average response time of 4 and a half days.

Services with faster turnaround times include Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged roughly 13 hours to close across 431 reports. Conversely, general Trash & Debris reports are taking longer to resolve, with an average response time of just over 10 days.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
