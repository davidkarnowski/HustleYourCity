Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs over the last 90 days. 

Dumped items remain the most frequently reported issue, with 7031 requests. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to these reports. 

Graffiti removal was the second most common request with 3158 reports, showing an average response time of 58 hours. 

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw high volume. The city received 919 tree maintenance requests with an average response time of 5 days, and 802 street repair reports that averaged nearly 7 days for a response. 

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Dead animal pickups averaged a 10-hour response across 448 calls. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures or automated referrals rather than actual work completion.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application. Consistent reporting helps track actual response times and service levels.
