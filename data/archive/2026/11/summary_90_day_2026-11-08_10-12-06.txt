The latest data for city service requests in Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 7,738 total reports and an average response time of approximately 17 days.

Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,574 reports. The average response time for graffiti is currently 67 minutes. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, averaging about 7 minutes per request across 550 reports.

Tree maintenance saw 1,051 requests with an average response time of about 4 days. Street repair requests totaled 466, with an average response time of nearly 5 days.

Please note that some response times may appear unusually low due to administrative closures or automated referrals rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
