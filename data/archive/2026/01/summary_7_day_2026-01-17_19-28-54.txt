Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past week.

Dumped items, e-scooters, and graffiti were the most frequent service requests from the public. The city received 565 reports for dumped items, with an average closure time of just under 2 days. The 215 graffiti reports were addressed in about 1 day on average.

Response times for other issues varied. A single marina repair request took over 4 days to close. In contrast, a restroom maintenance request was closed in about 16 minutes, which may be due to an administrative update and not the time to complete the work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
