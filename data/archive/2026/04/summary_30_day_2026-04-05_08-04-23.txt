Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest third-party accountability update on city service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped items continue to generate the highest volume of service requests. The city received 2308 reports in the last 30 days, taking an average of 8.3 days to address. Graffiti removal saw 977 requests with an average response time of about 33 hours. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 930 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Street repair requests totaled 215, averaging 3.2 days for a response. Dead animal pickups were handled in an average of 5 hours for 164 requests. Please keep in mind that unusually fast response times across any category may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completion. Meanwhile, illegal firework reports took an average of 4.2 days to process.
