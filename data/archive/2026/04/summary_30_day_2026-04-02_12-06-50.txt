Hustle Long Beach! presents the latest 30-day city service data summary. This information helps keep the Long Beach community informed about local government response times and service volumes.

Over the past month, Dumped Items and Graffiti were the most frequently reported issues. Residents submitted 2,325 reports for dumped items and 945 reports for graffiti. E-scooter complaints were also high, totaling 843 reports.

For response times, the city averaged about 8 days to respond to dumped items. Street repair requests took about 3.5 days, and park maintenance averaged 4.7 days. Issues resolved more quickly included graffiti reports at 37 hours and dead animal pickups at 5.7 hours. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may indicate administrative closures rather than actual physical work being completed.

Accountability starts with reporting. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 2, 2026 4:57 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 2, 2026 3:00 AM PDT.
