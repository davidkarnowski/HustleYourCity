Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest accountability update on city service requests over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 607 reports. The average response time for these issues was about 3.8 days. Graffiti followed with 292 reports, averaging a 46.6-hour response time. E-Scooter issues generated 281 reports, almost all of which were referred to other agencies.

Other notable service areas include Dead Animal Pickup, which had 32 reports and a rapid average response of 5.2 hours. Traffic Signal issues had 13 reports and took an average of 3.5 days to address. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep the city accountable and well-maintained. We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
