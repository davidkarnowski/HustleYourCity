Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,439 service requests. The most reported issue was "Dumped Items" with 579 reports, which took the city an average of about 2 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most common issue with 272 reports, and these were addressed in an average of about 24 hours.

Response times for other common requests included approximately 19 hours for street repairs and about 8 hours for tree maintenance.
