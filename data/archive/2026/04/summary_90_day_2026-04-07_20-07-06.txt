Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data to keep you informed on city response times. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 6942 reports. The city took an average of 8.6 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti removal saw 3189 reports, with an average response time of 57 hours.

Street Repair handled 755 calls, averaging 7 days for a response, while Tree Maintenance took an average of 4.6 days to address 905 reports.

Some categories showed unusually fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
