Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 30 days.

Illegal dumping was the most reported issue with 2,555 requests, followed by 1,106 reports for graffiti. The average response time to close an illegal dumping report was about 8.4 days. Graffiti removal requests were handled more quickly, with an average completion time of about 2.6 days.

Reports for street light issues and street repairs were addressed in an average of just under 3 days. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups (about 11 hours) and other animal services calls (about 13 hours).
