Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,000 requests, with the most common reports for dumped items (1,922), graffiti (1,147), and e-scooters (857).

Based on city data for closed tickets, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 2 days, while dumped item pickups averaged about 7.7 days. It is important to note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures and may not reflect on-site work completion.
