Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the last 90 days.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,234 requests), graffiti (3,339 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,715 requests).

Based on city data for closed requests, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 5 days. Reports of dumped items took an average of 13 days to resolve. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups and animal services calls, which were handled in about 12 hours on average. Some specialized requests, like marina repairs, saw longer average completion times of around 5 weeks.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
