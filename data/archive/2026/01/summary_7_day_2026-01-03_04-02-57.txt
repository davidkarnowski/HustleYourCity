Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 457 requests for "Dumped Items" cleanup, the most common issue. The average time to close these cases was about 2 days. The city also received 213 "Graffiti" reports, which were addressed in about 34 hours on average.

Response times for some services were longer. "Trash & Debris" cleanups took an average of about 4.5 days to resolve. In contrast, "Restroom Maintenance" requests were closed in an average of 83 minutes. Note that unusually fast closures can sometimes be due to administrative updates.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
