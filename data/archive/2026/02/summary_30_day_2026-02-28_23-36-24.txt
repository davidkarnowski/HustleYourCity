The latest service request data for the City of Long Beach highlights volume and response times over the past 30 days. The most frequent issue reported was Dumped Items, with 2,217 total requests. The average response time for these reports was approximately 6 days.

Graffiti reports were the second highest category with 1,121 total entries. The data shows an average resolution time of roughly 2 days for graffiti removal. Street Repair requests totaled 246, with an average completion time of 4 days.

Faster response times were noted for Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 12 hours across 137 requests. Reports regarding Business Licenses and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times often indicate administrative closures rather than physical work completion.
