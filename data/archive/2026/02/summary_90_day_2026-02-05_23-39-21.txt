Here is the latest 90-day summary of service requests made to the City of Long Beach.

Over the past 90 days, the most common service requests were for Dumped Items (6,393 reports) and Graffiti (3,282 reports). The average response time to close a dumped item request was about 2 weeks. For graffiti removal, the average response was about 4.7 days.

Some of the quickest city responses were for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services, both averaging around 12 hours to resolve. Requests for Sidewalk Repair averaged just over 3 days, while Street Repair and Tree Maintenance both averaged over 5 days.

The longest average response time was for Marina Repair, which took about 5 weeks to close.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
