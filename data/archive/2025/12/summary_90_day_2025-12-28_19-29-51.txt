Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were for dumped items, graffiti, and e-scooters, with over 12,000 calls combined for these categories.

For requests that were completed, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 5 days. Dumped item reports were addressed in about 15 days on average. Some services had quicker turnaround times, like dead animal pickups, which averaged about 16 hours. Park maintenance requests took an average of about 17 days to resolve.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at 11:27 AM PST on December 28, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 AM PST on December 28, 2025.
