Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 90 days, based on the city's public data.

The most frequent service requests were for Dumped Items (5,912 reports) and Graffiti (3,633 reports). The average response time to close a Dumped Items request was about 15 days, while Graffiti removal took an average of about 5 days.

Response times for other common issues include approximately 10 days for Street Repair and about one week for non-emergency Light repairs. Park Maintenance requests had one of the longest average completion times at over 17 days. On the faster side, requests for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services were typically resolved in less than a day.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
