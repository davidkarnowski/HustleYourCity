Here is an evaluation of Long Beach city service response times over the last 90 days. This analysis covers the most frequent service requests submitted by residents.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of calls with 6,420 reports. The average time to close these requests is currently 13 days. Graffiti removal followed with 3,121 reports, showing an average response time of 4 days.

Infrastructure requests show varied completion times. Street Repair requests are being resolved in approximately 4.5 days. Issues regarding street lights are averaging 3.5 days for resolution. Tree Maintenance requests are taking about 1 week to complete.

Animal-related services remain the most responsive. Dead Animal Pickup and general Animal Services are averaging response times between 11 and 13 hours. Some categories with response times under 15 minutes, such as business license concerns, may reflect administrative closures rather than physical work completion.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach application to report issues directly to the city.
