Here is the latest third-party accountability update for the Hustle Long Beach! community, tracking city service response times over the last 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 533 reports. The city averaged a response time of 3.1 days for these issues, though 371 requests currently remain in the "New" status.

Graffiti removal saw 240 reports. The city closed 175 of these cases, averaging a response time of 25.2 hours.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 133, all of which were referred to outside agencies and closed.

Dead Animal Pickup had 29 reports with a fast average response time of 5.1 hours. Please note that unusually low response times across any category may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Tree Maintenance received 52 reports with an average response time of 14.8 hours, while Street Repair had 38 reports averaging 37.5 hours.

Community reporting helps keep Long Beach accountable and clean. All residents are encouraged to download and use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
