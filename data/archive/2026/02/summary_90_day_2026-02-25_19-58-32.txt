Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service requests over the last 90 days.

The highest volume of service calls involved Dumped Items, with 6,549 total reports. The average response time for these issues is currently 12.7 days.

Graffiti removal saw 3,115 reports with an average completion time of 3.7 days. Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 809 and are taking approximately 4.5 days to resolve.

Tree Maintenance requests are averaging 6.1 days for a response. Faster response times were noted for Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged 12.7 hours per request. Conversely, general Trash & Debris reports are taking roughly 10.2 days to close.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues. Consistent reporting helps track performance and service needs.
