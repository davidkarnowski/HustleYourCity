Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past week.

Illegal dumping continues to be the most reported issue with 514 new service calls. The average response time for these requests was about 3.5 days. Reports for general trash and debris took slightly longer, averaging almost 4 days for a response.

In contrast, the city responded more quickly to other issues. Graffiti removal requests were handled in about 32 hours on average. Dead animal pickups were addressed in under 6 hours, and general animal services calls were completed in about 3 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
