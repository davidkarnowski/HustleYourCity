Here is the latest evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests from the past 30 days.

Dumped Items remain the most frequent report with 2,277 total calls. The average response time for these issues is currently 5.7 days. Graffiti reports are the second highest volume at 1,158, with an average completion time of 1.9 days.

Infrastructure repairs show varied timelines. Street Repair requests totaled 256 and took an average of 3.4 days to address. Tree Maintenance received 440 reports with a response time of 1.7 days. Light repair requests totaled 372 and averaged 2.2 days for a response.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup and Abandoned Shopping Carts, both averaging approximately 12.5 hours. E-Scooter reports totaled 937, though these are closed and referred to operators immediately.
