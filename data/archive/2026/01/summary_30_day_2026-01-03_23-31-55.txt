Here is a look at the latest Long Beach service request data for the past 30 days.

Over 5,000 requests were submitted by residents. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (1,849 calls), e-scooter issues (1,008 calls), and graffiti (916 calls).

Here are some of the average city response times for closed requests:

- Graffiti: about 56 hours
- Street Repair: about 40 hours

Some requests show very fast completion times. For example, some recent dead animal pickups were closed in about 32 minutes. Unusually low response times can be due to administrative closures and may not reflect on-site work completion.
