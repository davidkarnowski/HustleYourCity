The City of Long Beach has posted its latest 90‑day service data.  

In the past three months 6,436 dump‑item reports were logged, with an average response time of about 15.6 days. Graffiti calls (3,648) were closed in roughly 5.6 days, while sidewalk repairs (347) took about 2.8 days on average. Light‑related issues (557) and tree maintenance (810) were addressed in roughly 2.1 days and 2.3 days respectively.  

Animal Services (238) and Dead Animal Pickup (460) are the fastest categories, averaging 18 hours and 17 hours from report to closure. The longest waits were for Report Illegal Fireworks (27 reports, 9.2 days) and Marina Repair (9 reports, 10.7 days).
