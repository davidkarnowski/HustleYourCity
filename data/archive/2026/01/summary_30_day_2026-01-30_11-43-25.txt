Here is a look at the latest Long Beach service response data from the past 30 days.

Dumped Items continues to be the most reported issue with 2,402 calls. The average response time for these requests was about 8 days. Graffiti and E-Scooter reports were also high, with over 1,000 calls each. The average closure time for a Graffiti report was about 2.5 days.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup (about 11.5 hours) and Animal Services (about 13.5 hours). Some service requests show unusually fast closure times, which may be due to administrative actions rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
