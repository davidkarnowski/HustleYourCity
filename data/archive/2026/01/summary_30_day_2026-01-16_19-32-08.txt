Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 30 days.

In the past 30 days, residents submitted over 5,400 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped item removal (2,063 requests), e-scooter issues (965), and graffiti cleanup (903).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 2 days, while dumped item pickups took an average of 4.7 days. Reports for street light issues were addressed in about 2.7 days on average.

Longer wait times were seen for other services. The average response for trash and debris cleanup was nearly 7 days, and traffic signal repairs took about 5.6 days. Some services, like dead animal pickups, were much faster, averaging about 12 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.
