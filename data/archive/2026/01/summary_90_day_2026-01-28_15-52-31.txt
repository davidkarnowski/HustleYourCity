Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days.

The most common service requests from residents were for dumped items (6,241 calls), graffiti removal (3,336 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,712 calls). Most e-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies.

Average response times for closed requests varied by service type. The city took about 13 days to resolve dumped item reports and about 5 days for graffiti removal. More urgent requests, such as dead animal pickups and animal services calls, were handled much faster, with an average response time of about 12 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
