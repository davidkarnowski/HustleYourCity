The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,628 requests for dumped items and 2,582 reports regarding graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 7 minutes. Conversely, dumped item removal has averaged about 17 days, while tree maintenance requests have taken roughly 6 days on average.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
