Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data over the past 90 days to keep residents informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 6,672 reports. On average, it took the city nearly 10 days to resolve these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,091 calls, which saw an average response time of about 3 days. 

E-Scooter complaints also saw high numbers with 2,809 reports, though all of these were immediately referred to outside operators rather than handled directly by city staff. 

Infrastructure and maintenance requests are taking longer to resolve. Street Repair with 829 reports and Tree Maintenance with 914 reports both averaged a response time of about 1 week. Sidewalk Repairs, totaling 302 reports, took nearly 4 days on average.

Some services showed much faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup handled 430 reports in just under 12 hours on average. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes, though unusually low response times like this are often due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps track performance and ensures local government remains accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 19, 2026, 12:53 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 19, 2026, 12:00 PM PDT.
