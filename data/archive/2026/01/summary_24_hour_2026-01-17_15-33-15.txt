Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update. In the past 24 hours, residents submitted 204 service requests through the Go Long Beach system.

The most frequent reports were for dumped items (69 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (49 requests), and graffiti (27 requests).

Based on cases closed during this period, the average response time for dumped items was about 4.4 hours. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of 1.6 hours.
