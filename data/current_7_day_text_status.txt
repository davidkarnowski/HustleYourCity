Here is the latest summary of service requests made through the Go Long Beach app. In the past week, residents submitted over 1,200 requests for city services. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (468 requests), graffiti (263 requests), and e-scooter issues (255 requests).

Looking at average response times for closed cases, graffiti removal took about 40 hours, while dumped items were addressed in about 56 hours. Some response times were unusually fast, such as sidewalk repair at 45 minutes. These quick resolutions may be due to administrative closures and not always on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
