Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service response data for the last 90 days.

The most frequent service request was for "Dumped Items", with 6,079 reports. The average response time to close these cases was about 2 weeks. The second most common request was for "Graffiti" removal, with 3,690 reports and an average response time of about 5 to 6 days.

Reports for "E-Scooter" issues were also high at 2,369, but these are referred directly to scooter companies and do not have a city response time. Animal-related calls for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were handled much faster, with average response times of about 16 and 15 hours, respectively.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
