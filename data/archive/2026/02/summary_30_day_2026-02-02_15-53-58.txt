Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,800 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (2,420 requests), e-scooters (1,039), and graffiti (1,012).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.5 days. Reports of illegally dumped items were addressed in an average of just under 10 days, while street repair requests were closed in about 2.5 days. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 11 hours on average. Some services show very fast response times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.
