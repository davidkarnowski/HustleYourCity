Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on public data.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests during this period. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,155 requests), graffiti (3,726 requests), and e-scooters (2,159 requests).

The data shows longer waits for some services. The average response time for Park Maintenance requests was about 19.5 days. Reports of illegally dumped items took an average of 15.5 days to be resolved.

Quicker responses were recorded for more urgent issues. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests were handled in about 16 hours on average.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
