The latest data from the Go Long Beach service application shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,094 reports for dumped items and 3,396 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. For instance, dead animal pickup remains efficient with an average response time of approximately 5 hours. Please note that unusually low response times, such as those seen in the last 24 hours for certain categories, may reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work.
