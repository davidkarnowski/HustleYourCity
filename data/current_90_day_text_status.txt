The latest data for city services in Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,723 reports for dumped items, which remains the highest volume category. Response times for these items averaged approximately 17 days.

Graffiti removal saw 2,559 reports with an average response time of about 3 days. Tree maintenance requests totaled 1,049 with an average response time of about 4 days. Dead animal pickup remains the most efficient service, with 547 reports handled in an average of 7 minutes.

Some categories show very low average response times, such as sidewalk repair at 54 minutes over the last 7 days. Please note that unusually low response times may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to continue using the Go Long Beach mobile application to report issues and track service requests. Consistent reporting helps ensure that city resources are directed where they are needed most.
