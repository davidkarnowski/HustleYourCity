The latest city service data for Long Beach shows a high volume of activity over the past 30 days. During this period, the city processed 2,280 reports for dumped items and 1,049 reports for graffiti.

Response times for common issues have remained varied. Dead animal pickup continues to be the fastest service, averaging approximately 5.6 hours. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
