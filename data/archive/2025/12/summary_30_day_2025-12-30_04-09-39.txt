Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Residents made over 5,200 requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,023 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (1,030 requests), and graffiti (1,019 requests).

Based on city data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 55 hours. Reports of illegally dumped items took an average of 10 days to resolve. Streetlight issues were addressed in about 41 hours on average, while street repairs took about 58 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the best way to notify the city.
