City service request activity in Long Beach over the past 90 days shows faster response times across many categories.

• About 15,800 requests were logged.  
• Graffiti reports (3,742) were closed in an average of 165 hours (≈ 7 days), down from 353 hours (≈ 15 days) historically.  
• Tree maintenance (747) averaged 155 hours (≈ 6.5 days), a major improvement from the all‑time average of 3,801 hours.  
• Light repairs (630) now average 176 hours (≈ 7 days) versus 1,012 hours before.  
• Sidewalk repairs (315) average 125 hours (≈ 5 days), down from 4,741 hours.  
• Traffic signal issues (102) average 209 hours (≈ 9 days), compared with 2,502 hours historically.  
• Vegetation work (109) averages 45 hours (≈ 2 days), far quicker than the prior 2,974 hours.  
• Animal services (212) and dead‑animal pickups (435) are being resolved in about 17 hours on average.

These trends suggest the city is handling many service types more quickly than in the past. Residents can continue to report issues and track progress through the Go Long Beach app.
