Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service request data to keep citizens informed on city response times and call volumes.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest call volume with 104 new requests, though none have been marked closed yet. Graffiti reports followed with 41 total requests and an average response time of 5.4 hours. Tree Maintenance received 13 requests, averaging a 7.5-hour response time.

Several categories showed unusually fast response times. Vegetation requests averaged just 12 minutes, Restroom Maintenance averaged 34 minutes, and Running Water reports averaged 51 minutes. Citizens should note that response times under 90 minutes may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Other notable averages include Sidewalk Repair at 5 hours for 5 requests, and Light issues at 6 hours for 9 requests. Categories like Dead Animal Pickup and Street Repair received new requests but have not yet registered completed response times for this period.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city tracks and addresses them.
