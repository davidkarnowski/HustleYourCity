Here's a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

Over this period, residents submitted thousands of requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,917 requests), graffiti (3,486 requests), and e-scooters (2,655 requests).

For closed requests, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 4.5 days. Reports of dumped items took an average of about 13.5 days to resolve. Street repairs were addressed in about one week on average.

Quicker responses were seen for dead animal pickups and animal services requests, which were both handled in under 16 hours on average. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
