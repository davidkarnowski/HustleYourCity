Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data from the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted over 1,500 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (629 calls), graffiti (284 calls), and improperly parked e-scooters (257 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 31 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in just under 3 days on average. The average response time for running water reports was nearly 6 days.
