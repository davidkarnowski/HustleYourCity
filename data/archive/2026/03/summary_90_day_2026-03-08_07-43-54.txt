The latest 90-day data review for Long Beach city services highlights current response times and call volumes for key community issues. Dumped Items remain the most frequent report, with 6,564 total requests recorded. The average response time for these issues is currently 11 days.

Graffiti removal services processed 3,092 reports, with an average completion time of approximately 3.4 days. Infrastructure maintenance shows steady activity, with Street Repair requests averaging 5.4 days for resolution and Tree Maintenance averaging 5.8 days.

E-Scooter reports accounted for 2,939 calls, the majority of which are referred out to vendors. For public health concerns, Dead Animal Pickup services are averaging a response time of 13.6 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues, as this ensures city departments receive the necessary data to allocate resources. This account is an independent data interpreter and is not part of the City of Long Beach government.
