Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,400 requests were submitted through the Go Long Beach app. The most common reports were for dumped items (643 requests), graffiti (289 requests), and e-scooters (186 requests).

Average response times for top issues include approximately 18 hours for dumped items and 25 hours for graffiti. The average response for abandoned shopping carts was about 39 minutes. Some quick response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
