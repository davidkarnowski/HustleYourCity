Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data from the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (5,973 requests) and graffiti removal (3,482 requests).

Average response times varied by service type. The city's data shows graffiti removal took an average of about 4.7 days. Reports for illegally dumped items were resolved in about 13 days on average. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups at around 15.5 hours. Note that some service requests may be closed for administrative reasons, which can affect average response times.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
