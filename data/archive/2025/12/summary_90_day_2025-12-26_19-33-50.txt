Here's the latest 90-day snapshot of Long Beach city service requests.

Dumped Items (5,941 reports) and Graffiti (3,663 reports) continue to be the most frequent issues submitted by residents.

Average response times for closed requests varied. Graffiti removal took about 5.5 days, while resolving reports of Dumped Items averaged around 14.5 days. Park Maintenance requests took nearly 17 days on average. On the faster side, Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests were typically closed in under a day.

If you see something that needs attention, from a pothole to graffiti, use the Go Long Beach service application to report it.

This data summary was updated at December 26, 2025, 11:27 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 26, 2025, 11:00 AM PST.
