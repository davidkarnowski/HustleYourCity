Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 993 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (345 requests), graffiti (185 requests), and e-scooter issues (176 requests).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 22.5 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 28 hours on average. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of about 32 minutes. This unusually fast time may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.
