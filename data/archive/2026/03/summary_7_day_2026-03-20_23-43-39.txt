Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a factual breakdown of city service response times and request volumes over the past 7 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 540 reports. The average response time for these issues was 3.1 days, though 395 of these reports currently remain categorized as new. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 223 reports. The city successfully closed 169 of these graffiti cases, logging an average response time of 23.6 hours.

Other notable service categories included 126 e-scooter complaints, all of which were closed and referred to outside agencies. The city also handled 36 dead animal pickup requests with an average response time of 3.8 hours, while 60 tree maintenance requests averaged 17.3 hours. Marina repairs took the longest to process, averaging 3.7 days. Please note that unusually low response times in any category may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and ensure community needs are documented.
