Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour city service request data to keep the community informed on local government response times.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 86 total reports, though 73 of these remain in the new status. E-Scooter complaints followed with 49 reports, all of which were closed and referred elsewhere. Graffiti was the third most reported issue with 45 calls, averaging a response time of 4.5 hours.

Response times across the city showed significant variation. Dead Animal Pickup requests took the longest to process, averaging just over 10 hours. On the other end of the spectrum, several services recorded unusually fast resolution times. Vegetation concerns averaged 31 minutes, Animal Services averaged 32 minutes, and Sidewalk Repairs averaged 46 minutes. Citizens should note that these unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Please continue to report neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application to help track city responsiveness.
