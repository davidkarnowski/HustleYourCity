The latest 24-hour data snapshot for Long Beach city services shows significant activity in maintenance and cleanup requests. The highest volume of service calls came from Dumped Items with 82 total reports, followed by Graffiti with 55 reports.

Several categories showed average response times under 90 minutes. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports and Park Maintenance requests both averaged 6 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times, particularly those under 90 minutes, may be due to administrative closures, triage, or referrals rather than immediate physical completion of the work.
