Here is a look at Long Beach service request data for the past week.

The most frequent reports from residents were for Dumped Items (329 reports), Graffiti (190 reports), and E-Scooters (177 reports).

The average time for the city to close a "Dumped Items" case was about 29 hours. For "Graffiti" reports, the average was about 26 hours. Some services were handled more quickly, with "Animal Services" reports being closed in an average of about 3 hours. All 177 E-Scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

See an issue in your neighborhood? You can report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
