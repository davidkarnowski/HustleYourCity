An evaluation of Long Beach city service data over the last 90 days reveals the current volume and response times for major resident concerns. Dumped Items remains the highest volume category with 6,474 reports filed. The average time to resolve these requests is currently 13.6 days.

Graffiti removal received 3,187 reports, with an average completion time of 4.2 days. Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 781 reports and show an average response time of 5 days. Tree Maintenance is currently averaging 7.7 days for resolution across 583 reports.

Faster response times were recorded for sanitation and safety issues. Dead Animal Pickup averaged 11.3 hours per response, and Animal Services averaged 12.7 hours.

Residents are encouraged to report issues directly through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city receives and tracks these requests.
