Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 24-hour service call data to keep citizens informed about city response times and request volumes.

Over the past day, Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 93 calls, though the vast majority remain marked as new. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 17 calls and an average response time of 61 minutes.

Several service categories reported unusually fast response times. Street Repair averaged just 3 minutes across 9 reports, Sidewalk Repair averaged 10 minutes for 6 reports, and Vegetation averaged 9 minutes for 2 reports. Tree Maintenance also showed a rapid average response of 29 minutes for 5 calls. Citizens should note that these unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Other notable averages include Dead Animal Pickup, which handled 8 calls with an average response time of 63 minutes. Light maintenance requests took longer, averaging 10 hours across 7 reports, while Park Maintenance averaged about 6.5 hours for 4 reports.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help track neighborhood needs.
