Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 2,322 reports. The average response time for these issues was 8 days.

Graffiti removal saw 1,005 reports, with an average response time of 35 hours. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 863 reports, all of which were closed and referred to outside agencies or vendors.

Other notable service times include Dead Animal Pickup, which averaged a 6.8-hour response across 165 reports. Sidewalk Repair requests totaled 144, with an average response time of 23 hours. Park Maintenance handled 74 reports with an average response time of 3 days.

Please note that unusually fast response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual physical work being completed.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
