Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests. In total, residents submitted over 16,000 reports for various issues across the city.

The most frequent report was for "Dumped Items", with more than 6,300 requests. The average time to resolve these cases was about two weeks. In contrast, requests for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were among the fastest, typically handled in about 12 hours. The longest average wait time was for "Marina Repair" requests, which took about five weeks to close.

If you see an issue that needs attention, from graffiti to a broken streetlight, reporting it is the first step. Use the Go Long Beach service application to send reports directly to the city.
