Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times for the last 90 days.

The most frequent service request remains Dumped Items, with 6,579 total reports. The average response time for these issues is currently 11.1 days. Graffiti removal received 3,101 reports, with the city averaging a response time of 3.4 days.

Infrastructure requests such as Street Repair totaled 845 calls, with an average completion time of 5.4 days. Tree Maintenance saw 851 reports with a response average of 5.8 days. For urgent sanitation issues, Dead Animal Pickup handled 433 cases with an average response of 13.6 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than physical work completion.
