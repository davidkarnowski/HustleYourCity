Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data. In the past week, residents submitted over 1,450 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (647 calls) and Graffiti (281 calls).

Average response times for these top issues were about one day. The average for Dumped Items was 23.7 hours, and for Graffiti it was 25.3 hours. Other services, like Dead Animal Pickup, had an average response time of 10.9 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application. Please note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
