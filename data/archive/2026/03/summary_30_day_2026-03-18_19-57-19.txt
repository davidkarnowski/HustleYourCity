Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day city service request data to keep citizens informed on how quickly the City of Long Beach is responding to neighborhood issues. 

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 2,211 reports. The city took just over 5 days on average to respond to these cases. 

Graffiti removal saw 1,052 reports, with an average response time of about 1.5 days. Tree Maintenance had 498 requests taking just over 3 days on average, and Street Repair had 265 requests, also averaging just over 3 days for a response. 

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times, especially those under 90 minutes, may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. 

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 18, 2026, 12:53 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 18, 2026, 12:00 PM PDT.
