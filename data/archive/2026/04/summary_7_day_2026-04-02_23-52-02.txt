Hello Hustle Long Beach! community. Here is your latest independent accountability update on city service response times, focusing on data from the past 7 days.

Dumped items continue to generate the highest volume of service requests. In the last week, the city received 596 reports for dumped items, taking an average of 67.6 hours to address. E-scooter complaints followed with 258 reports, though all were immediately referred and closed administratively without a tracked response time. Graffiti removal saw 218 requests, with the city responding in an average of 25.3 hours.

For faster turnarounds, dead animal pickups handled 39 calls with an average response time of 3.3 hours. On the longer end of the spectrum, reports of illegal fireworks took an average of 4.9 days to process, and shoes on a utility wire took 3.5 days. Please note that unusually fast or immediate closures can sometimes reflect administrative routing or closures rather than completed field work.
