Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days. The most frequent reports from residents were for illegally dumped items (over 6,200 reports) and graffiti (over 3,300 reports).

Average response times vary by issue. Reports for dead animal pickups and animal services were handled relatively quickly, with average close times of about 13 to 14 hours. Graffiti removal requests took about 5 days on average to complete.

Other services saw longer waits. The average response time for illegally dumped items was about 13 days. More complex repairs, such as for marinas and damaged drinking fountains, took an average of 4 to 5 weeks to resolve.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to notify the city.
