Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen requests.

Over the past day, Dumped Items led the reports with 54 total calls. The recorded average response time for these was just 32 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 21 calls, and the majority of these are currently marked as in progress. Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services saw 4 and 3 calls respectively. All of these cases were closed, with Dead Animal Pickup averaging a 6.7-hour response time and Animal Services averaging a 5.9-hour response time.
