Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times for the last 30 days.

Dumped Items remained the most frequent request with 2,244 total reports. The average response time for these issues was approximately 5 and a half days. Graffiti reports followed with 1,154 total calls and an average response time of just under 2 days.

Street Repair requests totaled 259 and showed an average response time of about 3 and a half days. Tree Maintenance saw 439 reports with a response time of nearly 2 days.

Some categories showed faster turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup averaged 12 and a half hours. Reports regarding Business Licenses and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of 12 minutes. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
