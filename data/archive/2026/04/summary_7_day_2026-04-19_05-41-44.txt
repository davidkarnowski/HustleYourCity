Hustle Long Beach! is here with your latest city service accountability update. A review of the service call data from the last 7 days shows how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood issues.

Dumped items generated the most community concern with 556 total reports. The city averaged a response time of 65 hours for these requests. E-scooter complaints followed with 224 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies or closed. Graffiti remains a top issue with 194 calls and an average response time of 18 hours.

Some services showed very fast turnaround times. Dead animal pickups averaged a response time of 3.5 hours across 36 reports, and animal services averaged 5.5 hours across 14 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. All residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
