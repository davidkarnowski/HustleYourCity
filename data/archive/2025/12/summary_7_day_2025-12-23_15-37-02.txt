Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past week. Over 1,100 service requests were made by residents. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (448 calls), e-scooters (246 calls), and graffiti removal (236 calls).

Based on the city's data for closed requests, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 27 hours. Reports of illegally dumped items were addressed in an average of about 1.6 days. Dead animal pickups were completed in an average of about 11.5 hours. A single request for restroom maintenance was closed in about 51 minutes.
