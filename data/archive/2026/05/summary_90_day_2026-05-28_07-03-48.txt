The latest data update for city services in Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 3,273 graffiti reports with an average response time of approximately 2.3 days. Dumped item reports totaled 6,710, with an average response time of about 9 days.

Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging under 10 hours over the last 90 days. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
