Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest 30-day accountability summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past 30 days, the most frequently reported issues were Dumped Items with 2313 reports, Graffiti with 1070 reports, and E-Scooter concerns with 1036 reports. 

Response times vary significantly depending on the category. Dumped Items took an average of 8 days to address. Street Light issues averaged over 4 days. Graffiti and Tree Maintenance requests averaged about 51 hours for a response. Dead Animal Pickup was much faster, averaging about 7 hours.

Please keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures or referrals rather than the actual completion of physical work.
