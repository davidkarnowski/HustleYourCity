Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests. The most common issues reported were illegally dumped items (5,965 reports), graffiti (3,483 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,702 reports).

Average response times varied by service type. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 13 days, while graffiti removal took nearly 5 days. Street repairs averaged about one week. The longest waits were for marina repairs, which took about one month on average. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 15 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most direct way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
