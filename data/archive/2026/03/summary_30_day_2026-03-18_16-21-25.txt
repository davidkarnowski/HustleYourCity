Hustle Long Beach! community, here is your latest 30-day accountability update on city service response times.

Over the past month, Dumped Items and Graffiti generated the highest volume of service requests. The city received 2205 reports for Dumped Items, with an average response time of 5 days. Graffiti issues saw 1059 reports, averaging a 37.5-hour response time.

Tree Maintenance received 497 calls, taking about 3 days on average to address. Marina Repair requests took about 7.5 days to resolve. On the faster end, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep Long Beach accountable. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
