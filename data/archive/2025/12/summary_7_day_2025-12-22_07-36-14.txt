Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents made 1,158 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (429 calls), e-scooters (246 calls), and graffiti (235 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 21 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 33 hours on average. Sidewalk repair requests had a notably fast average response of about 45 minutes. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not always on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
