In the last 90 days Long Beach residents filed 3,562 graffiti reports. The city closed 2,892 of them, with an average response time of about 6.3 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of calls – 6,498 reports – and were closed in an average of roughly 15 days.

Animal Services handled 231 requests, closing 222. The average response was about 18 hours (under one day), showing a quick turnaround.

Dead animal pickup saw 451 reports, with 441 closed and an average response of 16.6 hours.

Tree maintenance received 832 requests; the average response was 63 hours (≈2.6 days).

Light outages (streetlights) had 489 reports, closed in an average of 60 hours (≈2.5 days).

Sidewalk repair logged 352 requests, with an average response of 91.6 hours (≈3.8 days).

Overall, most categories are seeing responses under a week, though larger issues like graffiti and dumped items still take several days to resolve.

Residents can report new issues or track existing ones using the Go Long Beach service app.
