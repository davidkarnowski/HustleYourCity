The latest city service data for Long Beach shows a high volume of requests over the last 30 days, particularly regarding dumped items and graffiti.

Dumped items remain a primary concern with 2,820 reports in the last month. While many are being addressed, a significant number remain in progress. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-frequency service, with 920 reports and an average response time of approximately 40 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 320 reports in the last 30 days with an average response time of about 2 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging between 8 and 13 hours.

Please remember that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city to ensure they are tracked and addressed.
