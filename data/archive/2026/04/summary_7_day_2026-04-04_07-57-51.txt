Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 7-day service call data to keep citizens informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood issues.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 587 reports. The city averaged a response time of 54 hours to process these requests. E-Scooter complaints followed with 264 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Graffiti removal saw 226 reports with an average response time of 21 hours. Dead Animal Pickup was addressed rapidly, averaging 2.5 hours across 39 reports. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

On the slower end, a single report for an Illegal Firework took over 5 days to process. Reports for Shoes on a Utility Wire took nearly 72 hours to address.

Help keep the community accountable and well-maintained. Hustle Long Beach! encourages all followers to report local issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
