Over the past 90 days Long Beach recorded roughly 15,900 service requests.  
The highest‑volume categories were Dumped Items (6,247 requests), Graffiti (3,696) and E‑Scooter issues (2,068).

Response times are improving. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were closed in an average of 17 hours and 16 hours respectively. Graffiti requests now average 165 hours, down from the historic 351 hours. Light and Tree Maintenance are being addressed in about 39 hours and 40 hours on average. Most categories have a closure rate above 90 percent.

Residents can report new issues quickly through the Go Long Beach service app.

This data summary was updated at 2025-11-27 03:28:00 PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 2025-11-27 03:00:21 PST.
