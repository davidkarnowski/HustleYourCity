Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past month, residents submitted thousands of requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items with 2,309 calls, followed by e-scooter issues with 1,014 calls, and graffiti removal with 974 calls.

The average time to resolve a request for dumped items was about 7.5 days. Graffiti removal requests were closed in an average of about 2.5 days, and street repair requests took about 2.7 days.

Note that all 1,014 e-scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies for handling. Some unusually fast closures, like certain tree maintenance requests closed in about 13 minutes, may be due to administrative updates rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
