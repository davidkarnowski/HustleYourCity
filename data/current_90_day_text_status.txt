Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on public data.

Residents submitted over 15,800 service requests during this period. The most common reports were for dumped items and graffiti, totaling nearly 10,000 requests combined. The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 15.5 days. For graffiti removal, the average was about 6.5 days.

Park maintenance requests had one of the longest response times, averaging nearly 20 days. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickup and animal services were among the fastest, typically resolved in about 16 hours and 15 hours, respectively. Note that some fast closure times may be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.
