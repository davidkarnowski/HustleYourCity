Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day city service data to keep you informed on local response times and request volumes.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 523 reports. The city averaged a response time of 3.6 days for these issues, though 392 requests currently remain untouched in the "New" status.

E-Scooter complaints followed with 235 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti issues saw 193 reports with an average response time of 25.5 hours.

Some services saw longer delays. Abandoned Shopping Cart reports took an average of 4.7 days to address.

On the faster end, Restroom Maintenance requests averaged a 35-minute response, and Community Center issues averaged 72 minutes. Please note that unusually fast response times under 90 minutes may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
