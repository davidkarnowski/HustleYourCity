Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day city service response data to keep you informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports.

Over the past month, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 2308 reports. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1087 calls, averaging a 37-hour response time.

Other notable service categories included E-Scooter complaints with 960 reports, and Tree Maintenance, which received 257 calls and averaged a response time of nearly 4 days. Street Repair saw 199 reports with an average response time of 3 days.

Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure your voice is heard. Hustle Long Beach! encourages all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
