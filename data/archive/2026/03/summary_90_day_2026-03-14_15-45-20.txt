Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the last 90 days. The numbers show how the City of Long Beach is managing public requests.

Dumped items generated the highest volume with 6,622 reports. The average response time for these requests was 10 days. Graffiti removal followed with 3,092 reports, averaging a 3-day response time.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also saw significant activity. Tree maintenance received 904 calls, and street repair received 835 calls. Both categories averaged a response time of 6 days. Dead animal pickups totaled 442 requests with an average response time of 13 hours.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
