In the last 90 days Long Beach residents filed 16,448 service requests across all categories.

The biggest volumes were:
- Dumped Items – 6,465 reports (average response ≈ 15.7 days)
- Graffiti – 3,677 reports (average response ≈ 5.4 days)
- E‑Scooter – 2,400 reports (mostly referrals, no response time recorded)

Other notable categories:
- Light repairs responded in about 2.2 days (average ≈ 52 hours)
- Sidewalk Repair took ≈ 3 days (average ≈ 71 hours)
- Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were resolved quickly, within ≈ 18 hours and ≈ 17 hours respectively
- Tree Maintenance, Street Repair, and Traffic Signal all averaged under 3 days

Overall, most services are being addressed within one to three days, though larger‑volume issues like Dumped Items still average over two weeks.

Residents can help keep the city accountable by reporting issues directly through the Go Long Beach service app.
