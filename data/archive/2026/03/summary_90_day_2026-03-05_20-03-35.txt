Data covering the last 90 days of service requests in Long Beach highlights current response times for high-volume issues.

Dumped Items remained the most frequent report type with 6,568 total requests. The average response time for these reports is currently 11.4 days. Graffiti removal followed with 3,083 reports, showing an average completion time of 3.4 days.
