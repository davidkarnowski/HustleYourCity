City of Long Beach service updates – last 7 days

• About 1,014 service calls were logged.
• Graffiti reports remain the highest volume (246 calls) with an average response time of 21 hours.
• E‑Scooter issues also high (252 calls) – all automatically closed as “referred.”
• Tree Maintenance responded quickly – average 1.2 hours for 33 requests.
• Sidewalk Repair and Street Repair showed fast turn‑around, averaging 0.27 hours (≈16 minutes) and 0.56 hours (≈34 minutes) respectively.
• Dumped Items (300 calls) were addressed in an average of 22 hours.
• Light outages (62 calls) took about 9.9 hours to respond.
• Traffic Signal work (7 calls) averaged 0.57 hours (≈34 minutes).

Overall, response times for many categories are under a day, though graffiti still takes longer than other issues.

Residents can report new issues or check status through the Go Long Beach service app.
