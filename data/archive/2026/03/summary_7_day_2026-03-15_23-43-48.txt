Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest look at city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped items continue to lead service requests with 580 reports this past week. The average response time for these issues was 55 hours. Graffiti removal also saw high volume with 218 reports and an average response time of 18 hours.
