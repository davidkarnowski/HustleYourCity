Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest independent accountability update evaluating City of Long Beach service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls this past week with 567 reports. The average response time for these requests was 3 days. E-Scooter complaints were the second most frequent with 270 reports, though all were referred elsewhere and lack response time data. Graffiti issues saw 198 reports, with the city averaging a 25-hour response time.

Other notable service areas include Light issues, which had 63 reports and an average response time of 54 hours. Street Repair received 56 calls, averaging a 25-hour response. Dead Animal Pickup handled 34 requests with an average response of 3.3 hours. Please note that unusually low response times across any category may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps track performance and ensures community needs are documented.

This data summary was updated at April 18, 2026 at 1:04 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 17, 2026 at 11:00 PM PDT.
