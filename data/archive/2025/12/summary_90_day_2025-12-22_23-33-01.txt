Here is a look at the latest Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items, with 6,070 requests, and graffiti, with 3,692 requests.

Response times for these top issues varied. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 6 days. For dumped items, the average response time was longer, at approximately 2 weeks. Park maintenance requests took an average of 17 days to close. In contrast, reports for dead animal pickups were handled more quickly, with an average response of about 16 hours. Some unusually fast response times can be the result of administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
