Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update, focusing on the last 90 days.

The most common issue reported by residents was "Dumped Items" with 6,306 requests. This was followed by "Graffiti" with 3,345 reports and "E-Scooter" issues with 2,660 reports.

Average city response times for these top issues vary. The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" request was about 13.5 days. For "Graffiti" removal, the average was about 5 days. In contrast, requests for "Dead Animal Pickup" were handled much faster, averaging around 13 hours to close. E-scooter reports are referred directly to vendors, so the city does not track a response time for them.
