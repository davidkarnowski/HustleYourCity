Here is a look at Long Beach city service request data from the last 90 days.

The most frequent requests from residents were for Dumped Items (6,467 reports) and Graffiti (3,265 reports). The average response time to close a Dumped Items request was about 14 days. For Graffiti, the average response time was about 4.5 days.

Other key service response times include Street Repair at about 5 days and Tree Maintenance at about 5.5 days. Requests for Dead Animal Pickup were among the fastest, with an average response time of about 12 hours. Marina Repair requests had the longest average response time at about 5 weeks.
