Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 90-day city service data to keep residents informed on local response times and call volumes.

Dumped Items continue to be the most reported issue, with 6,499 calls in the last 90 days. The city took an average of 10.7 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,111, with an average response time of 3.3 days. E-Scooter complaints reached 2,969, though all were referred to outside agencies rather than closed directly by the city.

Infrastructure requests like Tree Maintenance (879 calls) and Street Repair (841 calls) averaged response times of 5.7 days and 5.3 days, respectively. Light issues generated 1,041 calls and took an average of 3.3 days to address.

Some categories show exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help track city performance.
