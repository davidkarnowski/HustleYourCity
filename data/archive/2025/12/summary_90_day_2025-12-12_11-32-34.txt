Recent city service data shows mixed response times over the past 90 days.

Graffiti reports (3,714 total) are being closed in about 160 hours (≈6.7 days) on average, with 3,379 already resolved. Dumped Items (6,168 reports) take longer—average 367 hours (≈15 days) before closure. Street Repair requests (669) average 232 hours (≈9.7 days) to complete, while Light issues (653) average 192 hours (≈8 days).

Some services are much faster. Animal Services (203 reports) and Dead Animal Pickup (432 reports) are closed in roughly 17 hours each. Traffic Signal work (104 reports) averages 204 hours (≈8.5 days), and Sidewalk Repair (317 reports) averages 127 hours (≈5.3 days).

Overall, most categories are seeing responses within a few days, but larger‑scale issues like graffiti and dumped items still need several days to resolve.

Residents are encouraged to report problems promptly through the Go Long Beach app to help improve these times.
