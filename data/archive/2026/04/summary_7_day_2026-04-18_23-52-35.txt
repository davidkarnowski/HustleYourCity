Hustle Long Beach! community update: Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times and call volumes over the past 7 days.

Dumped items were the most reported issue, with 553 total calls. The city averaged a 66-hour response time for these requests, though 223 remain in the new status. E-scooter complaints followed with 222 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti removal saw 191 requests, with an average response time of 19 hours.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests remained steady. Street repairs generated 58 calls with a 20-hour average response time. Sidewalk repairs had 46 reports averaging 16 hours. Street light issues accounted for 63 reports, taking about 53 hours on average to resolve.

Some services saw rapid turnaround times. Dead animal pickups were handled quickly, with 34 reports averaging a response time of 3.6 hours. Animal services responded to 15 calls in an average of 7 hours. Tree maintenance requests averaged just under 10 hours for 34 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help keep neighborhoods well maintained.

This data summary was updated at April 18, 2026 4:50 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 18, 2026 3:00 PM PDT.
