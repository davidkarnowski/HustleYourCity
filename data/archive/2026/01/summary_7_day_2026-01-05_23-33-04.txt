Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Residents submitted over 1,200 requests through the Go Long Beach app and other channels.

The most frequent requests were for dumped items (436 reports), e-scooters (239 reports), and graffiti (170 reports). The average response time to close a dumped items request was about 2 days, while graffiti removal took about 1.3 days.

Response times for other services included approximately 18 hours for street repairs and about 13 hours for dead animal pickups. Requests for issues like trash and debris took longer, averaging about 3 days for a response. Note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
