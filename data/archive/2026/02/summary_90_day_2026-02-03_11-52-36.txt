Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service response data.

Over the past 90 days, "Dumped Items" was the most frequent request, with over 6,300 reports. The average time to close these requests was about 2 weeks. Other common reports included Graffiti (about 5 days), Street Repair (over 5 days), and Park Maintenance (about 8 days). The longest average response time was for Marina Repair, at about 5 weeks.

Faster responses were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services, with an average closure time of about 12 hours for each. Vegetation requests were handled in about 1.5 days on average. Note: Some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
