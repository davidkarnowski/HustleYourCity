In the past 30 days Long Beach reported 2,009 dumped‑item calls, with an average response time of about 207 hours (≈8.6 days). That’s a big improvement from the previous 60‑day average of 355 hours. Street repair requests (169 total) were answered in roughly 18 hours on average, down from 78 hours two months ago. Graffiti reports (1,389 total) now see a 48‑hour average response, compared with 85 hours before.

Tree maintenance (221 calls) and traffic‑signal issues (41 calls) also got faster service – average response times fell to 11 hours and 11 hours respectively, well under the 28‑hour and 41‑hour averages seen earlier. Sidewalk repairs (103 calls) are now handled in about 18 hours, a cut from nearly 38 hours previously.

Overall, response times are trending shorter across several service types, while the Go Long Beach app continues to collect new requests. Residents can report issues directly through the app to keep the city accountable and help maintain these gains.
