Hustle Long Beach! is back with the latest service call data to keep you informed about city operations. Over the past seven days, the City of Long Beach received a variety of service requests. Here is a factual look at the numbers and how quickly issues are being addressed.

The most reported issue was Dumped Items, with 606 total requests. The average response time for these items was 66.8 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed with 260 reports, all of which were referred to other agencies. Graffiti was the third most common issue, with 216 reports and an average response time of 25.1 hours.

Response times varied significantly depending on the service. Dead Animal Pickup had a fast turnaround, averaging 2.5 hours for 36 requests. Conversely, reports of Illegal Fireworks took an average of 4.9 days to process, and Shoes on a Utility Wire averaged 3.5 days. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues. Staying active in reporting helps maintain accountability and ensures the city is aware of community needs.
