Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data, and here is your accountability update for the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 580 reports. The city averaged a response time of 60.7 hours for these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 220 calls, seeing an average response time of 20.2 hours.

E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 141 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies or closed administratively.

On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup saw 37 reports with an average response time of 4.2 hours. Tree Maintenance received 58 calls and averaged a 6.8-hour response. 

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or referrals rather than the actual completion of physical work.
