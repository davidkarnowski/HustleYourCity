The latest data for Long Beach city services shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,328 reports for dumped items and 2,573 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickups remain the fastest, with an average response time of approximately 7 minutes. Graffiti removal is currently averaging about 60 hours, while street repair requests have averaged roughly 7 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work.
