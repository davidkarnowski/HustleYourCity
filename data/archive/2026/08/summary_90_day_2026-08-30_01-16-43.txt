The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across several categories over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 8,016 reports, followed by 3,074 graffiti reports and 3,027 e-scooter reports.

Response times have varied by service type. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to see the fastest turnaround, averaging 11 hours and 6 hours respectively. Meanwhile, dumped items are seeing an average response time of approximately 16 days. Graffiti removal is currently averaging about 3 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the progress of their requests.
