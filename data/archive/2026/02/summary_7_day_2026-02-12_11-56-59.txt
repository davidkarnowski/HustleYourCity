The latest service data for the past 7 days highlights response times for common requests in Long Beach.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 549 total reports, showing an average response time of 1 day and 4 hours. Graffiti followed with 257 reports and an average response time of 1 day and 9 hours.

Dead Animal Pickup handled 26 reports with an average response of roughly 4 hours. Street Repair and Sidewalk Repair requests averaged just under 1 day. Conversely, Trash and Debris reports averaged nearly 4 days for a response.

Business License concerns showed an average response of 30 minutes. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
