Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data. In the last 24 hours, residents submitted 182 new service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items and graffiti.

The average response time for closed "Dumped Items" requests was about 10 hours. "Graffiti" reports were addressed in an average of about 7 hours.

Some service types showed very fast response times. "Park Maintenance" requests were closed in an average of 29 minutes and "Tree Maintenance" in about 59 minutes. These quick closures may be due to administrative processing and not the completion of physical work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
