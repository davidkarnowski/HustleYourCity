Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,200 requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (441), improperly parked e-scooters (244), and graffiti (171).

Average response times for closed requests varied by issue. The city addressed graffiti reports in about 29.5 hours and dead animal pickups in about 6 hours. Slower responses included reports of shoes on utility wires, which took an average of about 3.4 days to resolve. Some requests, like those for e-scooters, are referred to private companies, so the city does not report a response time.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it through the Go Long Beach app. Your reports provide valuable data for tracking city services.
