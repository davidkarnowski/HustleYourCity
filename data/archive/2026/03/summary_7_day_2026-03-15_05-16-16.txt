Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days to keep you informed on how local issues are being handled.

Dumped items continue to generate the highest volume of service requests. Over the last week, the city received 562 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 57 hours. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 222 calls, which saw an average response time of 20 hours.

Other notable service areas include E-scooter complaints, which reached 216 reports. All of these were closed and referred to outside agencies. Dead animal pickups accounted for 41 reports with an average response time of 8 hours. Traffic signal issues were addressed in an average of 2.5 hours across 6 reported cases. Please note that unusually fast response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
