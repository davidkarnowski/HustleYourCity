Here is the latest Long Beach service data update for the past week.

Residents submitted 1,195 service requests. The most reported issues were for dumped items with 435 requests, followed by graffiti with 258 requests, and e-scooters with 257 requests.

Average response times for closed requests varied. Graffiti removal averaged about 40 hours, while traffic signal repairs took about 3.5 days. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of 57 minutes. Unusually fast responses may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 17, 2025, 8:01 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 17, 2025, 7:00 PM PST.
