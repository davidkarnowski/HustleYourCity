Here is a look at Long Beach service request data from the past 30 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally Dumped Items with 2,036 reports, followed by E-Scooter issues at 1,030 reports, and Graffiti with 1,016 reports.

Average city response times to close a service request varied. Reports for Dumped Items were closed in about 10 days. Graffiti reports were handled in about 54 hours on average. Park Maintenance requests took nearly 4 days to resolve, while requests for Dead Animal Pickup were completed in about 11.5 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to notify the city is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
