Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents made 412 requests to clean up dumped items, making it the most reported issue. The average response time to close these cases was about 39 hours. Graffiti was the next most common report with 191 requests, which took an average of 54 hours to resolve.

The city also received 100 requests for tree maintenance, with an average closure time of about 23 hours. Some services had unusually fast closure times, such as park maintenance at 37 minutes and shoes on a utility wire at 44 minutes. These quick resolutions may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
