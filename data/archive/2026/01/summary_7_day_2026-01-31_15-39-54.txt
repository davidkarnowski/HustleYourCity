Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data from the past week.

Reports for dumped items (589), graffiti (286), and e-scooters (261) were the most common requests submitted by the public. The city's data shows that e-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies for resolution.

Average response times for closed service requests varied. Graffiti removal took about 27 hours on average. Dumped item pickups were completed in an average of just over 2 days. Animal services calls were resolved in approximately 20 hours. Tree maintenance requests show an average completion time of about 8 hours; however, unusually low response times can sometimes reflect administrative closures and not on-site work.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
