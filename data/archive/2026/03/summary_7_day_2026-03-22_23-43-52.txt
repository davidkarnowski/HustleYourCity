Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 510 reports. The average response time was roughly 32 hours, though 417 of these requests currently remain in the new status.

Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 211 calls and an average response time of 18 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 185 reports, all of which were closed and referred to other entities.

On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup saw 43 reports with an average response time of 3 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

For issues taking longer to resolve, Marina Repair requests averaged 3.7 days to process.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 22, 2026, 4:41 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 22, 2026, 4:00 PM PDT.
