Graffiti reports in the last 90 days total 3,666 with an average response time of about 135 hours (≈5.6 days). That’s a big drop from the all‑time average of 351 hours.

Dumped items saw 6,481 requests and were closed on average in 375 hours (≈15.6 days), down from the historic 260 hours average.

Light‑related issues (549 requests) have an average response of 52 hours (≈2 days), far faster than the historic 1,023 hours.

Tree maintenance (815 requests) is being handled in about 56 hours (≈2.3 days), versus the previous 3,782 hours.

Animal services (234 requests) and dead‑animal pickups (464 requests) are both resolved in under 20 hours on average, a notable improvement over the older averages of 3,945 hours and 4,616 hours.

Overall, response times have improved across most categories. Some unusually low times may reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completion.
