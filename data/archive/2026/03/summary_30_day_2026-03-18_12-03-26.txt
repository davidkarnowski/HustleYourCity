Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues. In the last 30 days, citizens reported 2,204 dumped items, which took the city an average of 5 days to resolve. Graffiti was reported 1,064 times, with an average response time of 38 hours.

Other notable service requests include street repairs, which totaled 263 reports and took about 3 days on average to address. Park maintenance requests totaled 70 and took an average of 3.5 days to complete. Dead animal pickups saw 146 reports and were handled in roughly 15 hours.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Keep in mind that extremely low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep Long Beach in top shape by reporting issues when you see them. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
