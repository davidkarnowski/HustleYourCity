Hustle Long Beach! Community Data Update: Last 90 Days

The latest data evaluation covers city service response times and volumes over the past three months. Dumped Items remains the most reported issue with 6,434 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 13 days.

Graffiti removal received 3,080 reports with an average completion time of nearly 4 days. Street Repair generated 793 reports, taking just over 4 and a half days on average to resolve. Dead Animal Pickup remains a faster service, with 427 reports averaging under 13 hours per response.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
