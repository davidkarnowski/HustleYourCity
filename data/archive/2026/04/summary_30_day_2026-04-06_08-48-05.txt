Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest 30-day accountability summary of city service requests.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items led the request totals with 2,290 reports, averaging a response time of about 8 days. Graffiti followed with 981 reports, taking an average of 32 hours to address. E-Scooter complaints reached 935, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Dead Animal Pickup saw 162 requests with a rapid average response time of 5 hours. Animal Services handled 79 calls, averaging nearly 9 hours. Please note that unusually low response times can sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions. On the longer end, Marina Repair requests averaged nearly 7 days, and reports of Illegal Fireworks took over 4 days to process.
