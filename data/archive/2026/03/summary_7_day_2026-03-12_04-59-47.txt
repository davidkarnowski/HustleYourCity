Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day city service request data to keep you informed on how quickly issues are being addressed across the city.

Over the past week, Dumped Items were the most reported issue with 506 calls. The city took an average of 3.2 days to respond to these requests. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 250 reports, all of which were immediately referred to outside operators.

Graffiti removal saw 232 requests with an average response time of 33 hours. Tree Maintenance received 83 reports, taking an average of 21.8 hours to process.

Some services saw much faster turnaround times. Animal Services handled 22 calls in an average of 7.9 hours, and Dead Animal Pickup managed 39 calls in about 8 hours. Traffic Signal issues were addressed in an average of 3.6 hours across 6 reports.

Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual physical work being completed.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 11, 2026, 9:57 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 11, 2026, 8:00 PM PDT.
