The latest city service data for the past 90 days shows significant activity across several categories. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,850 total calls, averaging a response time of approximately 16 days.

Graffiti removal saw 2,644 requests with an average response time of about 61 hours. Tree maintenance requests totaled 1,065 with an average response time of about 4 days. Dead animal pickup continues to be one of the fastest services, with 540 calls handled in an average of about 7 hours.

Some categories show high numbers of new or duplicate reports, which can impact average response metrics. Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
