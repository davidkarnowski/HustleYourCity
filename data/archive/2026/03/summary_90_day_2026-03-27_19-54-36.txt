Here is the latest 90-day accountability update from Hustle Long Beach! evaluating city service response times. This summary breaks down how long it takes for the City of Long Beach to address citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 6,909 requests in the last 90 days. The city takes an average of 9.5 days to resolve these reports. Graffiti follows with 3,111 reports, averaging a 65-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints also saw high volume at 2,861 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests show longer wait times. Street repair, with 865 requests, and tree maintenance, with 946 requests, are taking about 6.5 days and 5.5 days on average, respectively. Sidewalk repairs are averaging nearly 4 days for a response across 318 requests.

Some services show much faster turnaround times. Dead animal pickups are handled in an average of 11.5 hours. Business license and permit concerns show an unusually fast average response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps track these metrics and holds the city accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 27, 2026, 12:50 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 27, 2026, 12:00 PM PDT.
