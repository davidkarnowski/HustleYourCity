Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest third-party accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of service requests with 453 reports. The city took an average of 64.4 hours to respond to these issues. E-Scooter complaints followed with 252 reports, all of which were closed and referred to other entities. Graffiti was the third most reported issue with 222 calls, showing an average response time of 32.5 hours.

Other notable service categories included Tree Maintenance, which saw 82 requests with a 16.5-hour average response time, and Street Repair, which had 59 reports and a 24.9-hour average response. Dead Animal Pickup handled 35 calls with an average response time of 9.5 hours.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Running Water reports averaged a response time of just 13 minutes. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps ensure accurate data and better accountability for Long Beach.
