Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the city is handling community reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 2047 reports. The average response time for these issues was 4.7 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1087 calls, taking an average of 44.6 hours to address.

There were 1029 E-Scooter complaints, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Tree Maintenance received 466 reports with an average response time of 47.3 hours.

Some categories showed unusually fast turnaround times. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests and help track city performance.
