Here is a look at Long Beach's service request data for the past 90 days. The most frequent reports from residents were for illegally dumped items, with over 6,000 requests submitted. This was followed by reports for graffiti (3,390) and e-scooters (2,716).

Average response times for closing these cases varied. Dumped item reports took an average of about 12.5 days to resolve. Graffiti removal requests were faster, averaging just under 5 days. Park maintenance requests averaged about 10.5 days.

Some of the quickest responses were for urgent issues like dead animal pickups and other animal services, which were typically resolved in about 14 to 15 hours. A small number of reports for damaged drinking fountains were closed in just over 2 hours, though this may reflect administrative closures rather than completed repairs.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
