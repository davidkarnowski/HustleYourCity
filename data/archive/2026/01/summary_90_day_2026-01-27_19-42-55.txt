Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

The most frequent requests from the public were for "Dumped Items" (6,197 reports) and "Graffiti" (3,336 reports). The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" request was about 13 days. "Graffiti" reports were closed in about 5 days on average.

Some of the fastest responses included "Animal Services" and "Dead Animal Pickup", which were both handled in an average of about 12 hours. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of about 3 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
