Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Over 15,800 service requests were filed by residents. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,167 requests), graffiti (3,735 requests), and e-scooters (2,213 requests).

Based on city data, the average time to close a graffiti report was about 6.5 days. Reports of dumped items took an average of 15 days to resolve, while street repairs were addressed in about 9.5 days. Animal-related services were handled much faster, typically within 17 hours. Some recent data, such as for sidewalk repairs, shows unusually low response times that may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 19, 2025, 3:29 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 18, 2025, 11:00 PM PST.
