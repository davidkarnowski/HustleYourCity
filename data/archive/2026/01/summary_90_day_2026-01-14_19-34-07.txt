Here is our latest analysis of Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Over the last 90 days, residents submitted thousands of service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items, with 5,990 requests, and graffiti, with 3,453 requests.

Looking at average response times for closed cases, the city took about 13 days to resolve reports of dumped items. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in an average of about 4.5 days. Response times for other services varied, from about 16 hours for dead animal pickups to over a month for marina repairs. Note that some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
