Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest 30-day city service data summary evaluating Long Beach response times and call totals.

Over the past 30 days, the highest volume of service requests included 2,327 reports for Dumped Items, 1,100 for Graffiti, and 1,001 for E-Scooter issues. 

The average response time for Dumped Items was about 8.5 days. Graffiti reports averaged a 40-hour response time. Street Repair requests, totaling 213 calls, averaged a 64.5-hour response. Light-related issues took about 3.4 days to address on average. Please note that unusually low response times in the data may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
