Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests. In the last month, residents submitted nearly 5,900 service requests.

The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,301 calls), e-scooter issues (974 calls), and graffiti (959 calls).

Here is a look at the average city response times for closing a service request:
- Graffiti: about 2.7 days
- Street Light Outages: about 3 days
- Street Repair: just under 3 days
- Illegally Dumped Items: about 8 days

Note: E-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies and do not have a city response time.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
