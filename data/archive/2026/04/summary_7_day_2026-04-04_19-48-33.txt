Here is the latest 7-day city service data update from Hustle Long Beach! This summary provides a look at service request volumes and response times to keep the public informed on how city issues are being handled.

Over the past week, Dumped Items led the service requests with 583 reports, averaging a response time of about 50.6 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed with 235 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti was the third most reported issue, with 219 calls and an average response time of 15.2 hours.

Other notable service areas included Light issues with 74 reports averaging 44.5 hours for a response, and Tree Maintenance with 53 reports averaging 18.5 hours. Dead Animal Pickup saw 38 requests with a rapid average response of 2.5 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city. Staying active in reporting helps maintain accountability and improves the Long Beach community.
