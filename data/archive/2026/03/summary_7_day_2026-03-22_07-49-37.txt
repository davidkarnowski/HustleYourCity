Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at what citizens are reporting and how fast the City of Long Beach is responding to these issues.

Dumped items led the week with 521 reports, averaging a 39-hour response time. Graffiti followed with 216 requests and an average response time of about 19 hours. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 147 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Dead animal pickups saw very fast turnaround times, averaging just 3 hours for 37 calls. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical work. On the longer end of the spectrum, marina repairs averaged about 3.8 days.
