Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service requests.

Data from the last 90 days shows significant activity in city service requests. The most frequent report category was Dumped Items with 6,452 total requests. The average response time for these issues is currently approximately 13 days.

Graffiti removal remains a high-volume category with 3,106 reports filed. The average time to resolve these incidents is roughly 4 days. Street Repair requests totaled 797, with crews taking about 4 and a half days to close tickets on average.

Some categories show longer wait times. Marina Repair requests are averaging 23 days for response. Conversely, concerns regarding Business Licenses are closing in about 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may indicate administrative closures rather than physical work completion.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
