Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service requests from the past week.

Over 1,000 service requests were submitted by residents. The most common reports were for dumped items (347 calls), graffiti (188 calls), and e-scooters (172 calls).

Average city response times for closed requests included 1.5 days for graffiti removal and just over one day for dumped item pickups. Animal-related calls were handled quickly, with dead animal pickups closed in about 6.5 hours. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of 32 minutes; however, unusually fast responses can be due to administrative closures and may not reflect on-site work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 29, 2025, 7:35 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 29, 2025, 7:00 AM PST.
