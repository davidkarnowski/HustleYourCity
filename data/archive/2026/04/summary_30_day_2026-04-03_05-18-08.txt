Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest 30-day accountability report on city service requests.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 2330 requests in the last 30 days. The average response time for these is about 8 days. Graffiti is the second most reported issue with 942 calls, averaging a response time of 35 hours.

Street repair requests totaled 221, taking an average of 3.4 days to address. Dead animal pickups saw 166 reports and are handled in about 5 hours. There were also 880 E-Scooter complaints, though these show no average response time because they are immediately referred and closed. Please note that unusually low response times across some categories may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to stay engaged and report local issues directly using the Go Long Beach service application to help hold the city accountable.
