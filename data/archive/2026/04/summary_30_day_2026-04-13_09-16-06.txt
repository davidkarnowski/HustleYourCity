Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service call data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at how city services performed over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items led the requests with 2,274 reports, taking an average of 7.9 days to address. Graffiti removal saw 1,086 reports with an average response time of 36 hours. E-Scooter complaints were also high at 956, though response times were not tracked for these referrals.

For infrastructure and maintenance, Street Repair handled 204 requests with an average response of 71 hours. Tree Maintenance received 246 requests, taking about 65 hours to process. Park Maintenance handled 69 calls, taking an average of 3.8 days to respond. 

Faster turnarounds were seen in Dead Animal Pickup, which had 165 calls handled in about 7 hours on average, and Animal Services, which resolved 79 calls in about 8.5 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
