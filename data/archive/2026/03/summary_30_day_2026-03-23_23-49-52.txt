Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day city service request data. Over the past month, the highest volume of citizen reports involved Dumped Items with 2,247 requests, followed by Graffiti with 1,016 requests, and E-Scooter issues with 942 requests.

Response times vary significantly by category. The city averaged 5.7 days to respond to Dumped Items and 4.5 days for Park Maintenance. Reports of Illegal Fireworks took nearly 6 days for a response, while Marina Repairs averaged over 1 week. Trash and Debris requests took 3 days on average.

Other services saw faster turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup averaged 9 hours, and Running Water concerns averaged 12 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit and track requests.
