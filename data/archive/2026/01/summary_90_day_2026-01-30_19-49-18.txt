Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped items (6,271 reports) and graffiti (3,339 reports) were the most frequent issues reported by residents. In total, the city received over 16,000 service requests during this period.

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 5 days. Reports of illegally dumped items took an average of nearly 14 days to resolve. Street repair requests were addressed in an average of about 5.4 days. Some issues were handled more quickly, such as dead animal pickups, which had an average response time of 13.4 hours. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
