Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped Items led the service requests with 6,942 reports. The average response time for these issues was about 8.6 days.

Graffiti removal saw 3,205 reports, with an average response time of 57 hours.

Street Repair had 747 calls, averaging 7.1 days for a response, while Tree Maintenance had 906 calls, averaging 4.6 days.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnarounds. For example, Business License and Permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
