Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data based on requests made in the last 7 days.

In the past week, residents submitted over 1,500 service requests. The most frequent report was for illegally dumped items, with 665 new requests. The average time to resolve these reports was about 3.5 days. The second most common issue was graffiti, with 287 reports, which were addressed in an average of 31 hours.

Response times for other services varied. Reports for street repair took about 24.5 hours on average to close, while calls for animal services were resolved in under 3 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
