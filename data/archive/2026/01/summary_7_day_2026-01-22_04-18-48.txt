Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 511 requests. The average time to resolve these requests was about 3.5 days. Graffiti was another top issue with 224 reports, and the average response time for these was about 32 hours. For comparison, the 45 requests for dead animal pickups were handled more quickly, with an average response time of about 5 hours. Response times reflect the average time to close a service request and can vary based on complexity and workload.
