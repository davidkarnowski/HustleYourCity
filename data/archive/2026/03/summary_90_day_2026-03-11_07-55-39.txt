Here is the latest 90-day city service response data analyzed by Hustle Long Beach! This summary provides a look at how long it takes for the City of Long Beach to address citizen reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 6,539 reports. The average response time for these requests is 10.5 days. Graffiti removal is the second most reported issue with 3,111 calls, taking an average of 3.2 days to address.

Other notable service times over the last 90 days include Tree Maintenance, which had 887 reports and an average response time of 5.8 days. Street Repair saw 838 reports with an average response time of 5.6 days. Marina Repair requests took the longest to resolve, averaging 1.5 months for 6 reports.

On the faster side, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Tracking these statistics helps keep the local government accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 11, 2026 12:51 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 11, 2026 12:00 AM PDT.
