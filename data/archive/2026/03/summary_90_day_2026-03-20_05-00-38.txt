Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data. Over the last 90 days, residents submitted thousands of requests, and here are the facts on how the City of Long Beach responded.

Dumped items generated the highest call volume with 6,661 reports. The city took an average of nearly 10 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal saw 3,093 reports with an average response time of 3 days.

Infrastructure requests like street repair with 830 calls and tree maintenance with 928 calls both averaged a response time of about 7 days. Sidewalk repairs had 302 calls and averaged a response time of nearly 4 days.

Some services saw much faster turnaround times. Dead animal pickups had 428 calls and averaged 12 hours. Business license and permit concerns had 6 calls and averaged just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests and help track city performance.
