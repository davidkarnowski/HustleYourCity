Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past seven days to keep the public informed on local government performance.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 487 reports. The city took an average of 3.2 days to respond to these requests. E-Scooter complaints were the second most frequent with 242 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies. Graffiti issues accounted for 223 calls, with an average response time of 34 hours.

Certain categories recorded exceptionally fast turnaround times. Running Water reports averaged a response time of just 13 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services also saw quick administrative action, averaging about 7.5 hours per response.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Submitting requests through the app helps ensure that local infrastructure and neighborhoods receive necessary attention.

This data summary was updated at March 11, 2026, 4:47 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 11, 2026, 4:00 AM PDT.
