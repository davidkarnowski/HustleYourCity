Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the last 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2121 requests in the past month. The city took an average of 4.7 days to respond to these calls. Graffiti removal saw 1087 reports, averaging a 43.5-hour response time. E-scooter complaints were also high at 993 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies.

Street repair requests totaled 268 with an average response time of 3 days. Park maintenance took about 4 days on average to address 65 requests. Trash and debris calls averaged a 4.5-day response time across 44 reports.

Some categories showed very fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
