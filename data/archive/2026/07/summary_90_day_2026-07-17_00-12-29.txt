The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 6,851 reports for dumped items and 3,295 reports regarding graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickups remain the most efficient, with an average response time of approximately 5.6 hours. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
