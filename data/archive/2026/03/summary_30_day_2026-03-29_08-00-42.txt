Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Over the last 30 days, Dumped Items generated the most reports with 2,219 calls. Graffiti followed with 983 reports, and E-Scooter complaints totaled 902.

Response times vary significantly by category. The city took an average of 7.3 days to address Dumped Items and 4.8 days for Park Maintenance. Street Repair requests averaged 3.1 days. Faster turnarounds were seen for Graffiti at 40.5 hours and Dead Animal Pickup at 8.8 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep the community accountable and well-maintained. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
