Here is a look at Long Beach service call data for the past week.

Reports for illegally dumped items were the most frequent issue, with 610 new service calls. Graffiti was the next most common report with 269 calls, followed by 213 reports related to e-scooters.

For cases closed in the last 7 days, the average response time for dumped items was about 2.6 days. The city responded faster to other issues, with graffiti removal averaging about 17 hours and dead animal pickups averaging about 5 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
