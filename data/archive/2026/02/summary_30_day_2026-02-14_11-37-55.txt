Here is the latest independent review of Long Beach city service requests for the past 30 days. The data shows significant activity in waste management and maintenance.

Dumped Items remain the most reported issue with 2,462 total requests. The average response time for these reports is currently sitting at roughly 7 and a half days. Graffiti removal is the second most active category with 1,113 reports, showing an average turnaround time of just over 2 days.

Infrastructure repairs show varied response times. Street Repair requests, totaling 230 reports, are being addressed in about 3 and a half days on average. Street light issues, with 409 reports, are taking nearly 3 days to resolve.

Response times for urgent biological hazards remain quick. Dead Animal Pickup requests are averaging roughly 8 hours for completion, while general Animal Services calls are being handled in about 12 hours.

Please note that very low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than physical work completion.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting these issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
