Hustle Long Beach! is here with your latest 90-day accountability update on city service response times. As an independent data interpreter, Hustle Long Beach analyzes the numbers so citizens know exactly how long it takes for reported issues to be resolved.

Over the last 90 days, Dumped Items led all service requests with 6,957 reports, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,222 calls, showing an average response time of 55 hours.

Infrastructure issues like Street Repair and Sidewalk Repair are currently taking an average of 7.1 days and 5.2 days to address, respectively. 

On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup averages a 10.8-hour response. Interestingly, Business License and Permit Concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Keep in mind that unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
