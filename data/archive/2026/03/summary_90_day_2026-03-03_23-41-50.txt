An analysis of city service requests over the past 90 days reveals current response times for the most common issues reported by Long Beach residents.

Dumped Items remains the highest volume category with 6,592 total reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 12 days.

Graffiti removal services received 3,115 reports in this period. The data shows an average completion time of about 3.5 days.

Street Repair requests, which include potholes and general road maintenance, totaled 842 reports. The city is averaging a 5-day response time for these issues.

Dead Animal Pickup services are responding faster than other categories, with an average wait time of roughly 12.5 hours across 429 reports.

Residents can assist in data collection and issue resolution by using the Go Long Beach service application to report maintenance needs.
