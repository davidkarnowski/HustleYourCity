Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service call data from the City of Long Beach. Looking at the last 90 days, the data shows how long it takes for the city to respond to citizen reports.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 6993 reports. The average response time for these requests was about 8.5 days.

Graffiti removal saw 3233 reports, with the city responding in approximately 56 hours on average.

Tree Maintenance had 891 calls, taking about 9 days on average for a response. Street Repair had 712 reports with an average response time of 8 days.

Some services showed very fast turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup handled 468 reports in an average of 9.8 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes for 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues. Reporting issues directly helps track city performance and ensures accountability.
