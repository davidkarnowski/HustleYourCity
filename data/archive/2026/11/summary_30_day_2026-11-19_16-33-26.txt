The latest city service data for Long Beach shows a high volume of requests over the last 30 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 2,282 reports, followed by 1,052 reports for e-scooters and 866 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Dead animal pickup continues to be the fastest service, averaging about 6 minutes per response. In contrast, sidewalk repair requests are taking longer to address, with an average response time of approximately 7 days.

Please note that some categories show low response times that may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
