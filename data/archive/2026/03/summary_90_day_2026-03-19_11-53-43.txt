Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data to track how the City of Long Beach is responding to community needs. Over the past 90 days, the city received thousands of reports, and the data shows a wide range of response times depending on the issue.

The highest volume of service requests came from Dumped Items with 6,656 reports. This was followed by Graffiti with 3,082 reports and E-Scooter issues with 2,808 reports. 

Response times for these major issues vary. The average response time for Dumped Items is currently taking about 9.8 days. Tree Maintenance requests, which totaled 915 calls, are averaging 6.8 days for a response. Street Repair is also taking about 6.9 days to address.

On the faster side, Graffiti reports are being addressed in about 69 hours, and Dead Animal Pickups are taking roughly 12 hours. Some categories show unusually fast resolution times, such as Business License and Permit Concerns averaging just 12 minutes. It is important to note that these extremely low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent public reporting provides the data necessary to track service quality and hold city departments accountable.
