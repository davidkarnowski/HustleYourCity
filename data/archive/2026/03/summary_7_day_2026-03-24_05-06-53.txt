Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day service call data to keep you informed on city response times and request volumes.

Over the past week, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 489 reports, showing an average response time of 27.6 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed with 231 reports. Graffiti issues accounted for 194 reports, with the city averaging 26.9 hours to respond.

The fastest recorded response time was for Community Center requests, averaging just 72 minutes. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickup also showed fast processing, averaging 3.6 hours across 41 requests.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help hold the city accountable.
