Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest 90-day city service request data to keep residents informed on response times and call volumes.

Over the last 90 days, Dumped Items led the request board with 6,736 reports, taking an average of about 9.7 days to resolve. Graffiti followed with 3,076 reports, averaging a 71-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints generated 2,822 reports, all of which were immediately categorized as "Closed Referred" by the city system.

Several services took nearly a week or more to address. Trash and Debris requests averaged 7.9 days, while Street Repair and Tree Maintenance both averaged roughly 6.7 days. 

On the faster side, Business License and Permit Concerns were closed in just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickups averaged a response of about 11.7 hours.

Help keep the city accountable and neighborhoods clean. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
