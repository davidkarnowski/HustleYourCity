Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,484 service requests. The most reported issue was illegally dumped items, with 586 requests. This was followed by graffiti with 242 requests and e-scooter issues with 223 requests.

The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 46 hours. Graffiti reports were addressed more quickly, with an average response of about 21 hours. All e-scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
