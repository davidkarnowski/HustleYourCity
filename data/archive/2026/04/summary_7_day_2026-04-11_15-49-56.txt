Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service requests with 527 reports. The city averaged a response time of 65.5 hours for these issues. Graffiti followed closely with 339 reports and an average response time of 25.0 hours.

E-Scooter complaints accounted for 274 reports. Data shows that 273 of these were closed and referred elsewhere, which means they were likely passed on to the scooter operators rather than handled by city staff.

Other notable service categories included Light issues with 56 reports averaging a 31.6-hour response, and Dead Animal Pickup with 43 reports averaging a 15.6-hour response.

Please note that unusually fast response times in the data may be the result of administrative closures or referrals, and do not always mean the physical work was completed that quickly.

Hustle Long Beach encourages all residents to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Download and use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests and help track the city's performance.
