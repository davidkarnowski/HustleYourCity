Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days. The most frequent reports from residents were for Dumped Items (6,115 requests), Graffiti (3,742 requests), and E-Scooters (2,212 requests).

Average response times for closed service requests varied. Park Maintenance requests took about 19 days to resolve on average, while Dumped Items took around 15 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in about 6.5 days. Faster responses were seen for Dead Animal Pickup, averaging about 16 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city.
