Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,240 service requests were made in the last seven days. The most common reports were for dumped items (452 requests), graffiti (206 requests), and e-scooters (180 requests).

Average response times for several key services were around 1.5 days, including dumped items, graffiti, and tree maintenance. Dead animal pickups were handled more quickly, averaging about 16 hours. Streetlight and traffic signal repairs took longer, averaging about 2.5 days to close a request.

Some services showed very fast closure times. Park Maintenance requests were closed in about 37 minutes and Restroom Maintenance in about 85 minutes. These unusually low times may be due to administrative closures and not work completion.
