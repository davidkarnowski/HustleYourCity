Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service call data for the past week.

Over 1,370 service requests were submitted by residents. The most common reports were for dumped items (547 calls), improperly parked e-scooters (217 calls), and graffiti (200 calls).

The city's average response time to close graffiti reports was about a day and a half. Dumped item reports were addressed in just under 2 days on average, while street repair requests were closed in about 21 hours. E-scooter reports are referred directly to vendors and do not have a city response time.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application. Your reports provide valuable data for tracking and improving city services.
