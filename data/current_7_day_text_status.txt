Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past week. The most frequent service requests were for Dumped Items (475 calls), Graffiti (266 calls), and E-Scooters (255 calls). E-Scooter reports are automatically referred to the scooter companies.

The average time to resolve a 'Graffiti' report was about 39 hours. For 'Dumped Items', the average was about 53 hours. Dead Animal Pickup requests were handled in an average of 23 hours, with all 35 reports from this week now closed.

Sidewalk Repair requests showed an average close time of 45 minutes. This unusually fast response may be due to administrative closures and not the completion of physical repairs.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
