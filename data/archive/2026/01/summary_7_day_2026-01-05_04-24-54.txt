Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,229 service requests. The most reported issues were illegally dumped items (452 requests), e-scooter issues (225), and graffiti (182).

For closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 25 hours. Reports for dumped items were addressed in about 42 hours on average. The average time to close trash and debris reports was just over 3 days. Restroom maintenance requests were closed in an average of about 83 minutes; however, very fast response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
