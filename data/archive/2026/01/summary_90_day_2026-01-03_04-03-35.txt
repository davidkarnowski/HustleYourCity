Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past 90 days, the most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (5,861 requests), graffiti (3,604 requests), and streetlights (741 requests).

Average response times for closing requests varied. Graffiti removal took just under 5 days. Reports for dumped items were resolved in about 14.5 days. Park maintenance requests averaged nearly 17 days to close. Quicker responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 17 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the best way to get it into the city's system.
