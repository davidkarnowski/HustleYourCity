The Long Beach community logged 5,782 service requests in the past 30 days.

Graffiti reports (1,445) were closed in an average of 44 hours (about 2 days). Tree maintenance (262) had the fastest turnaround, averaging just 10 hours. Light issues (244) were resolved in roughly 29 hours, while sidewalk repairs (116) took about 16 hours on average.

Animal services (57) and dead‑animal pickups (156) were addressed in about 25 hours and 22 hours on average, showing steady performance.

Overall, most categories are being resolved within a day or two, but the high average for dumped items highlights an area for improvement.
