Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items, graffiti, and e-scooter problems. In the last 90 days, the city received 5,841 reports for dumped items and 3,600 for graffiti.

Average response times varied by issue. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about two weeks on average. Graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of about five days. More urgent requests, such as those for Animal Services or Dead Animal Pickup, were typically resolved in under 18 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at January 1, 2026, at 11:34 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 1, 2026, at 11:00 PM PST.
