Service call activity in Long Beach over the past 30 days shows several notable trends.

- About 5,060 requests were logged across key categories.
- Graffiti reports dropped to an average response time of 67 hours (≈2.8 days), down from 167 hours in the prior 90‑day period.
- Dumped Items were handled in an average of 246 hours (≈10 days), an improvement from 385 hours previously.
- Sidewalk Repair requests are now resolved in roughly 50 hours (≈2 days), and Light issues in about 80 hours (≈3.3 days).
- Street Repair still averages 167 hours (≈7 days) to close.
- Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services are being completed quickly, averaging 13 hours and 14 hours respectively.
- Vegetation and Abandoned Shopping Cart requests are resolved in about 1.5 days and 2 days.

Overall, response times for graffiti and dumped items have improved noticeably, while some repair categories remain longer.

Residents are encouraged to report issues through the Go Long Beach service app to help maintain these gains.
