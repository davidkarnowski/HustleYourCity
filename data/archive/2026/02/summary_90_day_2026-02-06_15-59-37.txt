Here's the latest 90-day snapshot of Long Beach service requests.

Over the past 90 days, residents made 15,508 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,391 calls), graffiti (3,278 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,654 calls).

Average city response times varied by issue. Graffiti removal requests were closed in about 4.7 days on average. Reports of dumped items took an average of nearly 14 days to resolve. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups and animal services calls, which both averaged about 12 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at February 6, 2026, at 7:56 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at February 6, 2026, at 7:00 AM PST.
