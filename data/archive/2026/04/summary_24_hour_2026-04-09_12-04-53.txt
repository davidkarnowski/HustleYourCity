Here is the latest Hustle Long Beach! community update evaluating city service response times over the past 24 hours.

In the last day, residents submitted 171 total service requests. Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 66 reports, followed by Graffiti with 34 reports, and Light issues with 14 reports.

When looking at response times, several categories reported unusually fast closures. Animal Services averaged 49 minutes, Tree Maintenance averaged 51 minutes, Vegetation averaged 51 minutes, and Sidewalk Repair averaged 57 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Other notable response times include Trash and Debris at 88 minutes, Graffiti at 3 hours, and Dead Animal Pickup at 4 hours. Categories like Dumped Items and Street Repair currently have no average response times for this period because the vast majority of these requests are still marked as new or in progress.
