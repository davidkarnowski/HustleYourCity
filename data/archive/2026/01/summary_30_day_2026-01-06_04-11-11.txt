Here is the latest Long Beach city service data summary for the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents submitted more than 5,100 service requests. The most frequent reports were for "Dumped Items" (1,891 requests) and "E-Scooters" (1,013 requests). Requests for illegally parked e-scooters are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

Average city response times for closed cases varied by issue. The average for "Dumped Items" was about 8 days. "Graffiti" removal requests were handled more quickly, with an average response time of about 2 days. Reports for "Traffic Signal" issues took an average of nearly 6 days to resolve.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
