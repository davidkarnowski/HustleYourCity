Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,400 new service requests were made. The most common reports were for dumped items (655 requests), graffiti (282 requests), and e-scooter issues (185 requests).

The average response time to close a report for dumped items was about 20 hours. For graffiti, the average was about 26 hours. Reports for abandoned shopping carts were closed in an average of 39 minutes. Unusually fast response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures and not work performed.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to notify the city is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
