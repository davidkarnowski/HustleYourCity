Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days, based on the latest public data.

Over the last three months, the most frequent service requests were for dumped items (6,344 reports), graffiti (3,277 reports), and e-scooters (2,685 reports). The average time to resolve a report for dumped items was about 14 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in an average of just under 5 days.

Some services saw quicker responses. Reports for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup were typically closed in about 12.5 hours. In contrast, the average response time for Marina Repair requests was about 5 weeks. Street repair requests were completed in an average of about 5 days.

Reporting issues helps the city track and address community needs. Residents can report non-emergency issues like graffiti, potholes, and dumped items using the Go Long Beach app.
