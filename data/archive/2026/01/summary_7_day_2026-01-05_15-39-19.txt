Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

The top three issues reported by residents were dumped items (447 requests), e-scooter issues (226 requests), and graffiti removal (175 requests).

Average city response times for closed requests varied. Graffiti removal took about 25 hours, while dumped item pickups averaged 41 hours. Street repair requests were addressed in about 10 hours. Trash and debris clean-up took longer, averaging just over 3 days. Some categories, like restroom maintenance, show unusually fast response times of about 83 minutes, which may be due to administrative closures and not on-site work.
