Here is the latest look at Long Beach service request data from the past 30 days.

Illegally dumped items remain the most reported issue with 1,924 requests. The average response time for these requests was about 7.5 days. Graffiti was the second most common report with 1,147 requests, which were addressed in about 2 days on average.

Other key service times include street repairs, which took about 4.5 days to close, and tree maintenance, which averaged a 3.5-day response. Quicker responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 9 hours on average. Some rapid closures may be administrative and not reflect on-site work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to notify the city and track its progress.

This data summary was updated at December 16, 2025, at 3:30 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 16, 2025, at 3:00 AM PST.
