Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the last 30 days.

Over 5,000 service requests were made by the public. The most common reports were for dumped items (1,932 requests), graffiti (1,071 requests), and e-scooters (989 requests).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 62 hours. Reports for dumped items took longer, averaging about 6 days to close. Requests for dead animal pickup were typically resolved in about 12 hours. Some service types show very fast response times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
