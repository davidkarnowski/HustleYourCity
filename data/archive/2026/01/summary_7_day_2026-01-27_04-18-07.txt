Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week. Residents submitted nearly 1,500 requests for service. The most common reports were for dumped items (639 calls), graffiti (294 calls), and e-scooter issues (222 calls).

According to city data, the average response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 3.5 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in about one day on average, as were requests for street and sidewalk repairs. Some requests may show unusually fast completion times, which can reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
