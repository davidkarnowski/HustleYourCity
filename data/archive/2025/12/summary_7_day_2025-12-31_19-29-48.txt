Here is the latest weekly summary of Long Beach service call data. In the past seven days, residents submitted over 1,100 service requests.

The most common reports were for dumped items (427 requests), graffiti (191 requests), and e-scooter issues (171 requests).

Average city response times for closed requests included about 38 hours for dumped items and about 45 hours for graffiti removal. The average response for dead animal pickup was about 7 hours. Some services, like park and restroom maintenance, had unusually fast response times of about 37 and 85 minutes respectively, which may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
