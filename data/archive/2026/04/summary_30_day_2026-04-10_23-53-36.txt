Hustle Long Beach! presents the latest 30-day accountability summary of city service response times. This independent review highlights how the City of Long Beach is managing public requests.

Dumped items remain the highest volume issue with 2345 reports over the past month. The average response time for these requests is approximately 8 days. Graffiti follows closely with 1087 reports, which the city is closing or addressing in an average of 37 hours. 

E-scooter complaints generated 956 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies rather than resolved directly by city staff. For public safety and sanitation, dead animal pickups saw 167 reports with an average response time of 7 hours. 

Infrastructure and maintenance requests take longer to process. Tree maintenance, with 254 reports, averages a 3.5-day response, while street repairs, with 201 reports, take about 3.4 days. Citizens should note that unusually low response times across any category may be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Public data and community action drive accountability. Residents are encouraged to download and use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 10, 2026 at 4:50 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 10, 2026 at 3:00 PM PDT.
