Here is a look at the city's service response data for the past 90 days. The most frequent issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items, with 5,864 requests, and graffiti, with 3,606 requests.

Average response times varied by issue. The city closed graffiti reports in about 5 days on average. Requests for dumped item pickups took longer, averaging about 14.5 days. More urgent requests, such as dead animal pickups and other animal services calls, were resolved much faster, typically within 16 to 17 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, from potholes to park maintenance, reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify city departments.
