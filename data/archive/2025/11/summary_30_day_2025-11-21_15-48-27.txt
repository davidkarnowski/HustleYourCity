City of Long Beach service updates – last 30 days

• Street Repair: 196 requests, average response 20.4 hours (≈0.85 days). This is a sharp drop from 102 hours in the prior 90‑day window.  
• Graffiti: 1,389 reports, average response 64.5 hours (≈2.7 days), down from 146 hours three months ago.  
• Dumped Items: 2,066 calls, average response 186 hours (≈7.8 days), improved from 366 hours in the previous period.  
• Tree Maintenance: 241 requests, average response 9.9 hours – a notable speed‑up versus 53 hours three months earlier.  
• Light repairs: 240 cases, average response 38.8 hours (≈1.6 days).  
• Sidewalk Repair: 101 jobs, average response 13.3 hours, faster than the 57 hours seen in the last 90 days.  

Overall, response times are trending shorter across most service categories, indicating quicker city action on resident reports.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues through the Go Long Beach app for faster assistance.
