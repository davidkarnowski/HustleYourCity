Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the past 90 days.

Over the last 90 days, residents submitted more than 15,500 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (5,894 requests), graffiti (3,605 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,485 requests).

Average response times for closed requests varied by service type. Reports for dumped items were addressed in about 15 days on average, while graffiti removal took about 5 days. Slower responses included marina repairs, which averaged about 26 days. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups (about 17 hours) and animal services (about 16 hours).

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.

This data summary was updated at December 31, 2025, at 11:32 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 31, 2025, at 11:00 PM PST.
