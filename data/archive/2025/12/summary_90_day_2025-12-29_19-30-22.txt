Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past 90 days, the most reported issues were illegally dumped items, with 5,894 requests, and graffiti, with 3,617 requests.

Average city response times for closed requests varied by issue. Graffiti removal took about 5 days. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 15 days. Park maintenance requests took roughly 2.5 weeks. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 16 hours.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
