In the past 30 days Long Beach residents filed 1,995 Dumped Items reports – the largest volume this month – with an average response time of about 156 hours (≈6.5 days).  

Graffiti calls totaled 1,315, and crews responded in an average of 47 hours (≈2 days), down from 163 hours over the last 90 days.  

Tree Maintenance saw 274 requests and the average response fell dramatically to 11.5 hours, compared with 67 hours in the prior 90 days and 37 hours in the last 60 days.  

Other notable averages: Light 222 reports (28.9 hours), Traffic Signal 44 reports (43.1 hours), Sidewalk Repair 111 reports (16.1 hours), and Street Repair 180 reports (27.9 hours).  

Overall, response times are improving across several service categories. Residents are encouraged to continue using the Go Long Beach app to report issues quickly.
