Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data from the last 90 days. Here is a factual breakdown of call volumes and average response times.

Dumped items and graffiti were the most frequently reported issues. The city received 6,880 reports for dumped items, which took an average of 9.4 days to resolve. Graffiti complaints totaled 3,106, with an average response time of 2.6 days.

Other notable service requests included 857 calls for street repair, which averaged a 6.3-day response time. Dead animal pickups accounted for 430 requests and were handled in an average of 11.2 hours. 

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a 12-minute response time. It is important to note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
