Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the past 90 days, the most frequent reports from residents were for Dumped Items (5,900 requests) and Graffiti (3,553 requests). The city's average response time to close a Graffiti report was about 4.7 days. For Dumped Items, the average response time was about 14 days.

Response times varied across other services. Animal Services requests were handled in about 16 hours on average, making it one of the faster responses. In contrast, Marina Repair requests had the longest average completion time at about 26 days. Over 2,600 reports for E-Scooters were referred directly to operators, so the city does not provide a response time for those.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application. Your reports provide data that helps track the city's performance.
