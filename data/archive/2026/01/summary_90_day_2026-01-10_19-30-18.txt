Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past 90 days, the most frequently reported issues were illegally dumped items (5,955 reports), graffiti (3,567 reports), and e-scooter problems (2,669 reports).

The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about four and a half days. For dumped items, the average response time was about two weeks. Street repairs were addressed in about eight days on average. Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which averaged about 16 hours.

If you see an issue that needs attention, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at January 10, 2026 at 11:27 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 10, 2026 at 11:00 AM PST.
