Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data. Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of reports, providing a clear picture of current response times and top community concerns.

The highest volume of service requests involved Dumped Items with 6,835 reports, followed by Graffiti with 3,089 reports, and E-Scooter issues with 2,832 reports. Other significant categories included Light issues at 978 reports and Tree Maintenance at 926 reports.

Response times vary significantly by category. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to Dumped Items and 5 days to address Tree Maintenance. Street Repair requests averaged a response time of 6 days. Graffiti removal averaged a 63-hour response time.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Followers are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Download and use the Go Long Beach service application to submit requests directly to the city and help keep the community accountable.
