Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to citizen reports over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items remain the most frequently reported issue with 7,046 requests. The city takes an average of 9 days to respond to these reports. Graffiti is the second most common issue with 3,177 reports, showing an average response time of 2.4 days. E-Scooter complaints reached 2,864, though these are entirely referred to outside vendors.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests show longer wait times. Street repair requests, totaling 797 calls, take nearly 7 days on average to address. Tree maintenance saw 921 calls and averages a 5-day response time. 

On the faster end, business license and permit concerns are being closed in just 12 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead animal pickups are handled more quickly, with 447 calls averaging a 10-hour response.

Accountability starts with reporting. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 5, 2026 8:47 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 5, 2026 7:00 AM PDT.
