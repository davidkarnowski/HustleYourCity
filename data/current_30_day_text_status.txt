The latest data for city services in Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 30 days. Residents submitted 2,419 requests for dumped items and 829 reports regarding graffiti.

Response times for these categories have been tracked to help citizens understand service delivery. Graffiti reports saw an average response time of approximately 56 hours, while dumped item requests averaged about 7 days. Dead animal pickup remains one of the fastest services, with an average response time of approximately 7 hours.

Some service categories show a high volume of new requests that are currently in progress or awaiting action. For example, tree maintenance had 359 total requests in the last 30 days, with 187 currently marked as new.

Please remember that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work. To help the city track and address these issues, please continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report concerns in your neighborhood.
