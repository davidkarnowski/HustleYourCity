Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times for the last 90 days.

The most frequent service request was for Dumped Items, totaling 6,438 calls. The average response time for these issues was approximately 2 weeks. Graffiti reports followed with 3,159 calls, averaging a response time of 4 days.
