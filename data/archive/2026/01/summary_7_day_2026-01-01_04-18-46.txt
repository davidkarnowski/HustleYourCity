Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,195 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (446 requests), graffiti (198 requests), and e-scooters (174 requests).

Based on the latest data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 1.75 days. Dumped item pickups were resolved in about 1.5 days on average, while street repair requests took an average of about 1.3 days. Reports for lights out took longer, averaging about 2.8 days for resolution.

Some services showed unusually fast response times. Park maintenance requests were closed in an average of about 37 minutes. These quick closures may be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.
