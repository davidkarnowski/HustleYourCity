Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day service call data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at how the city is responding to citizen reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of calls over the past week with 556 reports. The city took an average of 3.4 days to respond to these requests. 

Graffiti removal was the second most reported issue, with 211 calls and an average response time of 25.4 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 202 reports.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Running Water reports averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickup handled 39 reports with an average response time of 3.5 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 1, 2026, 5:00 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 1, 2026, 3:00 AM PDT.
