Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,400 new service requests were submitted by residents. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (604 requests), graffiti (267 requests), and e-scooter issues (213 requests).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 17 hours. For illegally dumped items, the average response time was about 2.5 days. Animal-related calls were typically handled in 3 to 5 hours. E-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies and do not have a city response time. Note that some fast closure times can be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.
