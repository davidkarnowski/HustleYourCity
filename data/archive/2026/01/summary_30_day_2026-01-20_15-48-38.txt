Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Response times for some of the most common service requests have improved. The average time to close a "Dumped Items" report is now approximately 7.5 days, down from over 12 days two months ago. For "Graffiti" reports, the average response time has decreased to about 52 hours, down from over 84 hours.

In the last 30 days, residents submitted over 5,400 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (2,054 reports), e-scooters (987 reports), and graffiti (893 reports).

To report issues like these in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.
