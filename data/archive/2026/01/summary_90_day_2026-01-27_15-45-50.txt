Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the last 90 days.

Dumped Items and Graffiti continue to be the most reported issues, with over 6,100 and 3,300 calls respectively. E-scooter reports were also high, with more than 2,700 submissions.

Average response times for some key services extend over several days. Reports for Dumped Items took an average of 13 days to close. Graffiti removal averaged 5 days, and Street Repair requests were closed in about 5.5 days.

Urgent issues saw faster responses. The city closed requests for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup in about 12 hours on average.
