Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest 90-day accountability update on Long Beach city service response times.

Dumped Items continue to generate the highest volume of service requests. Over the last 90 days, citizens submitted 6,641 reports for dumped items, with the city taking an average of 10 days to respond. Graffiti removal saw 3,069 reports, averaging a 3-day response time.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests also show significant volume. The city received 905 tree maintenance reports and 828 street repair reports, both averaging about 6 days for a response. Sidewalk repairs generated 294 reports with an average response time of 3.5 days.

Some categories show extremely fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Meanwhile, dead animal pickups are averaging a 13-hour response time across 443 reports.

Accountability starts with reporting. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 15, 2026, 12:42 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 15, 2026, 12:00 PM PDT.
