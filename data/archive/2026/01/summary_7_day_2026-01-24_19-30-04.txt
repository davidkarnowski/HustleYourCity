Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,343 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (598 requests), graffiti (237 requests), and e-scooters (167 requests).

Based on closed service requests, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 22 hours. The average for dumped item reports was just under 3 days. Dead animal pickups were completed in about 6 hours on average. Some requests, like single reports for restroom or community center maintenance, were closed in under 12 hours, which may reflect administrative updates rather than on-site work.
