Hustle Long Beach! is back with your latest government accountability update. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled citizen service requests over the past 7 days.

Dumped items led the weekly reports with 597 total calls. The city took an average of 3.5 days to respond to these requests, with 81 cases closed and 200 currently in progress. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 295 calls. The city averaged a 45-hour response time for graffiti, successfully closing 211 of those cases.

E-scooter complaints reached 275, though nearly all were referred to outside vendors rather than handled directly by city staff. On the faster end, Animal Services averaged a response time of just under 4 hours for its 19 calls, and dead animal pickups averaged nearly 6 hours across 36 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
