Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest accountability update on city service requests over the past 30 days.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to drive the highest volume of service calls. In the last month, residents reported 2113 dumped items and 1077 graffiti issues. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 954 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Response times vary significantly depending on the type of issue. The city took an average of 4.6 days to respond to dumped items, while graffiti was addressed in about 43 hours. Dead animal pickups averaged a 14-hour response time. On the longer end, marina repairs took an average of 7.2 days to process.

Some response times were unusually fast. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that extremely low response times may be the result of administrative ticket closures rather than actual work completions.

Help keep Long Beach accountable and well maintained. If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it directly using the Go Long Beach service application.
