Here is the latest Hustle Long Beach! community accountability update regarding city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped Items and Graffiti continue to drive the highest volume of service requests. There were 6,921 reports for Dumped Items, which took an average of 8.4 days to resolve. Graffiti saw 3,191 reports with an average response time of 55.5 hours. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 2,909 reports, though all of these were referred to outside agencies.

Other major service areas included Tree Maintenance with 901 calls averaging a 6-day response time, and Street Repair with 732 calls averaging 6.8 days. Dead Animal Pickup handled 458 calls efficiently, averaging a 10.8-hour response.

Some categories show exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help keep the community well-maintained.

This data summary was updated at April 9, 2026, at 10:33 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 9, 2026, at 7:00 PM PDT.
