Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 90 days.

Dumped Items continue to generate the highest call volume with 6,927 reports. The average response time for these requests is currently 8.4 days.

Graffiti reports are the second most common issue, totaling 3,192 calls. The city is responding to these in an average of 55.5 hours.

Street Repair and Tree Maintenance also saw significant activity. Street Repair handled 734 calls with an average response time of 6.8 days, while Tree Maintenance received 901 calls, averaging 6 days for a response.

Some categories show unusually fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that exceptionally low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
