Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped Items lead the requests with 6,557 reports, taking an average of about 10 days to resolve. Graffiti removal saw 3,103 requests, with an average response time of just over 3 days. 

Tree Maintenance and Street Repair had 884 and 838 requests respectively, both averaging nearly 6 days for a response. Dead Animal Pickup handled 438 calls with an average response time of 13 hours.

Business License and Permit Concerns showed an unusually fast average response time of 12 minutes for 6 requests. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
