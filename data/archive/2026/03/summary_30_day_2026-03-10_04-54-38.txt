Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data for the past 30 days. Here is a factual breakdown of the most reported issues and how long it takes the city to address them.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume request with 2,044 reports. The average response time for these items is 4.7 days.

Graffiti removal saw 1,089 reports with an average response time of 44 hours. E-Scooter complaints reached 1,028, though all of these were referred to outside agencies for handling.

Tree Maintenance received 466 requests with an average response time of 47 hours, while Street Repair had 271 reports taking an average of 70 hours to address. Park Maintenance received 60 requests and took an average of 3.9 days to resolve.

Some categories showed unusually fast turnaround times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Reporting problems directly helps track service efficiency and keeps the local government accountable.
