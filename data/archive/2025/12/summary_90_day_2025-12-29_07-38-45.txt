Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

The most frequent service request was for "Dumped Items", with over 5,900 reports. The average time to close these requests was about 15 days. "Graffiti" was the second most common report, with over 3,600 requests and an average closure time of about 5 days.

Other key response times include street light repairs, which took about one week on average. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of about 4 days, while dead animal pickups were handled in about 16 hours. Some requests show unusually fast closure times, which may be due to administrative processing and not on-site work completion.
