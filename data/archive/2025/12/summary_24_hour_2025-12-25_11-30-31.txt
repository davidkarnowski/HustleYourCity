Here is a look at Long Beach service requests from the last 24 hours. A total of 88 new service requests were made by residents.

The most common reports were for Dumped Items (35 requests), Tree Maintenance (19 requests), and Graffiti (16 requests).

For cases closed during this period, some response times were unusually fast. These fast closures may be administrative and not reflect on-site work. In contrast, closed requests for Light issues took an average of about 7 hours.
