Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times from the past 30 days.

The most frequent service request was for "Dumped Items", with 2,012 reports filed. According to city data, these issues were resolved in about 4 days on average. The next most common reports were for e-scooters (988) and graffiti (898). Graffiti clean-up requests were handled in an average of about 2 days.

Response times varied across services. Requests for "Dead Animal Pickup" were among the fastest, closed in about 13 hours on average. In contrast, reports for "Illegal Fireworks" and "Trash & Debris" took an average of about 5 days to be closed by the city.
