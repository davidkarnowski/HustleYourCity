Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with over 6,100 requests, followed by graffiti with more than 3,700 reports.

Average city response times for some key services include approximately 17 days for Park Maintenance and 15 days for Dumped Items. Graffiti removal requests were closed in about 6 days on average. Faster responses were recorded for Animal Services (15 hours) and Dead Animal Pickup (17 hours). It is important to note that some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures and not the completion of physical work.

Reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to get them into the city's queue.

This data summary was updated at December 21, 2025, at 3:27 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 20, 2025, at 11:00 PM PST.
