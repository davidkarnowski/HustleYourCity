Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service data for the last 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 527 total calls. The average response time for these issues was approximately 55 hours. Graffiti reports followed with 250 total calls and an average response time of roughly 30 hours.

Street Repair and Tree Maintenance requests were addressed quickly this week. Street Repair averaged 17 hours for 70 calls, while Tree Maintenance averaged 12 hours for 65 calls. Light repair requests totaled 66 calls with an average response time of 3.8 days.

Some response times appear low. Be aware that unusually fast closures may be due to administrative updates rather than physical work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues.
