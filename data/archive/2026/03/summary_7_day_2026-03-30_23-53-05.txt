Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past seven days. Here is the breakdown of what citizens are reporting and how fast the city is responding.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 524 requests in the last week. The average response time for these reports is currently about 4 days. Graffiti removal saw 230 reports, with the city averaging a 19.5-hour response time and successfully closing 160 of those cases. E-scooter complaints also remained high with 196 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Some services are showing extremely fast turnaround times. Restroom maintenance and reports of shoes on utility wires both averaged response times of 50 minutes and 44 minutes respectively. Note that these unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead animal pickup handled 41 calls with an average response time of just over 5 hours, closing 40 of those requests.

Help keep the Long Beach community maintained by reporting local issues. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
