Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest 24-hour accountability report on City of Long Beach service requests.

Over the past day, residents submitted 146 service requests. Dumped Items led the volume with 63 reports, though the vast majority remain in the "New" status. Graffiti and E-Scooter issues followed with 23 reports each.

Several categories showed unusually fast average response times. Animal Services averaged just 13 minutes, Tree Maintenance averaged 17 minutes, and Dead Animal Pickup averaged 82 minutes. It is important to note that these exceptionally low response times may be due to administrative closures or system referrals rather than actual physical work being completed on-site.

For issues taking a bit longer, Graffiti reports averaged a 12.5-hour response time, while Vegetation and Street Repair requests hovered around 15 hours.
