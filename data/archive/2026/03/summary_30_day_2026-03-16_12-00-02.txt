Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at how city services are responding to citizen reports.

Dumped items and graffiti remain the most frequently reported issues. Over the past 30 days, citizens submitted 2111 reports for dumped items and 1048 reports for graffiti. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to dumped items, while graffiti reports were addressed in about 42 hours.

Street repair requests totaled 262, with an average response time of 3 days. Tree maintenance saw 482 reports, averaging a 53-hour response time.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
