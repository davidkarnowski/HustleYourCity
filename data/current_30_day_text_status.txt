Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted nearly 2,000 requests for dumped item pickups and over 1,100 for graffiti removal, making them the most reported issues. The average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.5 days, while dumped item pickups took an average of 7 days to resolve.

Other key response times include street repairs, which were addressed in about 4 days on average, and tree maintenance in just under 2.5 days. Faster responses were seen for dead animal pickups and animal services calls, both averaging around 10 hours. It is important to note that some response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
