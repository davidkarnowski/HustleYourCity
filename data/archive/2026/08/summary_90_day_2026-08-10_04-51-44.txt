The latest data from the City of Long Beach service call system shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,478 reports regarding dumped items and 3,284 reports concerning graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickups remain the fastest, averaging about 5 minutes. Conversely, dumped items have seen an average response time of approximately 8 days. Please note that unusually low response times for some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to continue using the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues. This tool remains the most effective way to notify the city of concerns and track the status of your requests.
