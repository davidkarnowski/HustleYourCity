The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across several categories over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 8,445 reports logged. While many are resolved, the average response time for these requests is approximately 17 days.

Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,938 reports. The average response time for graffiti cleanup has improved to approximately 74 hours.

Dead animal pickup remains one of the fastest services, with 582 reports processed in an average of 6 minutes.
