Hustle Long Beach! has processed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. This summary provides an independent look at call totals and response times to keep the public informed about local government performance.

The highest volume of citizen reports over the last month involved Dumped Items with 2008 calls, Graffiti with 1070 calls, and E-Scooter complaints with 1011 calls. 

For services taking longer than 72 hours, the city averaged 4.8 days to respond to Dumped Items and 3.1 days for Street Repair requests. 

Other common issues like Graffiti and Tree Maintenance saw average response times of 42.6 hours and 43.5 hours, respectively. 

Some categories showed unusually fast turnaround times. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help track neighborhood needs.
