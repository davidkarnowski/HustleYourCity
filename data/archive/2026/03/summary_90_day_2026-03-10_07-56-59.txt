Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest 90-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests.

Over the last 90 days, Dumped Items led the service requests with 6,502 reports. This was followed by Graffiti with 3,109 reports and E-Scooter issues with 2,970 reports.

Response times vary significantly by category. The city took an average of 10.7 days to respond to Dumped Items and 3.3 days for Graffiti. Dead Animal Pickup requests were handled much faster, averaging 13.3 hours.

Some categories show exceptionally fast response times, such as Business License and Permit Concerns averaging just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
