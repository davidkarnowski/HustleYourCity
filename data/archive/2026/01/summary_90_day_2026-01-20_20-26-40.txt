Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, illegal dumping was the most reported issue with over 6,000 service calls, followed by graffiti with nearly 3,400 reports. The average city response time to close out a dumped item request was approximately 13 days. Marina repair requests had the longest average completion time at over 3 weeks.

Some services saw quicker responses. Requests for dead animal pickup and other animal services were typically handled in about 14 to 15 hours. The average response for damaged drinking fountains was just over 2 hours, though this low figure may reflect administrative closures rather than completed repairs.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
