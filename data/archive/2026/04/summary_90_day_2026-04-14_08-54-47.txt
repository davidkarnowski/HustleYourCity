Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a summary of call totals and average response times over the last 90 days.

Dumped items were the most reported issue, totaling 6,941 calls. The city averaged 8.5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti followed with 3,264 reports and an average response time of 2.3 days.

Other major service requests included tree maintenance, which had 897 reports and an average response time of 8.2 days. Street repair requests totaled 708, with the city taking an average of 7.8 days to respond.

Some categories recorded very fast turnarounds. For instance, business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
