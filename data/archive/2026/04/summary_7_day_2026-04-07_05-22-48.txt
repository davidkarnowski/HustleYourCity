Hustle Long Beach! is here with the latest accountability update on city service response times. An analysis of the Long Beach service call data from the last 7 days shows how quickly issues are being handled.

Dumped items generated the most service requests with 556 calls. The city took an average of 3.4 days to respond to these reports. E-scooter complaints followed closely with 281 calls, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Graffiti removal saw 261 reports, with response times averaging 35 hours. Sidewalk repairs had 51 requests, averaging a 7.5-hour response time. Dead animal pickups accounted for 36 calls, averaging 2.5 hours for a response.

Please note that unusually fast response times, especially those under 90 minutes, may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to help keep Long Beach running smoothly. If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
