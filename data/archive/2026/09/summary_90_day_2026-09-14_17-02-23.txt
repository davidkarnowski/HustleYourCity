The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 8,330 reports for dumped items and 2,989 reports for graffiti. Response times for these categories averaged 18 days and 68 hours respectively.

Dead animal pickup remains one of the most efficient services, with 599 reports handled in an average of 6.5 hours. Meanwhile, animal services reports were closed in an average of 11 hours. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and addressed by the appropriate departments.
