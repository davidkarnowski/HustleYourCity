Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 30 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue, with 2,291 requests in the last month. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these cases. Graffiti was the second most common concern, generating 1,058 reports with an average response time of 52 hours. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 1,041 reports, though response times were not tracked for these referrals. Street light issues generated 237 reports and took an average of 4 days to address.

Some services showed rapid turnaround times. Dead animal pickups averaged a 7-hour response across 166 reports, and animal services averaged an 8-hour response for 80 reports. Please note that unusually low response times can sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests and help keep the city accountable.
