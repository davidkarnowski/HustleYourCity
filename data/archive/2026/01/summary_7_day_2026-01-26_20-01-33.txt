Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Reports of illegally dumped items were the most frequent issue, with 588 requests. The average time to close these cases was just over 3 days. Graffiti was the second most common report with 272 requests, which were addressed in about 23 hours on average.

Of the 95 requests for street light issues, 73 are still new in the system. Animal-related services saw quick responses, with dead animal pickups averaging about 4 hours and other animal service calls averaging about 3 hours.
