In the past 90 days Long Beach residents filed about 16,168 service requests.

The biggest category was Dumped Items with 6,392 reports. The average response time was 362 hours (about 15 days), and 896 of those requests are still in progress.

Graffiti complaints numbered 3,698 with an average response of 150 hours (roughly 6 days). Sidewalk Repair (340 reports) and Street Repair (628 reports) are seeing faster turn‑around, averaging 49.5 hours (just over 2 days) and 99.5 hours (about 4 days) respectively.

Light outages (580 reports) and Tree Maintenance (822 reports) are also being addressed quickly, with average response times of 42 hours and 52 hours.

Animal Services (234 reports) and Dead Animal Pickup (457 reports) have the shortest response times, averaging 17.8 hours and 16.9 hours – under a day for most cases.

Overall, many service types are still “In Progress,” indicating ongoing work to improve city responsiveness.

Residents are encouraged to report issues and track progress using the Go Long Beach service app.

This data summary was updated at 11:28:00 AM PST on 2025-11-21 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00:25 AM PST on 2025-11-21.
