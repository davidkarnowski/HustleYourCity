The latest data for city service requests over the last 90 days shows varying response times across key categories.

Dumped items remain a high-volume concern with 7,352 total requests and an average response time of approximately 19 days. Tree maintenance saw 1,045 requests with an average response time of about 8 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a priority with 2,592 requests and an average response time of roughly 61 hours.

Dead animal pickup remains one of the fastest services, averaging about 7 hours per request across 550 incidents. Meanwhile, sidewalk repairs have seen 327 requests with an average response time of about 4 days.
