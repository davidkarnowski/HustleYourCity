Latest 30‑day service request summary for Long Beach

In the past month the city recorded about 5,130 service requests across all categories. The most common issues were:

- Graffiti: 1,346 reports, average response time ≈ 64 hours (about 2.7 days). This is faster than the 92‑hour average seen in the prior 60 days.  
- Dumped Items: 1,968 reports, average response time ≈ 195 hours (about 8 days), down from 312 hours previously.  
- Street Repair: 208 reports, average response time ≈ 23 hours (just under 1 day), a significant improvement from 77 hours two months ago.  
- Tree Maintenance: 196 reports, average response time ≈ 11 hours, well under a day.  
- Light repairs: 256 reports, average response time ≈ 40 hours (≈ 1.7 days).

Other categories with notable activity include Park Maintenance (50 reports), Trash & Debris (46 reports), Sidewalk Repair (78 reports) and Animal Services (56 reports). Most response times are under two days, indicating quicker handling of many issues.
