Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service data. Dumped items were the most reported issue, with 2,310 requests submitted. This was followed by 976 reports for e-scooters and 953 for graffiti removal.

The average city response time to close a report for dumped items was approximately 7.5 days. In contrast, requests for graffiti removal were addressed in under 3 days on average. The quickest responses were for animal-related services, with both "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" requests resolved in about 11 to 12 hours.

It is important to note that all e-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling. Additionally, some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than the completion of physical work.

To report issues like potholes, graffiti, or dumped items in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.
