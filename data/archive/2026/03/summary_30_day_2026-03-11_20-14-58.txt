Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day service call data to keep citizens informed on city response times.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 2,103 total requests. The average response time was about 4.7 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,080 calls, averaging a 43.7-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints remained frequent with 949 reports, all of which were closed and referred to other entities.

Other notable areas include Street Repair, which saw 269 requests and averaged 3.2 days for a response, and Park Maintenance, which took about 4.1 days to respond to 67 calls. Tree Maintenance handled 475 requests with an average response time of 48.3 hours.

On the faster end, Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes for 6 requests. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets addressed. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
