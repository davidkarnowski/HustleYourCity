Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

Residents submitted thousands of requests, with the most common being for illegally dumped items (5,951 reports) and graffiti (3,674 reports). The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about two weeks, while graffiti removal took about 5.5 days. Street light repairs were completed in about one week on average.

Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups (about 16 hours) and animal services calls (about 15 hours). Some services show very quick closure times, which can be due to administrative processing and not immediate work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most direct way to notify the city.

This data summary was updated at December 27, 2025, at 11:26 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 27, 2025, at 11:00 AM PST.
