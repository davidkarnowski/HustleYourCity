Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,000 service requests were submitted by residents. The most common reports were for dumped items (379 calls), graffiti (195 calls), and illegally parked e-scooters (165 calls).

The average time to close a graffiti report was just over 3 days. Reports for dead animal pickups were addressed in about 3 hours on average. Sidewalk repair requests show an average closure time of only 6 minutes. This unusually fast time may be due to administrative processing rather than the completion of physical repairs.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it is the first step to getting it fixed. Use the Go Long Beach app to submit a service request.

This data summary was updated at December 29, 2025, 11:32 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 29, 2025, 11:00 PM PST.
