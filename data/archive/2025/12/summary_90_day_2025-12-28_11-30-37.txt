Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped items and graffiti. The city handled 5,953 reports for dumped items with an average response time of about 15 days. For the 3,657 graffiti reports, the average completion time was around 5 days.

Other services were completed more quickly. The 438 requests for dead animal pickups were addressed in about 16 hours on average. Sidewalk repair requests, totaling 273, were completed in an average of just under 4 days.

See an issue in your neighborhood? You can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
