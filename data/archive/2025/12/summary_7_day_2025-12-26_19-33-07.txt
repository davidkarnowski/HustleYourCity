Here's a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past week.

The most reported issues were dumped items (313 requests), graffiti (188 requests), and e-scooters (188 requests). The average response time to close a dumped items report was about 1.25 days, while graffiti reports took about 1.5 days. All e-scooter reports were referred directly to the scooter companies.

Some services saw faster responses. The 44 requests for tree maintenance were closed in about 7 hours on average. Park maintenance requests were closed in an average of 69 minutes, though this can sometimes reflect administrative closures.
