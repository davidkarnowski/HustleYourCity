Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items remained the most reported issue with 2327 total calls. The city averaged a response time of 8 days for these reports.

Graffiti was the second most common concern, generating 943 reports with an average response time of 37 hours. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 842 reports, all of which were closed and referred to other agencies. Street Repair saw 226 reports, taking an average of 3.4 days to address.

Some services showed rapid turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup had 169 reports with an average response of 5.7 hours. Animal Services handled 75 calls in an average of 9 hours. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city. Tracking these statistics helps maintain transparency and accountability for local community services.
