Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on public data.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted more than 15,700 service requests. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (6,151 calls), graffiti (3,740 calls), and e-scooters (2,174 calls).

Response times varied by issue. The average time to resolve a report for dumped items was about 15 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 6.5 days. In contrast, requests for park maintenance took an average of 18 days. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 16 hours on average.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 17, 2025 at 8:01 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 17, 2025 at 7:00 PM PST.
