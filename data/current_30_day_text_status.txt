City service call totals for the past 30 days show steady activity across many categories.

Graffiti reports topped the list with 1,384 requests and an average response time of 60.9 hours (about 2½ days), down from 89 hours in the prior 60‑day window. Dumped Items remained the highest volume at 2,046 calls, but the average response improved to 189 hours (≈7.9 days) versus 345 hours previously. Street Repair requests fell to 192 with a much faster average response of 21.3 hours, a sharp drop from 72 hours two months ago. Tree Maintenance saw 242 calls and a quick 9.9‑hour average response, half of the 20‑hour average seen earlier. Sidewalk Repair and Traffic Signal work also improved, now averaging 13.5 hours and 18.3 hours respectively.

Overall, response times are trending shorter for most services, though Dumped Items still take several days to close. Residents can help keep the city accountable by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach app.

This data summary was updated at 11:26:00 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00:25 AM PST.
