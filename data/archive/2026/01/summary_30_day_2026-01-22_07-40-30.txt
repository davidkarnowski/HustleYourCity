Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service call data.

In the past month, the most common service request was for "Dumped Items," with 2,127 reports filed. The average response time to close these requests was about 8 days. "Graffiti" was the next most frequent issue, with 901 reports and an average response time of just over 3 days.

Response times for "Dead Animal Pickup" were faster, averaging about 12 hours to resolve. Some service requests show unusually low response times, which may be due to administrative closures rather than the completion of physical work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
