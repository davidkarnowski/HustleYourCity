Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Dumped Items and Graffiti were the most reported issues, with 5,899 and 3,604 calls respectively. The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report was about 15 days, while "Graffiti" reports were addressed in about 5 days.

Faster responses were recorded for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup, both averaging under 17 hours. In contrast, Park Maintenance requests took an average of about two and a half weeks to complete.

To report issues like these to the city, use the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at 11:27 AM PST on December 31, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 AM PST on December 31, 2025.
