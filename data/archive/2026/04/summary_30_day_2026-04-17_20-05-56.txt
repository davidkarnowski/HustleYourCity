Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service request data from the past 30 days. Here is a factual breakdown of the volume and average response times for the most frequent issues reported by residents.

Dumped Items received the highest number of reports with 2,352 requests. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these cases.

E-Scooter complaints were the second highest with 1,058 reports. Nearly all of these were referred to outside vendors, meaning the city did not log an average response time.

Graffiti removal saw 1,042 requests over the last month. The city averaged a response time of 2.3 days for these reports.

Street Repair and Light issues received 213 and 236 reports, respectively. Street repairs took an average of 2.9 days, while lighting issues averaged nearly 4 days.

Some services showed much faster turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup handled 172 requests with an average response time of 6.8 hours. Animal Services managed 75 calls, averaging 8.4 hours per response. Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Tracking these reports helps ensure transparency and accountability.

This data summary was updated at April 17, 2026 1:03 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 17, 2026 11:00 AM PDT.
