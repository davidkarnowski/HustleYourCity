Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Here is a summary of the most significant service call trends over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 2,185 reports. The average response time for these requests was 5.6 days.

Graffiti removal saw 997 reports with an average response time of 37 hours.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 933. All of these cases were closed and referred to other entities, resulting in no measurable city response time.

Tree Maintenance received 332 reports with an average response time of 50 hours.

Reports of Illegal Fireworks totaled 12, but had one of the longest average response times at 7.3 days.

Please note that unusually low response times across any category may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Reporting helps ensure accurate data and better accountability.
