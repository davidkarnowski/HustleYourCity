The latest service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across city maintenance categories over the last 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,023 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 7.5 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 3,381 reports and an average response time of about 3 days. Tree maintenance saw 658 reports with a faster average response time of approximately 2.5 days.

Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show the fastest turnaround times, with dead animal pickups averaging about 5 hours and animal services averaging about 10 hours over the last 90 days. Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
