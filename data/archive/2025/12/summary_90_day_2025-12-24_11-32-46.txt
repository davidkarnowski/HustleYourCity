Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,500 requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (over 6,000 requests), graffiti (over 3,700), and e-scooters (over 2,300).

Average response times to close a service request varied by issue. Graffiti clean-up requests were completed in about 6 days on average. Reports of dumped items took about 2 weeks. Park maintenance requests took over 2 weeks to resolve. Some services were faster, with dead animal pickups handled in about 16 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
