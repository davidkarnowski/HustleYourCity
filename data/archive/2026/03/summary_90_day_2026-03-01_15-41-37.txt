The latest data update for Long Beach city services highlights response times and request volumes over the last 90 days.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,681 reports. The average time to resolve these requests is currently 12 days. Graffiti removal follows with 3,135 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 3.5 days.

Infrastructure requests such as Street Repair totaled 832 reports, with crews taking about 5 days on average to close cases. Tree Maintenance saw 830 reports with a response time of roughly 6 days.

For immediate safety issues, Dead Animal Pickup averaged under 13 hours to resolve. Administrative tasks like Business License concerns were addressed quickly, averaging 12 minutes.
