City service call totals for the last 30 days show steady activity across many categories.

- Tree Maintenance: 222 requests, average response ≈ 11 hours, much quicker than the 28.7 hours seen earlier.
- Light repairs: 237 requests, average response ≈ 35.3 hours, similar to the recent 35 hour average.
- Sidewalk Repair: 103 requests, average response ≈ 17.7 hours, better than the 38 hour average previously.

Most categories are seeing faster turnaround times compared with the prior 60‑day period. Very low response times in a few cases may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
