Here is the latest analysis of Long Beach service request data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent requests were for Dumped Items (6,104 reports) and Graffiti (3,728 reports). The average time to close a Dumped Items request was about 15 and a half days. Graffiti reports were addressed more quickly, with an average closure time of about 6 and a half days.

Response times varied across different services. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests were among the fastest, with average closure times of 15 and 16 hours, respectively. In contrast, Park Maintenance requests averaged about 19 and a half days to close. These times reflect the average duration until a service request is marked as closed in the system.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
