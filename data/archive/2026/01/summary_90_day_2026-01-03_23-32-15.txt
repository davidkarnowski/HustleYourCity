Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days.

Dumped Items and Graffiti remain the most reported issues, with over 5,800 and 3,600 service calls respectively. The average time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 14.5 days. "Graffiti" requests were addressed in an average of just under 5 days.

Other notable response times include Park Maintenance, which took an average of about 2.5 weeks to resolve, and Street Repair, which averaged about 9.5 days. Some services, like "Dead Animal Pickup", show very fast closure times of less than a day. These quick resolutions can sometimes be due to administrative processing and not the completion of fieldwork.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the best way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
