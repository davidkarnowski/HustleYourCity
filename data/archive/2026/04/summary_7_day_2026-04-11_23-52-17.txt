Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability report evaluating City of Long Beach service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest call volume with 538 reports. The city averaged a 62-hour response time for these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 344 calls, which saw an average response time of 23 hours. E-Scooter complaints also remained high with 285 reports.

On the faster end, Park Maintenance issues averaged a 1.7-hour response time across 10 reports. Vegetation and Running Water concerns were also processed quickly, averaging 5.9 hours and 6.2 hours respectively. Please note that unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Infrastructure requests like Sidewalk Repair and Street Repair saw 35 and 33 reports respectively, with both categories averaging a 12-hour response time.
