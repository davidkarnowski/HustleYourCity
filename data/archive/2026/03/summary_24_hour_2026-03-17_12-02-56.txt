Hustle Long Beach presents the latest 24-hour accountability review of city service requests. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling recent community reports.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 114 new reports, though the vast majority have not yet been processed. Graffiti followed with 56 reports, averaging a 2.75-hour response time, with most cases currently marked as in progress.

Several service categories recorded exceptionally fast response times. Running Water issues averaged just 5 minutes, Tree Maintenance averaged 11 minutes, Vegetation averaged 13 minutes, and Animal Services averaged 20 minutes. Citizens should note that these unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or automated system routing rather than actual physical work completions.

Other notable response averages over the last 24 hours include Street Repair at 3.36 hours, Sidewalk Repair at 4.24 hours, and Light issues taking the longest at 8.77 hours.

Hustle Long Beach encourages all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Staying active and reporting problems helps keep the city accountable.
