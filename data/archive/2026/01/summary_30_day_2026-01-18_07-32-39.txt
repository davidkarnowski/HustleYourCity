Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service requests based on city data.

Over the past month, residents submitted more than 5,000 service requests. The top three categories were dumped items (2,032 requests), illegally parked e-scooters (982), and graffiti (894).

Based on data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.2 days. Closing reports for dumped items took an average of 4.3 days, and street repairs took about 3.4 days.

Some services saw faster resolutions. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 12.6 hours on average. In contrast, reports for general trash and debris took an average of 6.3 days.

The most direct way to report an issue in your neighborhood is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
