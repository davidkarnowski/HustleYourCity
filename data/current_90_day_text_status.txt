In the last 90 days Long Beach received 6,522 dumped‑item reports, 3,536 graffiti complaints, 840 tree‑maintenance requests and 484 street‑light issues.

Fastest average response times were seen for Animal Services (≈17 hours), Dead Animal Pickup (≈16 hours) and Tree Maintenance (≈67 hours, just under 3 days). Light and Sidewalk Repair also improved, averaging about 2.6 days and 4.4 days respectively.

Compared with all‑time averages, response times have dropped dramatically: Tree Maintenance fell from roughly 158 days to under 3 days, Light from 43 days to 2.6 days, and Sidewalk Repair from 198 days to 4.4 days. Some categories still take longer—Dumped Items now average about 15 days, up from the historic 11 days.

Residents can help keep these numbers moving in the right direction by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service app.
