Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data from the past 30 days.

Residents submitted nearly 5,600 service requests. The most reported issue was "Dumped Items" with over 2,100 reports, followed by "Graffiti" and "E-Scooter" issues, each with over 900 reports.

Regarding response times, the city closed "Dumped Items" requests in about 7.7 days on average. "Graffiti" reports were handled in about 3.2 days. For "Street Repair" requests, the average response time was about 70 hours.

It is important to note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
