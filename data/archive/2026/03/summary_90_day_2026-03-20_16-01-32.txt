Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the last 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 6,668 calls in the past 90 days. The city takes an average of nearly 10 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti is the second most common issue with 3,094 reports, averaging a 3-day response time.

Infrastructure issues like tree maintenance (929 reports) and street repair (832 reports) are currently taking about 7 days for a response. Dead animal pickups are handled much faster, averaging 12 hours.

Some categories show unusually fast resolution times. For example, business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that extremely low response times often reflect administrative closures or referrals rather than actual work completion.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. Download and use the Go Long Beach service application to submit your requests directly to the city.
