Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

Illegal dumping was the most reported issue with 2,066 requests, followed by 930 for e-scooters and 901 for graffiti. The total number of service requests for all categories was 5,346.

Average response times varied by issue. Reports for "Trash & Debris" took about 6.8 days to resolve. "Illegal Firework" and "Traffic Signal" reports were closed in an average of 5.6 days. In contrast, graffiti removal requests were completed in about 2 days, and dead animal pickups were handled in approximately 13 hours.
