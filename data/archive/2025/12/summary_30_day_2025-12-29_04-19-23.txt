Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days.

Residents submitted over 5,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (1,952 calls), e-scooters (1,033 calls), and graffiti removal (995 calls).

Average response times varied by service type. Reports for animal services were closed in about 9 hours, while tree maintenance requests were handled in about one day. In contrast, the average time to resolve a report for illegally dumped items was about 10 days, and traffic signal repairs took nearly 4 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach app to report it directly to the city.
