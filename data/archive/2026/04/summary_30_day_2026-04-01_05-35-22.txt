Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 30 days. This independent review highlights the current volume of citizen requests and how long it takes the city to respond.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 2,314 reports. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 952 reports, showing an average response time of 37 hours. E-Scooter complaints totaled 884, but all of these were immediately referred and closed out administratively.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which received 232 requests and averaged a 3-day response time. Dead Animal Pickup handled 166 calls with an average response of 7 hours. Park Maintenance took nearly 5 days on average to respond to 84 requests.

When reviewing this data, please note that unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures or referrals rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. Consistent reporting helps ensure that neighborhoods receive the attention they need.
