Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service call data for the past 7 days. As an independent accountability project, this summary tracks these numbers to keep the public informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling community requests.

Over the last week, the city received a total of 1,226 service requests. Dumped Items made up the largest share with 556 reports. Graffiti followed with 244 reports, and E-Scooter complaints accounted for 135 reports.
