Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 90-day city service request data to keep the community informed on response times and call volumes.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 6,737 requests over the last 90 days. The city took an average of 9.7 days to respond to these reports. Graffiti was the second most common issue with 3,075 requests, averaging a response time of 71.5 hours.

E-scooter complaints accounted for 2,822 reports, all of which were closed and referred to outside operators. Street repair and tree maintenance saw 834 and 939 requests respectively, both averaging about 6.6 to 6.7 days for a response.

Some services showed very fast turnaround times. Business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes, though unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead animal pickups were handled in an average of 11.8 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 20, 2026, 9:46 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 20, 2026, 8:00 PM PDT.
