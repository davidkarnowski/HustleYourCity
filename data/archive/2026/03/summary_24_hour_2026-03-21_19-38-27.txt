Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour city service data to keep citizens informed on response times and call volumes.

Over the past day, Dumped Items led the service requests with 51 reports. E-Scooter complaints followed with 23 reports, all of which were closed and referred to other agencies. Graffiti issues saw 17 reports, with the majority currently in progress.

The data shows some unusually fast response times in the last 24 hours. Tree Maintenance requests averaged a response time of just 7 minutes. Sidewalk Repair averaged 64 minutes, and Light issues averaged 74 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times, especially those under 90 minutes, may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services maintained steady response times, averaging around 2.4 hours and 3 hours respectively.
