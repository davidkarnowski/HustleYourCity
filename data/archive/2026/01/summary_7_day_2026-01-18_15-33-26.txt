Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,450 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items, improperly parked e-scooters, and graffiti.

The average time to close a graffiti report was about 25 hours. Reports for dumped items were closed in just under 2 days on average. Some service requests, like one for restroom maintenance, were closed in about 16 minutes. These unusually fast closures may be administrative and not reflect completed work.

If you see an issue that needs attention, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at January 18, 2026, 7:31 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at January 18, 2026, 3:00 AM PST.
