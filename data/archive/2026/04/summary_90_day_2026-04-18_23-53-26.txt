Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service data to keep the community informed on response times and call volumes. Over the past 90 days, the City of Long Beach received thousands of service requests across various categories.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 6,995 reports, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,238 calls, averaging a 2.3-day response time. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 2,883 reports, though the city did not track response times for this category.

Other significant categories included Tree Maintenance, which saw 889 requests and averaged a 9-day response time, and Street Repair, which had 707 requests and took an average of 8 days to complete. Dead Animal Pickup handled 468 calls with a much faster average response of 9.8 hours.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times, which may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. For example, Business Licenses and Permit Concerns averaged a resolution time of just 12 minutes.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
