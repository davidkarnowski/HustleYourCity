Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped items, with over 6,000 reports filed, followed by graffiti removal with nearly 3,400 reports.

The average response time to resolve a dumped item report was about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in about 5 days on average. Some of the fastest responses were for dead animal pickups (about 15 hours) and animal services calls (about 14 hours). Marina repair requests had one of the longest resolution times, averaging about 3.5 weeks.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
