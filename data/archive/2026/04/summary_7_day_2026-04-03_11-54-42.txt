Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items saw the highest call volume with 622 reports, taking an average of 66.8 hours to process. E-Scooter complaints followed with 261 reports, though response times were not recorded. Graffiti issues generated 219 calls, averaging a 25.4-hour response time.

Some of the fastest turnarounds were for Dead Animal Pickup, averaging 2.5 hours across 36 calls, and Running Water, averaging 2 hours for 3 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

On the slower end, reports for Illegal Fireworks took an average of 4.9 days to close, and Shoes on a Utility Wire took 3.5 days.
