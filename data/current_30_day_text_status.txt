Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 30 days.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with 1,893 calls. The average time to resolve these requests was about 8 days. Graffiti was the second most common report with 1,127 calls, which were addressed in an average of about 2 days.

Other key services included street repair requests, which took an average of nearly 5 days to complete. In contrast, reports for dead animal pickups were handled much faster, with an average response time of about 9 hours. Note that some unusually fast response times can be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
