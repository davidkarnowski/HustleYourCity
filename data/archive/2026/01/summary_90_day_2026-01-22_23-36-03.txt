Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on public data.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped items (over 6,100 reports), graffiti (nearly 3,400 reports), and e-scooters (over 2,600 reports).

On average, it took the city about 13 days to resolve reports of dumped items. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 5 days, while street repairs took an average of just under 6 days.

Faster response times were seen for dead animal pickups and animal services, both averaging under 15 hours. Some quick resolutions may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
