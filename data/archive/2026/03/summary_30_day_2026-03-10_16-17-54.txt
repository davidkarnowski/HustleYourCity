Here is the latest 30-day city service data summary from Hustle Long Beach! This independent review evaluates response times to keep citizens informed about how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Over the past month, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 2,055 requests. The city took an average of 4.7 days to respond to these cases. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,089 calls, averaging a 44-hour response time.

Other notable service categories include Tree Maintenance with 466 reports and an average response of 47 hours, and Street Repair with 269 reports taking about 70 hours to address.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnarounds. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes across 6 reports. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
