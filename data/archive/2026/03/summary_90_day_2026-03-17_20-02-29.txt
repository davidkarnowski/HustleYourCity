Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at the current response times and call totals for Long Beach.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue, with 6,674 total calls. The average response time for these requests is currently 10 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 3,083, with the city responding in an average of 70 hours. Tree maintenance received 907 requests, taking nearly 7 days on average to address.

Some categories show exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
