Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response times for the past 30 days. This independent data summary helps keep the public informed on how quickly local issues are being addressed by city departments.

Dumped items continue to be the most reported issue in Long Beach, with 2,260 requests over the last month. The average response time for these reports is currently about 8 days. Graffiti removal is the second most common request, seeing 1,078 reports with an average response time of 36.5 hours.

E-scooter complaints reached 957, though almost all were immediately referred to outside vendors and closed. Street repair saw 203 requests, taking just over 3 days on average to address. Meanwhile, dead animal pickups had 165 reports and were handled in about 7 hours.

Please note that unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures or referrals rather than actual work completion. 

Residents are encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit requests directly to the city and help improve the community.
