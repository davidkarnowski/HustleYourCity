Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. The following is an independent summary of city service response times and request volumes over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2,325 requests. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these issues.

Graffiti removal was the second most requested service with 1,000 reports. The average response time for graffiti was approximately 36 hours.

E-Scooter complaints generated 866 reports. All of these cases were referred to outside agencies or operators for closure.

Street and sidewalk issues remain a priority for residents. There were 199 street repair requests with an average response time of 3 days. Sidewalk repairs saw 143 requests, averaging a 23-hour response time.

Other notable service times include tree maintenance, which received 260 requests and averaged a 37-hour response. Park maintenance handled 75 requests with an average response time of just over 3 days. Dead animal pickups totaled 167 requests, taking an average of 7 hours to complete.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 9, 2026, 5:02 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 9, 2026, 3:00 AM PDT.
