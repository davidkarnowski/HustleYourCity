Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 24-hour service call data for the City of Long Beach to keep the public informed on local government response times. Over the past day, Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 104 reports, though all currently remain in new or duplicate status. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 34 calls, where 13 have already been closed and 17 are in progress.

Data from the last 24 hours shows some unusually fast response times. These exceptionally low averages may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions. Vegetation reports averaged a response time of just 4 minutes. Tree Maintenance cases were closed in an average of 31 minutes, and Animal Services calls averaged 85 minutes. Other services took slightly longer, with Street Repair averaging 5.4 hours and Graffiti averaging 1.6 hours.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Consistent reporting helps track service efficiency and keeps the community updated on how quickly neighborhood concerns are addressed.
