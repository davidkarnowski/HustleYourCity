Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests and response times.

Over the past 90 days, the most reported issues were dumped items (6,047 requests) and graffiti (3,389 requests). The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 13 days, while graffiti reports were addressed in about 5 days on average.

Infrastructure repairs showed varied response times. The 747 street repair requests were completed in an average of 6 days. Marina repair requests, which are often more complex, took an average of about 3.5 weeks to resolve.

Quicker responses were recorded for animal-related services. The 411 requests for dead animal pickups were handled in about 15 hours on average, and 160 other animal service calls were addressed in an average of 14 hours.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.
