Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 90 days.

Over 15,400 service requests were made by residents. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (5,875 calls), graffiti (3,603 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,486 calls).

Regarding response times, the data shows graffiti removal requests were closed in about 5 days on average. Reports of illegally dumped items took an average of nearly 15 days to resolve. Faster responses were recorded for dead animal pickups and animal services requests, which were both handled in under 18 hours on average. Longer wait times were observed for park maintenance (about 18 days) and street repairs (about 10 days).

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach app.
