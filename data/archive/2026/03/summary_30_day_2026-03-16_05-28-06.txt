Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the city's response times and call totals over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume with 2105 reports. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1042 calls, averaging a 42-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints totaled 944, though all were referred to outside agencies and closed without a recorded response time.

Other major categories included Tree Maintenance with 481 calls averaging a 53-hour response, and Street Repair with 262 calls taking just over 3 days on average. Park Maintenance saw 65 requests, taking an average of 3.5 days to resolve.

Some categories showed extremely fast turnarounds. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged just 3 minutes for 2 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
