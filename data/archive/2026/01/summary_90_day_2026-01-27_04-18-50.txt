Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,193 calls), graffiti (3,342 calls), and e-scooter issues (2,715 calls).

Based on city data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 5 days. Reports for dumped items took an average of about 13 days to resolve. Street repairs were addressed in an average of about 5.5 days. Some services had quicker turnarounds, with sidewalk repairs averaging 71 hours and dead animal pickups averaging about 13 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach app.
