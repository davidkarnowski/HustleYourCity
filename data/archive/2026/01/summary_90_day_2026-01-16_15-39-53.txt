Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most reported issues were Dumped Items (over 6,000 reports) and Graffiti (over 3,400 reports). Reports for E-Scooters were also high, with over 2,700 submissions. These are typically referred directly to scooter companies for handling.

Response times varied by issue type. The city responded fastest to Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests, with average completion times of about 14 and 16 hours, respectively.

For higher volume requests, the average response time for Graffiti removal was about 4.5 days. For Dumped Items, the average was about 13 days. Some unusually fast response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.
