The latest city service data for Long Beach shows a high volume of activity over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 7,099 reports, followed by 3,369 graffiti reports and 3,420 e-scooter reports.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Dead animal pickups are being addressed quickly, averaging about 5 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times, particularly those under 90 minutes, may reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that requests are tracked and directed to the appropriate departments for action.
