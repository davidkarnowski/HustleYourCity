Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on public data.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted more than 16,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,080 requests), graffiti (3,383 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,707 requests).

On average, the city's response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 13 days. For "Graffiti" reports, the average response time was about 5 days. Some services were handled more quickly, with "Dead Animal Pickup" requests being closed in an average of about 15 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the best way to get it into the city's queue.
