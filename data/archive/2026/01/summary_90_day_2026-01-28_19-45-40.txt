Here is a look at the latest city service data from Long Beach for the past 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were for Dumped Items, with 6,246 requests, and Graffiti, with 3,332 requests.

Response times varied significantly by issue type. The city responded fastest to Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests, with an average closure time of about 12 hours. In contrast, Marina Repair requests took an average of 5 weeks to resolve. Reports for Dumped Items, the most frequent issue, were closed in about 13 days on average.

Some unusually fast closures may be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.
