Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on public data.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted more than 16,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,476 requests) and graffiti removal (3,259 requests).

The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 4.5 days, while the average for dumped items was about 13.5 days. Some services were handled much faster, with dead animal pickups averaging around 12.5 hours. Unusually fast response times can sometimes be due to administrative closures and not a final work completion.
