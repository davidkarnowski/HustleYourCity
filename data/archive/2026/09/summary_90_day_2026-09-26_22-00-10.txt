The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across several categories over the last 90 days.

Graffiti reports remain a high-volume service area with 2,931 total calls. The average response time for these requests is now 73.7 hours, or just over 3 days.

Tree maintenance saw 862 requests in the same period, with an average response time of 79.4 hours, also just over 3 days.

Dumped items continue to be a primary concern for residents, totaling 8,446 requests in the last 90 days. The average response time for these items is 417.9 hours, which is approximately 17 days.

Dead animal pickup remains one of the most efficient services, with 575 requests in the last 90 days and an average response time of 6.6 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and track the status of city service requests.
