Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public service requests.

Dumped Items received the highest number of requests with 6,917 reports, taking an average of 9.4 days to resolve. Graffiti followed with 3,121 reports, which the city addressed in an average of 64 hours. E-Scooter issues generated 2,822 reports, though nearly all were referred to outside agencies.

Response times varied significantly across different service types. Dead Animal Pickup handled 435 calls with a fast average response time of 11.7 hours. Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes for 6 calls, though unusually low times like this may indicate administrative closures rather than completed field work. On the slower end, 3 reports for Damaged Drinking Fountains took an average of 10.7 days to resolve.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
