Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

The most frequent requests from the public were for "Dumped Items" (5,858 reports) and "Graffiti" (3,538 reports). The average response time to close a "Dumped Items" request was about 14 days, while "Graffiti" reports were addressed in just under 5 days on average.

Some services saw quicker responses. Reports for "Animal Services" and "Dead Animal Pickup" were typically closed in about 16 hours. On the other end, the average response time for "Marina Repair" was about 26 days, and "Park Maintenance" took nearly 17 days. It is worth noting that the 2,678 "E-Scooter" reports were referred to private vendors, so the city does not track a direct response time for these.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city and have your request tracked.
