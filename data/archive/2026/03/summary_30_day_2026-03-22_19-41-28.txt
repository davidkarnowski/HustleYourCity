Hustle Long Beach! is back with the latest city service accountability update. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled service requests over the past 30 days.

Dumped items were the most reported issue, with 2,212 calls. On average, it took the city 5.6 days to resolve these requests. Graffiti followed with 995 reports, which were addressed faster at an average of 1.5 days. E-scooter complaints also remained high, generating 946 reports.

When it comes to speed, city crews responded quickly to certain matters. Running water issues were resolved in an average of 8 hours, and 147 dead animal pickups were completed in about 10 hours. Conversely, some services took much longer. Marina repairs and illegal firework reports both averaged over 7 days for a response.

Residents are encouraged to stay active in improving the community. You can report local issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at March 22, 2026, 12:38 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 22, 2026, 12:00 PM PDT.
