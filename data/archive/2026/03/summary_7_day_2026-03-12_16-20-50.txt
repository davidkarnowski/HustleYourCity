Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service call data from the past 7 days. Here is a summary of the most recent request totals and response times to keep citizens informed.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of requests with 516 reports. The average response time for these issues was 3.1 days. E-Scooter complaints were the second most frequent with 249 reports, all of which were closed and referred to outside operators. Graffiti followed with 229 reports and an average response time of 32.6 hours.

Other notable service areas included Tree Maintenance with 84 calls averaging a 21.2-hour response, and Street Repair with 52 requests averaging 28.7 hours. Animal Services handled 23 calls with an average response of 7.6 hours. Traffic Signal issues showed a rapid 3.6-hour average response time. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual physical work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city and help keep Long Beach neighborhoods maintained.
