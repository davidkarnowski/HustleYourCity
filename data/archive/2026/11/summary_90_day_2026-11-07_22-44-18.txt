The latest data for city services in Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,740 reports for dumped items, which remains one of the highest volume service categories. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-priority area with 2,567 reports, maintaining an average response time of approximately 67 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 1,053 reports in the last 90 days, with an average response time of about 4.4 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging 11 hours and 7.3 hours respectively over the same 90-day window.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city to ensure they are tracked and addressed.
