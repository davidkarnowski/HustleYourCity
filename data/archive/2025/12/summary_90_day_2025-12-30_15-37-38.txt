Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for dumped items (5,900 requests), graffiti (3,611 requests), and e-scooters (2,488 requests).

Based on closed service requests, the average response time for dumped items was about 15 days. For graffiti removal, the average response was about 5 days. Park maintenance requests took an average of about 2.5 weeks to resolve. Some of the quickest responses were for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup, which both averaged under 17 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 30, 2025, 7:34 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 30, 2025, 7:00 AM PST.
