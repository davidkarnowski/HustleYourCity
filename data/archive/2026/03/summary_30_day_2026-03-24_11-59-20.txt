Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 30-day city service response data for the City of Long Beach. This summary provides an independent look at how quickly local issues are being addressed.

Dumped Items generated the highest call volume over the past month with 2264 reports. The city averaged 5.8 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1018 calls, taking an average of 37 hours to address. E-Scooter complaints totaled 942, all of which were referred to outside agencies and closed.

Other notable response times include Dead Animal Pickup, which saw 144 reports and a rapid average response time of 8.9 hours. Conversely, Marina Repair and Illegal Fireworks reports took the longest to resolve, averaging 7.4 days and 5.9 days respectively. Park Maintenance requests, totaling 73 calls, took an average of 4.6 days.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 24, 2026, 4:56 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 24, 2026, 4:00 AM PDT.
