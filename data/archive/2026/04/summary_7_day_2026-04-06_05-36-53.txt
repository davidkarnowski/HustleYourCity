Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data, focusing on the past 7 days of service requests. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood issues.

Dumped items generated the highest call volume with 584 reports. The city averaged a response time of about 2 days for these requests. E-scooter complaints followed closely with 270 reports, all of which were closed and referred to other agencies. Graffiti removal saw 250 requests, with an average response time of 12 hours.

Other notable service areas included tree maintenance with 48 calls averaging an 8-hour response, and sidewalk repairs with 44 calls averaging an 11-hour response. Dead animal pickups were addressed rapidly, averaging just over 2 hours for 39 calls. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to stay active in improving the community. You can report local issues directly to the city by downloading and using the Go Long Beach service application.
