Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents made over 5,500 service requests. The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (2,551 calls), Graffiti (1,095 calls), and E-Scooters (960 calls).

Response times varied by issue. The average time to close a report for Dumped Items was about 9 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in about 61 hours on average. In contrast, reports for Dead Animal Pickup were handled in about 11.5 hours. Some service types, like Damaged Drinking Fountains, took over 10 days to resolve.
