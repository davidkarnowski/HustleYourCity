Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at the current numbers and response times to keep the public informed.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue with 6,995 requests, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti follows with 3,261 reports, averaging a 55-hour response time. E-scooter complaints reached 2,923, though response times are not tracked in this dataset.

Infrastructure requests also show high volume. Street repair saw 725 requests with an average response time of 7.2 days. Sidewalk repairs totaled 360 requests, taking about 5.3 days on average. Tree maintenance had 902 reports, averaging 7.7 days for a response.

Some services report much faster turnarounds. Dead animal pickups accounted for 467 requests and averaged a 10.5-hour response. Business license and permit concerns showed an average response time of just 12 minutes for 6 requests. It is important to note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 12, 2026, 8:51 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 12, 2026, 3:00 AM PDT.
