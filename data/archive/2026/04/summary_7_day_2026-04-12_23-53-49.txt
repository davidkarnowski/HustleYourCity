Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service call data to keep you informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood requests. Here is a look at the numbers over the last 7 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 531 reports, taking an average of 57 hours to resolve. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 328 calls and an average response time of 17 hours. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 290 reports.

Other notable response times include Street Repair at 11 hours across 37 reports, and Tree Maintenance at 10 hours for 48 reports. Park Maintenance showed a rapid turnaround, averaging 1.7 hours for 11 reports. Please note that unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Accountability starts with accurate reporting. Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
