Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week based on the latest public data.

Dumped items and graffiti were the most frequent issues reported, with 478 and 180 requests respectively. The average response time to close a dumped item report was just under 2 days, while graffiti removal requests were closed in about 1.5 days on average.

The city responded more quickly to other reports. The 129 street repair requests were addressed in an average of 19 hours. Dead animal pickups were completed in under 5 hours on average. Requests for light repairs saw a longer average response time of about 3.5 days.
