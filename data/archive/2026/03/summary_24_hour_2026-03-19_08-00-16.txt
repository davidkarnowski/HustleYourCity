Here is the latest 24-hour city service data summary from Hustle Long Beach! evaluating local government response times.

Over the past day, Long Beach residents submitted a variety of service requests. Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 70 total reports, though 61 of these currently remain in the New status. Graffiti followed with 17 reports, and Vegetation issues accounted for 11 reports.

Average response times for closed cases over the last 24 hours have been remarkably fast. Tree Maintenance requests averaged a response time of just 25 minutes. The longest average response time recorded over the past day was for Street Repair, which averaged 2.28 hours to process.
