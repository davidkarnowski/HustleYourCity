Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times for the past week.

The most common service requests were for Dumped Items (377 calls), Graffiti (186 calls), and E-Scooters (165 calls). The average response time to close a Dumped Items report was about 1.5 days, while Graffiti reports took nearly 3 days on average. E-scooter reports are noted as being referred to other agencies.

Quicker responses included Dead Animal Pickup, with 42 reports handled in an average of 3 hours. Street Repair requests were addressed in about 6.5 hours on average. Sidewalk Repair requests show an average response of about 6 minutes, which may reflect administrative closures rather than completed physical repairs.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
