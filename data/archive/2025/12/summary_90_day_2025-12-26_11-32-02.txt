Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, residents submitted more than 15,500 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (5,941 requests) and graffiti (3,676 requests).

The average response time to close a request for dumped item pickups was about two weeks. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in an average of about 5.5 days. Reports for dead animal pickups were completed much faster, with an average response time of about 16 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at December 26, 2025, 3:29 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 26, 2025, 3:00 AM PST.
