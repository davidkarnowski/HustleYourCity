Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest 24-hour service request data to keep the community informed on city response times and request volumes.

Over the past day, Dumped Items saw the highest volume with 87 reports, though none have been closed yet. E-Scooter complaints followed with 49 reports, all of which were immediately referred and closed. Graffiti issues generated 37 reports, with closed cases averaging an 8.2-hour response time.

Some services showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Sidewalk Repair requests averaged a response time of 79 minutes, and Dead Animal Pickup averaged 86 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or referrals rather than actual physical work completions. Other notable response times include Tree Maintenance at 7.8 hours and Light issues at 11.8 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
