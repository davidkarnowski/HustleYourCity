Hustle Long Beach! presents the latest 30-day city service data summary. As an independent accountability project, the goal is to keep the public informed on how the City of Long Beach handles service requests.

Over the past 30 days, Dumped Items received the highest volume of calls with 2,304 reports. The average response time for these requests was approximately 8 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,083 calls and an average response time of 37 hours.

Other notable service categories include E-Scooter complaints, which generated 958 reports. Tree Maintenance received 258 calls with an average response time of 3.6 days, while Street Repair saw 200 calls averaging a 3.2-day response. Dead Animal Pickup had 171 reports with an average response of 6.8 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Followers are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 11, 2026 4:49 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 11, 2026 3:00 PM PDT.
