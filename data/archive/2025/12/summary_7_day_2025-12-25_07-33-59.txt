Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, the most reported issues were Dumped Items with 380 calls, followed by E-Scooters with 227 calls, and Graffiti with 207 calls. The city's average response time for Dumped Items was about 1.5 days, while Graffiti reports were addressed in just over one day on average. E-scooter reports are referred directly to vendors and are not handled by city staff.

Faster responses included Dead Animal Pickup, averaging about 6.5 hours, and Tree Maintenance, averaging about 4 hours. Some categories like Park and Restroom Maintenance showed response times under one hour, which may be due to administrative closures and not work completion. All 11 requests for Trash and Debris from the past week remain open.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it through the Go Long Beach service application.
