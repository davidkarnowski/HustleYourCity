Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data. Over the past month, residents submitted 1,965 requests for Dumped Items and 1,108 for Graffiti, making them the most common issues reported through the city's service app.

According to city data, the average time to close a Graffiti report was about 2.7 days. The average response for Dumped Items was about 6.6 days. Street Repair and Park Maintenance requests were both handled in an average of about 3.8 days. The longest average response time was for Marina Repair, at nearly two weeks.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
