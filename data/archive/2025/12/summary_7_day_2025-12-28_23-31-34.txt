Here is a look at Long Beach service requests from the past week.

Based on the latest city data, residents submitted 830 service requests in the last 7 days. The most common reports were for dumped items (338 requests), graffiti (182 requests), and e-scooters (175 requests).

The average response time for closed graffiti reports was just under 24 hours, while dumped items took about 28 hours. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of 33 minutes. It is possible that some unusually low response times are due to administrative closures and not work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to notify the city is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
