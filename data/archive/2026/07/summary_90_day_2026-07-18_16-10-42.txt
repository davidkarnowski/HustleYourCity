The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 6,946 reports for dumped items and 3,301 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Animal services and dead animal pickups remain the fastest to address, with average response times of approximately 10 hours and 6 hours respectively. Meanwhile, dumped item reports have taken an average of about 8 days to resolve. Please note that very short response times, such as those under 90 minutes seen in some recent street repair reports, may reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach mobile application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that service requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
