Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,350 service requests were submitted through the Go Long Beach system. Reports for illegally dumped items were the most common issue, with 511 requests. This was followed by 252 reports for e-scooters and 213 for graffiti.

The average response time to close a request for dumped items was about 3.2 days. For graffiti removal, the average was about 33 hours. Reports of illegal fireworks took an average of 3.7 days to close. Quicker responses were seen for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 6.5 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
