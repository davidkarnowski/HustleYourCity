Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data to keep citizens informed on city response times and request volumes.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest volume with 89 total reports, consisting entirely of new requests and duplicates with no logged response times yet. E-Scooter complaints followed with 46 reports, all of which were closed and referred to outside entities.

Graffiti removal received 22 requests, maintaining an average response time of 2.2 hours.

Some services showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. Street Repair logged 14 calls with an average response time of just 12 minutes. Sidewalk Repair received 6 reports, averaging a 53-minute response. Please note that unusually low response times measured in minutes may be due to administrative closures or reclassifications rather than actual physical work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city and help keep the community maintained.
