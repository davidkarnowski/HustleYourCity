Here is the latest on Long Beach city service response times for the past week.

In the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,398 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (599 requests), graffiti (245 requests), and e-scooter issues (211 requests).

According to the data, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 25 hours. Reports of illegally dumped items had an average closure time of about 3 days. Animal-related services, such as Dead Animal Pickup, were handled quickly, with average response times between 3 and 6 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
