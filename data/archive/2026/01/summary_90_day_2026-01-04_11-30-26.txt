Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the last 90 days.

Over this period, the most frequent requests from residents were for illegally dumped items, graffiti, and e-scooters. According to the data, the average response time to resolve a report for illegally dumped items was about 14.5 days. Graffiti removal took an average of nearly 5 days, while park maintenance requests took over two weeks to complete.

Some services saw quicker resolutions. The average response time for dead animal pickup was about 17 hours, and for Animal Services calls it was about 16 hours. Reports of running water were addressed in approximately 21 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it to the city by using the Go Long Beach service application.
