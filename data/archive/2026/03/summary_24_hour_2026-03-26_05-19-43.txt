Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data to keep you informed about city responsiveness.

Over the past day, Dumped Items generated the highest call volume with 72 reports, though most of these remain newly opened without a logged response time. Graffiti followed with 21 reports, and Street Repair received 14 calls.

The data shows some exceptionally fast average response times for the past 24 hours. Running Water reports averaged a 12-minute response, Dead Animal Pickups averaged 41 minutes, and Light issues averaged 78 minutes. Graffiti reports were addressed in about 90 minutes, while Street Repair calls averaged 4.3 hours. Please note that unusually low response times, particularly those under 90 minutes, may be due to administrative closures or referrals rather than actual work completions.
