Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 30 days.

The most reported issues were "Dumped Items" with 1,906 requests and "Graffiti" with 1,120 requests. The city received a total of 779 reports for "E-Scooter" issues.

Average response times varied across different services. The average time to resolve a "Dumped Items" report was about 8 days. Reports for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" were handled more quickly, with average response times of about 8.5 hours and 9 hours, respectively.
