Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data. Here is a look at the numbers and response times from the past 7 days.

Dumped items led the request volume with 527 reports, followed by 236 e-scooter issues and 193 graffiti reports. Out of the 527 dumped item reports, 357 remain in the new status without being addressed yet.

The city took an average of 3.9 days to respond to dumped items and abandoned shopping carts. Graffiti reports saw an average response time of about 25 hours. Dead animal pickups were handled much faster, averaging just over 4 hours.

Some categories showed unusually fast response times, such as restroom maintenance at 35 minutes and community center issues at 72 minutes. Keep in mind that these low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
