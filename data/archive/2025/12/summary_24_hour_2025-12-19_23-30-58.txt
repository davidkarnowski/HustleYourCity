Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data. In the past 24 hours, residents submitted 160 service requests. The most frequent reports were for Dumped Items (67 requests), E-Scooters (40 requests), and Graffiti (13 requests).

Response times varied by issue. A request for Animal Services was closed in about 12 minutes, and two Park Maintenance requests were closed in an average of 41 minutes. These fast closures may be administrative. The seven requests for Dead Animal Pickup were completed in an average of about 68 minutes. In contrast, the average response time for Graffiti removal was about 11 hours.
