City service data for the past 90 days shows 3,575 graffiti reports (the highest volume) with an average response time of about 6.5 days. Dumped items generated 6,493 calls and took roughly 15 days on average to resolve.

Animal Services (232 calls) and Dead Animal Pickup (453 calls) were addressed much faster, averaging 18 hours and 16.5 hours respectively—well under a day. Other categories such as Tree Maintenance (2.7 days), Light repairs (2.5 days), and Sidewalk Repair (4.2 days) also stayed within a week.

Overall, most service types are resolved within a week, though some, like Dumped Items and Report Illegal Fireworks, take longer (about 2 weeks). Very quick turnaround times may reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completion.

Residents can report issues and track progress through the Go Long Beach app to help keep response times short.
