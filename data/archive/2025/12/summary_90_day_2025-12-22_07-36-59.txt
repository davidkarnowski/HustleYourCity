Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the last 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from the public were for dumped items (over 6,000 calls), graffiti (over 3,700 calls), and e-scooters (over 2,300 calls).

According to the data, the average time to resolve a request for park maintenance was about 17 days. Reports for dumped items took an average of about 15 days to close, while graffiti removal averaged about 6 days. Some services were much faster, with Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup requests resolved in less than 24 hours on average.

It is worth noting that nearly all e-scooter reports were marked as "Closed Referred," which means the city forwarded the issue to the private scooter companies to handle.

See an issue in your neighborhood? You can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
