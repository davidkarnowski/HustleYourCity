Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days. The most frequent requests from residents were for illegally dumped items, with 6,088 reports, and graffiti removal, with 3,710 reports.

Average response times vary by issue. Reports for dumped items are taking about 14.5 days to resolve on average, while graffiti removal is averaging about 5.7 days. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickups are typically handled in about 16 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it is the first step. Use the Go Long Beach service application to submit a request to the city.
