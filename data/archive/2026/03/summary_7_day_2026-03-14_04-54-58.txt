Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled service requests over the past 7 days.

Dumped items led the call volume with 551 reports. The city averaged a 62-hour response time for these issues, with 98 cases closed and 176 currently in progress. E-scooter complaints followed closely with 265 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Graffiti removal saw 222 requests. The city responded in about 23.5 hours on average, successfully closing 183 of those cases. Tree maintenance received 83 calls, averaging a 21-hour response time.

Traffic signal issues had a rapid response time of about 2.5 hours for 6 reports. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times can sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than completed physical work.

Citizens are encouraged to help keep the city running smoothly. If you see an issue, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
