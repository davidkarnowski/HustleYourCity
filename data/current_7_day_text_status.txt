In the past 7 days Long Beach residents filed about 1,200 service requests.

The biggest volume was Dumped Items (474 reports), with 226 new cases. The average response time was 64.5 hours (≈2.7 days). Street Repair (44 reports) and Graffiti (332 reports) were addressed much faster – average response times of 5.6 hours and 17.5 hours respectively. Tree Maintenance (60 reports) and Traffic Signal issues (11 reports) also saw quick turn‑around, under 8 hours on average.

Other categories showed mixed performance: Light complaints (66 reports) took about 11 hours, Sidewalk Repair (18 reports) 7 hours, and Trash & Debris (12 reports) 49.5 hours. Animal Services (18 reports) and Dead Animal Pickup (40 reports) were resolved in roughly 6 hours and 16.6 hours.

Overall, many service types are being closed within a day, but Dumped Items still average nearly three days to resolve. Residents are encouraged to report issues promptly through the Go Long Beach app to help keep response times short.
