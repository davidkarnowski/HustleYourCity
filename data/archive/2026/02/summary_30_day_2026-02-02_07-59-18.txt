Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service response data for the past 30 days.

The most frequent service requests were for dumped items (2,419 calls), e-scooters (1,037 calls), and graffiti (1,007 calls). The average response time to close a "Dumped Items" report was over 9 days. For graffiti removal, the average response was about 2.5 days.

Some services saw quicker responses. Reports for dead animal pickups were closed in an average of 11 hours, while restroom maintenance issues were addressed in about 8 hours. E-scooter reports are typically referred directly to the scooter companies and are not handled by city staff.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
