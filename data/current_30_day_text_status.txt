Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 30 days.

The most frequent request was for "Dumped Items", with 1,980 calls. The average time to close these requests was about 7.5 days. "Graffiti" was the second most common issue with 1,163 reports, which were addressed in about 2.5 days on average.

Response times for other common issues include street light repairs (about 2 days), sidewalk repairs (under 2 days), and dead animal pickups (about 9 hours). It is important to note that some service requests may show unusually fast completion times. These are often due to administrative closures and may not reflect that on-the-ground work has been finished.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the city's Go Long Beach service application.
