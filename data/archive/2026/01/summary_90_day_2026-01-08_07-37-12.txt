Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 90 days.

Residents submitted over 14,000 service requests during this period. The most frequent reports were for dumped items, with 5,921 requests, and graffiti, with 3,537 requests.

Average response times varied significantly by service type. Reports for animal services and dead animal pickups were among the fastest, typically resolved in under 17 hours. The average time for graffiti cleanup was just under 5 days, while dumped item pickups took an average of 2 weeks.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to notify the city.
