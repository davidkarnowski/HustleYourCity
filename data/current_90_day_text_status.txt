The latest data for city service requests shows trends across several categories over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain a high-volume concern with 7,607 total reports. The average response time for these requests is approximately 18 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a frequent request, with 2,580 reports processed at an average response time of 63 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 1,039 reports in the last 90 days, with an average response time of approximately 6 days. Dead animal pickup continues to show the fastest response times, averaging about 8 hours.

Please note that response times can vary based on the nature of the request and current service demand. Unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
