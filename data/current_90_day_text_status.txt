Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Illegal dumping and graffiti were the most reported issues, with 6,124 and 3,741 service requests filed. The average response time to close a dumped items request was about 15 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of about 6.5 days.

Some of the longest waits were for park maintenance, which took an average of about 18 days to resolve. On the faster side, requests for dead animal pickup and other animal services were typically handled in under 16 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 17, 2025 at 11:30 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 17, 2025 at 11:00 AM PST.
