Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past three months, residents submitted over 6,000 reports for dumped items and over 3,300 for graffiti, making them the most frequent requests. The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 5 days, while dumped item reports took an average of 13 days.

More urgent requests, like dead animal pickups, were handled faster, with an average response time of about 15 hours. Slower responses were seen for infrastructure repairs. Street repairs averaged nearly 6 days, while marina repairs took about 3.5 weeks.

Some categories show unusually fast response times, which may reflect administrative closures rather than the completion of physical work.

Help keep the city accountable and report issues you see. The most direct way to make a service request is by using the Go Long Beach app.
