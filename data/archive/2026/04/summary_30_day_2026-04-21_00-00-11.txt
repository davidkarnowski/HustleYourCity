Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest third-party accountability update on City of Long Beach service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped items and graffiti continue to drive the highest call volumes. Citizens reported 2,345 dumped items with a city average response time of 8 days. Graffiti saw 1,070 reports with an average response time of 51 hours. Park maintenance received 77 reports, taking an average of 3.4 days to resolve.

Street repairs had 207 reports with an average response time of 62 hours. Dead animal pickups were handled faster, averaging 7 hours across 165 reports. Please note that unusually low response times or high "Closed Referred" counts, such as the 1,076 E-Scooter reports, may be due to administrative closures or routing rather than actual work completions.
