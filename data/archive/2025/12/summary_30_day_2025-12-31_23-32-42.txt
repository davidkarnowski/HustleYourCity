Here is the latest 30-day snapshot of Long Beach city service requests.

Illegal dumping was the most reported issue with 2,017 service calls. This was followed by reports of graffiti (970 calls) and improperly parked e-scooters (942 calls).

Average response times varied significantly. The city took about 9.5 days to resolve reports of dumped items. In contrast, graffiti and street repair requests were handled in just over 2 days on average. The quickest responses were for dead animal pickups and animal services, which were both addressed in about 12 hours. E-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies, so the city does not provide a response time.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at Dec 31, 2025, 3:30 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at Dec 31, 2025, 3:03 PM PST.
