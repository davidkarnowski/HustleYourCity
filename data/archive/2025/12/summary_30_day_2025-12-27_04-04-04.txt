Here is the latest 30-day snapshot of Long Beach city service requests and response times.

In the past month, the most frequent reports from residents were for illegally dumped items (1,911 requests), e-scooters (1,054), and graffiti (992).

Based on city data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.3 days. Reports for dumped items took an average of about 8 days to resolve, while street repairs were completed in an average of about 2.5 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to notify the city.

This data summary was updated at December 26, 2025, at 8:01 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 26, 2025, at 7:00 PM PST.
