The latest service data for the City of Long Beach shows varying response times across key categories over the last 90 days.

Dead animal pickup remains the most efficient service, with an average response time of approximately 5 minutes. Animal services also show strong performance, averaging about 11 hours per request.

Graffiti removal requests have averaged roughly 4 days per response, while tree maintenance and sidewalk repair requests have both averaged approximately 4 days. Dumped items continue to be a high-volume concern, with an average response time of nearly 11 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.
