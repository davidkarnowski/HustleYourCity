Here is an evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 90 days. This independent review looks at call totals and response times to keep the public informed.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 6,421 total calls. The average response time for these issues is currently roughly 2 weeks.

Graffiti reports were the second most frequent request with 3,189 calls. The city is averaging about 4 and a half days to resolve these incidents.

Infrastructure requests such as Street Repair and Tree Maintenance are seeing similar response times. Both categories are averaging approximately 5 days for completion.

Animal-related services are showing faster response times. Dead Animal Pickup and general Animal Services are averaging between 11 and 12 hours to close.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
