Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest accountability update on city service response times for the past 7 days.

Dumped items received the highest volume of reports at 601, taking an average of 3.8 days to process. Graffiti followed with 292 reports, averaging a 46.6-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also saw high volume with 281 reports.

Some services showed rapid turnaround times. Dead animal pickups, with 32 reports, averaged 5.2 hours. Animal services handled 16 reports in an average of 5.7 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

On the slower end, reports for shoes on a utility wire averaged 4.4 days to process, while traffic signal issues took about 3.5 days.
