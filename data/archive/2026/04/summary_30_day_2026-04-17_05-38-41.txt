Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest service call data from the City of Long Beach. Here is a summary of significant service requests and response times over the last 30 days.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of reports with 2,346 calls. The average response time for these requests was 8.2 days.

E-Scooter complaints totaled 1,049. Nearly all of these were immediately referred to outside operators, resulting in no measurable city response time.

Graffiti removal received 1,037 reports, with the city responding in an average of 55 hours.

Street Repair and Light maintenance saw 215 and 232 reports, respectively. Street repairs averaged a 71-hour response, while lighting issues took an average of 4 days to address.

Dead Animal Pickup had 171 reports with a rapid average response of 6.7 hours. Please note that unusually low response times across any category may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 16, 2026 10:35 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 16, 2026 7:00 PM PDT.
