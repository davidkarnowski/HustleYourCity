Here is a look at the City of Long Beach's service request data from the past 30 days. Residents submitted over 5,400 requests for various issues.

The most frequent request was for "Dumped Items" with over 2,000 reports. The average response time to close these requests was about 4.5 days. For the nearly 900 "Graffiti" reports, the average response time was about 2 days.

Response times for other services varied. "Dead Animal Pickup" requests were handled in about 12 hours on average. In contrast, "Traffic Signal" repairs took an average of about 5.5 days to complete.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's system.
