Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times for the last 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 2,383 total calls. The average response time for these requests is currently 7.8 days.

Graffiti reports followed with 1,152 total calls and an average response time of 52 hours.

Street Repair saw 239 requests with an average response time of 63 hours.

Dead Animal Pickup remains one of the faster services, averaging 9 hours across 148 requests.

Please note that unusually low response times, such as Sidewalk Repair at 23 hours, may reflect administrative closures or safety measures rather than full construction completion.
