Here is the latest look at Long Beach service response data from the past 24 hours.

In the last day, 151 service requests were made by the public. The most common reports were for dumped items (46 calls), graffiti (35 calls), and street light issues (18 calls).

Some service requests were closed very quickly. The average response time for street repair was about 15 minutes, and tree maintenance was about 17 minutes.
