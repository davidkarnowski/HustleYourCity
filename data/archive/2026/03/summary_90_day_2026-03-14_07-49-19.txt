Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a factual look at how the City of Long Beach is handling public requests.

The highest volume of service requests over the last 90 days came from Dumped Items with 6,623 reports. This was followed by Graffiti with 3,086 reports and E-Scooter issues with 2,933 reports.

Response times varied significantly by category. For Dumped Items, the city took an average of 10 days to respond. Graffiti reports saw an average response time of 3 days. Street repair requests, which totaled 835, took an average of 6 days to address.

Some categories showed very fast turnaround times. Dead animal pickups averaged a 13-hour response. Business license and permit concerns averaged just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.

This data summary was updated at March 14, 2026 at 12:45 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 14, 2026 at 12:00 AM PDT.
