Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 30 days.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with 2,066 requests. The average time to resolve these cases was about 4.5 days. Graffiti was another frequent report, with 904 requests handled at an average response time of about 2 days.

Response times for other services varied. The average for "Trash & Debris" removal was nearly 7 days. In contrast, "Dead Animal Pickup" requests were completed much faster, with an average time of about 13 hours.
