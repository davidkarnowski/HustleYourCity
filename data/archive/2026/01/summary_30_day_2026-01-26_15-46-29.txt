Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

In the last month, the most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (2,312 requests), e-scooters (1,014 requests), and graffiti (966 requests).

The average city response time to close a dumped items report was approximately 8 days. Graffiti reports were resolved in about 2.5 days on average. Street repair requests were handled in just under 3 days. Some services saw quicker responses, with dead animal pickups being completed in an average of about 11 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
