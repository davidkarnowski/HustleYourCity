In the last 90 days Long Beach residents filed about 15,800 service requests across 22 categories.

Key response‑time highlights:
- Graffiti reports took an average of 163 hours (≈ 6.8 days) to close.
- Dumped‑item calls averaged 368 hours (≈ 15 days); 692 requests are still “In Progress.”
- Sidewalk repairs, street repairs and light‑maintenance are averaging 35‑83 hours (≈ 1.5‑3.5 days), with roughly half of sidewalk and light jobs still in progress.
- Animal Services and Dead‑Animal Pickup are being resolved quickly, averaging 17 hours and 16 hours respectively.
- Some categories (Report Illegal Firework, Marina Repair) still show longer waits of 9‑11 days.

Overall, many services are being closed within a few days, but a notable share of repairs remain open. Residents can help track and report issues through the Go Long Beach app, which provides real‑time updates and lets the city prioritize work.
