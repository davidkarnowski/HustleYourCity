Over the past 90 days Long Beach logged roughly 15,800 service requests across 22 categories. The highest volumes were Dumped Items (6,159 reports) and Graffiti (3,723 reports).  

Average response times:
- Animal Services & Dead Animal Pickup – about 17 hours (≈0.7 days)  
- Graffiti – 162 hours (≈6.8 days), down from the all‑time average of 353 hours  
- Light repairs – 179 hours (≈7.4 days), a big improvement from the historic 1,012 hours  
- Traffic Signals – 212 hours (≈8.8 days)  
- Shoes on a Utility Wire – 33 hours (≈1.4 days)  

Most categories are being closed within a week, though a few still exceed that window. Very fast closures may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

Residents can help keep the city accountable by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach service app.
