In the past 90 days Long Beach recorded about 16,400 service calls across all categories.

The most frequent issues were Dumped Items (6,503 calls) and Graffiti (3,579 calls). Their average response times were roughly 15 days (357 hours) for Dumped Items and 6 days (147 hours) for Graffiti. Other notable averages: Park Maintenance ≈ 5.7 days, Sidewalk Repair ≈ 3.7 days, Light repairs ≈ 2.5 days, and Tree Maintenance ≈ 2.6 days.

Some services are being resolved quickly: Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup both averaged under 1 day (≈ 19 hours and 17 hours respectively).

Residents can help keep the city moving by reporting issues through the Go Long Beach app, which streamlines request tracking and speeds up response.
