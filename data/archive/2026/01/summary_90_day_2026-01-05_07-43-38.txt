Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent reports from residents were for illegally dumped items (over 5,800 requests) and graffiti (over 3,500 requests). The city's average response time to close these reports was about 14 days for dumped items and just under 5 days for graffiti.

Other services had quicker turnaround times. Reports for dead animal pickups were typically resolved in about 16 hours, while vegetation maintenance requests averaged about 54 hours. In contrast, park maintenance requests took longer, with an average completion time of about two and a half weeks.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
