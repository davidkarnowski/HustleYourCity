Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the last 30 days based on public data.

Dumped items, e-scooters, and graffiti were the most reported issues, each with over 1,000 requests submitted. In total, residents submitted over 6,000 service requests for various issues across the city.

Response times varied by issue type. The average time to resolve a dead animal pickup was about 12 hours. Graffiti clean-up took about 61 hours on average. For dumped items, the most common request, the average response time was about 8 days. The 1,002 e-scooter reports were referred directly to scooter companies, so a city response time is not tracked for those issues.
