Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items remain the top reported issue with 458 calls in the last week. The city took an average of 3 days to respond to these requests.

Graffiti was the second most common issue with 284 reports, seeing an average response time of about 2 days. E-scooter complaints totaled 268, though all were referred to outside agencies.

On the faster side, running water issues were addressed in just 78 minutes on average. Conversely, a damaged drinking fountain report took 4 days to process. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Help keep the city accountable and well-maintained. Please use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
