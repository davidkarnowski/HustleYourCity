Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service requests and response times from the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, the most frequent service requests from residents were for "Dumped Items" with 6,305 calls and "Graffiti" with 3,356 calls. Reports for improperly parked e-scooters were also high at 2,671.

Response times varied significantly by issue type. The city closed requests for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services" in about 13 hours on average. For comparison, the average response time for "Graffiti" removal was about 5 days, while "Dumped Items" took an average of 13.5 days. More complex issues like "Marina Repair" took about 5 weeks to resolve.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most direct way to get it into the city's system.
