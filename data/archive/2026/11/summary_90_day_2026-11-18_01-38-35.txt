The latest data update for city services shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,486 reports for dumped items and 2,619 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains the most efficient, with an average response time of approximately 7 minutes. Conversely, dumped items are currently averaging about 18 days for resolution, while tree maintenance requests are averaging about 6 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that your concerns are tracked and addressed.
