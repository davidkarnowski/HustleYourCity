Hustle Long Beach! is here with your weekly city service data update. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach handled service requests over the last 7 days.

Dumped Items led the total call volume with 572 reports. The average response time was 64.6 hours, or about 2.7 days. It is worth noting that 436 of these requests currently remain in the "New" status.

Graffiti removal saw 251 requests, with an average response time of 24.5 hours, or about 1 day. The city successfully closed 171 of these cases.

Other notable response times include Dead Animal Pickup, which had 31 calls and a rapid average response time of 4.5 hours, and Animal Services, which handled 18 calls with an 8.1-hour average response time. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes be the result of administrative closures rather than actual completed work.
