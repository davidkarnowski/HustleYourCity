Here's the latest look at Long Beach city service data. In the last 90 days, residents submitted over 15,500 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (6,283 calls) and graffiti (3,280 calls).

Response times for these common issues vary. The average time to close a report for dumped items was about 14 days. Graffiti removal requests took an average of 5 days to complete.

Other services saw quicker responses. Reports for dead animal pickups were resolved in an average of 13 hours, while abandoned shopping carts were handled in about 44 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it is the first step. Use the Go Long Beach app to submit a service request directly to the city.
