Here is the latest 24-hour city service data update for the Hustle Long Beach! community.

Over the past day, Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 82 calls, though 75 of these remain in the new status. E-Scooter issues followed with 48 calls, all of which were closed and referred to other agencies. Graffiti reports totaled 28 calls.

Response times varied significantly across case types. Animal Services recorded the fastest average response at 11 minutes. Graffiti and Dead Animal Pickup also showed rapid average response times of 62 minutes and 65 minutes, respectively. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Other notable average response times included Sidewalk Repair at 3.6 hours and Park Maintenance at 4.5 hours.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help track neighborhood needs.
