Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service request data.

Over the last three months, the most frequent service requests submitted by residents were for dumped items, with 5,894 calls, and graffiti removal, with 3,618 calls. Reports about e-scooters were also high at 2,457.

The average time to resolve a service request varied by the type of issue. The city's response to graffiti reports averaged about 5 days. Dumped item pickups took longer, averaging around 15 days. In contrast, requests for dead animal pickup were addressed much faster, with an average response of about 16 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 29, 2025, 7:35 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 29, 2025, 7:00 AM PST.
