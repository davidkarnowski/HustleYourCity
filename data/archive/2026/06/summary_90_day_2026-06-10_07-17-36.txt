The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows significant activity in service requests over the last 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 6,745 reports and an average response time of approximately 8.7 days. Graffiti reports totaled 3,309 with an average response time of 65 hours.

Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to see some of the fastest response times, averaging 8.6 hours and 5.8 hours respectively over the last 90 days. Other maintenance areas, such as park and street repairs, show average response times of 117 hours and 154 hours respectively.

Please note that unusually low response times in the most recent data may reflect administrative processing rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
