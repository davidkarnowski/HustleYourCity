Here is the latest Hustle Long Beach! accountability update regarding city service response times over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of service calls with 2,321 reports. The average response time for these requests was nearly 8 days. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,037 calls, averaging a 37-hour response time. E-Scooter concerns generated 917 reports.

Other notable service categories include Street Repair, which had 203 requests and an average response time of 3 days. Dead Animal Pickup received 163 calls and averaged a 7-hour response time. Park Maintenance handled 73 requests with an average turnaround of three and a half days.

Please keep in mind that unusually low response times in the data may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting local issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at April 10, 2026, 1:44 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 9, 2026, 11:00 PM PDT.
