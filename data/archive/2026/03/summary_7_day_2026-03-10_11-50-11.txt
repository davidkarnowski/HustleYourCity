Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest City of Long Beach service call data for the past 7 days. Here is a factual look at what residents are reporting and how fast the city is responding.

Dumped items lead city service requests with 463 reports in the last week, averaging a 67.7-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints followed with 245 reports, and graffiti issues saw 229 reports with an average response time of 33.4 hours.

Other notable service times include street repairs averaging 23.4 hours for 62 requests, and dead animal pickups averaging 11 hours for 37 requests. Running water reports showed an unusually fast average response time of just 13 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
