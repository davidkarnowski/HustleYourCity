Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days, based on public data.

The most common service request was for "Dumped Items" with 6,392 reports, followed by "Graffiti" with 3,171 reports.

Average city response times for major categories include approximately 13.5 days for "Dumped Items", 5 days for "Street Repair", and 4.5 days for "Graffiti".

Faster responses were recorded for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services", with both averaging around 12 hours.

Note that the 2,656 reports for E-Scooters are referred directly to the scooter companies, which is why a city response time is not calculated for those issues.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application to report it to the city.
