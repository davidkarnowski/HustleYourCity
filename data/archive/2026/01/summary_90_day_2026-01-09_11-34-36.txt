Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for 'Dumped Items' (over 5,900 reports) and 'Graffiti' (over 3,500 reports). The average response time to close a 'Graffiti' report was about 4.6 days, while 'Dumped Items' took about 2 weeks.

Response times varied across other services. Reports for 'Dead Animal Pickup' and 'Animal Services' were typically closed in under 17 hours. In contrast, 'Park Maintenance' requests averaged about 2 weeks, and 'Marina Repair' requests took about 1 month. A 2-hour average for 'Damaged Drinking Fountain' is unusually low and may be due to an administrative closure.
