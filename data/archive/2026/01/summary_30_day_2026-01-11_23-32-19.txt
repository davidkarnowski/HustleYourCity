Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

In the past month, the most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (1,892 calls), e-scooters (967 calls), and graffiti (911 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was just over 2 days. For dumped items, the average was about 5 and a half days. Reports for general trash and debris cleanup took longer, averaging over 9 days to be resolved.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
