Here is the latest evaluation of Long Beach city service requests for the past 30 days. This update highlights the volume of calls and how long it takes for the city to close them.

Dumped Items remained the highest volume category with 2,252 total reports. The average response time for these issues was approximately 6 days. Graffiti reports totaled 1,136, with an average resolution time of 2 days.

Infrastructure requests showed varied response times. Street Repair generated 253 reports with an average completion time of 4 days. Tree Maintenance saw 422 reports, averaging just under 2 days for a response.

Faster response times were recorded for public health issues. Dead Animal Pickup averaged under 12 hours per request, and Animal Services averaged 14 hours. Note that unusually low response times in any category may be due to administrative closures rather than physical work completions.

Residents are encouraged to report issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure data is tracked accurately.
