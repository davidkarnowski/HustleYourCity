Here is a look at the latest Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Over the last three months, residents submitted over 15,500 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (5,859 requests), Graffiti (3,540 requests), and E-Scooters (2,677 requests).

Response times varied by the type of service needed. Animal-related calls were handled the fastest, with an average response of about 16 hours for Dead Animal Pickup. In contrast, requests for Dumped Items took about 2 weeks to resolve on average. Park Maintenance requests averaged about 2.5 weeks.

Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than completed work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure it is logged by the city.
