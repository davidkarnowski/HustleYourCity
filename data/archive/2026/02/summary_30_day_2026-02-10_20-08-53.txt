Hustle Long Beach! is reviewing the latest service request data from the City of Long Beach. This update focuses on activity and response times from the last 30 days.

The highest volume of reports came from Dumped Items, with 2,572 total requests. The average response time for these issues was approximately 8 days. Graffiti removal was the second most frequent request, with 1,107 reports and an average response time of just over 2 days.

Infrastructure issues remain a priority for residents. There were 439 reports regarding Lights, which averaged a response time of under 3 days. Street Repair requests totaled 260, with the city taking about 3 days on average to respond.

Dead Animal Pickup recorded 152 requests and showed a faster turnaround time of 11 hours. Please be aware that unusually low response times may be the result of administrative closures and not actual work completions.
