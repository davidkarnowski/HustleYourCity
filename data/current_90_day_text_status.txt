The latest data for city service requests over the past 90 days shows varied response times across key categories.

Dead animal pickups remain the fastest service to address, with an average response time of approximately 7.4 hours. Animal services also show efficient turnaround, averaging about 11.3 hours.

Graffiti removal requests have been addressed in an average of 62.3 hours, or about 2.6 days. Vegetation maintenance requests have seen a similar average response time of 63.5 hours, also roughly 2.6 days.

Other services are taking longer to process. Dumped item reports have an average response time of 303.4 hours, which is approximately 12.6 days. Street repair requests are averaging 127.8 hours, or about 5.3 days, while sidewalk repair requests are averaging 106.9 hours, or about 4.5 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
