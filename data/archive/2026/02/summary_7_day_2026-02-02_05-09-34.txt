Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 1,383 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (568 calls), followed by graffiti and e-scooter issues (245 calls each).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 19 hours. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 2 days on average, while dead animal pickups were completed in about 13 hours. Some categories, like sidewalk repair (6 hours), show very fast average response times. These may reflect administrative closures rather than the time for full work completion.
