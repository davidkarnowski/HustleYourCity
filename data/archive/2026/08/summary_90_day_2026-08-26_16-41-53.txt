The latest city service data for Long Beach shows varying response times across key categories over the past 90 days.

Dead animal pickups remain the most efficient service, with an average response time of approximately 5.3 hours. Animal services also show strong performance, averaging about 11.2 hours per request.

Graffiti removal requests averaged approximately 3.5 days, while tree maintenance and sidewalk repairs averaged roughly 3.7 days and 3.9 days respectively. Dumped item reports continue to see high volume, with an average response time of about 14.7 days.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
