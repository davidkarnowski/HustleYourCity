Here is a look at Long Beach city service data for the past 90 days.

Illegal dumping continues to be the most reported issue with nearly 6,400 service calls. The average city response time to close these reports is currently about 14 days.

Graffiti removal, the second most common report with over 3,200 requests, is being addressed in an average of about 4.5 days. Street repair requests are taking around 5 days to complete.

Reports for animal-related services are handled much faster, with an average response time of about 12 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
