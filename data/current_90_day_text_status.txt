Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times from the last 90 days.

Over 15,900 service requests were filed in this period. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,222 requests) and graffiti removal (3,755 requests).

Response times varied significantly by service type. The data shows urgent requests like Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services were closed in about 17 hours on average. In contrast, Park Maintenance requests took an average of 19 days to close, while reports for dumped items averaged over 15 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in about 6.5 days on average.
