Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest service request data from the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at how city services performed over the last 7 days.

Dumped Items received the most attention with 469 reports. The city averaged a response time of 70.4 hours for these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 317 calls and an average response time of 38.6 hours. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 278 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Response times varied significantly depending on the service. Running Water reports were handled quickly, averaging a response time of just 33 minutes. On the other end, the single Damaged Drinking Fountain report took 4 days to process. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
