The latest city service data for Long Beach shows significant activity over the last 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,993 reports for dumped items and 2,672 reports for graffiti.

Response times vary by service type. Dead animal pickup remains one of the fastest services, with an average response time of approximately 7.3 hours. Please note that unusually low response times in the data may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.
