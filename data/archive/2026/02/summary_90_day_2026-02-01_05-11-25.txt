Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on the latest public data.

The most frequent request was for "Dumped Items" with over 6,300 submissions, which took an average of about 13.5 days to close. "Graffiti" was the next most common report, with an average closure time of about 5 days. Over 2,600 reports were made for "E-Scooter" issues, which are referred directly to the scooter companies for handling.

The quickest responses were for Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup, both averaging around 13 hours to close. Note that some fast closure times may be due to administrative processing rather than on-site work completion. The longest response times were for Marina Repair, averaging about 5 weeks.
