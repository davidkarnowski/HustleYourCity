Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day city service response data. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood reports.

Dumped Items continue to generate the highest volume of calls, with 2,109 reports filed in the last 30 days. The city is taking an average of 5 days to respond to these requests. 

Graffiti removal is the second most reported issue with 1,051 calls. The average response time for graffiti is nearly 2 days. Tree maintenance saw 472 requests, taking an average of just over 2 days to address. Street repair requests totaled 266, with an average response time of about 3.4 days.

Some categories show incredibly fast turnarounds. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures or immediate referrals rather than actual physical work completions.
