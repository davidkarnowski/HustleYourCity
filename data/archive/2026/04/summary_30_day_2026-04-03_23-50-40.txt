Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest 30-day accountability summary of city service response times. This data helps track how quickly local issues are being resolved.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue across Long Beach. In the past 30 days, citizens submitted 2,318 reports for dumped items, which took the city an average of 8.4 days to resolve. Graffiti was the second highest concern with 955 reports, showing an average response time of 33.4 hours.

E-scooter complaints generated 929 reports, though nearly all of these were referred to outside vendors rather than handled directly by city staff.

For infrastructure and maintenance, the city received 221 street repair requests, taking an average of 3.4 days to address. Tree maintenance saw 292 reports with an average response time of 42.9 hours. On the faster side, the city handled 165 dead animal pickups in an average of 5.2 hours.

Please note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report problems directly to the city.
