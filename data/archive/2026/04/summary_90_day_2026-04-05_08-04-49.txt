Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling local service requests.

Dumped Items lead the request volume with 7,043 reports. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti removal follows with 3,166 reports, showing an average response time of 59 hours.

Other notable service areas include street repair, which saw 800 requests with an average response time of nearly 7 days, and tree maintenance, which had 920 calls averaging 5 days for a response.

Some categories show incredibly fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Residents should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
