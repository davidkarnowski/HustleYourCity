Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data from the past 90 days to keep the community informed on local government response times.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of calls with 7,022 reports. The city took an average of 9 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti removal was the second most common issue, totaling 3,115 requests with an average response time of 60 hours.

Other notable service areas include street repair, which received 823 requests and averaged over 6 days for a response, and tree maintenance, which saw 927 requests and took nearly 5 days on average. E-scooter complaints totaled 2,853, though these are immediately referred to outside vendors rather than handled directly by city crews.

Some categories showed exceptionally fast turnaround times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to download and use the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues directly to the city.
