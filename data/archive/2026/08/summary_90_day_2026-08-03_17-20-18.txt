The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows significant activity in service requests over the past 90 days. During this period, the city processed 7,217 requests for dumped items and 3,299 requests for graffiti removal.

Response times vary by service type. For the last 90 days, dead animal pickup remains the fastest service, with an average response time of approximately 5 minutes.
