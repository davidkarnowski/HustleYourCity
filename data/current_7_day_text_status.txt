In the past week Long Beach recorded 1,368 service calls across all categories.

Graffiti had the highest volume (383 reports) with an average response time of 25.7 hours. Dumped Items generated 515 calls; the city responded on average in 69.3 hours. Traffic Signal issues were addressed quickly, averaging just 7.3 hours for the 11 reports received. Tree Maintenance and Animal Services also showed fast turn‑around, averaging 9.3 hours (45 calls) and 7.7 hours (22 calls) respectively.

Several categories remain “In Progress,” including Sidewalk Repair (8 of 21 calls) and Street Repair (16 of 33 calls), indicating ongoing work. Very low response times for a single Shoes on a Utility Wire report (1.9 hours) likely reflect administrative closure rather than field work.

Overall, the city’s response times vary by service type, but most issues are being addressed within a day to three days.

Residents are encouraged to report new issues and track progress using the Go Long Beach service app.
