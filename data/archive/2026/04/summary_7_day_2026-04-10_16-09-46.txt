Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest independent accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped items and graffiti generated the highest volume of service requests. The city received 457 reports for dumped items, which took an average of 69 hours to address. Graffiti followed with 296 reports and an average response time of 33 hours. E-scooter complaints were also high with 277 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Response times varied significantly across different service types. Damaged drinking fountain reports took the longest to process, averaging 4 days. Conversely, animal services requests were handled in an average of 9 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help keep the city accountable.

This data summary was updated at April 10, 2026 at 9:06 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 10, 2026 at 7:00 AM PDT.
