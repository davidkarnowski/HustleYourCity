Here is a look at Long Beach's service response data for the past 30 days.

Residents submitted over 4,900 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,063), improperly parked e-scooters (969), and graffiti (896).

Average response times for some key services include: reports of trash and debris took about 6 days, while traffic signal issues were addressed in about 5.5 days. Dumped items were handled in an average of 4.5 days.

Quicker responses were seen for graffiti removal, which averaged about 2 days. Dead animal pickups and animal services calls were typically resolved in under 14 hours.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
