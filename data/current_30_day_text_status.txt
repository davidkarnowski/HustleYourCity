Graffiti reports rose to 1,388 calls in the last 30 days with an average response time of 37 hours (about 1½ days). That’s a big improvement from the previous 90‑day average of 144 hours.

Dumped‑item calls totaled 2,015; the city now resolves them in an average of 155 hours (≈ 6 days), down from 355 hours in the prior 90 days.

Sidewalk‑repair requests were 107, with an average response of 17 hours, a sharp drop from 84 hours three months ago.

Tree‑maintenance work saw 259 calls and a 12‑hour average response, compared with 61 hours previously.

Restroom‑maintenance tickets (8) were handled in about 6 hours on average, and Light‑issue reports (226) averaged 29 hours.

Overall, the city’s response times are trending faster across most service types. Residents can continue to report issues and track progress through the Go Long Beach app.
