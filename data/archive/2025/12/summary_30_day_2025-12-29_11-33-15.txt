Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

The most reported issues were illegally dumped items with 1,955 reports, followed by e-scooter issues and graffiti.

Average response times varied by issue type. The city took about 10 days to close reports for dumped items. In contrast, graffiti removal requests were completed in about 2 days on average, while street repair requests took around 2.5 days.
