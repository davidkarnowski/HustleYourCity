Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest City of Long Beach service request data. Over the past 30 days, residents submitted thousands of reports, and here are the facts on how the city responded.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 2257 reports. The city took an average of 5.4 days to respond to these issues. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1040 calls, which saw an average response time of 40 hours.

Other notable service areas include tree maintenance, which received 502 requests and averaged a 3.2-day response time. Street repair had 256 reports, taking about 3.4 days on average to address.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, business license and permit concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. This extremely low response time is likely due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues. You can submit your requests directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
