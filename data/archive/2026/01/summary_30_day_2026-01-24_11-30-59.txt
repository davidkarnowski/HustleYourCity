Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 30 days.

In the past month, residents submitted over 5,800 service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (2,285 requests), e-scooters (956 requests), and graffiti (935 requests).

Average city response times varied by the type of issue. Reports for dead animal pickups were closed in about 12 hours, while graffiti cleanup took an average of 67 hours. More complex issues took longer to resolve. The average time to close a report for dumped items was about 7.7 days, and traffic signal repairs averaged about 5 days. Note that some closure times may reflect administrative processing rather than on-site work completion.
