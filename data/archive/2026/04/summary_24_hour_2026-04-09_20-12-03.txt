Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 24-hour service call data for the City of Long Beach to keep the community informed on local government response times.

In the past day, the highest volume of citizen reports included 74 calls for Dumped Items, 54 for E-Scooters, and 34 for Graffiti. The majority of the Dumped Item and E-Scooter reports are currently marked as new or referred to other departments.

Several city services recorded highly rapid turnaround times. Restroom Maintenance requests averaged a response time of just 2 minutes. Vegetation issues averaged 24 minutes, Street Repair averaged 46 minutes, and Animal Services averaged 47 minutes. Citizens should note that unusually low response times, such as the 2-minute average for restroom maintenance, may be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work completions.

Other notable response averages over the last day include Graffiti reports, which were handled in an average of 9.8 hours. Tree Maintenance and Sidewalk Repair requests averaged about 7.5 and 7.8 hours, respectively.

Citizens are encouraged to continue reporting local issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city remains accountable and well-maintained.

This data summary was updated at April 9, 2026, at 1:10 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 9, 2026, at 11:00 AM PDT.
