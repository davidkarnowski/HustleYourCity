Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest service call data to keep citizens informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling community issues. Here is a summary of the service requests and response times over the last 30 days.

Dumped items generated the highest volume with 2355 reports. The city took an average of 8 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1007 calls, averaging a 35-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also remained high, totaling 904 reports.

Infrastructure and maintenance requests showed varying response times. Street repairs had 209 reports with an average response time of 3 days. Tree maintenance received 266 calls, averaging 34 hours for a response. Sidewalk repairs saw 141 reports, taking an average of 23 hours to address. Park maintenance had 75 reports and averaged a 3-day response time.

Some services saw much faster turnarounds. Dead animal pickups totaled 168 calls with an average response of 4 hours, while general animal services had 81 calls averaging 9 hours. Unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
