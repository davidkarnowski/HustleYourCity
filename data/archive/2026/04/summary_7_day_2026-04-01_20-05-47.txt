Here is the latest Hustle Long Beach! data summary evaluating city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items received the highest volume of reports with 558 requests. The average response time for these items was 3.3 days. Graffiti removal saw 216 reports, with an average response time of 24.3 hours. E-Scooter complaints generated 206 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable service requests included 61 reports for lighting issues averaging 45.7 hours, 57 tree maintenance requests averaging 23.7 hours, and 51 street repair reports averaging 25.5 hours. Dead animal pickups totaled 37 requests with an average response time of 3.7 hours.

When reviewing this data, keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures in the system and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
