The latest data from the Go Long Beach service application shows a high volume of requests over the past 90 days. Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 6,634 reports, followed by 3,318 e-scooter reports and 3,295 graffiti reports.

Response times vary significantly by service type. Animal services and dead animal pickups are currently seeing the fastest turnaround, with average response times of approximately 10 minutes and 6 hours respectively. Conversely, dumped items are taking an average of about 8 days to address.

Please note that unusually low response time figures for certain categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work.

Residents are encouraged to continue using the Go Long Beach service application to report neighborhood issues. This helps ensure that data remains accurate and that service requests are tracked appropriately.
