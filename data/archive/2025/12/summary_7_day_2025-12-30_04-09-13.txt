Here is the latest Long Beach city service data update for the past week.

Residents submitted over 1,000 service requests in the last seven days. The most frequent reports were for dumped items, with 371 requests, followed by graffiti with 205 requests.

The average response time to close a graffiti report was about 69 hours. Dumped item reports were addressed in about 36 hours on average. Some requests were handled much faster, such as reports for shoes on a utility wire, which were closed in an average of 44 minutes. Sidewalk repair requests had an average close time of 6 minutes, which may be due to administrative closures and not work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 29, 2025 at 8:07 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 29, 2025 at 7:00 PM PST.
