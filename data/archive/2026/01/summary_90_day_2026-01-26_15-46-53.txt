Here's a look at Long Beach city service request data from the last 90 days.

Dumped items were the most reported issue with 6,165 calls, followed by graffiti with 3,335 reports. The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 13 days. Graffiti reports were addressed more quickly, with an average response time of about 5 days.

Animal-related calls saw some of the fastest responses. Dead animal pickups were completed in an average of about 14 hours. In contrast, the longest average response time was for marina repairs, which took about 5 weeks to resolve.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
