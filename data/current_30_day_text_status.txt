Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service call data.

In the past month, residents made over 1,900 requests to address dumped items, making it the most reported issue. The average response time for these calls was about 7.5 days. Graffiti was the second most common report with over 1,100 calls, which were addressed in an average of about 2.3 days.

Some of the quickest responses included dead animal pickups, averaging about 9 hours, and vegetation management, averaging about 13 hours. Reports for street repair took an average of about 4 days to close.

See an issue in your neighborhood? The most effective way to report it is by using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 16, 2025, 8:00 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 16, 2025, 7:00 PM PST.
