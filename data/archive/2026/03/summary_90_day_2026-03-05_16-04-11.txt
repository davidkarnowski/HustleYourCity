Here is the latest analysis of Long Beach city service requests over the past 90 days. This independent review focuses on response times and total call volumes reported through the city system.

Dumped Items remain the most frequent request with 6,568 total reports. The average response time for these issues is currently approximately 11 and a half days. Graffiti reports are the second highest volume category with 3,081 requests, showing an average completion time of about 3 and a half days.

Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 841 reports with an average response time of just over 5 days. Tree Maintenance shows a similar timeline, averaging nearly 6 days to resolve across 844 reports. Dead Animal Pickup remains one of the fastest services, averaging roughly 13 and a half hours per request.

Residents are encouraged to report issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city is aware of maintenance needs.
