Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times. This summary evaluates data from the last 90 days to track how quickly the City of Long Beach is addressing resident reports.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,378 reports filed. The average response time for these issues is currently approximately 13 days.

Graffiti removal received 3,157 reports. The data shows an average completion time of roughly 4 days.

Street Repair requests, which include potholes, are averaging nearly 5 days for a response.

Faster response times were recorded for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services, which averaged 11 hours and 13 hours respectively.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city to ensure they are tracked in this system.
