Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response times based on data from the past 30 days.

Illegally dumped items and graffiti continue to be the most frequent issues reported by the public. In the last month, the city received over 2,500 reports for dumped items and more than 1,100 for graffiti.

Average response times for closed service requests varied. Dead animal pickups were handled in about 11 hours. Graffiti removal took an average of 2.5 days, while illegally dumped items were addressed in nearly 9 days. Requests for damaged drinking fountains had the longest completion time, averaging almost 11 days. Some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not on-site work.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to notify the city is by using the Go Long Beach service application.
