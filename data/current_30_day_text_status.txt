In the past 30 days Long Beach logged 5,760 service requests across all categories.

Graffiti reports were the most common (1,443) with an average response time of about 44 hours (≈1.8 days). Dumped Items generated 2,094 calls and took the longest to address, averaging 230 hours (≈9.6 days). Tree Maintenance and Traffic Signal issues were handled quickly, with average responses of 10 hours and 11 hours respectively. Sidewalk Repair, Light repairs and Park Maintenance averaged 16 hours, 33 hours and 67 hours. Restroom Maintenance, Trash & Debris and Vegetation requests were closed in roughly 1 day, 2.5 days and 1.6 days.

Overall, many categories are seeing response times under a day, while a few (especially large‑scale clean‑up jobs) still take several days. Some unusually low times may reflect administrative closures rather than completed work.

Residents can help speed up service by reporting issues directly through the Go Long Beach app.
