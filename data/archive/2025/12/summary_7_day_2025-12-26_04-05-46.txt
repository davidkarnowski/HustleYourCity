Here is the latest summary of Long Beach service request data for the past week.

Over 900 service requests were submitted by residents. The most reported issues were for dumped items (313 requests), illegally parked e-scooters (219 requests), and graffiti (186 requests).

Average response times for closed requests varied. The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 1.5 days, while dumped item reports took about one day. Dead animal pickups were completed in an average of 7.5 hours.

The average response time for park maintenance was unusually fast at 69 minutes. This may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
