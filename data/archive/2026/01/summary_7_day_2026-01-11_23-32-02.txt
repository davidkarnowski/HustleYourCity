Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over 1,460 service requests were submitted through the Go Long Beach system in the last 7 days. The most common reports were for dumped items (573 requests), graffiti (243 requests), and e-scooters (229 requests).

Based on city data for closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 21 hours. Reports for dumped items took an average of 45 hours to be addressed, while street repair requests were closed in about 14 hours. Some services, like restroom maintenance, showed very fast closures of under two hours, which may reflect administrative processing rather than on-site work.
