Here is a look at Long Beach's city service response data from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted more than 1,150 service requests. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (427 requests), e-scooters (249), and graffiti (237).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 21 hours, and for dumped items, it was about 33 hours. Sidewalk repair requests were closed in an average of about 45 minutes. Unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures and not work completions.
