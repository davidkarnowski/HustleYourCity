Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted thousands of service requests. The most frequent reports were for dumped items, with 5,841 calls, and graffiti, with 3,599 calls.

Average response times varied depending on the issue. Reports for animal services and dead animal pickups were typically handled in under 18 hours. The average time to resolve a report of dumped items was about two weeks, while park maintenance requests took just over two weeks. Some requests show unusually fast resolutions, which may be due to administrative closures and not completed work.

To report issues like graffiti, potholes, or dumped items, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
