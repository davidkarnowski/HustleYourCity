Here is the latest accountability update on Long Beach city service requests over the past 7 days.

Dumped Items led the request volume with 524 reports. The average response time was about 3.6 days, with 246 requests still marked as new. Graffiti removal saw 207 requests, averaging a 23.7-hour response time, with 150 of those already closed.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair and Tree Maintenance, which both received 69 requests and averaged response times of 23.2 hours and 18.8 hours, respectively. Dead Animal Pickup had 47 reports, averaging a 4.7-hour response.

Some response times were unusually fast. For example, Restroom Maintenance showed a response time of just 35 minutes for a single request. Keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. On the other end, Abandoned Shopping Carts took an average of 3.3 days to address across 10 reports.
