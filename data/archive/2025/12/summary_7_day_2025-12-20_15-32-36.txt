Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past week.

The most reported issues were dumped items (432 reports), e-scooters (238 reports), and graffiti (225 reports). According to the city's data, the average response time to close a dumped items request was about 1.75 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of just over one day.

Response times for sidewalk repair and restroom maintenance were unusually low, at approximately 45 minutes and 51 minutes respectively. These short times may be due to administrative closures and not always reflect the time for work to be completed.
