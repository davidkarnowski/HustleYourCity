Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data, focusing on requests made in the last 90 days.

Over this period, residents submitted more than 15,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (6,062 requests), graffiti (3,707 requests), and e-scooters (2,360 requests).

Average city response times varied by issue. Graffiti removal took about 5.7 days. Reports of dumped items were addressed in about 2 weeks on average. More urgent issues like dead animal pickups and animal services calls were handled faster, with average response times of about 16 hours and 15 hours, respectively. Park maintenance requests had a longer average completion time of about 17 days.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at December 24, 2025, 7:33 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 24, 2025, 7:00 AM PST.
