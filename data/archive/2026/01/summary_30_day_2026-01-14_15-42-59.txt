Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests.

Over the past month, residents submitted 5,364 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,024 requests), e-scooters (936 requests), and graffiti (914 requests).

Looking at average response times for closed cases, graffiti removal took about 51 hours. Reports for dumped items were addressed in about 5 days, while trash and debris clean-up took about 8 days. Faster responses included animal services and dead animal pickups, both averaging about 14 hours to resolve.

You can report non-emergency issues like these using the Go Long Beach service application.
