Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for the last 90 days.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests during this period. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (5,891 calls) and graffiti (3,546 calls). The average time to resolve a graffiti report was about 4.7 days, while dumped item reports took an average of about 2 weeks to close.

Some of the quickest responses included reports for dead animal pickup, which were closed in an average of about 16.5 hours. Animal services requests were handled in about 15.8 hours on average. Reports for damaged drinking fountains were closed in just over 2 hours, though this low time may be due to administrative closures and not physical repairs.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
