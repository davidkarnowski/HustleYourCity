Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach city service response data.

Over the past 90 days, the most frequent service requests were for Dumped Items (5,900 calls) and Graffiti (3,610 calls). The average response time to close a Dumped Items report was about 15 days. For Graffiti, the average response time was about 5 days.

Other service response times varied. Park Maintenance requests took an average of almost 18 days to resolve. In contrast, requests for Dead Animal Pickup and Animal Services were typically handled in under a day, with average response times of about 16 hours and 15 hours, respectively.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to notify the city.

This data summary was updated at December 30, 2025, 11:28 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 30, 2025, 11:00 AM PST.
