Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the last 30 days.

In the past month, the most common reports from the public were for dumped items (1,932 calls), graffiti (1,074 calls), and e-scooter issues (989 calls).

The average time to resolve a dumped items report was about 6 days. Graffiti reports were addressed in an average of about 2.5 days, while street repair requests took an average of about 3.5 days. Faster responses were recorded for issues like dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 12 hours on average. Note that some faster response times may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach service application is the most effective way to alert the city.

This data summary was updated at 3:27 AM PST on December 21, 2025 and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 PM PST on December 20, 2025.
