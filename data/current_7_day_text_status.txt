Over the past week Long Beach recorded 1,096 service requests across 20 categories.

The highest volume was Dumped Items with 430 reports and an average response time of about 92 hours (≈3.8 days). Graffiti followed with 294 reports, averaging 44 hours (≈1.8 days). Street Repair had 52 reports and was addressed in roughly 8 hours. Tree Maintenance showed the fastest turnaround: 76 reports with an average response of 4.5 hours. Light requests rose to 65, including 24 new reports, and were resolved in about 33 hours on average. Sidewalk Repair logged 14 reports (≈8.5 hours), Vegetation 5 reports (≈6 hours), Trash & Debris 13 reports (≈100 hours), Animal Services 19 reports (≈6 hours), and other categories remained low‑volume.

Citizens can report issues directly through the Go Long Beach service app for faster assistance.

This data summary was updated at 11:25 PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at 11:00 PST.
