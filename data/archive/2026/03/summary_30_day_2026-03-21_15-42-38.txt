Hustle Long Beach! is providing another independent review of city service response times. Looking at the last 30 days of data, citizens continue to actively report neighborhood issues, but response times vary significantly depending on the type of service requested.

Dumped items remain the highest volume issue with 2,270 reports in the past month. On average, it is taking the city 5.7 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti is the second most reported issue with 1,010 calls, showing an average response time of 39.5 hours.

Other notable service times include street repairs, which generated 257 reports and took an average of 3.2 days to address. Tree maintenance requests saw 370 reports with an average turnaround of 54.8 hours. Dead animal pickups are being handled faster, averaging 13.9 hours across 153 reports.

It is important to note that some categories, like the 964 E-Scooter complaints, show no response time data because they are immediately closed and referred to outside vendors. Unusually low response times across the board may also be due to administrative closures rather than actual physical work being completed.

Accountability starts with visibility. Residents are highly encouraged to keep reporting neighborhood issues using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure the city has an accurate picture of what needs fixing.
