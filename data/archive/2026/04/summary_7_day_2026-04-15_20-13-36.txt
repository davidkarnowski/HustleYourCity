Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 7-day service call data to see how the City of Long Beach is responding to community issues. Here is the breakdown of the most recent reports.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of calls this past week with 594 reports. This was followed by 285 reports for Graffiti and 279 reports concerning E-Scooters. 

When looking at response times, Dumped Items took an average of 3.9 days to address. Traffic Signal issues experienced the longest delays, averaging 5.6 days for a response. Street Light issues averaged 3.1 days, and Abandoned Shopping Carts took about 3.4 days. Graffiti reports were handled more quickly, averaging 45.5 hours.

Some categories showed very fast turnaround times. Animal Services and Dead Animal Pickup both averaged under 6 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, encourage accountability by reporting it directly through the Go Long Beach service application. 

This data summary was updated at April 15, 2026, 1:10 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 15, 2026, 11:00 AM PDT.
