Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service data for requests made in the last 90 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were for dumped items (6,067 requests), graffiti (3,434 requests), and e-scooters (2,726 requests).

For closed requests, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 5 days. Reports of illegally dumped items took an average of about 13 days to be resolved. Faster responses were seen for animal-related issues, with dead animal pickups averaging about 16 hours and other animal services calls averaging about 14 hours. Longer wait times included marina repairs, which averaged about 3 and a half weeks.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, you can report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
