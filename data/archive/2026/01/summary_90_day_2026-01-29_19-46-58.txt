Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days.

The most frequent requests were for dumped items (6,272 calls) and graffiti (3,352 calls). The average response time to close a dumped item request was about 13 days. For graffiti, the average was about 5 days.

Some services saw much faster responses. The 430 requests for dead animal pickup were completed in an average of about 13 hours. The 187 calls for Animal Services were also handled in about 14 hours.

Infrastructure-related requests took longer. The average for 258 sidewalk repair requests was about 3.5 days, while the 8 requests for marina repair took an average of 5 weeks to close.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure it is logged by the city.
