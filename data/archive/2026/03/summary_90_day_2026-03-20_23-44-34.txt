Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data to keep the public informed on city response times and call volumes.

Over the past 90 days, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests with 6,729 reports. The city took an average of 9.7 days to respond to these cases. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 3,084 calls, averaging a 71-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints followed closely with 2,822 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable service areas include Street Repair, which saw 837 requests and an average response time of 6.6 days. Tree Maintenance had 935 calls, taking about 6.7 days on average to address. Dead Animal Pickup remained relatively fast, with 435 requests handled in an average of 11.8 hours.

On the faster end, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. However, citizens should note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Your reports help track these statistics and hold the local government accountable.

This data summary was updated at March 20, 2026 at 4:40 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 20, 2026 at 4:01 PM PDT.
