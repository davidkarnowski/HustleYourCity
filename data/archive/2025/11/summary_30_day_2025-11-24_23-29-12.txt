Latest 30‑day service request snapshot for Long Beach

In the past month the city logged 5,294 service calls across all categories. The most common issue was Graffiti with 1,412 reports and an average response time of 71.6 hours (about 3 days). Dumped Items generated 1,975 calls; these took the longest to address, averaging 159 hours (≈ 6.6 days). Tree Maintenance was the fastest to resolve, with 225 calls and an average response of only 10.7 hours. Light outages (242 calls) were handled in about 40 hours, while Street Repair (204 calls) averaged 21.3 hours.

Overall, response times ranged from under an hour for a few categories to over a week for larger issues. These figures help residents see how quickly the city is addressing reported problems.

If you notice an issue, report it promptly through the Go Long Beach service app so the city can keep improving response times.
