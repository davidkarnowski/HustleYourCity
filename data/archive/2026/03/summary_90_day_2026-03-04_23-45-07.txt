The latest 90-day snapshot of Long Beach city service data highlights current response times and request volumes. Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 6,569 reports. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 11 and a half days.

Graffiti removal saw 3,093 reports with an average completion time of roughly 3 and a half days. Street Repair requests, which include potholes, totaled 838 and are taking approximately 5 days to resolve on average.

Faster response times were noted for Dead Animal Pickup. With 432 total reports, the average response time is roughly 13 and a half hours.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city to ensure they are tracked in this system.
