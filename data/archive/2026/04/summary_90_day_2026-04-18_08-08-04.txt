Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service request data to keep citizens informed on how the City of Long Beach is handling neighborhood issues.

Dumped Items continue to be the most reported issue, with 6,992 requests in the last 90 days. The city took an average of 8.5 days to respond to these reports. Graffiti was the second most common issue, generating 3,233 requests with an average response time of 56 hours.

Other notable services include Street Repair, which saw 712 calls and an average response time of 8 days, and Tree Maintenance, which had 891 reports and averaged 9 days for a response. 

On the faster side, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, help hold the city accountable and get it fixed. We encourage all residents to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
