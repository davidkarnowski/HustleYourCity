Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at the numbers and how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items generated the highest volume of requests with 593 reports. The city averaged a 50-hour response time for these issues. E-scooter complaints followed closely with 267 reports, all of which were closed and referred to outside agencies. Graffiti was another major concern, bringing in 233 reports with an average response time of 14 hours.

Some services saw very fast turnarounds. Dead animal pickups had 39 reports, all of which were closed with an average response time of 2.5 hours. Citizens should note that unusually low response times may sometimes be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues and help maintain the city.
