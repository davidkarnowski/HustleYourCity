The following summary covers City of Long Beach service requests and response times for the last 30 days.

Dumped Items represented the highest volume of service requests with 2,384 total reports. The average response time for these issues was nearly 8 days.

Graffiti removal received 1,146 reports. The average time to close these requests was 52 hours.

Infrastructure maintenance data shows that Street Repair requests, which include potholes, took 3 days on average to complete. Sidewalk Repair requests were closed in an average of 24 hours.

Tree Maintenance recorded 341 reports with an average response time of 28 hours.

For urgent sanitation issues, Dead Animal Pickup requests were resolved in approximately 9 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
