Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data from the last 90 days.

The most common service request was for "Dumped Items", with over 6,400 reports. The average response time for these requests was about 13.5 days. The second most common request was for "Graffiti" removal, which averaged a response time of about 4.5 days.

Some of the quickest responses were for "Dead Animal Pickup" and "Animal Services", which were both handled in an average of about 12 hours. The data indicates all requests in these categories were closed, which can reflect efficient work or administrative closures. Requests for "E-Scooter" issues, totaling over 2,600, were referred to scooter companies and do not have a city response time.

To report issues like graffiti, dumped items, or sidewalk repairs, use the city's Go Long Beach service application.
