Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest 90-day accountability update on City of Long Beach service requests. This summary breaks down recent service call totals and response times to keep the public informed.

Over the last 90 days, Dumped Items led the request totals with 6,970 reports, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti followed with 3,230 reports, averaging a response time of 55.7 hours. E-Scooter issues also generated a high volume with 2,847 reports.

Some services saw rapid turnarounds. Dead Animal Pickup averaged 9.6 hours across 468 calls, and Animal Services averaged 14.7 hours for 253 calls. Business License and Permit concerns showed an unusually low response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low responses may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Other services required more time. Tree Maintenance requests averaged 9 days for a response across 886 calls. Street Repair took an average of 8 days for 704 calls, and Park Maintenance averaged 5.8 days for 212 calls.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Your reports help track these statistics and ensure neighborhood concerns are documented.
