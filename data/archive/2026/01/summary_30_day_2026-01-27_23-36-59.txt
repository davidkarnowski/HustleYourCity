Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach service response times based on city data.

In the last month, residents submitted over 6,000 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (2,424 requests), graffiti (1,000), and improperly parked e-scooters (987).

The average time to resolve a dumped item report was about 7.5 days. For graffiti removal, the average response was about 2.5 days. Animal-related services were among the fastest, with requests for dead animal pickup and other animal services closed in an average of 12 hours.

All 987 e-scooter reports were referred to other agencies, likely the scooter companies, and not handled directly by the city.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
