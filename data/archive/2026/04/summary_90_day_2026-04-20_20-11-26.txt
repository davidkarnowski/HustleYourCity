Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data from the last 90 days to keep the community informed on local response times and call volumes.

Dumped Items and Graffiti were the most frequently reported issues. The city received 6,963 reports for Dumped Items, with an average response time of about 8.5 days. Graffiti had 3,255 reports, taking an average of 2.3 days to address.

Response times varied significantly across different services. Tree Maintenance requests took the longest, averaging nearly 9 days for 889 calls. Street Repair followed closely, taking about 7.9 days to resolve 696 reports.

On the faster side, Dead Animal Pickup averaged 9.6 hours for 466 calls. Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response of just 12 minutes, though unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city. Submitting reports helps track service efficiency and holds local agencies accountable.

This data summary was updated at April 20, 2026 1:07 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 20, 2026 11:00 AM PDT.
