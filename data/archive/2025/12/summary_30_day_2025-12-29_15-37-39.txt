Here is a summary of Long Beach service request data from the last 30 days.

The top three issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (1,957 reports), e-scooter issues (1,031 reports), and graffiti (1,002 reports).

The average city response time to close a request for dumped items was about 10 days. Graffiti removal requests were completed in an average of just over 2 days. Animal services requests were handled in an average of about 9 hours.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
