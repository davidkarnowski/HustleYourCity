Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

The most common reports were for "Dumped Items" (540 requests) and "Graffiti" (226 requests). The average response time to close a dumped items report was about 3.3 days, while graffiti reports were closed in about 29 hours on average.

There were 109 requests for street light repairs, with more than half of those still awaiting action. The average close time for completed light repairs this week was about 2.8 days. Animal-related services were handled more quickly, with dead animal pickups averaging 5.5 hours and other animal service calls averaging about 3 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the city's Go Long Beach service application.
