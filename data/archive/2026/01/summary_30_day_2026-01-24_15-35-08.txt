Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service data for the past 30 days.

Over 5,800 service requests were filed by residents. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (2,283 requests), e-scooters (956 requests), and graffiti (935 requests).

Based on the city's data for closed cases, the average response time for dumped items was about 7.7 days. Graffiti removal requests were handled in about 2.8 days on average, while street repair and street light issues were addressed in just over 3 days. Quicker responses were seen for issues like dead animal pickups, which averaged about 12 hours.
