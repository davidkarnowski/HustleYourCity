Hustle Long Beach! has analyzed the latest city service response data for the past 90 days to keep the public informed on call totals and resolution times.

Dumped items lead the service requests with 6,995 reports, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti follows with 3,233 reports, averaging a 56-hour response time. E-scooter complaints also remain high with 2,922 reports.

Tree maintenance requests, totaling 891 reports, take about 9 days on average to address. Street repair issues, with 712 reports, take an average of 8 days. 

Conversely, business license and permit concerns show a response time of just 12 minutes. Unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead animal pickups handle 467 reports with an average response of just under 10 hours.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 18, 2026, 8:52 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 18, 2026, 7:00 AM PDT.
