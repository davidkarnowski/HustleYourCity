Here is a look at Long Beach city service data from the last 30 days.

The most common issues reported by residents were illegally dumped items (1,955 calls), e-scooter issues (1,074 calls), and graffiti (1,063 calls).

Average city response times varied by the type of request. The city addressed reports of illegally dumped items in about 5.5 days. Graffiti removal took about 2.25 days, and street repairs averaged a 3-day response. More urgent requests, such as dead animal pickups, were handled in about 13 hours.

Note that e-scooter complaints are referred directly to the scooter companies, so the city does not track a response time for those issues.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it using the Go Long Beach service application.
