Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 90-day service call data for the City of Long Beach. Here is a look at the current response times and call volumes for local government services.

Dumped items remain the most reported issue over the past 90 days with 6,822 requests. The city takes an average of 9.5 days to respond to these reports. Graffiti is the second most common issue with 3,086 reports, taking an average of 67 hours for a response.

Street repair and tree maintenance both received around 900 calls, with average response times of 6.5 days and 6.3 days, respectively. Dead animal pickups are handled much faster, averaging 11.5 hours for the 439 reports filed. E-scooter complaints generated 2,854 reports, though all were referred to outside agencies. For context on faster closures, business license concerns averaged a response time of just 12 minutes, which may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure to report it using the Go Long Beach service application so the city can track and address it.

This data summary was updated at March 25, 2026, 4:54 AM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at March 25, 2026, 4:00 AM PDT.
