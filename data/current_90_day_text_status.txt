Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests over the past 90 days, based on the latest public data.

Residents submitted over 15,000 service requests during this period. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (over 6,100 calls) and graffiti (over 3,700 calls). The average time for the city to resolve a report for dumped items was about 15 days, while graffiti reports were closed in about 6.5 days.

Park maintenance requests took an average of nearly 18 days to resolve. In contrast, requests for animal services and dead animal pickups were typically resolved in under 24 hours. Note that some unusually fast response times may be due to administrative closures rather than on-site work completion.
