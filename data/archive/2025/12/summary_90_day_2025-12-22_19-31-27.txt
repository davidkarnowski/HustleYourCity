Here is the latest look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 90 days.

The most frequent service requests from residents were for dumped items, with 6,057 reports filed. This was followed by 3,689 reports for graffiti.

Average response times for these top issues were about two weeks for dumped items and just under six days for graffiti removal. Some of the quickest responses were for dead animal pickups and other animal services, which were typically resolved in about 15-16 hours. Slower responses included park maintenance, which averaged over two weeks.

To report issues like these to the city, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.

This data summary was updated at December 22, 2025, 11:28 AM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at December 22, 2025, 11:00 AM PST.
