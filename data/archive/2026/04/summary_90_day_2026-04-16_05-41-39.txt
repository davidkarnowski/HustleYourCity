Hustle Long Beach! community, here is the latest independent review of Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days.

The highest volume of service requests involved Dumped Items with 6,937 reports, followed by Graffiti with 3,247 reports, and E-Scooter issues with 2,925 reports.

For these major categories, the city took an average of 8.6 days to respond to Dumped Items and about 58 hours for Graffiti. Tree Maintenance requests, totaling 893 calls, averaged a response time of 8.7 days. Street Repair saw 706 requests with an average response time of 7.9 days.

Some services showed rapid turnaround times. Dead Animal Pickup averaged a 10-hour response across 459 calls. Business License and Permit Concerns averaged just 12 minutes, though unusually low response times like this may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

Residents are encouraged to continue reporting neighborhood issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at April 15, 2026 at 10:38 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 15, 2026 at 7:00 PM PDT.
