Here is a look at Long Beach city service requests from the past week.

Over the last 7 days, residents submitted 967 service requests. The most common reports were for dumped items (330), graffiti (190), and e-scooters (179).

For requests closed this week, average response times varied by issue. Graffiti removal took about 26 hours, while dumped items were handled in approximately 29 hours. Dead animal pickups were completed in an average of about 8 hours. Animal services requests were closed the fastest, with an average response time of about 3 hours.
