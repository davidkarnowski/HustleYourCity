Here is the latest 30-day snapshot of Long Beach service requests.

In the past month, residents submitted over 5,400 requests for city services. The most frequent reports were for illegally dumped items (2,065 requests), improperly parked e-scooters (930), and graffiti (905).

Average city response times for closed cases include: about 4.5 days for dumped items, just over 3 days for street repairs, and about 2 days for graffiti removal. E-scooter reports are referred directly to the scooter companies for resolution.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, the most effective way to report it is through the Go Long Beach service application.
