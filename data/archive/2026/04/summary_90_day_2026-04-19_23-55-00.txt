Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service request data for the past 90 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling community issues.

Dumped items lead the call volume with 6,967 reports, taking an average of 8.5 days to resolve. Graffiti follows with 3,242 reports, which the city clears in about 56 hours on average. E-scooter complaints also remain high with 2,875 reports.

Some services require longer wait times. Tree maintenance requests are taking about 9 days to address, while street repairs take just over 8 days.

On the faster side, business license and permit concerns show an average response time of just 12 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions. Dead animal pickups are handled in about 9.5 hours.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.

This data summary was updated at April 19, 2026, 4:51 PM PDT and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at April 19, 2026, 3:00 PM PDT.
