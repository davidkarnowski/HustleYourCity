The latest service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across city maintenance categories over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request with 8,494 reports, showing an average response time of approximately 18 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 3,012 reports and an average response time of about 3 days. Tree maintenance saw 849 reports with an average response time of nearly 4 days.

For smaller issues, response times remain efficient. Dead animal pickups averaged about 7 minutes, and animal services requests averaged about 12 minutes over the same 90-day period.

Please note that unusually low response times in some categories may reflect administrative closures rather than completed field work. Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
