Here is the latest 90-day summary of Long Beach service requests based on city data.

Over the past 90 days, residents submitted more than 16,300 service requests. The most common reports were for Dumped Items (6,337), Graffiti (3,275), and E-Scooter issues (2,711).

Average city response times for top issues include approximately 2 weeks for Dumped Items and about 5 days for both Graffiti and Street Repair. Animal-related services, like Dead Animal Pickup, were among the fastest, with an average response of about 12.5 hours. It's important to note that reported response times can vary, and some quick closures may be for administrative reasons rather than on-site work completion.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.

This data summary was updated at February 3, 2026, at 11:52 PM PST and is based on data published by the City of Long Beach at February 3, 2026, at 11:00 PM PST.
