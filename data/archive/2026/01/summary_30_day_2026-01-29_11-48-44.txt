Here is a look at Long Beach city service response data for the past 30 days.

Over the last month, residents submitted more than 5,400 service requests through the Go Long Beach system. The most common reports were for illegally dumped items (2,403 calls), graffiti (993 calls), and e-scooter issues (967 calls).

The average response time for graffiti removal was about 2.5 days. Street repair requests were handled in just under 3 days on average. For illegally dumped items, the most reported issue, the average response time was about 8 days. Quicker responses were recorded for dead animal pickups, which averaged about 12 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
