Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at how the City of Long Beach is handling citizen reports.

Dumped items were the most reported issue over the last week with 567 total calls. Of those, 437 remain in new status. For the items addressed, the average response time was 66 hours. Graffiti was the second most common issue with 229 reports. The city closed 179 of these graffiti cases, averaging a response time of 23.4 hours.

Other notable service requests included 139 e-scooter complaints, all of which were referred to outside agencies. The city also received 52 tree maintenance requests with an average response time of 12.6 hours, and 32 dead animal pickup requests averaging 5.1 hours. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report local issues directly to the city.
