Here is the latest 30-day summary of Long Beach city service requests and response times.

In the past month, the most frequent service requests were for dumped items (2,056 reports), e-scooters (987 reports), and graffiti (890 reports).

Average response times for these top issues varied. The average time to close a report for dumped items was about 7 days. Graffiti removal requests were addressed in a little over 2 days on average. For urgent issues like dead animal pickups, the average response time was about 13 hours. Slower responses included traffic signal repairs, which took an average of 5 days to complete.

To report issues like these, use the Go Long Beach service application on your smartphone.
