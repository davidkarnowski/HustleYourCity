Hustle Long Beach community, here is your latest accountability update on city service response times over the past 7 days.

Dumped items led the service requests this week with 478 reports. The city took an average of 3.4 days to respond. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 275 calls, averaging a 2-day response time. E-scooter complaints reached 269, all of which were referred to outside operators.

Some services showed very fast turnaround times. Running water reports averaged just 78 minutes, and dead animal pickups averaged 2 hours. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual physical work completion.

Other notable areas include sidewalk repairs, which saw 45 reports with a 10-hour average response, and street repairs, which had 36 reports averaging 23 hours. Abandoned shopping carts took an average of 3.3 days to address across 10 reports.

Help keep Long Beach accountable and well-maintained. If you see an issue, report it directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
