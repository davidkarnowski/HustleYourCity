In the past 90 days Long Beach recorded about 16,400 service calls across all categories.

Fast response times were seen for Animal Services (average 19 hours) and Dead Animal Pickup (average 18 hours). Graffiti requests were handled in roughly 147 hours on average – a big improvement from the all‑time average of 351 hours.

The longest waits were for Dumped Items (average 356 hours, about 15 days) and Street Repair (average 122 hours, about 5 days). Sidewalk Repair took about 89 hours and Traffic Signals about 116 hours.

These figures show where the city is performing well and where response times could be faster. Residents can help by reporting issues promptly through the Go Long Beach service app.
