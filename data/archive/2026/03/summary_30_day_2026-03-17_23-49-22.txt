Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest 30-day city service data. Here is a look at the current response times and request volumes for the City of Long Beach.

Over the past month, Dumped Items generated the highest volume of service calls with 2,205 reports. The city took an average of 5 days to respond to these requests. Graffiti was the second most reported issue with 1,056 calls, averaging a 38-hour response time. E-Scooter complaints accounted for 875 reports, all of which were referred to outside agencies.

Other notable response times include Street Repair, which saw 262 requests and averaged a response time of just over 3 days. Trash and Debris complaints averaged 4 days for a response, while Marina Repair requests took about 7.5 days.

On the faster end, Dead Animal Pickup averaged a 14-hour response time across 145 reports. Business License and Permit Concerns showed an average response time of just 3 minutes. Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Citizens are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city and help keep the community well-maintained.
