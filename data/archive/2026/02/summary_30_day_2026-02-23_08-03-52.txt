An evaluation of City of Long Beach service requests over the past 30 days reveals current response times for high-volume issues.

Dumped Items remains the most frequent report category with 2,224 total calls recorded. The average response time for these requests is currently approximately 7 days.

Graffiti removal received 1,115 reports during this period. The data shows an average completion time of just over 2 days.

Infrastructure repairs show varied response times. Street Repair requests are averaging 3 days to address, while Tree Maintenance is currently showing a turnaround of roughly 26 hours.

For public health issues, Dead Animal Pickup is averaging 13.5 hours per request. However, general Trash & Debris reports are taking approximately 4 days to resolve.

Residents are encouraged to report maintenance and safety issues directly to the city using the Go Long Beach service application to ensure these statistics reflect the needs of the community.
