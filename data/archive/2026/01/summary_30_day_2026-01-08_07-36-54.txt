Here's the latest look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 30 days.

Illegally dumped items were the most reported issue with nearly 1,900 requests, followed by graffiti and e-scooter reports, each with over 900 requests.

The average time to close a request for dumped items was about 7 days. Graffiti reports were addressed more quickly, with an average response time of just over 2 days. Street light repairs took nearly 4 days on average. Faster responses included dead animal pickups, which were handled in about 15 hours.

Note: Some response times may reflect administrative closures and not necessarily the completion of physical work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
