Here is a look at Long Beach city service response times over the past 90 days based on the city's public data.

Over 15,000 service requests were submitted by residents. The most frequent reports were for dumped items (5,951 requests), graffiti (3,544 requests), and illegally parked e-scooters (2,654 requests).

For closed cases, the average response time for graffiti removal was about 4.5 days. Reports of dumped items took an average of around 13.5 days to resolve, while street repair requests were addressed in about 7.5 days. The city responded fastest to reports of dead animal pickups, with an average completion time of about 15.6 hours.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, report it to the city using the Go Long Beach service application.
