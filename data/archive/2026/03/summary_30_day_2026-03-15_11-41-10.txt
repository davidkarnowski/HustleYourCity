Hustle Long Beach community, here is the latest 30-day review of city service requests and response times.

Dumped Items generated the highest volume of requests over the past month with 2,121 reports. The city averaged a response time of about 5 days for these issues, successfully closing 1,398 of those cases.

Graffiti reports were the second most common issue, totaling 1,044 requests. The city responded to these in just under 2 days on average, with 922 cases closed.

Other notable service categories include Tree Maintenance with 482 reports and an average response time of about 2 days, and Street Repair with 261 reports taking roughly 3 days to address. Dead Animal Pickup received 149 requests and averaged a 14.5-hour response time.

Some categories showed unusually fast resolution times. For example, Business License and Permit Concerns averaged a response time of just 3 minutes. Please keep in mind that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures rather than actual work completions.

If you see an issue in your neighborhood, make sure it gets counted. Encourage your neighbors to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues directly to the city.
