The latest service data for Long Beach shows significant activity across city maintenance categories over the past 90 days.

Dumped items remain the most frequent request, with 8,176 reports logged. While many are addressed, the average response time for these items is approximately 15 days. Graffiti removal continues to be a high-volume service with 2,787 reports and an average response time of about 64 hours.

Tree maintenance saw 1,016 reports in the last 90 days, with an average response time of about 4 days. Animal services and dead animal pickups continue to show some of the fastest response times, averaging between 6 and 11 hours.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach mobile application to report issues directly to the city. This helps ensure that service requests are tracked and addressed efficiently.
