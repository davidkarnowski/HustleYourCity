Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service response times for the last 90 days.

Dumped Items represent the highest volume of service requests with 6,470 total reports. The average time to close these requests is currently 12.8 days. Graffiti reports followed with 3,106 total calls, showing an average response time of 3.8 days.

Infrastructure data shows that Street Repair requests, which totaled 803, are being resolved in 4.6 days on average. Tree Maintenance requests are taking slightly longer, with an average response time of 6.3 days across 783 reports.

The fastest physical service responses were for Dead Animal Pickup at 12.7 hours and Animal Services at 13.3 hours. Administrative responses for Business Licenses and Permit Concerns were the quickest overall, averaging 12 minutes.

Residents are encouraged to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
