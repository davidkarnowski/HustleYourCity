Hustle Long Beach! community update on city service requests for the past week.

Dumped Items remained the most frequent report with 478 total calls. The average response time for these issues was approximately 1 day and 5 hours.

Graffiti removal teams responded to 239 reports with an average time of roughly 1 day and 10 hours. Street Repair issues, totaling 40 reports, showed an average response time of 21 hours.

Dead Animal Pickup services averaged 3 hours and 12 minutes across 31 reports. Light repair requests took just over 2 days on average.

Please note that unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.

Residents should continue to use the Go Long Beach service application to report issues to the city.
