Hustle Long Beach! has reviewed the latest city service response data for the past 7 days. Here is a look at the numbers and how long it takes for the city to address community concerns.

Dumped items continue to generate the highest volume of service requests. In the last week, there were 582 reports for dumped items, with an average response time of 58 hours. E-scooter complaints followed with 257 reports, though these are typically closed and referred immediately. Graffiti removal saw 215 requests, with the city responding in roughly 25 hours on average.

Street and sidewalk repairs combined for 95 requests, taking between 18 and 31 hours for a response. Dead animal pickups accounted for 37 requests and were handled in about 2.6 hours. Reports for illegal fireworks took nearly 5 days to process. Keep in mind that unusually fast response times may sometimes reflect administrative closures rather than actual work completions.
