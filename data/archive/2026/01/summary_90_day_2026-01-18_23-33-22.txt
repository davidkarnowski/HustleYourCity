Here's a look at Long Beach city service requests and response times over the past 90 days.

The most common reports from residents were for dumped items (6,045 requests), graffiti (3,405 requests), and e-scooter issues (2,727 requests).

Average response times varied by issue. Dumped items took about 12 and a half days to resolve, while graffiti removal averaged around 4 and a half days. Quicker responses included dead animal pickups, which averaged about 15 hours. Longer waits were seen for marina repairs, averaging about 3 and a half weeks. Some unusually fast times, like the 2-hour average for damaged drinking fountains, may reflect administrative closures rather than on-site work.

See an issue in your neighborhood? Reporting it through the Go Long Beach app is the most effective way to get it into the city's queue.
