Here is the latest summary of Long Beach city service requests from the past 90 days.

The most frequent reports from residents were for dumped items (6,278 reports), graffiti (3,339 reports), and e-scooter issues (2,680 reports).

The average response time to close a report for dumped items is currently about 13 days. Graffiti removal requests average about 5 days for completion. More urgent requests, such as dead animal pickups, were resolved in about 13 hours on average. Street repair requests took an average of 5.5 days to close.

To report an issue in your neighborhood, use the Go Long Beach service application.
