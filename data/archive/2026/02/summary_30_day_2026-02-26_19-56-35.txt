The latest data from the City of Long Beach shows service request activity over the past 30 days.

Dumped Items remain the highest volume category with 2,256 total reports. The average response time for these requests is approximately 6 days.

Graffiti reports totaled 1,132, with an average response time of just over 2 days.

Street Repair requests saw 255 reports with an average wait time of nearly 4 days. Tree Maintenance had 419 reports with a response time of about 1.5 days.

Some categories show very fast closure rates. For example, Business License concerns averaged 12 minutes. Unusually low response times may be due to administrative closures and not actual work completions.
